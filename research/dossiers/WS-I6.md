## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides **how HarnessHarness guarantees that no model-, provider-, task- or instrument-conditioned rule exists without a stated deficiency hypothesis, evidence it can grade, an owner it can reach, an expiry it can observe and a removal test it can execute — and how that guarantee is maintained over time by a service rather than by inspection.** Concretely: (a) the **debt-home inventory** `DebtHomes/1` — the closed list of record kinds that carry an `AssumptionDebtRecord`, each with its IR binding, its default expiry condition and its removal-test kind; (b) **additive fields** on `AssumptionDebtRecord/1` (typed evidence refs, a typed hypothesis with a closed deficiency class and a predicted effect, scope, runway, revalidation policy, a typed removal test) that leave every ratified field and enum untouched; (c) the **evidence grade** `hypothesized | evidenced | confirmed` as a *derived* axis orthogonal to the ratified status, and the **runway/probation** rule that turns "hypothesized forever" into an expiry; (d) the **status-derivation rule** (stored status = value at seal; current status = fold of ledger transitions) and the `DebtIndex` as a derived view in the ADR-0037 `StaleIndex` family; (e) the **trigger model** over all homes — ADR-0126's observables for model-version change, plus *profile change*, *evidence supersession* and *runway* — evaluated by one pure `evaluate_debt`; (f) the **removal-test contract** `RemovalTest` as a closed sum with `validate_removal_test` (dry-run expansion at `seal`/`register`, so "retirement tests execute" is a static gate) and `schedule_removal_test` (instantiation of a registered `ExperimentSpec` template, executed only through WS-J3); (g) **retirement** as a human-sealed supersession referencing a `RetirementRecord`; (h) the `DebtReport` record, the `assumption_debt_health` metric family and the `DebtPolicy` data document; (i) the **spec-debt register** — the same record applied to every ratified ADR (RK-06).

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: the profile rule inventory, probes, `evaluate_expiry` for profile rules and the profile status events (**WS-C3**, ADR-0124…0126 — consumed verbatim; the manager *calls* `evaluate_expiry` for the profile home and generalises its shape to the other homes); the wrapper admission pipeline and `retire_surface` (**WS-E2**, ADR-0091); the experiment engine, the `retirement` kind's diff-shape refusal and matched-budget enforcement (**WS-J3**, ADR-0154…0156 — the manager authors specs and never runs arms); the analysis that produces a `ComparisonReport` and the non-inferiority estimator (**WS-J4/I2**, ADR-0157/0158; margins are OQ-301); the evolution pipeline whose retirement gate *is* the removal test (**WS-I5**); causal attribution when several rules interact (**WS-I7**); consolidation into weights (**WS-I8** — this dossier supplies only the hook, §6.5); the web view catalogue (**WS-K2** — this dossier supplies the `DebtReport` record and asks where it renders, §8); every language, runtime or library choice (ADR-0050 §8).

## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**F1 — Dead weight is real, common, and today found only by inspection.** Anthropic's context resets became "dead weight" when the same harness ran on a stronger model and were found by manual testing (S-047); Cursor lists at least five removed guardrails and says they are "mostly long gone" without describing a retirement process (S-049); codex carries per-slug reminder templates, a compatibility hash and a retirement date but no reason, owner or test for any of them (S-107). No audited harness — codex, OpenHands, opencode, pi, goose, gemini-cli, inspect_ai — records *why* a model-conditioned rule exists. **Implication:** the manager's first job is not sophistication but *coverage* — a schema-level rule that every debt home is complete (§6.1), which the audit shows no production system has.

**F2 — The disciplines that work elsewhere have four parts, and each audited system has at most two.** Chromium: owners + milestone expiry + generated enforcement, no evidence, manual removal. OpenHands: reason + deadline + runway + CI gate, no evidence, no test that the workaround is still needed. litellm: dates + status buckets + alerts. Piranha: owner + expected treatment + automated removal diff, no expiry. Feature-toggle practice: expiration + time-bomb test. The union — *hypothesis with expected effect, evidence, reachable owner, observable expiry with runway, executable removal test, enforced at build time, removal as a typed diff* — is what T-LCD-05 asks for and what ADR-0020/0126 ratified for profiles. **Implication (§6.2/6.3):** the manager is the *union* generalised to every home, with the two pieces no precedent has: evidence that can be *graded* and a removal test that must *expand* before the rule may be sealed.

**F3 — The record is already carried by at least fourteen record kinds across the ratified ADRs, under six spellings.** The debt record appears on `ProfileRule.debt` and `ModelProfile.expiry` (ADR-0020), `HarnessRule.assumption_debt` (ADR-0016), `ext` entries (ADR-0020 §5), `DialectRule`/`WireDialect` (ADR-0119), `RoutingPolicy.conditioned_rules` (ADR-0121), `HarnessRule{kind: critic_gate}` and `CalibrationRecord.expiry` (ADR-0117), synthesized surfaces (ADR-0091), `FittedSurfaceReport.debt_record_ref` (ADR-0160), `AdapterRecord` and the `hh-hosting/1` version (ADR-0166), the budget-enforcement derivation table (ADR-0165), `experimental` contract items and deprecations (ADR-0178), the ACP v1 compatibility profile and MCP protocol eras (ADR-0097/0098), the `hot_path` ceiling (ADR-0181/OQ-407), the cache retention class (ADR-0127), the Profile Compiler itself (ADR-0126 §7). Field spellings vary: `removal_test_ref` (ADR-0020), `removal_test` (ADR-0178), a free-text `expiry_condition: "Δ task_success … ≤ margin"` (ADR-0117) outside the closed enum, a procedure-valued `removal_test_ref: re-fit on fresh rows` (ADR-0160). **Implication (§6.1, §8):** a closed **debt-home inventory** with one record schema and one field spelling is a normalisation the synthesis needs anyway; every non-conforming spelling becomes a CF row rather than a silent second schema.

**F4 — Status cannot live only in an immutable record.** Registry records and sealed definitions are immutable (ADR-0037 I1; ADR-0151 R1); `evaluate_expiry` "proposes transitions; the registry appends them" (ADR-0126 §2). So the `status` field inside a debt record is the value *at seal*, and the *current* status is a fold over `status.changed` events — exactly how ADR-0037's `StaleIndex` derives "stale-by-dependency". **Implication (§6.3):** the manager owns a derived `DebtIndex` view (never a store of record) and never a fourth registry status axis (ADR-0151 R5).

**F5 — "Hypothesized → evidenced" is an evidence axis, not a status.** The brief's lifecycle and ADR-0020's `active | expiring | expired | retired` are orthogonal: a rule is `active` whether its evidence is an inspection note or a matched-budget report. What distinguishes them is the *grade* of `evidence_refs` — nothing instrument-produced (`hypothesized`), at least one probe/compliance/conformance record (`evidenced`), or a `ComparisonReport` under a `MatchSpec` showing the rule's artifact benefit for the scoped beneficiary (`confirmed`). The grade is a pure function of the refs and therefore derived, not stored. **Implication (§6.2):** `evidence_grade` is derived; the brief's lifecycle reads `active/hypothesized → active/evidenced → expiring → expired → retired`; the manager's *probation* rule (F7) is what makes `hypothesized` a state you cannot stay in.

**F6 — Model-version change is the primary trigger, but it is not the only one, and two of the others are already ratified in pieces.** ADR-0126 fixes four observables for `model_version_change`; ADR-0160 §6 makes an evidence ref to an *expired* surface report a trigger ("makes that rule `expiring`"); ADR-0037 makes a revoked dependency `stale-by-dependency`; WS-C3 §6.3 makes `retirement_at` a profile trigger; S-096 shows that *fine-tuning the beneficiary* is a model-version change from the harness's point of view. Nothing yet covers **profile change** as a trigger for *definition-level* rules — a `HarnessRule.conditioned_on: ProfileRef` whose profile is superseded at sameness L2+ or retired (ADR-0037 ladder) is a rule whose scope no longer exists. **Implication (§6.5):** one trigger table over all homes with three families — *beneficiary observables* (ADR-0126, unchanged), *dependency supersession* (profile, evidence, suite, calibration, snapshot — via the registry's lifecycle events), *time* (`date`, runway, `evidence_max_age`).

**F7 — Runway and probation are the two time rules the precedents converged on independently.** OpenHands refuses a deprecation without ≥ 5 minor releases of runway and fails CI when it is overdue; Chromium expires unconditionally at `M-3` after a graded window; litellm buckets by `days_until`. None has the opposite rule — a bound on how long a workaround may exist *without evidence*. **Implication (§6.2/6.5):** `runway` (minimum time between creation and the earliest `date`/`evidence_refresh_due` expiry; `InsufficientRunway` refusal) and `hypothesized_max_age` (a `hypothesized`-grade record becomes `expiring` when older than the policy's bound) are both `DebtPolicy` data with defaults left to OQ-WS-I6-01.

**F8 — The removal test is executable only if it expands.** ADR-0156's `retirement` kind refuses a diff that is not `{semantic_ops: 1, authority_delta: none, budget_delta: none}`; ADR-0154's `expand` is pure and total; ADR-0126 fixes the verdict. The cheap, static, sufficient check that "retirement tests execute" is therefore: at `seal` (definitions) and `register` (profiles/dialects/adapters), instantiate the record's `RemovalTest` into an `ExperimentSpec` and dry-run `expand` — a refusal (`NotARetirementDiff`, `UnbudgetedArm`, `SplitUnassigned`, `UnsealedArtifact`, an unresolvable template) is `UnexecutableRemovalTest`. This is the feature-toggle "time bomb" and OpenHands' CI gate moved to the point where the rule is created. **Implication (§6.4):** `validate_removal_test` is C0/Stage 1 (static) and the sole acceptance test for "retirement tests execute" at Stage 1–3; actually *running* one is Stage 3 for the two minimal profiles (ADR-0126 (e)).

**F9 — Retirement must be a human-sealed supersession, not an automatic deletion, and the product needs a record for it.** ADR-0126 rejected automatic deletion (ADR-0002 governance); ADR-0037 makes expiry a `supersedes{reason: expiry}` edge; ADR-0017 makes any op on a conditioned rule carry its record; ADR-0035's closed basis list already contains `seal`. "Retired by ADR" therefore means: a new definition/profile version that omits the rule, whose `HirDiff`/`ProfileDiff` classifies as a retirement diff, sealed by a principal (`origin = human`), referencing a `RetirementRecord{removal_test_report_ref, decided_by, rationale}` — no new endorsement basis, no program ADR per rule. An evolution-origin retirement proposal is admissible only as a *proposal* (ADR-0053 D-5) that a human seals. Piranha's 65 %-landed-unchanged result is the evidence that the removal diff can be generated automatically and reviewed cheaply.

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

**Against the discipline as bureaucracy (R1).** Chromium still accumulates flags despite mandatory owners and milestones; Piranha needed ~200 developers and an automated refactorer to delete 1,381 flags; Cursor and Anthropic shipped well by deleting scaffolding when they noticed it. Owners rot, hypotheses become boilerplate, and a `hypothesis: "model is bad at X"` field costs a keystroke and proves nothing. *Why this does not overturn:* (i) HarnessHarness is a research instrument whose novelty claim (c) and T-LCD-05 *are* this discipline (ADR-0006, ADR-0007) — the comparison is not "ship a product" but "run a delete-this-rule experiment on demand"; (ii) the fields that rot are made checkable rather than trusted: `evidence_grade` is derived, `hypothesized` has a bounded age, owners must resolve to a reachable principal (Chromium's rule made a validity check), and the removal test must *expand* at seal (§6.2–6.4); (iii) defaults per home (ADR-0091's expiry defaults; recipe templates per removal-test kind) make the complete record the cheap path, the way codex's `models.json` made data the cheap path. Residual risk: template hypotheses with a plausible `deficiency_class` — mitigated by the compliance-evidence requirement for `evidenced` and recorded as RK-06 lineage.

**Against removal tests as the retirement gate (R2).** A paired `retirement` experiment per rule per model snapshot under `matched_cap` with ≥ 5 replicates on a held-out split is expensive; with dozens of rules and several profiles the manager's schedule would exceed the Lab's instrument budget, and providers change mid-schedule. *Why this does not overturn:* rules without a design simply never reach `retired` (ADR-0126 (d)) — the discipline degrades to "expired with recorded intent", never to silent staleness; the cheapest kinds in the `RemovalTest` sum are static (`zero_uses`, `null_profile_compile`, `probe_pass`) and cost nothing; batch designs sharing one base arm are proposed to J3 (OQ-WS-I6-02); scheduling priority is data (`DebtPolicy.priority`) so the manager spends first on `expired`-and-still-used rules; and the alternative — retiring on a date (OpenHands, Chromium) — is exactly the mechanism S-461 shows retires rules a subset still needs.

**Against retirement at all: co-evolution says re-tune, don't delete (R3).** S-096 (`provisional`) shows a harness optimised around one model *hurts* a model trained to behave differently; the fix was on-policy correction, i.e., keeping the harness and adapting the beneficiary. WHALE/Co-Harness (S-084/S-085, `provisional`) treat harness and weights as coupled coordinates — a rule may be *load-bearing for the pair*. *Why this does not overturn:* the lifecycle has both exits — a failed removal test *revalidates* with the report as evidence (ADR-0126 §5), and a rule re-tuned for a new beneficiary is a new version under `supersedes{edit}` with a new record; the manager never deletes, it schedules tests and records outcomes. The consolidation hook (§6.5) is written so that WS-I8's consolidation is a *model-version change* for the rules it affects, not a retirement. The provisional co-evolution results shape nothing at C0.

**Against one generic event/record over all homes (R4).** ADR-0126 already ratified `model.profile.status.changed`; ADR-0119/0117/0160/0178 each describe their home's expiry in their own words; a generic `lifecycle.debt.status.changed` risks two events for one fact and a second schema for one record. *Why this does not overturn:* CF-327's rule (kind-specific classes remain; generic classes are not duplicated) already governs registry events; the proposal (§6.3) is one *record* schema with one spelling, and one generic event class for homes that have no ratified class, with the profile class kept as the profile home's instance — the choice between "generic + instances" and "one family" is flagged for synthesis (CF-WS-I6-03) rather than decided here.

**Against the spec-debt register (R5).** Applying the record to 184 ADRs is a documentation exercise no one will maintain; ADRs already carry (d) conditionality and (c) disconfirming evidence. *Why this does not overturn:* the register is *derived* from fields the ADRs already carry — it adds only a revalidation trigger and an owner, both already present for ADR-0050 (revalidation triggers 1–6) — and its only enforcement is the readiness gate rule that a C0 ADR whose (a) evidence is `provisional` carries a trigger (already required by doc 3 §3.1). RK-06 names this mitigation; the cost is a table.

**Negative results (audit).** (N1–N4) as in §4c. (N5) The search for 2026 work on "assumption debt"/"stale scaffolding" in agent harnesses found nothing beyond doc 2's citations; the term remains doc 2's and this program's.

### 6.2 `AssumptionDebtRecord/1` — additive fields, the typed hypothesis, evidence grade, runway

Ratified and unchanged: `{rule_id, hypothesis: Text, evidence_refs[], owner, expiry_condition ∈ {model_version_change, date, probe_failure, evidence_refresh_due, experiment_ref}, removal_test_ref, status ∈ {active, expiring, expired, retired}}`. Additive (ADR-0037 L1; ADR-0178 additive rules), all optional at the schema level and required by `DebtPolicy` per home:

- `debt_class ∈ {model_conditioned, provider_conditioned, task_conditioned, instrument_reflexive, spec_decision}` — the inventory column.
- `hypothesis_typed: {subject: {profile_ref | provider_api_family | task_class_ref | component_ref | adr_id}, deficiency_class ∈ DeficiencyClass/1, predicted_effect: {metric_ref (a `MetricDeclaration`), direction ∈ {increase, decrease}, magnitude?: {value, unit}}}` where `DeficiencyClass/1 = {premature_stop (S-047 context anxiety), format_noncompliance (S-460), tool_shape_mismatch (S-049 patch vs replace), name_misalignment (S-177), schema_dialect_gap, context_overflow_handling, loop_or_repetition, instruction_following_gap, claim_grounding_gap (WS-G2), reasoning_replay_incompatibility, latency_or_cost_profile, unknown{Text}}` — closed, ADR-extended; `unknown` is admissible only with `evidence_grade = hypothesized` and is reported by `DebtReport`. The free-text `hypothesis` remains the human statement; `predicted_effect.metric_ref` is what makes the hypothesis testable — the removal test's primary metric defaults to it.
- `scope: {model_selectors: [Selector]?, task_classes: [ref]?, roles ⊆ ModelRole?}` — where the hypothesis is claimed to hold (F2 in WS-C3: a rule copied to another family is a new rule).
- `evidence_refs[]` typed as `EvidenceRef{kind ∈ {source(S-id), inspection(Text with provenance), probe_run(ConformanceRecord ref), compliance_report, conformance_report, comparison_report, calibration_record, fitted_surface_report, experiment_report}, ref, observed_at, tier?: {A, B, C, D}, provisional?: bool}` (the existing untyped refs lift to `kind = source | inspection`).
- `evidence_grade` — **derived, never stored**: `hypothesized` iff no ref of an instrument-produced kind; `evidenced` iff ≥ 1 of `{probe_run, compliance_report, conformance_report, calibration_record}` newer than the record's creation; `confirmed` iff ≥ 1 `comparison_report` under a `MatchSpec` with `benefit_kind = artifact_benefit`, `budget_match.status = matched`, whose arms include the rule and its removal on a beneficiary inside `scope`, showing the predicted effect with an interval excluding 0. A ref to a superseded, revoked or `expired` record does not count (ADR-0037; ADR-0160 §6).
- `expiry: {condition (ratified enum), params: {until?: timestamp, evidence_max_age?: duration, dependency_capabilities?: [capability], design_ref?: ExperimentSpec ref}}` — the parameters ADR-0126 §1 needs per condition, in the record instead of implied.
- `runway: duration` — minimum interval between `created_at` and the earliest time-based expiry; `InsufficientRunway` at `seal`/`register` when `until − created_at < DebtPolicy.min_runway` (OpenHands rule). A record with `evidence_grade = hypothesized` also carries an implicit `until = created_at + DebtPolicy.hypothesized_max_age` (probation; F7).
- `revalidation: {on ⊆ {model_version_change, profile_change, evidence_superseded, schedule(cadence)}, action ∈ {probe, compliance_report, removal_test}}` — what the manager schedules when the record turns `expiring`.
- `removal_test: RemovalTest` (§6.4) — the typed form; `removal_test_ref` remains as the ref to its registered template/design for records created before the typed field.
- `owner: PrincipalRef | TeamRef` — must resolve in the principal registry at `seal`/`register` and be *reachable* by at least one declared notice sink (`OwnerUnreachable` refusal; Chromium's rule).
- `created_by: ProvenanceRecord`, `created_at`, `supersedes?: rule_id` (re-tuning as a new version, never an edit).

Worked example (S-047): `{rule_id: r-ctx-reset, hypothesis: "Sonnet-class beneficiary wraps up prematurely as its context limit approaches", hypothesis_typed: {subject: profile(claude-sonnet-4.5), deficiency_class: premature_stop, predicted_effect: {metric_ref: capability.task_success, direction: increase}}, evidence_refs: [inspection(S-047 §…)], owner: team(runtime), expiry: {condition: model_version_change}, runway: 90d, removal_test: {kind: retirement_experiment, template: lab/retirement-v1, primary_metric: capability.task_success}, status: active}` — `evidence_grade = hypothesized` at creation; the first compliance report (`reset delivered → activated → followed`) makes it `evidenced`; a passed `retirement` experiment on the Opus-class profile is the report Anthropic obtained by hand.

### 6.3 Status derivation, the `DebtIndex` view and events

- **Stored vs current.** `AssumptionDebtRecord.status` is the value at seal/registration. The current status of `(record version_id, rule_id)` is the fold of `status.changed` events with `causes[]` after that version (ADR-0126 §2/§3 for profiles; generalised here). `expiring` and `expired` therefore never require a new record version; `retired` always does (it is a supersession, §6.4). ADR-0151 R5 is honoured: no registry envelope field changes; the current status is a derived view.
- **`DebtIndex`** (derived; the sibling of ADR-0037's `StaleIndex`): `debt_index(registry_snapshot_id, ledger watermark) → [DebtIndexRow{debt_ref{home, version_id, rule_id}, debt_class, owner, stored_status, current_status, evidence_grade, deficiency_class, last_trigger?, next_time_expiry?, removal_test{kind, executable: bool, last_report_ref?, last_verdict?}, used_by: [sealed definition version_ids], expired_used_runs: count, staleness_reasons[]}]` — pure over registry records and the ledger; served by the registry `query` (no new registry operation; a projection).
- **Events.** For homes with a ratified class, that class stands (`model.profile.status.changed` for homes 2–5; `lifecycle.registry.deprecated` for home 14; `lifecycle.contract.deprecated_use`). For homes 1, 6–13, 15–16 propose one class `lifecycle.debt.status.changed{debt_ref, home, from, to, trigger, evidence_ref?, causes[]}` (plane `lifecycle`, `durability = ledger`, observability `events`, producer = kernel/registry) and `lifecycle.debt.removal_test.{scheduled, settled}{debt_ref, experiment_id, verdict?}`; the run-level `model.profile.expired_used` generalises to `lifecycle.debt.expired_used{debt_ref, intent_ref}` on any run compiled with an expired record of any home. Synthesis decides one family vs generic + instances (CF-WS-I6-03).

Operations (language-neutral; lab-side except where noted):
- `validate_removal_test(record, context{sealed definition | profile chain | registry snapshot}) → ok | UnexecutableRemovalTest{reason}` — **kernel-side, at `seal` and `register`** (ADR-0025 `validate_assembly` stage; ADR-0151 `register`): instantiates the kind into its operation *without running it* — for `retirement_experiment` a dry-run `expand` of the instantiated `ExperimentSpec` (must not refuse: `NotARetirementDiff`, `UnbudgetedArm`, `SplitUnassigned`, `UnsealedArtifact`, unresolvable template); for `probe_pass` every `probe_spec_ref` resolves; for `zero_uses` the scope resolves. A record whose test cannot be instantiated is incomplete (T-LCD-05 "removal test: none"). The `retirement` diff shape is checked here, so a rule whose removal would widen authority or loosen a budget can never be sealed as a conditioned rule (it is not one — it is a policy).
- `schedule_removal_test(debt_ref, policy) → experiment_id | Deferred{reason}` — instantiates the template with the record's `scope` beneficiaries and `predicted_effect.metric_ref` as primary metric, registers it through J3 with `charged_to = instrument`, appends `lifecycle.debt.removal_test.scheduled`; refuses (`Deferred`) when the instrument budget node cannot reserve (ADR-0040) or when `DebtPolicy.max_open_removal_tests` is reached; priority by `DebtPolicy.priority` (default: `expired` and used > `expiring` and used > `hypothesized` past probation > scheduled cadence).
- `settle_removal_test(debt_ref, report_ref) → [Transition]` — reads the `ComparisonReport`/probe/scan result, records `RemovalVerdict{debt_ref, kind, verdict ∈ {pass, fail, inconclusive{reason}}, report_ref, settled_at}` as an immutable record, appends `removal_test.settled`, and proposes: `pass` ⇒ eligible for retirement (status unchanged until sealed); `fail` ⇒ `revalidated{evidence_ref = report}` (ADR-0126 §5) — the report becomes a `comparison_report` evidence ref and the grade becomes `confirmed`; `inconclusive` ⇒ reschedule under policy.
- `propose_retirement(debt_ref, verdict_ref) → HirDiff | ProfileDiff` — generates the removal diff (`retire_rule`, `retire_surface`, or `apply`-shaped for definition rules) with `derived-from{hypothesis: "removal test passed", trajectories: [report_ref]}` (ADR-0016 edge); `provenance.origin = evolution | human`; classification must be a retirement diff.
- `retire(debt_ref, diff, RetirementRecord{removal_test_report_ref, verdict_ref, decided_by: ProvenanceRecord{origin: human}, rationale: Text}) → new version` — **kernel-side**: `apply` + `seal` (ADR-0025) or `publish` (ADR-0151) of the version without the rule, carrying `supersedes{reason: expiry}` (ADR-0037 S2) and the `RetirementRecord` ref; appends `status.changed{to: retired}`; refuses `RetirementNotEvidenced` when the verdict is not `pass` on every beneficiary in `scope`, and `AuthorityWideningRequiresHuman`/`NotARetirementDiff` from the ratified checks. An evolution-origin diff may only *propose*; the seal is the ADR-0035 `seal` basis — "retired by ADR" means this record, not a program ADR (CF-WS-I6-05).

Invariants: **D-1** no conditioned rule is sealed or registered without a complete record whose removal test validates; **D-2** the manager never opens a run or applies a diff — it authors specs and proposals and reads reports; **D-3** every removal-test run is an ordinary J3 experiment charged `instrument`, matched-cap, on a held-out split, never on the tasks that authored the rule (ADR-0125 §4 held-out rule generalised); **D-4** `retired` is reachable only through a human-sealed supersession referencing a `pass` verdict; **D-5** a `fail` verdict is evidence, never a punishment — it raises the grade; **D-6** `expired_used` runs are excluded from headline `ComparisonReport`s unless the `Design` declares dead-weight testing (ADR-0126 §3), and the analysis engine reads the exclusion from the results row annotation, never from the manager.

### 6.5 The trigger model over all homes; `evaluate_debt`

`evaluate_debt(record, home, observables, registry_snapshot, ledger_watermark, now, policy) → [Transition{from, to, trigger, evidence_ref?, causes[]}]` — pure; the registry/kernel appends the events; for homes 2–5 it delegates to ADR-0126's `evaluate_expiry` unchanged and merges the profile-level transitions.

| trigger family | fires when | affects | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **beneficiary observables** (`model_version_change`, `probe_failure`, `experiment_ref`) | ADR-0126 §1 (i)–(iv), dependency-capability DRIFT/UNSUPPORTED, a removal report | homes 1 (profile-scoped), 2–5, 7, 8, 10; 9 and 12 on judge/participant snapshot change | ADR-0126 (unchanged); WS-C1 observables |
| **profile change** (new) | a `HarnessRule.conditioned_on: ProfileRef` (or `scope.model_selectors`) points at a profile version superseded at sameness ≥ L2 (`supersedes{edit}` with `semantic_ops ≠ ∅`), `retired`, or with `retirement_at` passed; the definition's `profile_binding` re-binds a role to a different profile `semantic_id` | homes 1, 8, 10 (definition-level rules) → `expiring`; a retired profile → `expired` after `grace_period` | ADR-0037 ladder; ADR-0124 §6; registry `lifecycle.registry.*` events |
| **evidence supersession** (new, generalising ADR-0160 §6) | any `EvidenceRef` resolves to a record that is superseded, revoked, `expired`, or whose `SuiteValidityRecord`/labelled set is superseded (ADR-0144, ADR-0117) | any home → `expiring`; grade recomputed | ADR-0037 `StaleIndex`; ADR-0160 §6 |
| **time** (`date`, `evidence_refresh_due`, probation) | `now ≥ expiry.params.until`; `max(age(instrument evidence)) > evidence_max_age` (OQ-304: academic Tier-A refs never age out on their own — they are `source` refs, not instrument evidence, so `evidence_refresh_due` fires only when the *instrument* evidence is stale or absent); `hypothesized` grade older than `hypothesized_max_age` | any home → `expiring` | OpenHands/Chromium/litellm precedents; `DebtPolicy` |
| **consolidation hook** (for WS-I8) | a beneficiary produced by consolidation/fine-tuning is registered as a new `ModelSnapshotRecord` bound to the same profile selector | fires *as* `model_version_change` (observable (i)/(iii)); no enum growth; the lesson's harness rule must pass its removal test on the consolidated beneficiary before it may be retired as "absorbed into weights" | S-096 (`provisional`, mechanism); ADR-0126 |

Transitions are exactly ADR-0126 §2's machine, with two clarifications: `expiring → active` (`revalidated`) requires an evidence ref of grade ≥ `evidenced` newer than the trigger for `model_conditioned` records (a fresh inspection note does not revalidate); `expired → active` requires both a refresh and a probe pass (ratified). Version strings never fire anything (ratified).

### 6.6 `DebtReport`, health metrics, `DebtPolicy`, notices (K2/I2)

- **`DebtReport`** (a persisted, provenance-bearing record — ontology §4g — so K2 may render it and the CLI prints the same record, ADR-0170 §4): `DebtReport{scope ∈ {definition(version_id), profile(version_id), bundle(bundle_id), fleet(registry_snapshot_id)}, watermark, rows: [DebtIndexRow], counts_by: {status × debt_class × evidence_grade × deficiency_class × owner}, buckets: {overdue (past runway/probation), imminent (time expiry within DebtPolicy.warn_within), scheduled, awaiting_seal (pass verdict, not retired)}, health: AssumptionDebtHealth, generated_from}`.
- **`AssumptionDebtHealth`** — the WS-I2 evolvability metric `assumption_debt_health` made concrete as `MetricDeclaration`s (`applies_to_classes = {native}`, `requires_observability = {ledger}` where run-derived, else static; hosted rows `n/a{class}`): `debt.records_total`, `debt.complete_rate` (records passing `validate_removal_test` ÷ all — 1.0 by construction after Stage 1, kept as the regression sentinel), `debt.hypothesized_share`, `debt.confirmed_share`, `debt.expired_count`, `debt.expired_used_runs` (from `lifecycle.debt.expired_used`), `debt.overdue_count`, `debt.removal_tests_executed_rate` (records with ≥ 1 settled verdict in the window ÷ records due), `debt.retirement_pass_rate` (pass ÷ settled), `debt.median_age`, `debt.owner_unreachable_count` (0 by construction), plus C3's `profile.ext_field_count`. Reported per bundle in the scorecard's evolvability dimension (ADR-0045) and per fleet in `DebtReport`.
- **`DebtPolicy`** (MUST-data, ADR-0024; a `HarnessRule`-class record layered with `authority_cap` like `RegistryPolicy`): `{required_fields_by_home, min_runway, hypothesized_max_age, evidence_max_age (OQ-304), warn_within, grace_period, max_open_removal_tests, priority: ordered rule list, notice_sinks: [SinkRef], schedule: {on_triggers, cadence}, dead_weight_designs_allowed: bool}`.
- **Notices.** `DebtNotice{debt_ref, owner, bucket, reason, next_action, report_ref}` is emitted by the manager to declared *sinks* (ADR-0044 sink classification; ADR-0172 the surface is a sink) — never a model context item; litellm's alert bucketing is the shape. Owner reachability is a `seal`/`register` check, so a notice always has a recipient.
- **Where it renders (K2).** `DebtReport` is a ratified record type, so ADR-0170 §2's admission rule is met; ADR-0170 §8 says evolution proposals render "through V3/V6 (no new view kind)" and V4 "shows opacity and debt records". Proposal: debt rows render in V4 (per definition), V10 (per registry record) and V6 (per experiment's `retirement` reports); a fleet `DebtReport` is a V10 tab, not a V13 — flagged for K2/synthesis (CF-WS-I6-04).

### 6.7 Reflexivity

The manager's own record: `{hypothesis: "conditioned rules go stale faster than teams notice (S-047, S-049), so a scheduler that tests removal finds dead weight a review would miss", hypothesis_typed: {subject: component(debt_manager), deficiency_class: unknown{"human review cadence"}, predicted_effect: {metric_ref: debt.retirement_pass_rate, direction: increase}}, evidence_refs: [S-047, S-049 (inspection); Piranha (source)], owner: WS-I6 → the instrument team, expiry: {condition: evidence_refresh_due, params: {evidence_max_age: policy}}, removal_test: {kind: no_dead_weight_found, window: N model-version changes}}` — if over `N` model-version changes across the fleet no removal test the manager scheduled ever passed, the manager found no dead weight and is itself dead weight: `retired` is its honest end state, as for the Profile Compiler (ADR-0126 §7).

### 6.8 Acceptance-criteria sketch and T-LCD tests

- **AC-I6-1 No rule without a hypothesis (T-LCD-05; C0/Stage 1).** One fixture per home in `DebtHomes/1` (17 homes; homes 6, 9, 11–16 as registry records): a record missing `hypothesis`, `owner`, `expiry_condition` or `removal_test`, or with `debt_class`/`deficiency_class` outside the closed enums, is refused at the home's ratified refusal point (`ConditionedRuleIncomplete`, `LinkError{missing_debt_record}`, `SurfaceSynthesisRefused`, `ExperimentalRequired`/schema); a record placed on an unlisted kind is `UnknownDebtHome`; kernel-derived constraints carry no record and pass.
- **AC-I6-2 Retirement tests execute (T-LCD-05/-14; C0/Stage 1 static, Stage 3 executable).** `validate_removal_test` at `seal`/`register` refuses `UnexecutableRemovalTest` for: a `retirement_experiment` whose diff is not `{semantic_ops: 1, authority_delta: none, budget_delta: none}`, one without a `MatchSpec`, one whose template is unresolvable, a `probe_pass` with an unknown probe; at Stage 3 every rule of the two ADR-0020 minimal profiles and the Stage-0 fixture's conditioned `HarnessRule`s has a `retirement` experiment that `register`s, runs on the Stage-3 held-out split and yields a `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind: artifact_benefit, budget_match.status}` and a `RemovalVerdict`.
- **AC-I6-3 Trigger fidelity (T-LCD-05/-01; C0/Stage 3 with injected observables, C1/Stage 5 live).** Injected served-model drift, compatibility-token change, dependency-capability DRIFT, profile supersession at L2, an evidence ref to an `expired` surface report, a passed `until`, and a `hypothesized` record past probation produce exactly the §6.5 transitions as ledger events with `causes[]`; a changed model *version string* with unchanged observables produces none; a fresh `inspection` ref does not revalidate a `model_conditioned` record.
- **AC-I6-4 Retirement is human-sealed (T-LCD-05; ADR-0002; C0/Stage 3).** An `origin = evolution` diff removing a rule without a `pass` verdict on every scoped beneficiary is refused `RetirementNotEvidenced`; with a `pass` verdict it is admitted only as a proposal; a human `seal` with a `RetirementRecord` yields a version with `supersedes{reason: expiry}` and `status.changed{to: retired}`; the retired rule remains resolvable in `audit | reproduce` modes (ADR-0037 S2).
- **AC-I6-5 Expired-used stratification (T-LCD-14; C0/Stage 3).** A bundle compiled with an `expired` record and an `intent_ref` produces `lifecycle.debt.expired_used`; its rows are excluded from a headline `ComparisonReport` unless the `Design` declares dead-weight purpose; without `intent_ref`, `link` refuses.
- **AC-I6-6 One record, two surfaces (T-LCD-12; C1/Stage 4).** `DebtReport` for a fixture registry is byte-identical from the CLI and the web client's operation; `n/a{class}` for hosted rows; every row links to its record `version_id` and last `report_id`.
- **AC-I6-7 Health metrics (T-LCD-15; C0/Stage 3).** `AssumptionDebtHealth` computed per bundle in the evolvability dimension; `debt.complete_rate = 1.0` for any sealed bundle; hosted configurations render `n/a`.
- **AC-I6-8 Runway and reachability (C0/Stage 1).** A record with `until − created_at < min_runway` is refused `InsufficientRunway`; an `owner` that does not resolve to a principal reachable by a declared sink is refused `OwnerUnreachable`.
- **AC-I6-9 Reflexive records (T-LCD-05; C0/Stage 1).** The Profile Compiler, the manager and every registered dialect/adapter carry complete records with executable removal tests; the spec-debt register (§6.9) exists at Phase 5 with one row per ratified ADR.
- **AC-I6-10 Out-of-process (T-LCD-12; C1/Stage 5).** The manager runs as a separate process exchanging `DebtIndexRow`, `ExperimentSpec`, `RemovalVerdict`, `HirDiff`/`ProfileDiff` and `DebtReport` documents through `hh-embed/1` Groups R/L/M and the registry client; no private verb.

T-LCD statement for the workstream: satisfies **T-LCD-05** (this *is* the test: schema-level requirement across a closed inventory; refuse/warn/retire; the "delete this rule" recipe executable at seal; reflexive), T-LCD-14 (every executable removal test is a `retirement` experiment under `MatchSpec`; static kinds are not comparisons), T-LCD-09 (removal tests are per beneficiary in `scope`; a rule's model interaction is a `contrast`), T-LCD-13 (`evidenced` grade requires compliance/probe evidence), T-LCD-10 (a rename never triggers anything; retirement is a semantic diff), T-LCD-15 (health metrics native-only with typed `n/a`), T-LCD-12 (operations over records), T-LCD-06 (adapter records carry debt without any IR change), T-LCD-01 (no model literal in the manager; triggers are observables). Not applicable: T-02/-03/-04/-07/-08/-11. ADR-0024 rung: the inventory constraint, `evaluate_debt`, `validate_removal_test`, `retire` refusals and the `DebtIndex` projection are MUST-code; records, `DebtPolicy`, templates, margins and priorities are MUST-data.

### 6.9 The discipline applied to the spec's own decisions (RK-06)

Every ratified ADR already carries (a) mechanism evidence, (c) disconfirming evidence and (d) conditionality (doc 3 §3.3); ADR-0050 already carries revalidation triggers. The spec-debt register lifts these into home 17: `research/registers/spec-debt.md` (created by synthesis at Phase 5; one row per ratified ADR): `SpecDebtRecord = AssumptionDebtRecord{rule_id: ADR-NNNN, debt_class: spec_decision, hypothesis: (d) conditionality verbatim, hypothesis_typed.subject: adr_id, evidence_refs: (a) S-ids with tier and provisional flag, owner: owning workstream → Phase-5 section owner, expiry: {condition: evidence_refresh_due, params: {evidence_max_age: one model generation (OQ-WS-I6-05)}} plus a revalidation trigger list in the ADR-0050 style (a named source superseded/refuted; a provisional source replicated or retracted; a build-stage measurement contradicting (d)), removal_test: {kind: revalidation_review, trigger}, status: active}`. Readiness-gate rule (doc 3 §7.3 addition): a C0 ADR whose (a) contains a `provisional` S-id has a spec-debt row with a non-empty trigger list (already required in substance by §3.1); no ADR is `retired` except by a superseding ADR (ADR-0037 applied to the program). This is the mitigation RK-06 names, made a table rather than a promise.

## 10. Confidence

**High** for the inventory and the additive record (every home is read from a ratified ADR; the fields are the union of five production regimes plus two ratified gaps), for the static `validate_removal_test` gate (a dry-run of ratified pure operations; feature-toggle time-bomb and OpenHands CI precedents), and for the status-derivation and retirement rules (restatements of ADR-0037/0126/0035 with one new record). **Medium** for the trigger additions (profile change and evidence supersession are ratified in pieces; probation/runway defaults are unvalidated — OQ-WS-I6-01) and for the health metrics (shape from I2; thresholds none). **Medium-low** for the manager as a *service* (scheduling under budget, batch designs, notices): `novel as integrated`, no audited system runs anything like it, and its value rests on the mechanism evidence that dead weight is common (Tier B production reports) rather than on any measured saving.
