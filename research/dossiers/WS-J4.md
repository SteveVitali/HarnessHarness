## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides the **analysis engine**: the set of *pure, versioned analysis operations* that turn results rows (J5 projections of the ledger, keyed per ADR-0045 decision 2) into persisted, provenance-bearing **analysis reports** — configuration summaries, comparison reports, interaction reports, capability–cost frontiers, transfer/portability profiles, benefit decompositions (artifact vs search-time vs transfer; validity × activation × following), fitted compatibility-surface reports, equivalence verdicts, rank reports, reliability profiles, power reports — and the **statistical method choices** behind each (unit of analysis, pairing, interval estimators, tests, multiplicity, heavy-tail handling, outcome-class sensitivity bounds). It fixes (i) the operation catalogue with inputs/outputs/invariants/failure modes; (ii) the estimator set and the *selection rule* that picks an estimator from a `MetricDeclaration.interval_method` and the cell's sample sizes, with labelled substitution; (iii) the compatibility surface as an analysis product (OQ-019/OQ-050: model forms, minimum design, expiry); (iv) the class-applicability matrix for every analysis (T-LCD-15) and the admissibility of each analysis per comparison granularity (ADR-0013); (v) acceptance criteria as **known-answer datasets** — synthetic generators with known truth against which every estimator's coverage, bias and `n/a` behaviour are checked; (vi) answers to OQ-016 (estimators), OQ-019, OQ-050, OQ-094, OQ-124 (display half), OQ-129, and a proposed disposition of OQ-340.

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: *what* must be reported and the `MetricDeclaration`/`Design`/`Arm`/`PreRegistration` schemas (WS-I2, ADR-0045 — consumed verbatim); the matched-budget precondition and the three benefit kinds (ADR-0046 — J4 estimates, never re-decides); `MatchSpec` modes, budget dimensions and cost provenance (WS-L2, ADR-0039/0041/0043); the execution of designs, replicate scheduling and budget enforcement (WS-J3); the results store, its query API, the experiment ledger and leaderboard *semantics* (WS-J5 — J4 supplies the `RankReport` the leaderboard renders and the `AnalysisReport` the ledger stores); oracles, judges and calibration (ADR-0047/0117); execution-alignment detectors and severity (WS-G2, ADR-0114); counterfactual/causal attribution over trajectories (WS-I7 — J4 supplies *designed-ablation* attribution only); the value-of-compute decision rule (WS-F4 — consumes J4 reports); the evolution gates (WS-I5 — consumes ADR-0046 §7 rows as J4 computes them); the Hosting ABI, capability probes and conformance (WS-J6 — J4 consumes `capability_vector` strata); dashboards and rendering (WS-K2 — consumes `AnalysisReport` and `RenderSpec`); suite composition and contamination strata (WS-I4, ADR-0143/0144 — J4 renders per-stratum views). Every contract here is operations/inputs/outputs/invariants/failure modes (T-LCD-12); no language, runtime, statistics library or framework is named (ADR-0050 §8).

## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**F1 — Every audited analysis plane stops at "mean with (maybe) an SE per configuration"; none computes a paired effect, an interaction, a frontier with uncertainty or a rank interval — and the two that compute intervals disagree on when an interval is valid.** Inspect's `evals_df` carries one headline value and one stderr per (task, model, configuration) (S-136 `columns.py` L130–139); lm-eval-harness picks closed-form SE for means and bootstrap only for a pre-approved list, with the draw count defaulting to a speed-driven number (S-519 `metrics.py` L589–612); Harbor reduces with `mean/max/min/sum` (S-132); AgentDojo averages booleans (S-241); HAL's leaderboard reports no intervals at all (S-135) while its reliability module bootstraps *unpaired* baseline vs perturbed successes with 200 draws and a hard-coded seed (S-134 `robustness.py` L60–80). METR's pipeline is the only one that resamples hierarchically (family → task → run) and re-fits a curve per resample (S-515). Mechanism conclusion: the aggregation ladder is precedented (I2 F1), the *comparison* ladder is not — pairing, interaction, frontier and rank estimation are the engine's novel obligations, and the estimator-selection rule must be explicit data rather than a hidden default (lm-eval's pre-approved list is the shape, made declarative).

**F2 — CLT intervals fail exactly in the regime the Stage 3 suite lives in, and the fix is known.** Stage 3 cells have 50–169 tasks (ADR-0144: TB2 89, τ-bench 165, SWE-bench mini 50) and 3–8 replicates. Bowyer et al. show nominal-95 % CLT intervals under-cover at N ≤ 100 (92.5 % coverage at N = 100, far worse at 30) and that Wilson-score or Beta-Bernoulli (uniform prior) intervals are cheap and correct; for paired comparisons a paired Bayesian model narrows the interval; under clustering only a clustered Beta-Binomial model achieves nominal coverage (S-508, Tier B). Inspect's clustered SE applies a finite-cluster correction and *errors* when a sample lacks a cluster id (S-136 `std.py` L75–82) — i.e. the strongest OSS implementation already treats cluster identity as mandatory data. Mechanism conclusion: (i) the task is the cluster and the resampling unit; (ii) `clustered_clt(task)` (ADR-0045 default) is admissible only above a task-count floor; below it the engine must substitute Wilson (single proportion) or Beta-Binomial (paired/clustered) and *label the substitution*; (iii) replicates never enter an interval as independent draws.

**F3 — Interaction effects on categorical factors are estimable model-free as paired differences of paired differences, and that is the estimator the LCD battery's canned analysis actually needs.** T-LCD-09's check is "does compaction variant V help model M more than model N?" — a difference of two paired harness effects, Δ_M − Δ_N, each paired by task at matched budget (ADR-0012 decision 5). RSM (S-167) fits polynomial surfaces on continuous axes; the Lab's axes are categorical except budget-like ones (ADR-0012 decision 7 forbids interpolation across categorical levels). S-072 is the canonical instance: the same treatment moves F2PF 28 → 49 pp at a 20 k window and ≈ 0 at wide windows, reported without intervals. Crossed random-effects models are the statistician's tool for many-cell designs (random interactions shrink toward zero where cells are sparse — search stream, S-511-adjacent literature), but require a fitter and assumptions the C0/C1 ladder does not want to carry. Mechanism conclusion: the default `model_form = contrast` (difference-of-differences over probed cells, cluster-bootstrap by task) is exact, assumption-light and executable on any results store; `factorial_glmm` is a C2 option for many-cell surfaces; `curve_on_ordered_axis` is admitted only along budget-like axes with ≥ 3 probed levels.

**F4 — Heavy-tailed cost/latency outcomes break mean-based tests, and the cure is resampling on the paired per-task differences plus quantile effects.** Doc 2 §8 (Tier B): "one runaway loop … can dominate operational risk"; HAL's own utilities report P50–P99/max severities (S-134 `error_classifier`); METR reports percentile CIs (S-515). A t-test on raw spend is dominated by the single runaway run; a percentile/BCa bootstrap of the paired differences (S-516) and a sign-flip permutation test are valid under exchangeability without a moment assumption; effects on P50/P95 are the operationally meaningful quantities beside the mean. Mechanism conclusion: for `value_type = decimal` metrics with `unit ∈ {tokens, money, ms}` the engine's default is `bootstrap_paired` (BCa where n_tasks ≥ 30, percentile otherwise) with a permutation p-value, and a *tail effect* row (Δ at P50, P95, max) — never a `clt` interval on the raw values.

**F5 — Frontiers need uncertainty and provenance strata; a scalar "efficiency" collapses them.** HAL includes the origin in the frontier and pins the price date (S-135); Kapoor et al. show accuracy-only ranking selects needlessly expensive agents (S-207); cost-of-pass gives a *task-level* scalar, `E[cost]/P(success)`, whose frontier over systems is interpretable in money (S-509); Fowler et al. show that taking the max over noisy samples biases a frontier upward (S-514, `provisional`). ADR-0043 makes every cost figure carry `provenance` and `confidence ∈ {exact, bounded, estimate, unknown}` and forbids averaging provenance away; ADR-0041 admits `cross_model` matching only on spend/time/calls. Mechanism conclusion: the frontier is a *report* over configurations with (a) the origin, (b) a bootstrap-over-tasks **frontier-membership probability** per configuration, (c) rows stratified by cost `confidence` (non-`exact` rows drawn as bands and never deciding membership against an `exact` row), (d) cost-of-pass as the scalar companion with its own interval, and (e) never a best-of-samples point unless the selection is itself an arm.

**F6 — Search-time benefit needs a synthesized baseline, and the replicates already stored make one.** ADR-0046 defines `search_time_benefit` against "a baseline given the same `SearchBudgetRecord` (test-time-scaling/discovery baseline per S-083, e.g. best-of-N with equal rollouts)". No audited plane builds such a baseline; but per-task (c, n) success counts stored by ADR-0045 let the engine compute, for the baseline arm, `pass@N` (oracle best-of-N — an *upper* bound, S-235) and a realistic *self-selected* best-of-N only when the arm recorded a selector (majority/judge). Mechanism conclusion: the engine synthesizes `search_baseline ∈ {oracle_best_of_n, selected_best_of_n{selector_ref}}` from the baseline arm's replicates when `n ≥ N`, labels the oracle variant as an upper bound, and renders `n/a{estimator_undefined}` otherwise — this makes ADR-0046 decision 4 computable from stored rows.

**F7 — Rank is a distribution, and leaderboards that sort point estimates over overlapping intervals mislead.** Chatbot Arena bootstraps BT scores (100 resamples) and declares overlapping-interval models indistinguishable (S-510, Tier A); arena-hard-auto reports 5 %/95 % quantiles per model and style-adjusted coefficients (S-517); S-239 shows rank instability at small n. Mechanism conclusion: `rank()` resamples tasks, returns per-configuration rank intervals and a partition into *indistinguishable sets*; J5's leaderboard renders sets, not a total order (CF/OQ for J5 in §7/§8).

**F8 — Multiplicity is a live risk the scorecard creates by design.** ADR-0045/0114/0117 register ≈ 50 metrics; a report comparing two arms on all of them at α = 0.05 expects 2–3 spurious "significant" cells under the null. The remedy is standard and cheap: pre-registered primaries (ADR-0045 `PreRegistration.primary_metrics`) get family-wise control (Holm, S-518), everything else is labelled *exploratory* with FDR-adjusted q-values (S-520); nothing is hidden, everything is labelled. Signal-to-noise (S-511) is emitted as a *suite diagnostic* so J3/I4 can improve designs, but the engine never filters tasks post hoc for a confirmatory row (that would be the selection leak S-082/S-237 warn about).

**F9 — Outcome classes must be shown to be conclusion-neutral, not merely excluded.** ADR-0045 excludes `infrastructure_failure`/`oracle_failure`/`cancelled` from denominators and counts them beside. That is correct for the estimate and insufficient for the *conclusion*: if arm A has 12 infrastructure failures and arm B none, the paired Δ can flip depending on what those runs would have been. Manski-style bounds (S-522) are the cheapest honest device: recompute Δ with every excluded run of A set to failure and of B to success (and vice versa); if the pre-registered conclusion survives both bounds it is `robust_to_outcome_classes`, else `sensitive`. Mechanism conclusion: every `ComparisonReport` carries an `outcome_bounds{lower, upper, verdict}` block; a `sensitive` verdict is a *report* state, not a refusal.

**F10 — The compatibility surface is a designed-experiment product with an expiry, not a learned model.** ADR-0012 fixed the surface as a derived view persisted only as a `FittedSurfaceReport` with `expiry_condition`, refusing `UnknownLevel`; ADR-0126 gives profile rules observable expiry triggers; WS-I6 manages debt records. S-096/S-047 (via A2) show model churn outpacing fits. Mechanism conclusion: `fit_surface` is `interaction()` over the full probed factor grid plus per-axis curves on ordered budget axes, wrapped in an `AssumptionDebtRecord` whose expiry fires on registry events (snapshot retired/superseded, profile `semantic_id` changed, suite validity record superseded) or age; unprobed cells are `unknown` and rendered as such; profile learning (OQ-019's second half) consumes `conditionality_region` as *evidence refs* for a rule's debt record and never auto-compiles a rule (ADR-0020/0124 link refusal stands).

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

**Against a task-clustered, paired, bootstrap-heavy default ("too slow and too conservative for a lab loop").** HAL, Harbor and Terminal-Bench publish single numbers; bootstrap over 10⁴ rows × 2000 draws × 50 metrics is real compute; and paired designs need the same tasks in every arm. *Why this does not overturn:* the instrument's purpose is attribution (ADR-0003); unpaired means rank agents at ρ ≈ 0.42 (S-238 via I2) and hide the S-072 interaction entirely; the engine caches per-task resample indices per report (one resample plan shared by every metric), draws are declared per report class (OQ-366) with a cheap `preview` mode that is labelled and never persisted as a confirmatory report; closed-form intervals (Wilson, clustered CLT above the floor) are used wherever valid.

**Against a Bayesian small-n default (OQ-129).** Priors are contestable; a uniform Beta prior shrinks tiny cells toward 0.5 and readers may misread credible as confidence intervals; S-239 is `provisional`. *Why this does not overturn:* the recommendation rests on the accepted S-508, the prior is fixed and declared (`Beta(1,1)`), the interval is *labelled* `credible` with the prior in the report, and a frequentist Wilson interval is rendered beside it whenever computable; the (c, n) counts remain stored so any reader can re-derive. The engine never mixes the two in one cell.

**Against the model-free contrast as the interaction default.** Difference-of-differences uses only fully crossed probed cells and throws away information a mixed model would borrow across cells; with six factors the grid is sparse and many contrasts will be `n/a`. *Why this does not overturn:* sparsity is *information* — the Lab's stance is "unprobed = unknown, never interpolated" (ADR-0012); the contrast is exact under the design and needs no fitter at C0/C1; `factorial_glmm` is admitted at C2 as a declared `model_form` whose shrinkage is reported as such (random interactions), so a borrowed estimate is never presented as a probed one.

**Against cost-of-pass and frontier-membership probability.** Cost-of-pass is undefined when P(success) = 0 and explodes near it; frontier membership under bootstrap can give "probably on the frontier" to a configuration nobody would deploy. *Why this does not overturn:* `n/a{estimator_undefined}` when the success interval includes 0; the frontier report carries the origin and per-configuration intervals so the reader sees *why* a point is on it; the membership probability is a report field beside the deterministic frontier over point estimates, never a replacement.

**Against Manski bounds on outcome classes.** Worst-case bounds are extremely conservative — a handful of infrastructure failures will render most small-suite comparisons `sensitive`. *Why this does not overturn:* that is the truth about those comparisons, and the fix is operational (re-run the failed cells — J3's job, and the bounds tell J3 exactly how many), not statistical; the verdict is a report state, not a refusal, and the headline estimate is unchanged.

**Against synthesized best-of-N search baselines.** Oracle best-of-N is an upper bound nobody can deploy; using it as the S-083 baseline makes `search_time_benefit` look worse than a realistic alternative. *Why this does not overturn:* it is labelled `oracle` and an *upper bound*; a `selected_best_of_n` variant is computed whenever a selector is recorded; the point of the row is exactly to bound the search-time claim from both sides (ADR-0046 decision 4 wanted a TTS baseline "given the same search budget" — pass@N *is* that budget).

**Against family-wise correction.** Holm at 50 metrics makes any single secondary finding nearly impossible to call significant; labs will stop pre-registering. *Why this does not overturn:* Holm applies only to the pre-registered *primaries* (typically 1–3); secondaries get FDR q-values and an `exploratory` label; nothing is suppressed, and the readiness glossary rule ("every X beats baseline is a ComparisonReport") already makes the label the enforcement point.

**Against rank sets on a leaderboard.** Users want an ordering. *Why this does not overturn:* Chatbot Arena's practice (Tier A) shows sets are accepted; J5 may still sort *within* a set by point estimate provided the set boundary is rendered — the leaderboard-semantics decision is J5's (OQ-369).

**Negative results from the audit.** (i) No audited plane pairs by task in a comparison; (ii) the one robustness bootstrap in production code resamples arms independently (S-134), which over-states variance for a within-task factor; (iii) every bootstrap in the audited code hard-codes its draw count and seed; (iv) no plane persists an analysis with the row watermark it was computed from, so no analysis is reproducible from the store; (v) no plane distinguishes confirmatory from exploratory cells. The engine's novelty is therefore discipline — pairing, declared estimators, provenance and labelling — not a new estimator.

## 6. Recommendation for the spec

### 6.1 Structural decisions

1. **The analysis engine is a pure, versioned function** `analyze(AnalysisSpec, rows: ResultsQuery{watermark}) → AnalysisReport`, deterministic given the rows, the spec, the metric-registry version, the price-table version and a recorded resampling seed. Reports are persisted by J5's experiment ledger *with provenance* (ontology §4g: "computed once, persisted with provenance"), never recomputed silently; re-running with the same inputs must reproduce the report hash (extends AC-I2-12).
2. **Placement.** The engine is a lab-side consumer of `project(run, results_row, until_seq)` rows, `MetricDeclaration`s and `ComparisonReport`/`MatchSpec` records (ADR-0050 D2 list, items iv–vi); nothing else crosses. It owns no store (J5 does), no scheduler (J3 does), no oracle (I2/G-track do).
3. **Task is the unit.** Every interval, test and resample treats the task as the cluster and pairing key (ADR-0045 decision 2; S-234; S-508); replicates are within-cluster and enter only through per-task reducers or hierarchical resampling; a cell with one task renders `n/a{estimator_undefined}`.
4. **Estimator selection is declared data.** `EstimatorSelection{declared: interval_method, floors: {clustered_clt: n_tasks ≥ T_clt (placeholder 100), bca: n_tasks ≥ 30}, fallback_chain, substituted?: {from, to, reason}}` is part of every report cell; a substitution is labelled, never silent (F2; lm-eval precedent made declarative).
5. **Interaction = contrast by default.** `model_form ∈ {contrast, factorial_glmm, curve_on_ordered_axis}`; `contrast` is the C0/C1 default and the T-LCD-09 canned analysis; the others are C2 with declared assumptions.
6. **Heavy tails: resample the paired differences, report quantile effects, never t-test raw costs.** (F4.)
7. **Frontiers carry uncertainty and provenance strata; cost-of-pass is the scalar companion.** (F5.)
8. **Three benefit kinds are computed, not restated**; `search_time_benefit` gets a synthesized, labelled baseline from stored replicates (F6).
9. **Rank is a distribution**; the engine returns rank intervals and indistinguishable sets (F7).
10. **Confirmatory vs exploratory is a label on every cell**; Holm on pre-registered primaries, BH elsewhere; SNR is a diagnostic, never a filter (F8).
11. **Every comparison carries outcome-class sensitivity bounds** (F9).
12. **The compatibility surface is `interaction()` over the probed grid with expiry** (F10; §6.5).
13. **Class-aware by construction.** Every operation checks `admissible_granularities` (ADR-0013) and `MetricDeclaration.applies_to_classes`; a component-level analysis on a hosted row is `n/a{class}`; hosted rows are stratified by `capability_vector` and cost `confidence`, never pooled silently (T-LCD-07/-15).

### 6.4 Data-model sketch

- `AnalysisSpec{kind ∈ {summarize, compare, interaction, frontier, transfer, benefit_decomposition, fit_surface, equivalence, rank, attribution, reliability_profile, power, strata_view, diagnostics}, selection: ResultsQuery{experiment_ref?, arm_ids[], configuration_ids[], metric_names[], strata_filter, watermark}, design_ref, pre_registration_ref?, estimator_policy: EstimatorSelection, model_form?, resampling{draws, seed, plan}, label ∈ {confirmatory, exploratory, preview}}` — content-addressed; `confirmatory` requires a `pre_registration_ref` whose primaries match.

- `Cell{key (configuration_id | (arm_a, arm_b) | contrast levels), metric, value | n/a{reason}, interval{method, level, lo, hi, substituted?, prior?}, test?, tail_effects?, outcome_bounds?, multiplicity{family, raw, adjusted, label}, n{tasks, replicates, runs}, class_strata}`.

- `FrontierReport`, `TransferProfile`, `RankReport`, `InteractionReport`, `AttributionReport`, `PowerReport` as in §6.2; `FittedSurfaceReport` as in ontology §4f plus `status`.
- `RenderSpec{view ∈ {scorecard, comparison, frontier, transfer, surface, rank, reliability, strata}, report_ref, options}` — K2 renders; never recomputes.
- What J5 stores: `AnalysisReport`s in the experiment ledger keyed by `report_id`, annotated (derived, non-hashed — ADR-0141 pattern) with `bundle_ref`s of the runs they cover; the leaderboard consumes `RankReport`s.

### 6.5 Compatibility surface as an analysis product (OQ-019, OQ-050)

- **Model form.** `fit_surface` = A3 over the full probed grid of the declared factors (`model_form = contrast` at C1; `factorial_glmm` at C2 with random task and task×model effects and reported shrinkage) plus `curve_on_ordered_axis` for each *ordered* axis (context window, budget dimensions, replicate horizon) with ≥ 3 probed levels; categorical axes (model snapshot, environment image, task family, tool-surface target, control boundary β) are never interpolated (ADR-0012 decision 7).
- **Minimum design per cell.** ≥ 2 levels per included factor, `replicates_per_cell ≥ 3`, `n_tasks ≥ 30` per cell (else the cell is `unknown{reason = insufficient}`), one `MatchSpec` for the whole surface (`IncommensurableMatch` otherwise), pairing by task across every cell.
- **Report.** `FittedSurfaceReport{metric, factors, levels, design, model_form, sample_sizes, estimates_with_ci (main effects, two-way interactions; higher-order only under `factorial_glmm`), conditionality_region, portability{sign_stability along model axis}, configuration_ids[], unknown_cells[], fitted_at, expiry_condition, status ∈ {active, expiring, expired}, debt_record_ref}`.
- **Staleness / expiry (T-LCD-05 reflexively).** `expiry_condition ⊇ {any model snapshot in configuration_ids superseded or retired (registry event), any profile in configuration_ids with `semantic_id` changed (L2+ on the sameness ladder), any `SuiteValidityRecord` superseded, `fitted_at + max_age` exceeded (placeholder 90 days, OQ-368)}`; on expiry the report is annotated `expired`, never deleted or hidden (ADR-0037 annotated-never-hidden); the manager is WS-I6's debt manager; re-probe cadence is OQ-034's (J6/I6).
- **Consumers.** WS-C3/I5 may cite `conditionality_region` entries as `evidence_refs` in a `ProfileRule`'s `AssumptionDebtRecord`; the engine never emits a rule, a profile or a compiled surface — "learning profiles from the surface" (OQ-019's second half) is a C4 consumer decision whose only J4-side obligation is the evidence reference with expiry.

### 6.7 Acceptance criteria — known-answer datasets (KA)

Each KA is a deterministic synthetic generator with recorded seed and *known truth*; every estimator ships with its KA and the Stage gate runs them.

- **KA-1 Bernoulli factorial.** 2 models × 2 harness variants × T ∈ {30, 100, 300} tasks × n ∈ {1, 3, 5} replicates with known per-task success probabilities and a known interaction δ. Pass: A3 `contrast` recovers δ within its interval in ≥ 93 % of 1000 simulated experiments at nominal 95 %; `clustered_clt` is substituted below `T_clt` and the substitution is labelled; Wilson/Beta intervals reach ≥ 93 % coverage at T = 30; a one-task cell renders `n/a{estimator_undefined}`.
- **KA-2 Heavy-tailed costs.** Paired per-task spend from a lognormal with 2 % Pareto-tail runaway runs and known median/mean shift. Pass: `bootstrap_paired` interval covers the true mean shift ≥ 93 %; tail effects at P95 recover the injected shift; a `clt` request on `unit = money` is refused (`EstimatorUndefined`→ substitution) and labelled.
- **KA-3 Frontier.** Ten configurations with known (cost, capability) and a known non-dominated set; two rows with `confidence = estimate`. Pass: point frontier equals truth; membership probabilities are monotone in distance from the frontier; band rows never displace `exact` rows; cost-of-pass matches `E[cost]/p` and is `n/a` where p's interval includes 0; origin present.
- **KA-4 Mixed classes.** A native and three hosted rows (one per mechanism) with a component-level factor. Pass: A10 renders `n/a{class}` for hosted rows; A3 refuses the component factor on hosted rows (`InadmissibleFactor`); no cell is 0 by default (AC-I2-3 extended).
- **KA-5 Outcome classes.** AC-I2-5's fixture plus 12 infrastructure failures concentrated in one arm. Pass: headline Δ unchanged; `outcome_bounds.verdict = sensitive`; with the failures balanced across arms, `robust`.
- **KA-6 Transfer.** Known Δ_in and Δ_out across 3 model families with one family sign-flipped. Pass: `transfer_ratio` within interval; `sign_stability = 2/3`; `conditionality_region` excludes the flipped family; ratio `n/a` when Δ_in's interval includes 0.
- **KA-7 Rank.** Six configurations, two pairs tied by construction. Pass: indistinguishable sets recover the ties in ≥ 95 % of seeds; no total order emitted.
- **KA-8 Reference cross-checks.** pass^k equals the τ-bench formula on identical (c, n); clustered SE equals Inspect's finite-cluster-corrected value on identical data; Chen pass@k equals S-235 on fixtures (ported *values*, no library).
- **KA-9 Null multiplicity.** 50 metrics under the null, 1000 experiments. Pass: family-wise error on primaries ≤ 5 %; exploratory cells carry q-values and the `exploratory` label; nothing suppressed.
- **KA-10 Surface.** A 3-factor grid with two unprobed cells and one snapshot later "retired". Pass: `unknown_cells` listed, never interpolated; `status` flips to `expired` on the registry event; the report remains readable.
- **KA-11 Search baseline.** Baseline arm with n = 8 replicates and known per-task rates. Pass: `oracle_best_of_n` equals pass@N; `selected_best_of_n` present only with a recorded selector; `n/a` for N > n.
- **KA-12 Reproducibility.** Re-running any KA report with the same spec, watermark and seed yields an identical `report_id` (extends AC-I2-12).
- **KA-13 T-LCD-09/-14/-15 gates.** AC-I2-1/-2/-3 executed through A3/A2/A1 respectively; T-LCD-03 via A8 on the Stage 3 reference-harness fixture.
