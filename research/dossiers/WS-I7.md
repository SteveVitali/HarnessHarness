## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides **how a change in a harness outcome is credited to a named component, under which conditions such credit is causally valid, and how that credit enters the evolution pipeline and the scorecard**. Concretely: (i) the **intervention/replay contract on top of B4** — how a `ComponentTarget` (slot, variant, rule, parameter, procedure, opaque `Text` leaf, or decision point) is turned into ADR-0135 `InterventionRecord`s, how fork points are chosen relative to `earliest_affected_seq`, and how the factual re-execution arm ADR-0135 already mandates is used as the *null intervention*; (ii) the **attribution method catalogue** — designed ablation (whole-run LOI/LOO, WS-J4 A10), trajectory-local contrastive intervention, coalition (Shapley) credit over a component set, paired-replay interaction, and an optional coupled/mediation split — each with an explicit **estimand**, validity conditions keyed to ADR-0135's replay-validity modes, and `n/a` reasons; (iii) the **`AttributionReport/1` schema** as an additive extension of J4's `AttributionReport`; (iv) the **answer to OQ-323/OQ-082** — replicate count `k`, statistic and margin per validity mode; (v) **attribution as a required input** to WS-I5's failure-hypothesis stage and as the estimator behind WS-I2's `attribution_quality` (evolvability dimension); (vi) **acceptance criteria as known-effect synthetic tests** (planted pivotal, inert, AND-interaction, budget-splitting and nondeterminism-trap components); (vii) an honest `novel / no-precedent` accounting.

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: the branch model, coherent fork points, snapshot coupling, the `deferred` phase and the `ReplayValidityReport` (**WS-B4**, ADR-0133/0134/0135 — consumed verbatim; one additive amendment proposed for seed derivation under noise coupling, CF-432); the results row, overlay-run versioning and the experiment ledger (**WS-J5**, ADR-0161/0162); estimator selection, intervals, multiplicity and the `contrast` interaction estimator (**WS-J4**, ADR-0157/0158 — attribution intervals are computed by the same kernel; A10's shape is extended, never replaced); the experiment definition and scheduler (**WS-J3** — every attribution run is an `ExperimentSpec` with `kind ∈ {comparative, retirement, exploratory}`); the evolution pipeline stages and governance (**WS-I5** — this dossier fixes what I5 must *consume* and the label it must carry, not its stages); assumption-debt records and expiry (**WS-I6**); value-of-compute scheduling of attribution spend (**WS-F4** — attribution cost estimates are inputs to it); judge calibration (**WS-G3**); hosted-participant capability vectors (**WS-J6**); rendering (**WS-K2**). Every contract is operations/inputs/outputs/invariants/failure modes (T-LCD-12).

## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**F1 — Observational attribution (a model reads the trajectory and names the culprit) is measurably unreliable, and its failure is structural, not a model-capability gap.** On Who&When the best log-reading method reaches 53.5 % agent-level and 14.2 % step-level exact accuracy with frontier reasoning models, several methods below random (S-597, Tier A); AgenTracer reports frontier models "generally below 10 %" at step level (S-599); AgentDebugX's best tuned diagnoser reaches 28.8 % agent-and-step (S-544). The baseline code shows why: `all_at_once` hands the model the whole log *and the ground-truth answer* and asks for a name, a step and a reason in free text (S-598 `utils.py` L74–92) — no intervention, no re-execution, no interval, exact-match scoring. CAR's diagnosis is the causal one: "the step that executes the harmful action is usually not the step that decided on it" (S-593). **Implication (§6.1 D1):** an attribution *value* in HarnessHarness is produced only by an executed intervention with an interval; a model-authored diagnosis is admitted only as an `observational` **hypothesis** that must be discharged by an intervention before it can gate anything (T-LCD-13-style separation of claim from evidence, ADR-0112's `model_claim` discipline applied to attribution).

**F2 — Run-forward re-execution from a fork point estimates an interventional effect, not a unit-level counterfactual, and the difference is the source of two documented confounds.** Pearl's counterfactual Y_{x′}(u) fixes the exogenous noise *u* by abduction; re-executing the suffix with fresh model samples instead marginalizes over it, giving E[Y | do(X = x′), prefix] (S-607). Two consequences are proven on planted chains: (a) the **run-forward confound** — resampling any step re-rolls every later stochastic step, so an early inert step shows an effect merely by re-rolling the true pivot (S-593; S-595 Prop. 1: "numerically indistinguishable… on every factual draw"); (b) the **CRN degeneracy** — holding downstream noise fixed removes the confound but returns exactly 0 for a decisive step when direct and mediated paths oppose (S-595 Prop. 2, ≈ 10 % of runs), and the two remedies disagree on 87 % of planted runs. ADR-0028 (c) and ADR-0135 (c) already record that re-executed branches carry sampling noise; B4's factual arm supplies the noise floor. **Implication (§6.1 D2, D3):** the estimand is *declared* on every attribution effect (`TE_marg | TE_crn | DE | LOI | LOO | shapley`), `TE_crn`/`DE` are admissible only under ADR-0135 `deterministic` mode with noise coupling, and the *point-of-commitment* rule (locus = latest target whose interval still excludes zero) is a labelled heuristic for `TE_marg`, never a causal proof — the report carries both the per-target effects and the locus.

**F3 — Whole-run designed ablation under identical budget is the only method with an established harness-level precedent, and it exposes a budget-allocation trap the acceptance suite must plant.** S-092 measures each prompt slot by leave-one-in/leave-one-out under the *same* optimization budget and finds value concentrated in one slot (+0.119 LOI) with the others individually null; splitting 64 rollouts across four slots leaves every slot below the optimizer's search floor, and reallocating recovers the gain (0.761). WS-J4 already ratified A10 `attribution(arm, ablation_design) → {slots[{component_class, LOI, LOO, interval}], unattributed_share, budget_allocation_ref}` as designed ablation (ADR-0157 D2, "counterfactual attribution is WS-I7"). S-092 does not discuss slot *interactions*; LOI + LOO on a component set do not sum to Δ when components interact, which is why `unattributed_share` exists. **Implication (§6.2 M1, §6.7 KA-I7-4):** designed ablation stays the C2 default at run start (fork at `lifecycle.run.created`, `SlotBinding.enabled = false` or variant swap as the `definition_diff`), reported per `SearchBudgetRecord.allocation`; interaction credit needs M3.

**F4 — Coalition (Shapley) credit is the axiomatically fixed answer to "what share of Δ", it is implementable with a bounded budget, and its estimator has one non-obvious correctness rule.** Efficiency, symmetry, dummy and additivity determine the split (S-602); permutation sampling gives a polynomial estimator with a CLT interval (S-603); the interaction index generalizes it to pairs (S-604). Two implementations converge on the same estimator: shap's `PermutationExplainer` walks each random permutation forward and backward, accumulating `f(S ∪ {i}) − f(S)` (S-606 L165–221), and CAR's `shapley_attribution` does the same over *steps* with `samples_per_eval` rollouts per coalition value (S-594 `shapley.py` L77–199). CAR adds the rule that matters for honesty: **v(S) is not cached across permutations** — a cached value makes the per-target marginals dependent and "collapses the variance to zero" (false confidence); and its synthetic AND-interaction recovers φ = 0.44/0.45/≈ 0 with efficiency 0.909 vs the analytic 0.91 (S-593). Cost is `n_permutations · (2 if antithetic) · (n_targets + 1) · samples_per_eval` forward rollouts (`estimate_rollouts`, L235–249); with 8 targets, 32 permutations, antithetic, 4 samples that is 2,304 suffix re-executions — on-demand only, budget-truncated with `budget_truncated = true` reported. **Implication (§6.2 M3):** coalition credit is a C4 operation over `ComponentTarget` sets, charged `instrument`, refused without a budget, with `efficiency_check = Σφ − Δ` reported; targets default to ≤ 8 (OQ-450).

**F5 — Interaction effects between a component and the model/environment are already estimable by J4's `contrast`; what attribution adds is the *fork-point* pairing unit.** ADR-0158 D5 fixes the paired difference-of-differences over probed cells, clustered by task, as the T-LCD-09 canned analysis. A counterfactual design produces, per fork point, a factual and a counterfactual arm of `k` branches; across fork points (one per task, or several per run) the per-fork-point effects are the paired observations, and the task remains the cluster (ADR-0158 D1). Exact conditioning on the decision state is precisely what makes this valid: "interaction histories are deterministic functions of observable text with no hidden state" (S-601) — in HarnessHarness terms, the durable prefix plus the `EnvSnapshot` *is* the decision state (ADR-0133 D3). **Implication (§6.2 M4):** paired-replay interaction = A3 `contrast` with `pairing_key = fork_point` and `cluster = task`; no new estimator.

**F6 — The ledger already carries the traceability a post-hoc attribution needs, with two named gaps.** Checking S-595's R1–R12 against ratified contracts: R1 identity/order (ADR-0027 `seq`, `parent_event_id`), R2 step typing (`scope_kind`, ADR-0042), R3 content commitment (`request_ref`/`response_ref` at `model_io`, `context.observation.recorded`; canonical hashing ADR-0029), R4 outcome (`measurement.metric.emitted` with an `OracleDeclaration`, ADR-0047), R8 tool purity (`EffectAttributes.repeat_safety`, `mutability`; `Observation.source` in CAR maps to ADR-0030 `observed`/`probed`), R9 environment binding (`EnvSnapshot` + `EnvironmentRecord`, ADR-0133/0137), R10/R11 retention and cross-actor completeness (ADR-0068 floors; ADR-0164 hosted lifting; ADR-0044 "the ledger is never sampled"), R12 signed effects (this dossier's `AttributionReport`). The gaps: **R5 keyed exogenous noise** — ADR-0135 seeds the branch PRNG from `configuration_version_id.seed ∥ branch_id`, which *differs* between the factual and counterfactual branch of the same replicate, so harness-side randomness cannot be coupled (CF-432 proposes `H(seed ∥ replicate_index ∥ fork_point)` when `noise_coupling = crn`); **R6/R7 sampling distribution and serving fingerprint** — the gateway records `ModelSnapshotRecord.observed_fingerprint` (ADR-0038/0120) but not per-draw logits, and provider batch-invariance is not a probed capability (OQ-449). **Implication:** `deterministic`+CRN attribution is available for harness-side sources now and for `provider_sampling` only where a gateway declares and probes `seed_honoured` (CF-095) — recorded per source in `coupled_sources[]`, never assumed.

**F7 — Known-effect synthetic ground truth is the only credible acceptance basis, and it is precedented.** CAR validates every estimator against hand-built SCMs with a known locus and a known AND-interaction (S-594 `tests/synthetic_scms.py`); AgenTracer manufactures ground truth by *programmed fault injection* and re-runs (S-600 `attack_monitor.py`); Who&When's own annotations are human labels of natural failures (S-597). The two mechanisms differ in what they certify: planted SCMs certify the estimator; injected faults certify the pipeline end to end. **Implication (§6.7):** the acceptance suite plants effects in a Harness Definition executed against a recording/fake gateway (ADR-0129 K5 admissibility for declared replay; ADR-0125's null profile) so truth is known by construction, and adds one injected-fault corpus over the Stage-3 suite.

**F8 — Side-effecting tools bound what may be intervened on, and the containment table already answers it.** CAR scopes real side effects out ("mocked reproducible tools"); Shepherd materializes irreversible effects on emission and only audits them (S-078, via ADR-0134 context). HarnessHarness has the answer in ADR-0134: inside a `counterfactual` branch, `read_only` and covered `reversible` effects run, `compensable` only by opt-in, `irreversible`/`external` are *deferred* and the branch either continues (output not needed) or pauses `awaiting_promotion`. **Implication (§6.3 validity V6):** an attribution effect whose counterfactual arm paused on a deferred effect is `n/a{not_run}` for outcome metrics and reported as a *process* effect only; the pipeline may not credit a component for an outcome the branch could not reach.

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

**Against executed counterfactual attribution at all ("too expensive; use ablation experiments").** Every attribution value costs `k` suffix re-executions per target per fork point, and Shapley multiplies that by permutations; S-092 shows configuration-level LOI/LOO experiments already localize value; HAL/Harbor/Terminal-Bench publish single numbers and no production write-up read in this program attributes below the configuration. *Why it does not overturn:* designed ablation **is** the default (M1, C2) and this dossier adds nothing to its cost; the trajectory-local methods are on-demand, budget-truncated and `instrument`-charged (F4), and they answer the question ablation cannot — *where in the run* a component mattered — which is what the evolution pipeline's "smallest corrective intervention" (doc 2 §5.8) needs. The cost is made visible (`rollouts`, `budget_truncated`) and scheduled by WS-F4.

**Against `TE_marg` (run-forward) as an estimand.** Prop. 1 of S-595 shows an inert step can be numerically indistinguishable from a decisive one under fresh downstream noise; CAR's own limitation says the contrastive effect is "total through stochastic continuation". *Why it does not overturn:* the effect is *labelled* `TE_marg` and the locus rule is labelled a heuristic; a `TE_marg` effect alone may support an `exploratory` proposal but never a `confirmatory` attribution (§6.3 V4); where `deterministic` mode and coupling are available the report carries `TE_crn` beside it; where they are not, the honest statement is "this component's re-decision changes the outcome distribution from this fork point", which is still an executed, interval-bearing claim rather than a story.

**Against `TE_crn` / common random numbers.** Prop. 2: exact zeros for decisive steps on ≈ 10 % of runs; coupling token draws across divergent contexts is provably lossy (quantile coupling 41 % vs maximal 82 % at TV = 0.18) and needs batch-invariant serving that no provider declares; ADR-0038 F7 already treats provider model identity as a claim. *Why it does not overturn:* `noise_coupling = crn` is recorded per source with an agreement rate, never assumed for `provider_sampling` unless `seed_honoured` is probed; a `TE_crn = 0` with `change_rate > 0` is rendered `inconclusive`, not "inert" (the R12 exclusion rule); the estimand is optional (C4, Stage 6) and the report is valid without it.

**Against Shapley credit.** Exponential worst case; Monte-Carlo variance vs budget is a real trade-off; the value function `P(outcome | held = S)` treats "held at factual" as the coalition semantics, which for a *component* (not a step) means "the component behaves as recorded", which is only meaningful where its outputs are recorded and substitutable (ADR-0135 D2 sources); interactions above pairs are rarely estimable. *Why it does not overturn:* Shapley is the only split satisfying the four axioms and `efficiency_check` makes under-coverage visible; targets are capped and the walk is budget-truncated with the truncation reported; a coalition member without substitutable recorded outputs makes the whole design `n/a{observability}` rather than a silent approximation.

**Against the weak-coupling assumption of definition-level interventions.** A `HirDiff` that changes a component may change what the *prefix* would have done (S-078 App. A.1; S-479 "irreducibly tainted"). *Why it does not overturn:* ADR-0135 D5 forces the fork at or before `earliest_affected_seq` derived from the trace map's `ir_refs[]` (ADR-0027/0048 `trace map`); `coupling_assumption = declared_strong` forces `fork_policy = run_start`, which degrades the method to designed ablation (M1) — attribution never claims more locality than the trace map licenses.

**Against attribution as a *required* pipeline input.** Requiring an `AttributionReport` on every evolution candidate adds cost and delays; RHO/AHE-class methods (S-061/S-063 via I5) propose edits from trajectory reading with no attribution and report gains. *Why it does not overturn:* doc 2 §5.8's seven-point standard names "component-level attribution" as item 4 and ADR-0046 §7 is its operational form; the requirement is *graded* (§6.5): an `observational` hypothesis admits a candidate as `exploratory`; a `designed_ablation` or `causal_*` report is required only for `confirmatory` acceptance and for the "smallest corrective intervention" claim; nothing blocks the proposal stage.

**Against known-effect synthetic tests as acceptance ("synthetic truth is too easy").** A fake-gateway harness has no real model noise; CAR's fixtures are toy SCMs. *Why it does not overturn:* the fixtures certify the *estimator* (coverage, bias, `n/a` behaviour, budget truncation) exactly as J4's KA-1…13 do; the injected-fault corpus over the Stage-3 suite (KA-I7-7) certifies the pipeline against real model noise with truth known by construction; and the nondeterminism-trap fixture (KA-I7-5) certifies that the method *refuses* rather than reports when validity fails.

**Negative results.** No audited harness or eval plane computes an intervention effect with an interval over a *component* (`no-precedent`); no production write-up attributes below the configuration level; no source read couples provider sampling across counterfactual arms in practice (S-595 derives it, does not run it); S-092 does not measure slot interactions; the only live evaluation of an executed-intervention attributor against human labels (CAR on Who&When) reports the *baseline's* 14 % but the fetched HTML does not give CAR's own Who&When number — recorded as unknown, not as a win.

## 6. Recommendation for the spec

### 6.1 Position (decisions D1–D8)

**D1 — Attribution values come only from executed interventions.** An attribution *effect* is a `ComparisonReport`-shaped record (ADR-0046) produced by branches the kernel executed; a model-authored diagnosis is an `AttributionHypothesis{targets[], rationale: Text{authority ≤ delegate}, provenance}` with label `observational`, never an effect. (F1.)

**D2 — Every effect declares its estimand and its replay-validity mode.** `estimand ∈ {LOI, LOO, TE_marg, TE_crn, DE, shapley}` with `validity_mode` copied from the branches' `ReplayValidityReport.mode` (ADR-0135 D4). Admissibility: `LOI/LOO` need only `re_executed` (whole-run arms are ordinary experiment cells); `TE_marg`/`shapley` need `re_executed` with a coherent fork point and snapshot coupling; `TE_crn`/`DE` need `deterministic` with `noise_coupling = crn` on every source consumed after the fork; `degraded` ⇒ `exploratory` only; `invalid` ⇒ refused. (F2, F6.)

**D3 — Intervention kinds are ADR-0135's; component targets are typed.** The mapping (no new kernel kind): `definition_diff` ← slot ablation (`SlotBinding.enabled = false`), variant swap (`SlotBinding.variant`), parameter change, rule removal (ADR-0126 removal test), procedure edit, opaque-leaf ablation (T-LCD-02); `profile_diff` ← profile rule/surface change; `response_substitution` ← `do_action`; `observation_substitution` ← `do_observation`; `decision_override` ← `do_context`-class edits at a decision point; `budget_change`/`permission_change` as named. `do_resample` is the factual arm (no `intervention_ref`). `do_policy` (model swap from k) is a `profile_diff` + `ModelRoleTable` change and is admissible only at configuration granularity (ADR-0013). (F2, 4c.)

**D4 — Fork policy is derived from the trace map, never chosen freely.** Default `latest_coherent_before(earliest_affected_seq)`; `run_start` when `coupling_assumption = declared_strong` (degrades to M1); `every_decision_point{kind}` only for M2/M3 over a run's own decision points. (F2 disconfirmation.)

**D5 — OQ-323 answered (constants are MUST-data placeholders with a Stage-6 power re-check).** Statistic: for binary outcomes, difference of proportions between the counterfactual and factual arms at one fork point with a Newcombe/Wilson hybrid interval (J4 `wilson` family), aggregated across fork points as a paired effect clustered by task (ADR-0158 D1/D3); for `unit ∈ {tokens, money, ms}` outcomes, `bootstrap_paired` on per-fork-point differences with tail effects. `k` (branches per arm per fork point): `re_executed` ⇒ **k ≥ 8** per arm (16 rollouts per fork point; CAR's default is 16 per intervention); `deterministic` without coupling ⇒ k ≥ 4; `deterministic` + `crn` on every consumed source ⇒ k = 1 suffices for the *decision-sequence* claim and k ≥ 4 for outcome claims (ADR-0135 D6 restated). Margin: a target is *attributed* iff its interval excludes 0 **and** `change_rate > 0` on the factual arm (the policy is not a point mass at that fork point — S-595 failure 1); *equivalence* ("this component does not matter here") is TOST against the pre-registered `equivalence_margin` (ADR-0158 D7), never the absence of significance. The factual arm's dispersion is the reported `noise_floor`. OQ-113's B4 half: an R2 verdict on counterfactual rows is a **seed-set** claim (replicates), a same-seed claim only under `deterministic`+`crn`.

**D6 — Credit is split axiomatically or not at all.** Where a set of targets is attributed jointly, `shapley` values with `efficiency_check = Σφ − Δ_set` and per-target CLT intervals; pairwise `interaction_share` via the interaction index; no cross-permutation caching; antithetic pairing; budget truncation reported. Single-target `TE_marg`/`LOI` effects over a set are reported with `unattributed_share = Δ − Σ effects` and are never presented as a decomposition. (F4.)

**D7 — Attribution is a graded, required input to evolution and the estimator behind `attribution_quality`.** Every WS-I5 candidate carries `attribution_ref` with `label ∈ {observational, designed_ablation, causal_interventional, causal_coupled}`; `confirmatory` acceptance (ADR-0046 §7) requires `label ≥ designed_ablation` with the diff's `semantic_ops` targets ⊆ attributed targets; `attribution_quality` (ADR-0045 registry entry, I2 §6.3) = share of the configuration-level paired Δ covered by attributed targets, with an interval, `n/a{class}` for hosted rows. (F3, doc 2 §5.8 item 4.)

**D8 — Acceptance is a known-effect suite.** Planted-effect Harness Definitions executed against a fake/recording gateway (truth by construction) plus one injected-fault corpus over the Stage-3 suite; the suite includes refusal cases (nondeterminism trap, incoherent fork, hosted class). (F7.)

### 6.3 Attribution validity conditions (the "where attribution is invalid" list, consolidated)

V1 replay validity ≥ the estimand's floor (D2); V2 fork point coherent and ≤ `earliest_affected_seq`; V3 `MatchSpec` on every arm, suffix budgets equal (T-LCD-14); V4 `change_rate > 0` at the fork point (else `PolicyCollapsed` — nothing is estimable); V5 outcome oracle in ADR-0047's deterministic headline classes, or the effect is labelled `judged` and inherits ADR-0117 calibration/expiry; V6 no counterfactual branch paused `awaiting_promotion` on a deferred effect for an outcome metric (ADR-0134); V7 `fingerprint_match ≠ mismatch` across *both* arms and within the J3 drift bracket (ADR-0155); V8 weak coupling honoured or the design is `run_start`; V9 native participant, or hosted with `model_io` interception for `observation_substitution` only at configuration granularity (ADR-0013; OQ-453); V10 for `TE_crn`/`DE`: every source consumed after the fork is in `coupled_sources[]` with a measured `coupling_agreement`; V11 Shapley: no cross-permutation cache; `efficiency_check` within the report's interval; V12 multiplicity: many targets ⇒ ADR-0157 A12 (Holm on pre-registered targets, BH elsewhere).

### 6.4 Interface-contract sketch (language-agnostic; operations · inputs → outputs · invariants · failure modes)

- `component_targets(sealed_definition, filter?) → [ComponentTarget]` — pure over the sealed definition and its trace map; `ComponentTarget{kind ∈ {slot, variant, rule, parameter, procedure, text_leaf, decision_point}, ref: semantic_id | (run_id, seq, DecisionPoint), class_id?, ir_refs[]}`; a shared inlined procedure resolves to its source record through `derived-from` so N inlined copies form one target (OQ-217 requirement).
- `intervention_for(target, change: HirDiff | substitution) → InterventionRecord` — ADR-0135 D5 shape; derives `earliest_affected_seq` from the run's events whose `ir_refs[]` intersect the diff's touched nodes; sets `coupling_assumption`; refuses `AuthorityWidening` / `budget_delta = loosening` diffs (ADR-0017; §6.2 of phase-3 memo).
- `attribution_design(subject: run | run_set | configuration, method ∈ {M1..M5}, targets[], fork_policy, k, replay_mode_requested, noise_coupling ∈ {none, crn}, match: MatchSpec, outcome: MetricDeclaration ref, budget: BudgetNode (instrument slice), seed, pre_registration_ref?) → AttributionDesign` — validates §6.3 statically; emits the `ExperimentSpec` it desugars to (J3 `register`), so every attribution run is an experiment run (`run_kind = experiment`).
- `attribute(design) → AttributionReport/1` — executes through `hh-embed/1` Group W `counterfactual` (ADR-0176 as amended; ADR-0135 D5/D6) and Group L `analyze`; every branch `branch_kind = counterfactual`, `charged_to = instrument`; rows enter the results store as **overlay-run row versions** (phase-3 §6.1); invariants: I1 no effect without ≥ 1 factual and ≥ 1 counterfactual branch from the same fork point and snapshot; I2 `estimand` and `validity_mode` on every effect; I3 `change_rate` and `noise_floor` present; I4 `Σ rollouts` ≤ reserved budget (reserve-before-spend, ADR-0040) with truncation reported; I5 the report is pure over `(design, row watermark, seed)` (ADR-0157 D1). Failures: `InterventionPrecedesForkPoint`, `NoCoherentForkPoint`, `UnbudgetedArm`, `UnmatchedBudget`, `PolicyCollapsed(seq)`, `CouplingUnavailable(source)`, `ClassInadmissible`, `OutcomeOracleUndeclared`, `BudgetExceeded` (partial report), `ReplayInvalid(reason)`.
- `locus(report) → {seq, target, rule ∈ {point_of_commitment, earliest_direct}, interval}` — pure over a report with per-decision-point effects.
- `hypothesis(run, source: Validator{kind: judge} | human) → AttributionHypothesis` — M0; `label = observational`; provenance `delegate` or `principal`.
- `attribution_quality(configuration, comparison_ref, attribution_refs[]) → MetricValue` — `Σ |attributed effects| / |Δ|` capped at 1 with interval by the J4 kernel; `n/a{class}` hosted; `n/a{not_run}` without reports.
- `known_effect_suite(fixtures[]) → SuiteReport` — runs §6.7 and records coverage/bias per estimator.

### 6.5 Attribution as pipeline input (WS-I5 seam) and scorecard input (WS-I2 seam)

- **I5 failure-hypothesis stage** consumes `AttributionHypothesis | AttributionReport`; the candidate `HirDiff` must name targets ⊆ the hypothesis/report targets (`TargetMismatch` otherwise); the pipeline records `attribution_ref` on the candidate's `LayerProvenance{source_kind: experiment}` layer (ADR-0147 D2).
- **Label gates:** `observational` ⇒ candidate may be *evaluated* (`exploratory`, `comparable = false` for headline) but not *accepted*; `designed_ablation` ⇒ acceptance under ADR-0046 §7; `causal_interventional`/`causal_coupled` ⇒ additionally licenses the "smallest corrective intervention" claim (the diff touches only attributed targets) and the retirement test's expectation (ADR-0126: removal of an attributed rule predicts the measured Δ).
- **Evidence standard mapping (doc 2 §5.8 items):** item 4 "component-level attribution" = this report; item 3 "persistent artifact vs search-time" = `budget{search, eval}` on the report; item 6 "security-policy invariance" = interventions refused at `AuthorityWidening`.
- **I2 seam:** `attribution_quality` estimator fixed (§6.4); a naming note — `attribution_completeness` (E5/H4 effect-execution attribution token, a **veto**) and `attribution_quality` (evolvability) are different concepts; ontology rows disambiguate (§9).
- **I6 seam:** an attributed rule's `AssumptionDebtRecord.evidence_refs[]` may cite an `AttributionReport`; its removal test is M1 `retirement`.
- **F4 seam:** `estimate_rollouts(design)` is exposed so the value-of-compute scheduler can price attribution before committing.

### 6.6 Data-model sketch

### 6.7 Acceptance-criteria sketch (known-effect synthetic tests, KA-I7)

Fixtures are Harness Definitions compiled under the null profile and executed against a **fake gateway** (ADR-0129 K5 response cache / recording gateway; ADR-0125 null profile) whose control decisions follow a declared conditional distribution, so truth is known by construction; each fixture records its seed.

- **KA-I7-1 Pivotal component.** One slot's variant decides success with probability p at one decision point; two inert slots. Pass: M2 `TE_marg` attributes the pivotal slot with an interval excluding 0 in ≥ 93 % of 500 seeded runs at nominal 95 %; inert slots' intervals include 0; the locus equals the planted decision point; `change_rate` reported.
- **KA-I7-2 AND-interaction.** Two components jointly necessary (planted φ ≈ 0.5/0.5, third ≈ 0). Pass: M3 recovers each φ within its interval, `efficiency_check` within interval of 0, `interaction_index` positive for the pair; M2 single-target effects under-attribute and the report's `unattributed_share` is non-zero (the CAR test `contrastive_underattributes_the_interaction`, made a criterion).
- **KA-I7-3 Run-forward confound.** An early inert decision point upstream of a pivotal one. Pass: naive largest-magnitude ranking names the inert point in > 50 % of runs (the failure is reproduced); the point-of-commitment locus names the pivotal one in ≥ 90 %; under `deterministic`+`crn` the inert effect is 0 within Monte-Carlo error (Prop. 1 fixture).
- **KA-I7-4 Budget-splitting trap (M1).** Four slots, value in one; a uniform `SearchBudgetRecord.allocation` below each slot's search floor vs a concentrated allocation. Pass: LOI/LOO under the split allocation shows all slots null with `budget_allocation_ref` rendered beside; under concentration the planted slot's LOI interval excludes 0; the report never pools the two allocations (S-092 fixture).
- **KA-I7-5 Nondeterminism trap (refusals).** (a) fork inside an open model-call scope ⇒ `NoCoherentForkPoint`; (b) an intervention whose `earliest_affected_seq` precedes the requested fork ⇒ `InterventionPrecedesForkPoint`; (c) a fixture whose factual arm never changes action at the fork point ⇒ `PolicyCollapsed`; (d) injected `fingerprint_match = mismatch` on one arm ⇒ effects labelled `degraded`, report `exploratory`; (e) a `trace_only` branch that proposes a write ⇒ `ReplayInvalid`; (f) `TE_crn` requested without coupled sources ⇒ `CouplingUnavailable`. Nothing renders 0.
- **KA-I7-6 Mediation degeneracy (M5).** Planted opposite-signed direct and mediated paths of equal weight. Pass: `DE ≠ 0` while `TE_crn ≈ 0`; the target is rendered `inconclusive` with `exclusion_rate` reported, never `inert`.
- **KA-I7-7 Injected-fault corpus (pipeline).** Over the Stage-3 suite with a real model, inject a fault at a chosen decision point (AgenTracer shape) in N runs. Pass: M2 over `every_decision_point` places the locus at the injected point or later-but-committed in ≥ 80 % (placeholder; OQ-448), with `n/a`/refusal counts reported; an `observational` hypothesis from a calibrated critic is scored beside it and never enters `attribution_quality`.
- **KA-I7-8 Deferred effect.** A counterfactual arm whose suffix reaches an `irreversible` effect. Pass: the branch pauses `awaiting_promotion`, the outcome effect is `n/a{not_run}`, the process effect is reported, no effect is committed (joins AC-B4-5).
- **KA-I7-9 Budget truncation.** M3 with a reserved budget below `estimate_rollouts`. Pass: the report is partial with `budget_truncated = true`, `permutations_completed` < requested, intervals widened accordingly; spend never exceeds the reservation (ADR-0040).
- **KA-I7-10 Class scoping.** Hosted rows in the subject set. Pass: M1–M5 render `n/a{class}`; a hosted participant with `model_io` interception admits only `observation_substitution` at configuration granularity (T-LCD-15).
- **KA-I7-11 Reproducibility.** Re-running any KA report with the same design, watermark and seed yields an identical `report_id` (extends KA-12 of J4).
- **KA-I7-12 Pipeline gate.** A WS-I5 candidate with only an `observational` hypothesis is evaluated as `exploratory` and refused `confirmatory` acceptance; the same candidate with a `designed_ablation` report whose targets cover the diff's `semantic_ops` passes the label gate; a diff touching an unattributed target is `TargetMismatch`.

## 10. Confidence

**High** that observational attribution must be excluded as a value (Tier-A benchmark plus two corroborating preprints plus the baseline source code) and that the estimator kernel (paired proportions/bootstrap, permutation Shapley with antithetic pairing and no caching, `contrast` interaction) is correct — Tier-A game theory and statistics, one mature implementation (shap) and one faithful reimplementation (CAR) at pinned commits. **High** that the contract composes with B4/J3/J4/J5: every operation is a projection over ratified records (`counterfactual` hook, `ExperimentSpec`, `AnalysisReport`, overlay rows) and one additive dialect bump. **Medium** for the estimand hierarchy and validity modes: the analytic propositions are sound but come from an unrun preprint and CAR's live evaluation is unquantified; the design responds by labelling rather than choosing. **Medium-low** for the constants (`k ≥ 8`, `target_cap`, KA-I7-7's 80 %): MUST-data placeholders with a Stage-6 power re-check. **Low** on every 2026 magnitude; none is load-bearing.
