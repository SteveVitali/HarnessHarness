**Track:** I · **Phase:** 2 · **Tier(s) fed:** C1 (R-2.9.3) with the C0/Stage-3 slice ADR-0038 already assigns (manifest schema, `check_completeness`, R0/R1 for native runs) · **Status:** dossier-written

Product name per ADR-0011: **HarnessHarness**. Vocabulary per Ontology v1 and ADR-0048. This dossier **inherits, verbatim, the ADR-0038 bundle member list, completeness contract and level semantics R0–R3** (phase-1 §6.9 rule for I3) and the ADR-0036 identity model; it decides only what those ADRs left to I3 — the *format*, the member-identity rules that make each doc 2 §8 member addressable, the validation contract, the level *declaration* shape, the bundle lifecycle, and the interop edges. Language neutrality per ADR-0050 §8: no code, no library API, no ecosystem named; host-ecosystem *properties* only (§6.13).

## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides: (i) the **physical and logical form** of the reproducible harness bundle — how a `BundleManifest` (ADR-0038 decision 2) and its payload are laid out, addressed and transported so that any party can verify it without the producing installation; (ii) the **component identity rules** — for each of the twelve doc 2 §8 members (model snapshot, harness commit/IR version, profile, instructions, tool schemas/versions, skills/plugins, environment image, permissions/policies, context/memory policies, budgets, grader code, task data, raw event traces) which identity kind (ADR-0036) pins it, who mints it, and when an explicit `unpinned[]` claim is legal; (iii) the **validation contract** — the staged `validate_bundle` operation that refines ADR-0038's `check_completeness` into structural, integrity, completeness, closure, ledger, level, policy and cross-section checks with typed failure modes; (iv) the **reproducibility-level declaration** — the `reproducibility` section's shape, the derived `max_supported_level`, the per-level `basis[]`, and the `ReproReport` evidence that makes "re-run from bundle reproduces at declared level" a checkable acceptance criterion; (v) the **bundle lifecycle** — status records, publication policy (readers, redaction, benchmark-contamination), supersession/retraction; (vi) the **interop edges** — export to the trajectory interchange and to foreign submission formats, and import of foreign job/eval directories as hosted bundles, both as lowerings/liftings with loss reports (T-LCD-11); (vii) the **relationship to the results store** (WS-J5): what a bundle contains of J5 and what J5 records of a bundle, without a hash cycle.

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: the level semantics, member list, results-store keys and audit keys (**ADR-0038**, inherited; deviations below are refinements, flagged in §8); the five identity kinds, `idp`, rotation and the two configuration ids (**ADR-0036**); immutability, supersession, revocation and the sameness ladder (**ADR-0037**); the ledger envelope, canonical form, chain, offload, fork lineage and redaction (**WS-B1**, ADR-0026/0027/0029); the compiled bundle, `derivation_key`/`bundle_id`, `lcd_report`, `opacity_report`, lowering loss reports (**WS-A4**, ADR-0019/0021); sealing and `registry_snapshot_id` (**WS-A5**, ADR-0025); `Design`, `PreRegistration`, `Arm`, `MatchSpec`, `SearchBudgetRecord`, `compare`/`equivalence_run` and the R2/R3 margins (**WS-I2**, ADR-0045/0046; OQ-113); signed checkpoints, inclusion proofs and the attestation *protocol* (**WS-H6**; this dossier fixes only that the attestation subject is the manifest `version_id` and where attestations live in the bundle); `ExtensionRecord`/`ExtensionTrustRecord` (**WS-H5**); environment image digests (**WS-B5**); `ModelSnapshotRecord` provenance and the fingerprint probe (**WS-C1**, OQ-111); the interchange *format* choice (**WS-J6**, OQ-030); retention/GC durations and consent (**WS-L6**, OQ-085/OQ-112/OQ-116); results-store storage and leaderboard rendering (**WS-J5**); the sweep engine that produces experiment-scoped bundles (**WS-J3**); every ecosystem choice (**ADR-0050**).

## 2. Key questions

1. (doc 3 §5, verbatim) Design the reproducible harness bundle: model snapshot id, harness commit/IR version, profile, instructions, tool schemas/versions, skills/plugins, environment image, permissions/policies, context/memory policies, budgets, grader code, task data, raw event traces — as a content-addressed (L4) inspectable scientific object; reproducibility levels; bundle validation; relationship to the results store (J5).
2. (brief) Bundle manifest schema (abstract), component identity rules, validation contract, reproducibility-level declaration; acceptance criteria (re-run from bundle reproduces at declared level); ADR.
3. What is the *container form* — a single hashed document, a directory tree with a manifest, or an archive — and which of these carries identity? (§6.1, §6.2)
4. How is each doc 2 §8 member identified and pinned; which members are structurally *claims* (provider-side model, foreign digests) and how is a claim distinguished from a pin in the manifest? (§6.3)
5. What does "valid bundle" mean operationally — which checks, in what order, with what failure modes — and how does it relate to ADR-0038's `check_completeness` and `ReproClaimUnsupported`? (§6.4)
6. How is a claimed level declared, how is the maximum supportable level *derived* from pins, and what evidence (paired re-runs, independent reproducer) turns a claim into a verified status? (§6.5)
7. What are the bundle *kinds* (run, arm, experiment, lineage) and how do they compose without duplicating payload or breaking identity? (§6.7)
8. How does a bundle reference results rows and how do rows reference bundles without a hash cycle? (§6.8)
9. What may leave the installation when a bundle is published — model I/O blobs, task solutions, personal data — and how is publication recorded as a lowering with a loss report? (§6.9)
10. How are foreign artefacts (Harbor job/trial directories, Inspect eval logs, SWE-bench submissions) imported as hosted bundles, and how is a native bundle exported to them and to the trajectory interchange? (§6.10)
11. What happens to a bundle whose payload was garbage-collected, whose member was revoked, or whose results were retracted? (§6.6, §6.8)
12. Which acceptance tests prove that re-running from a bundle reproduces at the declared level, and at which build stage? (§6.11, §6.12)

## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**F1 — Every mature evaluation harness records its inputs *at the moment of use* as a lock, and every lock stops at the agent's boundary.** Harbor's `JobLock{schema_version: 3, created_at, harbor{version, git_commit_hash, is_editable}, n_concurrent_trials, retry, trials[]}` contains one `TrialLock` per trial with `task{digest}`, `extra_instructions[{digest}]`, `skills[{name, digest, git_url, git_commit_id}]`, `bridge_inputs{name → digest}`, `extra_docker_compose[{digest}]`, `verifier`, `source_trial`; equality is by digest with paths "recorded for humans and resume, not compared" (`lock.py` L60–121, L200–260). Inspect's `EvalSpec` records `task_version`, `dataset{samples, sample_ids, shuffled}`, `model`, `model_args`, `model_roles`, `config`, `revision{type: git, origin, commit, dirty}`, `packages{name → version}`, and derives `eval_id` as a stable hash of `run_id + task_id + created` on deserialization (`_log.py` L1009–1131). Terminal-Bench 1's `registry.json` pins each dataset version to `{github_url, branch, commit_hash, task_id_subset}` while its `RunMetadata.commit_hash` defaults to `"unknown"` (`harness/models.py` L35). **What none of them records:** the agent's own composition. Harbor's `AgentInfo{name, version: str, model_info{name, provider?}}` and ATIF's `Agent{name, version, model_name, tool_definitions?, extra?}`; Inspect's `plan.steps[{solver, params}]` names solvers by string; SWE-bench's prediction is `{instance_id, model_name_or_path, model_patch}`. Profile, permissions, policies, context/memory policy, budgets-as-records and provenance have **no precedent** as bundle members — exactly ADR-0038's `no-precedent / novel` scope, confirmed at source.

**F2 — Bundles converge on "manifest + per-unit records + raw traces + verifier outputs + summary", and the manifest must be authenticated as a whole.** Harbor trial dir: `config.json`, `lock.json`, `results.json`, `trial.log`, `agent/` (incl. `trajectory.json`), `verifier/`, `artifacts/manifest.json` with per-entry `status ∈ {ok, failed, empty, skipped}` and `service` (`paths.py` L83–105; `artifact_manifest.py`). Inspect `.eval`: a zip with `_journal/start.json`, `samples/…`, `summaries.json`, `reductions.json`, `header.json`, plus a directory-level `logs.json` of headers (`eval.py` L92–101; `_file.py` L495–520). SWE-bench submission: `all_preds.jsonl`, `logs/<id>/{patch.diff, report.json, test_output.txt.gz}`, `trajs/<id>.*`, `metadata.yaml`, `results/`. BagIt makes the pattern normative — bag declaration, one payload manifest per algorithm, tag manifest, `fetch.txt` for remote payload, and the **complete vs valid** distinction (every listed file present vs every checksum verified). RO-Crate deliberately keeps its metadata graph *out* of the fixity business and defers to BagIt. The in-toto bundle layer is the counter-shape: a JSON Lines file of independently signed attestations where "the Bundle is not authenticated as a whole … an attacker might be able to delete valid attestations, replay obsolete attestations, and/or inject invalid or irrelevant attestations" (`spec/v1/bundle.md`). **Implication:** the HarnessHarness manifest must be a single hashed record whose `version_id` is the bundle id and whose members form a Merkle tree (ADR-0038 decision 2 — now with the mechanism stated); attestations and reports that *refer* to the manifest sit outside the hashed record, in-toto-style, so they can accumulate without changing identity.

**F3 — Re-deriving verdicts from recorded evidence without re-executing the agent is already practised, and it fails exactly when the evidence was not captured.** Harbor `regrade`: "Run an updated verifier against recorded outputs without rerunning the agent"; requirements: a completed trial with readable `result.json` and `artifacts/manifest.json`, a separate verifier, and "every artifact required by the updated verifier was collected by the source trial and still exists"; the regrade trial's lock copies the source's task lock and skills verbatim and records `source_trial{action: regrade, trial_id, task}` as lineage (`lock.py` L91–121, L300–360). SWE-bench `swebench submit verify` re-grades instances against recorded `test_output.txt.gz` without re-execution. **Implication:** ADR-0038's R1 (derivation re-runs to equal hashes) is the general form of regrade/verify; its precondition — the evidence is in the bundle — is what `validate_bundle` must check *before* a level is claimed, and a regrade is a `derived-from` lineage edge on a new bundle, never a mutation of the old one.

**F4 — Submission validation is a lifecycle with claims that cannot be derived from the artefact, and publication carries a contamination policy.** SWE-bench's checklist requires pass@1, no `PASS_TO_PASS`/`FAIL_TO_PASS` knowledge, no `hints`, no unguarded web browsing, trajectories "generated *with* the inference process, not post-hoc", and for best@k "all rollouts + the mechanism for selecting which solution was used" (search-time vs artifact benefit, T-LCD-14 in leaderboard form); `metadata.yaml.tags.checked` marks verification by the maintainers. Terminal-Bench 2.1 requires ≥ 5 trials per task, a Harbor Hub job UUID in a PR, CI validation and maintainer review of trajectories — and has *closed* community submissions, i.e. trust in the producer is a policy decision, not a property of the artefact. HAL releases "full traces … every single model call" but **encrypted with a fixed password** to "prevent benchmark contamination through automated scraping" (`upload.py` L15, L100–120). **Implication:** a bundle has a status lifecycle (assembled → validated → attested → published | restricted → superseded | retracted), a `claims[]` section for facts that are asserted with provenance rather than derived (pass@1, no test knowledge), and a publication policy over `readers` that can withhold `model_io` and task-solution blobs while publishing the manifest.

**F5 — Derived identities keyed by names are the recurring anti-pattern.** Harbor `JobStats.format_agent_evals_key` builds `agent__model__dataset` from names (`job/result.py` L70–76); Terminal-Bench 1 keys runs by `agent_name`/`model_name`; ATIF `agent.version` and Harbor `AgentInfo.version` are free strings (CF-087). Inspect's stable `eval_id` hash over `(run_id, task_id, created)` is the one identity that is content-derived, and it is derived over *metadata*, not the configuration. ADR-0038 decision 3 ("leaderboards never key by name alone") stands; the bundle's `name_bindings[]` is where names live.

**F6 — The two members no bundle can pin are exactly the two ADR-0038 makes claims: the provider-side model and, in most systems, the environment image.** Harbor `EnvironmentConfig{type, import_path, force_build, …, extra_docker_compose[]}` digests the compose *files* but records no image digest; Inspect `sandbox: SandboxEnvironmentSpec{type, config}`; SWE-bench names images `sweb.eval.<instance>.<run_id>` and builds them from specs; HAL records nothing about the environment. Model identity: `ModelInfo{name, provider?}`, `EvalSpec.model: str`, ATIF `model_name`. **Implication:** `EnvironmentRecord{image: ContentAddress | foreign_digest}` (B5 returns digests) and `ModelSnapshotRecord{pinned, observed_fingerprint?}` (C1) are the two members whose *absence* is the norm elsewhere; the format must make partial pinning legal and visible (`unpinned[]`), which ADR-0038 already requires.

**F7 — Post-hoc annotation of results exists and mutates the record in place; the instrument must not.** Inspect supports `invalidate_samples(log, uuids, provenance: ProvenanceData{timestamp, author, reason, metadata})`, `EvalLog.invalidated`, `log_updates[]`, `config_updates[]`, and guards concurrent edits with an `etag` write-conflict (`_edit.py` L13–30, L202–250; `_log.py` L1242–1266). The annotation *shape* is right (who, when, why); the storage is a mutation of the log file. Under ADR-0037 I1–I5 a bundle is immutable; invalidation, retraction and status changes are new versioned records that `supersede` (F4's lifecycle), and results rows are annotated through a derived index, never rewritten.

**F8 — Header-first readability and payload offload are universal.** Inspect reads `.eval` "header only" without touching samples and keeps `logs.json` as a directory manifest of headers; `condense_sample` deduplicates attachments by hash inside a sample (`ATTACHMENT_PROTOCOL`); Harbor uploads the trial archive *and* "the canonical trajectory separately for direct access" (`uploader.py` L678–701) and creates archives deterministically (`uid = gid = 0`, `mtime = 0`, `mode = 0o644`, sorted paths — `publisher.py` L100–115); BagIt's `Payload-Oxum` gives a cheap size check that "MUST NOT" replace verification and `fetch.txt` lets payload live remotely. **Implication:** the manifest is small and self-verifying; payload is a content-addressed store that may be partially present (`fetch[]`), and an archive is a *transport encoding* whose bytes never carry identity — identity is the tree rule (ADR-0036 N7), which is why Harbor has to zero timestamps to get stable archive hashes.

**F9 — Bundle kinds compose by containment with an equality invariant.** Harbor: "the job lock's trial entries must match each trial's own lock.json exactly" (`lock.py` L268–272); Inspect: `eval_set_id` over `eval_id`s; SWE-bench: one submission = one system × split × all instances. **Implication:** an `experiment`/`arm` bundle references its `run` bundle manifests by `version_id` and the validator checks that the referenced manifests' `definition`/`configuration` sections equal what the experiment's `Design` declares — reference, not copy, plus an equality check (OQ-335).

**F10 — Instrument drift is recorded as a boolean claim.** Harbor `HarborLockInfo.is_editable` and Inspect `EvalRevision.dirty` are the only "the instrument may not be what its version says" signals in the audit; both are optional (`None` when unknown). ADR-0038's `InstrumentRecord{dirty}` capping the level at R0 is the strict form; the format additionally requires `dirty ∈ {true, false}` never `unknown` for a native bundle (an unknown instrument state is `dirty = true`).

**F11 — Shared-environment, budget-controlled, trace-recording protocols are the benchmark norm the bundle must serve.** Harness-Bench (S-062, `provisional (mech)`) evaluates under "shared task environments, budgets, and evaluation protocols" while preserving each harness's native execution and records "final artifacts, execution traces, usage statistics, and validator outputs" per run — the `traces` and `results` sections. Used for mechanism only; no effect size is reused.

**F12 — The reproducibility vocabulary already exists and is three-way, "with stated precision".** ACM v1.1: *repeatability* (same team, same setup), *reproducibility* (different team, same setup — "an independent group can obtain the same result using the author's own artifacts"), *replicability* (different team, different setup — "artifacts which they develop completely independently"); badges *Artifacts Available*, *Artifacts Evaluated – Functional / Reusable*, *Results Reproduced / Replicated*. Mapping adopted in §6.5: R1/R2/R3 are *reproducibility* claims about the author's artefacts at increasing re-execution strength; "with stated precision" is the pre-registered margin (OQ-113); *replicability* is WS-I2's `transfer` and lives outside the bundle; *Results Reproduced* requires a reproducer that is not the producer, so `ReproReport` gains `reproducer{…, independent: bool}` and the status `reproduced` requires `independent = true`.

**F13 — Provider nondeterminism makes bit-exact re-execution unattainable by construction.** WS-C1 F7 (S-441): 80 distinct completions in 1,000 at temperature 0; provider-side substitution (`ServerModel`, fallback boundaries) is observable but unrecorded elsewhere. This is the mechanism behind ADR-0038's ordering (R1 is the strongest *derivation* claim; R2 is *semantic*) and behind the requirement that a bundle claiming R2 carries `NondeterminismDeclaration[]` and, for the model, `observed_fingerprint` before and after (R3).

### 4b. Performance evidence (benchmark lifts) — each tagged `provisional` if 2026 single-team/preprint

None is load-bearing. Harness-Bench's 5,194-trajectory variation across model × harness pairings (S-062, `provisional`) motivates keying rows by the full factor tuple (T-LCD-09), which ADR-0045 already fixes. Terminal-Bench 2.1's "≥ 5 trials per task" and SWE-bench's pass@1 rule are *protocol* facts (§4a F4), not performance claims. The bundle format introduces no performance claim of its own; its acceptance criteria are re-derivation and re-execution agreement (§6.11), not lifts.

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

**Against a content-addressed lock manifest with pinned members (bookkeeping cost).** Harbor and Inspect ship credible science with images as tags and versions as strings; HAL keeps no lock at all; Terminal-Bench 1 defaulted `commit_hash` to `"unknown"`. Most reproduction work in practice is "read the trajectory", not "re-hash the closure". *Why it does not overturn:* the format makes partial pinning legal and visible (`unpinned[]`, derived `max_supported_level`) rather than mandatory; R0 costs one manifest and the hashes the ledger already computed (ADR-0029); the bookkeeping is paid at `seal`/`open_run` by contracts that exist for other reasons (ADR-0025/0026). What *is* conceded: the format must be usable at R0 with nothing but a manifest and a ledger export, and every check above R0 is optional to *claim*.

**Against a closed, whole-bundle-hashed manifest (in-toto's argument).** in-toto deliberately does not authenticate the bundle as a whole so that attestations from different parties and predicate types can accumulate; a closed manifest freezes the set of things one can say about a run. *Why it does not overturn:* the two layers coexist — the manifest is closed and hashed because ADR-0038 needs one attestation subject and Merkle partial verification; everything that *refers* to the bundle (attestations, `ReproReport`s, status records, independent reproductions, comments) lives in the unhashed sidecar and the registry as versioned records pointing at the manifest `version_id`. The concession is real: nothing in the sidecar can raise the bundle's claimed level; only a new bundle (superseding) can.

**Against publishing raw traces.** HAL encrypts traces to prevent scraping; SWE-bench requires public trajectories; both cannot be right for every audience. Raw `model_io` blobs contain task solutions (contamination), third-party content (copyright, personal data) and, for hosted participants, material the participant's licence may forbid redistributing (OQ-116). *Why it does not overturn:* publication is a *lowering* (ADR-0042 shape) with a `PublicationPolicy{readers, content_classes, redaction, payload_protection}` and a loss report; the manifest and accounting/structural classes (ADR-0044 L0/L1) publish; content (L2) publishes only under the policy; a bundle whose payload is withheld still verifies its manifest and still supports R1 for anyone with access. HAL's *fixed shared password* is rejected as protection (it is not access control); protection is a reader set enforced by the store, with the manifest recording that payload is `restricted`.

**Against a bundle format at all (interchange is enough).** WS-A1 §6.3 observed the bundle is "≈ Harbor trial dir + ATIF + job config … novel only in bundling model profile + permissions + policies + provenance"; ATIF has nine schema versions (v1.0–v1.8) in about a year, so the moving target is the interchange, not the bundle. *Why it does not overturn:* ADR-0038 option 2 already rejected interchange-as-identity (no profile, permissions, policies, provenance; lossy); this dossier keeps the interchange as one addressable member and a lowering target with a loss report, so ATIF churn changes an export, never a bundle id.

**Against semantic (R2) reproduction as a meaningful claim.** Agreement on named validators and end-state class can hide behavioural drift; temperature-0 outputs differ 8 % of the time on one provider; a re-run that "agrees" may do so for different reasons. *Why it does not overturn (inherited from ADR-0038 (c)):* R2 names its validator set and margin, is subordinate to R1 in derivation strength, and requires `NondeterminismDeclaration[]`; the format adds `ReproReport.per_validator_agreement` and `end_state_diff_ref` so the *disagreements* are inspectable, not averaged away.

**Against reference-not-copy composition of experiment bundles.** Harbor copies trial locks into the job lock verbatim so a job archive is self-contained; a reference-only experiment bundle is useless without its run bundles. *Why it does not overturn:* references plus `fetch[]` plus the containment-equality check give self-containment *on demand* (materialize) without duplicating payload; the `export` operation can materialize a self-contained tree (Harbor's shape) when a consumer needs one (OQ-335 records the cost question).

**Negative results recorded:** no audited system carries a declared reproducibility level, a max-supported-level derivation, or a machine-checkable "re-run reproduces" criterion — `no-precedent` for §6.5; no audited system records who reproduced a result independently — `no-precedent` for `independent`; no audited system distinguishes complete from valid bundles for agent runs (BagIt does for files) — adopted from BagIt; no audited system exports a loss report when converting a run record to a submission format — `no-precedent`, required by T-LCD-11.

## 6. Recommendation for the spec

### 6.1 Position

1. **The bundle is a content-addressed lock manifest plus a content-addressed payload, in three layers.** *Layer M (manifest):* the `BundleManifest` of ADR-0038 decision 2, one canonical record under the pinned `idp`, whose `version_id` is the bundle id; closed schema `hh-bundle/1` with an `ext` slot (registered prefixes, preserved byte-for-byte, never deciding validity — ADR-0016 rule applied). *Layer P (payload):* a content-addressed store of blobs and trees, every entry addressed by `ContentAddress` and reachable from M; entries may be present, remote (`fetch[]`), redacted (tombstone) or garbage-collected (address retained). *Layer S (sidecar):* versioned records that *refer* to M by `version_id` and are not covered by its hash — attestations (in-toto-shaped envelopes; subject = M's `version_id`), `ReproReport`s, `BundleStatusRecord`s, independent reproductions, imports' original foreign manifests. Only M carries identity; P is verified against M; S accumulates.
2. **Identity is the tree rule; archives are transport.** A bundle serialized as a directory tree, a zip, a tar or a set of registry objects has one bundle id (M's `version_id`) and one payload tree address; archive bytes are never hashed for identity (ADR-0036 N7; Harbor's zeroed-timestamp archives are the cautionary precedent). `encode(bundle, transport) → bytes` and `decode(bytes) → bundle` must round-trip to the same M and tree addresses.
3. **Complete vs valid (BagIt vocabulary adopted).** A bundle is **complete** when every member M references is listed with an address or an explicit `unpinned[]`/`fetch[]` entry and every run-manifest reference of every subject run resolves inside `resolved_dependencies` (ADR-0038 `check_completeness`); it is **valid** when it is complete *and* every present payload entry hashes to its address, M hashes to its own `version_id`, every subject run's chain verifies, and `claimed_level ≤ max_supported_level`. Levels are claims about a *valid* bundle only.
4. **Everything not derivable is a claim with provenance.** Provider model identity, foreign digests, imported version labels, submission checklist facts and hosted-participant `participant_version` enter M in `claims[]`/`unpinned[]` with a `ProvenanceRecord` (`origin ∈ {reported, imported, participant, human}`, `authority ≤ external`, or `unverified` for imported — ADR-0033); claims never raise a level.
5. **Lifecycle by supersession, never mutation** (ADR-0037): status changes, retraction, invalidation, regrade and re-pinning are new records; a bundle that "changes" is a new bundle with a `derived-from{reason}` edge; results rows are annotated through derived indexes.

### 6.2 Layout sketch (logical; path names are a convention of the directory transport, not identity)

```
<bundle-root>/
  bundle.json           # Layer M — BundleManifest canonical bytes; H(domain "bundle" ∥ bytes) = version_id
  members/<idp>/<alg>/<hex>   # Layer P — blobs and tree objects by ContentAddress (dedup across bundles)
  fetch.json            # optional — FetchEntry[] for members not materialized here
  attestations.jsonl    # Layer S — in-toto-shaped envelopes; subject = bundle.json version_id (WS-H6)
  reports/<version_id>.json   # Layer S — ReproReport, BundleValidationReport, BundleStatusRecord, ImportRecord
```

`BundleManifest` sections are ADR-0038's — `subject, definition, configuration, resolved_dependencies, model, instrument, traces, results, reproducibility, ext` — with the following **format-level refinements** (each flagged in §8 where it touches ADR-0038 wording):

- **Header fields:** `schema: "hh-bundle/1"`, `idp`, `bundle_kind ∈ {run, arm, experiment, lineage}`, `created_at`, `producer: ProvenanceRecord` (origin `kernel(component_ref)` for native assembly; `import` for lifted foreign artefacts), `participant_class`, `observability_levels` (∪ over subject runs), `claims[]: Claim{role, value, provenance}`, `name_bindings[]` (which logical names resolved to which `version_id`s — ADR-0037 I1), `fetch_policy ∈ {self_contained, detached_allowed}`.
- **`traces` per subject run** is a `LedgerExport` *tree* (not one blob): `{events: chunked canonical event pages by seq range (tree), checkpoints[]: EventRef (WS-H6 signed tree heads, exported as their own member), blob_index: [{address, present | redacted | gc, size}], head{seq, event_id, hash}, lineage_prefixes[]: {run_id, up_to_seq, head_hash} (materialized per ADR-0027)}` — chunking gives Merkle partial verification and header-first reads (F8), and the checkpoint member is what an auditor stores outside the writer (WS-H6 F1).
- **`resolved_dependencies.extensions[]`** takes WS-H5's shape `{extension_id, content, trust_record: version_id, attestation_refs[], surface_pin}` (refines ADR-0038's "extensions + signatures"; CF-300).
- **`resolved_dependencies.pricing`**: `pricing_ref: ContentAddress` of the pricing snapshot every `estimated_from_pricing` money value cites (ADR-0043) — a bundle member by default (answers the I3 half of OQ-117; custody stays WS-L2, OQ-093).
- **`results`**: `{rows[]: version_id, metric_declarations[]: version_id, oracle_declarations[]: version_id, design: version_id?, pre_registration: version_id?, arm: version_id?, match_spec: version_id?, search_budget: version_id?, scorecard_report: ContentAddress?}` — rows are members; the row→bundle back-reference is a derived index (§6.8).
- **`reproducibility`**: `{claimed_level, max_supported_level (derived, recomputed at validation), basis[]: LevelBasis{level, requirement_id, satisfied_by: MemberRef | Unpinned{role, reason}}, nondeterminism[]: NondeterminismDeclaration, evidence[]: version_id (ReproReport refs; sidecar), independent_reproductions[]: version_id, unpinned[]}` (§6.5).

### 6.4 Validation contract

`validate_bundle(bundle, mode ⊆ {structural, integrity, completeness, closure, ledger, level, policy, cross_section}, policy?) → BundleValidationReport{status ∈ {valid, complete_not_valid, incomplete, invalid}, schema, bundle_id, diagnostics[]: Diagnostic{code, severity ∈ {error, warning, info}, path, member?, detail}, derived{max_supported_level, basis[], missing[], fetchable[], foreign[], unpinned[], redacted[], gc[], payload_size, subject_runs}, derived_from, view_hash}` — all requested stages run and all are reported, never fail-fast (WS-A5 `validate_assembly` discipline); the report is a versioned record (sidecar) and `project`-pure over the bundle bytes.

| stage | checks | failure codes (errors unless noted) |
|---|---|---|
| **S1 structural** | `schema` known and ≥ minimum readable; all sections present per `bundle_kind`; `ext` keys registered; no unknown non-`ext` keys; `idp` readable | `UnknownBundleSchema`, `SectionMissing`, `UnregisteredExt`, `UnknownIdentityProfile` |
| **S2 self-identity** | `H(canonical(manifest))` under its `idp` = `bundle_id`; `producer` provenance present; `instrument.dirty ∈ {true,false}` | `ManifestMismatch`, `MissingProvenance`, `InstrumentStateUnknown` |
| **S3 integrity** (BagIt "valid") | every present payload entry re-hashes to its address (streaming; trees by the tree rule); redacted entries have a tombstone event in the run's ledger; `gc` entries have a `gc` record | `MemberMismatch{address}`, `RedactionUnrecorded`, `GcUnrecorded` |
| **S4 completeness** (BagIt "complete"; ADR-0038 `check_completeness`) | every run-manifest reference of every subject run ∈ `resolved_dependencies` or `unpinned[]`; every `fetch[]` entry is listed as a member; every `basis[].satisfied_by` names an existing member; all mandatory roles for the class present | `Incomplete{missing[]}`, `FetchUnlisted`, `BasisDangling` |
| **S5 closure** | recompute `registry_snapshot_id` over `variant_records[]`/`name_bindings[]` = declared; sealed definition's references resolve *inside the bundle* with `resolve(mode = reproduce)` (no external lookups; revoked members allowed but reported `depends_on_revoked`); compiled bundle `derivation_key` recomputes from the pinned inputs | `SnapshotMismatch`, `ClosureEscapes{ref}`, `DerivationKeyMismatch`; warning `DependsOnRevoked{member}` |
| **S6 ledger** | per subject run: `verify(chain)` ok; `head` matches `subject.run_head_hashes`; forked runs' `forked_from.source_head_hash` matches a lineage prefix present in the bundle; `audit_view.completeness` (WS-H6) holds; every `refs[]` content address in events is present, `redacted` or `gc`; checkpoints verify against exported tree heads | `Tampered{run, seq}`, `HeadMismatch`, `LineageMissing{run, prefix}`, `AuditIncomplete{reason}`, `CheckpointMismatch` |
| **S7 level** | derive `max_supported_level` from pins by the ADR-0038 rules (§6.5 table); `claimed_level ≤ max_supported_level`; every `LevelBasis` for `claimed_level` satisfied by a member, never by a claim; R2/R3 evidence, if present, references this `bundle_id` and a pre-registered margin | `ReproClaimUnsupported{claimed, max_supported, unpinned[]}`, `BasisSatisfiedByClaim`, `EvidenceForeign` |
| **S8 policy** | no secret material (R-ID-7); `readers` of every present content-class-L2 member ⊇ the bundle's publication readers (or member is `restricted`); export deliveries recorded (`measurement.export.delivered`) for every prior publication; hosted `model_io` capture consented (OQ-116) | `SecretMaterialPresent`, `ReaderViolation{member}`, `ConsentMissing` |
| **S9 cross-section** | every `results.rows[]` row's `configuration_version_id` ∈ subject runs' manifests; rows' `bundle_ref` annotation (derived) matches; `metric_declarations` referenced by rows present; for `arm`/`experiment` kinds: contained run manifests' `definition`/`configuration` sections equal the `Design`'s declared levels (F9), `eval_budget` equal across arms (`UnmatchedBudget`, ADR-0046), `pre_registration` predates every subject run | `RowOrphan`, `DeclarationMissing`, `ContainmentMismatch{run}`, `UnmatchedBudget`, `PreRegistrationLate` |

Invariants: `validate_bundle` reads only bundle bytes (plus `fetch` if allowed by `policy`) — it never consults the producing installation; two implementations agree on the report's `view_hash` for the golden corpus (T-LCD-12); `status = valid` ⇔ no error in S1–S9 for the requested stages; a `warning` never blocks a level claim but is listed in the report. `check_completeness` (ADR-0038) = `validate_bundle` with `mode = {structural, self_identity, completeness, level}`.

### 6.5 Reproducibility-level declaration

`reproducibility.basis[]` makes the ADR-0038 rules explicit and machine-derivable. `max_supported_level` is the highest level whose every requirement is satisfied by a *member* (never a claim):

| requirement id | level | requirement | satisfied by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `B-R0-traces` | R0 | every subject run's `LedgerExport` present with verified chain | `traces[run]` |
| `B-R0-complete` | R0 | S4 passes | report |
| `B-R0-instrument` | R0 | `InstrumentRecord` present with `dirty` known | `instrument` |
| `B-R1-canonical` | R1 | `idp` pinned; canonical-form version known; no `dirty` instrument | `instrument.idp`, `instrument.dirty = false` |
| `B-R1-derivations` | R1 | every derived object's inputs present: views' policy versions, `metric_declarations`, `oracle_declarations`, pricing snapshot for cost rows, validators (or foreign + recorded verdict evidence), compiled bundle inputs | `results.*`, `resolved_dependencies.*` |
| `B-R1-replay` | R1 (declared variants only) | `observability ⊇ model_io`; effect outcomes recorded | `traces[run]`, `control-strategy.deterministic_replay` |
| `B-R2-model` | R2 | every model role `pinned = true` | `model.snapshots[*]` |
| `B-R2-environment` | R2 | image by digest; build context or snapshot refs present | `configuration.environment` |
| `B-R2-closure` | R2 | tools, extensions, validators (executable trees), task data trees present; profile `active` or intent recorded; memory snapshot present when memory read | `resolved_dependencies.*` |
| `B-R2-seed` | R2 | seed material recorded; `seed_honoured` declared per participant | `configuration.seed`, capability declaration |
| `B-R2-nondeterminism` | R2 | `NondeterminismDeclaration[]` present and each source `confined_to_events` or listed as residual | `reproducibility.nondeterminism` |
| `B-R3-budget` | R3 | `MatchSpec`, `eval_budget` (and `search_budget` if any search) present | `results.match_spec`, `results.eval_budget`, `results.search_budget` |
| `B-R3-fingerprint` | R3 | `observed_fingerprint` before and after for every unpinned model role | `model.snapshots[*].observed_fingerprint` |
| `B-R3-design` | R3 | `Design` and `PreRegistration` with `equivalence_margin` and `min_n` | `results.design`, `results.pre_registration` |

Class scoping (T-LCD-15): for hosted participants R1's `B-R1-replay` and R2's `B-R2-closure` are `n/a{class}`; R2 is claimable only when the hosting mechanism supplies `end_state`; the report renders `n/a`, never 0 (ADR-0038 decision 1).

**Evidence and status.** `ReproReport` (ADR-0038 decision 5) is refined to `{report_id, bundle_ref: version_id, level, procedure: version_id (the reproduce operation's own sealed definition and instrument record), reproducer: ProvenanceRecord, reproducer_instrument: InstrumentRecord, independent: bool (reproducer installation ≠ producer installation, asserted by distinct signer under WS-H6), verdict ∈ {reproduced, not_reproduced, inconclusive, n/a{reason}}, per_member_hash_checks[], per_validator_agreement[]: {validator, agreement (c, n)}, end_state_diff_ref?, distributions?: ComparisonReport (ADR-0046 shape; `benefit_kind = artifact`, margin from `pre_registration`), nondeterminism_sources_observed[], budget_match, seeds[], cost: ResourceVector (charged `instrument`)}`. Mapping to ACM v1.1 (F12): `claimed_level ∈ {R1, R2, R3}` with `verdict = reproduced` and `independent = false` is *repeatability*; with `independent = true` it is *reproducibility* ("different team, same artifacts") and is the only basis for `BundleStatusRecord.status = reproduced`; *replicability* is never a bundle status — it is a `transfer` `ComparisonReport` (ADR-0046) between different bundles.

### 6.6 Interface-contract sketch (language-agnostic; operations · inputs → outputs · invariants · failure modes)

- `bundle(scope ∈ {run_ids[], arm_id, experiment_id, lineage(run_id)}, kind, policy: BundlePolicy{materialize ∈ {self_contained, detached, manifest_only}, readers, redaction, include_interchange_export, claims[]}) → (BundleManifest, payload tree | fetch[])` — reads only the ledger, blob store, registry and results store; pure given their state at the watermark it records (`subject.watermarks[run] = (run_id, seq)`); materializes fork lineage; computes `max_supported_level`; refuses a `claimed_level` above it. *Failures:* `RunNotDurable` (open run; a bundle of an unfinished run records `subject.status = open` and caps at R0), `SecretMaterialPresent`, `ReproClaimUnsupported`, `ManifestReferenceUnresolved{ref}`.
- `validate_bundle(…)` — §6.4.
- `reproduce(bundle, level, seeds?, margin_override?: none, reproducer) → ReproReport` (ADR-0038 decision 5, refined): **R0** = `validate_bundle` full; **R1** = delete-and-re-derive: for each subject run rebuild every declared view (`project`), results rows, opacity/LCD/loss reports, the compiled bundle from pinned inputs, and the manifest itself; compare hashes; for declared-replay variants re-drive the control strategy against recorded `model_io` (ADR-0028 driver) and compare the `control.decision` and `view_hash` sequences; **R2** = `open_run` with the bundle's `configuration_version_id` materials (environment by digest, model roles pinned, validators executable, task tree, memory snapshot), n paired re-runs with the recorded seed material, `compare` (ADR-0046) on the named validator set and end-state class against `pre_registration.equivalence_margin`; **R3** = R2 procedure over `seeds[]` with the model allowed unpinned, fingerprint probed before and after, distributions compared under `MatchSpec`. *Invariants:* refuses unmatched budgets (`UnmatchedBudget`, T-LCD-14); every re-run is a normal ledger run charged `instrument` with `causes[] → bundle_ref`; the report references the reproducer's own `InstrumentRecord`; never mutates the bundle. *Failures:* `LevelUnsupported{max_supported}`, `MemberUnavailable{address}` (payload `gc`/`fetch` failed), `EnvironmentUnavailable{digest}`, `ModelUnavailable{snapshot}`, `InsufficientReplicates`.
- `export(bundle, target ∈ {interchange_trajectory, ledger_native, harbor_job_dir, inspect_eval_log, swebench_submission, in_toto_bundle, telemetry_trace}, policy: PublicationPolicy) → (artefact: ContentAddress, LoweringLossReport, measurement.export.delivered)` — every target is a lowering (ADR-0021/0042 shape) with a typed loss report (`no_slot | hint_only | untyped_slot | narrowed | truncated`) and `granularity_ceiling`; the native ledger export is the only lossless target; publication of L2 content is governed by `readers`/`redaction`; the export is recorded in the subject runs' ledgers so `audit_view` can prove what left. *Failures:* `TargetUnsupportedForClass`, `ReaderViolation`, `UnexpressibleMember` (never silent).
- `import(artefact, format ∈ {harbor_job_dir, harbor_trial_dir, inspect_eval_log, swebench_submission, interchange_trajectory}, reader_policy) → (BundleManifest{participant_class = hosted, producer.origin = import, claimed_level ≤ R0}, ImportRecord{format, source, foreign_manifest: ContentAddress, mapping_loss: LoweringLossReport, lifted_authority = unverified})` — foreign digests → `foreign` claims; version strings → `claims[]`/`name_bindings[]` (CF-087); events lifted into an `open_run + append` batch with `provenance = imported` (ADR-0026 decision 7); nothing lifted is ever `environment` or higher (ADR-0033 rule 6); Harbor `lock.json` maps section-by-section (task → `task_data.foreign`, skills → `extensions[].foreign`, `harbor{…}` → a `claims[]` instrument claim, `source_trial` → `derived-from{regrade}`). *Failures:* `FormatUnknown`, `ForeignIntegrityMismatch` (a foreign digest that does not verify against supplied bytes — recorded as a warning-level claim conflict, never coerced), `ConsentMissing`.
- `attest(bundle, signer) → Attestation` (WS-H6 protocol; subject = manifest `version_id`; appended to `attestations.jsonl`); `audit_bundle(bundle, auditor_store) → AuditReceipt` (WS-H6 `audit` over each subject run's checkpoints).
- `status(bundle_id) → BundleStatusRecord` / `set_status(bundle_id, status, reason, authority) → BundleStatusRecord` with `status ∈ {assembled, validated, attested, published, restricted, reproduced, superseded, retracted}`; monotone except `published ↔ restricted` (a readers change) and any → `retracted`; `reproduced` only with an `independent = true` report; `retracted` never deletes (ADR-0037; results rows annotated `bundle_retracted`).
- `supersede_bundle(new, old, reason ∈ {regrade, repin, redaction, migration, correction}) → edge` — the new manifest carries `derived-from{old, reason}` (ADR-0016 edge; ADR-0037 S-rules); `lineage(bundle_id)`.
- `diff(bundle_a, bundle_b) → BundleDiff{sections: map<section, [MemberDelta{role, a, b, kind ∈ {added, removed, changed, surface_only}}]>, definition_diff: HirDiff?, sameness: L0–L4 (ADR-0037), configuration_delta: {model, profile, environment, budget, seed}, level_delta}` — the lab's "what differs between these two arms" operation; pure.
- `fetch(bundle, addresses[], locations?) → {materialized[], failed[]}` — resolves `fetch[]` entries; verifies on arrival (S3); never trusts a location's digest claim.
- `encode(bundle, transport ∈ {directory, zip, tar, registry_objects}) → bytes` / `decode(bytes) → bundle` — deterministic; round-trips to the same `bundle_id` and tree addresses; archive entries carry no ownership/timestamps (Harbor precedent).
- `migrate_bundle(bundle, to_schema) → new bundle + derived-from{migration}` — schema migration is a supersession; golden fixtures for every prior `hh-bundle/N` must load (WS-B1 AC-B1-8 shape).

All operations are implementable out-of-process over the canonical encoding (T-LCD-12); failure modes are typed errors, never warnings, except validation diagnostics and `ReproReport` verdicts, which are declared outcomes.

### 6.7 Data-model sketch

`BundleManifest` (ADR-0038 + §6.2 refinements) · `MemberRef{role, ref, media_type?, size?, status, foreign?}` · `ForeignRef{system ∈ {harbor, inspect, swebench, terminal_bench, hal, oci_registry, git, other}, digest, label?, provenance}` · `Claim{role, value: TypedValue, provenance}` · `Unpinned{role, reason, claim?}` · `FetchEntry{address, size?, locations[]: credential_free_uri, expires?}` · `LedgerExport{events: tree, checkpoints[], blob_index[], head, lineage_prefixes[]}` · `LevelBasis{level, requirement_id, satisfied_by}` · `ReproReport` (§6.5) · `BundleValidationReport` (§6.4) · `BundleStatusRecord{bundle_ref, status, readers, reason, supersedes?, provenance, created_at}` · `ImportRecord` · `BundleDiff` · `PublicationPolicy{readers, content_classes ⊆ {accounting, structural, content, diagnostic} (ADR-0044), redaction ∈ {none, allowlist, pseudonymize}, payload_protection ∈ {none, restricted_store}, loss_report}` · `BundlePolicy` · `BundleCatalogueEntry` (J5 view: `{bundle_id, kind, subject runs, configuration_id(s), claimed_level, status, size, created_at}`) · composition rule: `experiment` ⊃ `arm` ⊃ `run` by `version_id` reference with S9 containment equality; `lineage` = a run bundle plus every fork-prefix bundle it references.

### 6.8 Relationship to the results store (WS-J5)

- **What the bundle holds of J5:** the results rows for its subject runs (`results.rows[]` by `version_id`), the declarations they cite, the design/arm/pre-registration records, and, for `experiment` kind, the `ScorecardReport` address. Rows are `project(run, results_row)` views (ADR-0026) — the bundle pins the exact rows, so R1 re-derivation can compare them.
- **What J5 records of a bundle:** `bundle_ref` and `claimed_level` on every row (ADR-0038 decision 3) are **derived index annotations** maintained by the results store when `bundle` runs — they are *not* fields inside the hashed row bytes, which would make the row's `version_id` depend on a manifest that in turn lists the row (CF-298). Aggregation stays by `configuration_id`; leaderboard rows are `(results row, name-history entry, bundle_ref, status)`; a row whose bundle is `retracted` or `depends_on_revoked` is annotated, never hidden (ADR-0037 S-rules).
- **The bundle catalogue** is a J5 view over manifests (Inspect's `logs.json` shape): header-only, filterable by `configuration_id`, level, status, participant class; it is how the Lab finds "every bundle of this configuration at ≥ R1 with status ≥ validated".
- **One direction of truth:** rows never assert a level; bundles never assert a metric value other than by pinning rows.

### 6.9 Publication, privacy and contamination

A published bundle = `export(bundle, ledger_native | interchange, PublicationPolicy)`. Defaults (MUST-data, ADR-0024): manifest, accounting (L0) and structural (L1) classes public to the bundle's `readers`; content (L2: `model_io`, tool observations, context items, judged evidence) `restricted_store` unless the policy names the reader set and every item's `readers` label (ADR-0034) admits it; task-solution material for benchmarks under a contamination policy (WS-I4) is `restricted` by default (HAL's motive, without HAL's fixed password); redaction is WS-B1's tombstone and is visible in the manifest (`status = redacted`); hosted participants' L2 requires recorded consent (OQ-116). The loss report of a publication lists every withheld member so a reader knows what R-level *they* can verify (R1 needs content for view re-derivation; R0 does not).

### 6.10 Hosted participants and foreign artefacts

A hosted run's bundle uses the same manifest (T-LCD-06): `traces[run]` holds the lifted event batch plus `hosted.native_record` blob; `model` is a claim; `definition` holds the `OpaqueProcess` declaration `version_id` and `participant_version` claim; `resolved_dependencies` lists what the hosting mechanism supplied (context, tools, skills) as pinned members and everything else as `unpinned{hosted_mechanism}`; `claimed_level ∈ {R0, R3}`, R2 only with `end_state`. Imports of Harbor job/trial directories, Inspect `.eval` logs and SWE-bench submissions produce hosted bundles at R0 whose `ImportRecord` keeps the foreign manifest byte-for-byte as a member, so a Harbor lock stays inspectable and its digests remain checkable when the bytes are fetched (CF-087). A native bundle exported to a submission format degrades to `product` granularity with the loss listed (`permissions`, `policies`, `provenance`, `budget`, `opacity_report` → `no_slot` in every audited target).

### 6.11 Acceptance-criteria sketch (re-run from bundle reproduces at declared level)

- **AC-I3-1 Self-verifying manifest.** For the Stage-3 reference run, `decode(encode(bundle, t))` yields the same `bundle_id` and tree addresses for every transport `t`; a one-byte change anywhere in the manifest or a present member is reported by `validate_bundle` (`ManifestMismatch` / `MemberMismatch`) with the member named.
- **AC-I3-2 Complete vs valid.** A bundle with a `fetch[]` member not yet materialized is `complete_not_valid`; after `fetch` it is `valid`; a bundle missing a run-manifest reference is `incomplete{missing[]}` (AC-L4-8 lifted).
- **AC-I3-3 Level derivation.** Fixtures: (a) native run with everything pinned → `max_supported_level = R3` (and R2); (b) same with `ModelSnapshotRecord.pinned = false` → R2 excluded, R3 allowed only with fingerprints; (c) `dirty = true` → R0; (d) hosted session-ABI run → R1/R2 `n/a{class}`, never 0; claiming above the derived maximum is `ReproClaimUnsupported`.
- **AC-I3-4 R1 re-derivation (AC-L4-9 lifted to the format).** Delete every view, results row, report and the manifest; `reproduce(bundle, R1)` re-derives equal `view_hash`es, equal row `version_id`s, an equal compiled `bundle_id` from pinned inputs, and an equal manifest `version_id`; for a declared-replay variant the `control.decision` sequence matches (AC-B1-4).
- **AC-I3-5 R2 re-execution.** For the reference configuration with pinned model, digest image and executable validators, `reproduce(bundle, R2, n ≥ 3)` yields a `ReproReport` with per-validator agreement (c, n) and end-state class match against the pre-registered margin; the re-runs are ordinary ledger runs charged `instrument` and linked by `causes[]`.
- **AC-I3-6 R3 statistical.** `reproduce(bundle, R3, seeds[k])` produces a `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind = artifact}` with `budget_match.status`; unequal `eval_budget` is refused (`UnmatchedBudget`); a fingerprint change between before/after is reported as `nondeterminism_sources_observed ∋ provider_drift`.
- **AC-I3-7 Independence.** A `ReproReport` signed by the producer's key yields `independent = false`; the same procedure run on a second installation (distinct `InstrumentRecord`, distinct signer) yields `independent = true` and is the only way `set_status(reproduced)` succeeds.
- **AC-I3-8 Export/import round trip.** `import(export(bundle, harbor_job_dir))` yields a hosted bundle whose `ImportRecord.mapping_loss` lists every dropped native member and whose foreign digests verify against the exported bytes; `export(bundle, interchange_trajectory)` carries the ADR-0021 minimum set through the interchange's typed extension slot or reports `granularity_ceiling = product`.
- **AC-I3-9 Foreign import at R0.** A Harbor trial directory (config.json + lock.json + results.json + agent/trajectory.json) imports as a hosted bundle with `claimed_level = R0`, `participant_class = hosted`, lifted authority `unverified`, the lock kept as a member, and every lock digest carried as a `foreign` claim; `validate_bundle` passes structural/completeness and reports `foreign[]`.
- **AC-I3-10 Publication loss.** `export` under a policy withholding L2 content records `measurement.export.delivered` in every subject run and a loss report naming each withheld member; a reader of the published artefact can validate the manifest (R0) but `reproduce(R1)` reports `MemberUnavailable` for withheld inputs rather than a false verdict.
- **AC-I3-11 Lifecycle immutability.** After `set_status(retracted)` and after `supersede_bundle(new, old, regrade)`, the old manifest and members are byte-identical, results rows referencing it are annotated (not removed), and `lineage(new)` shows the edge.
- **AC-I3-12 Composition.** An `experiment` bundle over two arms with differing `definition` sections in a contained run manifest fails S9 (`ContainmentMismatch`); with equal sections and equal `eval_budget` it validates and `diff(arm_a_bundle, arm_b_bundle)` reports exactly the varied factor with `sameness` level.
- **AC-I3-13 Schema evolution.** Golden `hh-bundle/1` fixtures load under every later schema; `migrate_bundle` yields a `derived-from{migration}` bundle and never rewrites the source.

### 6.12 T-LCD statement and ADR-0024 rung

Satisfies **T-LCD-14** (`reproduce` refuses unmatched budgets; `MatchSpec`/`eval_budget`/`search_budget` are members and R3 requirements), **T-LCD-15** (levels and requirements are class-scoped; hosted bundles render `n/a`), **T-LCD-09** (rows keyed by the factor tuple are members; the catalogue indexes `configuration_id`), **T-LCD-11** (every export/import is a lowering/lifting with a typed loss report; foreign digests are never dropped), **T-LCD-06** (no member exists only for hosted participants; hosting off leaves the format unchanged), **T-LCD-10** (identity excludes names and archive bytes; `name_bindings` separate), **T-LCD-12** (operations over canonical bytes; out-of-process), **T-LCD-02** (no free-text instruction member; opacity report is a member). ### 6.13 Build-stage suggestion and host-ecosystem requirements

- **C0 / Stage 1** (inherited from ADR-0036/0038, no new work here): both configuration ids and `InstrumentRecord` in the run manifest; `MemberRef`/`VersionedRef` shapes; the `hh-bundle/1` schema published as a dialect document so Stage-1 ledger exports can be wrapped.
- **C0 / Stage 3 (evaluation-first):** `bundle(kind = run, self_contained)`, `validate_bundle` S1–S7, `reproduce` at R0/R1/R3 for native runs, `encode/decode` (directory + one archive transport), `LedgerExport` tree, results rows as members, bundle catalogue view (J5), AC-I3-1…6, AC-I3-13; interchange export with loss report once OQ-030 lands (`ledger_native` export at minimum).

- **Host-ecosystem requirements (properties only; ADR-0050 §8(a)):** the kernel ecosystem must provide the one canonicalizer and streaming agile hash of ADR-0036 H1–H2, deterministic tree serialization (H3), a content-addressed store with delete-by-address (H4), deterministic archive writers/readers that omit ownership and timestamps, JSON Lines append, and out-of-process invocation of every §6.6 operation (H9) — satisfied by the ADR-0050 kernel ecosystem. **Boundary placement (ADR-0050 §8 clause 4):** `bundle`, `validate_bundle`, `encode/decode`, `reproduce` R1 and `attest` execute on the kernel side because they read the ledger, blob store and identity service; `reproduce` R2/R3 orchestration, `diff` analysis, the catalogue and `export` to analysis formats run on the lab side over the §6.5 boundary, exchanging `BundleManifest`, `BundleValidationReport` and `ReproReport` as typed documents (D2: whole records cross, never streams of payload bytes — payload moves by address through the store).
