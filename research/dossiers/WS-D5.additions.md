## Ontology terms

| term | definition | notes |
|---|---|---|
| procedure profile (`ProcedureProfile/1`) | The registered `ext` block on a HIR/1 `Procedure`: `{index, parameters, outputs?, applicability_notes, invocation_policy, failure_classes, target_override?, composition, relations, source}`; never decides authority, budget or validity. | WS-D5 ADR-1; HIR/2 promotion list filed |
| skill | The packaging of one or more Procedures as an extension of kind `skill` (WS-H5) — an Agent Skills file tree or equivalent — lifted into HIR by `lift_skill` and exported by `export(skill_tree)`. Not an entity. | WS-D5 ADR-3; WS-H5 |
| checkable precondition | A `Predicate` the kernel evaluates from records: `path_glob, capability_present, artifact_present, env_requires, validator_passed, parameter_bound, budget_remaining` (closed), or a `Ref<Validator>`. | WS-D5 ADR-1 |
| applicability note | Model-facing `Text` describing when a procedure applies, its risks, avoidance rules and recovery hints; never evaluated by the kernel; validity `unknown`. | BASM-class boundary prose (S-WS-D5-01, `provisional`) |
| bindable (B(P)) | A Procedure whose every `Invoke` is fully bound, every `Branch` checkable, every `Loop` bounded, and whose `Instruction` steps are bodies of bounded, closed-schema `model_call` sub-steps. | WS-D5 ADR-2 |
| procedure target | The compile-time lowering of a Procedure: `instruction` (model-facing body; β = model), `workflow_node` (`RuntimePlan` nodes; β = code), `subagent_task` (`Delegate` to a child process); chosen by the total selection rule; a Lab factor. | WS-D5 ADR-2; refines ADR-0016 `compile_hint` |
| selection rule (procedure) | The total, pure function `select_target(P, profile, def)` over `B(P)`, `R(P)`, `I(P)` with typed refusals `TargetInfeasible, UnexpressibleAsWorkflow, DelegationUnavailable, ProcedureUnverifiable`. | WS-D5 ADR-2 |
| risk floor | The rule that a procedure with `irreversible`/`external` effects must declare `expected_evidence` and that every such `Invoke` passes Π regardless of target. | WS-D5 ADR-2 |
| procedure test suite / procedure validity | `{v | validates(v → P)}` with fixtures by `depends-on`; `validity(P, at) ∈ {valid, invalid, unknown}` computed from kernel-recorded verdicts and `StaleIndex`; model-free. | WS-D5 ADR-1; ADR-0014 |
| procedure conformance | `{procedure_version_id, profile_semantic_id, target, validity_verdict, compliance{activated, followed}, run_id}` — the per-profile measured pair for one procedure. | WS-D5; ADR-0014 |
| invocation policy | `{model, principal, implicit}` booleans on a procedure: who may trigger it; enablement is data, never authority. | codex/pi/OpenHands precedent |
| render purity / `RenderTimeExecution` | Invariant that rendering a procedure body executes nothing; a body with an execution marker fails validation; dynamic content is a declared `filling` slot bound to a capability. | WS-D5 ADR-1/-3; ADR-0019 |
| procedure selector | Registered component class `select(view, catalog, profile, accounting, k) → Selection{method, ranked[], truncated}`; deterministic; reads index surfaces and records only; emits `context.procedure.selected`. | codex `CheapSkillSelector` precedent |
| induced procedure | A Procedure version with `origin ∈ {model, evolution}`, mandatory `derived-from{hypothesis, trajectories[], candidate_id}`, validity `unknown`, authority `≤ delegate`. | WS-D5 ADR-3; WS-I5 |
| promotion policy | `{require_validity = valid, require_transfer_test?: [profile_scope]}` gating the `promotion` endorsement of a procedure; refusal is `PromotionRefused{reason}`. | WS-D5 ADR-3; ADR-0035 |
| inline_at_resolve | HIR/1 composition: a callee Procedure's steps are inlined into the caller at `resolve` with the binding applied, recorded as `derived_from{kind: projection}`; acyclic. | WS-D5 ADR-1; `Call` step filed for HIR/2 |
