## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides **how an experiment is written down, expanded, scheduled, executed, recovered and closed** — the operational half of the measurement backbone WS-I2 ratified. Concretely: (i) the **experiment definition data model** (`ExperimentSpec`): factors and levels over the six ratified factor kinds, the design kinds (`paired`, `full_factorial`, `fractional_factorial`, `one_factor_at_a_time`, `adaptive_search`) and the deterministic `expand` operation that turns a spec into cells and run plans; (ii) the **registration preconditions** that make T-LCD-14 (no un-budgeted comparison), T-LCD-09 (explicit factors) and T-LCD-15 (class-scoped admissibility) engine-level refusals rather than review items; (iii) the **scheduler contract** — work units, claims, ordering, concurrency pools over execution environments (B5) and providers, experiment-scoped budget nodes (L2), matched-budget enforcement at each of four points; (iv) the **run lifecycle and its events** on the run ledger (B1) — an experiment is itself a run of a declared kind whose ledger is the scheduler's only durable state; (v) **fault tolerance and resumability** — re-run policy by outcome class, the distinction between an infrastructure re-attempt and a replicate, restart from the ledger, and the experiment-level kill points added to the B3 battery; (vi) how **both participant classes** enter as levels of the harness factor (ADR-0001/0013) without changing native contracts; (vii) the two **canonical exemplars** (`lab/compaction-family-v1`, ADR-0077; `lab/control-strategy-family-v1`, ADR-0105) instantiated as `ExperimentSpec` documents at their Stage-3 sizes; (viii) **adaptive validation** (Task-CoEvolve S-082, `provisional`) and successive-halving early stopping as optional strategies admissible only inside search budgets.

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: metric declarations, outcome classes, intervals, benefit kinds and the `compare`/`equivalence_run` operations (**WS-I2**, ADR-0045/0046/0047 — consumed verbatim; the engine *enforces* their preconditions and *feeds* their inputs); statistical models, interaction estimation, Pareto frontiers and fitted surfaces (**WS-J4**); the results-store schema, catalogue and leaderboard (**WS-J5** — this dossier fixes only what the engine writes: `measurement.experiment.*` events and the results-row key fields it stamps); assembly and validation of a Harness Definition and the override grammar (**WS-A5/J1**, ADR-0023/0025 — the engine calls `compose → resolve → validate_assembly → seal` and never composes by itself); the component-variant registry (**WS-J2**); the Hosting ABI, capability probes and hosted adapters (**WS-J6** — the engine consumes `capability_vector` and `OpaqueProcess` declarations); resource taxonomy, budget nodes and `MatchSpec` semantics (**WS-L2**, ADR-0039–0041); environment handles, `derive` and snapshots (**WS-B5**); single-run durability, leases and wakeups (**WS-B3**); suites, task records, split assignment and graders (**WS-I4**); the bundle format (**WS-I3** — the engine calls `bundle(kind ∈ {arm, experiment})` at close); every ecosystem choice (**ADR-0050**).

## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**F1 — Every audited batch runner resumes by *scanning its own outputs* and decides what to skip from a status field; three of five delete partial state as the recovery primitive, and none records why a run was re-attempted.** SWE-agent `should_skip` reads `info.exit_status` from the trajectory and `unlink`s it when the status is `early_exit`/missing/unparseable; mini-swe-agent removes the prediction and trajectory *before* re-running; Harbor `rmtree`s every trial directory lacking `result.json` on resume and deletes a failed trial's directory before each retry; HAL keeps the JSONL but treats any value starting with `"ERROR"` as not completed; Inspect keeps failed logs (`started` never removed) and reuses completed samples, removing only *older* logs of the same identifier when `retry_cleanup` is set. Consequence: an interrupted attempt's cost, its partial trace and the reason it was re-attempted are unrecoverable in four of five systems, which is exactly the "search spend is unrecoverable if not logged" hazard ADR-0046 names. **Implication:** the engine never deletes; a re-attempt is a new run with `attempt_no + 1` whose predecessor stays in the ledger as `superseded_by`, and the decision to re-attempt is a ledgered event with the outcome class that justified it (§6.4).

**F2 — Work identity is the resumability primitive, and only content-derived identity survives a retry.** Inspect's `task_identifier` hashes the task file, name, args, model, solver plan, model args and *limits* — explicitly excluding transport options such as `max_retries`/`max_connections` — and is computed identically from a planned task and from a finished log, "to pair tasks with their existing log files across retries"; it is versioned so persisted identifiers are recomputed on change. Harbor pairs planned `TrialConfig`s with existing ones by structural equality and refuses resume when the job config differs. SWE-agent and mini-swe-agent key by instance id only, so a second replicate of the same instance is unrepresentable without a new output directory; HAL's `run_id` is a benchmark name plus a timestamp. **Implication:** the unit of planned work is a `RunPlan` whose id is content-derived from `(experiment_id, arm_id, configuration_version_id, task_id, replicate_index)`; this is what ADR-0036's `configuration_version_id` ("the run manifest's exact-bytes key") plus the task and replicate coordinates already imply, and it is what makes "exactly one accepted run per plan" checkable after any crash (§6.4). Inspect's separation of the *definition's* request from *runner overrides* in the capture manifest is the same distinction ADR-0025 draws between definition layers and the `experiment` layer.

**F3 — Concurrency is bounded at two levels everywhere it is bounded at all, and the second level is a *shared resource* (provider, agent, tracker state), not the run.** Harbor: one global semaphore plus per-agent `concurrency_group` pools whose limits must agree and may not exceed the global; Symphony: `max_concurrent_agents` plus `max_concurrent_agents_by_state`; Inspect: `parallel` task×model units plus `max_samples`, `max_sandboxes`, `max_subprocesses` and per-model `max_connections`; SWE-agent/mini: one thread pool with a random start delay to avoid a "thundering herd" of container starts. **Implication:** the scheduler's pools are declared over *resource classes* — model snapshot (rate limits), environment class (B5 backends), instrument capacity (graders/judges) — with the global cap on `fan_out` a gauge on the experiment's budget node (ADR-0040 gauge caps), and staggered start as a declared policy rather than a constant (§6.3).

**F4 — Retry policy in production is typed by *failure class*, bounded, and backed off — but every audited runner retries the *whole unit* and none separates an infrastructure re-attempt from an intended replicate.** Harbor retries only exception types in `include_exceptions` minus `exclude_exceptions`, up to `max_retries`, with `min_wait·mult^attempt` capped; Symphony retries with `min(10 s·2^(attempt−1), 5 min)` and never restarts a run blocked on a human; Inspect retries failed *tasks* up to 10 times with 30 s → 1 h waits, reusing completed samples; SWE-agent aborts the *entire batch* on `TotalCostLimitExceededError`. Harbor's `n_attempts` and Inspect's `epochs` are replicates; their retries are separate counters (`n_retries` in `JobStats`) but a retried attempt *replaces* the failed one in the trial directory, so the two are distinguishable only in the summary count. **Implication (§6.4):** re-attempt eligibility is a function of the ratified outcome class (ADR-0045) via the ADR-0106 projection — `infrastructure_failure` is re-attemptable under a bounded policy; `scored`, `budget_exhausted`, `refused` are final; `oracle_failure` is a regrade (ADR-0141 `derived-from{regrade}`), never an agent re-run; `cancelled` is re-plannable only when the cancel was by the operator/hosting and the experiment says so. `attempt_no` and `replicate_index` are orthogonal axes, and denominators report both (ADR-0045 outcome-class policy).

**F5 — No audited runner randomises or blocks run order across arms; all of them iterate one configuration over all tasks, then the next.** Harbor builds `trial_configs` as agents × tasks × attempts in declaration order; Inspect schedules "models ⇒ tasks groups"; SWE-agent runs one agent config per batch; HAL runs one agent per invocation; W&B `grid` iterates the cartesian product in order. With provider-side drift documented (WS-C1 F7; ADR-0120 fingerprint probe) and cache warmth an experimental confound (ADR-0128), running arm A on Monday and arm B on Tuesday confounds the harness effect with time. Classical design (S-506; S-167) answers this with randomisation within blocks: **the task is the block** (ADR-0045 pairing by task), and the order in which arms visit a block is randomised with a recorded seed. **Implication:** the scheduler's default ordering interleaves arms per task under a recorded permutation seed, and the fingerprint probe (ADR-0120; OQ-286) brackets the experiment so drift is *recorded* on the row (`n/a`-safe) rather than assumed away (§6.3).

**F6 — Scheduler state that lives only in process memory is the documented restart gap; scheduler state that is a projection of a durable record restarts for free.** Symphony §14.3 states its scheduler state is "intentionally in-memory" and that no retry timers survive restart; goose forgets interrupted scheduled jobs (WS-B3 F2); Inspect, Harbor and SWE-agent recover by re-scanning output directories, which works only because each unit's completion is a file. The ledger already gives HarnessHarness the durable record (ADR-0026) and B3 the restore procedure (ADR-0130). **Implication:** the experiment itself is a run — a ledger of a declared `kind: experiment` with no control strategy — and every scheduler view (`pending`, `claimed`, `in_flight`, `settled`) is `project(experiment_run, scheduler_view, until_seq)`; restart is `restore(experiment_run)` under a writer lease, and subject runs restore independently under their own leases (ADR-0130 four scopes). This reuses the "run without a strategy" shape B3 proposed for goal inboxes (OQ-314) rather than inventing an experiment store.

**F7 — Sweep definition and sweep execution are separate concerns in every tool that has both, and the definition is data.** Hydra's sweeper (which combinations) vs launcher (serial, joblib, submitit); W&B's `method`/`parameters`/`early_terminate` document consumed by a controller that hands runs to agents; Inspect's capture manifest listing every resolved task before any worker starts. **Implication (§6.2/§6.3):** `expand(ExperimentSpec) → CellPlan` is a pure function producing a complete, content-addressed plan *before* the first run opens (the pre-registration boundary of ADR-0045), and scheduling is a separate contract over that plan. This is also what makes fractional designs auditable: the generator set and resolution are part of the plan, not of the run loop.

**F8 — Adaptive evaluation is real but rank-only and search-scoped.** Task-CoEvolve (S-082) concentrates evaluations on tasks where candidates disagree and corrects estimates by inclusion probability, cutting evaluations ~80 % at matched final performance; WS-I2 F8 records the estimator's rank correlation of 0.13 at a 7 % sampling fraction. Hyperband (S-504, Tier A) formalises early stopping as adaptive resource allocation over random configurations with brackets `s` and reduction factor `eta`, and W&B exposes it as `early_terminate` with `min_iter`/`eta`/`strict`. ADR-0046 §6 already rules that adaptive selection is admissible only inside `search_budget` and that sampled `artifact_benefit` rows carry `estimated{…}`; ADR-0143 L3 requires split assignment before search. **Implication:** the engine offers `ValidationStrategy ∈ {full_set, disagreement_weighted, successive_halving}` with the last two admissible only on arms whose `Design.kind = adaptive_search` and only over `search`/`dev` split labels, recording inclusion probabilities and bracket membership as ledger blobs (§6.7).

**F9 — Hosted participants can be levels of the harness factor only along coordinates the Hosting ABI can actually vary, and the audited planes confirm the boundary.** Harbor runs installed agents with a `model_name` override and a fixed image; Inspect's agent bridge intercepts model calls (WS-A1 F2); Harness-Bench shares environments, budgets and protocols "while preserving each harness's native execution". ADR-0013 fixes admissible granularities per class and ADR-0046 (d) flags `limits_enforced = partial` when token/spend limits are reported rather than enforced. **Implication:** a hosted level is `{participant_ref, participant_version claim, SUPPORTED coordinate overrides}`; the engine refuses a component-level factor containing a hosted level (`InadmissibleFactor`), enforces wall-clock and environment budgets at the environment boundary, and stamps `limits_enforced` on the arm (§6.5).

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

**Against an experiment ledger and content-addressed run plans ("a directory of outputs is enough").** Harbor, Inspect, SWE-agent and HAL all ship credible science with resume-by-scanning-outputs and no experiment-level durable state; Symphony runs production orchestration with in-memory scheduler state. *Why it does not overturn:* the audited approach loses the attempt history (F1), cannot represent replicate vs re-attempt (F4), and Symphony's own spec documents the restart gap (F6). The ledger, `project`, leases and `restore` exist already (ADR-0026/0130); the experiment run adds one run kind and one event family, no store. The concession: at Stage 3 a single-experiment CLI may materialise the scheduler view as a file for humans, provided it is a projection (ADR-0026 rebuild-equality).

**Against refusing at registration ("let exploratory sweeps run").** Strict preconditions (`MatchSpec`, pre-registration, split assignment, replicate floors) slow exploratory work; W&B and Hydra impose none. *Why it does not overturn:* ADR-0041 (c) already admits execution with `MatchSpec{mode: none}` while refusing to *report a comparison*; this dossier keeps that line — an `exploratory` experiment kind runs without a `PreRegistration` and its rows are stored with `comparable = false`, never entering a `ComparisonReport` (§6.2). T-LCD-14's wording ("run or report a comparison") is honoured exactly.

**Against interleaved randomised arm order.** Interleaving multiplies environment churn (no image reuse across consecutive runs of one arm), defeats per-arm cache priming, and complicates human reading of progress; every audited runner orders by configuration. *Why it does not overturn:* image reuse is a B5 backend property independent of order (`fresh_from_image` per replicate is mandatory anyway, ADR-0138); priming is a declared `cache_policy = primed` per arm (ADR-0128) and `cold_start` is the matched default; the ordering is `SchedulingPolicy` data with `by_configuration` admitted for `exploratory` experiments and for `iso_cost` arms where drift is bracketed by fingerprints. The cost of *not* randomising is an unmeasurable confound on the only reportable quantity (the paired Δ).

**Against re-attempting `infrastructure_failure` automatically.** A re-attempt spends subject budget again, and a systematic infrastructure fault (a broken image) can burn `max_reattempts × cells`; Harness-Bench reports no retry mechanism at all. *Why it does not overturn:* re-attempts are bounded per plan and per experiment (`ReattemptPolicy{max_per_plan, max_fraction_of_plans, backoff}`), charged as `instrument` cost with the superseded run's consumption kept and reported (never silently absorbed into the arm's efficiency row), and a re-attempt rate above the declared fraction pauses the experiment with `paused{reason: infrastructure_suspected}` for a human (the `infra_suspected` annotation ADR-0144 already foresees).

**Against admitting adaptive validation at all.** S-082 is `provisional`; its estimator is noisy at low sampling fractions (ρ = 0.13 at 7 %); Hyperband's guarantees assume a resource that monotonically improves the estimate, whereas a harness evaluation's "resource" (replicates or tasks) is heteroskedastic across tasks; both can select brittle winners (PRISM S-093 via I2). *Why it does not overturn:* the strategies are optional, search-only, split-scoped (ADR-0143 L3), and every row they touch carries `estimated{estimator, inclusion_probabilities_ref}` (ADR-0046 §6); artifact benefit is always full-set on the held-out split. Nothing at C0–C2 depends on them.

**Against the experiment as a run ("one ledger per run" was meant for agent runs).** ADR-0026's fenced single writer and per-run hash chain were designed for a control loop; an experiment run has no strategy, no effects, and may live for days. *Why it does not overturn:* B3 already proposed strategy-less `inbox` runs (OQ-314) and continuation chains for long-lived goals; the experiment run uses the same shape (`kind: experiment`, `continued_from` if it outgrows a chain length). The conflict is recorded (CF-332) so synthesis settles OQ-314 once for both.

**Negative results.** (N1) No audited runner distinguishes an infrastructure re-attempt from a replicate in stored per-run state; (N2) none randomises arm order or records a permutation seed for arms (only for instances); (N3) none records *why* a unit was re-run; (N4) none enforces a budget equality between configurations before running (confirms WS-I2 N(ii)); (N5) none plans fractional designs — every sweep is a full grid or a random/Bayesian sample; (N6) Harness-Bench's methodology text documents no failure-handling protocol.

## 6. Recommendation for the spec

### 6.1 Position

1. **An experiment is a content-addressed data document expanded into a complete plan before its first run opens.** `ExperimentSpec` (§6.2) is MUST-data (ADR-0024); `expand` is pure and total; `experiment_id = H(canonical(ExperimentSpec))` under the pinned identity profile (ADR-0036); the `PreRegistration` it references is immutable from the first `open_run` (ADR-0045). Amendment is a new experiment with `derived-from{amendment}` (ADR-0037).
2. **The engine refuses, never warns, at registration** (T-LCD-14/-09/-15 as preconditions; §6.2 refusal table). Exploratory experiments run with `comparable = false` and never enter a `ComparisonReport`.
3. **The experiment is a run; the scheduler's state is a projection.** One ledger of `kind: experiment` carries the `measurement.experiment.*` family; every subject run stamps `measurement.experiment.bound`; restart is `restore` (ADR-0130). No second store.
4. **A run plan is settled exactly once.** `run_plan_id` is content-derived; at most one *accepted* run per plan; re-attempts are new runs with `attempt_no + 1`, admissible only for `infrastructure_failure` (and operator/hosting `cancelled` when declared), under a bounded, ledgered policy; superseded runs are never deleted and their consumption is reported.
5. **Matched budget is enforced at four points** (register, open, settle, close — §6.5) and every subject run's root `BudgetNode` is allocated from its arm's `eval_budget` dimension-wise (ADR-0040/0041).
6. **Order is a design variable.** Default: task-blocked, arm-interleaved, seeded permutation; fingerprint probes bracket the experiment; the policy and seed are data in the experiment manifest.
7. **Both classes are levels; admissibility is class-scoped.** Hosted levels enter at configuration/product granularity along SUPPORTED coordinates only; native contracts are untouched (T-LCD-06).

### 6.2 Experiment definition data model (C0/Stage 1 schema; C1 service)

- `ExperimentSpec{dialect: "hh-experiment/1", name_binding?: Text, kind ∈ {comparative, exploratory, equivalence, retirement, reproduction}, design: Design (ADR-0045), pre_registration: Ref<PreRegistration> (required unless kind = exploratory), factors: [FactorSpec], arms: [ArmSpec], suite: SuiteBinding{suite_ref, split_labels_used ⊆ {search, dev, held_out, private}, split_assignment_ref? (ADR-0143 — required when any arm has search_budget > 0 or design.kind = adaptive_search)}, replicates_per_cell: n ≥ 1, seed_policy (ADR-0045), validation_strategy: ValidationStrategy (§6.7; default full_set), scheduling: SchedulingPolicy (§6.3), reattempt: ReattemptPolicy (§6.4), budgets: {experiment: BudgetSpec (subject pool ceiling), instrument: BudgetSpec}, bundle_policy: BundlePolicy (ADR-0139), ext}`.
- `FactorSpec{kind ∈ {model_snapshot, harness, environment, task, budget, replicate}, granularity? (harness only; ADR-0013), role? (model_snapshot: the ModelRoleTable role varied, CF-313), levels: [LevelSpec]}` with `LevelSpec{level_id, ref: semantic_id | participant_ref | EnvironmentRecord.semantic_id | BudgetSpec | suite task set, overrides?: [ConfigurationOverride] (ADR-0025 grammar; a comma-list `path=v1,v2` is sugar for several levels), label: Text, class ∈ {native, hosted}, non_portable?: bool}`. A harness level at `component-level` is `(class_id, variant_ref | parameter path, value)`; at `configuration-level` a non-component coordinate; at `product-level` a sealed definition version or a hosted `participant_ref` + `participant_version` claim.
- `Design.kind` semantics fixed here (ADR-0045 named the kinds): `full_factorial` = cartesian product of all level sets; `fractional_factorial` requires `generators[]` and `resolution ∈ {III, IV, V}` and is refused (`ResolutionInsufficient`) when the pre-registration names an interaction the resolution cannot estimate (resolution IV for any two-factor interaction of interest; V when two-factor interactions must be mutually unconfounded — S-506); `paired` = exactly one varied factor with all other factors at one level, pairing by task; `one_factor_at_a_time` = a base cell plus one-level perturbations; `adaptive_search` = a `search` split arm set whose cells are chosen by `validation_strategy` inside `search_budget`.
- `ArmSpec{arm_id, hypothesis: Text, level_assignment: map<factor_kind, level_id | "varied">, eval_budget: BudgetSpec, search_budget: SearchBudgetRecord | 0, match_spec: MatchSpec (ADR-0041 incl. cache_policy), artifact_ref: sealed definition version_id | participant descriptor, limits_enforced ∈ {full, partial} (derived), model_role_table_ref}` (ADR-0046 decision 1).
- `expand(spec) → CellPlan{plan_id: ContentAddress, cells: [Cell{cell_id, arm_id, configuration_id, configuration_version_id, task_id, split_label}], run_plans: [RunPlan{run_plan_id = H(experiment_id ∥ arm_id ∥ configuration_version_id ∥ task_id ∥ replicate_index), cell_id, replicate_index, seed_material, environment_derivation: fresh_from_image (ADR-0138), cache_scope_salt (ADR-0128 cold_start)}], order: OrderPlan{permutation_seed, blocks: [task_id → [run_plan_id]]}, generators?, aliasing_table?}`. Invariants: pure and deterministic; every configuration in `cells` was produced by `compose(layers + experiment layer{LayerProvenance{source_kind: experiment, id: experiment_id}}) → resolve → validate_assembly → seal` (ADR-0025) — **OQ-076 answer: yes, always**, and an ad-hoc single-run override from a surface is an implicit `exploratory` experiment of one cell so attribution can always name the override; `configuration_id` is ADR-0036's (seedless); `bundle_id` never enters it (CF-108).
- **Registration refusals** (`register(spec) → experiment_id | Refusal`): `MissingMatchSpec`, `UnbudgetedArm`, `UnmatchedBudget` (eval_budget unequal across arms of a `comparative`/`equivalence`/`retirement` experiment), `IncommensurableMatch` (cross-model token match; mixed `cache_policy`; differing metering formulas), `MissingPricingTable`, `InadmissibleFactor` (hosted level in a component-level factor; ADR-0013), `MissingPreRegistration`, `SplitUnassigned` / `LeakedSplit` (ADR-0143 L3), `UnsealedArtifact`, `InsufficientReplicates` (a primary metric's `replicate_reducer = pass_k(k)` with `k > replicates_per_cell`), `ResolutionInsufficient`, `ProfilePinnedAcrossProfiles` (**OQ-078, J3 half:** a definition whose `profile_binding` pins one profile is admissible only with `non_portable = true` and only in arms where profile is not the varied factor — it is excluded from cross-profile factors rather than silently compiled under a foreign profile), plus every `validate_assembly`/`seal` error surfaced per cell (`BenchmarkConditionedRule`, `AuthorityViolation`, …). Refusals are typed errors, never warnings (T-LCD-14).

### 6.3 Scheduler contract (C1/Stage 3–4; C0 slice = single-worker execution of a plan)

Operations (language-neutral; lab-side per ADR-0050 D2 — see §6.9):
- `open_experiment(experiment_id) → RunId(kind = experiment)` — `open_run` (ADR-0026) with a manifest `{experiment_id, plan_id, scheduling_policy, reattempt_policy, budgets}`; appends `measurement.experiment.registered{spec_ref, pre_registration_ref, split_assignment_ref?, plan_id}` then one `run_planned{run_plan_id, cell_id, replicate_index}` per plan; allocates the experiment `BudgetNode{scope: Experiment, mode: pool}` and the instrument node (ADR-0040); runs the fingerprint probe per model level (ADR-0120; `charged_to = instrument`) and appends `drift_bracket{opened}`.
- `next(experiment, worker_capacity) → [RunPlan]` — a pure view: eligible = planned − claimed − settled(accepted), filtered by pool availability, ordered by `OrderPlan` (task-blocked, arm-interleaved by the recorded permutation; `by_configuration` admitted only for `exploratory` or where declared with a reason), with `start_stagger_ms` applied. Precondition: reconcile-before-dispatch (Symphony §8.1) — `next` is never computed from a stale watermark.
- `claim(run_plan_id, holder) → Lease{scope: resource(run_plan_id)}` (ADR-0130 lease scopes; no new scope) — appends `run_claimed`; a second claimant is `WouldBlock`; expiry without `run_launched` returns the plan to eligible (ledgered `claim_expired`).
- `launch(run_plan, lease) → RunId` — (1) `allocate(parent = experiment node, scope = AgentProcess, mode = slice, spec = arm.eval_budget)` (ADR-0040; refusal `InsufficientBudget` pauses scheduling, never trims a run's budget — trimming would break T-LCD-14); (2) environment `provision`/`derive(fresh_from_image)` from the cell's `EnvironmentRecord` (ADR-0136/0138) or, for hosted levels, the mechanism's environment with the same record; (3) `open_run(manifest{configuration_version_id, experiment{experiment_id, arm_id, cell_id, replicate_index, attempt_no}, budget: allocated node, seed_material, cache_policy, split_label})`; (4) the subject run appends `measurement.experiment.bound{experiment_id, arm_id, configuration_id, cell_id, replicate_index, attempt_no, budget_id}` (B1 class, payload extended — CF-331); (5) the experiment run appends `run_launched{run_plan_id, run_id, attempt_no}`. For hosted levels the launch goes through the Hosting ABI session verbs (ADR-0098) with the boundary budget of ADR-0040 and `limits_enforced` stamped.
- `settle(run_plan, run_id) → RunOutcome` — on the subject's `lifecycle.run.finished`: read `outcome_class` (ADR-0106 projection), post `budget_utilization` per dimension, run the grader through I4's `grade` (`charged_to = instrument`), decide `accepted | superseded{reattempt}` per §6.4, append `run_settled{run_plan_id, run_id, outcome_class, accepted, attempt_no, budget_utilization, veto_tripped[]}`, release the claim, release the slice remainder to the pool (ADR-0040 affine rule).
- `pause(experiment, reason ∈ {budget_exhausted{node}, infrastructure_suspected, operator, drift_detected})` / `resume(experiment)` — pausing stops `next` from yielding; in-flight runs finish (ADR-0040 E1; never killed mid-effect); `operator` pause is the only path that may `cancel` in-flight runs, through the stop protocol (ADR-0106) with `cancelled{by: operator}`.
- `close(experiment) → ExperimentReport` — precondition: no eligible or claimed plans (or `partial = true` declared by the operator); appends `drift_bracket{closed}` (second fingerprint probe; a changed fingerprint annotates every row of that model level `provider_drift = observed`, never invalidates silently), `closed{status ∈ {completed, partial, aborted}, coverage: settled_accepted / planned, outcome_counts}`; calls `bundle(kind = experiment)` (ADR-0139) and `bundled{bundle_id}`; hands the results watermark to J4/J5 (`render_scorecard`, `compare` — ADR-0046, whose preconditions are re-checked here as the fourth enforcement point).
- `SchedulingPolicy{max_concurrent_runs, pools: [Pool{key ∈ {model_snapshot(ref), environment_class(class), instrument, participant(ref)}, limit}], order ∈ {interleaved_blocked (default), by_configuration{reason}}, permutation_seed, start_stagger_ms, deadline?, priority?}` — MUST-data; pool limits ≤ `max_concurrent_runs` (Harbor's agreement rule); `fan_out` gauge on the experiment node caps live subject runs.

Invariants: **S-1** every `next` result is derivable from the experiment ledger alone (no memory-only state — F6); **S-2** at most one accepted run per `run_plan_id` (AC-J3-4); **S-3** a subject run's root budget equals its arm's `eval_budget` dimension-wise (AC-J3-5); **S-4** no subject run opens after `paused` or `closed`; **S-5** every subject run of the experiment carries `measurement.experiment.bound` and every bound run appears in exactly one `run_settled` or is in flight; **S-6** interleaving is by the recorded permutation (replaying `expand` + `permutation_seed` reproduces `OrderPlan`). Failure modes: `WouldBlock`, `Fenced`, `InsufficientBudget`, `PoolExhausted`, `EnvironmentUnavailable`, `SpecMismatch`, `PlanNotFound`, `ExperimentClosed`.

### 6.4 Run lifecycle, outcome classes and re-attempts (C0/Stage 3 rule; C1 service)

Per-plan state (a view): `planned → claimed → launched → settled{accepted} | settled{superseded} → (re-attempt: planned with attempt_no + 1)`. Mapping from the subject run's terminal facts (ADR-0106 projection onto ADR-0045 classes) to the engine's decision:

| outcome_class | accepted? | re-attempt? | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scored` | yes | never | includes `completed`, `loop_detected`, `format_failure` stop reasons (ADR-0106 item 7; stratified by reason) |
| `budget_exhausted` | yes | never | a harness outcome; counted per ADR-0045 capability policy |
| `refused` | yes | never | |
| `oracle_failure` | no (row kept) | **regrade** only (ADR-0141 `derived-from{regrade}`; ADR-0144 separate verifier) — the agent is never re-run for a grader fault | |
| `infrastructure_failure` | no | yes, under `ReattemptPolicy{max_per_plan (default 2), max_fraction_of_plans (default 0.10), backoff{min_ms, multiplier, max_ms}, error_classes_included?}` keyed on ADR-0102/0119 `error_class` | consumption of the superseded run is kept and reported as `instrument`-attributed waste (`wasted_runs`, `reattempt_rate` metrics); exceeding `max_fraction_of_plans` ⇒ `paused{infrastructure_suspected}` |
| `cancelled{by}` | no | `by ∈ {operator, hosting}` and `reattempt.on_cancel = replan` ⇒ yes (attempt_no + 1); `by ∈ {principal, parent}` ⇒ final | an operator pause/resume cycle never loses a plan |

Rules: **R-1** `attempt_no` and `replicate_index` are orthogonal; results rows key by `replicate_index` and carry `attempt_no`; a superseded run never enters a replicate count (ADR-0045 "n = independent runs") but its outcome and consumption enter the experiment's `outcome_counts` and the arm's reported waste. **R-2** a re-attempt reuses the plan's `seed_material` (same requested sampling seed, same image digest) — it is the *same* replicate, not a new one. **R-3** a subject run that crashes is restored by B3 (ADR-0130) under its own lease; only when its `recovery_decision = stop{infrastructure_failure}` or its lease expires unrecoverably does the engine treat it as `infrastructure_failure`; the engine never re-drives a run it can restore. **R-4** veto trips (ADR-0047) do not change acceptance; they annotate the row (success-with-veto). **R-5** every decision above is a ledgered `run_settled`/`run_replanned{reason}` event — the "why was this re-run" record no audited runner keeps (N3).

Event family (plane `measurement`, `durability = ledger`, minimum observability `events`, producer = the engine; additions to the ADR-0026 table, envelope unchanged — CF-331): `measurement.experiment.registered`, `.run_planned`, `.run_claimed`, `.claim_expired`, `.run_launched`, `.run_settled`, `.run_replanned`, `.paused`, `.resumed`, `.drift_bracket`, `.closed`, `.bundled`; on subject runs the existing `measurement.experiment.bound` with the extended payload. Audit-grade (WS-H6): `registered`, `closed`, `paused{operator}`, `run_replanned`.

### 6.5 Matched-budget and admissibility enforcement (four points)

E-1 **register:** ADR-0041/0046 preconditions as typed refusals (§6.2). E-2 **launch:** the root `BudgetNode` is the arm's `eval_budget`; `cache_policy` salt applied; `MatchSpec` and `limits_enforced` recorded in the run manifest; hosted arms whose token/spend limits are only reported get `limits_enforced = partial` and enforced wall-clock/`env.*` ceilings at the environment boundary (ADR-0046 (d)). E-3 **settle:** `budget_utilization` and consumption distributions posted; an arm whose median utilisation falls below a declared floor is annotated `under_utilised` (ADR-0041 M1 "an under-utilising arm is not cheaper"). E-4 **close:** `compare` preconditions re-checked over realised runs — `UnmatchedBudget` (limits), `budget_match.status ∈ {matched, imbalanced}` by the OQ-124 tolerance (engine default 10 % per dimension, data), `InsufficientReplicates(task)` ⇒ `n/a{not_run}` cells, `provider_drift` strata. Class scoping: a metric cell for a hosted level renders `n/a{class | observability}` (ADR-0045); the engine never fills a hosted cell with a native proxy (T-LCD-15).

### 6.6 The two canonical exemplars as `ExperimentSpec` instances (executes ADR-0077 and ADR-0105; nothing re-decided)

**E-A `lab/compaction-family-v1` (ADR-0077) — Stage-3 two-arm instance.** `kind: comparative`; `design.kind: paired` (varied factor `harness` at `component-level`, class `compaction_strategy`); levels `{evict_oldest (C0 reference), clear_tool_results}`; fixed factors: one model level (primary role; `ModelRoleTable` incl. `summarizer_profile` fixed), one environment level (Terminal-Bench 2.0 image family per ADR-0144 A, `fault_profile = none`), `task` = suite A held-out split, `budget` = one `eval_budget` `{tokens.input.total, tokens.output.total, model_calls, time.wall_ms}` with `MatchSpec{mode: matched_cap, tolerance: 0.10, model_scope: same_snapshot, cache_policy: cold_start}`; `replicates_per_cell: 5`; `search_budget: 0` per arm; `pre_registration{hypothesis: H2 (re-acquisition/repeated-action separate variants where success does not), primary_metrics: [capability.task_success, grounding.reacquisition_rate, reliability.repeated_action_rate], equivalence_margin: per OQ-113, min_n: 5, task_split_hash}`; `scheduling{max_concurrent_runs: ≤ pool(environment_class: local_container).limit, order: interleaved_blocked, permutation_seed recorded}`; `reattempt: defaults`; `validation_strategy: full_set`; outputs: `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind: artifact_benefit}` per ADR-0046 plus the seven ADR-0077 process metrics; bundle `kind = experiment`. **Full family (C1/Stage 5):** `design.kind: full_factorial` over `compaction_strategy` (five levels + `window_ceiling` reference arm flagged `reference_only = true`, excluded from the primary contrast) × `model_snapshot` (≥ 2 families × ≥ 2 tiers) × `environment` (three families) with `fractional_factorial{resolution: IV}` admitted when the cell count exceeds the experiment cost ceiling and the pre-registration names only main effects and the model × strategy interaction; the "retire rule R" arm is `kind: retirement` (§6.8). **Boundary companion (C2/Stage 4):** an `ExperimentSpec` whose `task` levels are `fork points` (ADR-0027 `fork_by_reference` at each `context.compaction.started`) — the engine treats a fork point as a task coordinate with `environment_derivation: fork_snapshot`; k = 5 continuation turns as the `eval_budget` on `turns`.

**E-B `lab/control-strategy-family-v1` (ADR-0105) — Stage-3 first execution.** `kind: comparative`; `design.kind: full_factorial` over `harness` (`component-level`, class `control_strategy`, levels `{react/minimal, react/steerable}`) × `model_snapshot` (2 families, roles fixed) with `environment` fixed (one coding suite from ADR-0144 A/B), `task` = held-out split, `budget`: `MatchSpec{dimensions: {model_calls, tokens.output.visible, time.wall_ms}, mode: matched_cap, cache_policy: cold_start}` plus one `iso_cost` arm (ADR-0105 decision 1) — the two `MatchSpec`s live on distinct arms and the engine refuses their mixing in one `compare` (`IncommensurableMatch`); `replicates_per_cell: 5`; `seed_policy{seed_honoured_required: false}` with `react/minimal` declaring `seed_honoured`; `pre_registration{H0 null, H1–H4 per ADR-0105, primary_metrics: [capability.task_success, control.reproducibility, efficiency.model_calls], min_n: 5}`; a variant whose `capabilities.requires` is unmet yields `n/a{reason: class}` cells at `expand` (never a 0 row). Full family (Stage 4) adds `plan_execute`, `workflow`, `program` levels; the β-ablation recipe is `kind: retirement` over `HirDiff`s on `ControlBoundary` (§6.8).

### 6.7 Adaptive validation and early stopping (optional; C4/Stage 6; search-only)

`ValidationStrategy ∈ {full_set, disagreement_weighted{lambda, min_inclusion_fraction, estimator ∈ {hajek, anchored_difference}}, successive_halving{eta (default 3), min_budget, brackets}}`. Admissibility (engine-refused otherwise, `AdaptiveOutsideSearch`): only when `design.kind = adaptive_search`, only on arms with `search_budget > 0`, only over `split_labels ⊆ {search, dev}` (ADR-0143 L3), with the `SplitAssignmentRecord` registered before the first run. Mechanism: `disagreement_weighted` samples tasks with weights rising in the candidates' outcome variance (S-082 mechanism) and records per-task inclusion probabilities as a content-addressed blob referenced by every row (`estimated{estimator, inclusion_probabilities_ref}`, ADR-0046 §6); `successive_halving` allocates `replicates` (the resource) in brackets, promoting the top `1/eta` of candidates per rung (S-504; W&B `early_terminate` shape) and recording bracket membership and the rung at which each candidate was dropped. Reporting: search-time benefit only (ADR-0046 `search_time_benefit`); the frozen artifact's `artifact_benefit` is always a `full_set` experiment on the held-out split with its own `eval_budget`; total spend enters `SearchBudgetRecord` for matched-total (M3) claims. Every number these strategies produce is rank-grade, labelled, and never the sole basis of acceptance (ADR-0046 §7).

### 6.8 Built-in experiment kinds beyond the two exemplars (recipes as data)

`equivalence` (T-LCD-03/-04: `equivalence_run` over a reference participant and a native definition; refuses unequal budgets; verdict against `PreRegistration.equivalence_margin`), `retirement` (T-LCD-05: two arms `definition` vs `definition − rule R` as a `HirDiff` with `semantic_ops = 1`, `authority_delta = none`, `budget_delta = none`; the removal test of ADR-0126 and the β-ablation of ADR-0105 are instances), `reproduction` (ADR-0140 `reproduce` at R2/R3 is an experiment whose arms are the bundle's configuration and the reproducer's re-runs), `exploratory` (no pre-registration; `comparable = false`). All are `ExperimentSpec.kind` values with fixed constraints, not separate engines.

### 6.9 Boundary placement (ADR-0050 D2; §6.5 verbs)

`register`/`expand`/`next`/`close` and the strategies run on the **lab side**; `open_run`, budget allocation, environment provisioning, leases and the ledger are **kernel side**; the records that cross are whole documents — `ExperimentSpec`, `CellPlan`, `RunPlan`, run manifests, `RunOutcome`, `ExperimentReport` — never streams of events (payload moves by address). The experiment ledger is written through the same `open_run/append` verbs as any run, so the engine is implementable out-of-process (T-LCD-12).

### 6.10 Acceptance-criteria sketch

- **AC-J3-1 (T-LCD-14 gate; C0/Stage 3).** `register` refuses each of the §6.2 refusal cases on a fixture set (one fixture per code); no refusal is a warning; an `exploratory` experiment executes without `MatchSpec` and its rows carry `comparable = false` and are rejected by `compare`.
- **AC-J3-2 (T-LCD-09 gate; C0/Stage 3).** `expand` on a 2 models × 2 variants × 3 tasks × n = 3 spec yields 36 run plans keyed by the full tuple; `fractional_factorial{resolution: IV}` over five two-level factors yields the declared fraction with the aliasing table; a pre-registered two-factor interaction under resolution III is refused.
- **AC-J3-3 (T-LCD-15; C2/Stage 4).** A hosted level in a component-level factor is refused; the same participant at configuration-level runs and its component-level cells render `n/a{class}`; `limits_enforced = partial` is stamped where limits are reported only.
- **AC-J3-4 (exactly-once settlement; C0/Stage 3).** Kill the engine at KP-E1 (after `run_planned`, before `claim`), KP-E2 (after `claim`, before `open_run`), KP-E3 (after `open_run`, before `bound`), KP-E4 (after the subject's `finished`, before `run_settled`), KP-E5 (during `close`/`bundle`); after `restore(experiment)` every plan has exactly one accepted run or is eligible; no subject run is duplicated; the scheduler view rebuilt from the ledger equals the live view (ADR-0026 rebuild-equality).
- **AC-J3-5 (budget equality; C0/Stage 3).** For every subject run, `root BudgetNode.hard == arm.eval_budget` dimension-wise; a launch under an exhausted experiment pool is refused `InsufficientBudget` and the experiment pauses; no run's budget is ever trimmed.
- **AC-J3-6 (re-attempt discipline; C0/Stage 3).** Inject one `infrastructure_failure` (helper death, KP-15 of ADR-0132) into one plan: a second run with `attempt_no = 2` opens with identical `seed_material`; the first stays in the ledger as `superseded`; its consumption appears in `wasted_runs`; the replicate count for the cell is unchanged; a `scored` run is never re-attempted; an `oracle_failure` triggers a regrade bundle, not a re-run.
- **AC-J3-7 (order and drift; C0/Stage 3).** `OrderPlan` is reproducible from `permutation_seed`; consecutive launches alternate arms within a task block under the default policy; the fingerprint probe runs at open and close and a forced change annotates rows `provider_drift = observed` without deleting any.
- **AC-J3-8 (exemplar E-A; C0/Stage 3).** The two-arm compaction instance runs end-to-end on one backend, producing a `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind: artifact_benefit, budget_match.status}` and the seven ADR-0077 metrics; ADR-0077 AC-D2-8/-9 partial obligations are met.
- **AC-J3-9 (exemplar E-B; C0/Stage 3).** The two-variant control-strategy instance runs with `matched_cap` and `iso_cost` arms; `compare` across the two `MatchSpec`s is refused; H0–H4 are reported with direction and intervals (ADR-0105).
- **AC-J3-10 (concurrency pools; C1/Stage 4).** With `pools = [model_snapshot(M): 2, environment_class(local_container): 4]` and `max_concurrent_runs = 4`, the ledger never shows more than 2 live runs on M or 4 live runs overall; a pool limit above the global cap is refused at `register`.
- **AC-J3-11 (bundle at close; C1/Stage 4).** `close` produces an `experiment` bundle that passes ADR-0140 S9 (`ContainmentMismatch`, `UnmatchedBudget`, `PreRegistrationLate` all absent) and whose `results.design/pre_registration/arm/match_spec/search_budget` members resolve.
- **AC-J3-12 (adaptive strategies; C4/Stage 6).** `disagreement_weighted` and `successive_halving` are refused outside `adaptive_search` arms or on `held_out` labels; when admitted, every row carries `estimated{…}` and inclusion probabilities/bracket records are content-addressed members; the frozen artifact's `artifact_benefit` row is full-set.
- **AC-J3-13 (retirement kind; C1/Stage 5).** A `retirement` experiment refuses a `HirDiff` with `authority_delta ≠ none` or `budget_delta ≠ none` or `semantic_ops ≠ 1`.
- **AC-J3-14 (hosted launch; C2/Stage 4).** A hosted level launches through the Hosting ABI session verbs, its run stamps `measurement.experiment.bound`, its wall-clock ceiling is enforced at the environment boundary, and no native contract changes (T-LCD-06).

## 10. Confidence

**High** for the data model, `expand`, registration refusals and the four enforcement points: they are the typed, operational form of ratified ADR-0041/0045/0046/0143 and of Tier-A design theory, with Inspect's versioned task identity and Harbor's config-equality resume as source precedents at pinned commits. **High** for ledger-projected scheduler state and the settlement rule: they follow from ADR-0026/0106/0130/0132 and every audited runner's restart behaviour (including Symphony's self-documented gap). **Medium** for the scheduling defaults (interleaved order, drift bracketing, pool shapes, re-attempt bounds): the mechanisms are classical or precedented, but no audited runner interleaves arms and the defaults are unmeasured until Stage 3 (OQ-360/-04). **Medium** for fractional designs over categorical factors (theory is Tier A for two-level factors; multi-level orthogonal arrays are an open question). **Low** for the adaptive strategies' *usefulness* (S-082 `provisional`; Hyperband's assumptions only approximately hold) — which is why they are optional, search-only and rank-labelled. No `provisional` number is load-bearing anywhere in the C0/C1 content.
