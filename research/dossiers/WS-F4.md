## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides **how HarnessHarness decides whether the next unit of compute is worth spending — and on what.** Concretely it fixes: (i) the **scheduler decision contract** — a policy component (`compute_policy`, home = control plane) invoked at a closed set of decision points that enumerates a closed set of *compute options* (continue as configured, spawn a subagent, take a longer search — another sample, another branch, another retry — call an evaluator/critic, change effort level or model role for the next call, or commit/stop), attaches to each an estimated marginal value, an estimated cost, an estimated latency and a typed risk, and emits a **decision plus a rationale record** as a ledger event so every allocation is auditable and attributable (WS-B1); (ii) the **estimator interface** — a registry component class (`compute_estimator`) with a heuristic, rule-based baseline as the C3 default and learned variants (online bandit with forgetting; predictor models) as registered variants that enter only through the evolution pipeline (WS-I5); (iii) **online estimation under non-stationary models** — priors keyed by model snapshot and task class, forgetting/windowing, hard resets on fingerprint drift and profile supersession, `unknown` never interpolated; (iv) **value-of-information for evaluation budget** on the Lab side — how the scheduler allocates the experiment's *instrument* and search budget across candidate cells (Task-CoEvolve S-082, `provisional`) strictly inside J3's `adaptive_search` design kind; (v) the **evaluation protocol** that proves a scheduler variant beats matched-budget baselines and the acceptance criteria the readiness report will encode; (vi) answers to OQ-012 and OQ-025 (the parts not already supplied by ADR-0117 and ADR-0156).

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: the spawn/isolate/merge mechanics and topology of subagents (**WS-F3** — this dossier only decides *whether* to spawn and with what budget slice; the `delegate` shape is F3's); *which model* serves a call once a role and effort are chosen (**WS-C2** — the router's compatibility guard G-1…G-7 is never bypassed; ADR-0121 consequence "WS-F4 may call `select` but never bypasses the guard"); the critic contract, calibration and the standard `critic_experiment` (**WS-G3**, ADR-0115…0117 — this dossier consumes the four recorded inputs and owns the estimator, as ADR-0117 decision 7 stipulates); the hard-ceiling `check`/`reserve` and the exhaustion rule (**WS-F2** — the scheduler is a policy *over* `allocate/reserve`, never a bypass, ADR-0040 (e); it may replace reservation sizing per ADR-0107 decision 6); the accounting substrate and `MatchSpec` (**WS-L2**); ensembles as `EnsembleProcedure`s (**WS-C2/F3**, ADR-0123 — the scheduler decides *k* and *whether to add a member*, never the procedure's representation); branches and speculative effects (**WS-B4**, ADR-0133/0134 — "longer search" through a fork is a branch under `SpeculationPolicy`); experiment definition, scheduling and settlement (**WS-J3**, ADR-0154/0155 — the Lab-side allocator only reorders `OrderPlan` under a recorded permutation seed and chooses cells inside `adaptive_search`); estimators for reports (**WS-J4**); evolution gates and deployment (**WS-I5**); assumption-debt lifecycle for every conditioned scheduling rule (**WS-I6**); counterfactual attribution over trajectories (**WS-I7**); hosted participants (**WS-J6** — no native scheduling inside a hosted participant; only the ADR-0040 boundary budget). Every contract here is operations/inputs/outputs/invariants/failure modes (T-LCD-12); no language, runtime, library or framework is named (ADR-0050 §8); precedent file paths are evidence, never dependencies.

## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**F1 — Marginal value of compute is input-dependent and non-monotone, so a fixed allocation is wrong in both directions and the decision must be per-input, per-budget.** Snell et al. show the best allocation "critically varies depending on the difficulty of the prompt" (S-WS-F4-01); Chen, Zaharia & Zou prove Vote/Filter-Vote performance *falls* with more calls on hard-query mixes (S-453, Tier A); HAL finds higher reasoning effort reduced accuracy in most runs (S-135, Tier A); Anthropic's vendor doc says `max` effort "on most workloads adds significant cost for relatively small quality gains" and can "lead to overthinking" (S-WS-F4-12); Damani et al. and Brown-Muppidi-Shahout (`provisional`) both get their gains from *re-distributing* a fixed budget away from uniform allocation. **Implication:** the scheduler's baseline is "continue as configured" and every other option must show positive *estimated marginal* value against it; uniform or maximal allocation is a Lab arm, never the contract's default.

**F2 — The marginal value of "one more sample" is predictable from the samples already drawn, and only with a verifier does it stay positive at scale.** Coverage is log-linear in samples (S-WS-F4-02); Adaptive-Consistency stops when the sampled answers agree (S-WS-F4-05, accepted); Certaindex stops when probe answers stabilise (S-WS-F4-03; Dynasor `cot.py` L130–135 requires the last *threshold* probes to be equal, non-empty and hedge-free); without a verifier, majority voting and reward models "plateau beyond several hundred samples" (S-WS-F4-02). **Implication:** (i) a *stability signal* over already-produced outputs — agreement rate, answer-set entropy, validator pass streak — is a legitimate, cheap estimator input; (ii) the estimate for `extend_search` must be conditioned on the **oracle class** available to select among samples (ADR-0047): with a deterministic validator the marginal value follows coverage, with a judge or majority reducer it saturates, with nothing it is `unknown`; (iii) Dynasor's probes are themselves model calls issued every 32–64 tokens, so the estimator's own cost must be charged and capped (§6.3).

**F3 — Spawning is the most expensive option and is budgeted today by prose and static caps, never by an estimate.** Anthropic's orchestrator receives effort-scaling rules as instructions and spends ≈15× tokens (S-036); Codex caps concurrent sub-agents with a capacity gauge (`AgentExecutionLimiter`, `execution.rs` L81–83), gives multi-agent mode its own static effort (`models.json` L23) and starts children with an empty usage record (`spawn.rs` L104); the Claude SDK fixes a subagent's `model`/`effort`/`maxTurns` in its definition (`types.py` L87–104); RAH reports a gain from parallel spawning with no cost row (S-064). The delegation-fanout race is the documented failure when this is unbudgeted (S-209 via ADR-0040). **Implication:** `spawn_subagent` is admissible only when a slice of at least a declared child floor can be *reserved* from `remaining` (ADR-0040 decisions 2–3) and `fan_out` is below its cap; its value estimate is conditioned on declared parallelizability of the sub-task (an input the strategy or Procedure supplies, never inferred from prose) and on the parent's remaining context occupancy — the S-036 rules become typed thresholds with debt records.

**F4 — Effort and model role are cheap, reversible levers with cache side effects, and they are profile-conditioned.** Effort is a closed per-model ladder in every audited system (`models.json`; `types.py` L37), documented as "a behavioral signal, not a strict token budget" that also changes tool-call behaviour; changing it mid-conversation at the top level invalidates the prompt cache while per-message effort preserves it (S-WS-F4-12); "upgrading to [the next model]" beat doubling the token budget in one production system (S-036), while most routers fail to beat a simple baseline in a unified benchmark (S-449, `provisional`). **Implication:** `effort_level` is an option whose *availability* and *cache consequence* come from the Model Profile's capability declaration (ADR-0020/0124; OQ-WS-F4-03 asks WS-C3 to confirm the rule kind) and whose estimate carries a cache-invalidation cost term (ADR-0128); `alternate_model{role}` is an option that only *requests* a role/quality target from the router — the router's guard decides admissibility (ADR-0121 G-1…G-7) and a cross-profile change is a `relower` with `routing.deviation = true` (ADR-0122).

**F5 — Evaluator placement is a cost/benefit decision whose four inputs the program already records.** ADR-0117 decision 7 fixes the rule `P(caught) × cost(uncaught) − cost(critic) − P(false stop) × cost(rework) > 0` and names its inputs: `missed_failure_rate` and `false_stop_rate` from the `CalibrationRecord`, `harness_overhead.verification` from the ledger, `cost(uncaught)` from the affected effects' `reversibility` (ADR-0031). S-046's "unnecessary overhead inside the boundary, real lift at the edge" is the non-stationarity of this rule. **Implication:** the `evaluator` option's estimate *is* that rule evaluated over recorded quantities; the scheduler adds only the online part — the running estimate of `P(failure class X at this decision point)` for this profile — and the expiry rule (a placement whose realised Δ falls below margin is a `retirement` candidate, ADR-0156).

**F6 — Online estimation must forget, and the trigger for forgetting is already a ledger fact.** litellm's bandit hard-caps `α + β` and drops updates ("drift is a v1 concern", README) — the failure mode of a stationary posterior under model churn; Garivier & Moulines give the standard cure (discounted or sliding-window statistics with near-optimal regret under piecewise-stationary rewards, S-WS-F4-08); the Lab already emits `provider_drift = observed` from the fingerprint probe (ADR-0120/0155) and supersedes profiles by registry event (ADR-0126), and J4's `FittedSurfaceReport` carries an expiry condition (ADR-0160). **Implication:** every online prior is keyed by `(profile version_id, snapshot fingerprint, task_class, option)`, decays by a declared window or discount, and is *reset* (not merely decayed) on drift or supersession; a prior with fewer than a declared `min_n` observations is `unknown` and the scheduler falls back to the heuristic rule — never interpolates (ADR-0012 decision 7).

**F7 — The scheduler must not be a second control strategy, a second envelope, or a new owner of β.** ADR-0103 fixes three parties (strategy proposes, envelope checks, driver executes) and closed sums for `ControlDecision` and `DecisionPoint`; ADR-0106 decision 2 fixes that the envelope "never chooses the next action"; I5 reserves `stop{budget_exhausted}` for code. Every allocation lever the scheduler pulls is *already* a field of a ratified decision or record: `delegate.budget_slice`, `propose`'s model call parameters (effort, role) that the strategy may leave unbound, `verify.validator_refs` (critic placement), `retry.attempt`, an `EnsembleProcedure.members[].count`, a branch's `SpeculationPolicy.budget_fraction`. **Implication:** the scheduler is a **binding step** in the driver loop — after `decide` and before `envelope.check` — that may only *bind parameters the strategy left unbound* or *tighten* bound ones (a smaller slice, a lower effort, `k−1`), never override a bound value, never emit a decision kind, and never grant; for model-owned points it may additionally render a typed *compute advice* `ContextItem` through a `HarnessRule` whose delivery/activation/following is measured (T-LCD-13). This is a one-line amendment to ADR-0103's driver loop (CF-WS-F4-02) and no change to the envelope.

**F8 — Scheduling decisions are unattributable unless recorded with the options considered, and offline scoring of them is invalid.** No audited system records *why* it did not spawn, did not escalate, or stopped sampling; litellm records only the served model; Codex only a counter. The Replay Gap (S-454, `provisional (mech)`) shows replay-scored routing rankings have ≈ 0 similarity to live outcomes because a different allocation rewrites the trajectory; Harness-Bench shows model-conditioned, non-monotone harness effects (S-062). **Implication:** every `assess` appends `control.compute.decided{options_considered[], chosen, estimates, estimator_ref, inputs_read, priors_used}` linked to the `decision_id` it bound; the Lab evaluates scheduler variants only by *live*, paired, matched-budget experiments (ADR-0122 already refuses `replayed_trajectory`), and the after-the-fact value of a decision is a **derived view** joining the record to the run's outcome and consumption, never a stored score.

**F9 — On the Lab side, evaluation budget has value-of-information and the ratified machinery already admits it, search-only.** Task-CoEvolve and HarnessLens (both `provisional`) spend evaluation on discriminating tasks; J3 ratified `ValidationStrategy ∈ {full_set, disagreement_weighted, successive_halving}` inside `adaptive_search` over `search/dev` splits, rows labelled `estimated` (ADR-0156 decision 4); phase-3 §6.1 lets WS-F4 re-order `OrderPlan` only under a recorded permutation seed with the drift bracket intact; the estimator's rank correlation with full-set scores is 0.13 at 7 % sampling (S-082 via I2) — cheap estimates are noisy. **Implication:** the Lab-side allocator is a *strategy variant* of the ratified `ValidationStrategy`, not a new engine: it chooses which `(candidate, task)` cells to run next by estimated information gain within the experiment's instrument/search nodes, it never touches a `full_set` confirmatory arm, and its output rows are `estimated` with inclusion probabilities (§6.6).

**F10 — What the scheduler may read is bounded by provenance.** A model's self-predicted "I can do better" (S-WS-F4-06) is an `Observation{source: model_claim}` with `authority = delegate` and evidence class `claimed` (ADR-0112/0115); lifted MCP `ModelPreferences` are `authority ≤ external` (ADR-0121 G-6); Dynasor's hedge-word list reads free text. ADR-0106 decision 3 forbids envelope guards from reading `Text`; ADR-0016 forbids a `model_claim` from satisfying a Validator alone. **Implication:** estimator inputs are typed ledger projections (counters, gauges, validator verdicts, agreement over canonical output hashes, calibration records, priors with provenance); a `claimed` self-estimate may enter only as a *labelled* feature of a C4 predictor, never as the sole basis for `stop_now` or for a widening of spend, and never through a regex over prose.

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

**Against having a scheduler at all (R1: "adaptive allocation does not reliably beat simple baselines").** S-449 (`provisional`) finds commercial and academic routers "fail to reliably outperform a simple baseline"; S-083 (`provisional (counter)`) finds harness evolution does not consistently beat matched test-time scaling; S-453 (Tier A) shows more calls can hurt; litellm's own README calls its delta mapping "a v0 guess". *Why it does not overturn:* the contract does not assume the scheduler helps. The default binding is `static` (the strategy's configured parameters; no estimator runs), the heuristic is a *variant* compared as a component-level factor under `MatchSpec`, and every learned variant is C4-gated by ADR-0046 §7 at matched total. The disconfirming evidence is the reason the evaluation protocol (§6.7) requires the scheduler to beat `static`, `uniform` and `oracle`-bounded baselines before any default changes (ADR-0105's rule: the boundary moves only by `ComparisonReport`).

**Against the estimator's own cost (R2).** Dynasor probes every 32–64 tokens with a real completion; Damani's predictor must be trained; a bandit needs `min_n` observations per cell before it is better than the prior; Brown-Muppidi-Shahout's predictor runs before every generation. An estimator can cost more than it saves — the "unnecessary overhead" of S-046 turned on the scheduler itself. *Why it does not overturn:* estimator calls are ledgered model calls charged `subject` under `harness_overhead.scheduling` (ADR-0039/0043) with a per-run `budget_share_cap` (ADR-0117's pattern), the heuristic baseline makes **no model calls**, and `harness_overhead.scheduling` is a primary metric of the recipe; a variant whose overhead exceeds its Δ fails its own removal test (T-LCD-05).

**Against online priors under model churn (R3).** Priors learned on one snapshot are assumption debt against the next (S-047/S-049 dead-weight scaffolding; ADR-0121's `model_version_change` expiry); a discount factor is one more constant nobody can justify; drift detection has its own false-positive rate (S-441 via ADR-0120). *Why it does not overturn:* priors carry `AssumptionDebtRecord`s with expiry on supersession/drift (T-LCD-05), the window/discount is MUST-data pre-registered per experiment (OQ-WS-F4-02), `min_n` gates cold cells to `unknown`, and a reset costs only regret, never correctness, because the heuristic fallback is always admissible.

**Against reading stability signals (R4).** Agreement among samples is not correctness — a model can be confidently and consistently wrong (S-453's hard-query regime), and a hedge-word probe is a free-text heuristic. *Why it does not overturn:* stability is admitted only as an estimator *input* ranked below validator verdicts, computed over canonical output hashes or verdicts (never `Text`), and `stop_now` from stability alone is never admissible where a deterministic validator exists (ADR-0117 decision 6 ordering applied to the scheduler); with no verifier the `extend_search` estimate is `unknown` and the heuristic defaults to the configured k.

**Against spawn-value estimation (R5).** Parallelizability is not observable from outside the task; a wrong spawn costs a slice *and* coordination risk (S-036's ≈15×; RAH's unaccounted cost); the Replay Gap says the child's trajectory cannot be predicted from the parent's log. *Why it does not overturn:* `spawn_subagent` is estimable only when the strategy or Procedure declares parallel steps (`step.mode = parallel`, ADR-0103 decision 6 / CF-219) or a `subagent_task` target (ADR-0085); otherwise the option is `infeasible{undeclared_parallelism}` and the model-owned `delegate` proposal is untouched. The estimate is a prior over the *declared* sub-task class, bounded by reservable budget.

**Against the scheduler as a fourth control party (R6).** Adding a binding step between `decide` and `envelope.check` complicates the seam ADR-0103 fixed, and a bind-only policy can still starve a strategy (binding `k = 1` everywhere). *Why it does not overturn:* the step is monotone (bind unbound, tighten bound, never widen, never change kind), pure over its inputs (AC-F4-11 determinism), removable by binding `static` (AC-F4-1 shows the run is byte-identical without it), and its every action is a ledger record the envelope sees before execution; a starving policy fails the recipe against `static`. The alternative — folding the estimator into each strategy variant — would put the same code into four variants and make the allocation policy unablatable (T-LCD-09).

**Against Lab-side VOI (R7).** Adaptive selection estimates rank, not effect (ρ = 0.13 at 7 %, S-082; S-237 via I2), and selection leaks into the estimate if it touches a confirmatory arm (J4 F8). *Why it does not overturn:* the allocator lives only inside `adaptive_search` over `search/dev` splits (ADR-0156 decision 4), rows are `estimated` with inclusion probabilities, and the frozen artifact's benefit is always a `full_set` held-out experiment — nothing here re-decides ADR-0046.

**Negative results.** (N1) No audited harness records a rejected compute option; the decision record is `novel`. (N2) No protocol carries a scheduling decision or a rationale; only MCP's three priorities lower and lift (declared loss). (N3) HAL's ≈$40k factorial (S-135) reports no allocation policy at all — the cost of adaptive variants at Stage 3 is unmeasured until the recipe runs. (N4) Every "subagents help" claim opened here (S-036, S-064) is self-reported with `search_budget`/matched status `unknown`.

## 6. Recommendation for the spec

### 6.1 Structural decisions

1. **The scheduler is a policy component, `class_id = compute_policy`, home = control plane (P3), cardinality `exactly-one` with the null variant `static` as the packaged default.** It receives `(ModelProfile, ResourceAccount)` (T-LCD-08), is registered under ADR-0023/0151 with a `VariantRecord`, and is bound in `AgentProcess.native.slots["compute_policy"]`. `static` binds nothing and runs no estimator, so a definition without a scheduler is byte-identical in the ledger to today's Stage-0…4 runs.
2. **It binds, tightens, advises — never proposes, refuses or grants.** In the driver loop (ADR-0103 decision 4) one step is inserted: `(state, d) ← decide; d′ ← compute_policy.bind(d, ctx); verdict ← envelope.check(d′)`. `bind` may set parameters the strategy left unbound (`delegate.budget_slice`, the call's `effort`/`role_hint`, `verify.validator_refs` from admissible placements, an ensemble's `members[].count`, a branch's `budget_fraction`, `retry.attempt` continuation) and may tighten bound ones; it never changes `d.kind`, `d.decision_point` or `d.owner` (β is untouched, I8/I5 hold), never binds a value the envelope would refuse (it projects `reserve` first), and never touches `authorize`. For model-owned points it may emit a `compute_advice` `ContextItem` through a `HarnessRule` (T-LCD-13). Requested as an amendment to ADR-0103 (CF-WS-F4-02).
3. **Options are a closed sum; every option carries an estimate or a typed infeasibility.** `ComputeOption = {continue_as_configured, spawn_subagent{spec_ref, slice}, extend_search{kind ∈ {additional_sample, additional_branch, retry_task, continue_generation}}, evaluator{placement_ref}, effort_level{level}, alternate_role{role, quality_target?}, stop_now{proposed_reason}}`. `stop_now` is an *advice* to the β-owner of `stop` or a binding of `stop_rule` parameters the strategy exposes; it is never an owner value (ADR-0103 I5/I6 unchanged).
4. **Estimates are typed, provenance-bearing records; the value unit is declared, never implied.** `MarginalValueEstimate{delta_p_success{point, interval, source ∈ {prior, online, rule, unknown}}, expected_cost: ResourceVector (reserve-sized), expected_latency_ms, risk: RiskEstimate{effect_classes ⊆ ADR-0031 risk classes, coordination ∈ {none, fan_out, merge}, starvation_p, cache_invalidation: bool}, oracle_conditionality ∈ oracle classes ∪ {none}, evidence_refs[]}`; the objective the policy maximises is `ΔP̂(success) × TaskValue.success_value − λ·cost − P̂(harm)·TaskValue.failure_cost`, where `TaskValue` is a declared field of the task contract (ADR-0109) defaulting to `{success_value: 1, failure_cost: from EffectAttributes.reversibility}` (OQ-WS-F4-01) and λ is the arm's declared cost weight (MUST-data).
5. **The heuristic baseline is the C3 default variant and makes no model calls.** Rules (each a `HarnessRule{kind: compute_rule}` with an `AssumptionDebtRecord`, MUST-data): (a) `extend_search{additional_sample}` while agreement over canonical output hashes/verdicts among samples so far < θ_agree and `remaining ≥ reserve(one more member)`; stop at agreement ≥ θ_agree or k = k_max (Adaptive-Consistency shape); with no reducer or validator the estimate is `unknown` and k stays configured; (b) `spawn_subagent` only when the plan declares `step.mode = parallel` or a `subagent_task` target, `fan_out < cap`, and `slice ≥ child_floor` is reservable; slice = `min(declared child budget, remaining ÷ (declared parallel steps + 1))`; (c) `evaluator` at a declared placement iff the ADR-0117 rule is positive on recorded inputs and the placement's calibration record is `active`; (d) `effort_level` by `ModelRole` (utility/compaction/subagent roles at the profile's declared low level; primary at the configured level; step up one level only on `retry{model_call}` after a `format_failure`/validator fail streak ≥ n and only if the profile declares per-message effort without cache invalidation, else never mid-conversation); (e) `alternate_role{quality_target}` only on a `verification_completed{fail}` streak with a `QualityPrior` whose `valid_until` holds and whose level is not `unknown`; (f) `stop_now` advised only when a deterministic validator has passed and the strategy's `stop_rule` admits submission. Thresholds (θ_agree, k_max, child_floor, streak n) are pre-registered per experiment; placeholders in §6.7 are review items, not constants.
6. **Learned variants are registry components with expiry.** `compute_estimator ∈ {rules (C3/Stage 4), bandit (C3/Stage 5; Beta/Gaussian cells per `(profile version_id, fingerprint, task_class, option)`, Thompson or UCB selection, sliding-window or discounted statistics, `min_n` cold-cell rule, drift reset), surface_prior (C3/Stage 5; read-only `QualityPrior`/`FittedSurfaceReport` evidence with `valid_until`, `unknown` refused), predictor (C4/Stage 6; a trained model over the typed feature set, deployed only through WS-I5 with a `SearchBudgetRecord` and ADR-0046 §7 gates)}`. Every non-`rules` variant is a conditioned rule with `expiry_condition ⊇ {profile superseded, provider_drift = observed, suite validity superseded, age > window}` (T-LCD-05); `predictor` inputs may include `claimed` features only as labelled columns (F10).
7. **Every decision is a ledger record; value-after-the-fact is a derived view.** `control.compute.decided` (§6.4) carries the options considered with their estimates, the chosen binding, the estimator variant/version, the prior cells and their `n`, the inputs read (as refs and a digest), and a typed `RationaleRecord`; `compute_decision_outcome` is a `project()` view joining each record to the run's outcome class, validator verdicts and consumption after the decision — never a stored score (ADR-0026/0042). The trace viewer (WS-K2) renders both.
8. **Lab-side value-of-information is a `ValidationStrategy` variant, search-only.** `voi_weighted{estimator ∈ {disagreement, expected_information_gain}, min_inclusion_fraction, lambda, min_replicates}` joins ADR-0156's closed set by dialect bump; it selects the next `(candidate, task, replicate)` cells inside `adaptive_search` from the experiment's search/instrument nodes, records inclusion probabilities, and may re-order `OrderPlan` only under the recorded `permutation_seed` with the drift bracket intact (phase-3 §6.1); confirmatory `full_set` arms are untouchable.
9. **Hosted participants are out of scope for native scheduling.** A hosted participant receives only the ADR-0040 boundary budget; scheduler metrics render `n/a{class}`; the Lab may still compare a hosted product's *configuration-level* effort/model coordinates when `SUPPORTED` (ADR-0164/0165) — as a factor, not through this component (T-LCD-06/-07/-15).
10. **No allocation is ever a widening.** `bind` never increases a slice above `remaining`, never loosens a ceiling (`amend` is not in its verb set — ADR-0040 decision 6), never converts `ask → allow` (ADR-0053), and an evolution-authored scheduling rule is a `HirDiff` with `budget_delta ≠ loosening` (ADR-0053 D-5).

### 6.2 Interface-contract sketch (language-neutral; operations · inputs/outputs · invariants · failure modes)

- `capabilities(variant) → ComputePolicyCapabilities{options_supported ⊆ ComputeOption kinds, decision_points ⊆ {propose, delegate, verify, retry, stop}, makes_model_calls: bool, estimator_ref, requires{task_value: bool, priors: bool}, deterministic: bool}` — declared record (ADR-0028 discipline; `n/a` when undeclared).
- `bind(decision: ControlDecision, ctx: ComputeContext) → BoundDecision{decision′, record: ComputeDecisionRecord} | Unchanged{record}` — pure over `ctx`. **Inputs** (`ComputeContext`): `decision`, `budget_view{remaining, reserved, fan_out, delegation_depth, context.occupancy}` (ADR-0040 views), `task_contract_ref` + `TaskValue`, `plan_cursor` (declared parallel steps, `subagent_task` targets), `samples_so_far[{output_hash, verdicts[]}]` (from the ledger), `verifier_available: oracle class ∪ {none}`, `validator_streaks{pass, fail, format_failure}` (envelope views), `profile.capabilities{effort_ladder, per_message_effort, reasoning_replay}`, `role_table`, `placements[{critic_ref, calibration_status, inputs: {missed_failure_rate, false_stop_rate, overhead}}]`, `priors[]` (cells with `n`, `valid_until`), `cost_model` estimates (ADR-0089 `estimate`), `health_view` (ADR-0121), `context_label`. **Invariants:** B-1 `decision′.kind = decision.kind ∧ decision_point, owner unchanged`; B-2 every bound parameter was unbound or is tightened (`slice′ ≤ slice`, `effort′ ≤ effort` unless the rule is a declared step-up on a recorded failure streak, `k′ ≤ k`); B-3 `reserve` is projected for `decision′` and `bind` returns `Unchanged` rather than a binding the envelope would refuse; B-4 every option in `capabilities.options_supported` appears in `record.options_considered[]` with an estimate or a typed infeasibility; B-5 no `Text` is read; no `model_claim` enters except as a labelled `predictor` feature; B-6 two calls with equal `ctx` yield equal `decision′` (AC-F4-11). **Failures (typed):** `PolicyInvalid{rule_id, reason}` (link-time: missing debt record, threshold outside the kernel range, option outside capabilities), `PriorUnknown{cell}` (not an error at run time — the rule falls back), `EstimatorBudgetExhausted` (estimator model calls hit `budget_share_cap` → `Unchanged` with `estimates = unknown`), `TaskValueMissing` (when `requires.task_value`).
- `advise(decision_point, ctx) → ContextItem | none` — renders `compute_advice{options, estimates}` through the Model Profile for model-owned points; delivery/activation/following are `context.artefact.delivered/activated` + `verification.artefact.followed{kind: compute_advice}` (T-LCD-13). Never affects `bind`.
- `observe(events[]) → ()` — pure fold updating online priors from durable ledger events only (validator verdicts, outcome class, consumption, `control.loop.detected`, `provider_drift`); credit is assigned to the `control.compute.decided` record whose `decision_id` causally precedes the outcome (`causes[]`, ADR-0027); idempotent on re-delivery; resets cells on `profile superseded`/`drift`.
- `estimate(option, ctx, priors) → MarginalValueEstimate` — the `compute_estimator` class operation; pure for `rules`/`bandit`/`surface_prior`; `predictor` may issue ≤ `budget_share_cap` model calls, each a ledgered `model_call` with `role = router_predictor`-class attribution `harness_overhead.scheduling`. Failure: `EstimatorUnavailable{reason}` → `unknown`.
- `explain(record_ref) → ComputeExplanation` — projection over `control.compute.decided` and `compute_decision_outcome` for K2/J4; no new state.
- Lab side: `voi_next(experiment_state, capacity, strategy) → [(cell_id, inclusion_p)]` — the `ValidationStrategy` operation J3 calls from `next` (ADR-0155 decision 2); pure over the experiment ledger; refuses outside `adaptive_search` (`AdaptiveOutsideSearch`, ADR-0156).

All operations are implementable out of process over the canonical encoding (T-LCD-12); `bind` is on the kernel side of the ADR-0050 D2 boundary (it sits inside the driver loop; `hot_path` class per OQ-075), `voi_next` on the lab side.

### 6.3 Estimator interface and the four variants

| variant | tier / stage | inputs | selection | forgetting | model calls | debt record |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rules` (heuristic baseline) | C3 / Stage 4 | §6.1 decision 5 thresholds; ledger views; calibration inputs | deterministic table | n/a | none | one per rule (T-LCD-05) |
| `bandit` | C3 / Stage 5 | reward = deterministic outcome/verdict per decision; cost from pricing table; cells keyed by `(profile version_id, fingerprint, task_class, option)` | Thompson or UCB over `(reward − λ·cost)` with an informative prior from registry declarations (litellm shape) | sliding window or discount (Garivier–Moulines); reset on drift/supersession; `min_n` → `unknown` | none | one per cell family; expiry as §6.1-6 |
| `surface_prior` | C3 / Stage 5 | `QualityPrior`/`FittedSurfaceReport.conditionality_region` (ADR-0160) as evidence refs | rule table over prior sign/interval | expiry of the report | none | reuses the report's record |
| `predictor` | C4 / Stage 6 | typed feature set incl. `trajectory_digest` (OQ-296), stability signals, labelled `claimed` self-estimates | learned; deployed via WS-I5 | retrain on expiry | ≤ `budget_share_cap`, charged `harness_overhead.scheduling` | mandatory; `SearchBudgetRecord` at deployment |

`RiskEstimate` is derived, never declared by the estimator: effect classes come from the option's affected capabilities (ADR-0031/0089), coordination from the topology (F3), starvation from `reservation_refusal_rate` (ADR-0107), cache invalidation from the profile's effort declaration.

### 6.4 Data-model sketch

- `ComputeDecisionRecord{record_id, decision_id (the bound `control.decision`), decision_point, options_considered: [{option, estimate: MarginalValueEstimate | infeasible{reason ∈ {gauge_at_cap, insufficient_budget, undeclared_parallelism, no_placement, calibration_inactive, capability_unmet, prior_unknown, policy_excluded}}}], chosen: option | continue_as_configured, binding: map<param, value> (what changed on the decision), estimator_ref{variant_ref, version_id}, rules_fired[], priors_used: [{cell, n, mean, interval, valid_until}], inputs_read[] + inputs_digest, rationale: RationaleRecord{objective_value_per_option, lambda, task_value_ref}, cost_of_estimation: ResourceVector}` — content-addressed; the payload of the ledger event.
- Ledger events (plane `control`, `durability = ledger`, min observability `ledger` — hosted lowering `none`): `control.compute.decided{…ComputeDecisionRecord…}` (new class, requested of WS-B1 — CF-WS-F4-03); `control.compute.prior_reset{cell_family, reason ∈ {drift, superseded, window}}`; the advice artifact uses the existing T-LCD-13 chain with `kind: compute_advice`. Manifest end-state gains `compute_policy_ref` and `compute_decisions: count` (CF-WS-F4-03).
- Views: `compute_decision_outcome(run) → [{record_id, option_chosen, realized: {outcome_class, verdicts_after, consumption_after: ResourceVector, latency_after}}]`; `scheduling_overhead(run) → ResourceVector` (= `cost_view.by_attribution[harness_overhead.scheduling]`, ADR-0043).
- `PriorCell{key: (profile_version_id, fingerprint, task_class, option), stats: {n, sum, sum_sq | alpha, beta}, window | discount, updated_at_seq, valid_until, debt_record_ref}` — a runtime-owned memory kind (ADR-0078 `MemoryStore`, `deriver = kernel`, rebuildable from `compute_decision_outcome` rows — a **materialized view**, not a derived memory).
- Metric declarations (registered by WS-I2; `applies_to_classes = {native}`, `n/a{class}` for hosted): `scheduling.overhead` (spend/tokens/latency under `harness_overhead.scheduling`), `scheduling.decision_count`, `scheduling.option_share` (per option), `scheduling.regret_vs_oracle` (Lab-computed from replicates: oracle allocation = the per-task best option in hindsight, an upper bound labelled as such — J4 `oracle_best_of_n` pattern), `scheduling.prior_coverage` (fraction of decisions with a non-`unknown` prior), `scheduling.advice_compliance` (T-LCD-13 chain on `compute_advice`), `cost_of_pass` and `task_success` at matched cap as the headline pair (ADR-0159).
- `TaskValue{success_value: decimal-string, failure_cost: decimal-string | from_reversibility, currency: unitless | money}` — a field of the task contract projection (ADR-0109), defaulted at `seal` (OQ-WS-F4-01).
- `ValidationStrategy` gains `voi_weighted{…}` (dialect bump of ADR-0156's closed set — CF-WS-F4-04).

### 6.5 Estimation under non-stationarity (the rules the bandit and predictor obey)

N-1 cells are keyed by profile `version_id` **and** the fingerprint of the served snapshot (ADR-0120), so a silent provider change opens a new cell; N-2 statistics use a declared `window` (count) or `discount ∈ (0, 1)` — pre-registered MUST-data (OQ-WS-F4-02); N-3 `provider_drift = observed` (ADR-0155 drift bracket) or profile supersession (ADR-0126) appends `control.compute.prior_reset` and marks the cell family `unknown` until `min_n` is reached; N-4 cross-cell borrowing (a prior for one task class informing another) is inadmissible unless the pooling is a declared, debt-recorded rule (ADR-0012 no-interpolation); N-5 rewards are deterministic ledger facts (`task_success` from held-out/instrument deterministic oracles at the Lab; validator verdicts and outcome class in-loop) — a judge verdict may be a reward only with an `active` `CalibrationRecord` and is labelled (ADR-0047/0117); N-6 the heuristic `rules` variant is always the fallback for any `unknown` estimate, so the worst case of a stale prior is the C3 baseline, never an unbounded spend.

### 6.6 Value-of-information for evaluation budget (Lab side)

`voi_weighted` implements S-082's mechanism inside ADR-0156's contract: weight `w_t = max(p̄_t(1 − p̄_t), ℓ_t) + λ/√n_t` over candidate-outcome variance on the `search`/`dev` split, sample cells with probability ∝ `w_t` subject to `min_inclusion_fraction`, record `inclusion_probabilities_ref`, estimate with the Hájek estimator ADR-0159 already admits, and add an `expected_information_gain` option that prefers cells whose outcome would most change the *ranking* of surviving candidates (successive-halving rungs from ADR-0156 as the promotion rule, S-504). Invariants: V-1 never touches a `full_set` arm or a held-out split; V-2 rows are `estimated` and never the sole basis of acceptance (ADR-0046 decision 6); V-3 the allocator spends only from the experiment's `search`/`instrument` nodes (`charged_to = instrument`); V-4 `OrderPlan` re-ordering is a recorded permutation under the experiment's `permutation_seed` with the drift bracket intact; V-5 the allocator's own estimation is `harness_overhead.scheduling` on the experiment run. This answers the Lab half of OQ-025; the *cross-component* half ("which component deserves the next unit of evaluation compute") is answered only to the extent that candidates are slots of one `adaptive_search` design — allocation across *designs* stays with WS-I5 (OQ-WS-F4-05).

### 6.7 Evaluation protocol — `lab/value-of-compute-v1` (proves a scheduler beats matched-budget baselines)

A pre-registered `ExperimentSpec` (ADR-0154; `kind: comparative`; `design.kind: full_factorial`) shipped as a Lab recipe, executed at Stage 4–5:
- **Factors.** `compute_policy ∈ {static (no scheduler), uniform (every option at its configured maximum: k_max samples, always-spawn where declared, evaluator always on), rules, bandit, [predictor at C4]}` (component-level; T-LCD-09) × `model_snapshot` (≥ 2 families, roles fixed) × `environment` (a coding suite and a terminal suite, ADR-0144) × `budget` (two `eval_budget` levels, tight and wide — S-072's interaction is expected) × `replicate` (≥ 5; `seed_honoured` declared). The definition supplies a `Goal`, a `Procedure` with ≥ 1 declared `parallel` step, one calibrated critic placement and an `EnsembleProcedure{sample_k_vote}` so every option is reachable.
- **Budget matching.** Primary contrast under `MatchSpec{mode: matched_cap, dimensions: {model_calls, tokens.output.visible, time.wall_ms, spend}, tolerance 0.10, same_snapshot, cache_policy: cold_start}` with `budget_utilization` reported (an arm that under-spends is not "cheaper", ADR-0041 M1); a separate `iso_cost` arm produces the frontier (ADR-0159) with `cost_of_pass`; `bandit` carries its warm-up as `search_budget` and `predictor` its training as `search_budget`, so any arm with `search_budget > 0` is compared **only** under `matched_total` (ADR-0041 M3; ADR-0046 §7) — the matched-budget conditionality the brief requires, stated in ADR-3.
- **Baselines the scheduler must beat or match.** `static` (the null: is the scheduler worth anything?), `uniform` (the "more is better" arm: S-453 predicts it can lose), and an `oracle_allocation` row synthesized by J4 from the `uniform` arm's replicates (per task, the cheapest option that succeeded — an upper bound labelled as such, ADR-0159 decision 5 pattern) to report `scheduling.regret_vs_oracle`.
- **Pre-registered hypotheses.** H0: no headline difference `rules` vs `static` at matched cap (the honest null, given S-449/S-083); H1: `rules` < `uniform` in spend at equal `task_success` (Adaptive-Consistency mechanism); H2 (S-072): the `rules` effect is larger at the tight budget than the wide one — a `contrast` interaction (T-LCD-09); H3: `bandit` ≥ `rules` only after `min_n` per cell and only under `matched_total`; H4: `scheduling.overhead` for `rules` = 0 model calls and for `bandit` ≤ 1 % of subject spend; H5 (S-036): `spawn_subagent` share correlates with declared parallel steps, never with prose.
- **Metrics.** headline `task_success` (deterministic oracles; per-task (c, n)); `cost_of_pass`; `scheduling.*` (§6.4); `reliability.runaway_rate`, `budget_exhausted` rate; `boundary_drift` (must be ∅ — the scheduler never moves β); `advice_compliance` for model-owned points; tails (P95 spend/latency).
- **Acceptance rule (for adopting a non-`static` default in the packaged definition).** A `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind: artifact_benefit, budget_match.status: matched, label: confirmatory}` with `task_success` Δ interval excluding 0 or a non-inferiority verdict (TOST, ADR-0158) on `task_success` together with a cost Δ interval below 0; no veto metric regresses; `transfer` on ≥ 1 held-out model family reported (ADR-0046 §7). Replay-scored evaluations are refused (`replayed_trajectory`, ADR-0122).
- **Refusals.** `MissingMatchSpec`, `UnmatchedBudget`, `IncommensurableMatch` (a `search_budget > 0` arm under `matched_cap`), `InadmissibleFactor` (hosted participant at component level).

### 6.8 Acceptance-criteria sketch

- **AC-F4-1 (null variant; Stage 4).** With `compute_policy = static`, a run's ledger is byte-identical (modulo ids) to the same definition without the slot; zero `control.compute.decided` events.
- **AC-F4-2 (bind-only; Stage 4).** For every `control.compute.decided`, the linked `control.decision` has unchanged `kind`, `decision_point`, `owner`; every bound parameter was previously unbound or is tightened; a fixture policy that attempts to change a kind or widen a slice fails `PolicyInvalid` at `link`.
- **AC-F4-3 (never bypass the envelope; Stage 4).** A binding the envelope would refuse (`InsufficientBudget`, `SpawnRefused`) is never emitted: the policy returns `Unchanged` with the option marked `infeasible{insufficient_budget | gauge_at_cap}`; the envelope's own refusal count for scheduler-bound decisions is zero.
- **AC-F4-4 (options recorded; Stage 4).** Every option in the variant's `options_supported` appears in `options_considered[]` with an estimate or a typed infeasibility; `explain` renders the rationale; `compute_decision_outcome` joins every record to its realized outcome.
- **AC-F4-5 (heuristic makes no model calls; Stage 4).** Under `rules`, `harness_overhead.scheduling` shows zero `model_calls`; under `predictor`, estimator calls are ledgered, attributed, and capped by `budget_share_cap` (exceeding it yields `Unchanged`, never a stop).
- **AC-F4-6 (stability stop; Stage 4).** With a stub model that returns the same canonical answer from sample 1, `extend_search{additional_sample}` stops at `θ_agree`'s window; with alternating answers it runs to `k_max`; with no reducer/validator declared the estimate is `unknown` and k stays configured.
- **AC-F4-7 (spawn only when declared; Stage 4 with F3).** `spawn_subagent` is `infeasible{undeclared_parallelism}` for a plan without `parallel` steps or `subagent_task` targets; with them, the bound slice is ≤ `remaining ÷ (steps + 1)` and is released on child completion (ADR-0040 slice semantics).
- **AC-F4-8 (evaluator rule; Stage 4 with G3).** A placement with `calibration_status ≠ active` is `infeasible{calibration_inactive}`; with an active record the ADR-0117 rule is evaluated over the recorded four inputs and the outcome appears in the rationale.
- **AC-F4-9 (priors expire; Stage 5).** After a `provider_drift = observed` bracket or profile supersession, `control.compute.prior_reset` is appended and the next decision's `priors_used` show `n = 0`/`unknown` for the affected cells with the `rules` fallback fired (T-LCD-05).
- **AC-F4-10 (no widening; Stage 4).** A `HirDiff` authored by an evolution process that raises a `child_floor`, `k_max` or `budget_share_cap` beyond the sealed value classifies `budget_delta = loosening` and is rejected (ADR-0053 D-5); `amend` is absent from the policy's verb set.
- **AC-F4-11 (determinism; Stage 4).** `bind` on identical `(decision, ctx)` yields identical `decision′` and `record` minus ids (supports `deterministic_replay` variants, ADR-0028).
- **AC-F4-12 (the recipe; Stage 4–5).** `lab/value-of-compute-v1` registers only with a `MatchSpec`; a `bandit`/`predictor` arm under `matched_cap` is refused `IncommensurableMatch`; the report contains the `static`/`uniform`/`oracle_allocation` rows and the budget × policy `contrast`; replay-scored input is refused.
- **AC-F4-13 (Lab VOI; Stage 5).** `voi_weighted` refuses outside `adaptive_search` (`AdaptiveOutsideSearch`); rows carry `inclusion_probabilities_ref`; a confirmatory `full_set` arm's `OrderPlan` is unchanged; the allocator's spend is on the instrument node.
- **AC-F4-14 (class scoping).** `scheduling.*` metrics render `n/a{class}` for hosted rows; no IR entity or Hosting ABI verb changes (T-LCD-06/-15).

## 10. Confidence

**Medium-high** for the contract shape (bind-only policy inside the ratified seam, closed option sum, ledgered decision record, heuristic baseline that makes no model calls, `static` null): every element composes only ratified objects, the seam insertion is monotone and removable, and the mechanisms rest on Tier-A evidence (S-453 non-monotonicity, S-WS-F4-05 adaptive stopping, S-WS-F4-08 non-stationary bandits) plus five source audits at pinned commits. **Medium** for the online estimator design and the reset rules: the failure modes are documented (litellm's own README; ADR-0120 drift) but no audited harness runs a forgetting bandit over a ledger, so the design is `novel as integrated`. **Medium** for the Lab-side VOI strategy: it is a variant of a ratified contract, but its motivating evidence (S-082, S-089) is `provisional` and the estimator is noisy at low sampling fractions. **Low** for any claim that a scheduler *improves* outcomes: the field's best 2026 evidence says most adaptive allocators do not beat simple baselines (S-449, S-083), which is why the null hypothesis of the recipe is "no difference", the packaged default is `static`, and no `provisional` number is load-bearing anywhere. Reflexively: this dossier assumes deterministic oracles exist for the Stage-3/4 suites; where they do not, `extend_search` estimates are `unknown` by construction and the scheduler is inert.
