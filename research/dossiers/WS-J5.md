## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides **how a run becomes a queryable, comparable, publishable scientific object without ever becoming a second source of truth**: (i) the **results row** — the `project(run, results_row, until_seq)` view ADR-0026 names and ADR-0038 keys, given here a complete data model (coordinates, outcome, metric cells, consumption, lineage fields, audit citations) and a **row-version** rule so re-scoring never mutates a row; (ii) the **results store** as a *projection layer* — row heads, the derived **annotation index** (ADR-0141 decision 1: `bundle_ref`/`claimed_level`/`depends_on_revoked`/retraction are annotations, never row bytes), the **bundle catalogue** (ADR-0141), the **cell table** J4 aggregates from, and the read contract (query API) with watermark semantics; (iii) the **experiment ledger** — the immutable record of what an experiment *declared*, *ran*, *excluded*, *analysed* and *published*, placed on the run ledger itself (an experiment is a run of kind `experiment`) so it inherits ADR-0026/0029/0067 append-only, chain, checkpoint and redaction semantics rather than acquiring a second store; (iv) **analysis records** — every scorecard/comparison/equivalence/fitted-surface report is an immutable, provenance-bearing record whose inputs are a watermark set, superseded never edited; (v) **leaderboard semantics** — a leaderboard is a *versioned definition* plus *snapshots* that are pure views over the results store; entries are class-aware, always linked to bundles, admitted only when a reproducible object exists ("no score without a reproducible object"), ranked with intervals and disclosure context, and annotated rather than hidden on retraction; (vi) **retention** for rows, annotations, analyses, snapshots and the experiment ledger (the WS-J5 half of OQ-085/OQ-112/OQ-173); (vii) the **query API** WS-J4 (analysis) and WS-K2 (viewer) and WS-K3 (MCP surface) consume, and the **export** of results as a lowering with a loss report (T-LCD-11).

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: the ledger envelope, projection purity and `derived_from` watermark (**WS-B1**, ADR-0026/0027/0029 — consumed verbatim); the identity kinds, name history, `StaleIndex`, sameness ladder and reproducibility levels (**WS-L4**, ADR-0036/0037/0038 — consumed verbatim); the bundle format, `validate_bundle`, `BundleStatusRecord`, publication policy and import/export of bundles (**WS-I3**, ADR-0139…0141 — consumed; this dossier supplies the catalogue and annotation maintenance those ADRs assign to J5); `MetricDeclaration`, factors, outcome classes, `n/a{reason}`, the aggregation ladder and `ComparisonReport` shape (**WS-I2**, ADR-0045/0046/0047 — consumed; J5 *stores*, never re-defines); `MatchSpec` and the refusal semantics (**WS-L2**, ADR-0041); which statistical estimators compute intervals, interaction effects, rank intervals and Pareto frontiers (**WS-J4** — J5 fixes what is stored and what an analysis record must cite, J4 fixes how it is estimated); the sweep engine that opens experiment runs, binds subject runs to arms and assembles arm/experiment bundles (**WS-J3** — J5 fixes the event classes it must append and the preconditions J5 checks); the registry service and name-history storage (**WS-J2**); trace/time-travel and dashboard rendering (**WS-K2**); the tamper-evidence protocol, auditor role and retention *floors* (**WS-H6**, ADR-0067/0068); lab-wide retention durations, consent and legal hold (**WS-L6**, OQ-173/OQ-116); benchmark contamination lists and suite validity records (**WS-I4**, ADR-0142…0144); the interchange format (**WS-J6**, OQ-030); every ecosystem choice (**ADR-0050**).

**Scope guard (ADR-0006 N4).** N4 says HarnessHarness is *not a public leaderboard service*. Everything here is the **instrument's own** results plane: rows and leaderboard views exist so the Lab's experiments are inspectable and comparable across both participant classes; *publication* is an `export` under ADR-0141's `PublicationPolicy`, and a hosted multi-tenant leaderboard product is out of scope (CF-342 records the wording reconciliation).

## 2. Key questions

1. (doc 3 §5, verbatim) Design the results store: every run as an inspectable object (bundle I3 + traces B1 + scorecard I2 + provenance L3/L4), experiment ledger (immutable record of experiments and their analyses), leaderboard semantics (class-aware; always linked to bundles; no score without a reproducible object), retention, query API for J4/K2, materialized views over the event store (B1 ADR on authoritative vs derived).
2. (brief) Deliver the results/ledger data model; write/read contracts and immutability rules; leaderboard rules; view derivation from the event store; acceptance criteria; ADR.
3. What exactly is in a results row, what is its identity, and how does re-scoring (regrade, validator supersession, pricing re-snapshot) produce a *new* row without mutating the old one, given ADR-0038's fixed key `(configuration_version_id, run_id)`? (§6.2, §6.3)
4. Where does the experiment ledger live — a new store, or the run ledger — and what must WS-J3 append so that "what was declared before results were known" is provable (pre-registration, arm declaration, exclusions, analyses)? (§6.4)
5. What is a leaderboard *as data*: how are entries admitted, ranked, stratified by participant class and observability, linked to bundles, and what happens on retraction, revocation, suite retirement, contamination flags or garbage collection? (§6.5)
6. Which leaderboard-distortion mechanisms documented in the field (private testing, selective disclosure, best-of-N without disclosure, silent removal, link rot, mutable rows) does the data model make structurally impossible or at least visible? (§4a F5–F8, §6.5)
7. What read contract do J4 (aggregation over cells), K2 (browse, drill-down to `audit_ref`), K3 (stateless catalogue) need, and how are reads repeatable under concurrent appends? (§6.6)
8. What retention rules hold for rows, annotations, analyses, snapshots and the experiment ledger — the J5 halves of OQ-085, OQ-112 and OQ-173? (§6.7)
9. (neighbour questions assigned to J5) OQ-128 veto tiering (with WS-I2); OQ-321 promoted-fork pair as one or two observations (with WS-B4); OQ-239 catalog `adopt` mid-run and row keys (with WS-L4/E3); OQ-261/OQ-272 outcome-class keying of `loop_detected`/`format_failure`/`failed_honestly`; OQ-331 J5 half (public readers and reader-verifiable level). ## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**F1 — Leaderboards derived from write-once run objects exist and scale; leaderboards kept as mutable tables drift from their evidence.** HELM writes one directory per `RunSpec` (`run_spec.json`, `scenario_state.json`, `stats.json`, `per_instance_stats.json`), skips a run whose five files exist, and builds every table by *summarizing* those directories through a declarative schema of metric groups and run groups (`summarize.py: read_run`, `Summarizer.schema`); the leaderboard is therefore a pure function of immutable runs plus a schema, and HELM can publish "all raw model prompts and completions" because the cells point at them (S-525/-04). Terminal-Bench 1's leaderboard is the opposite shape: a `run_metadata` row with an `accuracy` column, `commit_hash = "unknown"` by default, cascade-deleting child rows, read by a dashboard that re-declares the schema (S-490 `db.py`, `dashboard/data_access.py`). Harbor Hub's curated leaderboards are declared schemas (`metadata_schema`, `metrics_schema`, `columns[]`, `rank_by[]`) whose rows are *patchable* (`LeaderboardRowPatch{metadata, metrics, status}`), hideable (`status = hide`) and whose trial links can be *replaced* (`LeaderboardTrialIdsConfig` "replacing a row's trial associations") under optimistic concurrency (`expected_updated_at`) (S-132 `hub/leaderboards.py`). **Implication:** HarnessHarness leaderboards are HELM-shaped — a versioned *definition* (schema + admission + rank rules) plus *snapshots* that are `project()` views over rows — and never Harbor-Hub-shaped mutable rows. Harbor's declarative column/rank grammar is adopted as the definition's shape; its mutability is rejected (§4c).

**F2 — Every audited results plane keys aggregates by names, and every one has had to add an out-of-band identity later.** Harbor `JobStats.format_agent_evals_key` builds `agent__model__dataset` (S-132 `models/job/result.py` L60–66) and `ComparisonGrid` keys cells by `agent_models` labels; Terminal-Bench keys by `agent_name`/`model_name`; MLflow and W&B key by user-chosen run names with tags; Inspect had to introduce a *versioned identity function* (`task_identifier`, `TASK_IDENTIFIER_VERSION = 3`) computed identically "from a `ResolvedTask` (before running) and from the `EvalLog`" so that planned cells and produced logs pair across retries (S-136 `evalset.py` L2005–2075). ADR-0036/0038 already fix the answer (`configuration_id` semantic coordinate; `configuration_version_id` exact key; names only through name-history entries; "leaderboards never key by name alone"). **Implication (adds to ADR-0038):** the results store must expose *both* ids on every row and must expose the *name binding that resolved at the time* (`name_bindings[]` from the bundle) so a leaderboard can show a human name without ever aggregating by it (T-LCD-09/-10).

**F3 — "What was planned before results existed" is recordable as data, and the one plane that does it treats the manifest as a versioned wire format.** Inspect's capture mode resolves `tasks × models`, writes `EvalSetCapture{version, identifier_version, eval_set_id, options, overrides, tasks[]}` "and exits the process without running anything", and documents that "Schema changes require a version bump and corresponding golden-test updates"; `eval-set.json` records the set once runs exist (S-136 `eval_set_manifest.py`, `evalset.py` L2306–2345). Harbor's `JobLock` (WS-I3 F1) is the same idea at the lock level. Neither records *analyses* — which metrics were declared primary, which cells were excluded and why, which reports were computed from which inputs. ADR-0045 already makes `Design`/`PreRegistration` immutable once the first run opens and WS-B1 already has `measurement.experiment.bound` on the subject run. **Implication:** the experiment ledger is the *complement* of those two records — an append-only sequence of typed events (declared → bound → excluded → analysed → closed → bundled → published) — and the cheapest place to put it that inherits chain, checkpoint, redaction and projection semantics is the run ledger itself, as a run of kind `experiment` (§6.4; CF-343).

**F4 — Mature experiment trackers split records into immutable inputs, append-only observations and deletable containers; the deletable container is exactly what an audited instrument must not have.** MLflow refuses to change a parameter once logged ("Run params are immutable in MLflow tracking in order to ensure the reproducibility of an experiment run", issue #7152), lets metrics be logged repeatedly with `step`, models lineage with `tags.mlflow.parentRunId`, and lets runs be deleted (S-527); W&B exposes `Run.delete()` (S-528); Harbor Hub rows have `hide`; Terminal-Bench cascades deletes; Inspect edits logs in place with an `etag` guard (WS-I3 F7). ADR-0037 I1–I5 and ADR-0068 R1 ("envelopes never leave") settle the direction. **Implication:** the results store has *no delete and no update path*: rows are projections (rebuildable), row versions supersede (ADR-0037 S1), annotations are a derived index, analyses are versioned records, snapshots are retained; the only removal is WS-B1/H6 redaction/GC of *bytes*, which rows survive because they cite `audit_ref`s and content addresses, not bytes.

**F5 — The distortion mechanisms of live leaderboards are all failures of *disclosure*, and each maps to a record the results plane can require.** *The Leaderboard Illusion* (S-523, preprint, mechanism only) documents private testing of many variants with retraction of the losers, unequal sampling, and silent deprecation; SWE-bench's checklist requires "all rollouts + the mechanism for selecting which solution was used" for best@k and trajectories "generated with the inference process, not post-hoc" (S-488); Terminal-Bench 2.1 requires ≥ 5 trials per task and a Hub job id in a public PR (S-489); the Open LLM Leaderboard keeps "Flagged" models listed with a link to the discussion (S-529); HELM annotates contaminated cells with a level and a description rather than removing them (S-526 `contamination.py`). Mapping: private testing → *registered but restricted* bundles that still count in `n_arms_registered`; selective retraction → `retracted` is a status annotation with reason, never a deletion; best-of-N → a `selection{n_candidates, rule, search_budget}` field on the entry (the leaderboard form of T-LCD-14); silent deprecation → snapshots are retained and diffable; contamination → `SuiteValidityRecord`/contamination annotations on cells (ADR-0142/0143). **Implication:** every leaderboard snapshot carries a **disclosure record** (§6.5) computed from the experiment ledger, and an entry whose experiment ledger cannot supply one is `undisclosed`, shown as such.

**F6 — A score is only as durable as the object it links to; a link to a reproducible object is the only thing that survives platform, model and benchmark churn.** Papers with Code's 11,745 leaderboards went static when the service ended; the archive preserves numbers whose provenance is a paper link (S-530). Model providers silently replace snapshots (S-047/S-049 via ADR-0038) so "GPT-x scored 61 %" is not re-derivable without the run. HELM and the Open LLM Leaderboard publish raw completions/details next to scores; HAL publishes traces (encrypted); SWE-bench requires logs and trajectories; BetterBench finds most benchmarks neither report significance nor make results replicable (S-531). doc 2 §8 states the design goal verbatim: the bundle "turns a leaderboard row into an inspectable scientific object". **Implication (the load-bearing rule of ADR-0162):** a leaderboard *entry* is admitted only when every contributing results row is annotated with a bundle whose `BundleStatusRecord.status ≥ validated` and whose `claimed_level ≥` the definition's minimum (R0 by default), and the entry renders the level *as verifiable by the reader* (OQ-331 J5 half). Rows without a bundle remain queryable in the catalogue as `unpublishable`, never scored on a leaderboard.

**F7 — Analyses are computations with inputs and versions, and the field's own reproducibility guidance is to record exactly those.** lm-eval's authors recommend sharing exact prompts/code, reporting versions and significance (S-524); HELM writes `runs.json`/`run_specs.json`/`costs.json` beside tables; Inspect's `HeadlineMetric` is *declared* and resolved against results, with a stale declaration warning rather than silently narrowing (S-136 `_headline.py`); ADR-0045 AC-I2-12 already requires `ScorecardReport{generated_from, metric_registry_version, price_table_version}` and report-hash equality on regeneration. **Implication:** an `AnalysisRecord` (§6.4) cites its procedure `version_id`, its input watermark set (row `version_id`s or a query + watermark set), registry/pricing versions, and whether it was *pre-registered* (named in `PreRegistration.primary_metrics`/`analysis_plan_ref`) or *exploratory* — the record-level answer to the garden-of-forking-paths problem the Leaderboard Illusion documents at the leaderboard level.

**F8 — Retries, relaunches and regrades create *multiple observations per planned cell*, and every plane picks "latest wins" by convention rather than by record.** Harbor Hub relaunch "targets the latest terminal attempt per trial" and cancel touches "only the latest attempt" (S-132 `hub/client.py`); `TrialSummary` carries `retry_index, retry_count, attempt, n_attempts`; Inspect's retry logic keeps the latest log per `task_id` (S-136 `_retry.py`); Harbor regrade creates a new trial with `source_trial{action: regrade}` (WS-I3 F3). ADR-0107 gives retries shared identity with a recorded `attempt_delta`; ADR-0131 chains goal activations by `continued_from`; ADR-0133 keys branch rows by run with `branch_kind`, `fork_point`, `replay_mode`. **Implication:** the results store never infers "latest wins": a run is one row; a cell's *observation set* is the set of row heads bound to it minus rows explicitly `excluded` by an experiment-ledger event with a reason (`infrastructure_retry`, `superseded_by_fork`, `duplicate_attempt`, `pre_registered_exclusion`); exclusions are visible in the cell table and counted in the disclosure record (§6.4, §6.8 OQ-321).

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

**Against yet another projection layer (rows, catalogue, cell table, annotation index, snapshots).** Kiehl (S-195) warns that each projection "doubles the amount of code that touches your event stream" and that "a history table gets you 80 % of the value"; Terminal-Bench and Harbor Hub ship credible leaderboards from plain tables. *Why it does not overturn:* (a) the row projection is *already* mandated by ADR-0026 (`results_row` at C0/Stage 3) and the catalogue and annotation index by ADR-0141 — this dossier adds one derived table (cells) and one view family (snapshots), all rebuildable by the same `project` discipline and covered by one rebuild-equality test (AC-J5-1); (b) the instrument's purpose is attribution and audit: a mutable `accuracy` column cannot cite an `audit_ref`, cannot render `n/a{reason}`, and cannot survive a regrade without losing history; (c) the cost is bounded by keeping the row *small* (cells and distributions are content-addressed members referenced by the row, not inlined) and by header-first catalogue reads (Inspect `logs.json` shape). The concession: a plain "history table" of rows *is* what Stage 3 ships; snapshots and the experiment ledger's analysis events are Stage 4.

**Against "no score without a reproducible object" as an admission rule.** It excludes results that exist only as foreign numbers (a benchmark's own published table), results whose payload was GC'd, and hosted runs whose adapters capture little; it raises the bar for exploratory work; Papers with Code, Open LLM Leaderboard v1 and most academic tables never required it. *Why it does not overturn:* the rule is on *leaderboard entries*, not on rows — every row stays queryable and the catalogue lists `unpublishable` rows with the reason; foreign numbers enter as hosted R0 bundles via `import` (ADR-0141), so a foreign table row can be a leaderboard entry at R0 with `unverified` authority visible; GC'd payload caps the *reader-verifiable* level, it does not remove the entry (ADR-0068 R4 + `level_now` annotation); minimum level and status are per-definition MUST-data (R0/`validated` default). What is conceded: a leaderboard cannot mix "we ran it" with "they say" without showing the difference — which is the point.

**Against putting the experiment ledger on the run ledger (as a run of kind `experiment`).** A run ledger is optimized for one writer, per-run `seq`, tool/effect scopes; an experiment spans many runs, may live for weeks, and its "events" are analyst actions; MLflow/W&B model experiments as containers, not logs; Inspect writes a manifest file. Overloading `run` risks confusing subject-run metrics with experiment bookkeeping. *Why it does not overturn:* ADR-0131 already made long-lived, multi-activation runs a supported shape (`continued_from`; suspension/wakeups), ADR-0039 already charges instrument runs (`charged_to = instrument`), and phase-2 §6.2 forbids a second store per seam ("added projections, never stores"); a separate append-only store would have to re-implement chain, checkpoints, redaction, fencing and export. The manifest's `run_kind` discriminator keeps experiment runs out of every subject aggregate (they have no `configuration_id` and no metric cells), and their event classes are a closed `measurement.experiment.*`/`measurement.analysis.*`/`measurement.leaderboard.*` family. The additive manifest field is flagged for synthesis (CF-343) rather than assumed.

**Against snapshot-based leaderboards (vs a live table).** Users expect a live board; snapshots lag; HELM's static site must be regenerated. *Why it does not overturn:* a snapshot is a *view* and can be re-projected on every append (a live board is simply "the latest snapshot at the current watermark"); what the snapshot adds is a recorded `results_watermarks[]`, a `view_hash`, a disclosure record and diffability — exactly what the Leaderboard Illusion shows a live board lacks. Retained snapshots are small (entries reference row `version_id`s).

**Against disclosure records and pre-registration as leaderboard requirements.** Research is exploratory; forcing "register every arm before results" adds friction and may be gamed by registering everything; the Leaderboard Illusion evidence is a 2025 preprint about a human-preference arena, not a harness lab. *Why it does not overturn:* the disclosure record is *descriptive* — it counts what the experiment ledger already contains (arms declared, runs bound, exclusions, restricted bundles) and labels analyses `pre_registered | exploratory`; nothing is refused except an entry whose experiment cannot be found at all (`undisclosed`); the preprint supplies only the failure signature, and the rule re-derives from T-LCD-14 (search-time vs artifact benefit) and SWE-bench's checklist, both already ratified/Tier-B.

**Against immutable analysis records.** Analysts iterate; every re-run of a report becoming a new versioned record inflates the store; Inspect edits in place with an etag for a reason. *Why it does not overturn:* analysis records reference reports by content address (dedup), and an unchanged regeneration yields the *same* `version_id` (purity, AC-I2-12) — only genuinely different analyses create new records; the supersession edge makes "which report did the paper cite" answerable, which in-place edits destroy (WS-I3 F7).

**Negative results from the audit.** No audited plane: keys aggregates by a content-derived configuration identity (Inspect's `task_identifier` is the closest and is a *task* identity); records exclusions or retries as first-class events with reasons (all use latest-wins); records which analyses were pre-registered; stores a disclosure record beside a leaderboard; links every leaderboard entry to a validated reproducible bundle (SWE-bench's `checked` and Terminal-Bench's PR review are human processes); renders a reader-verifiable reproducibility level. These are the `novel`-as-integration parts of §6, each with the partial precedent named in §4c.

## 6. Recommendation for the spec

### 6.1 Position

1. **The results store is a projection layer, never a store of record.** Its authoritative inputs are run ledgers + blobs + manifests (ADR-0026), bundle manifests and sidecars (ADR-0139), the registry's name histories and `StaleIndex` (ADR-0037), the metric registry and pricing snapshots (ADR-0045/0043). Everything it holds — row heads, the annotation index, the cell table, the catalogue, leaderboard snapshots — is `project()`-pure over those inputs at a recorded **watermark set**, has a `view_hash`, and is rebuildable to equal bytes (AC-J5-1). It has no update or delete operation.
2. **One row per run; row versions supersede; keys never change.** `ResultsRow/1` is keyed `(configuration_version_id, run_id)` (ADR-0038); its `version_id` is over its canonical bytes; re-scoring (regrade, validator supersession, pricing re-snapshot, metric-registry bump) yields a *new* row version with `supersedes` + `derived-from{reason}` (ADR-0037 S1); the **row head** per key is the newest non-revoked version; aggregation uses heads unless an analysis pins versions (CF-344).
3. **The experiment ledger is a run ledger.** An experiment is a run with `run_kind = experiment` (additive manifest field, CF-343) written by the sweep engine (WS-J3) as its single fenced writer; its durable classes are the closed families `measurement.experiment.*`, `measurement.analysis.*`, `measurement.leaderboard.*` (§6.4); it has no `configuration_id`, no metric cells and no environment, is charged `instrument`, and is exported/attested like any run (ADR-0067; ADR-0139 `experiment` bundle kind).
4. **Analyses are immutable records with cited inputs.** `AnalysisRecord` cites procedure `version_id`, input watermark set, registry/pricing versions and `pre_registered: bool`; supersession only.
5. **A leaderboard is a definition plus snapshots.** `LeaderboardDefinition` is a versioned, name-published record (MUST-data: admission policy, columns, rank rule, strata); `LeaderboardSnapshot` is a view; entries are per configuration, class-stratified, always linked to bundles, admitted only with a validated reproducible object, ranked with intervals, carrying a disclosure record, and annotated — never hidden — on retraction, revocation, suite retirement, contamination, discontinuity or GC.
6. **Retention is by membership, and rows/annotations/snapshots/experiment envelopes sit at the audit floor.** (§6.7.)

### 6.2 Data-model sketch — the results row

`ResultsRow/1` = `project(run, results_row, until_seq, scoring: ScoringContext)`; canonical record under `idp`; `version_id = H(idp ∥ "results_row" ∥ canonical(row))`.

```
ResultsRow{
  schema: "hh-results-row/1", idp, dialect (HIR dialect of the run),
  key: {configuration_version_id, run_id},
  coordinates: {                                   -- the T-LCD-09 factor tuple, never a name
    configuration_id,
    model_snapshots[role]: {model_ref, snapshot_id?, served_model?, pinned, fingerprint_before?, fingerprint_after?}   (ADR-0038; model_set_realized from the manifest)
    harness: {granularity ∈ {component, configuration, product}, semantic_id, version_id, varied_slots[]?}
    profile: {semantic_id, version_id, status_at_run}                                                              (ADR-0020/0126)
    environment: {semantic_id, version_id, image: ContentAddress | foreign_digest, fault_profile?, perturbation_profile?}  (ADR-0137)
    task: {suite_id, task_id, task_version_id, split_label, split_hash, family}                                       (ADR-0142/0143)
    budget: {semantic_id, version_id, eval_budget_ref, search_budget_ref?, match_spec_ref, cache_policy}              (ADR-0041/0046)
    replicate: {index, seed_material_ref, seed_honoured: bool | unknown}                                              (ADR-0045 CF-095)
    participant_class, observability_level, hosting_mechanism?, capability_vector_ref?                                (ADR-0013)
  },
  experiment?: {experiment_run_id, arm_id, cell_id, design_ref, pre_registration_ref},                                (from measurement.experiment.bound)
  outcome: {outcome_class, stop_reason: StopReason, status, veto_tripped[]: metric_ref, unresolved_effects: n, finished_at, wall_ms, activation_no?, continued_from?},   (ADR-0045/0106/0131)
  cells[]: {metric_ref: version_id, value: bool | decimal-string | verdict{C,I,P,N} | vector | n/a{reason}, detector ∈ {deterministic, judged}, oracle_ref?, judge_snapshot?, confidence?, evidence: audit_ref[], computed_from: audit_ref[] | view_ref},   (ADR-0045/0047; one cell per registry metric — absence is n/a{no_detector}, never omission)
  consumption: {per dimension: {consumed, hard?, utilization?, p95_running?}, spend: {micro_units, currency, provenance: CostProvenance, confidence, coverage, pricing_ref}, instrument_spend: {...}},   (ADR-0039/0043; charged_to split)
  cache: {policy, served_from_cache: n, warmth_counts{warm, cold, unknown}},                                          (ADR-0128/0129)
  lineage: {branch_kind?, fork_point?, replay_mode?, parent_run_id?, forked_from?, superseded_by_run?, goal_ref?},    (ADR-0133/0135/0131; OQ-321)
  annotations_from_ledger: {discontinuities[]: audit_ref, routing_deviation?, catalog_epochs[], suite_validity_ref, world_state_continuity},   (ADR-0136/0121/0095/0144)
  audit: {head: audit_ref (at until_seq), audit_completeness: AuditCompleteness, checkpoint_ref?},                     (ADR-0067/0068)
  scoring: ScoringContext{validator_set: version_id[], overlay_runs[]: run_id (regrade/instrument runs whose verdicts target this run), metric_registry_version, pricing_ref, view_policy_version},
  derived_from: {run_id, seq, overlay_watermarks[]: (run_id, seq)}, view_hash
}
```

Rules. **R-ROW-1 (purity):** `project` reads only the run's durable prefix ≤ `until_seq`, the overlay runs' prefixes ≤ their watermarks, and the pinned registry/pricing records named in `scoring`; two rebuilds yield equal `version_id`. **R-ROW-2 (no names):** no field is a display name; `name_bindings` live in the bundle and the catalogue (T-LCD-10). **R-ROW-3 (every metric, every row):** the cell list is total over the registry version named in `scoring` (T-LCD-15; `n/a{class | observability | estimator_undefined | not_run | no_detector}`). **R-ROW-4 (no derived totals as primaries):** consumption values are the ledger's running totals or `MeteringFormula` outputs (ADR-0039); spend carries provenance. **R-ROW-5 (hosted rows are the same schema):** a hosted run fills the subset its observability supports and renders the rest `n/a` (T-LCD-06/-15); imported rows carry `provenance = imported` on the run and `authority = unverified` on every claim-derived field (ADR-0026 decision 7; ADR-0033). **R-ROW-6 (citations):** every cell's `evidence[]` is an `audit_ref` (ADR-0067) so a viewer link or a paper citation verifies. **R-ROW-7 (numbers):** decimal strings/integers only (ADR-0029). **R-ROW-8 (size):** distributions, per-task cells and long vectors are content-addressed members referenced from the row, never inlined above the ADR-0029 threshold.

**Row versions and heads.** `RowVersion = (key, version_id, scoring, supersedes?: version_id, derived_from_reason ∈ {initial, regrade, validator_superseded, pricing_resnapshot, registry_bump, redaction})`; `head(key)` = newest version whose `scoring` members are not revoked; a version is never deleted; the catalogue lists all versions. An overlay run (regrade) is an ordinary instrument run whose `verification.validator.verdict` events carry `target = audit_ref(subject run)` and `causes[] → subject`; it never appends to the subject's ledger (ADR-0026 single writer) — hence `overlay_runs[]` in `ScoringContext`.

**Annotation index (derived; excluded from row bytes; ADR-0141 decision 1).** `RowAnnotations{key, version_id → {bundle_refs[]: {bundle_id, kind, claimed_level, status, level_now (after GC/redaction), verifiable_by[]: reader-set → level}, depends_on_revoked: bool + members[], bundle_retracted?: {reason_ref}, excluded_by[]: {experiment_run_id, seq, reason}, leaderboard_memberships[]: {definition_ref, snapshot_ids[]}, analysis_refs[], flags[]: {kind ∈ {contamination, suite_retired, discontinuity, budget_imbalanced, drift, selection}, reason_ref: audit_ref | version_id}}}` — rebuilt from the bundle catalogue, `StaleIndex`, experiment ledgers and snapshots; stamped with its own watermark set.

**Cell table (derived; the J4 input).** `CellTable{design_ref, watermark_set, cells[]: {cell_id = (arm_id, configuration_id, task_id), rows[]: {key, version_id (head), included: bool, excluded_reason?}, per_metric: {c, n, values[], outcome_counts{…}, veto_count}}, view_hash}` — stores per-task (c, n) so pass^k/pass@k and any interval method are recomputable (ADR-0045 decision 4); an excluded row is present with its reason (F8).

**Bundle catalogue (ADR-0141; shape fixed here).** `BundleCatalogueEntry{bundle_id, kind, subject_runs[], configuration_ids[], experiment_run_id?, claimed_level, max_supported_level, status, readers, name_bindings[], size, created_at, producer}` — header-only; the source of `bundle_refs[]` above.

### 6.3 Immutability rules (I-ROW / I-LED)

- **I-ROW-1** Rows, row versions, cell tables, catalogue entries, snapshots and analysis records are immutable once their `version_id`/`view_hash` exists; a change is a new record with `supersedes`. **I-ROW-2** The annotation index is the only "mutable" object and it is a rebuildable projection, never authoritative. **I-ROW-3** Nothing in the results store is deleted; a row whose run's blobs were GC'd or redacted keeps every field (they are references and hashes) and gains `level_now`. **I-ROW-4** A row for a run whose closure contains a revoked version is annotated `depends_on_revoked`, never hidden (ADR-0037 S4). **I-LED-1** Experiment-ledger events obey ADR-0026 (append-only, fenced writer = the sweep engine, durable-before-visible, chain). **I-LED-2** `PreRegistration` and `Design` are pinned by `version_id` in `measurement.experiment.declared` and cannot be re-declared for the same experiment run (a second `declared` is `SchemaViolation`); amendments are `measurement.experiment.amended{diff_ref, reason}` and mark every later analysis `post_amendment = true`.

### 6.4 Data-model sketch — experiment ledger and analysis records

Manifest of an experiment run: `RunManifest{run_kind = experiment, participant_class = native, observability_level = {events, ledger}, charged_to = instrument, experiment: {design_ref: version_id, pre_registration_ref: version_id, match_spec_ref, suite_manifest_ref, leaderboard_targets[]?}}` (no `configuration_id`; `budget` = the experiment's root budget node per ADR-0040; environment absent).

Invariants: regenerating from the same inputs and procedure yields the same `report` address and the same `analysis_id` (ADR-0045 AC-I2-12 lifted); a `ComparisonReport` inside a record carries `budget_match.status` and `benefit_kind` (ADR-0046) — an analysis lacking them cannot be recorded (`UnmatchedBudget`/`MissingMatchSpec`, ADR-0041); `pre_registered = true` only when the record's `kind`, `metric_ref`s and `procedure` match the pre-registration's plan by identity.

### 6.5 Leaderboard semantics

`LeaderboardDefinition{definition_id = version_id, name: (namespace, name) via name history (ADR-0037 I2), title: Text, suite_manifest_ref, split_label, scope: {participant_classes ⊆ {native, hosted}, granularity ∈ {component, configuration, product}, model_scope ∈ {same_snapshot, cross_model}}, admission: AdmissionPolicy, columns[]: {metric_ref: MetricDeclaration version_id, display: profile-free rendering hints}, rank_rule: {metric_ref, direction, interval_rule ∈ {point_only, interval_overlap_ties, rank_interval}, secondary[]}, strata[]: ⊆ {participant_class, observability_level, model_family, hosting_mechanism, cache_policy}, disclosure_policy: {require_experiment: bool, show_registered_arms: bool}, readers, contamination_policy_ref?, provenance}`.

`AdmissionPolicy{min_bundle_status ∈ {validated, attested, reproduced} (default validated), min_claimed_level ∈ {R0, R1, R2, R3} (default R0), require_bundle_kind ⊆ {run, arm, experiment} (default run for every contributing row), min_replicates_per_task (default from OQ-121/Stage), required_match_spec_mode (ADR-0041 M1 by default; M3 for any arm with search_budget > 0), require_eval_budget_equality_across_entries: bool (default true within a definition — a leaderboard is one matched-cap comparison), cache_policy_required?, allow_outcome_classes (denominator per ADR-0045 defaults), allow_veto_tripped: false, exclude_suite_retired: annotate (never drop), allow_hosted_observability ⊇ {events}}`.

`LeaderboardSnapshot = project(definition, results store @ watermark_set)`: `{snapshot_id = view_hash, definition_ref, generated_at, watermark_set, entries[]: LeaderboardEntry, unadmitted[]: {configuration_id, reason ∈ {no_bundle, bundle_status_below_min, level_below_min, unmatched_budget, insufficient_replicates, veto_tripped, class_excluded, undisclosed}}, disclosure: DisclosureSummary, metric_registry_version, price_table_version}`.

`LeaderboardEntry{configuration_id, participant_class, strata{…}, name_binding_refs[] (display only), rows[]: {key, version_id}, bundle_refs[]: {bundle_id, kind, claimed_level, status, level_now, verifiable_by[reader]}, experiment_refs[], n_runs, n_tasks, outcome_counts, headline: {point, interval{method, level, lo, hi}, per_task_cn_ref, tails_ref}, columns[]: value | n/a{reason}, cost: {spend{…}, cost_per_success, pareto_membership (ADR-0041 M2 with origin), provenance_mix}, budget: {eval_budget_ref, utilization, match_status}, rank: {position, tied_with[], rank_interval?}, selection?: {n_candidates_registered, rule, search_budget_ref} , disclosure: {experiment_run_id, arms_registered, arms_published, arms_restricted, exclusions: n, analyses_pre_registered: n, analyses_exploratory: n}, flags[]: {kind, reason_ref}, retracted?: {at, reason_ref}}`.

**Leaderboard rules (L-rules).**
- **L1 No score without a reproducible object.** An entry is admitted iff every contributing row head is annotated with ≥ 1 bundle satisfying `admission.min_bundle_status` and `min_claimed_level`; otherwise the configuration appears in `unadmitted[]` with the reason. A bundle whose bytes were partly GC'd keeps the entry; `level_now` and `verifiable_by` show what a reader can still check (OQ-112/OQ-331).
- **L2 Class-aware, never intersected.** Entries carry `participant_class` and observability strata; a column whose `MetricDeclaration.applies_to_classes`/`requires_observability` a stratum cannot satisfy renders `n/a{class | observability}` (T-LCD-15); rank is computed within `rank_scope` — by default per stratum; a cross-class rank is legal only on columns applicable to *every* entry at `product` granularity and is labelled so (T-LCD-07 spirit: stratify, never intersect).
- **L3 Keys, not names.** Entries are per `configuration_id`; names are display bindings; two names for one `configuration_id` are one entry; one name for two configurations is two entries (ADR-0038; T-LCD-09/-10).
- **L4 Matched budget or no rank.** All entries in one definition share the `eval_budget` hard caps of the definition's `match_spec` (ADR-0041 M1) or the definition declares `iso_cost` and ranks on the frontier (M2); an entry whose arm has `search_budget > 0` must satisfy M3 and carries `selection{}`; `budget_imbalanced` is a flag (ADR-0046 tolerance), `unmatched` is unadmitted (T-LCD-14).
- **L5 Distributions, not a number.** The headline cell carries point + interval + `n` + per-task (c, n) reference + outcome counts; ties are decided by `interval_rule` (default `interval_overlap_ties`); a `rank_interval` (J4 bootstrap over tasks) is shown when computed (CF-098 direction).
- **L6 Disclosure.** Every entry cites the experiment run(s) that produced its rows; the snapshot's `DisclosureSummary` counts arms registered/published/restricted, exclusions, pre-registered vs exploratory analyses; an entry whose rows are bound to no experiment run is `undisclosed` when `disclosure_policy.require_experiment` (default true for `published` snapshots, false for lab-internal views).
- **L7 Annotate, never hide.** Retraction (`measurement.leaderboard.entry.retracted` or bundle `retracted`), `depends_on_revoked`, suite retirement (`SuiteValidityRecord.retired_for_headline`), contamination, world-state discontinuity, veto-tripped runs, drift (DRIFT conformance record for hosted entries) are flags with `reason_ref`; a retracted entry stays in every snapshot that contained it and is rendered retracted in later ones; snapshots are never deleted.
- **L8 Publication is export.** `publish(definition, snapshot, PublicationPolicy)` is an ADR-0141 lowering: it appends `measurement.leaderboard.published`, emits `measurement.export.delivered` on every subject run, and produces a loss report; readers see `verifiable_by` computed for *their* reader set.
- **L9 Suite validity.** Entries on a suite with a `SuiteValidityRecord.noise_ceiling` render the ceiling beside the headline; a suite retired for headline yields only round-trip/control views (ADR-0144).

### 6.6 Interface-contract sketch (language-agnostic; operations · inputs → outputs · invariants · failure modes)

*Projection and maintenance (kernel side of the ADR-0050 boundary — they read ledgers, blobs and the identity service; whole records cross, never payload streams — D2):*
- `project_row(run_id, until_seq?, scoring?: ScoringContext) → ResultsRow` — pure; default `until_seq = head`, `scoring = native{registry_version, pricing_ref pinned in the manifest}`. *Failures:* `RunNotDurable(until_seq)`, `RegistryVersionUnknown`, `OverlayNotTargeting(run)`, `Tampered(at_seq)` (never silently skipped).
- `rescore(key, scoring: ScoringContext, reason) → RowVersion` — projects a new version and records `supersedes`; refuses identical scoring (`NoChange`) and unknown overlay runs. *Failures:* as above + `RevokedValidator` (allowed only in `mode = audit`).
- `annotate(scope: run_ids | all, sources: {catalogue, stale_index, experiment_runs, snapshots}) → AnnotationIndex@watermark_set` — rebuild; idempotent.
- `build_cells(design_ref | experiment_run_id, watermark_set?) → CellTable` — pure; includes excluded rows with reasons; refuses a design whose arms lack `MatchSpec` (`MissingMatchSpec`, ADR-0041).
- `catalogue_refresh(bundle_ids | all) → BundleCatalogue@watermark`.

*Reads (lab side; every read returns the `watermark_set` it was served at and is repeatable at that set — stable pagination under concurrent appends):*
- `get_row(key | version_id, at?: watermark_set) → ResultsRow + RowAnnotations`; `row_history(key) → [RowVersion]` (root first).
- `query_rows(QuerySpec{filter over coordinates/outcome/flags/annotations, projection: fields | full, at?: watermark_set, page{cursor, limit}, order}) → Page{rows, next_cursor, watermark_set, view_hash}` — filters are typed over declared fields; free text only in `Text` leaves; `n/a` cells are filterable by reason. *Failures:* `UnknownField`, `WatermarkAhead(run_id)` (requested watermark not yet durable), `UnknownCursor`.
- `cells(design_ref | experiment_run_id, at?) → CellTable`; `distribution(configuration_id, metric_ref, split, at?) → DistributionRef` (per-task (c, n), quantiles, tails).
- `catalogue(filter{configuration_id?, kind?, min_level?, status?, class?, readers?}, page) → Page{BundleCatalogueEntry}`.
- `leaderboard(definition_ref, at?: watermark_set) → LeaderboardSnapshot` (pure; unpublished view) ; `snapshots(definition_ref) → [snapshot_id]`; `diff_snapshots(a, b) → {entries_added, entries_removed (never — only retracted/flag changes), rank_moves[], flag_changes[], watermark_delta}`.
- `subscribe(filter) → Stream<RowHeadChanged | AnnotationChanged | SnapshotPublished>` (after durability; for K2 live views).
- `verify_row(version_id) → ok | Mismatch{recomputed}` and `verify_snapshot(snapshot_id)` — re-project and compare hashes; `verify_citation(audit_ref) → ok | Tampered | Missing{reason}` delegating to ADR-0067 `verify_inclusion`.
- `export_rows(QuerySpec, target ∈ {ledger_native_rows, interchange_table, csv_like_table, foreign_leaderboard_submission}, PublicationPolicy) → (artefact: ContentAddress, LoweringLossReport, measurement.export.delivered on every cited run)` — only `ledger_native_rows` is lossless; `n/a{reason}` lowers to a typed slot or is reported `no_slot`, never to 0 or empty (T-LCD-11/-15).

*Experiment ledger (producer contract for WS-J3; the store validates):*
- `open_experiment(manifest{run_kind = experiment, …}) → experiment_run_id` (ADR-0026 `open_run` with the discriminator); `declare(experiment_run_id, Design, PreRegistration, arms[], cells[])` → `measurement.experiment.declared` — refused after any `run.bound` (`DeclarationLate`) or if `PreRegistration.registered_at` postdates any bound run (`PreRegistrationLate`, ADR-0141 S9 mirror); `bind(run_id, cell_id)`, `exclude(run_id, reason, evidence?, authority)` (analyst exclusions need `origin = human`), `amend(diff_ref, reason, authority)`, `record_analysis(AnalysisRecord)` (refuses reports lacking `budget_match`/`benefit_kind`; refuses `pre_registered = true` when the plan does not match by identity — `NotPreRegistered`), `close(experiment_run_id)`.
- `define_leaderboard(LeaderboardDefinition) → definition_id` + name-history entry (`publish` per ADR-0037; namespace authority OQ-110); `publish(definition_ref, snapshot_id, PublicationPolicy) → export + measurement.leaderboard.published`; `retract_entry(definition_ref, configuration_id, reason_ref, authority)` → event; never a deletion.

*Invariants (all operations):* no operation mutates a durable record; every returned object carries `watermark_set` and `view_hash`; implementable out-of-process over the canonical encoding (T-LCD-12); failure modes are typed errors, never warnings, except `unadmitted[]` and flags, which are declared outcomes.

### 6.7 Retention (the WS-J5 half of OQ-085, OQ-112, OQ-173)

Ordered floors, extending ADR-0068 R4: `audit_envelope_floor ≥ experiment-ledger envelopes ≥ rows + row versions + annotation sources + published snapshots + analysis records ≥ bundle manifests ≥ blob tiers`. Concretely: (a) rows, row versions, cell tables' watermarks, analysis records and *published* snapshots are retained for the life of the installation like audit envelopes (they are small and are what citations point at); (b) unpublished snapshots are cache and may be dropped (they re-project); (c) experiment runs are `warm` by definition (WS-B1 §6.5) while any row they bind is retained; (d) blob GC on a subject run (ADR-0068 `lifecycle.ledger.gc`) never touches the row; the annotation index gains `level_now` and `verifiable_by` shrink; a leaderboard snapshot generated after the GC shows the flag; (e) a `gc` is refused with `Pinned` for addresses referenced by a bundle that a *published* snapshot's entry cites (extends ADR-0068 R2's `Pinned` set — proposed for WS-H6/L6 confirmation); (f) redaction of content cited by a cell's `evidence[]` leaves the citation verifiable (`content_fingerprints[]`) and flags the cell `evidence_redacted`; (g) who authorizes: lab principal for `gc`/retraction; redaction per ADR-0068 R2 (OQ-172); durations and legal hold remain WS-L6 (OQ-173).

### 6.8 Neighbour questions answered here (for synthesis to record)

- **OQ-128 (veto tiering; with WS-I2).** Proposed: **C0 / Stage 3 vetoes** — `duplicate_effect_rate` (ADR-0031/0045), `audit_completeness` (ADR-0068), `permission_violation_rate` (ADR-0052), the LT-01…12 leak battery (ADR-0059), `false_completion_rate` (ADR-0114), `attribution_completeness` (ADR-0101); **C2 / Stage 4–5 vetoes** — `memory.revoked_delivered` and validity vetoes (ADR-0083), taint/declassification violations (ADR-0054/0055), `blast_radius` outside scope (H4). - **OQ-321 (promoted fork pair; with WS-B4).** One *cell observation* when the parent finished with `stop_reason = superseded_by{run_id}` — the parent's row exists with `lineage.superseded_by_run` and is auto-excluded from the cell (`reason = superseded_by_fork`); two observations when the parent continued (Lab sibling arms), each a row, both annotated `branch_lineage`, and pooled only if the design declares the branch as a replicate.
- **OQ-239 (catalog `adopt` mid-run; with WS-L4/E3).** `configuration_version_id` is fixed at `open_run` and does not change; the row carries `catalog_epochs[]` and is flagged `configuration_drift`; such rows are excluded from pooling by default and admitted only when the design declares `adopt` as a factor level; the bundle's level is capped per ADR-0095.
- **OQ-261 / OQ-272 (outcome-class keying).** J5 keys by the ratified six classes; `loop_detected` and `format_failure` are `scored` runs stratified by `stop_reason` (a harness outcome, not an infrastructure fault); `failed_honestly` is `scored` with the `honest_failure_rate` metric populated; `unreconciled_claims` (hold budget exhausted) is `budget_exhausted` with `stop_reason` preserved. No new outcome class.
- **OQ-331 (J5 half).** Public leaderboards are `published` snapshots under a `PublicationPolicy`; a digest-only L2 index is allowed because rows cite `audit_ref`s and content addresses: a reader without content can verify manifests, chains and row hashes (`verifiable_by = R0`), and `reproduce(R1)` reports `MemberUnavailable` (ADR-0140) rather than a false verdict; who sets `readers` is the definition's publisher under OQ-110 namespace authority.

### 6.9 Acceptance-criteria sketch

- **AC-J5-1 Rebuild equality (T-LCD-12; ADR-0026 AC-B1-3 lifted).** Delete the results store; rebuild from ledgers, bundles, registry, metric registry and pricing snapshots; every row `version_id`, cell-table `view_hash`, catalogue entry, annotation index and *published* snapshot `view_hash` are equal to the originals.
- **AC-J5-2 Factor keys (T-LCD-09, gate).** A fixture with 2 model snapshots × 2 harness variants × 3 tasks × n = 3 yields rows keyed by the full tuple; `query_rows` can group by any factor; the canned "does variant V help model M₁ more than M₂?" runs over `cells()` and returns an interaction estimate with interval (AC-I2-1 executed against this store); no query keyed by a name exists.
- **AC-J5-3 No score without object (L1, gate).** A fixture with one row lacking a bundle, one with `assembled` status, one `validated` at R0, one `validated` at R1: the R0-min leaderboard admits exactly two entries and lists the other two in `unadmitted[]` with reasons; raising `min_claimed_level` to R1 admits one.
- **AC-J5-4 Annotate, never hide (L7).** After `retract_entry`, `revoke` of a member, suite retirement and a `gc` of the run's blobs: the entry remains in every snapshot with the four flags; `diff_snapshots` reports flag changes and no removals; `verify_row` still passes.
- **AC-J5-5 Watermark repeatability.** Under concurrent appends to ten runs, two `query_rows` calls at the same `watermark_set` return byte-identical pages; a call without `at` returns a newer set; `WatermarkAhead` is raised for a not-yet-durable seq.
- **AC-J5-6 Row versions (CF-344).** A regrade overlay run produces a new row version with `supersedes` and `derived_from_reason = regrade`; `head(key)` moves; `row_history` shows both; the old version is byte-identical; a pinned analysis still resolves the old version.
- **AC-J5-7 Class-scoped rendering (T-LCD-15, gate).** A mixed native / session-ABI / container-installed fixture yields `n/a{class}` and `n/a{observability}` cells and per-stratum ranks; no cell is 0 by absence; a cross-class rank is refused on a ledger-dependent column.
- **AC-J5-8 Disclosure (L6).** An experiment with 4 declared arms, 1 restricted bundle, 2 exclusions and 3 analyses (1 pre-registered) yields a snapshot whose entry reports `arms_registered = 4, arms_published = 3, arms_restricted = 1, exclusions = 2, analyses_pre_registered = 1, analyses_exploratory = 2`; a best-of-3 arm carries `selection{n_candidates = 3, …}` and `search_budget_ref`; a row bound to no experiment is `undisclosed` under `require_experiment`.
- **AC-J5-9 Matched budget (T-LCD-14, gate).** Entries with unequal `eval_budget` caps are `unadmitted{unmatched_budget}`; an arm with `search_budget > 0` and no M3 match is unadmitted; consumption imbalance beyond tolerance is a flag, not an exclusion.
- **AC-J5-10 Experiment ledger immutability.** A second `declared` is refused; `declare` after `bind` is `DeclarationLate`; an analysis lacking `budget_match` is refused; `verify(experiment_run)` passes the chain; the experiment bundle (`kind = experiment`) validates S9.
- **AC-J5-11 Export loss (T-LCD-11).** `export_rows(…, foreign_leaderboard_submission)` yields a loss report listing every `n/a{reason}`, provenance and interval field that had `no_slot`; `ledger_native_rows` round-trips to equal `version_id`s.
- **AC-J5-12 Citations verify.** Every cell `evidence[]` in the Stage-3 reference run resolves through `verify_citation` to `ok`; after redaction of one blob it resolves to `Missing{redacted}` and the cell is flagged `evidence_redacted`.
