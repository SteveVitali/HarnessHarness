## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides **the registry of component classes and their variants** as one instrument-level service: (i) the **record model** — what a `ClassRecord`, a `VariantRecord`, a conformance suite, a conformance report, a name-history entry and a registry snapshot are, and how the Phase 2 registry-shaped records (capability records, extension records, profiles and their test reports, wire dialects, procedure profiles, metric declarations, validators, environment records) share one store, one identity model and one envelope; (ii) the **operations** — `register / publish / resolve / query / snapshot / record_conformance / deprecate / yank / revoke / lineage / import / export` — with their invariants and typed failures; (iii) the **conformance-test contract per class** — how a class declares the executable battery a variant must pass, how the result is recorded as *observed* conformance next to the variant's *declared* capabilities (T-LCD-07 applied reflexively to our own components), and how the Lab consumes it; (iv) **discovery and query** for humans, the CLI (WS-K1), the assembly service (WS-J1) and the sweep engine (WS-J3) — never for the model; (v) **provenance and supply-chain trust of third-party variants** — the registry-side meaning of a third-party `VariantRecord` (an extension of kind `component_variant`, ADR-0063), its trust record, namespace ownership and publish authority (OQ-110, partial answer), and foreign registries (MCP registry, ACP registry, plugin marketplaces) as lifting sources; (vi) **parameter-space declarations for sweeps** — what a variant declares so that WS-J3 can enumerate slot choices and parameter domains without reading code; (vii) the **C0 minimal registry versus the rich C1 service**, with stage placement.

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: the plugin packaging format, the variant-host boundary protocol and the kernel↔extension ABI (**WS-L5** — this dossier fixes registry semantics: what a record says and when a variant may be resolved; L5 fixes how a third-party variant is packaged, loaded and driven); the trust-record shape, attestation kinds, isolation classes and the model-install path (**WS-H5**, adopted verbatim); the assembly service, its UI and the `compose → resolve → validate_assembly → seal` path (**WS-J1**/ADR-0025 — the registry is `resolve`'s `registry_view` input); the sweep engine's designs and budgets (**WS-J3** — this dossier fixes what a variant declares as sweepable, not how arms are scheduled); the results store's row keys (**WS-J5**, ADR-0038); the tool catalog the *model* sees (**WS-E3** — the component registry is not model-facing); the hash/canonical-form profile, name-history and supersession records (**WS-L4**, adopted); per-class contract contents (Phase 2 owners — this dossier fixes the *form* of a class's conformance suite, not the tests of any one class); every ecosystem question (**WS-L1**, ADR-0050).

## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**F1 — Every registry that survived contact with production is a *closed set of record kinds* over an *open set of names*, and the two audited registries that let kinds be open paid with late failures.** Inspect's `RegistryType` is a closed literal of 17 kinds and the persisted reference is exactly `{type, name, params}` (S-136 L40–82, L633–650); Harbor's `AgentName` is a closed enum plus one typed escape (`module:Class`) and one foreign-registry route (`acp:<id>@<version>`) (S-132 `factory.py` L110–135); Omnigent's `HarnessContribution` is a fixed record of typed maps (S-118 L113–137); the MCP registry's `registryType` and `remotes.type` are closed sums (S-502); the ACP manifest's `distribution` admits exactly three kinds (S-503). The open-kind precedents fail late: OpenHands' `DiscriminatedUnionMixin` raises at deserialization for a kind whose class was never imported (S-110 `models.py`), and Agent Spec's `component_type` union is validated only by an allow/block policy at load (S-139). ADR-0015 already drew this line for the HIR (closed kernel sums, registered `ext`); ADR-0023 drew it for the assembly (variant *tags* open and registry-validated). **Consequence (§6.1):** the registry's own `RecordKind` is a closed sum per registry dialect (`registry/1`) — `ClassRecord, VariantRecord, ConformanceSuite, ConformanceReport, NamespaceRecord, NameHistoryEntry, RegistrySnapshot` plus the Phase 2 kinds named in phase-2 §6.5 — grown only by dialect bump; *names* within a kind are open, namespaced and registry-validated. Adding a *component class* is a `ClassRecord` registration (ADR-0023 decision 2), not a new record kind.

**F2 — Identity, behaviour and declaration are three separable rows, and the systems that separated them are the ones that could be extended without editing core.** Omnigent's proposal document is explicit: the registry rejected community contributions because "editing core in ~10 places" was required until identity (`NativeCodingAgent`, "pure identity data"), behaviour (`NativeHarnessProvider`, dotted paths "resolved lazily at dispatch time … so building the registry never imports the runner") and capabilities (`HarnessCapabilities`, sparse, tri-state) became sibling rows keyed by one `key` (S-118 L55–137; design §"What is NOT pluggable"). Inspect keeps `RegistryInfo` (identity + metadata) apart from the object and lazy-loads packages by entry point (S-136 L260–298; `entrypoints.py`); Harbor's `_AGENT_MAP` maps names to import paths imported on demand (S-132 L17–22, L97–107). ADR-0023's `VariantRecord` already carries identity (`variant_id`, `version_id`), a pointer to behaviour (`implementation: CompiledPayload | {entrypoint_identity, locality}`) and a declaration (`capability_declaration`) in one record. **Consequence:** the record stays one content-addressed row (a split would break `version_id` over the whole), but the registry must be buildable *without loading any implementation* — `register`/`resolve`/`query` read records only; loading is `instantiate` (ADR-0025) or the L5 variant host. Property AC-J2-3 makes this checkable.

**F3 — Declared capabilities and probed conformance are different facts, and the registries that conflated them either dropped the declaration or trusted it blindly.** The ACP registry *removed* `capabilities` from its manifest (revision 2026-02-04) and instead has CI run the `initialize` handshake before listing (S-503) — conformance by probe, declaration by negotiation. Harbor declares `AgentCapabilities` statically on the class and refuses conflicting flags at class definition, but the factory never probes them (S-132 `base.py` L83–130). Omnigent's harness bench keys probes off declared capabilities and records `DRIFT` when declared ≠ observed (Ontology v1 *observed conformance / drift*, S-118 `verdict.py` via WS-A1). HarnessHarness already has this split three times over: `capability_vector = reconcile(capability_declaration, probes)` for hosted participants (ADR-0013), `ConformanceRecord{declared, probed, verdict}` for profiles (ADR-0125), and every Phase 2 class contract's `declare()` (D1 §6.3, D2 §6.5, D3 §6.5, E5 §6.2, F1 §6.1, G1 §6.3, G2 §6.4, I4 §6.4) beside a per-class *conformance suite* acceptance criterion (AC-D2-1 "shared battery", AC-F1-1 "conformance suite drives a variant out-of-process", C1 §6.7 golden streams, ADR-0094 §5 offline retrieval suite, AC-B5-10 "capability honesty"). What is missing is one *record* for the suite and one for its result. **Consequence (§6.3):** every `ClassRecord` carries `conformance_suite_ref: VersionedRef<ConformanceSuite>`; every `VariantRecord` carries `capability_declaration` (declared) and the registry stores `ConformanceReport`s (probed) as separate immutable records; `variant_conformance_vector = reconcile(declaration, reports)` with `unknown` never coerced (T-LCD-07 applied reflexively); `DRIFT` on a variant is data the Lab stratifies on, not a hidden exclusion (OQ-034's shape).

**F4 — Resolution has stages with typed failures, and the compatibility gate belongs before load.** opencode's loader resolves `Plan → Resolved | Missing → Loaded` with failure stages `install | entry | compatibility | missing` and checks the plugin's declared host-version range *before* importing it (S-111 `loader.ts` L86–135; `shared.ts` L194–210); Omnigent validates a community contribution's namespace prefix and key collisions before merging and records a failed contribution in `load_errors` rather than failing startup (S-118 L932–1040); Harbor refuses unknown agent names with a message that distinguishes "not registered" from "invalid" (S-132 L97–107); Inspect's lookup falls back to lazy entry-point loading only when the name is namespaced (S-136 L260–298). ADR-0025's `resolve` already returns `UnresolvedRef | AmbiguousVersion | DialectIncompatible`; ADR-0037 S3 adds `Revoked`. **Consequence (§6.2):** the registry's `resolve` composes the ADR-0037 rules with a *class-contract compatibility* check (`VariantRecord.contract_range` against the `ClassRecord` version the definition names) and a *conformance* check under policy, each a distinct typed failure; nothing loads before all pass.

**F5 — Names collide, and last-wins is the wrong default for an instrument.** Omnigent refuses community overrides of existing keys (S-118 L932–990) but `_merge_dict` is last-wins for non-colliding keys; OpenHands' tool registry only *warns* on duplicate names (S-110 `tool/registry.py`, via WS-A5); goose refuses an update whose manifest name differs from the installed name (S-112 `mod.rs` L279–285); pi computes a package identity that ignores version so the same package in two scopes is detected (S-113 L1682–1700); the MCP registry and ACP registry both enforce slug/namespace uniqueness at publish (S-502/02). ADR-0088 and ADR-0064 already name `NameCollision` as an error. **Consequence:** `publish` refuses a `(namespace, name)` collision across kinds and across contributions; a variant is *referenced* by `version_id` (N4) and *discovered* by name history; two publishers may not share a name, but one `version_id` may legitimately appear under two names (Unison/L4 F2 — recorded, never collapsed).

**F6 — Auto-update, `@latest` and "unknown" versions are the default in every plugin system, which is exactly what a results-keyed instrument cannot admit.** goose auto-updates every 24 h and admits `version: "unknown"` (S-112 `mod.rs` L18, `open_plugins.rs` L123); opencode resolves bare names to `@latest` (S-111 `shared.ts` L207–210); pi pins only exact versions (S-113); Claude marketplaces refresh daily (S-313); the ACP registry's archive URLs are `latest` release paths without digests (S-503 example). ADR-0036 N5/ADR-0037 I2/ADR-0064 fix the answer: selectors resolve at `resolve`, sealed forms are pins, updates are supersessions. **Consequence:** the registry never mutates a record and never auto-advances a resolution; freshness (`max_age`) is a `resolve`-time policy; a "latest" head is a name-history query answered under a `registry_snapshot_id`, so two machines resolving the same snapshot get the same `version_id`.

**F7 — A registry that the model can query is a registry that can be attacked through descriptions.** S-301's description-only attacks move a skill into the top-10 in 80% of discovery trials and flip selection in 77.6%; S-310's tool poisoning rides tool descriptions; the E3 catalog exists precisely because the *tool* catalog must be model-facing and therefore carries an offline retrieval suite and `unverified` lifting (ADR-0094/0088). The component-variation registry has no such need: the model never chooses a compaction strategy or a router variant — the author, the assembly service, the sweep engine and the evolution service do (ADR-0002 (iii) keeps the evolution service outside the editable surface it edits). **Consequence (invariant R-NM, §6.2):** no registry operation is exposed as a `ToolCapability`; `query`'s text fields are `search_projection`-class surfaces for humans and the Lab; a variant's `summary` `Text` leaf carries `authority ≤ external` when third-party and is never rendered into a model context.

**F8 — Third-party variants are extensions, and every in-process plugin architecture in the corpus is a rejected precedent.** opencode `import()`s plugins in process (S-111 `loader.ts` L136–140); pi loads extension modules with the full extension API; Inspect's registry is decorator-registered in-process code. CF-141 rejected in-process third-party loading; ADR-0063 L5 makes `isolation ≠ in_process` for every non-kernel extension; ADR-0063's `ExtensionKind` already contains `component_variant`. **Consequence (§6.5):** a third-party `VariantRecord` *is* an `ExtensionRecord` of kind `component_variant` — one record, two views — with `trust: ExtensionTrustRecord` (three legs), `implementation.locality ∈ {out-of-process, remote}` and the H5 default-authority table applied to its `summary`/`param_schema` descriptions; the first-party kernel-ecosystem variants are the only `in-process` implementations (ADR-0050 §8 clause (a) is the only permitted reference to that ecosystem).

**F9 — Namespaces need owners, and the live registries prove ownership by the platform, not by the manifest.** The MCP registry ties reverse-DNS namespaces to authenticated platform accounts or DNS/HTTP challenges and restricts package references to named public registries; six MCP registries with weaker checks host squatted names (S-303); the ACP registry uses a curated repo with CI as the owner. Inspect namespaces by installed package; Omnigent by reserved module prefix. ADR-0037 leaves publish authority to OQ-110. **Consequence (§6.5, partial answer to OQ-110):** a `NamespaceRecord{namespace, owners: [SignerRef | PrincipalRef], policy{who_may_publish, who_may_yank, who_may_revoke}}` is a registry record under `authority_cap` layering (organisation > user > project may only narrow); C0/Stage 1 has two namespaces (`hh/` kernel-owned, `local/` principal-owned); shared multi-namespace registries are C1/Stage 4 with signer-anchored ownership (H5 `TrustRootPolicy.accepted_signers`).

**F10 — Registries are queried for *factor enumeration*, and the operator libraries show why the query must be class-scoped and interface-typed.** AFlow feeds the optimizer a rendered list of operators with prose interfaces from `operator.json` (S-025 `graph_utils.py` L59–73); ADAS keeps a flat archive of whole agents (S-170); AgentSquare fixes four modules behind a uniform IO interface and recombines them (S-024); S-092/S-061/S-066 disagree about which slot carries value (WS-A5 F7, all `provisional`). The instrument needs `slot_choices(class_id, constraints) → [ComponentVariantRef]` to be *deterministic under a snapshot* and *filterable by declared capability, conformance, trust, locality and profile compatibility* so that J3 can build a factorial whose arms are matched (T-LCD-14) and whose class-scoped metrics are `n/a` where they must be (T-LCD-15). **Consequence (§6.4):** `query` is a typed predicate over record fields (never over free text alone), and `substitutable(a, b)` is derived from `class_id`, `contract_range`, and conformance evidence — the registry's answer to "which variants may fill this slot in this experiment".

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

**Against a registry at all for a research instrument ("doc 2 §7: none of eleven systems imports a framework; HAL runs agents by import path; Inspect's registry is a decorator").** A content-addressed, conformance-bearing registry is heavy machinery for an instrument whose Stage-0 harness has two slots. *Why this does not overturn:* the C0 slice (§6.7) is exactly the Inspect shape — a closed kind set, namespaced names, `(class, variant, params)` references — plus the two things Inspect lacks and the results store needs: a `version_id` on every record (ADR-0036) and a snapshot id in the run manifest (ADR-0038). Both already exist for other reasons; the registry adds no new identity machinery. The conformance suite for the two Stage-1 classes is the acceptance test the Phase 2 owners already wrote (AC-F1-1, AC-D1-8). Everything heavier — multi-namespace ownership, third-party publish, foreign import, revocation propagation — is C1/Stage 4 and never on the Stage 0–3 path.

**Against per-class conformance suites ("Harbor declares capabilities and never probes; conformance suites give false confidence and cost budget").** A variant can pass a battery and still misbehave on the model that matters; suites drift; running them on every publish is a tax. *Why this does not overturn:* the suite tests the *contract* (purity, closed sums handled, cut points respected, out-of-process drivability), not behavioural quality — quality is the Lab's job under matched budgets (ADR-0046). The ACP registry's move to probe-at-registration (S-503) and the profile test contract (ADR-0125) show the pattern is workable at protocol scale; the report is charged `instrument` (ADR-0039) so it never distorts a subject's budget; `require_conformance` is a policy knob (`none` for local experimentation, `declared` at C0, `probed` for Lab strict mode), so nothing blocks a developer who is iterating. The residual — suites that lag their class contract — is handled by pinning `ConformanceSuite` to a `ClassRecord` version and refusing a report produced under a stale suite (`SuiteStale`).

**Against "declared vs probed" for variants ("this is T-LCD-07 for hosted participants; applying it to our own components is ceremony — we control the code").** *Why this does not overturn:* third-party variants are not our code, and first-party variants change; the audit found that the one registry which trusted its own declarations (Harbor) has no way to notice drift, while the one that probes (ACP CI) removed declarations it could not verify. Keeping the declaration *and* the probe costs one record kind and lets the Lab stratify by verified capability rather than by claimed capability — the same reason ADR-0013 keeps `capability_declaration` and `capability_vector` distinct (CF-020).

**Against a class-partitioned registry ("ADAS/AFlow/Meta-Harness search whole agents or whole graphs; a class partition prevents cross-cutting variants and is the AgentSquare LCD trap in another form").** If value lives in *combinations* (S-024's recombination) or in whole-harness code (S-080), a registry keyed by class may not express the winning artifact. *Why this does not overturn:* (i) a whole-harness candidate is a *sealed definition* with its own lineage (ADR-0037 §6.4 rule b) — the registry stores definitions too; (ii) a cross-cutting behaviour that touches several slots is either several variants bound together by a definition (and diffed as one `HirDiff`) or a `CompiledPayload` with a declared interface bound into one slot — both expressible; (iii) the LCD risk of a "uniform IO interface" is answered not by the registry but by the class contracts themselves: every contract receives the profile and accounting (T-LCD-08), declares capabilities tri-state rather than intersecting them (T-LCD-07), and admits model-conditioned rules with debt records (T-LCD-05). The registry records those declarations; it does not flatten them. What is genuinely lost is search over *unpartitioned* code, which ADR-0002/ADR-0046 admit only as a C4 arm with a `search_budget` record.

**Against content-addressed variant identity ("every commit is a new `version_id`; lineages explode; developers will never publish").** *Why this does not overturn:* `version_id` is what runs and bundles pin; the *name history* is what humans and sweeps use; the sameness ladder (ADR-0037 §6.4) pools L1 results and reports L2 as lineage; unpublished local versions live in `local/` and are never required to be published to run. The cost that remains — OQ-114's storage question — is measured at Stage 3 and is an internal storage choice (snapshots authoritative, diffs cached).

**Against out-of-process for third-party variants on the hot path ("goose keeps `platform` extensions in process because some need direct access; a serialisation per control step distorts the very latency the Lab measures").** *Why this does not overturn:* ADR-0023 decision 5 and CF-141 already decided this; the registry records `locality` and `hot_path` so the Lab can stratify on the boundary cost rather than pretend it is zero, and OQ-075 measures the ceiling at Stage 2. A third-party hot-path variant is admissible; its cost is attributed (ADR-0039) and its results rows carry the locality stratum.

**Against namespace ownership by signer ("no skill ecosystem signs; the MCP registry's weakest link is ownership; requiring signers makes the C1 feature unusable").** *Why this does not overturn:* ownership at C0 is by *principal* on a local registry (two namespaces, no signing); shared registries at C1 accept either a verified signer or the registry's own principal as owner, and hash-only publications resolve with `hash_only_ceiling = external` on their text legs (ADR-0063) — usable, honest, cheap; signatures raise, never gate, except where a `TrustRootPolicy` says `require_signature_for ∋ component_variant`.

**Negative results from the audit.** No audited registry (i) content-addresses its entries (all key by name; Harbor's lock digests are for tasks, not agents); (ii) stores a conformance result next to a declaration (ACP CI checks then discards; Omnigent's bench stores verdicts outside the registry); (iii) records which snapshot of the registry a run resolved against; (iv) separates publication status (active/deprecated/yanked) from admission status (resolved/quarantined/sealed/revoked) from conformance status (declared/probed/drift) — each system has at most one of the three; (v) refuses `@latest`/`"unknown"` at the sealed form. These are the `novel`-as-integration parts of §6, each with the partial precedent named in §4c.

## 6. Recommendation for the spec

### 6.1 The registry record model (Spec §6 registry; §8 versioning/extensibility)

**One store, one identity model, one envelope, many kinds.** The registry is a typed record store under ADR-0036 (`version_id` per record with a kind-specific domain tag; `idp` on every id), ADR-0037 (immutability I1–I5, name histories, supersession S1–S8, `StaleIndex`) and ADR-0038 (`registry_snapshot_id` over the referenced closure). It is *not* a HIR document (records reference HIR nodes by `VersionedRef`; ADR-0063's rule for `ExtensionRecord` generalised). Every record is wrapped in one **registry envelope**, which generalises the capability-record envelope of ADR-0087 (Ontology v2 *capability record*: `{registered_at, registrar, trust_record_ref, name_history_ref, dialect_range, status, usability}`):

```
RegistryEnvelope{
  kind: RecordKind,                          // closed per registry/1 (below)
  version_id, semantic_id?,                  // ADR-0036; semantic_id only for kinds with a declared semantic projection (HIR-bearing kinds)
  registered_at, registrar: ProvenanceRecord, // who registered (kernel | principal | import | model — ADR-0033 origin)
  admission: {resolved | sealed | quarantined | revoked},   // ADR-0063 status vocabulary (per record; never per name)
  name_history_ref?: VersionedRef<NameHistoryEntry>,        // ADR-0037 I2 (publication status lives THERE: active | deprecated | yanked)
  trust_record_ref?: VersionedRef<ExtensionTrustRecord>,    // mandatory when registrar.origin ∉ {kernel, definition}
  dialect_range: {min, max},                 // HIR dialect(s) the record's references are valid for (ADR-0023)
  registry_dialect: "registry/1",
  ext: ExtensionSlots }                      // ADR-0015 slot rules; never decides admission, conformance or authority
```

**`RecordKind` (closed per `registry/1`; growth = dialect bump):** `class`, `variant`, `conformance_suite`, `conformance_report`, `namespace`, `name_history_entry`, `registry_snapshot`, `foreign_import`, plus the Phase 1/2 kinds the registry must hold verbatim: `sealed_definition` (ADR-0025), `capability` (`ToolCapability` versions, ADR-0087/0088 — the envelope above *is* their envelope), `extension` (ADR-0063), `model_profile` and `profile_test_report` (ADR-0124/0125), `wire_dialect` and `model_role_table` (ADR-0118/0121), `procedure_profile` (ADR-0084), `surface_family` (ADR-0090), `metric_declaration` (ADR-0045 — "metrics are first-class registry objects"), `validator` (ADR-0110), `environment_record` and `environment_family` (ADR-0137/0142), `trust_root_policy` (ADR-0063), `identity_profile` (ADR-0036). Each kind names its domain tag, its canonical-form projection, whether it has a `semantic_id`, and which operations apply. A record whose `kind` is not in the sum is `UnknownRecordKind` — the reflexive form of T-LCD-01 (no escape hatch through `ext`).

**`ClassRecord` (ADR-0023 shape, extended — the extensions are additive fields on the same record):**

```
ClassRecord{
  class_id, contract_version,                                  // a contract change is a NEW ClassRecord version; variants declare contract_range
  home: Plane | Boundary,                                      // Ontology v1 home-plane rule; UnclassifiedKind otherwise
  contract{ operations[{name, inputs, outputs, invariants, failure_modes}] },   // operations, never inheritance (T-LCD-12)
  cardinality ∈ {exactly-one, optional, ordered-many},         // ordered-many semantics per class (OQ-073/OQ-191 owners)
  required_inputs ⊇ {ModelProfile, ResourceAccount},           // T-LCD-08
  base_param_schema, hot_path: bool, dialect_introduced,
  declaration_schema: Schema,                                  // the class's VariantDeclaration shape (D2 §6.5, F1 §6.1, G1 §6.3 … are instances); tri-state fields
  conformance_suite_ref: VersionedRef<ConformanceSuite>,       // §6.3 — mandatory from Stage 1 for Stage-1 classes
  decision_points: set<DecisionPoint>,                         // where the runtime invokes it (Ontology v1 β)
  metrics_declared: [VersionedRef<MetricDeclaration>],         // class-scoped metrics this class contributes (ADR-0045)
  slot_key: string }                                           // key in AgentProcess.native.slots (CF-052)
```

**`VariantRecord` (ADR-0023 shape, extended):**

```
VariantRecord{
  variant_id, class_id, contract_range: {min, max},            // ClassRecord.contract_version(s) it implements
  version_label?: SemVer-class string,                         // a CLAIM (ADR-0037 I2); never resolution precedence except through name-history entries
  version_id,                                                  // over the whole record incl. implementation address
  param_schema: map<name, ParamDecl{type, domain, default?, unit?, sweepable: bool, budget_relevant: bool, affects: [operation]}>,
  implementation: CompiledPayload | { entrypoint_identity: ContentAddress, locality ∈ {in-process, out-of-process, remote}, host_requirements: TypedValue },
  capability_declaration: TypedValue conforming to ClassRecord.declaration_schema,   // DECLARED; tri-state fields; never coerced
  conditioned_rules: [ {rule_id, AssumptionDebtRecord} ],      // T-LCD-05; register refuses an incomplete record (ConditionedRuleIncomplete)
  applies_to: { participant_classes = {native}, families?: [FamilySelector] },        // OQ-344 answered at registry level (§6.4)
  declared_costs?: advisory (ADR-0089 discipline: never accounting),
  summary: Text{authority ≤ per H5 table},                     // human/K1 surface; never model-facing (R-NM)
  provenance: ProvenanceRecord, ext }
```

A `VariantRecord` whose `registrar.origin ∉ {kernel, definition}` is simultaneously an `ExtensionRecord{kind: component_variant}` (ADR-0063): the envelope's `trust_record_ref` is mandatory, `implementation.locality ≠ in-process` (ADR-0063 L5, CF-141), and the H5 default-authority table applies to `summary` and to any `Text` inside `param_schema` descriptions.

**Conformance records (§6.3):** `ConformanceSuite{suite_id, class_ref: VersionedRef<ClassRecord>, tests[{test_id, kind ∈ {static, contract, executable, property}, fixture_ref: ContentAddress, driver ∈ {in-process, out-of-process}, oracle_class (ADR-0047; deterministic-only at C0), budget: BudgetNode, verdict_rule}], required_for_status: set<test_id>, version_id}` and `ConformanceReport{report_id, variant_ref, suite_ref, host: {locality, isolation, instrument: InstrumentRecord}, produced_by ∈ {registry_ci, lab, publisher_claim}, results[{test_id, verdict ∈ {pass, fail, inconclusive, n/a{reason}}, evidence_ref}], probed_declaration: TypedValue (the declaration fields the suite could verify), run_id (an ordinary ledgered run, charged_to = instrument), version_id}`.

**Derived views (never stored primary, ADR-0026):** `variant_conformance_vector(variant_ref, policy) = reconcile(capability_declaration, [ConformanceReport where produced_by admissible under policy])` → `{declared, probed, unknown, drift: [field]}`; `catalog(filter, snapshot)`; `slot_choices(class_id, constraints, snapshot)`; `lineage(name)`; `StaleIndex` (ADR-0037 S4).

### 6.2 Operations (language-agnostic; operations · inputs → outputs · invariants · failure modes)

All operations are records-in/records-out, deterministic given a `registry_snapshot_id`, implementable out-of-process over the canonical encoding (T-LCD-12), and appended to the ledger as events (§6.6). The registry *store* and these operations are MUST-code (ADR-0024); the admission policy (`RegistryPolicy`, below) is MUST-data.

- `register(record: RecordKind-typed, registrar: ProvenanceRecord, policy) → RegistryEnvelope | Refused{reason}` — validates the record against its kind's schema and, for `variant`, against `ClassRecord.declaration_schema`, `contract_range ∩ class.contract_version ≠ ∅`, complete `AssumptionDebtRecord` on every conditioned rule, `implementation` pinned (`ContentAddress`, never a selector), trust record present when required, `locality` admissible for the registrar's origin; computes `version_id`; sets `admission = resolved` (or `quarantined` when policy requires attestation and it is missing/failed — ADR-0063). Invariants: idempotent on equal canonical bytes (`register` of an existing `version_id` returns the existing envelope); registration never loads an implementation (AC-J2-3). Failures (typed, never warnings): `UnknownRecordKind`, `SchemaViolation{path}`, `UnknownClass`, `ContractIncompatible{variant contract_range, class contract_version}`, `ConditionedRuleIncomplete{rule_id}`, `UnpinnedImplementation`, `TrustRecordRequired`, `LocalityInadmissible{origin, locality}`, `DialectIncompatible`.
- `publish(namespace, name, version_id, label?, supersedes?, provenance) → NameHistoryEntry` — ADR-0037 `publish` verbatim, plus: the caller must satisfy `NamespaceRecord.policy.who_may_publish` (§6.5); `(namespace, name)` is unique across *all* kinds (F5); a publish whose variant lacks the conformance status `policy.require_conformance` demands is refused (`ConformanceRequired{missing tests}`) — `publisher_claim` reports never satisfy `probed`. Failures: ADR-0037's (`LabelReused`, `KindMismatch`, `AuthorityWideningRequiresHuman`, `NamespaceForbidden`) + `NameCollision{existing kind, version_id}`, `ConformanceRequired`, `SuiteStale`.
- `resolve(selector: NameSelector | ComponentVariantRef | version_id, snapshot?: registry_snapshot_id, mode ∈ {execute, audit, reproduce}, requirements?: {class_id, contract_version, profile_ref?, locality?, conformance_floor?}) → VersionedRef{…, envelope, conformance_vector} | Unresolved | Ambiguous{candidates} | Revoked{…} | Stale{depends_on_revoked[]} | ContractIncompatible | ConformanceBelowFloor{vector}` — ADR-0037 S3 and ADR-0088 decision 1 verbatim (`mode = execute` never returns yanked/revoked/stale heads; a pinned `version_id` resolves in every mode with status attached), composed with the class-contract check and the conformance floor (F4); the result's `snapshot` is recorded by ADR-0025 `resolve` as `registry_snapshot_id`. Invariant: deterministic given `(selector, snapshot, mode, requirements)`; two machines resolving one snapshot agree byte-for-byte (AC-J2-1).
- `query(predicate: RegistryPredicate, snapshot) → [RegistryEnvelope]` — a closed, quantifier-free predicate language over envelope and record fields (`kind`, `class_id`, `contract_range`, `capability_declaration.<field> = value | unknown`, `conformance.<test_id> = verdict`, `trust.text_authority ≥ c`, `trust.attestation_status`, `locality`, `hot_path`, `applies_to.families ∋ f`, `admission`, name-history `status`, `label range`) with `order_by` a declared field and stable tie-break by `version_id` — never by relevance over free text (R-NM). Failures: `UnknownField`, `UnknownSnapshot`.
- `catalog(filter, snapshot) → [CatalogRow{class_id, variant_id, name?, label?, version_id, admission, publication_status, conformance_summary, trust_summary, locality, summary: Text}]` — the K1/K2/J1 projection (Omnigent `harness_catalog()` shape); derived from `query`.
- `slot_choices(class_id, constraints: {profile_ref?, locality?, conformance_floor, trust_floor, families?}, snapshot) → [ComponentVariantRef]` — for WS-J3: deterministic; each ref's `param_schema` gives the sweepable domains; refuses when the class has no `ConformanceSuite` and `conformance_floor ≠ none` (`SuiteMissing`). `substitutable(a, b, constraints) → bool` = same `class_id` ∧ overlapping `contract_range` ∧ both satisfy `constraints`.
- `snapshot(refs[]) → registry_snapshot_id` — ADR-0038 (closure, not catalog); `catalog_id` may be recorded as a claim.
- `record_conformance(variant_ref, suite_ref, report: ConformanceReport) → RegistryEnvelope` — the report's `run_id` must exist in the ledger with `charged_to = instrument` (ADR-0039) unless `produced_by = publisher_claim`; a report against a `ConformanceSuite` that is not the head suite of the class's current `contract_version` is accepted but marked `stale = true` and never satisfies a floor (`SuiteStale`). Invariant: reports never overwrite; the vector is derived from the set.
- `deprecate(name, reason, authority)` / `yank(name entry, reason, authority)` / `revoke(version_id, reason, authority, replacement?)` — ADR-0037 I2/S1 verbatim (`deprecated` and `yanked` are name-history statuses; `revoke` is a `RevocationRecord` on the version with `StaleIndex` propagation and the ADR-0064 live-run propagation for extensions); authority per `NamespaceRecord.policy` (§6.5). Failures: `AuthorityInsufficient`, `CycleDetected`, `NotAncestorOrSibling`.
- `lineage(name | version_id) → DAG` and `sameness(a, b) → L0…L4` — ADR-0037 §6.4 verbatim; storage: snapshots authoritative, diffs cached (`diff_ref` on name entries).
- `import(foreign: ForeignRegistryRef{system ∈ {mcp_registry, acp_registry, marketplace, git, archive}, locator}, policy) → [RegistryEnvelope{admission: quarantined | resolved}]` and `export(refs[], target) → Document + LossReport` — lifting/lowering per ADR-0021/0141 (§6.6).
- `verify(envelope) → ok | Mismatch` — ADR-0036 `verify`; every read path may re-verify (cheap, cached).

**Registry invariants (R-rules).** R1 *Immutable records, mutable names only* (ADR-0037 I1/I2). R2 *Closed kinds* (F1). R3 *No load on read*: `register/resolve/query/catalog/slot_choices` never execute or import an implementation. R4 *Declared ≠ probed*: `capability_declaration` and `ConformanceReport` are separate records; the vector is derived; `unknown` never coerced (T-LCD-07). R5 *Three orthogonal statuses, one vocabulary each*: admission (`resolved | sealed | quarantined | revoked`, per record, ADR-0063), publication (`active | deprecated | yanked`, per name entry, ADR-0037), conformance (`declared | probed | drift | unknown` per declaration field, derived) — a fourth status vocabulary anywhere in the registry is a schema error (CF-324). R6 *Not model-facing* (R-NM): no registry operation is a `ToolCapability`; registry text never enters a model context; the `discover_surfaces` capability (ADR-0094) is the tool catalog's, not this registry's. R7 *Pinned or refused*: sealed definitions, run manifests and bundles reference registry records only by `version_id` (ADR-0036 N5); `resolve(mode = execute)` of an unpinned selector is legal only inside ADR-0025 `resolve` (assembly time) and is recorded with its snapshot. R8 *Snapshot determinism* (AC-J2-1). R9 *Authority never widens by registration*: registering, publishing or importing a record never mints authority above the ADR-0033 default for its origin; conformance never raises text authority; a signature raises text authority only through a `pin` endorsement (ADR-0063 L2). R10 *Failures are records*: a failed `register`/`import` is a `RegistryDiagnostic` record + event, never a startup failure of the instrument (Omnigent `load_errors` shape).

**`RegistryPolicy` (MUST-data; a `HarnessRule`-class record layered per ADR-0024 with `authority_cap`, organisation > user > project may only narrow):** `{require_conformance ∈ {none, declared, probed} (C0 default declared; Lab strict mode probed), admissible_report_producers ⊆ {registry_ci, lab, publisher_claim}, require_trust_record_for_origins, require_signature_for_kinds (from TrustRootPolicy, ADR-0063), max_age?, collision_policy = refuse (fixed), foreign_import_default_admission ∈ {quarantined (fixed default), resolved(by attestation only)}, allowed_localities_by_origin, allowed_foreign_systems[]}`.

### 6.3 The conformance-test contract per class

Every registered component class ships a `ConformanceSuite` as a registry record pinned to its `contract_version`. The suite has four test kinds, and the class owner (the Phase 2 workstream) fills them from its acceptance criteria — this dossier fixes only the form and the minimum:

1. **static** — schema checks on the `VariantRecord` (declaration conforms to `declaration_schema`; every conditioned rule has a complete debt record — T-LCD-05; `required_inputs` present — T-LCD-08; no inheritance in the contract — T-LCD-12 by construction).
2. **contract** — the operations are driven over canonical documents by the *out-of-process* driver (AC-F1-1's shape: `Cue`/`ControlDecision` documents for `control_strategy`; `ContextView`/`ContextPlan` for `context_policy`/`compaction_strategy`; evidence bundles for `validator`); every closed-sum input is exercised (`UnhandledCue`-class failures surface here, never at run time — F1 I3); purity where the contract demands it (equal `proposal_id` across two hosts, AC-D1-8/AC-D2-1); typed failure modes returned, never raised as opaque errors.
3. **executable** — fixtures with deterministic oracles only at C0 (ADR-0047 headline classes): golden streams (C1), `apply(base, diff) = target` (L4), retrieval `recall@k` floors (ADR-0094), cut-point fuzzing (D2), capability-honesty probes (B5 AC-B5-10 shape: a declared-`supported` field is exercised; an undeclared operation returning success is a failure).
4. **property** — invariants the kernel relies on: the variant never widens authority, never returns a permission/label/lower risk class (E5 "executors report, never decide"), never reads fields outside its declared feature vocabulary (D3 ranker), `deterministic: true` ⇒ replay-equal outputs (ADR-0028).

**Minimum by class (Stage 1):** `control_strategy` and `context_policy` (the two Stage-1 mandatory slots, CF-052) must have suites with at least one test of each kind and an out-of-process driver; every other class registered at Stage 2–3 must ship its suite in the same ADR-round that registers the class (a `ClassRecord` without `conformance_suite_ref` is `SuiteMissing` and can be resolved only under `require_conformance = none`). **Report production:** `registry_ci` (the registry's own runner, out-of-process, charged `instrument`), `lab` (a Lab experiment whose arms include the variant — the conformance run is a normal run with `outcome_class`), or `publisher_claim` (a report shipped with a third-party package — recorded, displayed at `review` (ADR-0064), never counted toward `probed`). **Vector semantics:** for each declaration field the suite covers, `probed ∈ {SUPPORTED, UNSUPPORTED, PARTIAL, NOT_APPLICABLE, UNKNOWN, SKIPPED, DRIFT}` (Ontology v1 *observed conformance*; one vocabulary with hosted participants and profiles — CF-325 asks synthesis to confirm `ConformanceRecord` and `ConformanceReport.results[]` are one shape); fields the suite does not cover stay `declared` with `probed = UNKNOWN`. **Lab consumption:** `variant_conformance_vector` is a stratification coordinate and an annotation on results rows (like `depends_on_revoked`), never an exclusion by default (OQ-034's shape); an arm binding a variant with `DRIFT` on a capability the experiment depends on is refused by WS-J3 at design time (`DependsOnDriftedCapability`), mirroring ADR-0125's dependency-scoped DRIFT refusal for profiles.

### 6.4 Discovery, query and parameter spaces for the Lab (WS-J1, J3, K1)

- **Discovery of records** is `query`/`catalog` over a snapshot (§6.2). Discovery of *sources* (directories, marketplaces, foreign registries) is ADR-0064 `discover` over declared sources; the registry never scans implicitly (goose's auto-enable rejected, F6).
- **Parameter space:** a definition's space (ADR-0025 `space()`) = its declared `parameters` ∪ for each slot `slot_choices(class_id, constraints)` × each chosen variant's `param_schema` where `sweepable = true`. The registry guarantees: every sweepable `ParamDecl` has a typed `domain` and a `default`; `budget_relevant` parameters are enumerable so WS-J3 can check `MatchSpec` coverage (T-LCD-14); `affects: [operation]` lets the Lab attribute a parameter to the operations it touches (slot-scoped budgets, WS-A5 F7). A variant whose `param_schema` cannot be validated against `base_param_schema` fails `register`.
- **`applies_to` (OQ-344):** registry-level scoping on `VariantRecord.applies_to.families[]` (and on `MetricDeclaration`, already registry objects per ADR-0045), *not* an HIR/1 amendment: `applies_to` is an instrument-level fact about admissibility, not a semantic of the harness; the `BenchmarkConditionedRule` check (ADR-0143) reads family selectors from the registry record at `seal`. Proposed answer; WS-A3/I2 confirm (CF-326).
- **Snapshot for a design:** WS-J3 pins one `registry_snapshot_id` per experiment `Design` (immutable once the first run opens, ADR-0045); every arm's sealed definition resolves against it; a variant published after the snapshot is invisible to that experiment by construction.

### 6.5 Provenance, trust and namespaces for third-party variants (WS-H5, WS-L5; OQ-110 partial)

- **A third-party variant is an extension.** `VariantRecord` with `registrar.origin ∈ {import, model, principal-with-external-code}` ⇒ `ExtensionRecord{kind: component_variant}` view: `content` (tree rule over the package), `code_identity` (every executable body), `isolation ≥ subprocess_confined`, `grants` conferred only by a `Permission` at `seal`, `declared_claims` (its own `capability_declaration` **as claims**) — the declaration enters the conformance vector only after a `registry_ci`/`lab` report (R4/R9). `implementation.locality ∈ {out-of-process, remote}`; the L5 variant host drives it over the WS-L1 §6.5 verb discipline (ADR-0023 decision 5; ADR-0050 D3) — J2 fixes that the host's `hello` carries `contract_version`, `variant version_id` and `registry_snapshot_id` so the ledger's `lifecycle.component.bound` event can be checked against the sealed definition.
- **Namespaces (OQ-110, partial).** `NamespaceRecord{namespace (reverse-DNS or reserved), owners: [SignerRef | PrincipalRef], policy{who_may_publish, who_may_deprecate, who_may_yank, who_may_revoke, require_signature: bool}, provenance}` — a registry record under `authority_cap` layering. Reserved: `hh/` (kernel-owned; only the instrument's own release process publishes; `ClassRecord`s live here), `local/` (the run's principal; never exported without re-namespacing), `exp/<experiment_id>/` (ablation/evolution lineages, ADR-0037 §6.4 rule b; owner = the experiment's principal; the evolution service publishes here at `origin = model` and can never publish elsewhere — ADR-0002 (iii)). Shared namespaces (C1/Stage 4): ownership proven by an accepted signer (H5 `TrustRootPolicy`) or by the registry's administering principal; `revoke` of another publisher's version is admissible only to the namespace owner or the registry administrator with `authority ≥ principal`, is always a `RevocationRecord` (never deletion), and propagates per ADR-0064. Cross-registry revocation and memory-registry revocation (OQ-212) remain open (OQ-352).
- **Freshness and rollback (TUF shape without a TUF repository, ADR-0063 §6.3):** the `registry_snapshot_id` is the snapshot role; `NameHistoryEntry.published_at` + `RegistryPolicy.max_age` the timestamp role; a name whose new head has a lower `version_label` than its predecessor is `LabelRegression` at `review`; mix-and-match is impossible because a sealed definition pins one closure.
- **Foreign registries (§6.6)** enter as `foreign_import` records with `ForeignRef{system, digest?, label}` (ADR-0141's foreign reference), `admission = quarantined` by default, text legs `unverified`, and a `LossReport` naming what the foreign manifest could not say (e.g. ACP manifests carry no digest and no capabilities; MCP registry entries carry a digest only for one package kind).

### 6.6 Interop and events

- **Import/export as lifting/lowering (ADR-0021/0141, T-LCD-11):** `import` from the MCP registry lifts `server.json` into an `ExtensionRecord{kind: mcp_server}` + `foreign_import` with `remotes`/`packages` as `SourceLocator`s and `fileSha256` (where present) as a `ForeignRef` digest; from the ACP registry lifts `agent.json` into a hosted `ParticipantDescriptor` candidate (WS-J6) — never into a `VariantRecord` (hosted participants are not component variants; T-LCD-06). `export` lowers a `VariantRecord` to a plugin manifest (WS-L5 format) with a loss report (conformance vectors, trust legs and `applies_to` have no slot in any foreign format today).
- **Events (proposed to WS-B1 with ADR-0088's OQ-225 shape):** `lifecycle.registry.registered{kind, version_id, semantic_id?, registrar, admission}`, `lifecycle.registry.published{namespace, name, version_id, label?, supersedes?}`, `lifecycle.registry.conformance_recorded{variant_ref, suite_ref, produced_by, run_id, summary}`, `lifecycle.registry.deprecated/yanked/revoked{…, reason, authority}`, `lifecycle.registry.imported{system, foreign_ref, admission, loss_report_ref}`, `lifecycle.registry.snapshot{registry_snapshot_id, closure_size}`; each declares durability and observability level (ADR-0026); `lifecycle.capability.*` (ADR-0088) and `security.extension.*` (ADR-0064) remain the kind-specific classes and are *not* duplicated — the generic classes carry `kind` and are emitted only for kinds without a specific class (CF-327 for synthesis to rule).

### 6.8 Acceptance-criteria sketch

- **AC-J2-1 Snapshot determinism (C0/Stage 1).** Two independent implementations of `resolve`/`query`/`slot_choices` over the same `registry_snapshot_id` return byte-equal results on a golden registry corpus (≥3 classes, ≥3 variants each, ≥2 namespaces, one yanked and one revoked version).
- **AC-J2-2 Closed kinds (C0/Stage 1).** A record with an unlisted `kind`, or a status value outside the three vocabularies of R5, fails `register` with `UnknownRecordKind`/`SchemaViolation`; growing `RecordKind` requires a `registry/2` dialect.
- **AC-J2-3 No load on read (C0/Stage 1).** With every implementation body made unloadable (corrupted `CompiledPayload` bytes, absent entry points), `register/resolve/query/catalog/slot_choices/lineage` still succeed; only `instantiate` (ADR-0025) fails with `BindFailure`.
- **AC-J2-4 Declared ≠ probed (C0/Stage 2).** A variant declaring `deterministic = true` whose contract test yields differing outputs across two hosts receives `probed = DRIFT` on that field; the declaration is unchanged; `resolve(mode = execute, requirements.conformance_floor = probed)` refuses with `ConformanceBelowFloor` and `resolve` without the floor succeeds with the vector attached; the Lab results row carries the vector.
- **AC-J2-5 Conformance suite pinning (C0/Stage 2).** Bumping a class's `contract_version` makes existing reports `stale = true`; a variant with only stale reports resolves under `declared` but not under `probed`; a `publisher_claim` report never satisfies `probed`.
- **AC-J2-6 Not model-facing (C0/Stage 3).** The compiled tool surface of every registered definition contains no registry operation; a static check over `CompiledBundle.model_surface` and the closed-world catalog (ADR-0019/0093) finds zero `hh/registry/*` capabilities; a fixture that attempts to bind `query` as a `ToolCapability` fails `validate_assembly`.
- **AC-J2-7 Names vs identity (C0/Stage 1; T-LCD-10).** Publishing one `version_id` under two names yields one record and two name-history entries; renaming a variant's `summary` changes `version_id`, not `semantic_id` of any HIR node it references; `sameness` between the two is L1.
- **AC-J2-8 Collision refusal (C0/Stage 1).** A second `publish` of `(namespace, name)` by a different publisher, or across kinds, fails `NameCollision`; a community package attempting to publish into `hh/` fails `NamespaceForbidden`.
- **AC-J2-9 Third-party variant admission (C1/Stage 4).** An unsigned out-of-process variant registers with `admission = resolved`, `summary.authority = external`, `implementation.locality = out-of-process`; the same package declaring `locality = in-process` fails `LocalityInadmissible`; with `require_signature_for ∋ component_variant` it is `quarantined` until a `pin` endorsement; its `capability_declaration` contributes to no conformance vector until a `registry_ci` report exists (AC-H5-3 shape).
- **AC-J2-10 Revocation propagation (C1/Stage 4).** After `revoke(v)`, `resolve(name, execute)` never returns `v` or any sealed definition in `StaleIndex[v]`; results rows referencing bundles that pin `v` are annotated `depends_on_revoked`, never hidden (ADR-0037 S4, AC-L4-7/-11).
- **AC-J2-11 Foreign import is lossy-declared (C1/Stage 4; T-LCD-11).** Importing an ACP `agent.json` and an MCP `server.json` yields `foreign_import` records with `admission = quarantined`, `ForeignRef` digests only where the source carries one, and a `LossReport` listing at least `{capabilities, conformance, trust legs, digest}` per source; a round-trip `export → import` of a first-party variant recovers every field not declared lost.
- **AC-J2-12 Sweep enumeration (C0/Stage 3; T-LCD-09/-14).** `slot_choices(compaction_strategy, {conformance_floor: declared})` over the Stage-3 registry enumerates the `lab/compaction-family-v1` variants (ADR-0077) with typed domains for every `sweepable` parameter and flags `budget_relevant` ones; WS-J3 refuses an arm whose `MatchSpec` omits a `budget_relevant` parameter that differs across arms.
- **AC-J2-13 Failure as record (C0/Stage 2).** A malformed community contribution produces a `RegistryDiagnostic` record and a `lifecycle.registry.registered{admission: refused}` event; the instrument starts and every other record resolves.

## 10. Confidence

**High** for the record model's shape (closed kinds, envelope, names-over-immutable-records, no-load-on-read, snapshot determinism): every element is read in ≥3 independent registries or live registry specifications and composes with ADR-0023/0036/0037/0063/0088 read in full; no 2026 performance claim is involved. **High** for "not model-facing" (R-NM): it follows from ADR-0002 (iii), ADR-0019's closed-world rule and the E3 division of labour, and the disconfirming evidence (S-301/S-310) is the reason for it, not against it. **Medium-high** for the conformance contract: the split declared/probed is ratified for profiles and hosted participants and precedented by the ACP registry's CI, and every Phase 2 class already wrote its battery — but the *uniform record* is new, suite adequacy per class is unreviewed (OQ-355), and `require_conformance = probed` has an unmeasured cost. **Medium** for the namespace/authority model: MCP-registry ownership verification and Omnigent's contribution validation are precedents, signer-anchored ownership rests on H5's C1 machinery, and the cross-registry remainder of OQ-110 is open. **Medium-low** for foreign import: only two foreign registry formats were read, both young (ACP registry stabilised 2026-03; MCP registry requirements still evolving), and the loss reports will need revisiting when they change. No C0 element rests on a `provisional` number; S-024/S-092/S-301 supply shape and failure existence only.
