## 1. Scope & boundary

This workstream decides, at research grade: (i) the **boundary** between HarnessHarness and an external training stack — HarnessHarness never trains, loads, merges or serves model weights; it *exports* what a trainer needs and *imports* what a trainer produces (a model snapshot identity), and it treats the trained snapshot exactly like any other provider snapshot; (ii) the **training-stack interface contract** (exports: bundles, ledger-derived trajectory exports, reward records, harness regression suites, compatibility tags; imports: new model snapshot ids with training lineage); (iii) the **two-plasticity-rate cycle** — alternating a harness-search phase (a WS-I5 pipeline invocation) with a weight-update phase (external), with the cycle itself recorded as a lineage of ordinary experiments; (iv) the **compatibility guard** — every evolution-origin harness edit and every conditioned rule carries the model behaviours it assumes (as `snapshot_scope` on its assumption-debt record) and every imported snapshot must pass a **harness regression suite** before it binds to a sealed definition; (v) the **consolidation decision procedure** — which runtime lessons stay explicit and reversible, which may be distilled into weights through the training stack, and the only admissible proof that consolidation happened (the lesson's retirement test passes on the post-training snapshot); (vi) **safety co-evolution** — what SafeEvolve's mechanism means inside a system whose security kernel is outside the editable surface; (vii) the **maturity flag** and explicit conditionality of the whole layer.

It explicitly leaves to neighbours: the evolution pipeline's stages, gates, candidate representation and per-family adapters (**WS-I5**; ADR-0002, ADR-0046 §7, ADR-0053 D-5); the assumption-debt record schema, lifecycle, dashboards and the scheduling of removal tests (**WS-I6**; ADR-0020/0124/0126, ADR-0160); attribution methods and counterfactual validity (**WS-I7**); the experiment definition, kinds, scheduler, analysis, results rows and leaderboard rules (**WS-J3/J4/J5**; ADR-0154…0163 — *not re-decided*); the bundle format and export/import machinery (**WS-I3**; ADR-0139…0141); the gateway, served-model drift and the fingerprint probe (**WS-C1**; ADR-0118/0120); the Model Profile and its expiry (**WS-C3**; ADR-0124…0126); anti-leakage and contamination strata (**WS-I4**; ADR-0143); the reference monitor and the evolution service's handle set (**WS-H1**; ADR-0053); memory consolidation inside the memory store (**WS-D3**; ADR-0080 — a different "consolidation": versions within the store, not weights); the value-of-compute scheduler (**WS-F4**; OQ-025); every ecosystem choice (**ADR-0050**).

## 4. Findings

### 4a. Mechanism evidence (a failure mode exists / an architecture is implementable)

**M1 — Weights and harness are coupled coordinates; either can be the bottleneck.** WHALE's own reading of its results (S-085): "harness search alone sometimes matches weight-only performance with fewer rollouts, but weight updates often precede meaningful harness improvements", and "small interleaved updates outperform stagewise weight-then-harness optimization". Co-Harness (S-084) reports the same alternation from the harness-critic side. Mechanism admissible (two independent teams, an implementable loop in source); magnitude `provisional`.

**M2 — Changing the model can invalidate the harness tuned around it (compatibility break).** S-096: full-trajectory imitation of a stronger model regressed the weaker policy on all seven tasks by 4–30 points because the updated policy "adopted expert planning strategies without the competence to execute them within the harness designed for their native approach"; on-policy correction of only the failing turns preserved compatibility. Corroborated at Tier B by S-047 (a harness rule that was a fix for one snapshot became dead weight for the next) and S-049 (guardrails deleted as models improved). Mechanism admissible; it is the reason every evolution-origin edit needs a **model-behaviour assumption tag** and every imported snapshot a **harness regression suite**.

**M3 — Harness evolution can cause forgetting, independent of weights.** S-091: "harness expansion alone can degrade performance on previously solved tasks, producing harness-induced forgetting"; retention and adaptation pull apart. Consequence: the regression suite must include *retention* rows (ADR-0046 §7 already requires `retention Δ ≥ −margin`), and a consolidation step that removes an explicit lesson must show non-inferiority on the retention set, not only on the lesson's target tasks.

**M4 — Harness-updating and harness-benefit are separable, and benefit is non-monotonic in model tier.** S-088: even weak models produce competitive harness updates; whether the beneficiary *uses* them (activation, faithful following) peaks at mid-tier. Consequence: after any weight update, compliance (ADR-0014 `delivered → activated → followed`) must be re-measured per rule — a post-training snapshot may stop following a rule it previously followed, or start ignoring one it previously needed. This is also why consolidation's success proof is behavioural (the retirement test), never a training-loss number.

**M5 — The trainer needs only request–response pairs plus rewards; everything token-level is trainer-side.** Agent Lightning v1.0 (S-WS-I8-01): "the harness, rather than the training engine, owns the environment interaction loop"; the trainer "observes only sequences of LLM request-response pairs" through an endpoint proxy and solves retokenization, sample merging, advantage estimation and loss normalization itself. verl's `AgentLoopOutput` (token ids, response mask over tool-response tokens, logprobs, teacher logprobs) shows exactly the material that lives on the trainer side. Consequence: HarnessHarness's export granularity is the `model.call.*` pair (`ProviderRequestPlan` by `plan_hash` + `ModelMessage` + usage + labels) and its reward records; it never emits tokens, masks or logprobs it did not observe.

**M6 — A checkpoint must carry the identity of the harness artifact it was trained under.** SafeEvolve's `validate_sft_rl_compat.py` refuses RL when the checkpoint's `skill_bank_sha256`, `compiler_version`, `retrieval_version`, `renderer_version`, `injection_target` or `model_family` disagree with the runtime bank or the SFT data; its `cmd_commit_context` versions every accepted prompt/skill edit with a hash and a history row; its rollout metadata records the active prompt/bank hashes. WHALE hands the accepted harness to the trainer as a path and scrapes `MAX_TURNS` from harness source to configure the trainer — the untyped form of the same coupling. Consequence: HarnessHarness's import record carries `trained_under{definition, profile, compiled bundle}` refs and the export carries them per sample; a snapshot × definition pair without a compatibility record is `unknown` and stratified.

**M7 — Policy version drifts under a live trainer.** ART's `Trajectory{initial_policy_version, final_policy_version}` and verifiers' `PolicySpan{start, end}.drift` exist because a rollout under an online trainer can straddle a weight update; agent-lightning stamps `model_version` (training step) on every proxied call. HarnessHarness already records `served_model`/`snapshot_id` per call (ADR-0118/0120) — the *attached* mode of the interface must require the training stack's serving route to expose a policy version so `snapshot_span` per run is computable and drift is a ledger fact, never inferred.

**M8 — Consolidation has an operator on the trainer side (distillation), and a proof on the harness side (retirement).** On-policy distillation (tinker-cookbook `distillation/train_on_policy.py`; verl `teacher_loop`) moves behaviour from a teacher (or a teacher *harness*: the student sampled under `definition` with the lesson, scored by a teacher under the same definition) into student weights. HarnessHarness cannot observe weights; the only thing it can observe is whether `definition − R` is now non-inferior to `definition` on the post-training snapshot — the ADR-0126 removal test. Consequence: **consolidated ⇔ the lesson's retirement experiment passes on the post-training snapshot**, and the explicit form is retained (retired, never deleted) so consolidation is reversible.

**M9 — Safety co-evolution keeps the safety artifacts explicit.** SafeEvolve does not distil safety into weights and remove the harness; it evolves an explicit safety prompt and skill bank with bounded, versioned, reversible updates *and* trains the policy to use them (harness-use SFT, then harness-augmented RL with verifier-decomposed rewards). Its runtime context puts the default system policy above the evolved safety prompt above retrieved skills. In HarnessHarness terms the editable safety layer is the advisory tier (`Text` leaves, Procedures/skills, narrowing guards); Π, handles and the monitor stay MUST-code (ADR-0053 D-5). The mechanism is admissible for that tier only.

### 4b. Performance evidence (benchmark lifts) — each tagged `provisional` if 2026 single-team/preprint

- WHALE (S-085): +4.15 to +24.38 pp best-mean@8 over weight-only, harness-only and fast/slow-training baselines on Qwen3.5-2B/4B across search QA, AIME 24/25, Lichess puzzles — `provisional`; baselines are matched per condition on the paper's own budgets (`docs/reproducing.md`: 4/6/4 epochs weight-only; 40/60/40 iterations harness-only) but no matched-*total* (search + training + evaluation) accounting is reported; proposer is a frontier model (`PROPOSER_MODEL` default in `alternate.sh`), so the harness phase spends frontier-model compute not counted in the student's budget.
- Co-Harness (S-084): AIME25 56.9 % → 78.3 % for an 8B model after two rounds — `provisional`; single team; budgets unmatched; no code.
- SafeEvolve (S-086): 3× ASR reduction, utility 59.79 → 61.86 on AgentDojo, Qwen3.5-4B — `provisional`; single team; one model; code present.
- S-096: on-policy correction removes a 4–30 point regression on seven enterprise tasks (Qwen3-Coder, Gemma 4) — `provisional`; the *regression* is the usable fact, the fix's magnitude is not.
- Agent Lightning v1.0 (S-WS-I8-01): Qwen3.5-9B 41.8 % → 56.4 % on SWE-bench Verified with 6K examples — `provisional` and on a suite ADR-0146 retired for headline numbers (contaminated public).
- Continual Harness (S-WS-I8-10): Pareto-dominant on one model, below a minimalist baseline on a smaller one — `provisional`; useful only as capability-conditionality evidence.

No performance number above is load-bearing. Every recommendation below is justified by mechanism (§4a) and by ratified governance (ADR-0002/0046/0053).

## 5. Disconfirming evidence & negative results

- **Against alternating co-evolution at all (recommendation §6.3).** S-083: under matched feedback and inference budgets, harness evolution does not consistently beat simple test-time search and transfers weakly; S-075: generated harnesses trail human references and evolution is unstable; S-092: gains localise to a few control dimensions. WHALE's budgets are per-condition, not matched-total, and its proposer is a frontier model. *Why it does not overturn:* the layer is specified, not promised (ADR-0002); its acceptance criteria are matched-total `ComparisonReport`s (ADR-0041/0046) with training compute *claimed* by the trainer and stratified by cost confidence; the maturity flag is `research-grade` and the headline-claim rule (§6.8) forbids any co-evolution claim from becoming a leaderboard entry without an independent reproduction.
- **Against the boundary "HarnessHarness never trains" (§6.1).** HASE (S-WS-I8-09) shows one model editing harness components *and* solving tasks under one RL loop, and WHALE vendors the trainer next to the harness; a tighter integration could exploit token-level signals (logprobs, masks) HarnessHarness discards. *Why it does not overturn:* ADR-0006 N1/N12 and ADR-0050 §8 forbid a training runtime inside the instrument; the export loses nothing the ledger observed (loss report), and attached mode gives a trainer everything a proxy-based trainer gets today (S-WS-I8-01). The cost is that HarnessHarness cannot itself run on-policy distillation; that is the trainer's job by design.
- **Against snapshot-scoped compatibility tags (§6.4).** S-088 shows harness-updating quality is roughly tier-independent, and S-061's ablations (via doc 2 §5.8) report cross-family transfer for some edits — so scoping every evolution-origin edit to the snapshots it was evidenced on may over-expire portable edits and generate needless removal tests. *Why it does not overturn:* the scope is an *expiry trigger*, not a refusal — an edit whose `transfer` row is sign-stable over ≥ 2 families (ADR-0159 `TransferProfile`) widens its `snapshot_scope` to `family`; expiry produces `expiring`, which only schedules a removal test (ADR-0126). The cost of a needless test is bounded by WS-I6's scheduling budget.
- **Against consolidation-by-retirement (§6.5).** Passing a removal test on one suite is weak evidence that the behaviour is in the weights rather than that the suite no longer exercises it (S-091 retention/adaptation; S-492-class memorisation). A distilled behaviour may also survive only until the next weight update. *Why it does not overturn:* the procedure requires non-inferiority on the lesson's target set *and* on the retention set *and* on ≥ 1 held-out family, plus compliance re-measurement; and `retired{reason: consolidated}` keeps the explicit lesson, so a later regression re-activates it (`expired → active` on fresh evidence, ADR-0126). Consolidation is never irreversible by construction.
- **Against safety co-evolution (§6.6).** S-434 (monitorability tax) and S-415 (verification horizon): optimising a policy against monitors or fixed verifiers produces obfuscation and reward decay; SafeEvolve's rewards are verifier-decomposed and could be gamed. *Why it does not overturn:* the layer exports rewards only from deterministic or calibrated oracle classes with `veto` semantics (ADR-0047), never from reasoning-reading critics (OQ-281 answered: **never**), and the security kernel is outside the co-evolved surface, so a gamed safety prompt cannot widen authority.
- **Negative result on the evidence base:** every co-evolution study opened trains ≤ 9B open-weight models with a frontier proposer; no study reports matched-total compute including proposer spend; none reports beneficiary compliance per rule after training (S-088's measurement); none reports a harness regression suite run on the post-training policy. The spec must therefore state that the layer's *value* is unknown for frontier snapshots (which cannot be trained by the sponsor at all) — for those, only the compatibility-guard half of the layer applies (provider-side snapshot changes are the "weight update").

## 6. Recommendation for the spec

### 6.1 The boundary (confirmed and defined)

**HarnessHarness never trains, loads, merges, quantises or serves model weights**, and never holds a training run's optimiser state, datasets or checkpoints as members of its stores. A trained model exists for HarnessHarness only as a **model snapshot** (Ontology v1: provider + version identity + sampling parameters; `ModelSnapshotRecord` with `provenance = reported`) served behind a `serving_route` the gateway calls like any other (ADR-0118/0120). The training stack is an **external participant** at the model boundary, not a component class, not a plugin, not a hosted participant: it binds through (i) a lowering target for exports (ADR-0021/0141 shape) and (ii) the ordinary model-boundary contracts for serving and snapshot claims. Consequences: no IR entity, ledger event class, registry kind or `run_kind` is added for training (T-LCD-06 analogue: removing the training-stack interface leaves every native contract unchanged); nothing the trainer produces enters above `unverified`/`external` authority (ADR-0033); the trainer never receives permission *decisions*, handles or credentials (ADR-0050 D2 holds across this boundary too).

Two **modes**, both declared per experiment:
- **Export mode (offline).** HarnessHarness produces `TrainingExport` artefacts from bundles; the trainer consumes them on its own schedule and returns a snapshot claim. Nothing else crosses.
- **Attached mode (online).** The trainer's serving endpoint is a `serving_route` for a model snapshot whose `ModelDiscoveryClaim` declares `policy_version_exposed = supported`; HarnessHarness runs ordinary experiments against it, stamps the exposed policy version on every `model.call.completed` (`snapshot_id`), and publishes reward records per run over a subscription (`hh-embed/1` Group R read/subscribe; no new verb — any needed operation is proposed `experimental` via ADR-0178). The trainer joins its own proxied request–response pairs to HarnessHarness rewards by `model_call_id` / `surface_ids{response_id}`. Runs whose `snapshot_span.drift > 0` are annotated `policy_drift` and excluded from every `artifact_benefit` claim (they remain training material).

### 6.2 Interface-contract sketch (language-agnostic: operations · inputs → outputs · invariants · failure modes)

**Exports (HarnessHarness → training stack).**

- `export_training(bundle_refs[] | experiment_id, TrainingExportPolicy) → (TrainingExport: ContentAddress, LoweringLossReport, measurement.export.delivered per subject run)` — a lowering target `training_export/1` added to ADR-0141's target list (proposal). `TrainingExportPolicy{sample_unit ∈ {model_call, turn, run}, include ∈ {request_plan_by_hash, request_plan_inline, response, usage, labels, rewards, validator_verdicts, compliance_events, attribution_refs}, readers, redaction, reward_sources ⊆ OracleClass − {judge(reasoning_reading)}, split_labels ⊆ {search, dev} (never held_out/private), snapshot_filter?}`. Each `TrainingSample{sample_id, run_id, model_call_id, role: ModelRole, snapshot_ref: ModelSnapshotRecord, snapshot_span, definition{semantic_id, version_id}, profile{semantic_id, version_id}, compiled bundle_id, plan_hash (+ plan bytes if inline), response: ModelMessage (redacted per policy), usage, context_label, rewards[]: RewardRecord, outcome_class, task{task_id, split_label, contamination.stratum}, compliance[] (delivered/activated/followed per artefact id), attribution_refs[]?}`. Invariants: **E-1** every sample resolves to ledger events by id (rebuildable: `project(run, training_export)` equals the export — an ADR-0026 view with `derived_from`); **E-2** no sample carries a `held_out`/`private` task or any held-out surface (ADR-0143 L1/L3 — `HeldOutInExport` refusal); **E-3** rewards are `RewardRecord{value, oracle_class, validator_ref, verdict_ref, veto_tripped, charged_to = instrument, provenance}` derived only from admissible oracle classes — `judge` verdicts included only with a `CalibrationRecord` in date and `reasoning_reading = false` (OQ-281 answer); **E-4** the export is a lowering with a typed loss report (T-LCD-11): `no_slot` for permission decisions, handles, Π, secrets; `narrowed` for redacted content; `truncated` for offloaded blobs beyond the policy's size; **E-5** the trainer never receives tokens, masks or logprobs HarnessHarness did not observe (`n/a`, never fabricated); **E-6** `TrainingExport` records `harness_constraints{max_turns, budgets, stop rules, tool surface hashes}` as typed data derived from the sealed definition and compiled bundle (the WHALE `MAX_TURNS` scrape, typed); **E-7** every exported task id is appended to a `TrainingExposureRecord{export_id, task_ids[], split_hash, snapshot_lineage}` so WS-J4 can later flag `trained_on` overlap for the produced snapshot (proposal: contamination stratum value `trained_on`, dialect bump — CF). Failures: `HeldOutInExport`, `ReaderViolation`, `InadmissibleRewardSource`, `UnexpressibleMember`, `BundleNotValid` (exports only from `validate_bundle.status = valid`).
- `export_regression_suite(definition_ref, snapshot_in, split, policy) → HarnessRegressionSuite` — a pre-registered `ExperimentSpec` (ADR-0154; `kind: comparative`, `design: paired`, factor `model_snapshot ∈ {snapshot_in, snapshot_out?}` with `snapshot_out` left as an unbound level to be filled at import) over the sealed definition, the profile chain and the declared `retention` set, `MatchSpec{matched_cap, same eval_budget, cold_start}`, primary metrics = the definition's headline metrics + every `veto` metric + per-rule compliance (`followed` rate per conditioned rule, ADR-0125), `PreRegistration` with non-inferiority margins. It is exported as a bundle (`kind = experiment`, `status = assembled`) so the trainer can run it itself *or* hand the snapshot back for HarnessHarness to run it. Invariant **R-1**: the suite's task hashes predate the export it accompanies (`LeakedSplit` otherwise) and are disjoint from `TrainingExposureRecord.task_ids`.
- `export_compatibility_tags(definition_ref) → CompatibilityTagSet` — for every conditioned rule (`HarnessRule.conditioned_on`, `ProfileRule`) and every evolution-origin `HirDiff` in the definition's lineage: `{rule_id | diff_id, debt_record_ref, snapshot_scope[], hypothesis, expiry_condition, removal_test_ref}`. This is a read-only projection of assumption-debt records (ADR-0020 decision 4; ADR-0124), so the trainer knows which harness behaviours are hypotheses about the model it is about to change.

**Imports (training stack → HarnessHarness).**

- `import_snapshot(SnapshotClaim) → (ModelSnapshotRecord{pinned = false, provenance = reported}, CompatibilityRecord[] with status = unknown | trained_under, registry lifecycle event)`. `SnapshotClaim{provider (the trainer as a provider id), model_id, snapshot_id (training step or artefact digest as claimed), serving_route?, base_snapshot_ref, training_lineage{training_run_ref, step, data_refs[]: TrainingExport ContentAddress[], method ∈ {sft, rejection_sampling, rl, on_policy_distillation, other}, compute_claim{…, confidence = reported}}, trained_under[]: {definition semantic_id, profile semantic_id, bundle_id} (derived by HarnessHarness from `data_refs` when they are its own exports; otherwise a claim), training_cutoff_claim?, weights_digest?: foreign}`. Invariants: **I-1** a claim never pins: `pinned` becomes true only after `probe_profile` + fingerprint probe (ADR-0120 decision 5) produce records; **I-2** `weights_digest` is a `ForeignRef` claim, never a content address (HarnessHarness has no bytes); **I-3** every `(snapshot, definition)` pair the claim names gets a `CompatibilityRecord{snapshot_ref, definition semantic_id, profile semantic_id, status ∈ {trained_under, verified, drifted, broken, unknown}, evidence_ref?, regression_suite_ref?, created_at, expiry_condition}` — `trained_under` for pairs in `trained_under[]`, `unknown` for every other sealed definition in the registry; **I-4** the import appends `TrainingExposureRecord` links so contamination annotation is derivable. Failures: `ClaimSchemaInvalid`, `BaseSnapshotUnknown`, `ServingRouteUnreachable` (attached mode only), `LineageCycle`.
- `run_regression_suite(snapshot_ref, HarnessRegressionSuite) → ComparisonReport + CompatibilityRecord update` — binds `snapshot_out := snapshot_ref` into the suite's unbound level and executes it as an ordinary experiment (`run_kind = experiment`; ADR-0155 scheduler; charged `instrument`). Verdicts: every headline and veto metric non-inferior within margin and no veto tripped ⇒ `verified`; any veto or headline regression beyond margin ⇒ `broken{report_ref}`; margin met on headline but ≥ 1 conditioned rule's `followed` rate dropped beyond its margin ⇒ `drifted{rules[]}` and those rules become `expiring` (ADR-0126 trigger `model_version_change`, observable (i) served-model change). **This is "post-training includes harness regression suites."** A `broken` pair is refused for `origin = evolution` arms (the harness must be re-searched or the snapshot re-trained); a `drifted` pair runs with its rules `expiring` and the row annotated; an `unknown` pair runs only under `Design.allow_unverified_snapshot = true` with rows annotated `compatibility = unknown` and excluded from headline claims.

### 6.3 The two-plasticity-rate cycle

`CoEvolutionCycleRecord{cycle_id = version_id, lineage_ref (bundle kind = lineage), policy: CyclePolicy, phases[]: [{phase ∈ {harness_search, weight_update}, index, inputs{definition_in, snapshot_in}, outputs{definition_out?, snapshot_out?}, experiment_refs[] | training_run_ref, budgets{search: SearchBudgetRecord, eval: eval_budget, training: compute_claim}, verdict}], stop_reason ∈ {budget_exhausted, max_cycles, patience, broken_compatibility, operator}}` — a sidecar record (ADR-0139 S-layer shape) attached to the lineage bundle; **no new `run_kind`, registry kind or design kind** (ADR-0183 §C, ADR-0151, ADR-0154 not re-decided). `CyclePolicy{weight_phase_budget: compute_claim shape (external), harness_phase_budget: SearchBudgetRecord, switch_rule ∈ {fixed{E, I}, patience{min_iters, patience, max_iters}}, max_cycles, retention_set_ref, regression_suite_policy}` is MUST-data (WHALE's `ROUND_STEPS/ROUND_MH_ITERS/MH_MIN_ITERS/MH_PATIENCE/MH_MAX_ITERS/MAX_ROUNDS`, typed).

Phase semantics: the **harness_search** phase is exactly one WS-I5 **campaign** (`EvolutionCampaignSpec` with `model_levels` = `snapshot_in` for S3–S5 and the held-out families of S6; `base = definition_in`); its output is the campaign's sealed lineage head `definition_out` reached by `apply(base, HirDiff)` (ADR-0147 M-2; WS-I5 S8/S9) whose `derived-from.trajectories[]` pin `snapshot_in`; every conditioned rule it introduces gets `scope.model_selectors ∋ exact(snapshot_in)` (WS-I5 G4 record + §6.4 above). The **weight_update** phase is opaque: `export_training(definition_out, snapshot_in)` → trainer → `import_snapshot` → `run_regression_suite(snapshot_out, suite(definition_out, snapshot_in))`. The cycle continues only if the pair `(snapshot_out, definition_out)` is `verified` or `drifted`; on `broken` the policy chooses `re_search` (a harness phase under `snapshot_out` starting from `definition_out`, the WHALE order) or `rollback` (discard `snapshot_out`; never deletes — `CompatibilityRecord{broken}` and the snapshot record stay). The cycle's C4 claim ("co-evolution vs weight-only vs harness-only") is a three-arm `ComparisonReport` at `MatchSpec{matched_total}` over search + evaluation + *claimed* training compute with cost `confidence = reported` for the training dimension and a Pareto-with-origin view (ADR-0041); the analysis engine refuses to rank it above `reported` confidence strata.

### 6.4 Compatibility-guard contract

- **Tag = assumption-debt record + snapshot scope.** No new record and no new field: the *snapshot scope* of a rule is WS-I6's additive `scope.model_selectors: [Selector]` (WS-I6 §6.2) with ADR-0124 `Pattern.exact(snapshot id)` entries for snapshot-level scope and `range(family, …)`/`prefix` for family scope. Default for `origin = evolution`: the snapshots pinned by the run manifests of the introducing `HirDiff`'s `derived-from.trajectories[]` (derivable — no `snapshot_refs` field is needed on the edge) plus WS-I5's `FailureHypothesis.model_scope`; default for human-authored conditioned rules: the bound profile's selector (OQ-WS-I8-05). `expiry_condition ∋ model_version_change` and `scope.model_selectors ≠ ∅` are **mandatory** for every evolution-origin conditioned rule (WS-I5 G4 already requires the record; `validate_removal_test` at `seal` — WS-I6 D-1 — is where `missing_snapshot_scope` is refused). A `transfer` `ComparisonReport` sign-stable over ≥ 2 families (ADR-0159; WS-I5 S6 `TransferProfile`) widens the scope from `exact` to `range(family)`.
- **Guard at bind.** `resolve`/`seal` of a run manifest binding `(snapshot, definition)` consults the `CompatibilityRecord`: `verified | trained_under` ⇒ proceed; `drifted` ⇒ proceed, rules named `expiring`, row annotated; `unknown` ⇒ proceed only under `allow_unverified_snapshot`; `broken` ⇒ refuse for `origin = evolution` and Lab headline arms, allow for `exploratory`. The guard reads records only (ADR-0053: monitor-style purity) and is C4 code with C0 data.
- **Guard at run.** Served-model change mid-run (ADR-0120 decision 4) or `snapshot_span.drift > 0` ⇒ `policy_drift` annotation; `configuration_drift` rows excluded from `artifact_benefit` (ADR-0161 `comparable = false` rule not re-decided — the exclusion is an analysis-side filter).
- **Guard after import (the regression suite).** §6.2 `run_regression_suite`. Additionally the *reverse* sweep: for every definition with `CompatibilityRecord{unknown}` against a newly imported snapshot that the Lab intends to use, WS-I6's manager schedules removal tests for rules whose `snapshot_scope ∌ snapshot_out` — the S-047 "dead weight" detection made routine.
- **Provider-side snapshot changes are weight updates too.** A `DRIFT` fingerprint or `compatibility_token` change (ADR-0126 observables) on a provider snapshot creates a synthetic `SnapshotClaim{provider, snapshot_id = observed}` and the same guard runs. This is the only half of the layer that applies to frontier models the sponsor cannot train; it must be built first (§6.9).

### 6.5 Consolidation decision procedure (what stays explicit/reversible vs distilled)

A **lesson** is any evolution- or human-origin explicit artefact on the ADR-0024 ladder: a `Text` leaf, a conditioned `HarnessRule`/`ProfileRule`, a `Procedure`/skill, a memory version, a variant selection. Consolidation is the *trainer's* act of moving the lesson's effect into weights; HarnessHarness's part is deciding candidacy, exporting, and proving/recording the outcome.

**Never consolidate (stays explicit by rule):** (N1) anything MUST-code or a kernel invariant — permissions, Π, budgets, effect model, validators/oracles, idempotency, containment (ADR-0024 decision 1; ADR-0053) — distilling a validator into the policy is reward hacking by construction (S-415); (N2) anything whose value is *informational and volatile* — memories with dependency stamps, task/repo facts, catalog snapshots, pricing — provenance-bearing content changes faster than weights and must stay revocable (ADR-0037/0081); (N3) safety artefacts (M9): the explicit safety layer is evolved and trained-to-use, never removed by training; (N4) anything with `authority_delta ≠ none` or that endorses; (N5) hosted-participant artefacts (opaque).

**Candidate for consolidation** only if all of: (C1) status `active` for ≥ `k_cycles` (MUST-data, default 3) with the removal test *failing* on the current snapshot (the lesson is still doing work); (C2) `transfer` sign-stable over ≥ 2 task families and ≥ 2 snapshots or families (ADR-0159); (C3) the lesson is *behavioural* — a `Procedure`, an instruction `Text` leaf, a conditioned rule of kind `interaction_mode`/`compaction_reminder`/`description_template`-class, a control-strategy nudge — i.e. rungs "typed Text leaf … registered variant" of the ladder, not "Core invariant"; (C4) an economics test: expected recurring cost of keeping it explicit (its share of `opacity_ratio` × tokens per run × forecast runs over the snapshot's expected life) exceeds the trainer's claimed consolidation cost by a MUST-data factor, or the lesson's compliance (`followed` rate) is below a threshold on the target snapshot (S-088: the lesson is valid but not followed — a case where weights may do what context cannot); (C5) no veto metric depends on the lesson being explicit (an ADR-0047 `veto` invariant referencing it).

**Procedure.** Inputs come from WS-I5's `consolidation_candidates` view ("accepted edits with stable transfer" — WS-I5 §6.7) filtered by C1–C5 and N1–N5. `propose_consolidation(lesson_ids[], snapshot_in) → ConsolidationProposal{lessons[], export: TrainingExport (samples generated under `definition` with the lessons, on `search`/`dev` splits only), regression_suite: HarnessRegressionSuite (retention set + lesson target set + ≥ 1 held-out family), removal_designs[] (one `retirement` experiment per lesson, ADR-0156, arms `definition_out` vs `definition_out − R` on `snapshot_out`), pre_registration}` → trainer → `import_snapshot` → `run_regression_suite` → for each lesson `run retirement experiment on snapshot_out`. **Verdict per lesson:** `consolidated` iff the retirement experiment is non-inferior on the target set *and* the retention set *and* the held-out family within pre-registered margins, no veto tripped, and the lesson's per-rule compliance is `n/a{not_delivered}` in the removal arm (i.e. nothing else delivers it). The import of `snapshot_out` fires WS-I6's **consolidation hook** (it *is* a `model_version_change`; no enum growth), the lesson turns `expiring`, and WS-I6's `settle_removal_test` records a `RemovalVerdict{pass}` on the consolidated beneficiary; retirement is then WS-I6's human-sealed `retire(debt_ref, diff, RetirementRecord{…, rationale})` with `supersedes{reason: expiry}` (ADR-0037 S2) — the `RetirementRecord.rationale` names the `ConsolidationReport` and `snapshot_ref` ("absorbed into weights"); no new `supersedes.reason` value. `partially_consolidated` (target passes, retention or family fails) ⇒ the lesson stays `active` with `scope.model_selectors` narrowed and the report attached as a `comparison_report` evidence ref; `not_consolidated` ⇒ `revalidated` (WS-I6 D-5: a `fail` verdict raises the grade). **Reversibility:** a retired lesson remains a registry record with its full lineage; a later `broken`/`drifted` compatibility record on a newer snapshot for a definition that previously carried it re-enters it as a candidate `expired → active` on fresh evidence (ADR-0126) — reactivation is one `HirDiff` (`AddNode` of the retired version) away. Consolidation therefore changes what is *delivered*, never what is *known*.

### 6.6 Safety co-evolution (SafeEvolve, translated)

- **Surface.** The co-evolvable safety layer = `Text` leaves in profile-owned slots at `min_authority ≤ definition` rendered as safety instructions, safety `Procedure`s/skills (ADR-0084/0086), narrowing-only guards (`GuardVerdict` never `allow` — ADR-0181/CF-121), and narrowing `HarnessRule`s. Π, handles, flow policies, the credential broker and containment are outside it (ADR-0053 D-5; ADR-0182 X6). An evolution-origin safety edit is an ordinary `HirDiff` with `authority_delta ∈ {none, narrowing}`.
- **Rewards exported for safety training** come only from H-track deterministic detectors — LT-01…12 outcomes (ADR-0059), injection-outcome oracles, egress/permission veto metrics — plus utility oracles; each is a `RewardRecord` with `veto_tripped` explicit so a trainer can decompose (SafeEvolve's `R = 0.25 U + 0.50 S + 0.25 U·S` is a trainer-side profile over these records; HarnessHarness exports the components, never a blended scalar as truth).
- **Harness-use SFT is a compatibility act.** SafeEvolve trains the policy to *use* the evolved artefacts; in HarnessHarness terms the produced snapshot's `trained_under` names the definition and its compiled bundle, and its regression suite must include the per-rule compliance metrics of the safety rules (a safety rule that is valid but no longer *followed* after training is `drifted`, not fine).
- **Monitorability rule (OQ-281 answer for I8).** Verdicts of any critic with `evidence_mode` reading reasoning content are never exported as rewards and never enter `RewardRecord`; they remain instrument diagnostics. Exporting them is `InadmissibleRewardSource`.
- **Bounded, auditable, reversible.** Every accepted safety edit is a sealed definition version with a `CoEvolutionCycleRecord` phase row (SafeEvolve `cmd_commit_context` typed): rollback is `apply(base, invert(diff))` (ADR-0017); audit is the ledger.

### 6.7 Data-model sketch

`TrainingExport` · `TrainingSample` · `RewardRecord{value, oracle_class, validator_ref, verdict_ref, veto_tripped, charged_to, provenance}` · `TrainingExportPolicy` · `TrainingExposureRecord{export_id, task_ids[], split_hash, snapshot_lineage}` · `HarnessRegressionSuite` (= `ExperimentSpec` + unbound `snapshot_out` level + `retention_set_ref` + per-rule compliance metrics) · `CompatibilityTagSet` · `SnapshotClaim{…, training_lineage, trained_under[]}` · `ModelSnapshotRecord` (existing, WS-L4/C1) · `CompatibilityRecord{snapshot_ref, definition semantic_id, profile semantic_id, status, evidence_ref?, regression_suite_ref?, expiry_condition}` · snapshot scope as a usage of WS-I6's `AssumptionDebtRecord.scope.model_selectors` (no new field) · `CoEvolutionCycleRecord` + `CyclePolicy` · `ConsolidationProposal` + `ConsolidationReport{lesson_id, verdict ∈ {consolidated, partially_consolidated, not_consolidated}, target_report, retention_report, family_report, compliance_delta}` · `snapshot_span{first, last, drift}` per run (derived view over `model.call.completed.snapshot_id`) · maturity flag `maturity ∈ {instrument-grade, research-grade}` (WS-I5 §6.13 vocabulary, adopted) on the spec item and on every operation above (`research-grade`; results labelled `preview` per WS-I5's ADR-0046 P3 rule). All records are content-addressed under `idp/1`, carry `ProvenanceRecord`s, and are stored as bundle sidecars / registry records of existing kinds (`report`-class records; no new `registry/1` kind — the exact kind mapping is a synthesis question, OQ below).

### 6.8 Acceptance-criteria sketch (checkable without training a model)

A **null trainer** fixture — a conformant training stack that returns the *same* weights under a new `snapshot_id` and, in attached mode, serves the same provider snapshot with a synthetic policy version counter — makes every criterion executable at Stage 6 without GPUs:

- **AC-I8-1 Export is a view.** `export_training` on a golden `run` bundle equals `project(run, training_export)` byte-for-byte (E-1); rerun is deterministic; the loss report lists `no_slot` for permission decisions/handles/Π and nothing else for an L2-complete bundle.
- **AC-I8-2 No leakage.** An export of an experiment with `held_out` rows refuses (`HeldOutInExport`); the accompanying regression suite's task hashes are disjoint from `TrainingExposureRecord.task_ids` and predate the export (R-1).
- **AC-I8-3 Reward admissibility.** A bundle with a reasoning-reading critic verdict exports it as diagnostic only; requesting it as a reward source refuses (`InadmissibleRewardSource`); every exported `RewardRecord` names a validator version and `veto_tripped`.
- **AC-I8-4 Import never pins.** A `SnapshotClaim` yields `pinned = false`; `CompatibilityRecord{trained_under}` for named pairs and `unknown` for every other sealed definition; `weights_digest` is a `foreign` claim.
- **AC-I8-5 Regression suite verdicts.** With the null trainer, `run_regression_suite` returns `verified` (paired non-inferiority against itself within margin); with a fixture that swaps in the *second* C0 minimal profile's model (T-LCD-04's two profiles), the suite returns `drifted` naming the rules whose `followed` rate moved, and those rules read `expiring` in the registry; with a fixture that trips one veto metric, `broken` — and an `origin = evolution` arm binding that pair is refused at `seal`.
- **AC-I8-6 Guard at bind.** `resolve` of a manifest binding `(unknown snapshot, definition)` refuses unless `allow_unverified_snapshot`; rows produced under it carry `compatibility = unknown` and never enter a `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind ≠ exploratory}`.
- **AC-I8-7 Policy drift is a ledger fact.** In attached mode with the null trainer bumping its policy counter mid-run, the run's `snapshot_span.drift = 1`, the row is annotated `policy_drift`, and the analysis engine excludes it from `artifact_benefit`.
- **AC-I8-8 Cycle record and budgets.** A two-cycle run under `CyclePolicy{fixed{E, I}}` with the null trainer produces a `CoEvolutionCycleRecord` whose harness phases are WS-I5 pipeline runs with `SearchBudgetRecord`s and whose weight phases carry `compute_claim{confidence = reported}`; the three-arm claim renders only in the `reported` stratum with `budget_match.status` shown (T-LCD-14).
- **AC-I8-9 Consolidation proof shape.** On a synthetic definition where lesson R is a `Text` leaf whose removal is *known* to regress under snapshot A (a `retirement` experiment fixture with a planted effect) and *known* not to under snapshot B (the second profile fixture), `propose_consolidation` + null trainer + `run retirement on B` yields `consolidated` only when target, retention and family reports all pass; on the planted-retention-regression fixture it yields `partially_consolidated`; `retired{reason: consolidated}` requires the ADR act and leaves the record retrievable; reactivation is one `HirDiff`.
- **AC-I8-10 Provider drift path.** A fingerprint `DRIFT` record on a provider snapshot creates a synthetic claim and runs AC-I8-5's path without any trainer present.
- **AC-I8-11 Removability.** Building without the training-stack interface leaves every C0–C3 acceptance suite unchanged; no IR field, event class, `run_kind` or registry kind references it (T-LCD-06 analogue; ADR-0182 X2/X4 removability builds).
- **AC-I8-12 Debt completeness.** WS-I6's `validate_removal_test` at `seal` refuses an evolution-origin conditioned rule whose record lacks `scope.model_selectors` or the `model_version_change` expiry (`missing_snapshot_scope`, a WS-I6 `UnexecutableRemovalTest` reason).

### 6.9 Build-stage suggestion

- **C0/Stage 1 (data only, no behaviour):** the `exact(snapshot id)` usage of WS-I6's `scope.model_selectors` documented in the debt schema; `CompatibilityRecord` schema; `SnapshotClaim` schema as an ADR-0120 `ModelDiscoveryClaim` extension (`training_lineage`, `policy_version_exposed` capability). Cost: schema rows.
- **C1/Stage 5 (profiles):** the **provider-drift half** of the guard — synthetic claims from `DRIFT`/`compatibility_token` observables, `run_regression_suite` as a `comparative` paired experiment with model as the factor, `drifted` ⇒ `expiring` wiring into WS-I6's removal-test scheduling, `snapshot_span` view and `policy_drift` annotation. This half needs no trainer and serves frontier snapshots; it is the highest-value, lowest-risk piece.
- **C4/Stage 6 (evolution, after AT-H1-05 and Stage 3):** `export_training`/`training_export/1` target with loss report, `export_regression_suite`, `export_compatibility_tags`, `import_snapshot`, guard at bind, `CoEvolutionCycleRecord`/`CyclePolicy` orchestration over WS-I5, `propose_consolidation` and `ConsolidationReport`, the null-trainer conformance fixture (AC-I8-1…12), attached-mode reward subscription (proposed `experimental` under ADR-0178).
- **Deferred / research-grade (no stage):** any operation that would require HarnessHarness to run distillation, choose training hyper-parameters, or read weights; any claim of value on frontier snapshots; `switch_rule` learning (OQ-025 with WS-F4).
- **Host-ecosystem requirements (ADR-0050 §8(a), property form):** none beyond the kernel's canonical form and content-addressed store; the training stack may be in any ecosystem (ADR-0050 D4 applies).

### 6.10 T-LCD statement and ADR-0024 rungs

## 10. Confidence

**Medium-low** for the *value* of alternating co-evolution (every performance number is `provisional`, single-team, ≤ 9B open-weight models, unmatched-total budgets, frontier proposers); **medium** for the *mechanisms* (the compatibility break is corroborated by two Tier-B production accounts and one preprint with a code-level precedent for compatibility tags; the boundary is corroborated by four independent trainer codebases converging on request–response pairs plus rewards plus a policy version); **high** that the recommended contracts are implementable and checkable without training (every acceptance criterion runs against a null trainer and existing Phase 1–3 operations).
