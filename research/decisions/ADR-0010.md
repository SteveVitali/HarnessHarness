# ADR-0010 — Scope change: external-harness hosting (R-2.10.6) moves from Could to Should; cross-participant comparison is delivered in two steps

**Status:** ratified (authored by Phase 0 synthesis, 2026-09-09)
**Owner workstream(s):** synthesis (on behalf of WS-A1, WS-L7); consumers WS-J6, WS-J4, WS-I4, WS-K4, WS-L2 · **Phase:** 0 · **Related:** ADR-0001, ADR-0003, ADR-0005, ADR-0006; CF-014; OQ-004, OQ-030, OQ-033; R-2.10.6, R-2.10.4, R-2.9.4

## Context

The sponsor's brief and doc 3 §1.1 headline the ability to "compare everyone's" harness. ADR-0001 makes hosting first-class but secondary; scope.md seeded R-2.10.6 (external-harness hosting / Hosting ABI) at tier C2 with the default MoSCoW for C2, **Could**. WS-L7 flagged (CF-014) that a reader of the spec could take the headline capability to be optional. WS-A1 (ADR-0005) established that hosting is cheap and well-understood (Harbor S-132, HAL S-134, Inspect S-136, Omnigent S-118 all do it), that its C0 prerequisites are small (participant descriptor, `observability_level`, `MetricDeclaration` — Stage 1), and that interoperating with existing planes is how the reference runtime gets external validity. Every scope change must be an ADR (LEDGER binding constraints).

## Options considered

1. **Keep Could; rely on ADR-0001's "first-class" wording.** Rejected: MoSCoW is what `decompose-spec` and the readiness report read; "first-class but Could" is contradictory to a reader.
2. **Raise the tier to C1.** Rejected: hosting depends on the eval backbone (Stage 3) and the participant contract; ADR-0001 (e) places it after the native core for good reason; C-tier expresses dependency, MoSCoW expresses priority.
3. **Keep tier C2, raise MoSCoW to Should, and state the two-step delivery in Spec §1 (chosen).**

## Decision

- R-2.10.6 MoSCoW: **Could → Should**. Tier stays **C2**; ADR-0001's "first-class but strictly secondary" stance is unchanged; the Canonical Spec is not readiness-blocked on integrating any specific external harness (ADR-0001 consequences).
- Spec §1 states: *cross-participant comparison is delivered in two steps — native-vs-native comparison at Stage 3 (evaluation-first), hosted participants joining the same plane at Stage 4 via the Hosting ABI, session-ABI mechanism first (ADR-0005).*
- The C0/Stage-1 prerequisites that make the plane class-aware from the start (participant descriptor, `participant_class` and `observability_level` stamps on ledger events, `MetricDeclaration`) are **Must** by inheritance from their owning C0 items (R-2.2.1, R-2.9.2) and are not separately re-scoped.
- No other MoSCoW value changes in Phase 0.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: hosting is a mature mechanism with four independent implementations and three hosting mechanisms (ADR-0004/0005; S-132, S-134/S-135 [Tier A], S-136/S-137, S-118, S-062 `provisional (mech)`); the sponsor's stated priority (doc 3 §1.1) is the demand-side mechanism.
- (b) Source-code precedent: Harbor `src/harbor/agents/installed/` + `bridges/base.py`; Inspect `agent/_bridge/bridge.py`; Omnigent `omnigent/inner/executor.py`. Precedent exists; the priority decision itself is program-specific (`no-precedent / novel` is not applicable to a MoSCoW change).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) doc 2 §11 and CF-002 counsel a small rigorous core first — respected: tier and stage unchanged, only priority; (ii) S-083/S-092 suggest harness benefit is localized and budget-conditional, so joint native/hosted science may matter less than component science — accepted; Should, not Must; (iii) supporting three mechanisms is surface area — mitigated by ADR-0005's ordering (session-ABI first) and by mechanisms being C2 extensions behind one Core participant contract.
- (d) Conditionality: holds for any model tier and task class; assumes external harnesses keep exposing at least one of the three hosting mechanisms (ADR-0001/0005 conditionality).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: R-2.10.6 stays C2 / Stage 4; prerequisites C0 / Stage 1.

## Consequences

- scope.md R-2.10.6 row and scope-change log updated (this ADR). MoSCoW freeze at Phase 5 may revisit.
- WS-J6 (Phase 3) designs the Hosting ABI knowing it is Should, not optional; WS-I4 integrates Harbor/Inspect-hosted environments as first-class environment providers (N13, ADR-0005); WS-K4 keeps the "act as a participant" obligation (OQ-032 resolved).
- The readiness report lists R-2.10.6 under Should and checks the two-step wording in Spec §1.

## Reversibility

High. A MoSCoW value is a single register cell and a sentence in Spec §1; reverting costs one ADR before the Phase 5 freeze.
