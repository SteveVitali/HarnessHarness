# WS-E5-adr-2 — Effect capture and attribution: a closed `CaptureItem` sum, the content-addressed `EffectCaptureManifest` with explicit completeness and source, a kernel-minted attribution token, redaction inside the capture path, and `attribution_completeness` as a class-scoped veto metric

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-E5 (consumers: WS-H4, WS-H3, WS-H6, WS-B4, WS-B5, WS-G2, WS-I1, WS-I2, WS-J5, WS-A3, WS-D1) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** WS-E5-adr-1; ADR-0026/0029 (ephemeral events, offload, canonical form), ADR-0027 (structural ids; `effect_id`), ADR-0030 (`observed` payload: `output_artifact_ids[]`, `detached_effect_ids[]`, `postcondition_results[]`), ADR-0033/0034/0035 (provenance on content-bearing events; `kernel` deriver; monitor reads records), ADR-0036 (content addresses), ADR-0039/0043 (dimensions; M-points), ADR-0044 (L3 ephemeral), ADR-0045 (veto metrics, `n/a`), ADR-0016 (`Observation` authority never exceeds its source); WS-H4-adr-2 (`security.egress.decided` by `effect_id`; attribution token slot), WS-H3-adr-1 (SV-2/SV-3 redaction-at-source), WS-H6-adr-1 (content-free envelope; producer rule), WS-E1 §6.2 (`observation_contract`; derived result authority); CF-WS-E5-02, CF-WS-H4-04, OQ-118, OQ-WS-E5-01, OQ-WS-E5-02, OQ-WS-E5-05; R-2.5.5

## Context

Doc 2 §11 Stage 1: "make every side effect attributable to a run and tool call"; §11 canonical additions: "every tool result should identify the tool/version/environment". ADR-0030 requires executors to report `detached_effect_ids[]` and `postcondition_results[]` and WS-B1 requires large payloads offloaded by content address. WS-H4 fixed egress decisions keyed by `effect_id` and named an *attribution token*; WS-H3 requires every observation masked before it is model-visible and names it a WS-E5 obligation. The audit (dossier §4a F2, F3, F8, F11) found that executors self-report facts the kernel cannot vouch for (Codex `sandbox_type: Option`, heuristic sandbox-denial detection, bubblewrap's absent violation reporting, Landlock's audit-gated logging), that attribution in production is a telemetry field rather than a verified binding (Codex `ExecMetadata.tool_call_id` "not authorization"), that retained output is capped silently in the durable record, and that observation masking lives inside a tool implementation (OpenHands `_mask_observation`) where a hostile executor could skip it. No audited system measures how much of what it observed it could attribute.

## Options considered

1. **Executor-reported observation only** (the tool returns a result; the runtime logs it — every audited harness). *Rejected:* attribution is asserted by untrusted code; mediators' decisions (egress, credentials) are not linked to the effect; completeness is unknown and unreported.
2. **Full syscall/IO tracing of the environment as the capture source** (strace/eBPF-class observation). *Rejected at C0:* backend-specific, expensive, unavailable on several backends (macOS Seatbelt, Windows), and still blind to unmediated channels; admitted later as an additional `helper_observed` source with the same item types.
3. **Attribution by process identity only** (pid/cgroup → effect). *Rejected as the sole mechanism:* pids are OS-local and reused; grandchildren escape; hosted mechanisms have none. Admitted as one delivery channel for the token (OQ-WS-E5-01).
4. **A closed capture-item sum from multiple *sources* (executor, helper, mediators, kernel derivation) stamped by the kernel with the effect identity resolved from a kernel-minted attribution token, sealed into a content-addressed manifest with explicit completeness, with redaction in the kernel's capture path and attribution completeness measured as a veto metric (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`CaptureItem`** `= {run_id, turn_id, model_call_id, tool_call_id, effect_id, attempt_no, execution_id, seq, ts_mono, source ∈ {executor_reported, helper_observed, mediator_observed, kernel_derived}, kind, payload, provenance}` with `kind` the closed sum `output_chunk (ephemeral) | progress (ephemeral) | fs_change | process{spawned | exited | signalled | detached} | egress{decision_ref} | credential_use{used_ref} | containment_violation{violated_ref} | resource_usage{dimension, amount, measured_at} | world_changed{scope, evidence_ref} | structured_result{schema_ref, value_ref} | terminal{status, exit_status?, executor_outcome_hint, retryable_hint?: false, detail: Text(external)}`. Extension is by dialect bump (ADR-0015), never `ext`. The kernel stamps the five structural ids; executors may not set them.
2. **`EffectCaptureManifest`** `= {effect_id, attempt_no, execution_id, items: [CaptureItem − ephemeral kinds], raw_output_ref?, truncation?: {omitted_bytes, original_size, policy_ref}, completeness ∈ {complete, partial{reasons ⊆ {truncated, unobserved_channel(kind), drain_timeout, executor_unreported}}, unknown}, observers_present: set<CaptureKind>, residual_channels_ref}` — a content-addressed blob under `idp/1`; `action.effect.observed` carries `capture_manifest_ref` and `completeness` inline (audit fields, H6 Rule C). Ephemeral chunks are excluded from the hash so identical effects hash identically regardless of chunking. `completeness = complete` is admissible only when every capture kind the effect's `EffectClass.domain` implies has an observer present and the environment's `residual_channels = ∅` for that domain.
3. **Attribution token.** `mint_attribution(run_id, effect_id, attempt_no, env_handle) → AttributionToken` at `prepare`: opaque, ≥ 128 bits entropy, single-use per attempt, expiring at the effect's terminal event, stored only as `token_hash`; delivered to the child by the helper (environment slot, bridged-channel peer credential, or process label — backend-specific, OQ-WS-E5-01). `resolve_attribution(token) → {effect_id, attempt_no} | Unknown`. Consumers: the egress mediator and the credential broker **refuse** on `Unknown` (H4 AC-H4-06; H3 SV-8); the helper's fs/process observers record `unattributed`. The token is minted by the **kernel** (one minter; CF-WS-E5-02 against WS-H4's "minted by the executor"); hosted `container-installed` mechanisms have none and their capture metrics render `n/a{observability}`.
4. **Unattributed signals.** An observed signal that resolves to no effect is `action.effect.unattributed{signal_kind, evidence_ref}` plus `security.containment.violated{kind: unattributed}`; it is audit-grade, escalated at run end (H6 `lifecycle.escalation.raised`), and counts against `attribution_completeness`.
5. **Redaction placement.** The kernel's capture path runs WS-H3 `redact` (a `kernel` deriver, ADR-0034 P1) on every `CaptureItem` **before** it is streamed on `subscribe`, appended, offloaded or rendered; executors never append. A canary hit in any item raises `security.secret.leak_detected` and the `secret_leak` veto (LT-03). Result authority is stamped by the kernel per WS-E1 §6.2 (`environment` only for closed-world, closed-schema, native-or-pinned tools; else `external`; lifted `unverified`) — the executor's report cannot raise it (ADR-0016 invariant).
6. **Metrics.** `MetricDeclaration{name = attribution_completeness, dimension = security, level = run, value_type = ratio | partial, requires_observability = {ledger}, applies_to_classes = {native}, detector_classes_allowed = {deterministic}, veto = true, charged_to = instrument}` computed as attributed structural signals ÷ observed structural signals, with `partial{reasons}` when any observer is absent (never coerced to 1); `capture.observer_coverage{env_class}`, `unattributed_signals`, `truncation_rate`, `harness_overhead.execution_ms` as class-scoped declarations (ADR-0044/0045); hosted rows `n/a{observability}` (T-LCD-15).
7. **Lowering.** `effect_id`, `attempt_no`, `execution_id` and `capture_manifest_ref` travel through protocol targets in typed extension slots (`_meta`-prefixed keys) and are listed in the lowering loss report where a target has no slot (T-LCD-11); the ACP `tool_call_update.content/rawOutput` is the lowering of `context.observation.recorded`, never of the manifest.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: executors self-report facts the kernel cannot vouch for — S-107 `exec-server-protocol/src/protocol.rs` L343–361 (`sandbox_type: Option`, "None means the peer did not report"), `core/src/exec.rs` L797 (`is_likely_sandbox_denied` heuristic); observers are absent on some backends — S-WS-H4-01 README §Violation Detection ("bubblewrap doesn't provide built-in violation reporting"), S-WS-H4-06 (Landlock logs denials only under audit); unmediated channels exist — S-WS-H4-01 §Security Limitations (domain fronting, unix sockets, approved-host bodies), S-107 `exec.rs` L84–95 (grandchildren holding pipes); attribution as a telemetry field only — S-107 `protocol.rs` L271–279; a single bridged channel out of the environment makes token-keyed attribution feasible — S-WS-H4-01 §Network Isolation, WS-H4 §6.4 (`security.egress.decided` by `effect_id`); masking before model visibility — S-110 `openhands-tools/openhands/tools/terminal/impl.py` L360–384, S-122 `registry/service.py` L350–360; silent retention caps — S-107 `context.rs` L346–374, `exec.rs` L75–83; complete mediation as the principle — S-WS-E5-01 (Tier A); every tool result identifies tool/version/environment — doc 2 §11 (map); content-free audit envelopes — WS-H6 F2/Rule C (Crosby–Wallach lineage via WS-H6 sources).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `codex/codex-rs/exec-server-protocol/src/protocol.rs` (`ExecMetadata`, `ReadResponse{sandbox_denied}`, `ExecExitedNotification{sandbox_denied: Option}`), `codex-rs/core/src/tools/context.rs` (raw vs model output budgets; omission marker), `codex-rs/core/src/turn_diff_tracker.rs` (per-path baseline → `fs_change`), `codex-rs/core/src/tools/network_approval.rs` (execution-scoped proxy + denial monitor); `anthropic-experimental/sandbox-runtime/src/sandbox/{sandbox-violation-store.ts, linux-violation-monitor.ts, credential-sentinel.ts, http-proxy.ts}` (violation store; sentinel substitution at the proxy); `software-agent-sdk/openhands-tools/openhands/tools/terminal/impl.py` (`_mask_observation`, `full_output_save_dir`); `browser-use/browser_use/tools/registry/views.py` (`terminates_sequence` → `world_changed`); `goose/crates/goose/src/agents/large_response_handler.rs` (large output redirected). The multi-source item sum with kernel-stamped identity, the kernel-minted single-use attribution token consumed by two mediators, the manifest with explicit `completeness`, and the completeness veto metric are `no-precedent / novel` as an integrated contract.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: some effects are unobservable on some backends (grandchildren, unix sockets, domain fronting, absent violation reporting) — accepted and made explicit: `completeness = partial{unobserved_channel}` and `residual_channels` (WS-H4) replace a false claim of completeness; tracing everything is expensive (S-WS-E5-03 gVisor cost; strace fallback in S-WS-H4-01) — the C0 sources are the executor report, the helper's own observations, the mediators and kernel-side diffs, not syscall tracing; browser/GUI environments cannot emit `fs_change`/`exit_status` — `world_changed` + `structured_result` is the required minimum and the Lab stratifies on `observers_present` (T-LCD-15); token delivery may be bypassed by a process that clears its environment — mitigated by socket-peer-credential and process-label channels (OQ-WS-E5-01) and by *refusal* at the mediators (an untokened connection cannot succeed, so bypass yields denial, not attribution loss). Nothing here is load-bearing on a `provisional` number.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier; observer richness depends on the environment class and backend (`observers_present` is declared per binding); assumes WS-H4's mediated network mode and WS-H3's placeholder discipline for the token-refusal rule to have teeth; with `net.mode = public` (principal-authorised residual) egress attribution degrades to `partial{unobserved_channel(net)}` by construction.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — `CaptureItem` sum, manifest, `completeness`, token mint/resolve/hash, redaction placement, `unattributed` event, AC-E5-05/-08; **C0 / Stage 2** — token delivery through the helper, egress/credential attribution with H4/H3, `fs_change` via baselines with B4/B5, AC-E5-01 static half; **C0 / Stage 3** — `attribution_completeness` veto in the suite, observer-coverage stratification, AC-E5-01 executable; **C1 / Stage 4** — hosted `n/a` rows, remote executors; **C2** — per-leaf taint labels on items (H2), body-level capture under TLS termination (H4).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-02 (executor `detail` is a `Text{external}` leaf; every other field typed), T-LCD-06 (no native field for hosted participants; `n/a` rows), T-LCD-07 (`completeness`/`observers_present` never coerced), T-LCD-10 (manifest identity excludes ephemeral chunking and surface names), T-LCD-11 (typed slots + loss report), T-LCD-12 (operations over records; the manifest is data), T-LCD-14/-15 (metrics class-scoped and `instrument`-charged). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-05/-08/-09/-13 (no model-facing surface or conditioned rule introduced). Ladder rung: capture path, stamping, mint/resolve, redaction hook = MUST-code; `retain_bytes_cap`, `max_deltas`, observer lists = MUST-data.

## Consequences

- WS-A3 adds `capture_manifest_ref`, `completeness`, `attribution_token_hash` to the `Effect` record (dialect bump alongside ADR-0030's field group); WS-H6 lists them as audit fields on `action.effect.observed`.
- WS-H4 adopts the kernel as the token minter (CF-WS-E5-02) and links `security.egress.decided` to effects through `resolve_attribution`; WS-H3's `mediate` requires a resolvable token.
- WS-B4/B5 supply `snapshot/diff` producing `fs_change` items for the reversible class; WS-G2 consumes `completeness` and `world_changed` as evidence classes.
- WS-I2 registers the metric declarations; WS-J5 stores `completeness` per run so results can be stratified on observer coverage.
- WS-L2/A3 rule on `cpu_ms`/bytes meters (registered `ext` vs dialect bump — CF-WS-H4-04, OQ-WS-E5-05).

## Reversibility

**Medium.** The item sum and manifest are additive data; dropping the completeness veto later is a metric-policy change. Removing the kernel-minted token after H3/H4 key refusals on it would reopen unattributed egress and credential use — the exact gap this ADR closes — so that direction is costly; changing the delivery channel per backend is cheap (helper-local).
