# ADR-NNNN — The `evolution_proposer` component class and the taxonomy of self-improving harnesses: one proposer contract with a conformance suite; per-family adapters (observability-driven, retrospective self-supervised, outer-loop code search, learned editing, gene-bank/quality-diversity, continual compilation, test-time adaptation, co-evolution, meta-harnessing) mapped to tiers, gates and maturity flags; the evidence corpus; slot allocation; hosted-coordinate search

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I5 (consumers: WS-I8, WS-F4, WS-D4, WS-D5, WS-J2, WS-J3, WS-J6, WS-L5, WS-G3, WS-I2) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (families in scope; maturity flags; (e) research-grade), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05/-07/-08/-12/-14/-15), ADR-0023/0151/0152 (component classes; conformance suites), ADR-0041 (M3; Phase 3 amendment on unenforceable dimensions), ADR-0046/0047 (search vs artifact; judges), ADR-0053 (D-5 handles), ADR-0086 (induced procedures; promotion policy), ADR-0135 (counterfactual), ADR-0149 (T-4 hosted diffs), ADR-0154/0156 (`adaptive_search`; validation strategies), ADR-0159 (`selected_best_of_n`), ADR-0165 (`budget_enforcement`), ADR-0181/0182 (`plugin_abi/1`; X6); OQ-025, OQ-127, OQ-216, OQ-280, OQ-382; CF-WS-I5-04; R-2.9.5, R-2.9.8

## Context

Doc 2 §5.8 lists six mechanisms behind "self-improving harness" — offline harness search, continual compilation, test-time adaptation, learned harness editing, model–harness co-evolution, meta-harnessing — and warns that "these are not interchangeable claims about the same algorithmic object"; the same section lists families (AHE, RHO, MemoHarness, Meta-Harness, Harness-R1, Task-CoEvolve, JIT-Agent) and the newer credit/validation methods (HarnessBank, Task-CoEvolve, HarnessLens, HarnessEvolve). Doc 3 §5 asks WS-I5 for per-family adapter sketches. ADR-1 (this workstream) fixes the gates every candidate passes regardless of family; what remains is how a family *plugs in*: what it reads, what it may emit, whether it uses a judge, which tier it belongs to, and how mature it is. The audit shows the families differ mainly in the proposer (DGM's diagnosis model + coding agent; AFlow's prompt-driven graph rewriter over an operator vocabulary; ADAS's `forward`-code generator with a debug loop; Meta-Harness's filesystem-reading proposer; Harness-R1's trained editor) and in what they archive; S-092 shows allocation across slots changes outcomes; S-088 shows the beneficiary may not use what the proposer produced.

## Options considered

1. **One built-in proposer** (the instrument's own). *Rejected:* every family is `provisional` and model-conditioned (S-075, S-087); an instrument must compare families under matched budgets, which requires them to be swappable variants (ADR-0006 novelty claim (b)).
2. **Families as opaque external optimizers** driven over the Hosting ABI. *Rejected:* they would then propose *products*, not `HirDiff`s on registry components (ADR-0147 M-2), and attribution stops at product level.
3. **A registered component class `evolution_proposer` with an operation-shaped contract, a conformance suite, per-family variants carrying maturity flags, and a taxonomy table fixing tier/gate mapping (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Class.** `ClassRecord{class_id: evolution_proposer, home: plane 7, contract{propose, select_parent, declare}, cardinality: exactly-one per campaign, required_inputs ⊇ {ModelProfile, ResourceAccount}, hot_path: false, decision_points: ∅ (never invoked inside a subject run), conformance_suite_ref}` (ADR-0023/0151). Operations: `propose(corpus: EvidenceCorpusRef, base: DefinitionVersionRef, constraints: ProposalConstraints, profile, account) → [CandidateProposal ∪ FailureHypothesis] | NoAddressableFailure` (reads only the corpus and base; writes only candidate records at scope `run`; every model call on the proposer's budget slice, `charged_to = instrument`); `select_parent(lineage, policy: ParentSelectionPolicy) → DefinitionVersionRef` (pure); `declare() → {family, op_classes_admissible, needs_reference_trajectories, uses_judge, judge_ref?, maturity}` (tri-state fields; T-LCD-07 reflexively). Failures: `CorpusUnreadable`, `ConstraintUnsatisfiable`, `BudgetExhausted`. Every non-first-party variant runs out of process under `plugin_abi/1` with `placement ∈ {subprocess_confined, container, remote}` and the ADR-0053 D-5 handle set (no network from the proposer's sandbox except to the model gateway; no `held_out` path).
2. **Conformance suite (ADR-0152 four kinds).** *static:* declaration conforms; every model-conditioned proposer rule carries an `AssumptionDebtRecord`. *contract:* driven out of process over a golden corpus; every output parses as `CandidateProposal`; `classify` of every emitted diff is never widening/loosening. *property:* isolation probes — no read outside the corpus, no held-out path, no network beyond the gateway; never emits a candidate on the exclusion set; `uses_judge = false` variants make no judge call (else `DRIFT`). *executable:* on the Stage-3 golden corpus emits ≥ 1 S2-valid hypothesis, or `NoAddressableFailure` with a reason.
3. **Evidence corpus.** `EvidenceCorpus{corpus_id, campaign_id, watermarks[run], layers{outcomes, ledger_views, model_io?, artifacts?, prior_candidates[], reference_trajectories?[], slot_history}, excluded{held_out surfaces, validators, secrets, kernel notices above external}}` is `project(runs, evolution_evidence_view, until_seq)` (ADR-0026 rebuild-equality), content-addressed, delivered read-only through the D-5 `fs_read` handle; the outcome layer is always present, deeper layers expand by address under the campaign's `readers` policy (`model_io` needs consent — WS-I3 §6.9). `prior_candidates[]` carries every earlier candidate's diff, hypothesis, state and reports (AFlow's experience list generalised; a repeated diff is `DuplicateCandidate`).
4. **Slot allocation (OQ-025, I5 half).** `ProposalConstraints.target_classes[]` is chosen per campaign by `SlotAllocationPolicy{floors: map<class_id, min_rollouts>, mode ∈ {concentrate (default), uniform, voi}}`; `SearchBudgetRecord.allocation` records realised spend per slot; the corpus's `slot_history` gives per-slot `search_time_benefit` refs to WS-F4, whose scheduler is the only admissible `voi` implementation; `uniform` below the floors is refused (`BudgetSplittingTrap`, S-092 mechanism).
5. **Taxonomy → tier/gates/maturity (normative table).** (a) **Offline harness search** — observability-driven (AHE), retrospective self-supervised (RHO), outer-loop code search (Meta-Harness, DGM, AFlow, ADAS), gene-bank/QD (HarnessBank, GEPA): full S1–S10; op classes per family (`Rebind`/`ReplaceField`/`ReplaceLeaf` on `Text`; `CompiledPayload` for code search with declared interface and `placement ≠ in_process`); judges as selectors only (G7); maturity: AHE-shaped `instrument-grade` candidate at Stage 6b, all others `research-grade` (6c). (b) **Learned harness editing** (Harness-R1): the editor is a `ModelSnapshotRecord` with roles; its reward is the S4 `search_time_benefit` row from fresh reruns of the frozen target; training is WS-I8's; every accepted patch passes S5–S10; `research-grade`. (c) **Continual compilation** (Evo-Harness, skills, Procedural Graphs): candidates are `AddNode` `Procedure`/`Memory` with `origin = evolution` under ADR-0086's lifecycle; the pipeline is entered only at promotion, with OQ-216's rule (≥ 2 families ∨ 1 family + 1 held-out environment for `definition`; single profile ⇒ `principal` with `conditioned_on`); `instrument-grade` at `principal`, `research-grade` at `definition`. (d) **Test-time adaptation** (MemoHarness, JIT-Agent): per-run selection by registered components (D3/D5) — not a pipeline stage; the pipeline governs only what persists (`Memory` validity/revocation via WS-D4; promotion via (c)); `research-grade`. (e) **Model–harness co-evolution** (SafeEvolve, HarnessForge, Co-Harness, WHALE): WS-I8; the pipeline exposes `consolidation_candidates` (accepted edits with stable `TransferProfile`) and refuses candidates whose S6 profile is pinned to a retrained policy without a debt record (S-096); `research-grade` with ADR-0002 (e)'s explicit flag. (f) **Meta-harnessing**: the service *is* the outer harness — a native `AgentProcess` with D-5 handles; recursion is not admitted (G5 `SelfModificationRefused`); the instrument is `instrument-grade`, its self-evolution is a human-origin campaign.
6. **Per-family adapter sketch (commitments only to corpus layers, op classes, judge placement).** *AHE-shaped:* outcomes + ledger views + prior candidates; component slots (tools, middleware, memory classes first — the family's own ablation finding); prediction per edit native. *RHO-shaped:* selects hard past tasks from outcomes; re-solves in `search`; self-validation/self-preference as `selected_best_of_n` inside S4 with a calibration record and OQ-280's anti-collusion set; deterministic S5. *Code-search-shaped:* full corpus including prior candidates' payload bodies and traces; `ReplaceLeaf`/`AddNode` on `CompiledPayload`; the compile/debug loop is proposer-internal (ADAS L295–305 shape); isolation probes at S7; attribution at leaf granularity declared. *Gene-bank/QD-shaped:* `ParentSelectionPolicy = pareto_per_task`; diversity as a stop-rule input; gated screening deterministic. *Learned-editor-shaped:* as (b). *Continual-compilation-shaped:* as (c). *Demonstration-guided (DemoEvolve):* `needs_reference_trajectories = true`; human demonstrations or answer-conditioned reference runs (HarnessEvolve) enter the corpus as `reference_trajectories[]` — under G6 they come from `search`/`dev` tasks only.
7. **Hosted-coordinate search (OQ-382).** A campaign over a hosted participant is admitted only over `SUPPORTED` configuration-level coordinates whose every matched budget dimension has `budget_enforcement = enforced` (ADR-0165; ADR-0041 P3 amendment refuses `matched_total` otherwise); candidates are ADR-0149 T-4 `value_change` diffs; claims are `configuration`/`product` granularity; component attribution `n/a{class}`; such candidates never enter a component-level `artifact_benefit` row and are reported with `search_unknown` when the participant's own search spend is unobservable (ADR-0159 decision 6).
8. **Judge selectors (OQ-280, OQ-127 rules).** A selector judge in S3–S4 requires: `CriticDeclaration.independence` from every beneficiary snapshot; a `CalibrationRecord` on a pre-split labelled subset with expiry; honeypot items in the `CounterexampleSet` with the flip rate reported; a fixed per-campaign human audit budget (`instrument`) with the audited share reported; evidence paraphrased when the critic shares the beneficiary's family. `teacher_relative` is admitted as a selector once a domain calibration record exists and never as a gate.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-08 (the class receives `(ModelProfile, ResourceAccount)`), T-LCD-12 (operations; out-of-process under `plugin_abi/1`), T-LCD-07 (reflexive: `declare()` tri-state, probed by the suite; `DRIFT` on undeclared judge use), T-LCD-05 (model-conditioned proposer rules carry debt records; families carry maturity flags with a promotion test — OQ-WS-I5-06), T-LCD-14 (search spend per family is budgeted, matched and reported; `BudgetSplittingTrap` refusal), T-LCD-15 (hosted campaigns `n/a{class}`; `search_unknown` flagged), T-LCD-06 (hosted coordinates through declared capabilities only; no native change), T-LCD-02 (proposer prose enters as `Text` at `≤ external`). Not applicable: T-01/-03/-04/-09/-10/-11/-13. **ADR-0024 rung:** the class contract, conformance driver, corpus projection and the `BudgetSplittingTrap`/`SelfModificationRefused` checks are MUST-code; family variants, `SlotAllocationPolicy`, `ParentSelectionPolicy`, maturity flags and the taxonomy table's per-family parameters are MUST-data / registered variants.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** the six-class taxonomy and "not interchangeable claims" — doc 2 §5.8 (Tier-B synthesis); families as distinct proposers — AHE S-061, RHO S-063, Meta-Harness S-080, Harness-R1 S-081, MemoHarness S-066, JIT-Agent S-071, HarnessBank S-087, HarnessLens S-089, HarnessEvolve S-090, Evo-Harness S-WS-I5-04, DemoEvolve S-WS-I5-02, Self-Harness S-WS-I5-01 (all `provisional`; each supplies a mechanism, none a constant); Tier-A lineage for search over a typed envelope with an opaque body — DGM S-026, GEPA S-028, AFlow S-015, ADAS S-023; per-slot value localisation and the budget-splitting trap — S-092 (`provisional`); beneficiary compliance as a separate capability — S-088 (`provisional (mech)`); model change invalidates the harness — S-096 (`provisional`); component classes with conformance suites and out-of-process variants — ADR-0023/0151/0152/0181 (ratified); registry not model-facing — ADR-0151 R-NM (ratified); hosted admissibility — ADR-0013/0041/0149/0165 (ratified).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** dgm @a565fd2 `DGM_outer.py` L50–150 (parent selection policies as code — `random | score_prop | score_child_prop | best`; adopted as `ParentSelectionPolicy` data), `self_improve_step.py` L30–67 (diagnosis model as the proposer's first stage); AFlow @3f45721 `optimizer_utils/data_utils.py` L61–95 (`select_round` mixed softmax), `optimizer_utils/experience_utils.py` L20–66 (prior candidates rendered into the proposer prompt — the `prior_candidates[]` layer), `optimizer_utils/graph_utils.py` L59–73 (operator vocabulary from `operator.json` — the registry `slot_choices` precedent); ADAS @2702bee `_arc/search.py` L295–325 (compile/debug loop inside the proposer). **The class contract with a conformance suite, the read-only projected corpus, the per-family tier/gate/maturity table and the `BudgetSplittingTrap` refusal are `no-precedent / novel`** (AgentSquare's "uniform IO interface" S-024 is the nearest and is the LCD shape this ADR avoids by keeping families as variants under one *gate* set rather than one *behaviour* interface).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) families are moving targets and a class contract may not fit the next one — the contract commits only to inputs (corpus, base, constraints) and outputs (`CandidateProposal`); a family that cannot express its proposal as a `HirDiff` is not admissible by ADR-0147 M-2 regardless; (ii) forcing out-of-process proposers adds latency to search — search is off the subject's hot path and `instrument`-charged; the cost is measured (OQ-075 shape) not assumed; (iii) test-time adaptation families lose their headline mechanism if per-run edits are not pipeline candidates — they keep it (D3/D5 selection over sealed variants and `Memory` with validity); what they lose is silent persistence, which ADR-0086/D4 already forbid; (iv) hosted search at configuration granularity is not "evolution" — accepted: it is reported as such (`product`/`configuration`, `n/a{class}`), which is exactly what T-LCD-15 requires. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** every family's *value* is conditional on model tier, task class and budget (ADR-0002 (d)); the table fixes admissibility and maturity, never expected gains; maturity promotion from `research-grade` requires a campaign under M3 on the Stage-3 suite (OQ-WS-I5-06 for replication requirements); hosted campaigns assume enforced dimensions; `voi` allocation assumes WS-F4's scheduler exists (else `concentrate`).
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** the `ClassRecord`, `declare()` and conformance suite — C4/Stage 6b (registered at Stage 6a as a class with the `human` family only); AHE-shaped first-party variant — Stage 6b; code-search, RHO/QD selectors, `voi`, hosted campaigns — Stage 6c; continual-compilation promotion hook — with WS-D5 at Stage 4 (ADR-0086 C1 slice) using the pipeline from Stage 6a; co-evolution interface — Stage 6d (WS-I8).

## Consequences

- WS-J2 registers `evolution_proposer` and its suite; WS-L5's variant host drives family variants and doubles as the `registry_ci` driver; WS-J3's `slot_choices` enumerates families as levels of a *proposer* factor so families can be compared under matched search budgets (the ADR-0006 novelty claim (b) applied to evolution itself).
- WS-F4 owns `voi`; WS-D4/D5 apply the OQ-216 promotion rule; WS-G3 supplies the selector-judge calibration/honeypot/audit shape; WS-J6 confirms `budget_enforcement` per coordinate for hosted campaigns; WS-I8 consumes `consolidation_candidates`.
- The Spec's §7.2-11 deferred list carries the `research-grade` families with their promotion test.

## Reversibility

**High for the taxonomy table and maturity flags** (data; a family moves tier or flag by campaign evidence and an amendment log). **Medium for the class contract** (adding an operation is additive; changing `propose`'s inputs would require every family variant to re-conform). **High for the corpus layer list** (additive; excluded surfaces are fixed by G5/G6 of ADR-2 and are not this ADR's to relax).
