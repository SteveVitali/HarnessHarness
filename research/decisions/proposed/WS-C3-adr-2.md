# ADR-NNNN — The profile test contract: validity (V1–V9 with golden surfaces and a null profile) vs conformance (probe specs, records, cadence; DRIFT refuses only on dependency capabilities) vs compliance (per-rule class-scoped metrics with admissible detector classes on held-out splits); `ProfileTestReport` in the registry, referenced by every bundle

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-C3 (consumers: WS-A4, WS-C1, WS-C2, WS-I2, WS-I6, WS-J2, WS-J3, WS-G1/G3, WS-E2) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0007 (T-LCD-01/-04/-05/-13/-14/-15), ADR-0013 (DRIFT semantics), ADR-0014 (delivered/activated/followed; detector provenance), **ADR-0020 §6 (the four test obligations this ADR makes executable)**, ADR-0019 (AC-2/AC-7), ADR-0022 (E1–E7 linkage), ADR-0037 (immutable records), ADR-0039 (`instrument` attribution), ADR-0045 (`MetricDeclaration`, `n/a{reason}`), ADR-0046 (held-out splits), ADR-0047 (judges); ADR-WS-C1-3 proposed (gateway `probe`, fingerprint); CF-WS-C3-01; OQ-071, OQ-WS-C2-02, OQ-WS-C3-02; R-2.3.3, R-2.9.2, R-2.10.2

## Context

ADR-0020 §6 requires four independently runnable suites — static, probe, equivalence, behavioural — and §7 makes probe failure an expiry trigger, but neither fixes what each suite checks, who executes it, where its results live or whether a DRIFT verdict blocks compilation (OQ-071; WS-C2 asks the same for routing, OQ-WS-C2-02). The audit shows the field tests only the first: gemini-cli keeps a snapshot matrix of every resolved tool declaration per model id with the platform stubbed for determinism (`tools/definitions/coreToolsModelSnapshots.test.ts` + `__snapshots__/`); codex validates reminder parameters (`core/src/config/mod.rs` L1150–1225: positive threshold, byte caps, dependent field); no audited system probes whether the served model has the capability a rule assumes, and none measures whether a rendered rule was followed. Behaviour changes under a stable model id (S-WS-C3-02), so validity alone proves nothing about the served model; S-088 (`provisional (mech)`) and ADR-0014 separate validity from compliance; S-037 warns that tool descriptions overfit unless evaluated on held-out sets; ADR-0045 forbids proxies for missing detectors (`n/a{no_detector}`).

## Options considered

1. **Static validation only** (codex, gemini). *Rejected:* says nothing about the served model or about whether the rule is followed; dead weight stays invisible (S-047).
2. **Whole-harness keep-rate / benchmark evaluation per profile** (Cursor S-049). *Rejected:* cannot localise to a rule, is unmatched-budget by default (S-083), and cannot express `n/a`.
3. **Probe everything on every call.** *Rejected:* cost and noise (S-WS-C1-02); probes are experiment-boundary instruments, not per-call guards.
4. **DRIFT always blocks / DRIFT never blocks.** *Rejected:* always-block stops the Lab on irrelevant drift; never-block lets a run assume a capability the model lost.
5. **Three tests with three owners and one report: compiler-only validity incl. golden surfaces against a null profile; gateway-executed, instrument-charged conformance probes with a dependency-scoped DRIFT refusal; Lab-computed per-rule compliance metrics with admissible detector classes and held-out splits; results in a registry `ProfileTestReport` referenced by every bundle (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`ProfileTestReport{profile_hash, schema_version, validity{V1..V9}, golden{surfaces: map<(capability semantic_id, surface_id) → ContentAddress>, layout_hash, renderer_spec_hash}, equivalence: map<surface_id → EquivalenceEvidence>, probes: ConformanceRecord[], compliance: [{rule_id, metric_ref, design_ref, last_report_ref?}], produced_at, producer_version}`** is an immutable registry record (ADR-0037) beside the profile; `CompiledBundle.diagnostics` carries `profile_test_report_ref`; a bundle compiled under a profile with no report or a failing validity section is refused at `link` (`LinkError{profile_untested | profile_invalid}`).
2. **Validity suite (`test_profile`; compiler-only, no runtime, no model):** V1 schema + per-kind constraints (`ProfileRuleInventory/1`; codex reminder validation made schema); V2 owned-field discipline against the **null profile** (kernel-defined: reserved slots, `native_fc`, identity naming, no templates) — `diff ⊆ owned_fields` (T-LCD-01); V3 debt-record completeness on every rule and the profile; V4 capability consistency (no rule assumes an `unsupported` capability; `*_required` constraints derived, not ruled); V5 **golden surfaces** — every compiled tool surface, layout and renderer spec equals the content address recorded at publish (gemini precedent); V6 determinism across two implementations/hosts; V7 equivalence linkage — `tool_shape` variants carry E1–E3/E7 `pass` (E4 for `edit_file`/`execute`/`read_file` at Stage 3); V8 error-format E5 surjectivity and pairwise distinguishability over capability and surface failure classes; V9 role placement — `role_map` total and monotone over the seven authority classes, wrappers never promote, six reserved slots present (ADR-0034 RP).
3. **Conformance suite (`probe_profile`; executed by the C1 gateway `probe`, every call charged `instrument`):** `ProbeSpec{capability, kind ∈ {schema_accept, name_roundtrip, id_roundtrip, signature_replay, parallel_calls, developer_role, image_input, context_window_edge, max_output_edge, strict_keyword(kw), cache_marker_accept, seed_repeat}, fixture_ref, samples, budget, verdict_rule}` → `ConformanceRecord{profile_version, capability, declared, probed, verdict ∈ {SUPPORTED, UNSUPPORTED, PARTIAL, NOT_APPLICABLE, UNKNOWN, SKIPPED, DRIFT}, probe_run_id}`; detectors deterministic except `seed_repeat` (= the C1 distributional fingerprint); `unknown` never coerced. **Cadence/ownership (OQ-071):** at profile publish (registry, C3), at experiment open/close (Lab, J3), on any `model_version_change` observable (I6 scheduler). **Storage:** registry rows keyed by `(profile_hash, ModelSnapshotRecord)`; probe runs are ordinary ledger runs (`model.profile.probed`). **DRIFT policy (OQ-071, OQ-WS-C2-02):** a `DRIFT | UNSUPPORTED` record for a capability in the *dependency set of any rule in the bound chain* makes `link` refuse (`LinkError{capability_drift}`) unless a recorded intent is present (same mechanism as `expired`); any other DRIFT annotates the run/arm for stratification (ADR-0013) and never refuses; WS-C2's compatibility guard evaluates the same predicate.
4. **Compliance suite (Lab; class-scoped):** for every rule with `compliance.detector_class ≠ none`, a `MetricDeclaration` `profile.rule.followed_rate{rule_id}` (`applies_to_classes = {native}`, `requires_observability ⊇ {ledger}`, `detector_classes_allowed` per kind — table in dossier §6.2: deterministic for `tool_shape`, `naming`, `schema_dialect`, `error_format`, `result_render`, `compaction_reminder`, `caching_markers`, `transcript_render`, `interaction_mode`; judged only for `description_template` and instruction-bearing `prompt_layout`; none for `sampling_defaults`) computed over `context.artefact.delivered → activated → verification.artefact.followed` with the rule's `followed_predicate_ref`; rules without a detector report `n/a{no_detector}`, hosted rows `n/a{class}`; judged detectors are `Validator{kind: judge}` with calibration, independence and `charged_to = instrument` (ADR-0047). Every compliance claim is computed on a **held-out** split pre-registered in `compliance_design_refs`, never on the tasks used to author the rule.
5. **Equivalence** is not re-specified: `tool_shape` rules ship ADR-0022 evidence; the report merely links it (V7).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: validity ≠ compliance (S-088 `provisional (mech)`; ADR-0014 ratified chain); the served model changes under a stable id, so static validity cannot stand in for conformance (S-WS-C3-02 Tier B; S-047, S-049 Tier B); descriptions overfit without held-out evaluation (S-037); probes must be distributional where behaviour is sampled (S-WS-C1-02 Tier B; S-WS-C1-04 `provisional`); golden surfaces catch compiler regressions per model (S-119 snapshot matrix); parameter validation on reminder surfaces prevents malformed profiles (S-107).
- (b) Source-code precedent: golden surfaces — `gemini-cli/packages/core/src/tools/definitions/coreToolsModelSnapshots.test.ts` L39–110 + `__snapshots__/coreToolsModelSnapshots.test.ts.snap` (S-119 @ed2ac40; `modelIds × tools` matrix, platform stubbed); per-kind validation — `codex-rs/core/src/config/mod.rs` L1150–1225 `TokenBudgetConfig::validate` (S-107 @0735c51); declared-vs-probed conformance shape — omnigent `tests/harness_bench/verdict.py` (S-118, via ontology *observed conformance*); probe execution — WS-C1 gateway `probe` (dossier §6.1); provider-acceptance checks that are *not* probes — pi `constrained-sampling.ts`, goose `validate_tool_schemas` (S-113/S-112, via ADR-0022). Per-rule compliance metrics, the null-profile diff, dependency-scoped DRIFT refusal and the registry `ProfileTestReport` are `no-precedent / novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) probes are noisy and costly and cannot prevent mid-experiment change — answered: capability probes use deterministic accept/reject detectors; only `seed_repeat` is distributional (C1's design); cadence is boundary-based; DRIFT protects at exactly one place (dependency capabilities) and stratifies elsewhere; (ii) most rules lack deterministic `followed` detectors, so compliance collapses to judged metrics — answered: admissible detector class is declared per kind, `n/a{no_detector}` is a typed value (ADR-0045), and nine of twelve kinds have deterministic detectors; (iii) golden surfaces are brittle under compiler upgrades — answered: a mismatch after a compiler upgrade is a *compiler regression*, which is the point (ADR-0019 AC-1); after a profile edit it is a review item with a new golden address; (iv) held-out splits are expensive for small suites — answered: ADR-0046 already requires them for every artifact-benefit claim; a rule is an artifact. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes providers keep accepting or rejecting schemas/names/ids deterministically (true for every audited dialect); assumes a fake gateway exists at Stage 3 (WS-C1 AC-C1-11); compliance metrics apply to native participants only (`ledger ⇔ native`); no `provisional` result is load-bearing (S-WS-C1-04 corroborates only).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (`ProfileTestReport`, `ProbeSpec`, `ConformanceRecord` schemas; `LinkError` classes; metric declarations); **C0 / Stage 3** (V1–V9 on the two minimal profiles; golden surfaces; minimal probes `schema_accept`, `name_roundtrip`, `id_roundtrip` on the fake gateway; compliance metrics for `tool_shape`/`naming`/`compaction_reminder`; dependency-scoped DRIFT refusal); **C1 / Stage 5** (full probe set incl. fingerprint hand-off, judged detectors, publish/experiment/scheduler cadence); **C4** (WS-I6 consumes records).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies: T-LCD-01 (V2 null-profile diff is the executable owned-field test), T-LCD-04 (golden surfaces per profile are the artefacts AC-7 compares), T-LCD-05 (V3; probe failure feeds expiry), T-LCD-13 (per-rule compliance over the ratified chain), T-LCD-14 (compliance and removal designs carry `MatchSpec`), T-LCD-15 (`n/a{no_detector | class}`, never 0). Does not by itself satisfy T-LCD-03 (whole-harness round trip, ADR-0019 AC-8). Rung on the ADR-0024 ladder: reports and specs are MUST-data; detectors are registered validators.

## Consequences

- WS-J2's registry stores `ProfileTestReport` and `ConformanceRecord` rows; WS-J3 runs probes at experiment boundaries beside C1's fingerprint; WS-I6 schedules probes on `model_version_change`.
- WS-C1 executes `ProbeSpec`s through `probe`; WS-C2's guard reuses the dependency-scoped DRIFT predicate.
- WS-G1/G3 supply deterministic `followed` detectors for the kinds listed and calibrated judges for the two judged kinds.
- WS-I2 registers `profile.rule.followed_rate` with per-kind `detector_classes_allowed`.

## Reversibility

**High for cadence and probe kinds** (data, extendable by ADR); **medium for the refusal rule** (relaxing dependency-scoped DRIFT to annotate-only after Stage 3 removes the one guard against assuming a lost capability and would need a compensating C2 guard); **medium for golden surfaces** (dropping them after Stage 3 loses the per-model compiler-regression tripwire T-LCD-04 relies on).
