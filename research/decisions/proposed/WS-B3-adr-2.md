# ADR-NNNN — Wakeups, suspended runs and long-lived goals: subscription → occurrence → claim → fire; `lifecycle.run.suspended`; goal activations chained by `continued_from`

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-B3 (with WS-H7, WS-F1, WS-F2, WS-F3, WS-B1, WS-E4, WS-K1/K2/L8) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0026, ADR-0027, ADR-0030, ADR-0033, ADR-0035, ADR-0040, ADR-0042, ADR-0016 (`Goal`), ADR-0021 (lowering); WS-H7 ADR-2 (async `defer`); CF-WS-B3-01, CF-WS-B3-02, CF-WS-B3-03; OQ-081, OQ-095, OQ-WS-B3-01, OQ-WS-B3-02, OQ-WS-B3-04, OQ-WS-H7-03, OQ-WS-H1-03; R-2.2.3, R-2.8.7, R-2.6.3, R-2.12.6

## Context

Doc 2 §12 direction 5 names "event-sourced goals, wakeups, leases, retries" as the durable-runtime contribution; the WS-B3 row asks for event-driven wakeups (schedules, webhooks, PR/tracker events), long-lived goals and asynchronous approvals. Production has converged on the *behaviour* — Cursor agents "subscribe to an event source … and wake when something happens", drive their own PRs, hold a `/goal` objective and take follow-ups "at the next tool call" (S-054); Cursor replaced eternal workflows with shorter task-scoped ones (S-052); Temporal's Continue-As-New chains runs under one workflow id because histories are capped (S-WS-B3-03/04); prime-agent persists cron/heartbeat jobs, writes a claim record per due job, skips double claims, re-queues dispatches interrupted by a crash and wakes passivated sessions after a daemon restart (S-116 `cron-jobs.ts`, `daemon-supervisor.ts`); Cloudflare persists schedules and dedupes background work by idempotency key (S-WS-B3-01/02). But no audited system records the *occurrence* separately from the firing, goose forgets interrupted scheduled runs at load (S-112 `scheduler.rs`), Cloudflare does not state delivery semantics, and Symphony does not restore timers (S-108 §14.3). WS-H7 already specifies asynchronous approval as `lifecycle.run.suspended{awaiting_approval}` + `control.wakeup.scheduled{permission_decided}` and needs the generic mechanism; WS-B1 reserved `control.wakeup.scheduled/fired` and the `wakeup` scope kind (ADR-0042).

## Options considered

1. **Schedules and event sources as data outside the run, fired by an external scheduler with no ledger record until the run acts (goose/Cloudflare shape).** Rejected: an occurrence arriving while the runtime is down is lost or double-fired; the audit trail cannot show *why* a run woke; violates "nothing unaccounted".
2. **Wakeups as ordinary inputs on the WS-L1 §6.5 `submit` verb, with the surface responsible for durability.** Rejected: pushes durability to every ingress; loses the claim semantics; still no goal-scoped delivery when no run is open.
3. **One eternal run per goal that never finishes (entity-process shape without continuation).** Rejected: unbounded ledger growth per run, definition/profile drift across a multi-day run cannot be re-verified at a boundary, and results rows keyed by run become meaningless (Temporal's own reason for Continue-As-New).
4. **Durable subscription, durable occurrence, claim-then-fire under the run's writer lease, `suspended` run state with a no-open-committed-effect invariant, goal activations chained by non-diverging `continued_from`** — chosen.

## Decision

1. **Subscriptions.** `subscribe(owner: RunId | GoalRef, trigger: Trigger, policy: WakeupPolicy) → subscription_id` appends `control.wakeup.scheduled{subscription}` (existing class; payload extended). `Trigger` is a closed sum (HIR/1 dialect): `timer{at}` · `schedule{expression, timezone, kind ∈ {cron, interval}}` · `permission_decided{permission_id}` · `child_terminal{child_run_id}` · `effect_terminal{effect_id}` · `environment_ready{handle}` · `retry_due{scope_id}` (derived from `control.retry.scheduled{not_before}`) · `external{source_ref: VersionedRef, filter}` (registered ingress adapter: PR/CI/tracker/chat) · `manual{principal}`. `WakeupPolicy{delivery_mode ∈ {steer, follow_up}, coalesce ∈ {none, latest, all}, max_pending, expires_at?, attendance_required ∈ {interactive, async, unattended}, occurrence_key_fn}`. Schedules are MUST-data of the Harness Definition (ADR-0024); the next due instant is derived from the ledger, never the only stored copy.
2. **Occurrences are durable before any run acts.** `record_occurrence(subscription_id, occurrence_key, payload_ref, observed_at, provenance)` appends `control.wakeup.occurred{…, provenance{origin ∈ {kernel, ingress(source_ref)}, authority ∈ {kernel, principal, external}}}`, idempotent on `(subscription_id, occurrence_key)` (duplicate ⇒ `control.wakeup.skipped{duplicate_occurrence}`). Writers: the timer service, the permission responder (`respond`, WS-H7), the orchestrator (child terminal), the executor (effect terminal), ingress adapters. If the owner is a goal with no open activation, the occurrence lands in the goal's **inbox run** (a run with `kind: inbox` and no strategy; OQ-WS-B3-02 decides its exact identity). External payloads are `Text`/blobs with `authority = external`: they can wake a run and never endorse, widen a permission or satisfy a validator (ADR-0033/0035).
3. **Firing is a claim.** `fire(subscription_id, occurrence_key, holder)` acquires a `wakeup` claim lease, restores or continues the run under a fresh writer generation, appends `control.wakeup.fired{subscription_id, occurrence_key, occurred_event, lease}` and delivers `Cue.woken{trigger, payload_ref, delivery_mode}` (CF-WS-B3-02) at the next decision point. Invariants: W-1 at most one `fired` per `(subscription_id, occurrence_key)`; W-2 `occurred` precedes `fired`; W-3 no delivery while an effect is `committed` (`follow_up` waits for the terminal; `steer` is delivered before the next model call; neither interrupts an effect — WS-F2 INV-3); W-4 an occurrence for a `finished` owner with no goal subscription is `skipped{run_finished}` and audited; W-5 coalescing is recorded (`skipped{coalesced, superseded_by}`). Delivery semantics are stated honestly: **at-least-once occurrence, idempotent fire** — never "exactly-once wakeup". Two wakeup services racing on one occurrence produce exactly one `fired`.
4. **Suspended runs.** `RunState = created → active ⇄ suspended → finished`. `suspend(run, lease, reasons: [SuspendReason], subscriptions[], release_lease = true)` appends `lifecycle.run.suspended{reasons, subscription_ids, resume_policy}` then `lifecycle.lease.released{reason: suspended}`; `SuspendReason ∈ {awaiting_approval{permission_id}, awaiting_event{subscription_id}, awaiting_timer{at}, awaiting_child{child_run_id}, awaiting_effect{effect_id}, awaiting_environment{handle}, operator_pause, hibernated}`. **Invariant S-1:** no effect is in `prepared` or `committed` at suspension (`OpenCommittedEffects` refusal); `intended` effects and `pending` permissions may remain. A suspended run is exempt from liveness-based takeover and stall restart (Symphony's blocked set); its environment lease continues in `env.reserved_ms` (answers OQ-095 without a new counter). WS-H7's asynchronous `defer` is this mechanism with trigger `permission_decided`; `OQ-WS-H7-03` is answered: the compensation plan is registered at `prepare` after resume, which S-1 guarantees has not yet run.
5. **Long-lived goals.** A `Goal` (ADR-0016) outlives runs; each run is an **activation** with `activation_no` in its manifest; `continue_goal(goal_ref, from_run, carried{resume_set_heads, budget_id}, manifest_delta) → RunId` opens a run with `lifecycle.run.created{continued_from{run_id, at_seq, head_hash}, activation_no}` — the ADR-0027 fork-by-reference shape restricted to `kind: continuation` (a second continuation from the same head is `AlreadyContinued`; no divergence, so WS-B4 semantics are untouched). Carried state is the WS-D3 `resume_set` and the goal-scoped `BudgetNode` (ADR-0040 `scope: Goal`), never a copied transcript; `verify_resume` (ADR-0025) applies at every activation boundary; goal-scoped subscriptions survive activations. Scheduled and goal-scoped runs default to `attendance = unattended` (WS-H1/H7); `subscribe` with `schedule`/`external` triggers refuses `interactive` attendance unless a principal is reachable (`AttendanceUnknown`).
6. **Boundary idempotency.** Every WS-L1 §6.5 verb that injects work (`submit`, `request_permission` responses, occurrence injection by surfaces) carries a caller `idempotency_key`; a duplicate returns the recorded result (CF-WS-B3-03 to WS-K4/L5; prime-agent command-recovery-journal and Stripe precedents).
7. **Contract surface:** `subscribe, record_occurrence, fire, cancel_subscription, list_subscriptions, due, suspend, continue_goal`; failure modes `TriggerUnregistered, AttendanceUnknown, SubscriptionLimit, OpenCommittedEffects, Fenced, WouldBlock, AlreadyContinued, GoalFinished, BudgetExhausted`.

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-06 (no IR entity gains a hosting field; `control.wakeup.*` lowering to MCP tasks/push configs is WS-E4's `narrowed` target), T-LCD-07 (hosted `resume`/`interrupt` tri-state), T-LCD-08 (`WakeupPolicy` is a definition parameter; strategies receive `Cue.woken` through their contract), T-LCD-11 (lowering loss declared by E4), T-LCD-12 (wakeup service and ingress adapters are out-of-process), T-LCD-13 (any model-facing "you were woken because…" rendering is a `HarnessRule` with delivery/activation events). Not applicable: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-10/-14/-15 beyond the class-scoping of wakeup metrics. ADR-0024 ladder: triggers, policies and schedules are MUST-data; the claim service is MUST-code.

## Consequences

- WS-B1 taxonomy gains `lifecycle.run.suspended`, `control.wakeup.occurred/skipped/cancelled`, `continued_from` + `activation_no` on `lifecycle.run.created` (CF-WS-B3-01); WS-H6 marks `suspended`/`fired` audit-grade.
- WS-F1 adds `Cue.woken` (or folds it into `Cue.resumed`); WS-F2's `control.retry.scheduled` is consumed as a `retry_due` subscription; WS-F3 subscribes parents to `child_terminal`.
- WS-K1/K2/L8 ingress adapters must write `control.wakeup.occurred` with `authority = external` and a source-declared or hashed `occurrence_key` (OQ-WS-B3-01); WS-K4/L5 add boundary idempotency keys (CF-WS-B3-03).
- WS-L2: suspended runs accrue `env.reserved_ms` only; `time.human_wait_ms` covers `awaiting_approval`.
- WS-J5 results rows: goal-level aggregation over activations by `goal_ref`; `activation_no` is not a factor coordinate.

## Reversibility

**medium** — subscriptions, occurrences and suspension are additive event classes and a run-state view; removing them leaves C0 intact (T-LCD-06 style removability). Continuation chains are harder to reverse once results rows and audit anchors reference them (a migration of `continued_from` links and goal aggregation); the choice of at-least-once occurrence + idempotent fire is the honest floor and would only be *tightened*, never loosened.
