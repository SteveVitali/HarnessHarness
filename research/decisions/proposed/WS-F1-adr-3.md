# ADR-NNNN — The control-strategy family is the Harness Lab's canonical component-level comparison; the control boundary moves only by `ComparisonReport`

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-F1 (executed by WS-J3/J4; metrics registered by WS-I2; suites by WS-I4; ablation diffs by WS-I5 at C4) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** WS-F1-adr-1, WS-F1-adr-2; ADR-0002 (evidence standard), ADR-0012 (β a component-level factor; harness effect at matched B), ADR-0017 (`HirDiff`), ADR-0041 (`MatchSpec`, engine refusal), ADR-0045 (`MetricDeclaration`, `Design`, `PreRegistration`, outcome classes, `n/a`), ADR-0046 (paired comparison; benefit kinds; C4 gates), ADR-0047 (deterministic headline); ADR-0007 T-LCD-09/-14/-15; CF-005; OQ-WS-F1-02; R-2.6.1, R-2.10.3

## Context

CF-005 ("deterministic-control pole vs model-owned control") is, by doc 2 §6, an open question whose only 2026 evidence is `provisional` and mixed (Dhage S-070: constraints helped one cell, hurt two; LLM-as-Code S-065: a case study; Barbaste S-074: "loop sophistication does not predict benchmark performance"). Doc 2 §4's principle — "move the boundary only when evaluation demonstrates a benefit" — and doc 3 §2.6 ("a core lab comparison target") require the program to *measure* rather than decide. Phase 1 supplied every instrument primitive: β as a typed factor (ADR-0012), `HirDiff` on `ControlBoundary` (ADR-0017), `MatchSpec` with engine refusal (ADR-0041), the full `MetricDeclaration` and `PreRegistration` (ADR-0045), paired comparison with benefit kinds (ADR-0046). WS-F1-adr-1 makes the strategies variants of one class over one plan language, so a single sealed definition (with both a `Goal` and a `Procedure`) binds under all of them — the precondition for a component-level comparison.

## Options considered

1. **Publish a recommended default (ReAct) and leave comparison to users.** *Rejected:* forfeits the program's distinctive claim (ADR-0006 novelty (b): vary one component under matched budget) on the one factor doc 2 names as the key architectural question.
2. **Compare strategies as separate products (product-level granularity).** *Rejected:* confounds the loop with tools, prompts and profiles; T-LCD-09 requires the interaction of model × harness component to be representable.
3. **Pre-registered component-level factorial over `control_strategy` and β, shipped as a Lab recipe with hypotheses that include the null, plus a β-ablation recipe expressed as `HirDiff`s on `ControlBoundary` (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Design (immutable once the first run opens, ADR-0045).** Factors: `control_strategy ∈ {react/minimal, react/steerable, plan_execute, workflow, program}` (component-level; the definition supplies `Goal` + `Procedure` so every variant binds; a variant whose `capabilities.requires` is unmet yields outcome class `n/a{reason: class}` — never 0), × `model snapshot` (≥ 2 families, roles fixed), × `environment` (a coding suite and a terminal suite from WS-I4 at Stage 3; the anchor's tasks included), × `budget` (`MatchSpec{dimensions: {model_calls, tokens.output.visible, time.wall_ms}, mode: matched_cap}` plus one `iso_cost` arm, ADR-0041), × `seed` (≥ 5; `seed_honoured` declared per variant — `react/minimal` honours it). Pairing by task; replicates by seed.
2. **β-ablation recipe.** Fix the variant; produce a `HirDiff` that changes exactly one `ControlBoundary.assignments[decision_point]` (`stop` first, then `plan`, then `compact`) between `model` and `code`; classification must be `{semantic_ops: 1, authority_delta: none, budget_delta: none}`; compare pairwise at matched budget. A boundary move is adopted into a definition only on a `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind: artifact_benefit, budget_match.status: matched}` with the pre-registered margin — the operational form of doc 2 §4's rule.
3. **Metric declarations (registered by WS-I2; all `applies_to_classes = {native}`, `admissible_granularities ∋ component-level`; hosted participants `n/a{reason: class}`).** Headline: executable oracle pass rate with per-task `(c, n)` (ADR-0047 classes only). Process: `control.reproducibility` (agreement of the `control.decision{decision_point, kind}` sequence across seeds under `deterministic_replay` variants, and of end-state hashes otherwise — exact estimator OQ-WS-F1-02), `efficiency` (tokens, `model_calls`, `time.wall_ms`, `turn_phase_profile`), `reliability` (`stop_reason ∈ {budget_exhausted, guard_tripped, context_exhausted}` rates; loop-guard fires), `grounding` (WS-G2 divergence at `stop{completed}`), `autonomy` (`approvals.requested`), `budget discipline` (soft-threshold compliance, T-LCD-13), and `control.boundary_drift` (declared vs observed β per decision point). Intervals clustered by task; tails reported; distributions, never a single score.
4. **Pre-registered hypotheses.** H0 (null, Barbaste): no headline difference between `react/minimal` and any richer variant at matched budget; H1 (Dhage, `provisional`): `program`/`plan_execute` raise `control.reproducibility` and may lower headline on open-ended tasks; H2 (LLMCompiler): `tool_batch_mode = parallel` lowers `time.wall_ms` at equal tokens; H3: the model × variant interaction is non-zero (T-LCD-09); H4: `stop_rule = submit` vs `no_action` changes `budget_exhausted` rate more than headline. Each is reported with direction and effect size; a `provisional` source may motivate a hypothesis but never a default.
5. **Refusals.** The engine refuses the recipe without a `MatchSpec` (`MissingMatchSpec`), with unequal `eval_budget` (`UnmatchedBudget`), or with a hosted participant at component granularity (`InadmissibleFactor`).
6. **Stage.** Design, hypotheses and metric declarations: **C0 / Stage 3** (evaluation-first, alongside T-LCD-03/-14). First execution over `{react/minimal, react/steerable}` × 2 models × 1 suite at Stage 3; full family at Stage 4 (needs `plan_execute`, D2 compaction, F3 delegation off); β-ablation as evolution candidates at Stage 5–6 (C4 gates of ADR-0046).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-09 (variant, β and their interaction with model are explicit factors), T-LCD-14 (engine refuses unmatched arms; search-time vs artifact benefit separated), T-LCD-15 (class-scoped metrics; `n/a` for hosted and for unmet `requires`), T-LCD-05 reflexively (any nudge rule in a compared variant carries a debt record; the "retire rule" recipe is a valid arm). Does not itself satisfy T-LCD-03 (WS-F1-adr-2) or T-LCD-08/-12 (WS-F1-adr-1). **ADR-0024 ladder:** the recipe and design are MUST-data (experiment layer, `source_kind = experiment`); the engine and metric estimators are Core.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: doc 2 §4 (principle; "task-dependent" boundary), §6 ("open question"), §8 (minimum experimental design; factorials), §10 (causal theory is the missing piece); Dhage S-070 (`provisional`: mixed per-cell effects — the strongest reason the answer must be measured per model × task), LLM-as-Code S-065 (`provisional`), Barbaste S-074/S-166 (`provisional (mech)`: loop sophistication ≠ performance); Harness-Bench S-062 (`provisional (mech)`: factorial harness × model effects exist); LLMCompiler S-WS-F1-02 and Plan-and-Solve S-WS-F1-01 (Tier A/B lineage for the plan-execute arm); ADR-0041/0045/0046 (the instrument).
- (b) Source-code precedent: Inspect `react(... attempts, submit, on_continue)` (S-136 `_react.py` L52–140) — the stop rule and nudge as parameters that an eval harness can sweep; Harbor ATIF `Step.llm_call_count == 0` (S-132, via ADR-0012) — β observable in trajectories; LangGraph prebuilt vs custom graphs (S-143) — one engine running both styles; `no-precedent / novel` for a pre-registered, matched-budget, component-level comparison of control strategies with β as a diffable factor (partial precedent: Harness-Bench compares harnesses at product granularity; Dhage compares constraint sets without a shared runtime).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: a factorial over five variants × models × suites × budgets × seeds is expensive (doc 2 §12 row 10) — mitigated by the staged execution (two variants first), pairing by task, and the `iso_cost` arm; `control.reproducibility` for non-replay variants confounds sampling noise (OQ-082) — mitigated by reporting mode (`deterministic | re-executed`) and by end-state agreement as the fallback estimator; H0 may simply hold, making the recipe "uninformative" — that outcome *is* the finding doc 2 §6 asks for and retires assumption debt on richer loops.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes native participants with `ledger` observability; assumes the Stage-3 suites have deterministic oracles (ADR-0047); the `program`/`plan_execute` arms assume schema-capable models under the profile; effect sizes are expected to be model- and task-conditioned (the whole point — reported as a fitted-surface report with expiry, ADR-0012 decision 7).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 3** design + declarations + first execution; **C1–C2 / Stage 4** full family; **C4 / Stage 5–6** β-ablation under ADR-0046 gates.

## Consequences

- CF-005 is closed as *designed-to-be-measured*: the spec ships no "recommended control strategy" beyond the Stage-0 baseline; Spec §6 carries this recipe and Spec §5's control section points to it.
- WS-I2 registers two new declarations (`control.reproducibility`, `control.boundary_drift`); WS-J4 adds the β-ablation report shape; WS-I5 treats a `ControlBoundary` move as a candidate class with the C4 gates.
- Any dossier or ADR that wants to fix a decision-point owner in Core must cite a `ComparisonReport` from this recipe or supersede this ADR.

## Reversibility

**High.** The design is data in the experiment layer; hypotheses and factors can be revised by a new `PreRegistration`. Removing the recipe would only reopen CF-005 as a fixed decision, which nothing else depends on.
