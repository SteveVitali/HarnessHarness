# ADR-NNNN — Attribution as a graded required input to the evolution pipeline (label gate, `TargetMismatch`, the smallest-corrective-intervention claim), the `attribution_quality` estimator for the evolvability dimension, and the known-effect acceptance suite KA-I7-1…12

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I7 (with WS-I5 for the pipeline stage, WS-I2 for the metric registry entry, WS-I6 for evidence refs, WS-J3 for fixtures, WS-C1/C3/C4 for the fake gateway and null profile) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002, ADR-0046 §7 (C4 gates), ADR-0045 (registry; `attribution_quality` entry from WS-I2 §6.3), ADR-0126 (removal test), ADR-0147 D2 (`experiment` layer), ADR-0017 (`HirDiff.semantic_ops`), ADR-0125 (null profile), ADR-0129 (K5 response cache / recording gateway), ADR-0143/0144 (Stage-3 suite), ADR-0101 (`attribution_completeness` — name collision only), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05/-13/-14/-15); OQ-WS-I7-01, OQ-WS-I7-05; CF-WS-I7-03; R-2.9.7, R-2.9.5, R-2.9.2

## Context

Doc 2 §5.8's seven-point evidence standard lists "(4) component-level attribution" and ADR-0046 §7 is its operational form; the brief asks for "attribution as a required input to the evolution pipeline (I5) and to the scorecard's evolvability dimension (I2)". WS-I2 §6.3 registered `attribution_quality` = "share of Δ attributed to named components with interval (LOI/LOO or counterfactual, WS-I7)" without an estimator, and its name collides with the ratified veto `attribution_completeness` (effect-execution attribution tokens, ADR-0101). RHO/AHE-class evolution methods propose edits from trajectory reading with no attribution (doc 2 §5.8), and model-reading attribution is 14 % accurate at step level (Who&When). Attribution estimators must be certified against known truth: CAR validates against planted SCMs, AgenTracer manufactures truth by programmed fault injection and reruns, and J4 already ratified known-answer datasets KA-1…13 as the acceptance form for estimators.

## Options considered

1. **No attribution requirement; evolution proposals stand on evaluation alone.** *Rejected:* violates the seven-point standard item 4 and leaves the "smallest corrective intervention" claim unverifiable.
2. **Hard requirement: every candidate carries a causal `AttributionReport` before evaluation.** *Rejected:* blocks the proposal stage of every trajectory-reading family and multiplies cost; attribution belongs at acceptance, not proposal.
3. **Accept observational (model-diagnosed) attribution as sufficient with a confidence threshold.** *Rejected:* 14 % step accuracy; a threshold on a correlational verdict is not evidence.
4. **A graded label gate — hypothesis at proposal, ablation-or-better at confirmatory acceptance, causal for locality claims — with a fixed `attribution_quality` estimator and a known-effect acceptance suite (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Every evolution candidate carries `attribution_ref`.** The `HirDiff`'s `LayerProvenance{source_kind: experiment}` layer (ADR-0147 D2) records `attribution_ref → AttributionHypothesis | AttributionReport/1`; the diff's `semantic_ops` targets must be ⊆ the referenced targets, else `TargetMismatch` (a refusal at the pipeline's failure-hypothesis stage).
2. **Label gate.** `observational` ⇒ the candidate may be *evaluated* under an `exploratory` experiment (rows `comparable = false` for headline) but never *accepted*; `designed_ablation` ⇒ eligible for `confirmatory` acceptance under ADR-0046 §7 (all six gates still apply); `causal_interventional` / `causal_coupled` ⇒ additionally licenses the **smallest-corrective-intervention** claim (the diff touches only attributed targets; recorded as `locality_claim = true`) and sets the retirement test's expectation (ADR-0126: removing an attributed rule predicts the measured Δ within its interval). Labels never substitute for the matched-budget gates; they add a precondition.
3. **`attribution_quality` estimator (evolvability dimension; registry entry, ADR-0045 D1).** For a configuration-level paired Δ (a `ComparisonReport` under `MatchSpec`) and the set of `AttributionReport`s on the same subject: `attribution_quality = min(1, Σ_targets |effect_t| / |Δ|)` using M3 values where present (they sum by construction) or M1 `LOO` effects otherwise, with a bootstrap interval from the shared `ResamplePlan` (ADR-0158 D8); `n/a{class}` for hosted rows, `n/a{not_run}` without reports, `n/a{estimator_undefined}` when Δ's interval includes 0; `applies_to_classes = {native}`, `requires_observability ⊇ {ledger}`, `headline = false`, `veto = false`, `charged_to = instrument`. The name stands; ontology rows disambiguate it from `attribution_completeness` (CF-WS-I7-03).
4. **Debt and value-of-compute seams.** An attributed rule's `AssumptionDebtRecord.evidence_refs[]` may cite an `AttributionReport`; its removal test is M1 `retirement`. `estimate_rollouts` is the pipeline's cost input to WS-F4 before any attribution spend is reserved.
5. **Known-effect acceptance suite KA-I7 (executable at Stage 5/6).** Fixtures are Harness Definitions compiled under the null profile (ADR-0125) and executed against a fake/recording gateway (ADR-0129 K5) whose control decisions follow a declared conditional distribution, so truth is known by construction and every fixture records its seed: **KA-I7-1** pivotal component (coverage ≥ 93 % at nominal 95 % over 500 seeded runs; inert intervals include 0; locus = planted point); **KA-I7-2** AND-interaction (M3 recovers φ within intervals, `efficiency_check` within its interval, positive `interaction_index`; M2 under-attributes with non-zero `unattributed_share`); **KA-I7-3** run-forward confound (naive magnitude ranking reproduces the failure > 50 %; point-of-commitment ≥ 90 %; `crn` zeroes the inert effect); **KA-I7-4** budget-splitting trap (uniform vs concentrated `SearchBudgetRecord.allocation`; never pooled); **KA-I7-5** nondeterminism trap — six refusal cases (`NoCoherentForkPoint`, `InterventionPrecedesForkPoint`, `PolicyCollapsed`, `degraded`⇒`exploratory`, `ReplayInvalid`, `CouplingUnavailable`), nothing renders 0; **KA-I7-6** mediation degeneracy (`DE ≠ 0`, `TE_crn ≈ 0` ⇒ `inconclusive`, `exclusion_rate` reported); **KA-I7-7** injected-fault corpus over the Stage-3 suite with a real model (locus at or after the injected point, committed, in ≥ 80 % — placeholder OQ-WS-I7-01; refusal counts reported; an observational hypothesis from a calibrated critic scored beside and excluded from `attribution_quality`); **KA-I7-8** deferred effect (`n/a{not_run}` outcome, process effect reported, nothing committed; joins AC-B4-5); **KA-I7-9** budget truncation (partial report, widened intervals, spend ≤ reservation); **KA-I7-10** class scoping (`n/a{class}`; interception-only substitution at configuration granularity); **KA-I7-11** reproducibility (identical `report_id` for identical design/watermark/seed); **KA-I7-12** pipeline gate (observational-only candidate refused `confirmatory`; ablation-covered candidate passes; uncovered target ⇒ `TargetMismatch`).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-14 (labels add a precondition to, never replace, the matched-budget gates; `attribution_quality` is defined over a matched Δ), T-LCD-15 (`n/a{class | not_run | estimator_undefined}` typed; hosted evolvability cells never 0), T-LCD-05 (retirement expectation from attributed evidence; debt records cite reports with expiry), T-LCD-13 (observational hypotheses are claims, executed reports are evidence — the same delivered/followed separation applied to attribution), T-LCD-09 (fixtures span 2 models × variants × tasks so interaction fixtures are executable), T-LCD-12. Not applicable: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-06/-07/-08/-10/-11. **ADR-0024 rung:** the gate, `TargetMismatch`, the estimator and the suite runner are MUST-code; label thresholds, fixture parameters and pass rates are MUST-data. **Matched-budget conditionality:** acceptance remains ADR-0046 §7; every fixture arm carries a `MatchSpec`.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** doc 2 §5.8 seven-point standard (item 4) and ADR-0046 §7 (ratified); S-WS-I7-05 Who&When (Tier A; observational attribution unreliable); S-WS-I7-04 CausalFlow (`provisional`; minimal repairs validated by re-execution — the smallest-corrective-intervention shape); S-544 AgentDebugX (`provisional (mech)`; rerun validates diagnosis); S-092 (`provisional`; LOI/LOO and the budget trap fixture); S-WS-I7-07 AgenTracer (`provisional`; fault injection as ground truth); S-WS-I7-01 CAR (`provisional`; planted SCM validation); S-WS-I7-10/-11 (Tier A; why M3 values sum); ADR-0157/0158 KA-1…13 (ratified precedent for known-answer acceptance); ADR-0125/0129 (null profile; recording gateway).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** causal-agent-replay @db90fa2 `tests/synthetic_scms.py` (pivotal and AND-interaction SCMs with rule-based outcomes), `tests/test_attribution.py` L53–138 and `tests/test_effects.py` L62–110 (recover-known-effect-within-CI; downstream resampling has no effect); AgenTracer @256b19e `MetaGPT/Who_When_Data_Pipeline/attack_monitor.py` L830–869 (`PromptInjectionInterceptor`), `diagnose_monitor.py` L658–760 (`inject_correction`, rerun), `universal_framework/config.py` L104 (still-failed residue per round). A label-gated attribution requirement on an evolution pipeline and a fake-gateway fixture harness over typed IR components: `no-precedent / novel` (partial: CAR fixtures; AgenTracer injection; J4 KA datasets).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** RHO/AHE report gains without attribution (why not overturning: the gate is graded — proposals need only a hypothesis; the requirement bites at `confirmatory` acceptance, which ADR-0046 §7 already makes expensive); synthetic truth is "too easy" (fixtures certify estimators exactly as J4's KA do; KA-I7-7 uses a real model with truth by injection); the cost of attribution on every accepted change (M1 designed ablation is the minimum and is one `retirement`-shaped experiment; trajectory-local methods are optional and priced by F4).
- **(d) Conditionality:** native candidates (hosted coordinates: `n/a{class}`; OQ-382 owns whether I5 searches over them); `attribution_quality` assumes a configuration-level paired Δ under `MatchSpec` exists; fixture pass rates assume binary outcomes and the Stage-3 suite sizes (placeholders until OQ-WS-I7-01); the label gate assumes WS-I5's failure-hypothesis stage exists as briefed (concurrent workstream; OQ-WS-I7-05).
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** C2 / Stage 5 — `attribution_quality` registry entry, fake-gateway fixture harness, KA-I7-4/-10/-11; C4 / Stage 6 — `attribution_ref` and the label gate in the pipeline, `TargetMismatch`, KA-I7-1/-2/-3/-5/-7/-8/-9/-12; KA-I7-6 gates M5's promotion out of `exploratory`.

## Consequences

- WS-I5: adds `attribution_ref`, the label gate and `TargetMismatch` to its failure-hypothesis/acceptance stages; the "smallest corrective intervention" becomes a recorded, testable claim.
- WS-I2: `attribution_quality` gains its estimator and `n/a` reasons; no rename.
- WS-I6: debt records may cite attribution reports; retirement tests inherit an expected Δ.
- WS-J3: fixture `ExperimentSpec`s for KA-I7 join the built-in kinds' fixture set.
- WS-K2: evolvability views render `attribution_quality` with its interval and the candidate's label.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Relaxing the gate to hypothesis-only is a MUST-data threshold change but re-admits unverifiable acceptance and is not recommended; the estimator can be replaced by a new registry version; fixtures are additive.
