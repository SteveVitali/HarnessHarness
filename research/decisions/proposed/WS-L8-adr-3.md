# ADR-NNNN — Ownership, accountability, escalation routing, fleet observability, and the C4-vs-deferred partition of the organizational layer: owner-as-principal with acknowledged handoff, the accountability record as an audit projection, `EscalationPolicy` chains as budgeted `message_human` effects with legitimate-responder resolution, `FleetView` and class-scoped fleet metrics, the optional irreversibility ceiling, `lab/org-policy-v1` as the removal test, and deferrals D-1…D-6 with entry tests

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-L8 (with WS-H7 for grants/explanations, WS-H6 for audit obligations, WS-L2/I1/I2 for dimensions and metrics, WS-K1/K2 for rendering, WS-J3 for the recipe, WS-L6 for retention) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-L8-1, ADR-L8-2, ADR-0017 (widening only from human origin), ADR-0033/0035 (`principal` minting; endorsement basis list), ADR-0039/0040/0041 (dimensions, pools, `MatchSpec`), ADR-0044/0045/0047 (metric declarations; veto; instrument judges), ADR-0066/0067/0068 (`lifecycle.escalation.*`, anchors, completeness), ADR-0069/0070/0071 (Π-12, `ApproverGrant`, `Explanation`, `NarrowingHint`), ADR-0116/0117 (judge independence/calibration), ADR-0131 (`Cue.woken`; external never endorses), ADR-0154/0156/0157 (experiments; comparison reports), ADR-0165 (hosted accounting), ADR-0174 (service callers); CF-WS-L8-04, CF-WS-L8-05, CF-WS-L8-06; OQ-017, OQ-027, OQ-182, OQ-WS-L8-02, OQ-WS-L8-03, OQ-WS-L8-06; R-2.12.6, R-2.8.6, R-2.8.7, R-2.9.1, R-2.9.2

## Context

Doc 2 §10 names accountability, escalation, ownership and observability as the open organizational questions. The ratified contracts supply the primitives but not the organization: `principal` is minted "only for the run's human principal" (ADR-0033); approval endorses one effect and `ApproverGrant` is "the only route by which a human other than the run's principal decides … the hook for WS-L8's rotations/on-call" (ADR-0070 D6); `lifecycle.escalation.raised/resolved` is audit-grade and obligated for abandoned/unknown effects and hard budget exhaustion (ADR-0066 D5) but has no route; approvals are budgeted and their exhaustion escalates (ADR-0071); cost totals are views (ADR-0039/0040). Production practice for accountability is SRE incident management — one commander who holds state, explicit acknowledged handoff, a living document as the record (S-WS-L8-03); Symphony routes by `assignee_id` and escalates ambiguity by assignment + mention (S-108 `.codex/skills/land/SKILL.md`) but records no accountability; Cursor's write-up states nothing about ownership, review, cost visibility or limits (S-054, fetched). Tier-A human-factors results bound the design: automation should be allocated per function at a level (S-WS-L8-01), and the human left to handle what was not automated has been kept out of practice by the automation itself (S-WS-L8-02) — so an escalation must carry evidence. A `provisional` preprint (S-WS-L8-05) identifies fleet-level risk overdraw under per-effect gates and leaves pricing open.

## Options considered

1. **Team ownership (a group principal) with anyone on the team able to approve.** Rejected: ADR-0033 mints `principal` for one human; group approvals produce the unreachable-owner failure SRE practice exists to prevent; team participation is already expressible as owner-issued `ApproverGrant`s.
2. **Escalation as notification only (post to the tracker/chat; whoever answers, answers).** Rejected: an `external`-authority reply cannot resolve anything (ADR-0035/0131); no bounded chain, no record of who was responsible, and a fatigue amplifier (S-053).
3. **A fleet-level value-at-risk budget priced per effect (the S-WS-L8-05 mechanism).** Rejected now: the pricing problem is open by the authors' own statement and the evidence is one two-week-old preprint; admitted only as an optional unit-priced counter with a deferred pricing item.
4. **A general workflow/BPM engine or a human directory inside the Core.** Rejected: non-goals N1/N5; identity providers and rotations are external systems; deferred with entry tests.
5. **Single human owner as the activation's `principal` with acknowledged handoff; accountability as an audit projection; escalation as a data-declared ordered chain executed as budgeted `message_human` effects with legitimate-responder resolution; fleet observability and cost as projections; an optional irreversibility counter; the fleet's policies as Lab factors; explicit deferrals** — chosen.

## Decision

1. **Ownership (O-1…O-5).** O-1 `WorkItem.owner` is one `PrincipalRef` resolving to a human and is the `principal` of every activation of the item; no `principal`-class content exists for the item that the owner did not author or endorse. O-2 every `ApproverGrant` used on the item has `delegated_by = owner`, attenuating (ADR-0070 D6). O-3 `service`/`agent` callers (ADR-0174) may create and wake items but are `delegate`; their items are `blocked{missing_owner}` until a human owner is assigned. O-4 ownership transfer is a two-event handshake — `control.work_item.owner_changed{from, to, basis ∈ {approval, policy_rule}}` then `owner_acknowledged` by `to` — dispatch is blocked in between, and the change revokes the item's approval leases (policy-fingerprint change, ADR-0071 D2); open activations receive the change as `Cue.woken{follow_up}` at their next decision point. O-5 the owner is the default first escalation target and the only party who may resolve `escalation_unresolved`.
2. **Accountability record (projection).** `accountability_record(work_item_id) → {owner_history, activations[{run_id, activation_no, principal, attendance, stop_reason}], decisions[security.permission.decided ∪ granted ∪ lease.* for the item's effects, with decider/grant_ref], external_effects[{effect_id, capability semantic_id, effective_risk_class, decider, terminal}], escalations[{raised, notifications: [effect_id], resolved{by, resolution}}], handoffs[{state, entered_by, exited_by}], budget (ADR-0040 view), audit: AuditCompleteness over the activations (ADR-0068), children[]}` — every element an `EventRef` or an audit-grade envelope; content-free (ADR-0066 Rule C); covered by `verify` through the activations' chains plus the fleet-anchor obligation (CF-WS-L8-04). No store, no identity kind, no new class beyond `control.work_item.*`.
3. **Escalation routing (E-1…E-8).** Record: ADR-0066 `lifecycle.escalation.raised{subject, severity, addressed_to, cause}` / `resolved{resolved_by, resolution}`, with `cause` extended by dialect to `EscalationCause = {abandoned_effect, unknown_at_finished, budget_hard_exceeded, approval_pending_deadline, approvals_exhausted, repeated_denials, blocked{BlockReason}, stalled_max_retries, heal_exhausted, owner_unacknowledged, source_conflict, veto_failed}`. Route: `EscalationPolicy{targets: [EscalationTarget{ref ∈ {owner, grant(ApproverGrantRef), principal(PrincipalRef), sink(SinkRef)}, after_ms, on ⊆ EscalationCause, evidence ⊆ {last_decision, effect_ids, budget_state, blocked_reason, source_state}}], final ∈ {block, cancel, stop_activation}, max_open_per_owner, hint_interval}` (MUST-data). E-1 raising is kernel-produced (Rule P) and precedes any notification. E-2 each notification is a `message_human` effect through Π (audited; counted; egress-mediated for external sinks). E-3 `after_ms` deadlines are `timer` subscriptions (ledger-derived; survive restart). E-4 resolution requires a responder whose endorsement would be legitimate for the item — the owner (`principal`) or a grant covering the cause's domain; an `external`-authority reply wakes the item (`Cue.woken`) and is recorded as `annotated`, never as `resolved` (`IllegitimateEndorsement` otherwise). E-5 `resolution ∈ {acknowledged, amended{budget | permission | owner}, reassigned, cancelled, expired}`; `amended` cites the ledgered `amend` (ADR-0040 §6 / ADR-0071 D4). E-6 chain exhaustion applies `final`; the item is `blocked{escalation_unresolved}` — never dropped, never auto-widened (ADR-0017). E-7 evidence rendered to the human is record-derived (`Explanation` shape, ADR-0071 D6); model text only as a labelled `Text{authority: delegate}` leaf. E-8 open escalations are capped per owner (`max_open_per_owner`); beyond the cap new causes coalesce (`coalesced_into`), and `NarrowingHint`s surface at most once per `hint_interval` (OQ-182 input).
4. **Fleet observability.** `fleet_view(fleet, at?) → FleetView{items[{work_item_id, state, owner, source_state, activation?, activation_no, blocked?, handoff?, escalations_open, budget{consumed, remaining}, last_activity, external_effects_count, children_open}], capacity{max_concurrent, running, by_state, retries_due[], suspended}, escalations{open, by_owner}, totals{by_owner, by_source, by_state}: TokenVector + Money{confidence} + time.{working_ms, human_wait_ms} + env.{active_ms, reserved_ms}, watermark}` — `project`-pure over the fleet run, goal nodes and activation checkpoint views with a `derived_from` watermark and rebuild equality (ADR-0026). V-1 every number is ledger-computed (ADR-0044; nothing sampled). V-2 `blocked`, `handoff`, `suspended` render distinctly from `active`. V-3 hosted activations render ledger-only fields `n/a{observability}` (T-LCD-15). V-4 K1/K2 project this record; the approval inbox (WS-K2 V8) joins on `work_item_id` through the pending permission's run. V-5 `refresh` = `reconcile(now)` and is ledgered. Metric declarations (ADR-0045; deterministic detectors; `requires_observability ∋ events`; `applies_to_classes = {native, hosted-with-declared-capabilities}`; `charged_to = subject` unless noted): `time_to_first_handoff_ms`, `human_wait_ms_per_item`, `activations_per_item`, `escalations_per_item`, `escalation_ack_latency_ms{severity}`, `blocked_ratio{reason}`, `owner_acknowledge_latency_ms`, `external_effects_per_item{effective_risk_class}`, `message_human_per_item`, `source_conflict_rate`, `spend_per_item{confidence}`, `fleet_utilisation`, `reconcile_latency_ms` (instrument), `stale_dispatch_rate`; `unowned_dispatch_count` is a `veto: true` trace predicate.
5. **Fleet cost and the optional irreversibility ceiling.** Totals are L2 views over the fleet pool (`mode = pool`) with per-item goal slices; `Money` carries `confidence`; hosted activations per ADR-0165. The counter dimension `effects.external_irreversible` (count of `irreversible ∧ scope = external` effects reaching `committed`) is added to the ADR-0039 kernel list by dialect bump (or registered as `ext.*` pending OQ-WS-L8-03; CF-WS-L8-05); a definition *may* set a hard ceiling on an owner pool; exhaustion converts the marginal effect to `deny{reason: irreversibility_ceiling}` at `reserve` (ADR-0040 reserve-before-spend, root-first) and raises an escalation; unset ⇒ no behaviour change. This is the S-WS-L8-05 admission-control shape at unit price; pricing is deferred (D-3).
6. **The fleet as a Lab object (C4 matched-budget conditionality).** `lab/org-policy-v1` (ADR-0156 recipe shape): factors `state_map`, `unattended_policy`, `escalation_policy`, `max_activations` at `configuration-level`; `MatchSpec{dimensions: [tokens.*, approvals.requested, time.human_wait_ms, spend, message_human], mode: matched_cap}`; responders are a declared factor (`attendance`; a simulated responder is an instrument model with `CriticDeclaration.independence` and a `CalibrationRecord` — ADR-0116/0117, `charged_to = instrument`); headline metrics deterministic (decision 4); every claim a `ComparisonReport` (ADR-0157). The defaults chosen in ADR-L8-2 (`defer` + chain, foreground cascade, continuation delay) are conditioned rules with assumption-debt records whose removal test is this recipe; `register` refuses an arm without the `MatchSpec` (T-LCD-14).
7. **C4 vs deferred.** *Specified at C4 (Stages 4–6):* everything in ADR-L8-1/L8-2 and decisions 1–6 here, with a `memory`/file tracker fixture and a generic signed-webhook ingress as the reference adapters; one real tracker adapter only as an L5 plugin. *Deferred, each with an entry test, recorded here rather than silently:* **D-1 human directory and rotations** (resolving "team X on-call" to a `PrincipalRef`; schedule-based targets) — an identity-provider concern outside every ratified contract and inside N1; entry test: a `PrincipalDirectory` component-class contract proposed by ADR with two out-of-process precedents; until then `ApproverGrant`s and static `EscalationTarget`s are the mechanism (CF-WS-L8-06). **D-2 multi-tenant fleets** — surface sessions are single-principal at C2 (ADR-0174; WS-K2); entry test: a tenant boundary expressible as a `readers` partition with no new authority class. **D-3 value-at-risk pricing** of fleet irreversibility — one `provisional` preprint that calls pricing open; entry test: a pricing rule with an assumption-debt record and a matched-budget comparison against the unit counter (`ComparisonReport` with `benefit_kind = artifact_benefit`). **D-4 dispatch prioritization by expected value** — WS-F4; `SchedulingPolicy.order` is the slot. **D-5 reconciler-side source mirroring** — needs a principal-less writer (ADR-0053 D-5 `policy_rule` procedure); entry test: AC-L8-06 passing with the procedure's effects in the record. **D-6 cross-item shared-state coordination** — WS-F5.

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-02 (escalation evidence typed; model text a labelled leaf), T-LCD-05 (every fleet default carries a debt record with `lab/org-policy-v1` as removal test; the irreversibility ceiling is optional data), T-LCD-06 (no native field; hosted activations unchanged), T-LCD-07 (adapter/hosted capabilities tri-state; metrics `n/a` where undeclared), T-LCD-12 (projections and records; out of process), T-LCD-13 (hints/escalation context reach a model only through delivery-ledgered rules), T-LCD-14 (fleet comparisons only under `MatchSpec`; the ceiling never loosens a budget), T-LCD-15 (all fleet metrics class-scoped; typed `n/a`). Not applicable: T-01/-03/-04/-08/-09/-10/-11. ADR-0024 ladder: `EscalationPolicy`, cascade/hint parameters, the ceiling and the recipe are MUST-data; owner-as-principal minting, the handshake, raising, legitimate-responder checks and the projections are MUST-code.

## Reversibility

**medium** — ownership and escalation are records and policies over ratified events; changing the route is a data change and changing the handshake is a dialect bump. The one hard-to-reverse choice is owner-as-principal (it fixes who authored `principal` content across a work item's activations); reversing it would require a delegated-principal authority sub-class (OQ-177 territory) and re-labelling — which is why the deferral of directories keeps the choice small rather than weakening it.
