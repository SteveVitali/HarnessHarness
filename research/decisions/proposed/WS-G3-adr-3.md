# ADR-NNNN — Critic calibration and cost governance: the `CalibrationRecord` with expiry as assumption debt (resolves OQ-123's shape); balanced-position and panel rules; exploratory status without calibration; every critic placement is a conditioned rule whose removal test is the matched-budget `critic_experiment`; `budget_share_cap` and the deterministic-first ordering

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-G3 (consumers: WS-I2, WS-J3, WS-J4, WS-F4, WS-H7, WS-I5, WS-I6, WS-I3, WS-C3, WS-D2, WS-D4, WS-E2) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** WS-G3-adr-1, WS-G3-adr-2, ADR-0047 (calibration_ref; exploratory; (c)(ii)), ADR-0045 (`PreRegistration`; `replicate_reducer`; `interval_method`; `n/a{no_detector}`), ADR-0046 (`MatchSpec`; `search_budget.judge_calls`), ADR-0039/0040 (`evaluator_calls`; `charged_to`; `BudgetNode`; soft thresholds as `HarnessRule`s), ADR-0020 (`AssumptionDebtRecord`; expiry states), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05 removal test; T-LCD-14), ADR-0031 (`EffectAttributes.reversibility` as failure cost), ADR-0038 (bundle pins), ADR-0036 (fingerprints; OQ-111); OQ-123, OQ-121, OQ-125, OQ-WS-D4-03, OQ-WS-H7-04, OQ-012 (F4); R-2.7.3, R-2.6.4, R-2.9.2

## Context

ADR-0047 made a `calibration_ref` mandatory for gating uses and left "protocol and minimum agreement" to OQ-123, assigned to WS-G3. The audit finds no evaluation plane that calibrates a judge against a deterministic oracle and stores the record; the best practice on disk is Anthropic's manual loop — "read the evaluator's logs, find examples where its judgment diverged from mine, and update the QA's prompt" (S-046) — and Inspect's majority panels that withhold a vote on parse failure. The academic evidence fixes what a calibration must measure: agreement with humans is achievable but biased by position (66/80 flips — S-WS-G3-03), verbosity and self-preference (S-WS-G3-01/-02); critics hallucinate findings at a tunable precision/recall point (S-WS-G3-06); leniency drifts (S-046); a judge's agreement can vanish when its evidence is only prose (S-WS-G3-07/-08). On cost, S-046 reports a 20× harness cost and that "for tasks within [the generator's] boundary, the evaluator became unnecessary overhead" while it "continued to give real lift" at the capability edge — the assumption-debt pattern doc 2 §5.9 names; HarnessLens argues verification should be spent where behaviour changed (S-089, `provisional`); OpenHands warns that per-action critics are "significantly slower". WS-F4 (Phase 4) owns the value-of-compute estimator and needs measurable inputs now.

## Options considered

1. **No calibration; trust strong judges** (>80 % human agreement, S-WS-G3-01). *Rejected:* the agreement was measured on chat preferences, not agentic traces; ADR-0047 already requires the record; the biases are documented and specific.
2. **Calibrate judges against other judges.** *Rejected:* correlated failure (doc 2 §8); a judge-of-judge inherits the same biases; reference must be deterministic or human.
3. **Fixed universal thresholds** (one κ for all). *Rejected:* metric families differ (binary gate vs graded rubric); thresholds are pre-registration items per family (ADR-0045).
4. **Critics as permanent fixtures** (always-on evaluator). *Rejected:* S-046's own expiry event; T-LCD-05 requires a removal test for every conditioned component; cost is accounted, never assumed.
5. **A typed calibration record with a deterministic/human reference, per-family pre-registered thresholds, expiry as assumption debt, exploratory status without it; balanced-position and panel rules; critic placements as conditioned rules whose removal test is the matched-budget `critic_experiment`; `budget_share_cap`; deterministic-first ordering (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`CalibrationRecord`** `{calibration_id, critic_ref (version_id), judge_snapshot_fingerprint, labelled_set_ref (content-addressed; `task_split_hash` predating any gate use — `LeakedCalibrationSet`), n_per_verdict_class, reference_oracle ∈ {executable, end_state, trace_predicate, human_panel} (`JudgeAsReference` refused), agreement{statistic ∈ {cohen_kappa, f1_per_class, spearman, mcc}, value, interval, method}, error_rates{fn_rate, fp_rate per verdict class, uncited_finding_rate}, position_consistency (comparative), self_consistency (k re-samples), self_preference_check? (same-family), evidence_ablation{agreement delta with `claimed` items removed}, calibrated_at, expiry: AssumptionDebtRecord.expiry_condition ⊇ {snapshot fingerprint changed, rubric version changed, task family changed, age > recalibration_period}, status ∈ {active, expiring, expired}}` is a first-class, content-addressed artifact pinned in the bundle (WS-I3). The calibration run is a Lab experiment charged to the instrument.
2. **Use rules.** A `use = gate` judge must hold `status = active` at every invocation; otherwise it degrades to `pass` (fail-closed for `authorize`, WS-H7) or `inconclusive` and the run is annotated `critic_uncalibrated`. A `use = report` judge without an active record is `exploratory`: its metric is reported under its own name and renders `n/a{no_detector}` for any gate or C4-admissibility purpose (ADR-0047 (c)(ii) made operational).
3. **Thresholds are pre-registered per metric family** in `PreRegistration` (ADR-0045), never global. Stage 4 placeholders (review items, not constants): `n_per_verdict_class ≥ 50`; binary verdicts κ ≥ 0.6; gate critics at `authorize` `fn_rate ≤ 0.10` (the bound to beat is the ~17 % production classifier miss rate, S-053); graded rubrics Spearman ≥ 0.6; all with intervals (ADR-0045 methods); `recalibration_period` 90 days.
4. **Bias controls by contract.** `comparative` verdict types are evaluated in both orders and aggregated (balanced-position calibration, S-WS-G3-03); a `position_consistency` below the pre-registered floor voids the record. Gate judges default to a panel `n ≥ 3` with `majority` withholding on parse failure (Inspect precedent; ADR-0045 reducers); dissent is stored. `evidence_ablation` is mandatory so a judge that agrees with the reference only when it can read the beneficiary's prose is visible.
5. **Cost accounting.** Every critic invocation posts `control.budget.consumed{dimension = evaluator_calls}` and its token/spend charges with `charged_to` (ADR-0039); runtime critics draw from their own `BudgetNode{mode = slice}` with a declared `budget_share_cap` (fraction of the run's soft budget); exceeding it yields `pass`/`inconclusive` for the critic and never a hard stop of the beneficiary (`budget_exhausted` is distinct from `oracle_failure`, Codex `ExhaustedReviewBudget` precedent); the Lab reports `harness_overhead.verification`, `evaluator_calls`, `false_stop_rate` and `missed_failure_rate` per configuration.
6. **Deterministic-first ordering.** At every decision point the admissible order is deterministic validator → programmatic trace/end-state predicate → judge, and a judge may be placed only where the deterministic detector renders `n/a{no_detector}` (ADR-0014/0047); a judge placed beside an existing deterministic check is a reported defect (`RedundantJudge`), not a variant.
7. **Placements are conditioned rules.** A runtime critic is `HarnessRule{kind: critic_gate, conditioned_on: ProfileRef?, placement{decision_point, trigger, max_iterations?, budget_share_cap, admission_mode}, assumption_debt{hypothesis: "the beneficiary at this profile fails class X at rate ≥ p at this decision point", evidence_refs: [calibration_id, critic_experiment report], expiry_condition: "Δ task_success at matched budget ≤ pre-registered margin", removal_test_ref: critic_experiment}}`. `critic_experiment(definition, placement, design) → ComparisonReport[]` runs arms `{critic on, critic off}` × placement × critic snapshot (× admission mode) under a `MatchSpec` and is the T-LCD-05 removal test; its Δ on deterministic `task_success`, overhead, approvals and the calibration error rates are WS-F4's inputs to the decision rule `P(caught) × cost(uncaught) − cost(critic) − P(false stop) × cost(rework) > 0`, with `cost(uncaught)` read from the affected effects' `reversibility` (ADR-0031).
8. **Concurrent answers.** OQ-WS-H7-04: denial-window defaults (`max_consecutive_denies 3`, `max_total_denies 20`) are conditioned rules on the reviewer profile with debt records and Stage 4 pre-registered values. OQ-WS-D4-03: judged contradiction detectors on free-text memories follow decisions 1–4 with `reference_oracle = human_panel` or keyed-fact `trace_predicate` on the labelled subset per memory kind. WS-E2's emulated pre-check: `critic_kind = emulated`, veto-only, calibrated against `end_state` on real executions of the same scenarios (ToolEmu's 68.8 % validity is the reason the reference must be real, not emulated).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-05 (calibration expiry and placements are assumption debt; `critic_experiment` is the removal recipe), T-LCD-14 (`critic_experiment` under a `MatchSpec`; judge calls in `search_budget`; `evaluator_calls` budgeted), T-LCD-15 (judged metrics class-scoped; exploratory renders `n/a{no_detector}`), T-LCD-09 (critic snapshot/placement/admission mode as explicit factors), T-LCD-12 (records and operations). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-04/-06/-07/-08/-10/-11/-13. Rung on the ADR-0024 ladder: the record schema, use rules, ordering check and experiment operation are MUST-code; thresholds, periods, caps and placements are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** position bias and its three calibrations — S-WS-G3-03 (Tier A); self-preference and self-enhancement — S-WS-G3-01/-02 (Tier A); hallucinated findings and the precision/recall trade-off — S-WS-G3-06 (Tier C); leniency drift corrected by disagreement-driven tuning; evaluator worth conditional on the capability edge; 20× cost — S-046 (Tier B); evidence access determines agreement — S-WS-G3-07/-08 (Tier C); classifier miss rate bound — S-053 (Tier B); emulated evaluators partly invalid (68.8 %) — S-098 (Tier A); verification budget should be attributable to behaviour change — S-089 (`provisional`, mechanism only); per-action critics are costly — S-WS-G3-12 (source comment); the ratified basis — ADR-0047 (calibration_ref, exploratory), ADR-0045 (pre-registration, reducers), ADR-0046 (`MatchSpec`), ADR-0039/0040 (accounting, budget nodes), ADR-0020 (debt records), ADR-0007 T-LCD-05/-14. No performance number is load-bearing; the placeholders in decision 3 are explicitly review items.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/scorer/_model.py` @ 75f4891 (grader panels; `reducer = "majority"` withholds on parse failure; out-of-set grade leaves the sample unscored), `scorer/_metrics/std.py` (interval methods, via WS-I2); `codex/codex-rs/core/src/guardian/{request_budget,input_budget}.rs` @ 0735c51 (`ExhaustedReviewBudget`; reviewer prompt measured against its own limit) and `guardian/review.rs` L180–229 (consecutive-denial window); `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/critic/base.py` @ 3fc7b22 (`mode ∈ {finish_and_message, all_actions}` with the cost warning; `IterativeRefinementConfig` bounds); `hal-harness/hal/utils/llm_log_analyzer.py` @ 16bb03e (result cache keyed by `(analysis_type, model, content)`; `analysis_model` recorded) and `harbor/src/harbor/analyze/analyzer.py` @ 7d5285b (judged analysis as a separate, model-recorded job). A stored calibration record with expiry, the exploratory rule, `RedundantJudge` and `critic_experiment` as a removal test are `no-precedent / novel`.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) labelled subsets do not exist for novel rubrics — answered by exploratory status (ADR-0047 (c)(ii)) and by admitting `human_panel` references; (ii) the Stage 4 labelling cost is real — answered by charging it to the instrument, pinning it for reuse, and by the deterministic-first rule that limits how many judges need calibration; (iii) fixed κ thresholds are arbitrary — answered by per-family pre-registration with placeholders only; (iv) panels triple judge cost — answered by requiring panels only for gate judges and allowing `n = 1` reporting judges with wider intervals; (v) HarnessLens suggests calibration/verification budgets can be cut by attributable selection — accepted as a C4 refinement of *which* tasks to calibrate on, `provisional`, not load-bearing; (vi) the 20× cost figure is one vendor's open-ended task — accepted; it motivates the removal test, not a magnitude. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** placeholders assume coding/terminal suites with deterministic references at Stage 3–4 (WS-I4); for domains without deterministic oracles `reference_oracle = human_panel` is the only option and calibration is correspondingly costlier; `budget_share_cap` and iteration caps are profile-conditioned (debt records); the decision rule assumes WS-B2/H1 declare `reversibility` on affected effects; independent of model tier otherwise.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C0 / Stage 1** — `CalibrationRecord` schema, exploratory rule, `RedundantJudge` check, `evaluator_calls` posting (ADR-0039 already), `critic_gate` rule shape; **C0 / Stage 3** — `critic_experiment` for programmatic critics (T-LCD-05/-14 executable); **C1 / Stage 4** — calibration runs for the WS-H7 reviewer and completion gates, panels, balanced-position rule, denial-window defaults (AC-G3-6/-8/-9); **C2 / Stage 4** — calibration of judged detectors (D2/D3/D4/D5) and emulated pre-checks (E2); **C4 / Stage 6** — WS-F4's estimator over the recorded inputs; WS-I5 gates consume active records only.

## Consequences

- WS-I2/J4 add `calibration_ref` status and `exploratory` labelling to scorecard rendering; WS-J3 refuses gate placements without an active record; WS-I3 pins calibration records and labelled sets in the bundle.
- WS-H7's reviewer, WS-E2's pre-check, WS-D2's fidelity judge and WS-D4's contradiction judge each ship with a Stage 4 calibration plan; WS-I6 owns recalibration expiry; WS-C3 owns profile-conditioned thresholds.
- WS-F4 receives four recorded quantities per placement and a standard experiment; no separate estimator is defined here.
- The readiness report's T-LCD-05 row for critics cites `critic_experiment`; T-LCD-14 cites the `MatchSpec` on every critic arm.

## Reversibility

**High for thresholds and periods (MUST-data, pre-registered per family), medium for the record and the exploratory rule.** Dropping the calibration requirement after Stage 4 would let uncalibrated judged numbers gate C4 decisions — the correlated-failure path doc 2 §8 warns about — and every stored judged value would need re-labelling; keeping the record is cheap because it is content-addressed and reusable across experiments until an expiry trigger fires.
