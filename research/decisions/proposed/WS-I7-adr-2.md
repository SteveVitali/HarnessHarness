# ADR-NNNN — The attribution method catalogue M0–M5 with validity conditions V1–V12, coalition-credit rules, the point-of-commitment locus as a labelled heuristic, and `AttributionReport/1` as an additive extension of WS-J4's A10

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I7 (with WS-J4 for A10 and the interval kernel, WS-J3 for design kinds, WS-B4 for containment) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0157 (A10, A3, A6, A12), ADR-0158 (estimators, `contrast`, TOST), ADR-0159 (A6 benefit decomposition), ADR-0134 (deferred effects in branches), ADR-0135, ADR-0046, ADR-0047, ADR-0117, ADR-0012 (no interpolation), ADR-0013 (granularities), ADR-0155 (drift bracket), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-02/-05/-09/-13/-14/-15); OQ-WS-I7-03, OQ-WS-I7-04; CF-WS-I7-01, CF-WS-I7-05; R-2.9.7, R-2.10.4

## Context

ADR-0157 ratified A10 `attribution(arm, ablation_design) → AttributionReport{slots[{component_class, LOI, LOO, interval}], unattributed_share, budget_allocation_ref}` as *designed ablation* and deferred counterfactual attribution to WS-I7. The research shows five distinct methods with different validity conditions: whole-run leave-one-in/leave-one-out (S-092: value localized to one slot; a budget-splitting trap; no interaction analysis), trajectory-local contrastive resampling with a point-of-commitment locus (S-WS-I7-01), coalition/Shapley credit with a non-obvious correctness rule — coalition values must not be cached across permutations or the reported confidence is false (S-WS-I7-01 `shapley.py`; shap `_permutation.py` uses fresh evaluations per permutation and antithetic pairing), difference-of-differences interaction already ratified as J4's `contrast` (ADR-0158 D5), and a mediation split whose degeneracies are proven (S-WS-I7-03 Props. 2–3). Interaction credit is the axiomatic Shapley interaction index (Grabisch & Roubens 1999). A single report shape must carry all of them with typed `n/a`.

## Options considered

1. **Designed ablation only (keep A10 as ratified).** *Rejected:* cannot say *where in a run* a component mattered, which the evolution pipeline's "smallest corrective intervention" needs; misses interactions by construction (S-092 does not measure them).
2. **Largest-magnitude ranking of per-step effects as "the cause".** *Rejected:* names an inert upstream step on planted chains (run-forward confound).
3. **Shapley with cached coalition values for economy.** *Rejected:* correlated marginals collapse the CLT interval to zero — false confidence (CAR's documented rule).
4. **A closed catalogue M0–M5 with per-method validity conditions, an explicit locus rule labelled as a heuristic, Shapley rules (no cache, antithetic, truncation, efficiency check), and one additive report shape (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Catalogue (closed; growth by dialect bump).** **M0** observational hypothesis (never a value); **M1** designed ablation = J4 A10 over typed `ComponentTarget`s at `run_start` (`LOI`/`LOO`; `design.kind ∈ {one_factor_at_a_time, paired}`; `kind ∈ {comparative, retirement}`; per-target `SearchBudgetRecord.allocation` rendered beside so the budget-splitting trap is visible); **M2** trajectory-local contrastive (one target, one fork point; factual vs counterfactual arm; `TE_marg` or `TE_crn`); **M3** coalition credit (`shapley` over a target set; permutation walks; v(S) = outcome with S held factual and the rest re-executed); **M4** paired-replay interaction = ADR-0158 `contrast` with `pairing_key = fork_point`, `cluster = task`, over fully crossed probed cells (no new estimator); **M5** mediation split (`DE`, `ME = TE_crn − DE`; exploratory until KA-I7-6 passes).
2. **Validity conditions V1–V12 (refusal or `n/a`, never a warning).** V1 replay validity ≥ the estimand floor (ADR-1); V2 coherent fork point ≤ `earliest_affected_seq`; V3 one `MatchSpec`, suffix budgets equal; V4 `change_rate > 0` at the fork point (`PolicyCollapsed` otherwise); V5 outcome oracle in ADR-0047's deterministic headline classes, or the effect is labelled `judged` and inherits ADR-0117 calibration/expiry (never headline; OQ-WS-I7-04); V6 no counterfactual branch paused `awaiting_promotion` on a deferred effect for an outcome metric (`n/a{not_run}`; process effects still reported); V7 `fingerprint_match ≠ mismatch` on both arms and within the ADR-0155 drift bracket; V8 weak coupling honoured or `fork_policy = run_start`; V9 native rows; hosted ⇒ `n/a{class}` except `observation_substitution` at configuration granularity with `model_io` interception; V10 `TE_crn`/`DE` only with every post-fork source in `coupled_sources[]` with a measured agreement; V11 Shapley: no cross-permutation cache, antithetic pairing, `efficiency_check = Σφ − Δ_set` inside the report's interval; V12 multiplicity via ADR-0157 A12 (Holm over pre-registered targets, BH elsewhere).
3. **Coalition rules.** `n_permutations`, `samples_per_eval`, `antithetic = true`, `target_cap` (placeholder 8; OQ-WS-I7-03) are MUST-data; `estimate_rollouts = n_permutations · (2 if antithetic) · (|targets| + 1) · samples_per_eval` is exposed for WS-F4 pricing; the whole walk is reserved before the first rollout; `BudgetExceeded` yields a partial report with `budget_truncated = true` and `permutations_completed`; per-target CLT intervals over i.i.d. marginals; pairwise `interaction_index` (Shapley interaction index) reported as `interaction_share`; a coalition member without recorded, substitutable outputs makes the design `n/a{observability}`.
4. **Locus.** For M2 over `every_decision_point`, `locus = max{seq : effect_seq.interval excludes 0}` with `rule = point_of_commitment` and `label = heuristic(TE_marg)`; under `TE_crn` the alternative `rule = earliest_direct` (earliest target with non-zero `DE`) is computed when available; disagreement is reported, never resolved by the engine. A locus never appears without its per-target effects.
5. **Single-target effects over a set are not a decomposition.** `unattributed_share = Δ − Σ effects` is reported; only M3 values sum by construction.
6. **`AttributionReport/1` (additive to ADR-0157 A10; CF-WS-I7-01).** Fields: `subject{configuration_id, run_ids[], task_ids[]}`, `method`, `design_ref`, `targets[]`, `effects[{target, estimand, statistic, point, interval{method, level, substituted?}, n{factual_branches, counterfactual_branches, fork_points}, replay_mode, validity_mode, change_rate, noise_floor, coupled_sources[{source, coupling_agreement}], deferred_paused, label}]`, `shapley?{values[], efficiency_check, interaction_index[], permutations_completed, antithetic, budget_truncated}`, `locus?`, `unattributed_share`, `interaction_share`, `budget{reserved, rollouts, spend, search_ref, eval_ref}`, `assumptions{coupling_assumption, fork_policy}`, `multiplicity`, `label ∈ {confirmatory, exploratory, preview}`, `attribution_label ∈ {designed_ablation, causal_interventional, causal_coupled}`, `provenance{origin = instrument}`, `reasons[]`; stored as an `AnalysisRecord` (ADR-0162) with derived `bundle_ref` annotations; A10's existing `slots[]` is the M1 projection of `effects[]`. Branch-derived results rows gain `arm_role ∈ {factual, counterfactual}` and `intervention_ref?` (additive to `ResultsRow.lineage`).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-09 (M4 is the canned interaction analysis over explicit factors with fork-point pairing; per-target distributions), T-LCD-14 (every method under one `MatchSpec`; walk budgets reserved and reported), T-LCD-15 (typed `n/a` per method and class; the class-applicability row for A10 is preserved and extended), T-LCD-05 (M1 `retirement` is the "delete this rule" experiment), T-LCD-13 (compliance-stage targets: an intervention may substitute a delivered artefact and use A6's realized-benefit stages as the outcome), T-LCD-02 (`text_leaf` ablation), T-LCD-12. Not applicable: T-01/-03/-04/-06/-07/-08 (ADR-1)/-10/-11. **ADR-0024 rung:** the catalogue, V1–V12, the no-cache and truncation rules and the report shape are MUST-code; `n_permutations`, `samples_per_eval`, `target_cap`, margins are MUST-data. **Matched-budget conditionality:** all C4 rows are `ComparisonReport`s under `MatchSpec`; M3's walk spend is a `search_budget` (T-LCD-14).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** S-WS-I7-10 Shapley (Tier A; efficiency/symmetry/dummy/additivity), S-WS-I7-11 Castro et al. (Tier A; permutation sampling, CLT interval), S-WS-I7-12 Grabisch & Roubens (Tier A; interaction index), S-WS-I7-13 SHAP (Tier A); S-WS-I7-01 CAR (`provisional`; contrastive under-attributes an AND-interaction, Shapley recovers φ 0.44/0.45/≈ 0 with efficiency 0.909 vs 0.91; no-cache rule; point-of-commitment); S-WS-I7-03 (`provisional`; Props. 1–3; R12 exclusion rule); S-092 (`provisional`; LOI/LOO under identical budget; budget-splitting trap; no interaction analysis); ADR-0158 D5 (ratified `contrast`); ADR-0134 (deferred effects bound the reachable outcome).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** shap @0fe1ca7 `shap/explainers/_permutation.py` L165–221 (forward/backward walk, `outputs[i+1] − outputs[i]`), L223–226 (`max_evals ≥ 2n + 1` refusal); causal-agent-replay @db90fa2 `src/car/attribute/shapley.py` L77–199 (antithetic walks, no cache, `BudgetExceeded` truncation, `efficiency_sum`), L235–249 (`estimate_rollouts`), `src/car/attribute/contrastive.py` L112–116 (locus = last significant rescuer), `src/car/attribute/sampling.py` L44–47 (charge before rollout), `tests/test_attribution.py` L98 (`test_contrastive_underattributes_the_interaction`). The catalogue as a closed set with per-method validity and one report shape over IR components: `no-precedent / novel` (partial: CAR's contrastive + Shapley over steps; J4's A10 over slots).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** Shapley's exponential cost and Monte-Carlo variance (why not overturning: on-demand, capped targets, reserved and truncated with truncation reported; single-target methods remain available with `unattributed_share`); the locus rule's 87 % disagreement with the CRN alternative on planted chains (labelled heuristic; both rules reported); S-092's evidence that most slots are individually null makes trajectory-local methods look unnecessary (why not: null-under-ablation and pivotal-at-one-decision-point are different claims, and the evolution pipeline needs the second); judged outcomes are noisy attribution targets (V5: labelled, calibrated, never headline).
- **(d) Conditionality:** native participants; M2/M3/M5 require the ADR-0133 snapshot coupling and ADR-0135 recording rules; M5 and provider-side `crn` require probed `seed_honoured`/batch-invariance (OQ-WS-I7-02); M1 assumes ADR-0046's whole-run protocol; constants assume the Stage-3 suite sizes (placeholders).
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** C2 / Stage 5 — M1 over typed targets, `AttributionReport/1`, row fields, KA-I7-4/-10/-11 (with WS-J4/J3); C4 / Stage 6 — M2, M3, M4 execution, locus, KA-I7-1/-2/-3/-5/-8/-9; M5 exploratory until KA-I7-6.

## Consequences

- WS-J4: ADR-0157 A10 grows additively (dialect bump); the class-applicability row stays "component-level only"; A3 gains `pairing_key = fork_point` as an admissible pairing.
- WS-J5: two additive row fields; `AnalysisRecord` stores the report.
- WS-F4: prices M3 walks from `estimate_rollouts`.
- WS-K2: attribution views show per-target effect strips with estimand, validity, `n/a` reasons, the locus badge and disagreement.
- WS-I5: consumes `attribution_label` (companion ADR-3).

## Reversibility

**High.** Methods and fields are additive; removing M5 costs nothing; removing the no-cache rule would silently falsify intervals and is not recommended; the locus rule can be replaced by a better-evidenced rule as a new `rule` value.
