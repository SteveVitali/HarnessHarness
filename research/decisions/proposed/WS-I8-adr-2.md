# WS-I8-adr-2 — The compatibility guard: `snapshot_scope` on assumption-debt records, `CompatibilityRecord`, the harness regression suite as the post-import gate, guards at bind and run, provider-drift path first

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I8 (with WS-I6, WS-I5, WS-C1, WS-C3, WS-J3/J4) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002, ADR-0014, ADR-0017, ADR-0020, ADR-0046, ADR-0053, ADR-0118, ADR-0120, ADR-0124…0126, ADR-0143, ADR-0154…0161; OQ-026; OQ-WS-I8-04/-05; CF-WS-I8-01/-02/-03; R-2.9.8; T-LCD-05/-09/-13/-14

## Context

Doc 2 §5.9: "Harness changes should be tagged with the model behaviors they assume; post-training should include harness regression suites." S-096 (`provisional`) reports a compatibility break — imitation training regressed a policy 4–30 points on every task because it "no longer fit the harness that had been optimized around the original model"; S-047 and S-049 (Tier B) report the mirror image — rules written for one snapshot became dead weight on the next. S-088 shows compliance (activation/following) is tier-dependent, so a weight change can silently change which rules are followed. ADR-0020/0124/0126 already give every conditioned rule an `AssumptionDebtRecord` with a `model_version_change` expiry and a removal test; ADR-0120 already records served-model drift and fingerprint `DRIFT`. What is missing is (i) the *scope* of snapshots a rule's evidence covers, (ii) a record of whether a `(snapshot, definition)` pair has been verified, and (iii) the experiment that verifies it after a weight update.

## Options considered

1. **Rely on `model_version_change` expiry alone.** Rejected: expiry fires on any served-model change but cannot distinguish a snapshot the rule was evidenced on from one it never saw; there is no per-pair verification state, so the Lab cannot refuse an evolution arm on a broken pair.
2. **A separate compatibility-tag record per edit.** Rejected: duplicates the debt record (CF-049 gave conditioned rules one record shape); the tag is an expiry scope, which is what the debt record already carries.
3. **Additive `snapshot_scope[]` on the debt record + `CompatibilityRecord` per pair + a pre-registered paired regression experiment with model as the varied factor (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Tag.** `AssumptionDebtRecord` gains `snapshot_scope[]: ModelSnapshotRef | family(selector)` (additive; CF-WS-I8-02 to WS-I6). Default for `origin = evolution`: the `derived-from.snapshot_refs` of the introducing `HirDiff` (which must be stamped by WS-I5 — CF-WS-I8-01); default for human-authored conditioned rules: the bound profile's selector family (OQ-WS-I8-05). `expiry_condition ⊇ {model_version_change}` is mandatory for every evolution-origin conditioned rule; `link` refuses `missing_snapshot_scope`. A `transfer` `ComparisonReport` sign-stable over ≥ 2 families (ADR-0159) widens the scope to `family`.
2. **Record.** `CompatibilityRecord{snapshot_ref, definition semantic_id, profile semantic_id, status ∈ {trained_under, verified, drifted{rules[]}, broken{report_ref}, unknown}, evidence_ref?, regression_suite_ref?, created_at, expiry_condition}` — content-addressed, provenance-bearing, annotated never rewritten (ADR-0037); created at `import_snapshot` (WS-I8-adr-1) and at synthetic claims (decision 5); updated only by `run_regression_suite` or a WS-I5 cross-model compatibility stage (which emits `verified` for the pairs it tested).
3. **Harness regression suite.** `export_regression_suite(definition, snapshot_in, split, policy) → HarnessRegressionSuite` = a pre-registered `ExperimentSpec` (ADR-0154, `comparative`/`paired`) whose varied factor is `model_snapshot ∈ {snapshot_in, snapshot_out (unbound)}`, arms under `MatchSpec{matched_cap, equal eval_budget, cold_start}`, over the definition's headline metrics, **every veto metric**, the `retention` set (S-091), and **per-conditioned-rule compliance** (`followed` rate, ADR-0125), with non-inferiority margins in `PreRegistration`; task hashes predate the accompanying export and are disjoint from its `TrainingExposureRecord` (R-1; `LeakedSplit` otherwise). `run_regression_suite(snapshot_out, suite) → ComparisonReport + CompatibilityRecord` binds the level and runs it as an ordinary experiment charged `instrument`. Verdict: all non-inferior and no veto ⇒ `verified`; any veto or headline regression beyond margin ⇒ `broken`; headline within margin but ≥ 1 rule's compliance beyond its margin ⇒ `drifted{rules[]}` and those rules transition `active → expiring` (ADR-0126 trigger `model_version_change`, observable (i)), scheduling their removal tests with WS-I6.
4. **Guards.** *At bind* (`resolve`/`seal` of a run manifest): `verified | trained_under` ⇒ proceed; `drifted` ⇒ proceed with rules `expiring` and the row annotated; `unknown` ⇒ proceed only under `Design.allow_unverified_snapshot = true`, rows annotated `compatibility = unknown` and excluded from headline `ComparisonReport`s; `broken` ⇒ refuse for `origin = evolution` arms and for any non-`exploratory` Lab arm. The guard reads records only. *At run*: served-model change or `snapshot_span.drift > 0` ⇒ `policy_drift` annotation; such rows never enter `artifact_benefit`. *After import*: for every definition the Lab intends to bind to the new snapshot, rules with `snapshot_scope ∌ snapshot_out` are scheduled for removal tests by WS-I6 (the S-047 dead-weight sweep made routine).
5. **Provider-side changes are weight updates.** A fingerprint `DRIFT` or `compatibility_token` change on a provider snapshot (ADR-0126 observables (ii)/(iii)) creates a synthetic `SnapshotClaim{snapshot_id = observed}` and runs decisions 2–4 with no trainer present. This path is **built first** (C1/Stage 5) and is the only part of the layer whose value is independent of any co-evolution result.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-05 (scope is an expiry trigger on the one debt record; removal tests are the ADR-0126 retirement experiment; the guard itself carries a debt record whose removal test is "no `drifted`/`broken` verdict in N cycles"), T-LCD-09/-14 (the suite is an explicit paired factorial under `MatchSpec` with model as the factor; refusal of unmatched arms inherited), T-LCD-13 (per-rule `followed` after a weight change is the `drifted` observable), T-LCD-10 (snapshots as claims + fingerprints), T-LCD-12 (records and pure verdict functions), T-LCD-15 (hosted pairs `n/a`; hosted participants are never `trained_under`). Not applicable: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-06/-07/-08/-11. **ADR-0024 rung:** verdict function, guard checks, `link` refusal MUST-code; margins, `allow_unverified_snapshot`, retention-set choice, scope defaults MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: S-096 (`provisional`; the break), S-047 and S-049 (Tier B; dead weight and deletion), S-088 (`provisional`, mechanism: benefit ≠ updating; compliance tier-dependent), S-091 (`provisional`; harness-induced forgetting ⇒ retention rows), ADR-0126 (expiry triggers exist and are observable), ADR-0120 (drift is a ledger fact).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `MaoPopovich/SafeEvolve` @ `94dc369` `core/skillrl/validate_sft_rl_compat.py` (fatal mismatch between checkpoint provenance `skill_bank_sha256`/`compiler_version`/`renderer_version`/`model_family` and the runtime bank — a `trained_under` check), `core/skillrl/phase3_online_update.py` `cmd_commit_context` (versions + hashes + `policy_checkpoint` per accepted edit); `PrimeIntellect-ai/verifiers` @ `3bbac38` `verifiers/v1/episode.py` (`PolicySpan.drift`); `OpenPipe/ART` @ `66f644d` `src/art/trajectories/__init__.py` (`initial/final_policy_version`). A per-pair verification record gated by a pre-registered paired regression experiment with per-rule compliance: `novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: S-088 (updating quality tier-independent) and AHE's cross-family transfer (doc 2 §5.8) suggest scoping every edit to its evidenced snapshots over-expires portable edits. Does not overturn: scope is an expiry trigger yielding `expiring`, not a refusal; portable edits widen to `family` on a `transfer` report; the cost is bounded by WS-I6's removal-test budget.
- (d) Conditionality: decisions 1–4 assume the beneficiary is a native participant with `ledger` observability (per-rule compliance is otherwise `n/a`); decision 5 assumes the C1 fingerprint probe (OQ-286 parameters). Value on frontier snapshots comes only via decision 5.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0/Stage 1 schema (`snapshot_scope`, `CompatibilityRecord`); C1/Stage 5 decision 5 + `run_regression_suite` + `drifted ⇒ expiring` wiring + `snapshot_span`; C4/Stage 6 the guard at bind for evolution arms and the post-import sweep.

## Consequences

WS-I6 owns the scheduling and the debt-record change; WS-I5 must stamp `derived-from.snapshot_refs` and emit `verified` from its cross-model stage; WS-J4 adds the `compatibility` and `policy_drift` row annotations; WS-C1 raises synthetic claims on DRIFT; WS-K2 debt dashboards show `CompatibilityRecord` status per definition × snapshot.

## Reversibility

**High**: all additions are additive fields, records and one experiment shape; dropping the guard leaves rules with a superfluous `snapshot_scope` and records that are annotated, never deleted.
