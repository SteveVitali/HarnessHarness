# WS-E5-adr-3 — Result normalization, the three-origin error taxonomy with kernel-derived retryability, the deadline hierarchy with helper-side enforcement, two-phase class-aware interruption, ephemeral streaming with explicit truncation, detached children as effects (OQ-090) and a two-tier dedup store (OQ-087)

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-E5 (consumers: WS-F2, WS-B3, WS-B2, WS-G2, WS-H7, WS-D1, WS-C3/E2 (renderer), WS-E4, WS-K1/K2, WS-A3, WS-E1) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** WS-E5-adr-1, WS-E5-adr-2; ADR-0030 (`unknown{cause}`, `probe`, `detached_effect_ids[]`, "the model always sees a terminal or an explicit `unknown`"), ADR-0031 (delivery semantics per class; no automatic retry for `irreversible`; key derivation), ADR-0032 (compensation), ADR-0026 (ephemeral events; AC-B1-12), ADR-0029 (offload), ADR-0039/0040 (`time.wall_ms`; hard ceilings only at decision points; open effects closed before `budget_exhausted`), ADR-0043 (integer ms, monotonic clocks), ADR-0044 (L3 ephemeral progress), ADR-0016 (`Observation`), ADR-0022 (E5 error surjectivity), ADR-0020 (profile `error_format`/`result_renderer` surfaces); WS-E1 §6.2 (`observation_contract.error_classes`, `size_class`, `offload_policy`, `streaming`); WS-H7 AC-H7-06 (approval timeout/cancel = refusal); WS-E4 §6 (`call_tool` outcome sum; `isError` lowering); CF-061 (key derivation vs retry policy), CF-WS-E5-04; OQ-087, OQ-090, OQ-092, OQ-WS-E5-03, OQ-WS-E5-04, OQ-WS-E5-06; R-2.5.5, R-2.6.2

## Context

Doc 2 §5.2: the harness decides "how errors are typed, whether retries are automatic, and whether the model sees raw observations or normalized artifacts"; §5.5 names duplicated side effects after retries and cancellation as the long-horizon hazards. ADR-0030 requires every intended effect to end in a terminal or an explicit `unknown` visible to the model, and ADR-0031 keys delivery semantics on the effect risk class; both left the executor-side behaviour to WS-E5. The audit (dossier §4a F4–F8) found: three failure origins (tool, execution, transport) are conflated in every audited result type except MCP's `isError` split, which no audited client keeps in its ledger; model-facing error text is free-form (Codex `ToolError::Rejected(String)`, goose `DECLINED_RESPONSE`, browser-use `RuntimeError(...)`) while OpenHands alone has a closed, frozen `ErrorClassification{kind, retryable, user_action}`; cancellation is two-phase (TERM → grace → KILL) and class-blind everywhere; timeouts are a hierarchy in practice (Codex 10 s default + model-supplied `timeout_ms`/`yield_time_ms`; OpenHands refuses foreground timeouts above 90 % of the environment's idle timeout); background processes are a first-class, session-owned object only in Codex; retained output is capped silently in the durable record.

## Options considered

1. **Free-form error strings, executor-asserted retryability** (Codex/goose/browser-use today). *Rejected:* the kernel cannot key lifecycle transitions or retry policy on text; T-LCD-02; an executor could declare an irreversible failure "retryable".
2. **A single flat error enum without origin.** *Rejected:* `timeout` from the tool (an HTTP client timing out inside a completed process) and `timeout` from the helper (process killed at deadline) demand different lifecycle transitions; MCP's own split is the evidence.
3. **Kill-on-cancel for every class** (Codex/OpenHands). *Rejected:* killing a compensable or irreversible external effect mid-flight destroys the evidence `probe` needs; ADR-0031 forbids blind redispatch, so an evidence-free `unknown` becomes an escalation.
4. **Detached processes as children recorded on the parent only; killed on turn end** (Codex unified exec without per-process identity in the ledger). *Rejected:* their later effects would be unattributable (WS-E5-adr-2) and their termination unaudited.
5. **Closed three-origin taxonomy with kernel-derived retryability; a kernel-computed deadline hierarchy enforced in the helper; two-phase interruption whose kill step is class-gated; ephemeral streaming with a typed truncation record and offloaded raw output; detached children as `spawn_process` effects owned by the environment handle; ledger-tier dedup at C0 and executor-tier at C1 (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Observation shape** (the HIR `Observation` payload as produced by `observe`): `{effect_id, attempt_no, tool_call_id, outcome ∈ {applied, not_applied, partial}, status ∈ {ok, error{class, origin ∈ {tool, execution, transport}, detail_ref: Text(external), retryable}}, exit_status?, content: [ContentBlock] (profile-rendered, truncation-aware), structured?: value_ref, raw_output_ref?, truncation?, artifacts: [ContentAddress], detached_effect_ids[], postcondition_results[], cost, timing{dispatched_at, first_output_at?, terminal_at, drain_ms}, completeness, provenance}`. `outcome` is decided by the kernel from the executor's `executor_outcome_hint`, the capture manifest and the class (table below), never copied from the executor.
2. **`ErrorClass`** (closed; WS-E1's ten values plus, by dialect bump — CF-WS-E5-04): `cancelled{by ∈ {principal, budget, lease, run_end, protocol}}, containment_denied{kind}, signalled{signal}, output_cap_exceeded, protocol_error, unattributed`. Origin rule: `tool` ⇐ the tool's own result (`isError`, non-zero exit with output, in-tool validation); `execution` ⇐ `timeout, cancelled, containment_denied, signalled, output_cap_exceeded, environment_unavailable` from helper/kernel; `transport` ⇐ `protocol_error, executor_error`. A structured result that fails `output_schema` validation is `tool`-origin `invalid_arguments`-class? — no: it is `conflict{schema}` with the value retained (admit-annotated; OQ-WS-E5-06 may narrow).
3. **Lifecycle mapping (kernel rule).** `tool` error: `read_only`/`reversible` → `observed(not_applied | partial by diff)`; `compensable`/`irreversible` → `observed(not_applied)` only when `probe_support ≠ none` confirms, else `unknown → probe`. `execution` timeout/signalled/cancelled: `read_only` → `observed(not_applied)`; `reversible` → `observed(partial)` with `fs_change[]` from `diff(baseline)`; `compensable`/`irreversible` → `unknown{cause}` → `probe` (never redispatched for `irreversible`, ADR-0031). `containment_denied` → `observed(not_applied)` + `security.containment.violated`. `transport`: `observed(not_applied)` only if non-dispatch is proven (no `action.tool.started`), else `unknown{cause: executor_error}`.
4. **Derived retryability.** `retryable = f(class, origin, effective_risk_class)`: true for `tool`-origin `rate_limited`, `conflict`, `timeout` on `read_only`/`reversible`, and `precondition_violated{stale}` after re-resolution; false for `permission_refused`, `containment_denied`, `cancelled{by ∈ {principal, budget}}`, `unattributed`, and every class on `irreversible`; an executor `retryable_hint` may only lower it. Retry *policy* (attempts, backoff, budget) remains WS-F2's (CF-061); a retry whose class rose triggers H7 `require_fresh_review`.
5. **Model-facing rendering.** The profile's `error_format` renders `(class, origin, detail)`; enums are typed; `detail` is a `Text{authority = external}` leaf after H3 `redact`; a refusal or cancellation never appears as success (Codex `success: None` → `outcome = not_applied`). MCP lowering: `status.error` → `isError: true` with `(class, origin)` in `_meta` (declared `hint_only` loss where absent; T-LCD-11).
6. **Deadline hierarchy.** At `prepare`: `deadline = min(capability default | cost_model p95 × k, model_requested?, budget.remaining(time.wall_ms), env.idle_cap × idle_ratio (default 0.9), turn.deadline?)`; `grace_ms`, `drain_ms` (defaults MUST-data; a model-conditioned default is a conditioned rule with a debt record). A request whose capability-declared floor exceeds any cap is refused `precondition_violated{timeout_exceeds_environment}` with a typed "background it" hint. The **helper enforces `deadline` locally** and reports `error(timeout)`; the kernel independently marks `unknown{cause: timeout}` at `deadline + grace_ms + drain_ms + slack` if no terminal arrives.
7. **Cancellation.** Sources: principal (turn cancel; ACP `session/cancel`; inbound MCP `notifications/cancelled` when hosting), hard budget **only at a decision point** (ADR-0040 — never mid-effect), writer-lease fencing, run terminal, executor loss. Procedure: `interrupt(graceful)` → `grace_ms` → `interrupt(kill)` → `drain_ms` → terminal per decision 3. For `compensable`/`irreversible` effects with `interrupt ∈ {unsupported, unknown}` the kernel does **not** kill before `deadline`; it marks `unknown` after it. Cancellation while awaiting approval is `action.effect.refused{reason: cancelled}` (H7). The model always receives a typed `cancelled` observation with partial output.
8. **Streaming and retention.** `output_chunk`/`progress` items are ephemeral (`subscribe` only; AC-B1-12), rate-capped by `output_policy.max_deltas`; the terminal observation carries the profile-rendered view and `raw_output_ref` (offloaded bytes up to `retain_bytes_cap`, default 1 MiB, MUST-data) plus a typed `truncation{omitted_bytes, original_size, policy_ref}` — an omission is never silent. Long-running commands yield after `yield_ms` as `observed(partial)` with a detached child rather than a blocking stream.
9. **Detached children (OQ-090).** A process the executor reports `process{detached}` becomes a child effect `{domain: spawn_process, parent_effect_id, effect_id, state: committed}` under the run's writer lease, owned by the environment handle: enumerable (`list_detached`), terminable by principal or kernel, terminated at `detach`/run terminal with an explicit `observed`, its later output captured under its own `effect_id`, readable by the model through the capability's surface. No child leases at C0 (ADR-0030 C1 extension for resource-scoped leases). `on_kernel_loss` (Stage 1 default `terminate`; C1 `preserve_until(ttl)` for B3 resume) governs the kernel-death case (OQ-WS-E5-04 aligns the ttl with B3/B1).
10. **Dedup store (OQ-087).** C0: `project(run, effects_by_key)` — a repeated `commit` whose `idempotency_key` already has `observed(applied)` returns the stored observation without dispatch; retention = run retention. C1/Stage 2: executor-side `(key → Observation)` for `dedup_support = executor` with a declared `dedup_window`; a lapsed window mid-retry ⇒ `probe`, never redispatch. `target` forwarding is WS-E4's Stage 4.

## Consequences

- WS-E1 extends `observation_contract.error_classes` by dialect bump (CF-WS-E5-04) and marks `partial_application` capabilities; WS-A3 carries `origin` and `retryable` on the `Observation` payload.
- WS-F2 consumes `retryable` and `cancelled{by: budget}` semantics (never mid-effect); WS-H7 receives `retry_of` with the class delta; WS-G2 treats `unknown`, `partial` and `abandoned` per OQ-092 with `completeness` as evidence.
- WS-B3's resume handles `preserve_until` children and `unknown{cause: timeout}` effects via `probe`; WS-B5 owns termination at `detach`.
- WS-C3/E2's profiles own `error_format` and `result_renderer` — no per-model branch in the executor (T-LCD-01 by delegation).
- WS-K1/K2 render ephemeral chunks from `subscribe` and the durable observation from `read`; WS-E4 lowers `status.error` to `isError` and `input_required` stays a paused effect (E4-adr-2).

## Reversibility

**High for defaults, medium for the taxonomy.** Every numeric default is data. Widening `ErrorClass` is a dialect bump; collapsing the origin axis after F2/H7/G2 key on it would re-merge "the tool failed" with "the helper killed it" — the ambiguity that drives duplicate side effects — so that direction is costly. Making detached children non-effects later would break WS-E5-adr-2's attribution for their output.
