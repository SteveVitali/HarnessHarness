# ADR-NNNN — Approval leases and approvals as a budgeted resource: the lease key (OQ-091), `approvals.requested` as the budgeted dimension (OQ-096) with an exhaustion safeguard, repeated-denial fallback, typed explanations, and approval-rate telemetry

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-H7 (with WS-L2 for dimensions, WS-F2 as enforcer, WS-E1/E5 for `ActionPattern` projections, WS-I1/I2 for metrics, WS-D1 for `context_label`, WS-K1/K2 for rendering) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0031 (cache key `H(canonical action pattern, scope)`; `decider = cache`), ADR-0039 (`approvals.{requested, granted}`, `time.human_wait_ms`), ADR-0040 (approvals exhaustion converts `ask → deny`, never `allow`; `amend` ledgered), ADR-0041 (`approvals.*` admissible cross-model), ADR-0017 (widening only from human origin), ADR-0022 (`SurfaceArgMap`), ADR-0034 (`context_label`), ADR-0044/0045 (`MetricDeclaration`), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-02/-14/-15); OQ-091, OQ-096, OQ-086; CF-WS-H7-06; R-2.8.7, R-2.1.6

## Context
ADR-0031 allows session-scoped approval caches keyed on a "canonical action pattern" and leaves the pattern, lifetime and invalidation to WS-H7 (OQ-091); ADR-0039/0040 make approvals a kernel dimension whose exhaustion converts `ask` into `deny`, and WS-L2 asks whether the budget counts requests or grants and whether the conversion needs a fatigue safeguard (OQ-096). The evidence: leases are the fatigue relief every harness ships — Codex `ApprovedForSession` keyed with the exec-policy fingerprint; goose `tool:H(args)` with expiry and a separate judge-derived category; opencode pattern rules with an `always` reply; ACP `allow_always`; Claude Code verdict reuse invalidated when "new content enters the conversation" or on mode/rule change — and they are also the channel through which injected or drifting proposals ride an earlier decision (S-WS-H7-08 "embedding", `provisional`). Human attention is the scarce resource (S-053: ~93% approval, attention decays with volume; Tier-A habituation results S-WS-H7-03/04); a `deny` consumes it as much as an `allow`. Converting `ask → deny` mid-task produces denial-driven evasion (Codex re-runs a denied sandboxed command unsandboxed on approval; Claude Code "receives the reason and tries an alternative") and silent loops unless bounded (Claude Code pauses auto mode after 3 consecutive / 20 total denials). Explanations move adherence (S-WS-H7-02) and are an attack channel (minimizing language, S-WS-H7-08).

## Options considered
1. **Cache by exact argument hash** (goose default). *Rejected:* too narrow to relieve fatigue for exec/fs patterns; every path variant re-prompts.
2. **Cache by tool name / wildcard `*`** (opencode `always` on `*`; Claude Code blanket `Bash(*)`). *Rejected:* too wide; Claude Code itself drops such rules on entering auto mode; converts one decision into a session-wide grant of arbitrary execution.
3. **Budget `approvals.granted`.** *Rejected:* a denial costs the same attention; budgeting grants would make a policy that asks constantly and is mostly refused look cheap.
4. **Silent `ask → deny` on exhaustion with no safeguard** (ADR-0040 as written). *Rejected as insufficient:* produces stuck or evasive trajectories; the principal never learns the run was throttled.
5. **Pattern leases keyed on capability semantics + a declared argument projection + scope + policy fingerprint, with a risk ceiling, expiry/uses and re-validation on authority drop; `approvals.requested` as the budgeted dimension; a surfaced, ledgered, non-widening exhaustion path with a repeated-denial fallback; typed explanations; approval-rate telemetry as a class-scoped fatigue indicator (chosen).**

## Decision
1. **Lease key and record.** `ApprovalLease{lease_id, key = H(ToolCapability semantic_id ∥ canonical(pattern) ∥ scope ∥ policy_fingerprint), capability_ref, pattern: ActionPattern, scope ∈ {turn, run, session}, holder, granted_by: ReviewerRef, origin_permission_id, expires_at?, expires_with: writer lease, max_uses?, uses, policy_fingerprint, risk_ceiling: effective_risk_class, revoked_at?}`. `ActionPattern` is declared per capability by WS-E1 as a *projection* of the argument space aligned with `SurfaceArgMap` (exec → normalized command prefix via WS-E5 canonicalization; `fs_*` → path-prefix set under declared roots; `net_egress` → `(host, port, protocol)`; MCP/tool → `(server, tool, argument-shape hash)`; `spend` → `(payee, ceiling)`); a bare `effect_id`, call id or tool name is never a key. `policy_fingerprint = H(sealed definition version_id ∥ approval_mode ∥ Π narrowing leaves)`.
2. **Lease semantics.** A hit is `decider = cache` with `origin_permission_id` (ADR-0031) and costs no approval; a lease never covers an `effective_risk_class` above its `risk_ceiling`; any change of policy fingerprint, mode or rules revokes all leases; `scope = external` leases are re-validated when the per-call `context_label.authority` drops below its value at grant; leases for `irreversible` require a human `allow_lease` option, `max_uses`, and `scope ≤ run`; automatic stages never mint leases for `irreversible ∧ external`; judge-derived verdict reuse is a lease with `granted_by = policy_rule` kept separate from human leases (goose `smart_approve` precedent); sub-agents receive leases only by attenuated copy. Events: `security.permission.lease.{granted, used, expired, revoked}`.
3. **Budgeted dimension (OQ-096).** `approvals.requested` counts every durable `pending` whose `requested_of` is a human and that is not satisfied by a lease or coalesced into an existing request; `approvals.granted` counts human `allow` decisions (once or lease) and remains a counter for the autonomy dimension; automatic-reviewer invocations are `evaluator_calls` + `tokens.*` (`charged_to = subject`, attribution `harness_overhead.verification` pending WS-I1 — CF-WS-H7-06), never approvals; lease hits and `policy_rule` allows are neither. `time.human_wait_ms` accrues `pending → decided` for human targets and is excluded from `time.working_ms`. **The approvals budget is over `approvals.requested`.**
4. **Exhaustion safeguard.** On exhaustion the next human-targeted `ask` converts to `deny{reason: approvals_exhausted}` (ADR-0040) recorded as `control.budget.consumed` + `security.permission.decided{decider: policy, reason: approvals_exhausted}`; the model's refusal observation carries the same reason; leases still satisfy requests; attended runs receive one `security.permission.escalated{to: principal, reason: approvals_exhausted}` summary offering a ledgered `amend` or `stop`; unattended runs continue under `deny` and, if a denied effect blocks completion (WS-G2), end with `stop_reason = approvals_exhausted`; the conversion never widens or leases.
5. **Repeated-denial fallback.** `max_consecutive_denies` (default 3) and `max_total_denies` (default 20; OQ-WS-H7-04) over policy, reviewer *and* converted denials produce `escalated{to: human}` (attended), `lifecycle.run.suspended{reason: approval_escalation}` (async) or a bounded stop (unattended); any `allow` resets the consecutive counter; a denial loop is a trace-predicate violation.
6. **Typed explanations.** `Explanation{rule_id, risk_factors[] ⊆ {reversibility, scope, repeat_safety, authority_of_proposal, taint, budget_state, containment}, what_would_auto_approve?: NarrowingHint, model_justification?: Text{authority = delegate}}`; the kernel fields are record-derived; the model text is rendered with distinct provenance and may be collapsed by surfaces; hints are rate-limited and act only through a human `allow_lease`.
7. **Telemetry.** `MetricDeclaration`s `approval_rate{effective_risk_class}`, `denial_rate{class}`, `approvals_per_kilo_effect`, `median_decision_latency_ms{class}`, `lease_hit_ratio`, `auto_review_fn_rate` (calibration set) with `requires_observability ∋ events`, `applies_to_classes = {native, hosted-with-permission-elicitation}`, `charged_to = subject` except the calibration metric (`instrument`). A class with `approval_rate ≥ θ ∧ denials = 0 over N` raises a `NarrowingHint` to the principal — never an automatic widening (ADR-0017).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)
- (a) Mechanism evidence: habituation and design sensitivity — S-WS-H7-03, S-WS-H7-04, S-WS-H7-02 (Tier A); attention is the scarce resource and decays with volume — S-053 (Tier B); leases as the shipped relief and fingerprint invalidation — S-107 (`with_cached_approval` + policy fingerprint), S-112 (expiry; separate categories), S-111 (`always` patterns), S-035 (`allow_always`), S-WS-H7-05 (reuse until new content; drop all on mode change); lease/batch ride-along attack — S-WS-H7-08 (`provisional`); denial-driven evasion and the repeated-denial fallback — S-WS-H7-05 (3/20), WS-B2 §4a (Codex unsandboxed re-run); reserve-before-spend and monotone budgets — ADR-0040 (S-209 corroborated by Codex guardian budgets and Inspect limit trees); approvals as a matched dimension — ADR-0041, T-LCD-14.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `codex/codex-rs/core/src/tools/sandboxing.rs` L70–111 (`with_cached_approval`; all keys must be `ApprovedForSession`; `codex.approval.requested{tool, approved}` counter), `core/src/tools/approvals.rs` `request_user_approval` (cache keys paired with `RequirementsExecPolicy::fingerprint`), `core/src/guardian/request_budget.rs` (`ExhaustedReviewBudget`); `goose/crates/goose/src/permission/permission_store.rs` L10–135 (`context_hash`, `expiry`, `cleanup_expired`) and `config/permission.rs` (`user` vs `smart_approve` categories); `opencode/packages/opencode/src/permission/index.ts` L28–175 (`evaluate` last-match; `always` appends allow rules); `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/approval/_approval.py` (`explanation` on every decision). The lease record with `risk_ceiling`, fingerprint and authority-drop re-validation, and the `approvals.requested` budget with a surfaced non-widening exhaustion path, are `no-precedent / novel` relative to these (WS-L2 §4 N1 confirms no audited system budgets approvals).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: leases enable ride-along approvals (S-WS-H7-08, `provisional`) — mitigated by projection keys, risk ceilings, fingerprint and authority-drop revocation, and the never-auto set; ACP `allow_always` cannot carry the narrowed pattern — declared loss (companion ADR); budgets that deny mid-task cause evasion or stuck runs — surfaced conversion, reason visible to the model, fallback thresholds, and the Lab treats budget as a factor rather than a hidden constant; explanations can be gamed — kernel fields are record-derived, model text is labelled; approval-rate hints could themselves become reflexive — rate-limited and human-only; S-053's 93% is one product's number — used as a signal shape, not a threshold.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier; `ActionPattern` projections assume WS-E1 declarations and WS-E5 canonicalization (OQ-086) — capabilities without a declared projection are leased only by exact `args_canonical_hash`; the fallback thresholds may be profile-conditioned (OQ-WS-H7-04); budgets assume WS-F2 enforcement at decision points (ADR-0040); the value of leases is task-class-conditional (high for iterative coding, low for one-shot operational tasks).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — `approvals.requested/granted` and `time.human_wait_ms` counting rules; exhaustion conversion with reason; `Explanation` record; exact-hash leases (`scope = run`); **C1 / Stage 2** — `ActionPattern` leases with fingerprint and re-validation; repeated-denial fallback; `NarrowingHint`; budget safeguard events (with WS-F2); **C1 / Stage 4** — approval metrics in the scorecard (WS-I2) and the Stage-4 recipe `approval_mode × reviewer × approvals budget` under a `MatchSpec` on `approvals.requested`; **C2** — taint-aware lease revocation (WS-H2).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-02 (explanations typed; model text a `Text` leaf with authority), T-LCD-05 (judge-derived leases and thresholds sit under the auto-review rule's debt record), T-LCD-10 (lease identity is over the capability's `semantic_id`, never its surface name), T-LCD-14 (approvals matched per arm; utilisation reported), T-LCD-15 (class-scoped metrics; `n/a` for hosted participants without the capability). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-06/-07/-08/-09/-11/-12/-13 (T-LCD-13 is used to measure whether `NarrowingHint`/`Explanation` delivery changes behaviour, via WS-D1's delivered events). Rung: counting rules and key derivation MUST-code; thresholds, θ/N, projections and hints MUST-data.

## Consequences
- WS-E1 declares an `ActionPattern` projection per capability class; WS-E5 supplies command canonicalization (shared with OQ-086); WS-D1 exposes `context_label` at lease check; WS-F2 enforces the budget and emits the stop reason; WS-I1/I2 register the metrics and the `harness_overhead` attribution of reviewer cost; WS-K1/K2 render `Explanation` and `NarrowingHint` with distinct provenance and rate-limit hints; WS-J3 includes `approvals.requested` in every `MatchSpec` touching approval factors.
- OQ-091 and OQ-096 are answered; ADR-0040's conversion rule is preserved and wrapped, not changed.

## Reversibility
**Medium-to-high.** Leases, thresholds and metrics are additive and configurable; reverting to exact-hash-only leases is a data change. Switching the budgeted dimension to `approvals.granted` after Stage 2 would invalidate recorded budgets and comparisons; removing the fingerprint from the key would silently re-admit stale leases — both are cheap in bytes and expensive in instrument validity.
