# ADR-NNNN — The evolution pipeline as ten typed gates over ratified operations: stage contracts S0–S10, the candidate state machine and campaign spec, `matched_total` as the normative gate, the seven-point acceptance report, and the Stage-6 sub-ladder with maturity flags

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I5 (consumers: WS-I6, WS-I7, WS-I8, WS-F4, WS-J3, WS-J4, WS-J5, WS-B1, WS-K2) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (pipeline in scope; evidence standard; C4/Stage 6 last), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05/-09/-13/-14/-15), ADR-0017 (`HirDiff`), ADR-0041 (M3 `matched_total`), ADR-0046 (§7 C4 admissibility gates; three benefit kinds), ADR-0047 (judges never headline), ADR-0053 (D-5; AT-H1-05 as Stage-6 precondition), ADR-0126 (removal test), ADR-0143 (L1/L3 splits), ADR-0147 (M-2), ADR-0149 (`AssemblyDiff`, `freeze`), ADR-0151…0153 (`exp/` namespace), ADR-0154…0156 (`ExperimentSpec`, `adaptive_search`, `retirement`), ADR-0159 (`oracle_best_of_n`, `TransferProfile`), ADR-0163 (L4 `selection`), ADR-0135 (counterfactual hook); CF-004; OQ-008, OQ-025, OQ-382; CF-WS-I5-01/-03/-06/-07; R-2.9.5

## Context

ADR-0002 put the evolution service in scope as a governed pipeline (propose → failure hypothesis → counterexample set → matched-budget eval → held-out regression → cross-model compatibility → security-invariant check → canary → rollout → retirement test) and fixed its evidence standard; Phases 1–3 ratified every operation the pipeline needs (typed diffs, matched-budget comparison, the experiment engine and its kinds, the registry's experiment namespace, the kernel's threat model of the service) but no contract binds them into stages with gates. The source audit of the three open-source search loops (DGM `DGM_outer.py`/`self_improve_step.py`, AFlow `scripts/optimizer.py`, ADAS `_arc/search.py`, pinned commits in the dossier) shows the same skeleton in all three — archive → select parent → diagnose → propose → compile-gate → evaluate → append — and the same absences: no held-out gate per candidate, no matched test-time baseline, incomplete search accounting, no separate deploying authority, no retirement, no first-class revert. S-083 (`provisional (counter)`) shows that supplying the baseline the same feedback and inference budget removes evolution's consistent advantage; S-094 (`provisional`) shows certification, not generation, is the bottleneck and that stagnation occurs with headroom remaining.

## Options considered

1. **One monolithic "evolve" experiment kind** with a single acceptance score. *Rejected:* collapses search-time and artifact benefit (ADR-0046), hides which gate a candidate failed, and cannot express the human seal between security check and canary.
2. **Free-form pipeline per family** (each family ships its own loop, as DGM/AFlow/ADAS do). *Rejected:* the gates would be re-implemented per family and the audit shows they are then omitted; the taxonomy's families differ in *proposal*, not in what must be proven.
3. **Ten typed gates over ratified operations, with the candidate state machine as a ledger projection and every evaluation an `ExperimentSpec` of a ratified kind (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Stages.** The pipeline is the ordered gate table of dossier §6.2: **S0 observe** (`EvidenceCorpus` = `project(runs, evolution_evidence_view)`; no `held_out`/`private` surface — ADR-0143 L1), **S1 propose** (`CandidateProposal{candidate_id, base, diff: HirDiff, family, proposer_run_id, search_spend}`; `classify(diff)` refuses widening/loosening/MUST-code/excluded targets; `validate_assembly(apply(base, diff))` clean; `DuplicateCandidate` refused), **S2 hypothesize** (`FailureHypothesis` + `PredictedEffect` with evidence refs, metric refs, direction, `affected_task_ids ⊆ search ∪ dev`, `model_scope`; `HypothesisUnfalsifiable` otherwise), **S3 screen** (`CounterexampleSet` from `search`/`dev` only; paired `estimated` Δ vs base; `PredictionFalsified` if fewer than `min_flip_share` predicted flips occur), **S4 matched-budget evaluation** (an `adaptive_search` arm set; **normative:** `MatchSpec{mode: matched_total}` with a complete `SearchBudgetRecord` and `search_time_benefit` against `oracle_best_of_n`/`selected_best_of_n` at equal rollouts — ADR-0041 M3, ADR-0046 decisions 1/4, ADR-0159 decision 5; a negative row labels, an incomplete record refuses), **S5 held-out regression** (`artifact_benefit` `full_set` on `held_out` with a pre-campaign `task_split_hash`; `retention` Δ ≥ −margin on tasks the base passed; no veto regresses; repeatability reported), **S6 cross-model compatibility** (`transfer` on ≥ 1 held-out family with sign and interval; `TransferProfile`; beneficiary compliance — activation/following rates and `Pass-When-Activated` for every new artefact, T-LCD-13; model-conditioned ⇒ `conditioned_on` + debt record), **S7 security-invariant check** (static: `classification` re-checked on the re-sealed version, `classify_policy_edit = narrowing` for every policy leaf, `OpaqueWithoutInterface`/`placement ≠ in_process` on payloads, V-EFF; dynamic: AT-H1-05, LT-01…12, security vetoes equal-or-better vs base), **S8 seal + canary** (a `definition`-class human `seal`; `RolloutPolicy{mode ∈ {shadow, split}, share, min_runs, max_duration, abort_on, advance_rule}`; `shadow` = ADR-0135 counterfactual branch with `InterventionRecord{kind: definition_diff}` and irreversible effects deferred and never released; `split` = the sealed candidate serves a recorded share of live goals under the principal's authority), **S9 rollout** (`publish`/`supersede{edit}` under namespace authority; `sameness ∈ {L1, L2}`; `AssumptionDebtRecord` complete; leaderboard `selection` record), **S10 retirement test** (ADR-0156 `retirement` over `head` vs `apply(head, invert(diff))` on ADR-0126 triggers plus `transfer_sign_flip` and `superseding_candidate`; `retired` is a supersession).
2. **Ordering invariants P-1…P-5.** No skipped gates; S5–S7 and S8 act on one content-identical sealed `exp/` version; every pipeline experiment names the campaign, pins one `registry_snapshot_id` and one `SplitAssignmentRecord`; search spend `charged_to = instrument`, evaluated arms `subject`; the human seal is shown the `EvolutionAcceptanceReport` and the `AssemblyDiff` rendering, never a raw diff.
3. **Candidate state machine (closed; growth = dialect bump).** `proposed → classified → hypothesized → screened → searched → validated → transferred → security_checked → sealed_candidate → canary → active → expiring → {retired | revalidated}`; terminals `rejected{stage, code, report_ref}`, `withdrawn{by}`, `reverted{from, reason}`. Every transition is `measurement.evolution.candidate.transitioned{candidate_id, from, to, stage, report_ref?, causes[]}` on the campaign run (`run_kind = experiment`; no new run kind), with `measurement.evolution.campaign.{opened, stopped{reason}, closed}`; the candidate view is `project(campaign_run, candidate_view, until_seq)` (CF-WS-I5-01 to WS-B1). The lineage is the `exp/<experiment_id>/` name history with cached `diff_ref`s; the only archive policy is keep-all.
4. **Campaign spec (MUST-data, content-addressed).** `EvolutionCampaignSpec{campaign_id, base, family, proposal_constraints{target_classes[], target_exclusions (fixed), max_semantic_ops, allowed_planes, budget}, slot_allocation: SlotAllocationPolicy{floors, mode ∈ {concentrate (default), uniform, voi}}, parent_selection ∈ {best, score_proportional, score_child_proportional, pareto_per_task}, suite_binding + split_assignment_ref, search_budget (instrument), eval_budget and match_spec per stage, model_levels for S6, rollout_policy, stop_rule: CampaignStopRule{max_candidates, max_search_budget, max_consecutive_rejections, stop_on_no_addressable_failure: true}, pre_registration}`; its `ExperimentSpec`s are derived and registered through ADR-0154 `register`, inheriting every refusal.
5. **Acceptance report.** `EvolutionAcceptanceReport{candidate_id, items[1..7]{status ∈ {pass, fail, n/a{reason}}, evidence_ref}, comparisons[], portability_label ∈ {reported, portable, model_conditioned}, attribution_granularity ∈ {component, slot, leaf, configuration}, human_review}` maps the doc 2 §5.8 seven-point standard one-to-one (1 matched total compute — S4; 2 held-out tasks/families — S5/S6; 3 persistent artifact vs search-time — three rows + `selection`; 4 component attribution — diff targets, WS-I7 arms when `semantic_ops > 1`, else declared granularity; 5 forgetting — `retention` + vetoes; 6 security invariance — S7; 7 beneficiary compliance — S6 chain). S8 requires items 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 `pass`, item 4 `pass` or declared granularity, item 7 `pass` or `n/a{observability}` shown to the reviewer. A `portable` label requires ≥ 2 families + ≥ 1 held-out level (AC-I2-10); the *gate* is ≥ 1 held-out family (ADR-0046 §7) — CF-WS-I5-03.
6. **Stage-6 sub-ladder and maturity flags.** Precondition: Stage 3 complete, AT-H1-05 passing, ADR-0135 hook, `exp/` namespaces, `retirement` kind, ADR-0126 removal test, ADR-0181 variant host. **6a** the pipeline as a Lab recipe with human-proposed candidates (`family = human`) — `instrument-grade`; **6b** one automated family on one component class per campaign (`evolution_proposer` class, ADR-3) — `instrument-grade` only after a campaign on the Stage-3 suite yields ≥ 1 accepted candidate or a clean negative result under M3; **6c** code-search families, `CompiledPayload` candidates, selectors, `rebase`, WS-I7 attribution arms, `voi` allocation, hosted-coordinate campaigns — `research-grade`; **6d** the co-evolution interface (`consolidation_candidates`) — `research-grade` (WS-I8). Every campaign and family carries `maturity`; `research-grade` results are `preview`-labelled (ADR-0046 P3 rule) and never ground a program claim.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-14 (S4 `matched_total` normative; `search_budget`/`eval_budget` per arm; three benefit rows; typed refusals), T-LCD-05 (S9/S10: every accepted edit carries a debt record and a removal test), T-LCD-13 (item 7), T-LCD-09 (S6 is an explicit model factorial), T-LCD-15 (hosted campaigns `n/a{class}`; compliance `n/a{observability}`), T-LCD-10 (identity-keyed candidates; surface edits are profile candidates), T-LCD-12 (records in/out; out-of-process), T-LCD-06/-07 (hosted coordinates only through enforced declared capabilities). Not applicable: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-08/-11. **ADR-0024 rung:** the state machine, gate checks, exclusion set, `revert` and the event family are MUST-code; `EvolutionCampaignSpec` and every policy/threshold inside it are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** the editable-artifact + trajectory-derived-candidate + gated-validation mechanism is Tier-A lineage (DGM S-026 ICLR 2026; GEPA S-028 ICLR 2026; AFlow S-015 / ADAS S-023 ICLR 2025) and ADR-0002 (ratified); evolution must be compared with matched test-time search and evaluated held-out — S-083 (`provisional (counter)`), doc 2 §5.8 seven-point standard (Tier-B synthesis), ADR-0046 §7 (ratified); certification is the bottleneck and stagnation is real — S-094 (`provisional`); prediction attached to every edit and verified — AHE S-061 (`provisional`); behaviour-relevant screening and disagreement-weighted evaluation — HarnessLens S-089, Task-CoEvolve S-082 (`provisional`, admitted only inside search per ADR-0156); two-gate acceptance (quality + performance without degrading recent batches) — HarnessEvolve S-090 (`provisional`); retention/adaptation tension — S-091 (`provisional`); budget-splitting trap and slot-localised value — S-092 (`provisional`); beneficiary compliance ≠ update validity — S-088 (`provisional (mech)`), T-LCD-13 (ratified); bounded, reversible, component-level updates — SafeEvolve S-086 (`provisional`); regression testing as the acceptance gate — Self-Harness S-WS-I5-01 (`provisional`). No `provisional` number is load-bearing; every gate is the operational form of a ratified rule.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** dgm @a565fd2 `DGM_outer.py` L50–150 (`choose_selfimproves`), L174–216 (`update_archive`, `get_full_eval_threshold` — staged evaluation), `self_improve_step.py` L30–67 (separate diagnosis model), L223–420 (lineage as patch chain; container self-edit; compile gate), `utils/evo_utils.py` L28–41, L96–125; AFlow @3f45721 `scripts/optimizer.py` L26–30, L120–200, `optimizer_utils/experience_utils.py` L55–95, `optimizer_utils/convergence_utils.py` L68–110, `optimizer_utils/data_utils.py` L40–95; ADAS @2702bee `_arc/search.py` L28, L263, L295–325, L340–373. These precedent S0–S4 and the archive; **S5–S10 as gates, the human seal, canary with auto-revert and retirement are `no-precedent / novel` as an integrated contract** (confirming ADR-0002 (b)).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) under matched budgets evolution may accept nothing (S-083, S-075, S-094) — accepted: the pipeline is designed so that this is a reportable result, and Stage 6a is human-proposed; (ii) ten gates are expensive — answered by cheap-to-expensive ordering with typed early exits, adaptive validation inside search, a declared stop rule and instrument-charged, reported search spend; (iii) separating proposal from deployment slows the loop (RHO deploys by self-preference) — answered by ADR-0002 (ii)/(iii) and S-104/S-068: everything up to `security_checked` is automatic, one human `seal` per accepted candidate; (iv) typed diffs forbid code search — answered by `CompiledPayload` diff ops with declared interfaces; attribution granularity is declared, not hidden. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** the *specification* is unconditional (ADR-0002); the *value* of any accepted candidate is conditional on model tier, task class and budget and is expressed only as a `ComparisonReport` at `matched_total` — never absolute (ADR-0002 (d); ADR-0041 M3); gate thresholds (`min_flip_share`, retention margin, canary rules, stop-rule bounds) are MUST-data defaults measured at Stage 6a (OQ-WS-I5-01/-02/-03); hosted-coordinate campaigns assume enforced budget dimensions (OQ-382).
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C4 / Stage 6** with the sub-ladder of decision 6 (6a instrument-grade recipe → 6b one automated family → 6c multi-family/code search → 6d co-evolution interface); schema slices (`CandidateProposal`, `FailureHypothesis`, `EvolutionCampaignSpec`, `EvolutionAcceptanceReport`, the event family) may be published as dialect documents at Stage 3 so the Lab's results store can render candidate views before Stage 6, but nothing executes before the precondition list.

## Consequences

- WS-J3 gains two requests: a `live_split` design kind (or a ruling that `split` canaries are `exploratory`) — CF-WS-I5-07; a candidate-bound `retirement` diff with `semantic_ops ≥ 1` — CF-WS-I5-06. WS-B1 gains the `measurement.evolution.*` family — CF-WS-I5-01. WS-J4/J5/K2 render the candidate view and the acceptance report; leaderboard entries of accepted candidates carry `selection` (ADR-0163 L4).
- WS-I6 receives every accepted edit as a conditioned rule with a removal test (its manager starts from the pipeline's S9 record); WS-I7 receives candidates as `definition_diff` interventions and owns item 4 above leaf granularity; WS-F4 receives `slot_history` and the fixed `CampaignStopRule` as its baseline; WS-I8 receives `consolidation_candidates`.
- The Spec's build ladder (§7.2-9) marks Stage 6 unreachable until the precondition list holds; the Readiness Report shows no C0 decision depends on a `provisional` evolution result (ADR-0002 consequences).

## Reversibility

**High for the sub-ladder and defaults** (data; a stage's threshold or a maturity flag changes by campaign spec). **Medium for the stage set** (adding a gate is additive; removing S4/S5/S7 or the human seal would contradict ratified ADR-0002/0046/0053 and reopen the S-083/S-104 failure classes). **Low for the never-delete lineage and the event family** once campaigns have run (history cannot be reconstructed after the fact — the ADR-0046 "search spend is unrecoverable if not logged" argument).
