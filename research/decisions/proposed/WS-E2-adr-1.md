# ADR-NNNN — The tool-interface compiler contract: closed exposure modes, surface families with a reference variant, declared task/environment inputs, `SurfaceBinding` attribution, a closed `SurfaceArgMap.transform` vocabulary, and `PlanMap` (a restricted `Procedure`) as the composite mapping — resolves OQ-066

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-E2 (consumers: WS-A4, WS-C3, WS-E1, WS-E3, WS-E4, WS-H1, WS-B1, WS-I2) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0007 (T-LCD-01/-02/-04/-05/-08/-10/-11/-12/-13), ADR-0016 (entity 6 `ToolCapability`, entity 5 `Procedure`), ADR-0019 (purity; closed-world rule; stage 3), ADR-0020 (`ProfileRule` kinds; debt records), ADR-0021 (targets; loss classes), ADR-0022 (E1–E7; `SurfaceArgMap`; composite rule ii), ADR-0024 (MUST-code parsers; pointer rule; ladder), ADR-0034 (role placement), ADR-0050 §8; CF-049; OQ-066, OQ-064; R-2.5.2, R-2.1.3, R-2.3.3, R-2.5.3

## Context

ADR-0022 admits one-capability-one-surface renderings at C0 and defers "composite or synthesized surfaces — one surface ↔ many capabilities, wrappers with logic, code-mode" to the C2 tool-interface compiler, asking (OQ-066) whether a HIR `Procedure` plan suffices as their mapping and where the C0/C2 boundary for `unified_exec`-style composites lies. The Phase 2 audit (WS-E2 dossier §4) found six exposure modes in production, each selected by model-keyed data: codex (`models.json`: `tool_mode = code_mode_only`, `apply_patch_tool_type = freeform`, `shell_type`; `ToolSpec` dialects incl. `custom` freeform and namespace regrouping; a code-mode container `exec` whose nested tools are ordinary specs with augmented descriptions), gemini-cli (`ToolDefinition{base, overrides(modelId)}` resolved through `getToolFamily`; two per-family manifests), SWE-agent (tool bundles + a closed family of parsers), goose (`toolshim` interpreter model), opencode (`usePatch` model-id branch selecting `apply_patch` vs `edit`+`write`; code-mode catalogue derived from permissions). Every composite found (`str_replace_editor`, `schedule_event`, `unified_exec`) maps to capabilities by a surface-field branch plus per-branch argument maps. Doc 2 §5.2 asks that the harness choose "whether to expose a high-level composite action or low-level primitives" and evaluate synthesis "for semantic equivalence and safety, not just lower token count".

## Options considered

1. **Leave composite/code-mode surfaces as opaque variants behind a `Tool` contract (no compiler involvement beyond C0).** *Rejected:* their effects, authority and attribution would be undeclared (E1/E2 impossible), and a code-mode container would be an authority-widening vector ADR-0002 forbids.
2. **A dedicated composite-mapping language with loops and opaque steps.** *Rejected:* undecidable E1–E3; duplicates `Procedure`; anything needing loops/opaque bodies is model-authored code and belongs to code-mode with run-time mediation.
3. **Task/environment as run-time inputs to a per-turn compiler** (codex composes handler options per turn). *Rejected:* violates ADR-0019 purity and the closed-world rule; surfaces would have no compile-time identity (T-LCD-13 failure signature).
4. **Closed exposure-mode enum; `SurfaceFamily` per capability set with a primitive `reference_variant`; compile-time inputs restricted to declared task-class and environment records; run-time choice only among compiled variants via a closed `VariantSelector`; `SurfaceBinding` as the attribution record; a closed `transform` vocabulary; `PlanMap` = `Procedure` restricted to `Branch/Invoke/Verify` as the composite mapping; admissibility table by mode (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Exposure modes** `ExposureMode = primitive | split | composite | freeform | code_mode | shim` (closed; extended by ADR). `primitive` and `split` map through `SurfaceArgMap`; `composite` through a `PlanMap`; `freeform` through exactly one `parse(grammar_ref)` transform whose grammar is a `CompiledPayload` leaf and whose parser is Core (ADR-0024 MUST-code); `code_mode` is a container `{catalogue: [Ref<ToolSurface>], sandbox_ref, yield_policy, result_policy}` whose mapping is discharged per nested call at run time; `shim` is a text surface whose parsed call (produced by an `interpreter: ProfileRef` model call) carries `authority = delegate` and goes through the target surface's `SurfaceArgMap`.
2. **Surface families.** `compile_tool_surfaces(capabilities, profile, TaskClassDeclaration, EnvironmentDeclaration, permission_ceiling, validators) → SurfaceFamilySet` emits one `SurfaceFamily{family_id, capability_refs[], reference_variant, variants[]}` per capability set exposed together; `reference_variant` is always a `native_fc` primitive (or split of primitives) rendering — the E4 comparand and the fallback for `retire_surface(bundle, rule_id) = relower(bundle, profile − rule)`. Every variant names the `ProfileRule`s (`tool_shape`, `naming`, `schema_dialect`, `description_template`, `error_format`, `result_render`, `interaction_mode` — ADR-0020's enum, unchanged) that shaped it; every rule carries a complete `AssumptionDebtRecord`.
3. **Inputs are declared, never observed.** `TaskClassDeclaration{task_class_id, labels[], expected_capabilities[]}` and `EnvironmentDeclaration{environment_count, executor_platform, session_support, capability_declaration}` are records of the sealed definition/configuration; the compiler reads nothing else (ADR-0019 purity). `VariantSelector` is a closed, total, quantifier-free predicate over those records plus turn-state fields the profile declares selectable; `select_surfaces` (WS-E3) evaluates it at run time and may only choose among compiled variants (closed-world rule).
4. **Attribution.** `SurfaceBinding{surface_id, exposure_mode, capability_refs: [semantic_id], mapping: SurfaceArgMap | PlanMap, rule_ids[], evidence_ref, safety_ref, effects_bound, family_id, variant_id}` is total over surfaces, is what the trace map, the reference monitor and every `action.tool.*` event reference, and is the only accounting key (E7: no per-surface cost objects; a rename changes `surface_id` and the profile hash only).
5. **Closed transform vocabulary.** `SurfaceArgMap.transform ∈ {identity, rename, const(value), project(path), coerce(type, declared_loss?), parse(grammar_ref), unescape(policy_ref), resolve_name(table_ref)}`; each is a Core interpreter with no ambient authority; `resolve_name` is exact-table only (goose's similarity resolution is inadmissible); `parse` is in the **C0** vocabulary because ADR-0020's patch-shaped minimal profile needs it (amendment proposed to ADR-0022, CF-WS-E2-01).
6. **`PlanMap` (answers OQ-066).** A composite's mapping is a `Procedure` restricted to `steps ⊆ {Branch(predicate over surface fields), Invoke(Ref<ToolCapability>, ArgBinding with its own SurfaceArgMap), Verify(Ref<Validator>)}`, no `Loop/Delegate/Opaque/Instruction`, optional `session_state: Ref<Artifact>` for stateful composites; `effects(PlanMap) = ∪ branches`; E1–E3 are checked per branch; E4 is inherited per branch from the fixture suite of the invoked capability. A mapping needing anything else is `UncheckableSurface` (error) and the shape is code-mode by definition. No dedicated plan language.
7. **Admissibility and stages.** primitive C0/S1–3 (WS-A4); split and static composite C1/S5 (with the Profile Compiler); `session_state` composites, freeform, code_mode, shim C2/S5+; `unified_exec` is a C1 static composite (`Branch(session_id present) → Invoke(process_write) ; else → Invoke(exec)`) with `write_stdin` as a split surface; codex's grammar-driven `exec` orchestration is C2 code-mode. Schema slice (`SurfaceBinding`, transform vocabulary, `SurfaceFailure`, `ResultRenderSpec{full|truncate}`, `ErrorFormatSpec`) is C0/S1 as a stage note on R-2.5.2 without a tier change.
8. **Failure modes** are errors: `UnexpressibleSurface` (ADR-0019), `UncheckableSurface(surface, obligation, reason)`, `DialectNarrowingUndeclared(surface, keyword)`, `SurfaceSynthesisRefused` (companion ADR), `AuthorityWidening`.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: exposure modes selected by model-keyed data in production (S-107 `models.json` + `ToolSpec` dialects + code-mode container; S-119 `ToolFamily` manifests; S-114 bundles + parser families; S-112 `toolshim`; S-111 permission-derived code-mode catalogue); composites are branch-shaped (S-114 `edit_anthropic` `command` enum; S-037 `schedule_event` guidance; S-107 `unified_exec` option composition from *declared* environment facts); code-mode nested tools remain compiled surfaces (S-107 `augment_tool_definition`; S-040 `allowed_callers`; S-WS-E2-01 bindings); code as action space is legitimate (S-WS-E2-02 CodeAct, ICML 2024, Tier A) and benchmark methodology already treats interaction mode as a factor (S-WS-E2-04 BFCL, ICML 2025); names/format sensitivity is model-conditioned (S-177 Tier A; S-049); the purity/closed-world requirements are ADR-0019 (ratified).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `gemini-cli/packages/core/src/tools/definitions/{types.ts, modelFamilyService.ts, resolver.ts, model-family-sets/default-legacy.ts, gemini-3.ts}` (S-119 @ed2ac40 — family-as-data, one selector); `codex-rs/models-manager/models.json`, `codex-rs/tools/src/tool_spec.rs` L18–140, `codex-rs/core/src/tools/spec_plan.rs` L985–1135 and L1255–1258, `codex-rs/core/src/tools/code_mode/execute_spec.rs`, `codex-rs/code-mode-protocol/src/description.rs` L251/L370, `codex-rs/core/assets/tools/apply_patch.lark` (S-107 @0735c51); `SWE-agent/tools/edit_anthropic/config.yaml`, `tools/windowed_edit_replace/config.yaml`, `sweagent/tools/{bundle.py, tools.py, parsing.py}` (S-114 @3ea751c); `goose/crates/goose/src/providers/toolshim.rs` (S-112 @fae91d0); `opencode/packages/opencode/src/tool/{registry.ts L280–300, code-mode.ts, apply_patch.ts, edit.ts}` (S-111 @9f8db11 — split surfaces; the `usePatch` negative); `pi-mono/packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/edit.ts` (S-113 @400d690 — two surface versions of one capability). The family/reference-variant object, the declared-input rule, `SurfaceBinding`, the closed transform vocabulary and `PlanMap` as an integrated contract are `no-precedent / novel` relative to gemini-cli's family manifests and codex's per-slug data (which have no binding, evidence or debt record).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) CodeAct/Cloudflare/codex `code_mode_only` suggest the unit should be the catalogue rendering, not per-capability surfaces — answered: every code-mode system renders per-capability surfaces into the catalogue, so code-mode is an interaction mode of the same compiler with sharper obligations. (ii) gemini-cli's families differ only in prose, so the apparatus may be over-engineering — answered: Cursor/codex/opencode show shape divergence across provider families in the same year; prose divergence is `description_template` rules. (iii) `Procedure` admits constructs that make E1–E3 undecidable — answered by the `PlanMap` restriction; loops/opaque bodies are code-mode. (iv) Run-time task adaptation is what production does (codex per-turn option composition) — answered: the inputs codex reads are declared environment facts, which this ADR makes explicit records; genuinely dynamic choice is selection among compiled variants. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier; assumes ADR-0019/0020/0022 stand as ratified; assumes WS-E1 supplies `preconditions` as domains and capability failure classes (CF-WS-E2-02) and WS-C3 rules reference variants by id (CF-WS-E2-03); C1 composites assume a Stage-3 fixture suite exists for the primitives they invoke; no `provisional` result is load-bearing (HEART/Notation Matters/UniToolCall cited as counter-evidence or context only).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (schema slice: `SurfaceBinding`, transform vocabulary incl. `parse`, `SurfaceFailure`, `ResultRenderSpec{full|truncate}`, `ErrorFormatSpec`); **C1 / Stage 5** (`SurfaceFamily`, `VariantSelector`, split, static composite, `SchemaDialectTransform`); **C2 / Stage 5+** (freeform, code_mode, shim, `session_state` composites).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies: T-LCD-01 (mode/family/naming/dialect are profile-rule outputs; model-id functions are the named defect), T-LCD-02 (all prose is `Text`/`CompiledPayload`), T-LCD-04 (composites compile under ≥2 profiles or error; policy tables derive from `capability_refs`), T-LCD-05 (every variant names rules with debt records; `retire_surface`), T-LCD-06, T-LCD-08/-12 (operation-shaped, out-of-process), T-LCD-10 (`capability_refs` are semantic ids), T-LCD-11 (containers declared `no_slot`; nested surfaces carried), T-LCD-13 (surfaces have compiled identities; `activated` = a call naming the surface). Does not by itself satisfy T-LCD-03 (not owned) or the executable half of T-LCD-04 for composites — mitigated by AC-3/AC-4 at Stage 5. **ADR-0024 ladder rung:** exposure modes and families are *typed entities/parameters* of the profile (rung 3); parsers and transform interpreters are *Core* (rung 5) by the pointer rule.

## Consequences

- WS-A4: amend ADR-0022 to enumerate the transform vocabulary (with `parse` at C0) and to name `PlanMap` as the "plan-shaped mapping" of rule (ii).
- WS-C3: `tool_shape`/`interaction_mode`/`result_render` rules reference `SurfaceFamily` variants by id; new families are rules, not code.
- WS-E1: capability metadata gains `callable_from_code`, `exposure_mode_hints[]`, closed `failure_classes[]`.
- WS-E3: `select_surfaces` evaluates `VariantSelector`s and reveals only compiled variants.
- WS-H1: `authorize` evaluates each `PlanMap` `Invoke` and each nested code-mode call individually through its own `SurfaceArgMap`.
- WS-B1/I1: `action.tool.*` events carry `binding_ref`; trace-map locators resolve to `SurfaceBinding`s.
- WS-I2: E4 suites are per capability and inherited per branch; surface-variant arms are profile-coordinate arms under `MatchSpec`.

## Reversibility

**High for the mode enum and stages** (extend or re-tier by ADR). **Medium for `PlanMap`** (widening it to loops after Stage 5 would invalidate static E1–E3 on admitted composites and require a compensating run-time check). **Low for "declared inputs only"** (shared with ADR-0019's purity; reversing it removes compiled identity from surfaces).
