# ADR-NNNN — The Validator component contract: authoritative evidence handles in, typed verdicts with provenance and an inputs digest out; annotate-or-reject, never rewrite; three bindings (subject, contract, instrument) feeding the scorecard and the control loop

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-G1 (consumers: WS-A3, WS-B1, WS-B2, WS-B4, WS-D1, WS-E5, WS-F1, WS-F2, WS-G2, WS-G3, WS-H1, WS-H7, WS-I1, WS-I2, WS-J4, WS-J5, WS-L2) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0016 (entity 10 `Validator`; "`model_claim` never satisfies a Validator alone"; `Observation.source = validator`), ADR-0022 (E6 result-observation adequacy), ADR-0023/0025 (`ClassRecord`, `required_inputs`, `ordered-many` slots — OQ-073), ADR-0026 (immutability; verdict events), ADR-0033/0034/0035 (authority floor; `validator` endorsement basis; monitor reads records only), ADR-0038 (R1 reproducibility), ADR-0039/0040/0044 (`charged_to`; reserve-before-spend; `harness_overhead.verification`, M11), ADR-0045 (scorer boundary item 9), ADR-0047 (`OracleDeclaration`; judge obligations; `oracle_failure`), ADR-0014 (`followed` detector provenance), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-02/-05/-08/-10/-12/-14/-15); OQ-059/OQ-053 (resolved), OQ-WS-G1-04/-06; CF-WS-G1-02/-03/-04; R-2.7.1

## Context

HIR/1 types `Validator{kind ∈ {executable, schema, predicate, judge, human}, inputs, verdict_type, deterministic, cost, evidence_out}` and forbids satisfying it with model claims alone (ADR-0016); ADR-0047 fixes the oracle classes and judge obligations; ADR-0035 lets a deterministic `kernel` Validator endorse closed-schema values. What no ratified ADR fixes is the *operation-level contract*: how a validator obtains its evidence, what a verdict record contains, what a failing verdict may do to the observation it judged, and how the same validator serves inside the harness (subject-bound), in the task contract, and as a Lab oracle. The audit found the post-tool-use seam in four hook systems (Codex `PostToolUse`, Claude SDK `PostToolUse`, Gemini `AfterTool`, opencode `tool.execute.after`) and three post-edit loops (aider, SWE-agent, OpenHands critics) with three incompatible semantics — annotate, reject, rewrite — and one shared gap: none records which evidence the check read, and OpenHands collapses deterministic and judged outcomes into one scalar `score` (dossier F1, F6, N2). Doc 2 §5.3 requires that critics "consume the same authoritative external evidence rather than simply critique another model's prose".

## Options considered

1. **Hook functions with free-form return (`{block, feedback, additionalContext, updatedToolOutput}`)** as in Codex/Claude SDK/Gemini. *Rejected:* untyped, no evidence record, and the rewrite variant launders provenance (a rewritten observation carries the tool's provenance but the hook's content) and breaks E6.
2. **A critic returning a scalar success probability** (OpenHands `CriticResult.score`). *Rejected:* deterministic and judged outcomes become indistinguishable in storage (CF-029/ADR-0047 option 1) and `score ≥ 0.5` silently becomes "success".
3. **Validators fused into tools** (SWE-agent's lint-and-undo edit). *Rejected as the general form:* the check cannot be ablated, swapped or attributed separately from the tool (T-LCD-02/-08), and revert belongs to B4 with a `baseline_ref`.
4. **One operation-shaped Validator contract with kernel-resolved evidence handles, a typed verdict with provenance and `inputs_digest`, annotate/reject semantics only, and three bindings (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Class and operations.** `Validator` is a registered component class (`ClassRecord{class_id = validator, cardinality = ordered-many, required_inputs ⊇ {ModelProfile, ResourceAccount}}`) with operations `declare → ValidatorDeclaration`, `bind(validator_ref, sealed | contract, profile, account) → BoundValidator`, `collect(bound, target, cursor, env?) → EvidenceBundle`, `check(bound, bundle) → Verdict`, `emit(verdict) → EventRef`, `explain(verdict) → Text` (profile-owned surface). `ValidatorDeclaration{validator_ref, kind, oracle_class, deterministic, evidence_inputs: [EvidenceKind] ≠ ∅, evidence_out, verdict_type ∈ {bool, graded, lattice{C,I,P,N}, three_valued{pass, fail, inconclusive}, vector}, requires_observability, cost_model, isolation ∈ {kernel, sandbox(env_handle), external}, side_effects ⊆ read-only domains, profile_ref?, calibration_ref?, charged_to, assumption_debt?}`; `kind = judge ⇒ deterministic = false ∧ profile_ref ∧ calibration_ref? ∧ assumption_debt`.
2. **Evidence is kernel-resolved and typed.** `EvidenceKind = {end_state(env_handle), snapshot(ContentAddress), artifact(ContentAddress), effect_record(effect_id), observation(EventRef), ledger_range(run_id, from, to), world_state_patch(ContentAddress), external_probe(read-only ToolCapability), model_io(EventRef) — judges only, human_attestation}`; `EvidenceHandle{kind, ref, authority, provenance, produced_at_seq}`; `EvidenceBundle{handles[], inputs_digest = H(canonical(sorted resolved addresses ∥ target ∥ validator version_id))}`. The validator body never fetches evidence itself. Failures: `EvidenceMissing`, `EvidenceStale`, `EvidenceUnauthoritative` (authority < `environment`), `ClaimOnlyEvidence` (bundle contains only `model_claim` observations — the operation-level form of ADR-0016's invariant), `EvidenceTampered`.
3. **Verdict record.** `Verdict{verdict_id, validator_ref: VersionedRef, oracle_class, target, criterion_ref?, contract_id?, phase ∈ {local, global, completion, instrument}, role, value: VerdictValue, status ∈ {decided, inconclusive, oracle_failure}, detector ∈ {deterministic, judged, human}, evidence_refs[], inputs_digest, evidence_head_seq, freshness_ok, findings?[Finding{code, severity, location?, message: Text}], cost + attribution{charged_to}, veto_tripped?[], provenance{origin ∈ kernel(validator_ref) | model(judge, model_call_id) | human(rater, reviewer), authority}, measured_at}`. Invariants: deterministic `check` is pure in `(inputs_digest, validator version_id)` (R1); `status = oracle_failure` is an outcome class, never a value (ADR-0047 item 4); a verdict never changes any label except through ADR-0035's `validator` basis on closed-schema values; `detector = judged ⇒ requires_observability ∋ model_io`; a judge whose bundle contains only `model_io` has `evidence_class = claimed` and is inadmissible as a `followed` detector for artifacts with an executable check (OQ-WS-G1-06 for G3).
4. **Annotate or reject; never rewrite; never un-execute.** A verdict is appended as `verification.validator.verdict` and rendered as an `Observation{source = validator, authority = kernel | delegate | principal}`; the judged observation is byte-identical before and after (ADR-0026). A `fail` verdict does not undo an effect (Codex rule: "a PostToolUse block rejects the result, not the already-completed tool execution"); consequences are F2's (`retry` with bounded reflection, `stop`, `escalate`) or B4's (`revert(effect_id)` when `baseline_ref` exists). Output rewriting (`updatedToolOutput`-style) is not a validator capability; truncation/redaction/normalization are renderer/offload concerns (ADR-0019 item 6, D1).
5. **Three bindings, one record.** *Subject-bound* (referenced from the sealed definition; `charged_to = subject`; in θ), *contract-bound* (referenced from the task contract; `visible` or `held_out`; ADR-1), *instrument-bound* (Lab oracle, ADR-0047; `charged_to = instrument`; never in θ). The binding is recorded on the verdict (`phase`, `contract_id`, `charged_to`), so the scorecard can enforce: headline reads held-out/instrument deterministic verdicts only; subject/visible verdicts feed process metrics (`validator_pass_rate`, `local_check_fail_rate_per_effect`, `completion_refusals`, `verification_ms`, `verification_share_of_spend`).
6. **Control-loop feed.** `DecisionPoint.verify` guards may reference Validators through a `Predicate over VerificationSummary` (recommended answer to OQ-062's open half; OQ-WS-G1-04); F1 declares its verification cadence; F2 keys `retry/stop/escalate` on `Verdict.value/phase/role`. G1 fixes inputs and the no-mutation invariant only.
7. **Ledger.** `verification.validator.invoked{validator_ref, target, phase, criterion_ref?, contract_id?, evidence_refs[], inputs_digest, isolation}` and `verification.validator.verdict{Verdict}` extend WS-B1's payloads; `verification.artefact.followed` is emitted by `emit` when the target carries a `delivery_id` (ADR-0014). Cost is metered under the run budget (reserve-before-spend) and attributed `harness_overhead.verification` (ADR-0044 M11).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-02 (rubrics/findings are `Text` leaves; verdicts typed; each validator ablatable via `enabled`), T-LCD-05 (judges and threshold-conditioned validators carry debt records), T-LCD-08 (`required_inputs ⊇ {ModelProfile, ResourceAccount}`; a profile-conditioned validator — e.g. one that tolerates a family's known output quirk — is expressible inside the contract), T-LCD-10 (`explain` is a surface; identity is the validator's `semantic_id`), T-LCD-12 (operations exchanging `EvidenceBundle`/`Verdict` documents; out-of-process), T-LCD-14 (verification spend metered; `charged_to`), T-LCD-15 (`requires_observability` on every declaration; hosted `n/a`). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-06/-07/-09/-11/-13 (T-13 is carried by ADR-3's detectors). **ADR-0024 ladder rung:** `collect`, `emit`, the no-rewrite and claim-only rules = MUST-code; validator variants, declarations, rubrics, thresholds = MUST-data (`CompiledPayload` pointers, `Text` leaves, conditioned rules).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: executable oracles decide results the model cannot — S-007 (PAL, Tier A: "solving is delegated to the interpreter"); postconditions gate commit of a tool result — S-WS-G1-03 (ToolGate, ACL 2026 Findings; mechanism admissible, numbers `provisional`), S-204 (`provisional`); critics must consume authoritative evidence, and judges correlate with the agent's failures — doc 2 §5.3/§8 (Tier B synthesis), S-046 (Tier B: evaluator drives the browser; self-evaluation bias); deterministic vs judged must stay separate — ADR-0047 (ratified); contract semantics — S-WS-G1-01 (Tier A). The C0 rules (typed verdict, evidence handles, no rewrite, claim-only refusal) rest on Tier A/B evidence and ratified ADRs; no `provisional` number is load-bearing.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `codex/codex-rs/core/src/tools/registry.rs` L698–745 and `hooks/src/events/post_tool_use.rs` L22–47 (annotate/reject outcome; feedback wraps the original output; block does not un-execute), `aider/aider/coders/base_coder.py` L1599–1618 + `aider/linter.py` L82–116 (post-edit lint/test verdicts fed back as observations; bounded reflection), `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/critic/base.py` L57–114 (evidence-in/verdict-out interface; `mode ∈ {finish_and_message, all_actions}` = global/local), `hal-harness/hal/utils/compliance_checkers.py` (findings with severity over traces), `harbor/src/harbor/verifier/verifier.py` L67–122 (typed oracle failures). Rejected precedents: `claude-agent-sdk-python/src/claude_agent_sdk/types.py` L428–443 (`updatedToolOutput`), `software-agent-sdk/.../critic/result.py` L9–30 (scalar score with `success = score ≥ 0.5`). Typed verdict with provenance, `inputs_digest`, kernel-resolved evidence handles and the three-binding record: `no-precedent / novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) rewriting outputs is useful (truncation, redaction, normalization) — those are renderer/offload concerns already owned by ADR-0019/D1; (ii) a scalar score is simpler and OpenHands ships it in production — simplicity is bought by losing the deterministic/judged distinction ADR-0047 requires; (iii) kernel-resolved evidence constrains validators that need live probes — admitted as `external_probe` restricted to read-only capabilities, so the validator stays effect-free; (iv) a verdict per check costs ledger space and validator runs — bounded by the persistence policy and by reserve-before-spend; kernel local checks read only what the runtime already holds (ADR-3). None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: model- and tier-independent; judges assume ≥ 2 snapshots (ADR-0047 (d)); `isolation = sandbox` assumes the ADR-0050 sandbox/helper layer can run a pinned payload with read-only evidence (referenced by ADR id only); freshness assumes effect terminal events exist (ADR-0030).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0 / Stage 1 — class contract, `Verdict`/`EvidenceHandle`/`EvidenceBundle` schemas, verdict event payloads, `ClaimOnlyEvidence` and no-rewrite rules, `inputs_digest`, AC-G1-1…4, AC-G1-11; C0 / Stage 2 — `explain` surface via the profile; C1 / Stage 3 — bindings recorded and enforced by the scorecard (AC-G1-8), `external_probe`; C2 / Stage 4 — judged validators (G3), hosted `end_state` verdicts.

## Consequences

- WS-A3 records `Validator.declare` fields as the entity's semantic fields (or references `OracleDeclaration`), and treats the `Verdict` as the `Observation{source = validator}` payload; WS-B1 extends the two verification event payloads; WS-B2's `postcondition_results[]` becomes `[EventRef]` of verdict events (CF-WS-G1-04); WS-D1/C3 render verdicts through the profile and never re-render the judged observation; WS-F1/F2 consume `VerificationSummary` and own reflection bounds; WS-B4 exposes `revert(effect_id)` for local `fail`; WS-H1 continues to read records only — a verdict is an input to Π, never an authority change; WS-G3 designs judges as this class with `model_io` handles plus at least one non-transcript handle where one exists; WS-I2/J4/J5 store `phase`/`charged_to` per value and enforce the headline rule; WS-L2/I1 meter M11.

## Reversibility

**High.** The contract is an enum-and-record surface with additive growth; admitting a rewrite operation later would be a policy flip whose cost is provenance audit of every rewritten observation — which is why it is excluded now rather than later.
