# ADR-NNNN — Escalation policy model: closed escalation input, Π floor rows, the never-auto set, a narrowing-only reviewer chain, and attendance-defined resolution of `ask`

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-H7 (with WS-H1 as owner of Π and the kernel; consumers WS-B2, WS-B3, WS-F2, WS-F3, WS-G3, WS-H5, WS-H6, WS-E4, WS-J6, WS-L8) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (security kernel outside the editable policy surface), ADR-0016 (`EffectDomain ∋ permission_request`, `DecisionPoint ∋ authorize, escalate`), ADR-0017 (`authority_delta = widening` only from human origin), ADR-0024 (MUST-code/MUST-data), ADR-0031 (kernel decision `{allow | ask | deny}`; per-class approval defaults as the floor), ADR-0033/0034/0035 (authority classes; `effective_authority`; closed endorsement basis list; check 4 taint gate via Π), ADR-0040 (`ask → deny` on exhaustion), ADR-0047 (judge governance); OQ-101, OQ-086, OQ-055, OQ-017; CF-WS-H7-03, CF-WS-H7-04, CF-WS-H7-05; R-2.8.7, R-2.8.1

## Context
The reference monitor (ADR-0035 check 4) decides by a policy table `Π(EffectClass, effective authority) → allow | ask | deny` and "`ask` resolves only through an `approval` endorsement of this `effect_id`"; ADR-0031 fixes per-class approval defaults as "the floor [WS-H7] narrows from"; OQ-101 leaves the rows, the entry of model-authored risk and judge verdicts, and unattended configuration open. The evidence (dossier §4a M1–M6) is that (i) prompt volume is set by containment before any policy runs (S-053: 84% fewer prompts from sandboxing), (ii) every audited harness resolves `ask` through an ordered chain whose order and failure semantics are load-bearing — Codex hooks → automatic reviewer → user, Claude Code rules → containment → classifier → deny, Inspect approvers chaining on `escalate` — with observed defects of shadowing the human (claude-agent-sdk warning), failing open (goose inspectors) and widening from inside the chain (Codex policy amendments); (iii) automatic reviewers are implementable as attenuated, evidence-restricted processes (Codex Guardian read-only session; Claude Code strips tool results) but are model-conditioned and miss ~17% of overeager actions (S-053); (iv) unattended runs disagree on what `ask` means (Codex `Never`, Claude Code `dontAsk`, pi-mono block-by-default, OpenHands park-and-wait, MCP `input_required`). The Tier-A basis is Felt et al. 2012: match the granting mechanism to severity, reversibility and intent inference, and the number of warnings falls.

## Options considered
1. **A single scalar risk threshold with a human above it** (OpenHands `ConfirmRisky`). *Rejected:* conflates reversibility, scope and provenance; cannot express "reversible but externally driven" (Π-7) or the never-auto set; S-104-class attacks defeat risk-only confirmation (ADR-0035 (c)).
2. **Model decides when to ask** (Codex `OnRequest`; model-supplied `security_risk`). *Rejected:* violates "the model can reason about permission but cannot grant itself permission" (doc 2 §5.6); self-report may only raise the class (ADR-0031).
3. **Open, pluggable approver list with arbitrary order and hooks that may allow or widen** (Codex amendments; goose fail-open). *Rejected:* an escape hatch on authority (T-LCD-01 applied to authority; ADR-0035 option 3); fail-open contradicts defense-in-depth.
4. **Automatic reviewer as an endorser in its own right.** *Rejected:* a `delegate` origin never endorses (ADR-0035 decision 1); would make the reviewer model a member of the TCB.
5. **Closed escalation input; Π floor rows (MUST-code) narrowed only by `authority_cap`-bounded leaves (MUST-data); a constitutional never-auto set; an ordered chain in which every non-human stage may only narrow or pass and fails closed above `reversible`; automatic allow only as a `policy_rule` endorsement consuming a judge verdict; `attendance` and `UnattendedPolicy` as typed configuration (chosen).**

## Decision
1. **Escalation input.** The kernel hands the approval component the closed record `EscalationInput{effect_id, run_id, turn_id, tool_call_id, capability_ref, effect_class, effective_risk_class, args_canonical_hash, effective_authority, context_label, proposal_provenance, grant?, compensation_plan_id?, dedup_support, baseline_ref?, containment{inside_writable_roots, egress_allowlisted, sandboxed}, attendance ∈ {interactive, async, unattended}, budget_state{approvals_remaining?, human_wait_remaining_ms?, approvals_mode}, lease_hit?, pending_batch_id?, retry_of?, delegation_depth}`. Every field is a ledger or sealed-definition record; model-authored justification is never an input to Π.
2. **Π floor rows (supplied to WS-H1 for OQ-101).** Π-1 `read_only ∧ closed` → allow; Π-2 `reversible ∧ workspace_local ∧ inside_writable_roots` → allow; Π-3 `reversible ∧ ¬inside_writable_roots` → ask; Π-4 `compensable ∧ plan registered ∧ grant covers compensator` → allow; Π-5 other `compensable` → ask; Π-6 `irreversible` → ask (per-effect); Π-7 `effective_authority ≤ external` raises one step (allow→ask; ask→deny for `irreversible ∨ scope = external`); Π-8 domain `permission_request` → ask a human of class `principal`, result is a `Permission` diff with `authority_delta = widening` of human origin; Π-9 `spawn_process` with child permissions ⊄ parent → deny; Π-10 `secret_access` to a model-visible sink or `spend` over the soft threshold → ask (deny under Π-7); Π-11 `unknown` → as `irreversible ∧ external`; Π-12 `attendance = unattended ∧ ask` → `UnattendedPolicy ∈ {deny (default), defer, auto_review}`. `deny` rows dominate `ask`, `ask` dominates `allow`. The floor is MUST-code; a sealed definition narrows it only through leaves bounded by `authority_cap` (ADR-0024); the Lab varies the narrowing as a `configuration-level` factor.
3. **Never-auto set (constitutional).** Π-8; `irreversible ∧ external` under Π-7; effects whose `Permission` is revoked/stale; explicit `ask` leaves in the sealed definition; `message_human` effects that are themselves a consent step; `scope = user` persistence endorsements. No mode, layer, hook, lease or reviewer resolves these automatically; `bypass` mode is admissible only with `containment.sandboxed` recorded in the run manifest and still excludes this set.
4. **Reviewer chain.** `ask` is resolved by `ReviewerRef` stages in the fixed order `policy_rule → lease → hook → auto_reviewer → human(principal | reviewer under ApproverGrant)`. Each stage returns `resolve(allow | deny) | pass`; non-human stages may only narrow relative to the previous stage; a stage error, timeout or exhausted budget is `pass` for `read_only/reversible` and `deny` otherwise; a retry with a raised class or widened permissions is re-reviewed (`require_fresh_review`, Codex precedent) and never coalesced with the original request. Chain exhaustion under `attendance ≠ interactive` falls to Π-12.
5. **Automatic allow is a `policy_rule` endorsement.** An `allow` from a hook or from the automatic reviewer never endorses by itself; it is an input to a sealed `HarnessRule{kind: auto_review}` (conditioned, with an assumption-debt record) executed by the kernel, which endorses with basis `policy_rule` and `basis_ref = {rule, ReviewVerdict event, hook event}`. Removing the rule removes automatic approval. The automatic reviewer is a `Validator{kind: judge}` variant running as an `AgentProcess` with permissions ⊆ parent ∩ read-only, `net_egress = ∅`, no extensions or memory writes, an evidence set restricted to items of `authority ≥ principal` plus the kernel-rendered proposal, its own `BudgetNode` (`charged_to = subject`), a closed verdict `{risk_level, authorization, outcome, rationale: Text}` recorded as `security.permission.reviewed`, verdict reuse keyed like a lease and invalidated on new `external` content, mode or rule change, and a repeated-denial fallback (`max_consecutive_denies`, `max_total_denies`) that escalates to a human (attended), suspends (async) or denies-and-continues (unattended).
6. **Attendance and modes.** `attendance` is a run-manifest fact; `approval_mode ∈ {manual, tiered, auto_review, pre_approved_only, unattended_deny, unattended_defer, bypass}` and `UnattendedPolicy` are typed parameters of the Harness Definition (MUST-data) that lower to ACP session modes/config options, Codex `AskForApproval`, Claude Code permission modes and goose modes as *surfaces*. Entering `auto_review` drops blanket allow leaves that grant arbitrary execution (Claude Code precedent) so that no rule shadows the reviewer. `ControlBoundary.assignments[authorize | escalate]` records who owned the decision point for the Lab.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)
- (a) Mechanism evidence: prompts habituate and are design-sensitive — S-WS-H7-03 (CHI 2010, 80k users), S-WS-H7-04 (SOUPS 2012, 17%/3%), S-WS-H7-02 (USENIX Sec 2013, 7–33% clickthrough), all Tier A; mechanism-to-severity matching reduces warnings — S-WS-H7-01 (HotSec 2012, Tier A); containment sets the base rate and the classifier is one layer inside a sandbox — S-053 (Tier B, 84%; 0.4%/17%); ordered chains and their failure modes — S-107, S-WS-H7-05, S-136, S-112, S-109 (Tier B source); runtime approval is the dominant production control — S-WS-H7-07 (`provisional`, 15/21 systems); "the model can reason about permission but cannot grant itself permission" — doc 2 §5.6; endorsement rules — ADR-0035.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `codex/codex-rs/core/src/tools/approvals.rs` (`request_approval`: hooks → guardian → user), `core/src/guardian/{decision,reviewer_config,coverage}.rs` + `assets/guardian/policy_template.md` (attenuated read-only reviewer; `risk_level × user_authorization → outcome`; fail-closed `denied("automatic approval review could not complete")`), `core/src/exec_policy.rs` `prompt_is_rejected_by_policy` (unattended `Never` ⇒ rejection reason), `protocol/src/protocol.rs` `AskForApproval`/`GranularApprovalConfig`; `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/security/{confirmation_policy,ensemble,analyzer}.py` (class threshold; worst-case fusion; fail-closed HIGH); `goose/crates/goose/src/tool_inspection.rs` + `permission/permission_inspector.rs` (`InspectionAction{Allow, Deny, RequireApproval}`; `SmartApprove` narrows only); `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/approval/_policy.py` (chain on `escalate`; reject when exhausted); `claude-agent-sdk-python/src/claude_agent_sdk/types.py` (modes; hook `permissionDecision`; shadowing warning); Claude Code docs (never-auto set; dropped blanket rules; 3/20 fallback). The *integrated* closed input + floor/narrowing split + never-auto set + `policy_rule`-mediated automatic allow is `no-precedent / novel` relative to these (each has two or three of the pieces).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: reversibility is undecidable and classifiers are heuristic (ADR-0031 (c); OQ-086) — floor is monotone so errors ask rather than allow; the automatic reviewer misses ~17% and is manipulable (S-053) — it resolves `ask` only inside a sandbox, never touches the never-auto set, is a calibrated expiring judge, and fails closed; a closed chain order may not fit every deployment (Cursor never blocks, Inspect waits forever) — absorbed by `attendance`/`UnattendedPolicy` as configuration; position-paper argument that oversight atrophies under automation (S-WS-H7-06, `provisional`, no data) — not load-bearing; runtime-approval prevalence (S-WS-H7-07) is `provisional` and used for framing only.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier for the floor, the never-auto set and the chain order; the *automatic reviewer's* thresholds and FN rate are conditioned on the reviewer profile (assumption-debt record; Stage-4 calibration); Π-2/Π-4 assume a sandbox with declared writable roots (WS-B5/E5) and registered compensators (ADR-0032) — without them prompt volume rises and Π-3/Π-5 dominate; `unattended_defer` assumes WS-B3 suspend/wakeup; task class matters (sandboxed coding tasks are mostly Π-1/Π-2; operational tasks hit Π-5/Π-6).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — `EscalationInput`, Π floor rows with `UnattendedPolicy = deny`, never-auto set, chain order with `policy_rule/lease/human` stages, `ControlBoundary` recording; **C1 / Stage 2** — hooks stage (H5-attested), modes, `defer` (with B3), repeated-denial fallback; **C1 / Stage 4** — `auto_review` rule + judge variant with calibration (G3) and debt record; **C2** — taint rows (H2), MCP elicitation routing (E4), hosted mode mirroring (J6).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-05 (the auto-review rule and thresholds carry debt records and expire), T-LCD-08 (the reviewer variant receives the Model Profile and resource account), T-LCD-12 (operations over records; out-of-process), T-LCD-14 (modes and reviewer variants are matched-budget factors), T-LCD-15 (approval metrics class-scoped). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-06/-09/-10/-11/-13 (T-LCD-11 is carried by the companion ADR on lowering). Rung on the ADR-0024 ladder: floor and chain order MUST-code; presets, thresholds and narrowing leaves MUST-data.

## Consequences
- WS-H1 adopts Π-1…Π-12 as the default rows (or supersedes by ADR) and evaluates `ask` through the chain; the taint component of check 4 lands at C2 without changing the rows' shape.
- WS-B2's `KernelDecision` gains no field; `EscalationDecision` refines it with `target` and `reason`.
- WS-G3 supplies calibration for the reviewer; WS-C3 owns its profile rules; WS-I6 owns the debt record's retirement test.
- WS-F3 routes child `ask`s to the root chain; children never hold approver authority.
- WS-K1/K2/E4 render modes and prompts; no surface may add a decision path outside the chain.
- The Lab records `approval_mode`, `UnattendedPolicy` and reviewer variant as `configuration-level` factors.

## Reversibility
**Medium.** Rows, modes and stages can be added by ADR (additive). Two rules are load-bearing and should be treated as constitutional alongside ADR-0035's: non-human stages only narrow, and automatic allow exists only as a `policy_rule`; removing either re-admits reviewer-as-endorser and silent widening (ADR-0002 iii). Replacing the floor with an open table after Stage 2 would invalidate every lease fingerprint and every recorded approval metric.
