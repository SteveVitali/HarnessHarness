# ADR-NNNN — Approval request/response contract: per-effect approval records, durable `pending` vs ephemeral `requested`, decision-before-`prepared`, refusal semantics, asynchronous `defer`, batching, approver grants, and ABI-facing lowering with declared loss

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-H7 (with WS-B1 for event classes, WS-B2 for effect phases, WS-B3 for suspend/wakeup, WS-E4/K4/J6 for lowering, WS-H6 for audit, WS-L8 for organizational grants) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0026 (ledger authoritative; AC-B1-12 ephemeral requests), ADR-0027 (`PermissionId`, `LeaseId`), ADR-0030 (write-ahead `committed`; `authorized | refused` phase), ADR-0031 (`decider`, `permission_id` on `Effect`; timeout is a refusal), ADR-0032 (`abandoned` always escalated), ADR-0033/0035 (endorsement events; `human(author_ref, role)` origin), ADR-0021 (protocol targets; minimum carried set; loss classes), ADR-0042 (`permission` scope kind), ADR-0043 (`permission_wait` in the turn phase profile); WS-L1 §6.5 verb `request_permission`; OQ-091, OQ-017; CF-WS-H7-01, CF-WS-H7-02, CF-WS-H7-05; R-2.8.7, R-2.2.3, R-2.11.*

## Context
ADR-0031 already names the approval record's join key (`permission_id`) and `decider`; WS-B1 defines `security.permission.requested/decided` with requests `ephemeral` (AC-B1-12) and `control.wakeup.*` for WS-B3; ADR-0035 fixes that `approval` endorses one `(effect_id, args_canonical_hash)`. What is missing is the contract: the request and response shapes, what precedes what in the effect lifecycle, what timeout/cancel mean, how a long-running run waits without blocking or replaying, how several requests batch, who besides the principal may decide, and how the request crosses ACP/MCP/hook and Hosting-ABI boundaries. The audited systems supply the pieces: ACP v2's `session/request_permission{title, description?, options[4 kinds], subject?, _meta}` with a mandatory `cancelled` outcome on turn cancel; Inspect's five-valued decision, pending-approval queue, single-driver ACP routing and wait-forever human approver; OpenHands' `WAITING_FOR_CONFIRMATION` state with remote `respond_to_confirmation`; MCP's `InputRequiredResult{requestState}` continuation; claude-agent-sdk's hook `defer`; Codex's `ReviewDecision::TimedOut` and `RequestPermissions{scope: Turn | Session}`; LangGraph's `interrupt` whose resume "re-executes all logic" of the node — the duplicate-effect hazard that fixes *where* an asynchronous decision may sit.

## Options considered
1. **Approval as a synchronous callback inside the tool executor** (pi-mono gate; claude-agent-sdk `can_use_tool`). *Rejected:* not durable, cannot resume across a restart, and can be shadowed by earlier rules without a record.
2. **Approval as a graph interrupt inside the step** (LangGraph). *Rejected:* resume re-executes the step; effects issued before the interrupt duplicate (ADR-0030/0031 forbid).
3. **Approval only at the ticket/PR level; no per-effect prompts** (Cursor, Symphony). *Rejected as the sole mechanism:* leaves `irreversible ∧ external` effects inside a run without a decision point; adopted as the *default unattended checkpoint* via `attendance`.
4. **Ledger-recorded per-effect request/response with a durable owed-decision event, decision strictly before `prepared`, refusal semantics for timeout/cancel, `defer` via B3 suspend/wakeup, batch by class, approver grants, and typed lowering with a loss list (chosen).**

## Decision
1. **Records.** `ApprovalRequest{permission_id, effect_id, subject{capability_ref, effect_class, effective_risk_class, args_canonical_hash, preview: kernel-rendered records}, why: Explanation, options: [ApprovalOption{option_id, kind ∈ {allow_once, allow_lease{pattern, scope, max_uses?, expires_at?}, deny_once, deny_lease{pattern, scope}, modify (C2), abort_run, escalate{to}}, narrowing?}], requested_of: ReviewerRef, mode ∈ {sync, async}, deadline?, batch_id?, retry_of?, provenance}`; `ApprovalResponse{permission_id, decision ∈ {allow, deny, abort, cancelled, timed_out}, option_id?, lease?, decider{kind ∈ {policy, cache, hook, auto_reviewer, human}, ref, authority, grant_ref?}, rationale?: Text, decided_at, latency_ms, responder_provenance}`. `PermissionRequest` is a runtime record keyed by `PermissionId` with `state ∈ {pending, decided, withdrawn}`.
2. **Events.** `security.permission.pending{permission_id, effect_id, requested_of, deadline?, batch_id?}` is **durable**; `security.permission.requested{rendering}` is **ephemeral** (AC-B1-12 preserved for the prompt, not for the owed decision — CF-WS-H7-01); `security.permission.decided{…, effect_id, effective_risk_class, decider, lease_id?, latency_ms, reason}` (one sum for `decider`, CF-WS-H7-02); `security.permission.escalated{from, to, reason}`; `security.permission.reviewed` (automatic reviewer verdict); `security.label.endorsed{basis ∈ {approval, policy_rule}}` on every `allow`. Provenance is mandatory on all of them (ADR-0035 table).
3. **Ordering invariants.** The decision precedes `action.effect.prepared`; `intended → authorized | refused` is the only transition an approval may cause; exactly one decision per `permission_id` (a duplicate `respond` returns the recorded decision — `AlreadyDecided`); `timed_out`, `cancelled` (turn cancel, ACP `cancelled`) and `abort` are refusals (`action.effect.refused{reason}`), never `unknown`; a human `allow` from a responder without `principal` authority or an `ApproverGrant` fails with `IllegitimateEndorsement`; an `allow` writes the `authorizes` edge from the `Permission` (or lease) to the effect.
4. **Asynchronous form.** `mode = async` ⇒ after `pending`, `lifecycle.run.suspended{reason: awaiting_approval, permission_id}` and `control.wakeup.scheduled{trigger: permission_decided(permission_id)}`; the writer lease may lapse; `respond` fires the wakeup and the run resumes with `recovery_decision{last_durable_phase: intended, action: continue}` under a fresh `lease_generation`; nothing after `intended` exists to replay. `deadline` is optional; without it the wait is bounded only by the `time.human_wait_ms` budget. Out-of-band notification (editor, ticket, PR, chat) is a surface; the ledger record is the source of truth. A target that returns an MCP `input_required{requestState}` is the target-side form of `defer`: the opaque state is kept on the `Effect` record and the retry carries the same `effect_id`.
5. **Batching and coalescing.** `batch_id` groups pending requests of one `effective_risk_class`, `requested_of` and `scope`; the surface may render one prompt; the response is one `ApprovalResponse` per `permission_id` and one endorsement per effect; `irreversible` is never batched. Identical pending `(ToolCapability, args_canonical_hash)` requests coalesce into one `permission_id`; a retry with a raised class or widened permissions never coalesces (`retry_of` set; fresh review).
6. **Delegation of approval authority.** `ApproverGrant{grant_id, approver: PrincipalRef, delegated_by: human principal, may_decide{domains, max_risk_class}, may_lease, may_escalate_to?, expires_at, attestation?}` recorded as `security.permission.granted{kind: approver_grant}`; a reviewer under a grant endorses with basis `approval` and `basis_ref = grant`; grants attenuate and never re-delegate wider; `AgentProcess`es never hold grants; a child run's `ask` bubbles to the root chain with `delegation_depth`. Authority class of a grant holder's endorsement is OQ-WS-H7-01 (WS-L3/H1).
7. **Lowering (ADR-0021 pattern; T-LCD-11).** ACP v2 `session/request_permission`: `title` ← kernel rendering; `description` ← `Explanation.summary`; `subject` ← `tool_call | command{command, cwd}` or `other`; options ← `allow_once/allow_lease(scope = session)/deny_once/deny_lease` as `allow_once | allow_always | reject_once | reject_always`; `_meta.hh` ← `{permission_id, effect_id, effective_risk_class, reason_code, pattern, batch_id}`; `cancelled` ↔ `cancelled`. **Declared loss:** narrowed lease patterns and `max_uses` (`hint_only`), `modify`, `escalate{to}`, `abort_run`, `deadline` (`no_slot`). MCP: incoming `elicitation/create` is a `message_human` effect through Π (never auto-accepted; `decline` is the unattended default). Hooks (H5-attested): receive the `EscalationInput` projection, return `{allow, deny, ask, defer}`; `allow` is an input to a `policy_rule`, `defer` sets `mode = async`. Hosting ABI: the §6.5 verb `request_permission` carries `ApprovalRequest`; hosted participants declare `permission-elicitation` tri-state; without it approval metrics are `n/a{reason: observability}`. Lifted decisions from any target have `origin = participant`, and only a `principal`-class responder can turn one into an `approval` endorsement.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)
- (a) Mechanism evidence: effects are lost or duplicated at the approval boundary unless the decision is durable and precedes preparation — S-143 (`interrupt` re-executes the node), S-110 (implicit confirmation replay, CF-063), ADR-0030/0031 (write-ahead; timeout is a refusal, Codex `TimedOut`); blocking is the expensive failure for long-running agents — S-052 ("it could sit there for hours"); a parked-and-resumed decision is implementable as a first-class state — S-110 `WAITING_FOR_CONFIRMATION` + REST `respond_to_confirmation`, S-153 `InputRequiredResult{requestState}`, S-109 hook `defer`; protocol shape and cancel semantics — S-035/S-WS-H7-12 (v2 RFD; `cancelled` MUST); single-driver routing avoids stale prompts — S-136 `_human/acp.py`; batch inspection with per-item decisions — S-112, S-110; approval endorses one effect — ADR-0035 (S-218/S-219 DLM/Flume lineage); attack on batches and blanket approvals — S-WS-H7-08 (`provisional`).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/approval/{_approval.py, _human/manager.py, _human/acp.py, _human/approver.py}` (decision sum incl. `terminate`/`escalate`; pending futures; ACP routing; wait-forever; `awaiting_human`); `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/conversation/state.py` L48–123 + `openhands-agent-server/openhands/agent_server/event_service.py` L1622–1700 (parked state; remote decision); `agent-client-protocol/docs/rfds/v2/permission-requests.mdx` + `agent-client-protocol-schema/src/v2/client.rs` L1613–2045; `modelcontextprotocol/schema/2026-07-28/schema.ts` L568–612, L2778–2830, L3134–3141; `codex/codex-rs/protocol/src/{protocol.rs (ReviewDecision::TimedOut/Abort), request_permissions.rs (PermissionGrantScope), approvals.rs (GuardianAssessmentEvent)}`, `core/src/tools/network_approval.rs` (deferred network approval with cancellation token); `claude-agent-sdk-python/src/claude_agent_sdk/types.py` L420 (`defer`); `langchain-ai/langgraph/libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py` (`interrupt` — the counter-precedent). The durable `pending`/ephemeral `requested` split bound to the effect lifecycle, with approver grants and a typed loss list, is `no-precedent / novel` as an integrated contract.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: durable parking invites indefinite waits (Inspect "wait-forever") — bounded by the `time.human_wait_ms` budget and by `attendance` defaults (`deny` for unattended); PR-level review may be the only checkpoint a team wants (S-052, S-048) — supported as the unattended default without removing per-effect records; ACP cannot carry narrowed leases or `modify` — declared loss rather than silent (T-LCD-11); grants add an organizational surface the C0 kernel does not need — grants are C1/Stage 4 and the principal-only path is complete without them; the ephemeral-request rule (AC-B1-12) exists to keep the ledger small — kept for the rendering; only the small owed-decision fact becomes durable.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier and task class; `defer` assumes WS-B3's suspend/wakeup and the fenced writer lease (ADR-0026); MCP `input_required` handling assumes the target honours `requestState`; ACP lowering assumes ACP v2 (`subject`, required `title`) — v1 degrades `subject` to `toolCall` only, an additional declared loss; grants assume an identity for reviewers (WS-L8/H3).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — records, `pending/requested/decided` events, ordering invariants, refusal semantics, `sync` mode, hook `ask/deny`; **C1 / Stage 2** — `async`/`defer` with B3, batching/coalescing, hook `allow/defer`; **C1 / Stage 4** — ACP lowering with loss report (E4 ACP target is C1/Stage 4), approver grants, `security.permission.reviewed`; **C2** — `modify`, MCP elicitation routing, Hosting-ABI `permission-elicitation` capability declaration (J6, Phase 3), organizational grant routing (L8, Phase 4).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-07 (`permission-elicitation` declared/probed/unknown; never coerced), T-LCD-11 (typed `_meta` slot + loss report + lift), T-LCD-12 (operations over records), T-LCD-13 (delivered `Explanation`/`NarrowingHint` are `context.artefact.delivered` items so their effect on behaviour is measurable), T-LCD-15 (`n/a` for participants without the capability). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-04/-05/-06/-08/-09/-10/-14 (T-LCD-14 is carried by the companion budget ADR). Rung: records and ordering MUST-code; options offered, deadlines and batch policy MUST-data.

## Consequences
- WS-B1 adds `security.permission.pending` (durable), `reviewed`, `escalated` and extends `decided.decider`; WS-B3 consumes `awaiting_approval` suspension and resumes at `intended`; WS-H6 audits every `decided` and every `escalated`; WS-E4 emits the ACP loss report; WS-K1/K2 render `ApprovalRequest` and never invent options; WS-J6 declares the capability; WS-L8 owns grant issuance in organizations; WS-I1's `permission_wait` phase and `permission` scope kind bind to `pending → decided`.
- The model always observes a terminal (`authorized` or `refused{reason}`) for every `ask`; it never observes a hanging request.

## Reversibility
**Medium.** Adding option kinds, targets or grant fields is additive. Making `pending` ephemeral again would break `defer` and audit completeness; moving the decision after `prepared` would reintroduce duplicate effects (ADR-0030) — both are load-bearing. Dropping grants (C1) costs nothing to C0.
