# ADR-NNNN — Containment policy as typed, provenance-bearing data attached to the environment handle (`ContainmentPolicy/1`; kernel-protected paths; layered meet by authority class)

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-H4 (consumers: WS-B5, WS-E5, WS-H1, WS-H3, WS-H5, WS-H7, WS-F3, WS-I4, WS-J3, WS-J5, WS-K4/L5) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (security kernel outside the editable policy surface), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-04/-06/-07/-10/-11/-12), ADR-0015/0016 (`AgentProcess.native.environment`, `EffectDomain`), ADR-0023/0024 (definitions are data; MUST-data list; monotone authority layering), ADR-0033/0034 (provenance record; never-widen), ADR-0036 (content addressing; `configuration_id` includes `environment_ref`), ADR-0039/0040 (dimensions, budget nodes), ADR-0050 §8; CF-WS-H4-01, CF-WS-H4-04, CF-WS-H4-06; OQ-045, OQ-086, OQ-118, OQ-WS-H4-03/-04/-08; R-2.8.4, R-2.2.5, R-2.5.5

## Context

Doc 2 §5.6 and §11 require "hard environment-level bounds using sandboxes, VMs, filesystem boundaries, credential isolation, and egress controls" with the security kernel "outside the editable policy surface" (ADR-0002). Ontology v1 places the environment handle, effect capture and egress mediation on the environment boundary; `AgentProcess.native` carries `environment: EnvironmentRef` (ADR-0016) but nothing yet says what bounds that environment. The source audit (dossier §4a F1–F2, F8) shows three independent enforcing systems — Codex (`codex-rs/protocol/src/protocol.rs` `SandboxPolicy`/`WritableRoot`, `models.rs` `PermissionProfile`, `network-proxy/src/config.rs`), Anthropic's sandbox-runtime (`src/sandbox/sandbox-schemas.ts`) and Harbor (`src/harbor/models/task/config.py` `NetworkPolicy`) — converging on the same serialisable shape: reads allow-all-except, writes allow-only, network allow-only with a default for the unmatched host, deny wins, protected metadata paths inside writable roots, and layered policies that only narrow (Codex `environment_policy.rs`: "Enable permissions only when both controller and owner allow them"). None of them carries provenance on the policy or records who loosened it, and two persist approvals into repository-scoped settings — the X-CPE path S-103 describes. OpenHands' workspace contract carries no policy at all (negative precedent).

## Options considered

1. **Leave containment to the environment provider (container flags, VM config) and record only an image digest.** *Rejected:* OpenHands and Inspect show the boundary is then silently lost by customisation ("a custom compose *replaces* the generated configuration … including its `network_mode: none`"); results keyed by `environment_ref` would not identify what was actually bounded.
2. **Encode containment as Permission grants only (ceiling = floor).** *Rejected:* a Permission is authority the monitor *may* confer per effect; containment must hold even if the monitor, a classifier or a hook is wrong (S-053: probabilistic gates have non-zero miss rates). The two must be separable so the Lab can vary one (T-LCD-09).
3. **Per-backend native profiles as opaque payloads (a Seatbelt profile, a bwrap argv).** *Rejected:* untyped escape hatch (T-LCD-01/-02 shape); no meet across layers; no loss report; cannot be compared across replicates.
4. **One typed `ContainmentPolicy/1` record, provenance-bearing, content-addressed, attached to the handle, with a kernel-protected minimum and a layered meet that only narrows below `principal` (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`ContainmentPolicy/1`** is a typed data document `{policy_id: semantic_id, version_id, provenance: ProvenanceRecord, fs, net, proc, resources, amendment, residual_channels[], ext}` with closed sums per HIR dialect and registered `ext` that never decides enforcement (ADR-0015). `fs = {read{mode ∈ {allow_all_except, allow_only}, deny[], allow_within_deny[]}, write{allow: [WritableRoot{root, read_only_subpaths[], protected_metadata_names[]}], deny_within_allow[]}, exec{allow | any}, protected_metadata ⊇ KERNEL_PROTECTED, symlink_resolution = before_validation, mounts[{source: ContentAddress | HostPath, target, mode ∈ {ro, rw, masked(sentinel_ref)}}]}`; `net = {mode ∈ {none, mediated, public}, default_unmatched ∈ {deny, ask}, rules: [EgressRule{host: HostPattern, ports, protocols ⊆ {http, https_connect, socks5_tcp, socks5_udp, tcp_transparent}, methods, decision ∈ {allow, deny}, credential_bindings: [CredentialRef], justification: Text, provenance}], non_public_destinations ∈ {deny, allow_listed}, local_binding = false, unix_sockets{mode, allow[]}, dns{resolver ∈ {mediator, listed_nameservers[], none}}, tls{terminate = false, inspect_hooks[]}, upstream_proxy?, methods_default}`; `proc = {isolation_class ∈ {none, process_sandbox, namespaces, user_space_kernel, microvm, external}, unshare ⊆ {net, pid, ipc, user, mount}, no_new_privs = true, syscall_filter, ptrace = deny, setuid = deny, max_processes, env{inherit ∈ {all, core, none}, exclude[], set, include_only[]}, die_with_parent = true}`; `resources = {cpu_ms?, memory_bytes?, disk_bytes?, pids?, open_files?, wall_ms?, network_bytes_out?, network_calls?}` each mapped to a `BudgetNode` dimension (ADR-0040) or a registered `ext` dimension (CF-WS-H4-04); `amendment = {allowed_bases ⊆ {approval, policy_rule, seal}, strict: bool, session_cache: bool, persist_scope_ceiling ∈ PersistenceScope}`; `residual_channels = [{kind ∈ {approved_host_body, tls_opaque, dns_names, unix_socket, side_channel, host_bridge}, statement: Text, closer: Ref | none, owner}]` (T-LCD-05 shape: an unowned residual fails readiness).
2. **Kernel-protected minimum.** `KERNEL_PROTECTED` names the harness's own definition/config/hook/skill/scheduled-task locations and VCS hook directories; they are read-only inside every `WritableRoot` on every backend; no layer may remove a name (`ProtectedPathExemption`). Invariants: N1 `deny` before `allow`; N2 no global wildcard in a `deny` rule; N3 `mode = none ⇒ rules = []`; N4 `mode = public` requires `provenance.authority ≥ principal` and a `residual_channels` entry; F1 `symlink_resolution = before_validation` is not configurable.
3. **Layered meet.** `effective(layers[]) → ContainmentPolicy` orders layers by `provenance.authority` (`kernel > definition > principal > delegate > environment > external > unverified`), starts from the kernel default (`read = allow_all_except(KERNEL_DENY)`, `write = allow_only([])`, `net.mode = none`, `isolation_class ≥ process_sandbox`, `no_new_privs`, `strict = true`), and lets each layer **narrow any field** while it may **loosen** only what its class is entitled to: `principal` — `net.mode → mediated`, `allow` rules, `WritableRoot`s, `strict → false`; `definition` (at `seal`) — `allow` rules with `justification`, mounts, resources; layers at or below `delegate` (repository config files, imported profiles, model proposals) — narrowing only, else `ContainmentWidening` (parallel to `AuthorityWidening`). Deny entries from any layer survive; booleans AND; allow sets intersect across lower-authority layers. The effective policy is sealed into the run manifest: `configuration_version_id` covers its `version_id`, `configuration_id` covers its `semantic_id` through `environment_ref` (ADR-0036).
4. **Attachment.** The environment handle (WS-B5) carries `containment: Ref<ContainmentPolicy>` and returns a `ContainmentReport` at attach (ADR WS-H4-adr-3); `security.containment.applied` is the ledger record and `action.environment.attached` references it (CF-WS-H4-05). Child environments (WS-F3 delegation) receive `effective(parent ⊓ child_layer)` — never wider (ADR-0035 check 7).
5. **Identity.** `semantic_id` is computed over normalised patterns and closed-sum values, excluding `provenance`, `ext`, `justification` text and host spelling (T-LCD-10); renaming a host pattern's spelling is a surface edit.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-04 (one policy below every target), T-LCD-06 (attaches to the handle, not to IR entities; hosted participants reuse `isolation_class = external`), T-LCD-10 (content-addressed semantics), T-LCD-11 (each backend emits a containment lowering loss report — ADR WS-H4-adr-3), T-LCD-12 (data + operations, no inheritance), T-LCD-05 reflexively via `residual_channels`. Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-08/-09/-13/-14/-15. **ADR-0024 ladder rung:** MUST-data (the policy) over MUST-code (the enforcers); no pointer to executable code except `syscall_filter` and `inspect_hooks` refs resolved under the pointer rule (CF-055).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: hard environment-level bounds as the backstop; allowlist is a capability grant; protected project files executed before trust; symlink-before-validation — S-053 (Tier B production); Landlock's unprivileged stackable rights and `no_new_privs` — S-WS-H4-06 (Tier A); Firecracker "does not perform any network traffic filtering" and gVisor's no-passthrough model fix what a machine boundary does and does not bound — S-WS-H4-07/-08; X-CPE persistence into project/user scope as an attack — S-103 (`provisional (mech)`, corroborated in source by Claude Code's protected paths and Codex's `protected_metadata_names`); monotone layering — ADR-0024/0034 (ratified); Codex cloud agent-phase internet off by default with method restriction — S-WS-H4-04; Lab-side network policy as task data with per-phase switching — S-132 `src/harbor/models/task/config.py`, `trial/network_policy.py`.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `codex/codex-rs/protocol/src/protocol.rs` L1074–1140 (`SandboxPolicy`, `WritableRoot{root, read_only_subpaths, protected_metadata_names}`), `models.rs` L419–433 (`PermissionProfile{Managed | Disabled | External}`), `permissions.rs` L80–90, L235–239; `network-proxy/src/config.rs` L20–105 (`None < Allow < Deny`), L363–380 (`NetworkMode`), `environment_policy.rs` L40–84 (owner-over-controller meet preserving denials); `linux-sandbox/src/bwrap.rs` L380–450, L595–645 (`.git/.agents/.codex` re-protected under symlink remap); `exec-server-protocol/src/protocol.rs` L283–362 (`ExecEnvPolicy`, portable sandbox intent); `sandbox-runtime/src/sandbox/sandbox-schemas.ts` (`FsReadRestrictionConfig{denyOnly, allowWithinDeny}`, `FsWriteRestrictionConfig{allowOnly, denyWithinAllow}`, `NetworkRestrictionConfig`, `CredentialRestrictionConfig{maskedFileBinds}`); `harbor/src/harbor/models/task/config.py` L36–80; `modelcontextprotocol/schema/2026-07-28/schema.ts` L2758–2775 (`Root`) as the lowering target. The provenance-bearing, content-addressed policy with an authority-ordered meet and a kernel-protected minimum is `no-precedent / novel` as an integrated record.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: containment reduces usability/observability and has shipped flaws (doc 2 §6; S-053 EDR loss, pre-trust hooks) — does not overturn: the record makes the boundary a declared factor and the cost a measured quantity (AC-H4-11); production tools fall back to unsandboxed execution when dependencies are missing (Claude Code default; Codex `Disabled`) — does not overturn: a research instrument keyed by `environment_ref` cannot silently change the boundary between replicates, so Lab runs are strict and interactive degradation is stamped on every event (ADR WS-H4-adr-3); containers are escapable (S-WS-H4-10, `provisional`) — does not overturn: `isolation_class` is evidence, not assurance, and AC-H4-12 tests the configuration; hosted participants bring their own boundary — absorbed by `isolation_class = external` with reported evidence.
- (d) Conditionality: model- and task-independent; the kernel defaults assume coding/computer-use environments (workspace-write, no network); other domains extend the closed sums by dialect bump. Assumes an environment handle exists (WS-B5) and a helper that can apply the policy on at least one backend (WS-E5); without a `process_sandbox`-class backend the effective policy is `unknown`-evidenced and effects are refused (fail-closed). Budget mapping assumes ADR-0040 budget nodes at Stage 2.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — schema, kernel defaults, `KERNEL_PROTECTED`, `effective()` with `ContainmentWidening`, handle slot, AC-H4-09; **C0 / Stage 2** — resources → budget mapping, `amendment` semantics with ADR WS-H4-adr-3; **C1 / Stage 4** — `user_space_kernel`/`microvm` classes, per-phase `policy_rule` layers (Harbor precedent), hosted `external`.

## Consequences

- WS-B5's handle gains `containment: Ref<ContainmentPolicy>` and must return a `ContainmentReport`; WS-E5's executor receives the policy by content address and inline.
- WS-H1's `authorize` reads the effective policy (floor) alongside `Permission` (ceiling) — ADR WS-H4-adr-3; WS-H3 fills `credential_bindings` and `masked` mounts; WS-H5's extension directories are in `KERNEL_PROTECTED`.
- WS-L2/WS-A3 rule on `ext` dimensions for bytes and decision counters (CF-WS-H4-04); WS-J5 annotates amended replicates; WS-I4 ships environment classes with attested images.
- A repository's own configuration can only narrow containment; "approve and don't ask again" persists at most at `session | run` scope unless a `principal` amends the run-level layer (CF-WS-H4-06).

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Adding fields, backends or isolation classes is additive by dialect bump. Collapsing the record into Permission grants, or letting sub-`principal` layers loosen it, would reopen the X-CPE/persisted-approval path and remove the floor/ceiling separation that WS-H1, WS-J3 (containment as an experimental factor) and the readiness residual rule depend on — that separation is load-bearing.
