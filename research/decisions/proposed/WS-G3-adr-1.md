# ADR-NNNN — The critic contract: critics are `Validator` variants over a kernel-built evidence bundle with a closed evidence-class set; verdict grounding is derived from cited evidence; verdict authority follows determinism and its label follows evidence; verdicts never endorse or confer; two sites, two uses; ledger recording and declared-lossy lowering

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-G3 (consumers: WS-G1, WS-G2, WS-H1, WS-H7, WS-E2, WS-D2, WS-D3, WS-D4, WS-D5, WS-I2, WS-J4, WS-I5, WS-F3, WS-F4, WS-B1, WS-I1, WS-J6) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0014 (compliance chain; `detector = judged`), ADR-0016 (`Validator` kinds; `model_claim` never satisfies a Validator alone; delegation attenuates), ADR-0033/0034/0035 (authority classes; P1 derivation join; P3 context label; RP; closed endorsement list — a `delegate` origin never endorses), ADR-0039 (`evaluator_calls`; `charged_to`), ADR-0045 §9 (scorers per run over a boundary), ADR-0046 (search vs artifact), ADR-0047 (oracle classes; `oracle_failure`; judge obligations; CF-114), ADR-0026/0029 (views; content addresses), ADR-0042 (`causes[]`), ADR-0050 §8/D2; CF-080 (authority ≠ evidence class), CF-WS-G3-01…06; OQ-049, OQ-053, OQ-059, OQ-123, OQ-WS-D3-06; R-2.7.3, R-2.7.1, R-2.9.2

## Context

Doc 2 §5.3 fixes the principle: "Independent model critics can add coverage, but they should consume the same authoritative external evidence rather than simply critique another model's prose." Doc 2 §8 warns that judges "can be correlated with the same failure modes as the tested agent." ADR-0047 admitted judges as `Validator{kind: judge}` with a `ProfileRef`, snapshot independence, a calibration reference and instrument cost attribution, and left the rubric and the recipe to WS-G3. ADR-0016 fixes that a `model_claim` Observation "never satisfies a Validator alone"; ADR-0033/0034 fix that a verdict derived from evidence carries the join of the evidence labels and that the model never endorses. What no ratified ADR yet fixes is *what a critic sees and how the verdict records it*: the audit (dossier §4c) shows every production critic assembles its own input from the transcript (OpenHands `critic/impl/api/critic.py` classifies the condensed transcript and receives `git_patch = None`; Inspect's grader defaults to the evaluated model; HAL and Harbor record the judge model but not the evidence), that the one production critic with disciplined evidence handling (Codex Guardian) enumerates trusted vs untrusted evidence in a prompt rather than in a record, and that the standardized verdict event (OTel `gen_ai.evaluation.result`) carries no judge identity, evidence or calibration. The academic evidence says evidence access is the lever (Agent-as-a-Judge ≈ human baseline where transcript judging is far below; TRAIL 11 %), that hallucinated findings are real (CriticGPT), and that a verdict used as a reward gets optimized against (METR; Baker et al.).

## Options considered

1. **Judges as free-form scorers with a prompt** (Inspect `model_graded_*`, Harbor `analyze`, HAL `llm_log_analyzer`). *Rejected:* the verdict's evidence basis is unrecorded, so a judged 0.8 grounded on the beneficiary's own summary and one grounded on test output are indistinguishable (CF-029's masquerade in another form); the grader-defaults-to-beneficiary precedent shows the shape invites the exact failure ADR-0047 forbids.
2. **Deterministic critics only** (refuse judges). *Rejected by ADR-0047* (removes most grounding/compliance signal at Stage 3) and by S-135/S-046 evidence that judges catch what verifiers miss.
3. **Critic assembles its own context from the transcript, with prompt-level evidence rules** (Codex Guardian policy prompt; browser-use judge). *Rejected as the contract* (kept as precedent): evidence trust encoded in prose is a `Text` leaf the Lab cannot ablate or verify; the reviewer's admission of untrusted content must be a recorded, kernel-enforced fact so the verdict's label can follow it (ADR-0034 P1).
4. **Critic verdicts as endorsements** (a passing critic raises the artifact's authority or resolves `ask`). *Rejected by ADR-0035* (closed basis list; the model never endorses) and WS-H7/H1 (automatic allow only as a `policy_rule` endorsement; judges feed Π upward only).
5. **One contract for programmatic and model critics over a kernel-built, class-labelled evidence bundle; verdict grounding derived from cited items; authority by determinism, label by evidence; verdicts consumed only as metrics or as bounded/raise-only gate inputs; recorded in the ledger and lowered with declared loss (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **A critic is a `Validator` component variant** described by `CriticDeclaration = OracleDeclaration ∪ {critic_kind ∈ {programmatic, judge, agentic_judge, emulated, monitor}, evidence_mode ⊆ {ledger_view, end_state, artifacts, workspace_probe, screenshots, transcript, reasoning, emulation}, admission_mode ∈ {principal_only, quarantined_external, all_labelled}, probe_capabilities: [Ref<ToolCapability>] (read-only ∩ parent), independence: IndependenceVector (companion ADR), site ∈ {instrument, runtime}, use ∈ {report, gate}, placement?, verdict_type, comparative: bool, rubric_ref?: Ref<Text{authority ≥ definition}>, calibration_ref?, held_out_from: [CriticRef], debt: AssumptionDebtRecord, charged_to}`; registered under the operation-shaped contract `build_evidence / probe / judge / record_verdict / consume / calibrate / critic_experiment` (dossier §6.2), receiving `(ModelProfile, ResourceAccount)`. A beneficiary prompt that asks the model to check its own work is a `HarnessRule`, not a critic, and yields no verdict.
2. **The kernel builds the evidence.** `build_evidence(run_id, until_seq, scope, evidence_mode, admission_mode, budget) → EvidenceBundle{bundle_id, items: [EvidenceItem{ref, evidence_class, provenance, label, bytes_or_view_hash, truncated}], omitted: [OmissionRecord], task_contract_ref?, reference_ref?}` is a pure projection over ledger views, the end-state handle and the artifact store; items are rendered under the role-placement invariant (ADR-0034 RP); items above the admission mode are omitted and listed, never silently rendered; the bundle is content-addressed and recorded. A critic never reads the transcript directly.
3. **Closed `evidence_class` values** complete the ratified ontology term: `measured` (kernel/environment facts: ledger events, validator verdicts, end-state reads, executable results, kernel-taken screenshots, read-only probe results), `reconciled` (a claim checked against state by the execution-alignment layer — `verification.claim.reconciled{agreement}`), `claimed` (any AgentProcess output: messages, reasoning, self-reports, `risk_assessment`, hosted-participant claims). Orthogonal to `AuthorityClass` (CF-080): an `external` document read by the kernel is `measured` as to its bytes and `claimed` as to any assertion inside it.
4. **Verdict grounding is derived.** `grounding(verdict) = strongest evidence_class among cited items ∈ {measured, reconciled, claimed}`; no cited items ⇒ `ungrounded` ⇒ `outcome_class = oracle_failure`. Headline metrics (ADR-0047 decision 2 unchanged: judged values are never headline at C0) and every `use = gate` consumption require `grounding ∈ {measured, reconciled}`; `claimed`-only verdicts are admissible only for `critic_kind = monitor` veto effects and exploratory metrics.
5. **Verdict record.** `CriticVerdict{verdict_id, critic_ref, oracle_id, subject{run_id, scope, until_seq}, bundle_id, verdict (typed; `inconclusive{reason ∈ {missing_evidence, budget, out_of_rubric, parse_failure}}` first-class), confidence, grounding, findings: [Finding{kind, location?, severity, confidence, evidence_ref (mandatory)}], rationale: Text, position_calibrated, panel?, provenance: ProvenanceRecord, cost{evaluator_calls, tokens, spend, charged_to}, calibration_ref?, trigger_reason?}`. Invariants: an uncited finding is dropped and counted (`uncited_findings`); an out-of-set or unparseable verdict is `oracle_failure`, never a grade; `comparative` verdicts are evaluated in both orders and aggregated; `panel` follows ADR-0045 reducer semantics (majority withholds on parse failure).
6. **Authority by determinism, label by evidence.** A deterministic critic's verdict is a `kernel`-class fact (ADR-0033 minting rule); a judge's verdict is `delegate` with `label = ⊔ label(cited evidence) ⊔ delegate` (ADR-0034 P1) and `derived_from = bundle items`. **No critic verdict endorses, confers authority, resolves `ask → allow`, or satisfies a Validator obligation by itself**; a `gate` verdict is an input to a sealed `HarnessRule` (WS-H7's `policy_rule` pattern) or a raise-only input to Π (WS-H1), and a `report` verdict is a `MetricValue` under a metric with `oracle_classes_allowed ∋ judge` and `detector_classes_allowed ∋ judged`, never merged with deterministic values (ADR-0047).
7. **Two sites, two uses.** `site = instrument` (Lab-invoked per run over a boundary, ADR-0045 §9; `charged_to = instrument`) or `site = runtime` (bound in the sealed definition at a `DecisionPoint ∈ {verify, stop, authorize, compact, delegate}`; `charged_to = subject` under `harness_overhead.verification` with its own `BudgetNode` — CF-114). `use = report` or `use = gate`; a gate critic's verdict is never reported as the run's success; a gate critic inside an evolution arm is counted in `search_budget.judge_calls` and the arm's `artifact_benefit` must come from a critic not in the gate's `held_out_from` closure or from a deterministic oracle (`JudgeLeakedIntoArtifact`, extending ADR-0046 `LeakedSplit`). `consume` at `stop`/`verify` is bounded by `max_iterations` and `budget_share_cap` and recorded as `control.critic.gated{verdict_id, decision_point, iteration, followup_ref}`.
8. **Ledger and lowering.** Verdicts are appended as `verification.validator.verdict` with payload extensions `{detector, grounding, bundle_id, evidence_cited[], calibration_ref?, independence_summary, uncited_findings}` (requested of WS-B1, CF-WS-G3-03); judged `followed` events reference the verdict as `detector_ref` with `evidence_ref = bundle_id` (ADR-0014); probes are `action.effect.*` with `scope.agent = critic`; a critic spawn is `control.subagent.spawned{role = judge}`. Export lowers to OpenTelemetry `gen_ai.evaluation.result` (name, score value/label, explanation, response id) with a telemetry loss report naming judge identity, evidence, calibration, independence and cost (T-LCD-11).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-02 (rubrics are `Text` leaves with provenance/authority; instrument opacity bucket), T-LCD-05 (every critic carries a debt record; `critic_experiment` is its removal test), T-LCD-08 (contract receives profile and resource account), T-LCD-10 (rubric content vs rendering), T-LCD-11 (declared-lossy lowering), T-LCD-12 (operations over records; per-run out-of-process), T-LCD-13 (judged detectors carry `detector`/`confidence`/`evidence_ref`), T-LCD-15 (judged metrics require `model_io`). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-06/-07/-09/-14 (T-LCD-14 is carried by the companion cost ADR). Rung on the ADR-0024 ladder: evidence building, grounding derivation, verdict recording and consumption rules are MUST-code; rubrics, admission modes, placements and thresholds are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** evidence access, not judge strength, is the lever — Agent-as-a-Judge (S-WS-G3-07, Tier C), TRAIL 11 % trace-error localization (S-WS-G3-08, Tier C), Anthropic's evaluator clicking through the running application (S-046, Tier B), browser-use judge over screenshots with ground-truth precedence (S-WS-G3-13); critics hallucinate findings — CriticGPT (S-WS-G3-06, Tier C); verdicts used as rewards are optimized against — METR (S-WS-G3-16, Tier B), Baker et al. obfuscation (S-WS-G3-05, Tier C), AI Control (S-WS-G3-04, Tier A); self-critique in-context degrades — Huang et al. (S-WS-G3-10, Tier A), Anthropic "talk itself into" (S-046); the ratified basis — ADR-0016 (`model_claim` never satisfies a Validator alone), ADR-0033/0034 (labels derive by join; kernel-class facts only from the runtime), ADR-0035 (closed endorsement list), ADR-0047 (judge obligations); evidence-trust rules already practised in production — Codex Guardian `policy_template.md` "Evidence Handling" (S-WS-G3-11); standardized verdict event — OTel `gen_ai.evaluation.result` (S-WS-G3-15). The C0/C1 rules assert no performance number.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** `codex/codex-rs/core/src/guardian/{prompt,review_request,decision}.rs` and `codex-rs/core/assets/guardian/policy_template.md` @ 0735c51 (evidence items separated from the exact action JSON; trusted-content enumeration; truncation = omitted; fail-closed; assessment event with `review_reason`), `codex-rs/protocol/src/protocol.rs` L3405–3440 (`ReviewOutputEvent`/`ReviewFinding{confidence_score, priority, code_location}`), `codex-rs/protocol/src/approvals.rs` (`GuardianAssessmentEvent`, closed enums); `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/critic/{base,result}.py` and `critic/impl/{agent_finished,empty_patch}.py` @ 3fc7b22 (one contract for programmatic and model critics; `mode` selects the decision point); `browser-use/browser_use/agent/judge.py` @ 50f2055 (end-state evidence; reference precedence; `impossible_task`); `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/scorer/_model.py` @ 75f4891 (grader role; panels; unscored on parse failure); `harbor/src/harbor/analyze/analyzer.py` @ 7d5285b and `hal-harness/hal/utils/llm_log_analyzer.py` @ 16bb03e (judge model recorded per result; separate job); `semantic-conventions-genai/docs/gen-ai/gen-ai-events.md` @ 0c87594 (`gen_ai.evaluation.result`). **Negative precedents rejected:** Inspect grader defaulting to the evaluated model; OpenHands `critic_mixin.py` passing `git_patch = None` and reporting `score` as success; OTel event without judge identity or evidence. The kernel-built class-labelled evidence bundle, derived grounding, authority-by-determinism/label-by-evidence and gate/report separation are `no-precedent / novel` as an integrated contract.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) a single rubric judge over the whole report aligned best with humans (S-036) — the report and its sources are evidence items; the rule constrains citation, not scope; (ii) chain-of-thought monitoring is the most effective monitor (S-WS-G3-05, S-WS-G3-16) — reasoning is admitted as `claimed` evidence for veto/monitor use; only success certification requires `measured`/`reconciled`; (iii) requiring cited findings adds judge tokens — the alternative is undetectable hallucinated findings (S-WS-G3-06); (iv) one contract for programmatic and model critics may over-constrain cheap predicates — programmatic critics satisfy it trivially (bundle = ledger view; grounding = `measured`); (v) strong judges reach >80 % human agreement on chat preferences (S-WS-G3-01) — measured on a different object; agreement is what the calibration record measures. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** independent of model tier and task class for the schema; the usefulness of `measured` evidence assumes the Stage 3 suite exposes end-state and executable oracles (WS-I4); hosted rows supply what their observability level permits (`n/a{observability}` otherwise); agentic probes assume WS-B5/B4 snapshot or content-addressed evidence so the beneficiary cannot alter what the critic reads (OQ-WS-G3-03).
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C0 / Stage 1** — `CriticDeclaration` fields on the ratified `OracleDeclaration`/`Validator.judge`, closed `evidence_class` values, `EvidenceBundle`/`CriticVerdict` schemas, ledger payload extensions (typed completion of ADR-0047/CF-114 items, not new C0 scope; stage note on R-2.7.3); **C0 / Stage 3** — programmatic critics over the ledger and end state with WS-G1 (AC-G3-1/-2/-7/-10); **C1 / Stage 4** — runtime gate critics (completion gate; WS-H7 reviewer) with `control.critic.gated`; **C2 / Stage 4** — judge critics as `followed` detectors, agentic judges with probes, emulated pre-checks (R-2.7.3 proper); **C4 / Stage 6** — `JudgeLeakedIntoArtifact` in WS-I5's pipeline.

## Consequences

- WS-B1 adds the `verification.validator.verdict` payload extensions and the `control.critic.gated` class; WS-I1 lowers verdicts to `gen_ai.evaluation.result` with a loss report.
- WS-G1's validators are the preferred critics and the `reference_oracle` set; WS-G2's `verification.claim.reconciled` is the sole producer of `reconciled` evidence; a critic's verdict about a claim is not a reconciliation.
- WS-H7's automatic reviewer and WS-E2's emulated pre-check are `CriticDeclaration`s (`use = gate`; raise-only / veto-only); WS-D2/D3/D4/D5's judged detectors are `use = report` critics; WS-D3's `cited` signal is `detector = deterministic, evidence_class = claimed` (OQ-WS-D3-06).
- WS-I2/J4 carry `grounding`, `calibration_ref` and the independence summary on every judged value; WS-I5 inherits `held_out_from`/`JudgeLeakedIntoArtifact`; WS-F3 spawns critics with `role = judge` under delegation attenuation; WS-F4 consumes the cost fields.
- The ontology term *evidence class* gains its closed value set; *critic*, *evidence bundle*, *grounding*, *critic site/use* are added.

## Reversibility

**High for the schema, low for two rules.** Fields can be added and evidence modes extended by dialect bump; the verdict cache and stored bundles are content-addressed so historical verdicts can be re-rendered under new policies. Two rules are load-bearing and should be treated as constitutional beside ADR-0035's: a verdict never endorses or confers, and a gate verdict is never the reported success — relaxing either after Stage 4 re-admits reviewer-as-endorser and the METR/RHO reward-hacking path into stored results.
