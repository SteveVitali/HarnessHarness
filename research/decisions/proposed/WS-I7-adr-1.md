# ADR-NNNN — Attribution values come only from executed interventions: the estimand hierarchy keyed to replay-validity modes, the intervention/replay contract over ADR-0135, and the OQ-323 admissibility constants

**Status:** proposed
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I7 (with WS-B4 for the hook and seed derivation, WS-J3 for the experiment desugaring, WS-J4 for the interval kernel, WS-I2 for the paired protocol) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0028, ADR-0133, ADR-0134, ADR-0135, ADR-0041, ADR-0045, ADR-0046, ADR-0047, ADR-0154, ADR-0156, ADR-0157, ADR-0158, ADR-0161, ADR-0176 (Group W `counterfactual`), ADR-0017, ADR-0027/0048 (trace map), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-08/-09/-12/-14/-15); OQ-007, OQ-024, OQ-082, OQ-113, OQ-323, OQ-347, OQ-217; CF-WS-I7-02, CF-WS-I7-04, CF-WS-I7-05; R-2.9.7, R-2.2.4, R-2.9.2

## Context

ADR-0135 ratified the counterfactual hook: an `InterventionRecord`, a fork at or before `earliest_affected_seq`, `n ≥ k` factual re-executions beside `n ≥ k` counterfactual branches under one `MatchSpec`, `replay_mode` on every row, and left `k`, the statistic and the margin to WS-I7 (OQ-323). Doc 2 §10 asks whether trajectory interventions can support "reliable counterfactual attribution rather than post-hoc stories" and "where nondeterminism makes such attribution invalid". Two facts from the research fix the shape of the answer. First, the post-hoc story is measurably unreliable: on the only accepted benchmark of culprit attribution (Who&When, ICML 2025) the best model that *reads* a failure log reaches 14.2 % step-level accuracy, several methods below random, and the baseline code hands the model the whole log plus the answer and asks for a name (S-WS-I7-05/-06). Second, re-executing the suffix from a fork with fresh model samples estimates an *interventional* quantity E[Y | do(X = x′), prefix], not the unit-level counterfactual Y_{x′}(u) that fixes the exogenous noise (Pearl, S-WS-I7-15); this produces the *run-forward confound* — an inert early decision shows an effect by re-rolling the true pivot — proven on planted chains (S-WS-I7-01; S-WS-I7-03 Prop. 1), and the remedy (common random numbers, `TE_crn`) has its own degeneracy (Prop. 2). No audited harness records which of these it estimates.

## Options considered

1. **Admit model-authored diagnoses as attribution values (Who&When / AgentDebugX shape) with a confidence field.** *Rejected:* 14 % step accuracy, some methods below random; a confidence field on a correlational verdict is not an interval on an executed effect (ADR-0112's `model_claim` discipline).
2. **One estimand (run-forward re-execution) reported as "the counterfactual effect".** *Rejected:* conflates interventional with counterfactual claims; the run-forward confound makes magnitude ranking wrong on planted chains.
3. **Require deterministic replay + common random numbers for every attribution.** *Rejected:* excludes every non-declaring control-strategy variant (ADR-0028 option 1's objection) and provider sampling cannot be coupled today (no probed batch-invariance).
4. **A declared estimand per effect, admissibility keyed to ADR-0135's validity mode, model diagnoses admitted only as hypotheses, a typed component-target contract over the hook, and MUST-data admissibility constants (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Values only from executed interventions.** An attribution effect exists only when the kernel executed ≥ 1 factual and ≥ 1 counterfactual branch from the same coherent fork point and `EnvSnapshot` (ADR-0133 D2/D3; ADR-0135 D6). A model- or human-authored diagnosis is an `AttributionHypothesis{targets[], rationale: Text{authority ≤ delegate}, source_ref, provenance, label = observational}` — never an effect, never an input to `attribution_quality`.
2. **Estimand and validity on every effect.** `estimand ∈ {LOI, LOO, TE_marg, TE_crn, DE, ME, shapley}`; `validity_mode` is the minimum `ReplayValidityReport.mode` over the effect's branches. Floors: `LOI/LOO` — whole-run arms (ordinary cells; `re_executed` suffices); `TE_marg`, `shapley` — `re_executed` with coherent fork point and snapshot coupling; `TE_crn`, `DE`, `ME` — `deterministic` with `noise_coupling = crn` on every nondeterminism source consumed after the fork; `degraded` ⇒ report label `exploratory` only; `invalid` ⇒ `ReplayInvalid` refusal. The spec text for R-2.9.7 reads "interventional re-execution with declared estimands; unit-level counterfactuals only under deterministic replay with noise coupling" (CF-WS-I7-04).
3. **Typed component targets over ADR-0135's kinds; no new kernel intervention kind.** `ComponentTarget{kind ∈ {slot, variant, rule, parameter, procedure, text_leaf, decision_point}, ref: semantic_id | (run_id, seq, DecisionPoint), class_id?, ir_refs[]}`; `intervention_for(target, change)` yields an ADR-0135 `InterventionRecord` with `definition_diff` (slot ablation `SlotBinding.enabled = false`, variant swap, parameter, rule removal, procedure edit, opaque-leaf ablation), `profile_diff`, `response_substitution`, `observation_substitution`, `decision_override`, `budget_change`, `permission_change`; the factual arm is the null intervention (`do_resample`). A diff with `authority_delta = widening` or `budget_delta = loosening` is refused (ADR-0017; phase-3 §6.2). A shared inlined procedure resolves to one target through `derived-from` (OQ-217 requirement). Targets are by `semantic_id`; layer provenance is not consulted, so no `base` source kind is needed (OQ-347 answered).
4. **Fork policy is derived, not chosen.** `fork_policy ∈ {latest_coherent_before(earliest_affected_seq) (default), run_start (forced when coupling_assumption = declared_strong — the method degrades to designed ablation), every_decision_point{kind} (M2/M3 over a run's own decision points)}`; `earliest_affected_seq` comes from the events whose `ir_refs[]` intersect the diff's touched nodes (trace map, ADR-0048).
5. **Operations.** `component_targets(sealed_definition, filter?) → [ComponentTarget]`; `intervention_for(target, change) → InterventionRecord`; `attribution_design(subject, method, targets[], fork_policy, k, replay_mode_requested, noise_coupling, match: MatchSpec, outcome: MetricDeclaration ref, budget: instrument BudgetNode, seed, pre_registration_ref?) → AttributionDesign` (validates statically; desugars to an `ExperimentSpec` so every attribution run is `run_kind = experiment`, ADR-0154/0155); `attribute(design) → AttributionReport/1` through `hh-embed/1` Group W `counterfactual` and Group L `analyze`; branches `branch_kind = counterfactual`, `charged_to = instrument`; rows are overlay-run row versions (ADR-0161; phase-3 §6.1). Invariants: I1 factual + counterfactual from one fork point and snapshot; I2 estimand and validity on every effect; I3 `change_rate` and `noise_floor` present; I4 rollouts ≤ reservation (reserve-before-spend, ADR-0040) with truncation reported; I5 purity over `(design, watermark, seed)` (ADR-0157 D1). Failures: `InterventionPrecedesForkPoint`, `NoCoherentForkPoint`, `UnbudgetedArm`, `UnmatchedBudget`, `PolicyCollapsed(seq)`, `CouplingUnavailable(source)`, `ClassInadmissible`, `OutcomeOracleUndeclared`, `BudgetExceeded` (partial report), `ReplayInvalid(reason)`.
6. **OQ-323 constants (MUST-data placeholders; Stage-6 power re-check, OQ-WS-I7-01).** Statistic: binary outcomes — difference of proportions between the counterfactual and factual arm at one fork point with a Newcombe/Wilson-family interval, aggregated across fork points as a paired effect clustered by task (ADR-0158 D1/D3); `unit ∈ {tokens, money, ms}` — `bootstrap_paired` on per-fork-point differences with tail effects. `k` per arm per fork point: `re_executed` ⇒ **k ≥ 8**; `deterministic` without coupling ⇒ k ≥ 4; `deterministic` + `crn` on all consumed sources ⇒ k = 1 for the decision-sequence claim, k ≥ 4 for outcome claims. Margin: a target is *attributed* iff its interval excludes 0 **and** the factual arm's `change_rate > 0`; "does not matter" is TOST against the pre-registered `equivalence_margin` (ADR-0158 D7), never non-significance; the factual arm's dispersion is the `noise_floor`. OQ-113 (B4 half): R2 on counterfactual rows is a seed-set claim; same-seed only under `deterministic` + `crn`.
7. **Seed derivation under coupling (amends ADR-0135 D2 additively; CF-WS-I7-02).** When `noise_coupling = crn`, the factual and counterfactual branch of one replicate share `branch_seed = H(configuration_version_id.seed ∥ replicate_index ∥ fork_point)`; otherwise ADR-0135's `∥ branch_id` form stands. `coupled_sources[]` lists each source actually coupled with a measured `coupling_agreement`; `provider_sampling` joins only where the gateway declares and probes `seed_honoured` (CF-095; OQ-WS-I7-02).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-14 (one `MatchSpec` per design; instrument reservation; search vs eval budgets on the report), T-LCD-15 (`n/a{class | observability | not_run}` typed; hosted rows never 0), T-LCD-08 (`deterministic_replay` and `seed_honoured` are declarations consulted per variant/gateway; operations receive `(ModelProfile, ResourceAccount)`), T-LCD-09 (fork-point pairing feeds the `contrast` estimator), T-LCD-12 (operations/records; analysis out of process), T-LCD-10 (targets by `semantic_id`), T-LCD-02 (`text_leaf` targets). Not applicable: T-01/-03/-04/-05/-06/-07/-11/-13 (T-05/-13 are addressed by the companion ADRs). **ADR-0024 rung:** operations, invariants, refusals and the estimand floors are MUST-code; `k`, margins, `fork_policy` defaults are MUST-data. **Matched-budget conditionality:** every C4 claim here is a `ComparisonReport` under `MatchSpec` (ADR-0041/0046).

- **(b) Source-code precedent:** causal-agent-replay @db90fa2 `src/car/schemas/intervention.py` (kinds), `src/car/replay/forward.py` L118–176 (`coalition_forward`: held prefix = null intervention), `src/car/attribute/contrastive.py` L84–131 (per-step effect + locus), `src/car/attribute/effects.py` L87–110 (Wilson; bootstrap difference), `src/car/replay/deterministic.py` L95–120 (action-match rate measured); shepherd @d34d5ca `shepherd_core/foundation/protocols/scope.py` L82–120 (fork/merge/discard). ## Consequences

- WS-B4: ADR-0135 D2 gains the coupled seed derivation (additive); nothing else changes.
- WS-J3: `AttributionDesign` desugars to `ExperimentSpec` with fork points as task coordinates (ADR-0156's boundary-companion pattern generalized); no new design kind.
- WS-J4/J5: effects are analysed by the ratified kernel and stored as `AnalysisRecord`s; rows gain `arm_role`, `intervention_ref?` (CF-WS-I7-01).
- WS-I5: a candidate's failure hypothesis is an `AttributionHypothesis` or a report; the label gate is the companion ADR-3.
- WS-C1/C3: `seed_honoured` and batch-invariance become probed capabilities if provider-side coupling is wanted (OQ-WS-I7-02).
- WS-K2: renders estimand, validity mode and `n/a` reasons on every attribution strip (replay-mode badge extended).

## Reversibility

**High.** Estimands and floors are additive enum values; constants are MUST-data; dropping the hypothesis/value separation would be a documentation change that re-admits the 14 %-accuracy method and is not recommended; the seed-derivation amendment is opt-in (`crn`) and leaves the default untouched.
