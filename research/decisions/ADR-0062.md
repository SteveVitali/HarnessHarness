# ADR-0062 — Deterministic containment is the floor beneath the capability ceiling: enforcement points and their trust, `enforcement_evidence` and fail-closed (`ContainmentUnverified`), the escape hatch as an endorsed amendment, and the T-CON escape/exfiltration battery

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-H4 (consumers: WS-H1, WS-H7, WS-B5, WS-E5, WS-F3, WS-I2, WS-I4, WS-J3, WS-J5, WS-J6, WS-L1/K4/L5) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (no self-widening), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-04/-06/-07/-12/-14/-15), ADR-0031 (kernel decision; `kernel_assessed` precedence; session approval cache), ADR-0034/0035 (never-widen; `approval` endorses one effect; `delegate` never endorses; seven checks), ADR-0036/0037 (`configuration_version_id`; annotated-never-hidden), ADR-0040 (approvals exhaustion ⇒ `deny`), ADR-0045/0046 (class-scoped metrics; matched-budget comparison), ADR-0049/0050 (OQ-045 provisional; helper ecosystem hosts primitives in-thread); CF-134, CF-135, CF-138; OQ-045, OQ-086, OQ-101, OQ-157/-05/-06; R-2.8.4, R-2.8.1, R-2.8.7, R-2.2.5

## Context

Doc 2 §5.6: "Deterministic containment is the backstop"; "any probabilistic defense has a non-zero miss rate" (S-053: auto-mode ~0.4% benign blocks vs ~17% misses; ~93% of prompts approved). ADR-0035 already fixes that the model never endorses and that approval endorses one `effect_id`; ADR-0031 fixes `KernelDecision{allow | ask | deny}` and names "writable-root containment, network host policy" as deterministic inputs to `kernel_assessed`. What is missing is the rule that binds them: an effect that containment cannot admit must be refused *before* any classifier or human is consulted, a decision may rest only on enforcement the kernel has evidence for, and the escape hatch every product ships (Codex `sandboxing.rs` escalation; Claude Code `dangerouslyDisableSandbox`, disabled by `allowUnsandboxedCommands: false`) must be expressible without violating "delegate never endorses". Source evidence (dossier F9) confirms the helper is out-of-process in every audited enforcer, with a narrow protocol that reports the applied sandbox type and fails closed when managed-network details are missing (Codex `exec-server-protocol`), which answers WS-H4's part of OQ-045.

## Options considered

1. **Classifier-gated execution with containment as an optional hardening.** *Rejected:* S-053's miss rate; the miss is unbounded in blast radius without a floor.
2. **Human approval as the floor (ask on everything risky).** *Rejected:* ~93% approval, approval fatigue (S-053), and ADR-0040 already treats approvals as a scarce budget; approvals endorse one effect, not a boundary.
3. **Trust the helper's self-report ("sandboxed = true") without evidence.** *Rejected:* Codex's `sandbox_tags.rs` labels "must never be used to authorize"; no audited system probes its own boundary; a research instrument cannot key results on an unprobed factor.
4. **Forbid any run-time widening (no escape hatch).** *Rejected as the only mode:* products show it is needed for developer ergonomics; kept as the Lab *default* (`strict`) with an endorsed, ledgered, budgeted amendment path for interactive runs.
5. **Floor/ceiling precedence with evidence-gated fail-closed enforcement, an endorsed amendment operation, and an executable escape/exfiltration battery (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Enforcement points and trust.** EP1 machine boundary (`namespaces | user_space_kernel | microvm`; substrate: host kernel/hypervisor; evidence `attested | reported`); EP2 process sandbox helper (Seatbelt / bubblewrap + seccomp / Landlock / restricted token + WFP; substrate: OS kernel + helper identity; evidence: backend + lowering loss + `probed` self-tests); EP3 egress mediator (host side of EP1/EP2; decides `allow`/`deny` from policy, forwards `ask`); EP4 reference monitor (WS-H1). Classifiers, hooks, model self-report and human approval of *content* are inputs to Π (ADR-0035 decision 5), never enforcement points. The ADR-0050 helper ecosystem may host EP2 primitives in-thread *inside the helper* on the child's behalf; the helper itself is a separate process behind the narrow protocol (OQ-045 input: confirmed from H4's side; final answer WS-B5/E5).
2. **`ContainmentReport`** `{policy_version_id, backend, isolation_class, enforcement_evidence: map<field_group ∈ {fs, net, proc, resources}, attested | reported | probed | unknown>, lowering_loss: [LossItem{field, backend, reason}], probes: [ProbeResult{kind, expected, observed, evidence_kind}]}` is returned by the helper at attach and re-validated on resume (WS-B3). `unknown` is never coerced (T-LCD-07); `probed` requires the kernel-run self-test battery (write outside root fails; `connect()`/name resolution fail under `none`; protected metadata write fails; `ptrace` fails).
3. **Floor and ceiling.** `authorize(p)` (ADR-0035 checks 1–7) gains a precondition and an input: precondition — `enforcement_evidence ≠ unknown` for every field group `EffectClass(p).domain` touches (`fs_write → fs`; `net_egress → net`; `exec, spawn_process → proc`; `secret_access → fs (masked mounts) ∧ net (credential bindings)`), else `refused{ContainmentUnverified}`; input — `admits(effective_policy, p) → admitted | refused{reason} | amendable{diff}`. `allow` requires **both** `Permission` coverage (ceiling) and `admitted` (floor); `refused` yields `action.effect.refused{reason: containment}` without consulting Π or asking; `amendable` is routed to decision 5 only when `amendment.strict = false`. Containment feeds `kernel_assessed` (ADR-0031 decision 2): `scope = workspace_local` may be assessed only when the effect's paths ⊆ the handle's `fs.write.allow` roots and `net.mode = none` (or the effect is `read_only`); otherwise `external`. Permissions never loosen containment; containment never grants a Permission; a `net_egress` Permission on a `none` environment is a `seal`-time warning `PermissionUnreachable`. Child environments receive `effective(parent ⊓ child_layer)` (ADR-0035 check 7).
4. **Fail-closed.** If the helper is unavailable, the backend unsupported, the report stale, or evidence `unknown` for a required group, every non-`read_only` effect is refused and `security.containment.unverified` is appended. Lab runs (any run with a `Design`/`PreRegistration`, ADR-0045) are always fail-closed. Interactive runs may declare `on_unavailable = warn_and_degrade`, which stamps `containment_degraded = true` on every event of the run and caps reproducibility claims at R0 (ADR-0038 `InstrumentRecord.dirty` shape).
5. **The escape hatch is an amendment.** `amend(policy_version_id, diff: ContainmentDiff, basis ∈ amendment.allowed_bases, endorser: ProvenanceRecord, effect_id?) → version_id | ContainmentWidening | BasisNotAllowed | StrictMode`. Validity: `authority(endorser) ≥` the layer being loosened (`principal` for run-level; `definition` via `seal`; `policy_rule` for phase plans sealed in the experiment design — Harbor agent/verifier phases); an endorser with `origin ∈ {model, evolution, participant}` is never valid — a model-issued "disable sandbox"/"allow host" parameter is a *proposal* the kernel converts into `ask(effect_id)` (or ignores under `strict`, appending `StrictMode`). Every amendment is `security.containment.amended`, consumes one `approvals.requested` (exhaustion ⇒ `deny`, ADR-0040), produces a new `version_id` (so `configuration_version_id` changes), and marks the replicate `containment_amended = true` in results (annotated, never hidden — ADR-0037 style). Session caching of an approved canonical pattern is permitted (ADR-0031 decision 3/6) and ledgered as `decider = cache`. Default: `strict = true` for Lab runs; interactive runs may set `strict = false`.
6. **T-CON battery** (dossier §6.8, AC-H4-01…13) is the acceptance suite: write containment incl. protected metadata and symlink-before-validate; read deny and masked-credential anti-laundering; `none`-mode syscall probes; `mediated` decision-order fixtures incl. normalisation, non-public guard, ports/methods; approved-host residual with the C2 closer; attribution; escape hatch under strict/non-strict and budget exhaustion; fail-closed; layered-meet property test; lowering loss; matched-budget utility comparison (S-053's 84% as a pre-registered hypothesis); a SandboxEscapeBench-class suite against each shipped `isolation_class` with no added vulnerabilities (C1); hosted participants. Every fixture is `charged_to = instrument`; "pass" asserts the boundary, never model behaviour. Readiness refuses an `isolation_class` with an open capture or an unowned `residual_channels` entry.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-07 (`enforcement_evidence` declared/probed/unknown, never coerced), T-LCD-12 (operations; helper out-of-process), T-LCD-14 (amendments and egress asks are budgeted; AC-H4-11 matched), T-LCD-15 (`n/a` for hosted attribution; instrument charging), T-LCD-06 (hosted participants reuse `external` class and `participant` origin; no IR change), T-LCD-04 (the floor is identical across targets). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-05 (no conditioned rule here; `residual_channels` carry the T-LCD-05 shape in ADR-0060)/-08/-09/-10/-11/-13. **ADR-0024 ladder rung:** MUST-code (enforcers, `authorize` precondition, `amend`), with `strict`, `allowed_bases`, `on_unavailable` as MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: probabilistic gates have non-zero miss rates; ~93% approvals; "design for containment at the environment layer first" — S-053 (Tier B); the model can reason about permission but cannot grant it; only the runtime confers authority — doc 2 §5.6, ADR-0035 (ratified); exhausted approvals become `deny` — ADR-0040 (ratified); the boundary class is chosen per threat model (gVisor server-side, VM for non-developers, OS sandbox for developers) — S-053; machine boundaries do not filter egress and can be misconfigured — S-294 (Firecracker), S-296 (`provisional (mech)`); Landlock is best-effort and cannot restrict some operations (lowering loss is real) — S-293 (Tier A); production strict mode exists and ignores the model's parameter — S-289; per-phase policy switching in a lab — S-132 `trial/network_policy.py`, `environments/base.py` `set_network_policy`.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `codex/codex-rs/core/src/tools/sandboxing.rs` L195–345 (`unsandboxed_execution_allowed`, `escalate_on_failure`, `wants_no_sandbox_approval` keyed on `AskForApproval`), `core/src/sandbox_tags.rs` L1–20 ("must never be used to authorize"), `sandboxing/src/manager.rs` L44–70 (`SandboxType`), `policy_transforms.rs` L646–666 (`should_require_platform_sandbox`: managed network ⇒ sandbox required), `denial.rs`/`violation.rs` (heuristic denial detection labelled as such; `SIGSYS`), `exec-server-protocol/src/protocol.rs` L96–160 (`EnvironmentInfo`, `EnvironmentCapabilities`), L283–362 (`ExecParams` portable sandbox intent; `enforce_managed_network` "must continue to fail closed"; `ExecResponse.sandbox_type: ProcessSandboxType`), `linux-sandbox/src/landlock.rs` L36–100 (primitives applied in the helper's thread for the child); `sandbox-runtime/README.md` + `src/sandbox/sandbox-manager.ts` (wrapper process; `SandboxAskCallback`); Claude Code docs (`failIfUnavailable`, `allowUnsandboxedCommands: false`, `Bash(dangerouslyDisableSandbox:true)` ask rule); `harbor/src/harbor/environments/base.py` L846–858. The evidence-gated `authorize` precondition, `ContainmentUnverified`, and the amendment operation with endorser-class validity and results annotation are `no-precedent / novel` as a contract.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: fail-closed hurts ergonomics (products default to warn-and-degrade) — does not overturn: Lab correctness requires it; interactive degradation is admitted and stamped. Requiring approval for the hatch re-introduces prompts containment was meant to remove — does not overturn: the hatch is the exception path; `containment.amendments_per_run` measures policy quality. Probing adds attach latency — bounded and `instrument`-charged; recorded as `env.reserved_ms`. Escape benches measure models, not boundaries (S-296) — used here only as a configuration test with no injected vulnerabilities. "Custom code is weakest" — the amendment path is small and fully ledgered. APPA's recoverable IFC (S-258, `provisional`) suggests hard refusal over-blocks — belongs to WS-H2's C2 design; the floor here refuses only what the *boundary* cannot admit, which no flow policy could have allowed either.
- (d) Conditionality: model- and task-independent. Assumes a helper protocol (WS-B5/E5; WS-L1 §6.5 boundary contract) that can carry the policy and report; assumes at least one backend with `probed` evidence at Stage 1; the `microvm`/`user_space_kernel` classes and `attested` evidence assume WS-H5/H6 attestation at C1. Unattended runs must configure `default_unmatched = deny` and `strict = true` (no `ask` is resolvable).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — EP2 backend with probes, `ContainmentReport`, fail-closed rule, `authorize` precondition in WS-H1, AC-H4-08/-10 static; **C0 / Stage 2** — `amend()`, strict/non-strict, approval cache, results annotation, AC-H4-07; **C0 / Stage 3** — T-CON executable, AC-H4-11 matched comparison, metrics; **C1 / Stage 4** — EP1 classes with attestation, phase plans, hosted participants, AC-H4-12/-13.

## Consequences

- WS-H1's kernel exposes `authorize(p)` with the containment precondition and `admits()` input; Π (OQ-101) is consulted only for admitted proposals.
- WS-B5/E5 implement the helper protocol fields (`ContainmentPolicy` in, `ContainmentReport` out, `egress/decision_request` round trip) and the attach-time probe battery; WS-B3 re-validates the report on resume.
- WS-H7 designs hatch/egress `ask` UX from the strict-by-default floor; WS-J5 stores `containment_amended`; WS-J3 refuses to aggregate amended and unamended replicates as one `configuration_version_id`.
- WS-I4 ships environment classes with the escape suite green; WS-J6 maps hosted mechanisms to `external` + reported evidence.
- OQ-045: H4 recommends the out-of-process helper; WS-B5/E5 give the final answer and trigger the ADR-0050 revalidation only if they move it in-process.

## Reversibility

**Low.** The three rules — containment admits before anyone asks, decisions rest only on evidenced enforcement, the model never amends — are constitutional in the sense of ADR-0035: removing any of them after Stage 2 silently reopens the classifier-miss, unprobed-boundary and self-widening paths (ADR-0002 iii). Backends, evidence kinds, probes and battery fixtures are freely extensible.
