# ADR-0157 — The analysis engine as a pure, versioned, class-aware function over results rows: `AnalysisSpec`/`AnalysisReport` contract, operation catalogue A1–A16, provenance and reproducibility, confirmatory/exploratory labelling with multiplicity control, outcome-class sensitivity bounds

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 3 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-J4 (with WS-J5 storage, WS-K2 rendering, WS-J3 design inputs, WS-I2 declarations) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0045 (rows, `MetricDeclaration`, `PreRegistration`), ADR-0046 (`ComparisonReport`, benefit kinds), ADR-0047 (oracle classes), ADR-0012 (§4g reports "computed once, persisted with provenance"), ADR-0013 (admissible granularities), ADR-0026 (`project` purity), ADR-0037 (annotated-never-hidden), ADR-0041 (`MatchSpec` refusal), ADR-0043 (cost provenance), ADR-0050 D2 (what crosses the kernel↔lab boundary), ADR-0141 (row↔bundle annotations), ADR-0143 (`StrataPooledUnannotated`), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-09/-14/-15); CF-338, CF-340; OQ-016, OQ-366, OQ-369; R-2.10.4, R-2.10.5, R-2.11.2

## Context

ADR-0045/0046 fix *what* the scorecard and every comparison must report and leave "the statistical model forms … how estimated" to WS-J4. Every audited evaluation plane (Inspect `evals_df`, lm-eval-harness, Harbor, HAL, AgentDojo, τ-bench) computes at most a mean and a standard error per configuration; none pairs arms by task, none reports an interaction, none persists an analysis with the row watermark it was computed from, and none distinguishes pre-registered from exploratory cells (WS-J4 §4a F1, §5 negative results). The scorecard registers ≈ 50 metrics, so an unlabelled all-metrics comparison expects 2–3 spurious "significant" cells under the null (F8). Outcome classes are excluded from denominators (ADR-0045 decision 3) but nothing shows whether the exclusion could flip a conclusion (F9). Reports must reach K2 dashboards and J5's experiment ledger without either recomputing them.

## Options considered

1. **Ad hoc notebooks over the results store** (the HAL/Harbor practice). Rejected: unreproducible, unlabelled, no class enforcement; contradicts ontology §4g (reports are persisted with provenance) and AC-I2-12.
2. **Analyses as materialized ledger projections** (extend `project(run, view_kind)`). Rejected: an analysis spans many runs and depends on registry/pricing versions and a resampling seed; it is a report, not a view (ADR-0012 §4g distinguishes the two).
3. **A pure, versioned `analyze(AnalysisSpec, rows@watermark) → AnalysisReport` with a closed operation catalogue, declared estimators, mandatory labels and provenance (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Contract.** `analyze(spec: AnalysisSpec, rows: ResultsQuery{watermark}) → AnalysisReport` is pure: identical `(spec, row watermark, run-id set, metric_registry_version, price_table_version, seed, draws)` yield an identical `report_id`. Reports are stored by WS-J5's experiment ledger with a `ProvenanceRecord{origin = instrument}` and derived (non-hashed) `bundle_ref` annotations (ADR-0141 pattern); they are never silently recomputed, never edited, and superseded only by a new report that `derived-from`s the old one.
2. **Operation catalogue (closed; growth by dialect bump).** A1 `summarize`, A2 `compare`, A3 `interaction`, A4 `frontier`, A5 `transfer`, A6 `benefit_decomposition`, A7 `fit_surface`, A8 `equivalence`, A9 `rank`, A10 `attribution`, A11 `reliability_profile`, A12 `multiplicity`, A13 `power`, A14 `strata_view`, A15 `diagnostics`, A16 `render`/`diff_reports` — inputs/outputs/invariants per WS-J4 dossier §6.2.
3. **Class and granularity enforcement.** Every operation checks `admissible_granularities` (ADR-0013) and `MetricDeclaration.applies_to_classes`/`requires_observability` per row: inadmissible ⇒ `n/a{class | observability}` cells (never 0, never a proxy); a factor outside a row's admissible set ⇒ `InadmissibleFactor` (refusal); hosted rows are stratified by `capability_vector` and cost `confidence`; pooling across contamination strata without annotation ⇒ `StrataPooledUnannotated` (ADR-0143).
4. **Budget preconditions re-checked at report time.** The engine refuses to *report* a comparison whose arms fail ADR-0046's precondition or ADR-0041's `MatchSpec` checks (`UnmatchedBudget`, `IncommensurableMatch`, `MissingMatchSpec`); `budget_match.status = imbalanced` is reported with the cost-adjusted (frontier) view attached (T-LCD-14).
5. **Labels.** Every report and cell carries `label ∈ {confirmatory, exploratory, preview}`. `confirmatory` requires a `pre_registration_ref` whose `primary_metrics` and comparisons match the spec (`PreRegistrationMismatch` otherwise — the report may be re-run as `exploratory`). A12 applies Holm across the pre-registered primaries and Benjamini–Hochberg q-values across all other cells; nothing is suppressed. `preview` reports (reduced `draws`) are never persisted as confirmatory.
6. **Outcome-class sensitivity.** Every A2/A3 cell carries `outcome_bounds{lower, upper, verdict ∈ {robust, sensitive}}`: the effect recomputed with every excluded (`infrastructure_failure`, `oracle_failure`, `cancelled`) run of one arm set to failure and of the other to success, and vice versa (Manski bounds); `sensitive` is a report state, never a refusal, and names the cells J3 must re-run.
7. **Estimator declaration.** Every interval carries `EstimatorSelection{declared, floors, fallback_chain, substituted?}`; substitutions are labelled, never silent (details in ADR-0158). Resampling uses one `ResamplePlan{levels, draws, seed, indices_ref}` per report, shared by all metrics; `draws` and `seed` are declared and recorded, never hard-coded.
8. **Placement and consumers.** The engine is a lab-side consumer of the ADR-0050 D2 items (iv)–(vi) (`MetricDeclaration`/`MetricValue`/`ComparisonReport`/`MatchSpec`, budget and cost records, `project()` results with watermark); nothing else crosses. WS-J5 stores reports and consumes `RankReport`; WS-K2 renders from `RenderSpec{report_ref}` and never recomputes; WS-J3 consumes `PowerReport`/`diagnostics`; WS-I5/I7/F4/C3/I6 consume the rows named in the dossier §6.9.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-09 (A3 executes the canned interaction analysis over the explicit factor tuple; per-configuration distributions in every summary), T-LCD-14 (report-time refusal of unmatched arms; three benefit rows computed by A6), T-LCD-15 (class/observability enforcement per cell; `n/a{reason}` typed), T-LCD-07 (capability-vector strata; `unknown` never coerced), T-LCD-12 (operations/records; implementable out-of-process on the lab side). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03 (A8 supplies the operation T-LCD-03 uses; the anchor is WS-A3/I4), -04, -05 (surface expiry is ADR-0160), -06, -08, -10, -11, -13. **ADR-0024 rung:** the operation catalogue, `analyze` purity, class enforcement, labelling and bounds are MUST-code; floors, draws, priors, tolerances and the pre-registration content are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: analysis planes stop at mean ± SE without pairing/interaction/frontier/rank (WS-J4 F1: S-136, S-132, S-134, S-135 Tier A, S-519); the field's demand is interaction effects, not single-axis leaderboards (doc 2 §1, §8, §10 — Tier-B synthesis; S-167 Tier A); multiplicity under ≈ 50 metrics is arithmetic (S-518/-11, Tier A); outcome-class exclusion can flip conclusions and worst-case bounds are the standard honest device (S-522, Tier A; ADR-0045 decision 3); reports must be persisted with provenance (ADR-0012 §4g, ratified); the S-072 interaction (`provisional`) is exactly the class of finding the engine must be able to state with an interval — it contributes no constant.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/analysis/_dataframe/evals/columns.py` L80–148 @ 75f4891 (an evals dataframe keyed by task × model × configuration with one headline value + stderr — the shape J4 generalizes); `lm-evaluation-harness/lm_eval/api/metrics.py` L589–612 @ ad8737ae (`stderr_for_metric`: a hidden estimator-selection rule, made declarative here); `METR/eval-analysis-public/src/horizon/wrangle/bootstrap.py` L70–160 @ 52cb829 (one resample plan over nested categories reused across fits); `lmarena/arena-hard-auto/show_result.py` L99–122 @ 196f6b8 (bootstrap quantiles as the leaderboard); `hal-harness/reliability_eval/metrics/robustness.py` L60–80 @ 16bb03e (hard-coded seed/draws — the anti-pattern decision 7 forbids). **`no-precedent / novel`:** a pure `analyze` with report-level provenance and watermark; confirmatory/exploratory labelling with pre-registration matching; outcome-class sensitivity bounds; class-enforced analysis cells.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) purity and bootstrap cost make the lab loop slow — answered by `preview` reports, shared resample plans and closed-form intervals where valid (WS-J4 §5); (ii) Holm makes secondary findings unreportable — answered: Holm only on primaries, BH q-values + `exploratory` label elsewhere, nothing hidden; (iii) Manski bounds are extremely conservative — accepted; `sensitive` is a state that tells J3 what to re-run, not a refusal; (iv) a closed catalogue blocks novel analyses — answered: `exploratory` reports may use `kind = custom{spec_ref}` at C2 with no gate standing, and catalogue growth is a dialect bump; (v) HAL's leaderboard reports no intervals and is accepted by the community (S-135) — that is the negative result the decision corrects, not evidence against it. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier and task class; assumes results rows carry per-task (c, n), outcome class, cost provenance and class/observability stamps (ADR-0045/0043 — ratified); assumes J5 exposes rows by watermark (ADR-0026 `project` — ratified; J5's query API is CF-340); label enforcement assumes `PreRegistration` exists for confirmatory use (ADR-0045).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — `AnalysisSpec`/`AnalysisReport`/`Cell`/`EstimatorSelection`/`ResamplePlan` schemas; `outcome_bounds` and `multiplicity` fields on `ComparisonReport`. **C0 / Stage 3 (evaluation-first; stage note on R-2.10.4)** — A1, A2, A3 `contrast`, A8, A12 and KA-1/-2/-5/-8/-9/-12/-13: the estimator kernel ADR-0045/0046's AC-I2-1…12 need. **C1 / Stage 3–4** — A4/A5/A6/A9/A11/A13/A15 with J3/J5. **C2 / Stage 4–5** — A7, A10, A14, `factorial_glmm`, hosted strata (J6), K2 views. **C4 / Stage 6** — gate rows consumed by I5/I7/F4.

## Consequences

- WS-J5 stores `AnalysisReport`s in the experiment ledger with derived bundle annotations and renders the leaderboard from `RankReport` (sets, not a total order — OQ-369); WS-K2 renders from report refs only.
- WS-J3 must emit `PreRegistration` before the first run and consumes `PowerReport`/`diagnostics`; re-runs the cells named by `sensitive` bounds.
- WS-I2's `render_scorecard`, `compare`, `artifact_benefit`, `search_time_benefit`, `transfer`, `equivalence_run` are realized by A1/A2/A6/A5/A8 respectively; ADR-0046's `ComparisonReport` gains `test`, `sign_profile`, `tail_effects`, `outcome_bounds`, `multiplicity` (additive).
- The readiness glossary rule "every X beats Y is a `ComparisonReport`" gains "with a `label`"; unlabelled or `preview` claims fail the check.
- CF-338: `pareto_position` moves from a configuration-level metric to an A4 report field.

## Reversibility

**High for the schema, medium for stored reports.** Adding operations or fields is additive (dialect bump). Removing purity or labels would silently re-admit unreproducible and unlabelled claims into the ledger — the cost the decision exists to avoid; reports already stored keep their provenance regardless.
