# ADR-0006 — Differentiation thesis and non-goals for the Canonical Spec (§1)

**Status:** ratified (amended at Phase 0 synthesis, 2026-09-09 — see *Amendment log*)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-L7 (with WS-A1 concurrence) · **Phase:** 0 · **Related:** ADR-0001, ADR-0002; CF-009, CF-014, CF-015; OQ-035; R-2.12.5; RK-02, RK-05

## Context

Doc 3 §1.1 restates the sponsor's brief as a comparison-first research instrument and warns (with doc 2 §5.11/§12) that "most generic" is the lowest-common-denominator trap. Doc 3 §3.4 confines novelty claims to (i) the comparison-first ontology + IR, (ii) the assembly/experiment lab, (iii) the doc 2 §12 mechanisms. The landscape audited by WS-L7 shows every *component* already exists somewhere — batteries-included harnesses (Microsoft S-059, AWS S-058, Cloudflare S-056, LangChain/deepagents S-045), hosting (Omnigent S-118), opaque-participant leaderboards (HAL S-135; Inspect S-137; Harness-Bench S-062), partial behavioural representations (DSPy S-144, AgentSquare S-024, HTIR S-146, LangGraph S-143), and the source-code study's finding that no mature harness imports a general framework (S-074). The spec needs a thesis that survives this audit and an explicit non-goals list so downstream workstreams do not silently re-widen scope into "a framework".

## Options considered

1. **"Most generic, modular, extensible meta-harness framework" (literal brief).** Rejected: it is the LCD trap by construction (doc 3 §1.1); adoption evidence is against frameworks for core loops (S-074); the name "meta-harness" is already taken by three projects with different meanings.
2. **Pure evaluation platform (HAL/Inspect-class) with a bring-your-own harness.** Rejected: already exists and accepted at ICLR 2026 (S-135); treats participants as opaque; cannot vary one component, attribute credit, or express two harnesses in one representation — forfeits the doc 2 §12 mechanisms and ADR-0001's white-box core.
3. **Instrument + reference runtime with a narrowed, testable novelty claim (chosen).** Thesis: any harness expressed in the behavioural Harness IR can be assembled from swappable variants, compiled per model profile and target without loss of model-specific behaviour, executed, and compared against any other participant as an explicit configuration under matched budget. Novelty is claimed only where the audit found no precedent — the seven-plane provenance-bearing IR with profile-compiled lowering, and a lab whose participants can be white-box — and is made falsifiable by the LCD-trap battery (companion ADR).

## Decision

1. Spec §1 adopts the WS-L7 §6.1 thesis paragraph verbatim: *MetaHarness is a research instrument and reference runtime for harness engineering, not another agent framework* — the harness is the decomposable, attributable, evolvable experimental variable; the instrument is what is new.
2. Novelty claims in the spec are confined to three narrowed statements: (a) first IR spanning all seven planes with provenance/authority on every entity and profile-compiled lowering, designed for comparison; (b) a lab that varies one component under matched budget across native participants and admits hosted participants on the same comparison plane without weakening native contracts; (c) the doc 2 §12 mechanisms as *integrated contracts with provenance and expiry*, each cited against its partial prior art. Any ADR using the word "novel" must name the prior art it is novel relative to.
3. Spec §1 carries the **thirteen canonical non-goals N1–N13** (WS-L7 §6.2 N1–N12 plus WS-A1 §6.1's fifth; CF-019) — the only non-goals list in the spec, referenced by ADR-0003: **N1** not a general-purpose framework for others' core loops; **N2** not a universal Harness ABI; **N3** not a hosted collaboration platform / managed multi-device hosting product; **N4** not a public leaderboard service — hosting exists for joint native/hosted science and interoperation with existing planes (ADR-0005); **N5** not a new tool/agent/client protocol; **N6** not behavioural interchangeability of models; **N7** not a claim that generic beats bespoke; **N8** not autonomous self-deployment of harness changes; **N9** not a model-training stack; **N10** not an IDE, chat product or end-user assistant surface; **N11** not merely an evaluation harness in the benchmark-runner sense; **N12** not a language/runtime/framework commitment before WS-L1; **N13** not a benchmark author — environments/benchmarks are integrated (WS-I4), not invented.
4. The thesis is falsifiable on the program's own terms: if the reference harness cannot round-trip through the IR (T-LCD-03) or one definition cannot compile to two profiles × two targets with semantics preserved (T-LCD-04), the IR is an LCD wrapper and the thesis fails. These tests are scheduled at Stage 3, before profiles (Stage 5) and evolution (Stage 6).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** harness is a policy intervention with model-conditioned effects — S-062, S-072, S-088, S-148 (all `provisional`, independently corroborating one mechanism) plus Tier-B production reports S-049 (model-specific edit tools), S-047 (dead-weight guardrails). LCD mechanism and antidote — S-152 (LSP), S-035 (ACP), S-153 (MCP `_meta`), S-154 (AWT/WORA). Adoption reality — S-074 (`provisional (mech)`).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** the *positioning* has partial precedents that define its boundary: HAL `run(input) → dict` opaque-participant runner (S-135); Inspect agent bridge (S-137); Omnigent `omnigent/harness_capabilities.py` declared/probed hosting (S-118); DSPy `dspy/signatures/signature.py` + `dspy/adapters/base.py` (S-144) for semantic-interface + per-model rendering on one plane. The combination — white-box decomposable participants + hosted participants on one comparison plane over a seven-plane IR — is `no-precedent / novel` (bounded audit; **confirmed by WS-A1's independent audit, ADR-0004**: the two-class *plane* has precedent in Harbor S-132, HAL S-134/S-135, Inspect S-136, Omnigent S-118; the component-decomposable white-box axis with attribution, per-metric class declarations and declared/observed conformance do not).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** HAL/Inspect/Harness-Bench already compare agents (does not overturn: opaque participants, no component variation); DSPy/AgentSquare/HTIR/PRISM/LangGraph already represent parts of a harness, and Open Agent Spec (S-138/S-139, added by WS-A1) is a versioned declarative agent IR with six runtime adapters and an evaluation package (does not overturn: single-plane or agent/tool/flow/LLM-config only, no Permission/Effect/Validator/Budget/Memory-validity/HarnessRule entities, no provenance, no profile compilation; claim narrowed accordingly — CF-011, CF-015); S-074 says mature teams hand-roll loops (does not overturn: non-goal N1 — those teams are the instrument's users, and their prose→configuration migration moves toward the IR); "The Last Harness You'll Ever Build" S-147 claims a universal meta-evolution blueprint (does not overturn: `provisional`, contradicted at matched budget by S-083/S-075/S-091, and would itself need this instrument to prove its claim).
- **(d) Conditionality:** holds for any model tier and task class; assumes models continue to exhibit family-specific tool/prompt conditioning (currently true in codex `models.json`, OpenHands `ModelSpecificSection`, AutoGen `ModelInfo`, Cursor S-049). If conditioning converges, the profile tier can be descoped by ADR without touching the thesis's comparison claim. No `provisional` performance number is load-bearing.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** program-tier (Spec §1); the falsification tests land at C0 / Stage 3 (evaluation-first).

## Consequences

- WS-A1's competitive audit absorbed the prior-art list in WS-L7 §5, confirmed the narrowed `novel` flag in (b) (ADR-0004), and added Open Agent Spec; novelty statements 2a/2b stand as written. **Conditional (CF-011):** if WS-A3 does not deliver the seven-plane entity set with provenance on every entity, or WS-C3/E2 do not deliver testable, expirable profiles, the corresponding claim reverts to "renamed" — Phase 1/2 synthesis re-checks.
- Every downstream ADR that introduces a "generic" abstraction must cite which T-LCD tests it satisfies (companion ADR).
- The scope register gains no items; CF-014 is resolved by ADR-0010: R-2.10.6 (hosting) moves from Could to **Should** (tier C2, secondary per ADR-0001), and Spec §1 states that cross-participant comparison is delivered in two steps (native-vs-native at Stage 3; hosted participants at Stage 4).
- Marketing/OSS materials (WS-L6) must present MetaHarness as instrument + reference runtime; the "framework" word is reserved for what others build on top.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** The thesis paragraph and non-goals are text and can be amended by a later ADR at low cost; but the *architectural commitments they license* (white-box core, IR-first, comparison plane) are ADR-0001's and are expensive to reverse once the IR schema and lab data model are built. Reversing non-goal N1 (becoming a general framework) would re-open the LCD trap and is the one reversal this ADR is designed to make visible and deliberate.
