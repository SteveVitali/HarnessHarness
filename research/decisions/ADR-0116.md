# ADR-0116 — Critic independence and capability isolation: a six-axis `IndependenceVector` with per-use minimums; critics run as attenuated read-only processes with their own budgets under declared evidence-admission modes; gate and reporting critics are separated (`held_out_from`, `JudgeLeakedIntoArtifact`); untrusted-monitor protocols deferred to C4

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-G3 (consumers: WS-H1, WS-H7, WS-F3, WS-I5, WS-I2, WS-J4, WS-D4, WS-E2, WS-C3, WS-J6, WS-I6) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0115 (contract), ADR-0047 (`JudgeNotIndependent`; (c)(iii) same-family allowance), ADR-0016 (delegation attenuates: child `Permission ⊆` parent, `Budget ≤` parent), ADR-0033/0034 (R-TEXT; P2 delegation; P3 context label; RP), ADR-0035 (closed endorsement basis; the model never endorses; check 7), ADR-0040 (`BudgetNode` slice), ADR-0046 (search vs artifact; `LeakedSplit`), ADR-0012 (model set with roles; OQ-048), ADR-0002 (iii: no self-widening); ADR-0069 (reviewer chain; non-human stages only narrow), ADR-0053 (judges feed Π upward only), ADR-0091 (emulated pre-check veto-only); CF-245, CF-246; OQ-053, OQ-123, OQ-280, OQ-281; R-2.7.3, R-2.8.7, R-2.9.5

## Context

ADR-0047 requires a judge's snapshot to differ from every beneficiary snapshot and permits a different snapshot of the same family "with the weaker independence recorded on the declaration". Snapshot inequality is one axis of independence; the evidence names five more. *Context:* a judge that reads the beneficiary's transcript inherits its framing — intrinsic self-correction degrades reasoning (Huang et al., Tier A) and Anthropic's separate QA agent still "talk[ed] itself into" approving mediocre work until its prompt was tuned against divergent cases (S-046). *Capability:* METR observed frontier models editing tests, scoring functions and evaluators and monkey-patching graders (S-439); Codex Guardian therefore runs its reviewer with permissions intersected with read-only, MCP servers cleared, memories/skills/hooks/plugins/web search disabled, and its own token budgets (`reviewer_config.rs`). *Provenance:* a rubric authored by the model under test is `delegate` content defining its own success (ADR-0033 R-TEXT). *Optimization:* a verdict that drives retry-until-pass (OpenHands `IterativeRefinementConfig`), candidate acceptance (RHO self-preference, doc 2 §5.8) or a training reward (Baker et al.) becomes a reward and is optimized against, producing obfuscated reward hacking under pressure. *Adversarial:* AI Control (Tier A) shows that safety techniques evaluated without an intentionally subverting policy overstate their safety, and that trusted (weaker) monitoring plus a limited audit budget survives while untrusted monitoring needs anti-collusion measures. The concurrent WS-H7 draft strips all tool results from its reviewer; the Guardian precedent it cites admits them as untrusted evidence and probes read-only to judge exfiltration payloads — so admission must be a declared mode, not a fixed rule (CF-245). Inspect's grader defaults to the evaluated model (CF-246).

## Options considered

1. **Snapshot inequality only** (ADR-0047 as ratified). *Rejected as sufficient:* leaves context, capability and optimization axes open; the documented failures occur on those axes with distinct snapshots.
2. **Always a different model family.** *Rejected:* ADR-0047 (c)(iii) already found this impossible when one frontier family is available; the requirement would render most judged metrics `n/a`.
3. **Full isolation for every critic** (fresh context, read-only, no probes, held-out). *Rejected:* forbids agentic judges (workspace probes are what makes Agent-as-a-Judge work) and the completion critic (which is in-loop by definition); the right minimum depends on use.
4. **Prompt-level independence** (tell the judge to be independent; strip content in the prompt). *Rejected:* independence encoded in a `Text` leaf is unmeasurable and unenforceable; the reviewer's context is assembled by the kernel and the facts about it belong in a record.
5. **A six-axis declared vector with per-use minimums enforced at declaration and at consumption; isolation as attenuated `AgentProcess` permissions and budgets; evidence admission as a declared mode; gate/report separation with a held-out closure; untrusted-monitor protocols deferred (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`IndependenceVector = {snapshot ∈ {different_family, different_snapshot_same_family, same_snapshot}, context ∈ {fresh, shared_transcript, same_context}, capability ∈ {isolated_readonly, shared_readonly, shared_mutable}, provenance ∈ {sealed_rubric, lab_rubric, model_authored_rubric}, optimization ∈ {held_out, in_loop_bounded, in_loop_unbounded}, adversarial ∈ {trusted_weaker, untrusted_monitored, untrusted_unmonitored}}`** is a mandatory field of every `CriticDeclaration`; every `CriticVerdict` carries an `independence_summary` derived from it so a consumer can stratify (T-LCD-15 pattern).
2. **Per-use minimums** (refused at `declare_critic` with `IndependenceBelowMinimum(use)` and re-checked at `consume`): exploratory `report` — any, recorded; judged metric or `followed` detector — `snapshot ≠ same_snapshot ∧ context = fresh ∧ provenance ≠ model_authored_rubric ∧ capability ≠ shared_mutable ∧ calibration active`; `gate` at `stop`/`verify` — the above `∧ optimization = in_loop_bounded (max_iterations, budget_share_cap) ∧ grounding ∈ {measured, reconciled}`; `gate` at `authorize` — the above `∧ capability = isolated_readonly ∧ admission_mode ∈ {principal_only (default), quarantined_external} ∧ fail-closed ∧ raise-only or via a sealed policy_rule` (WS-H7/H1 unchanged); `gate` in evolution — `optimization = held_out` for the artifact-benefit critic and `held_out_from` naming every in-loop critic; `monitor` — `adversarial ∈ {trusted_weaker, untrusted_monitored}`, effect veto-only.
3. **Isolation is enforced by the kernel, not the prompt.** A critic runs as its own `AgentProcess` reached by a `delegated-to` edge with `role = judge` (ADR-0012 model-set roles): `permissions ⊆ parent ∩ read-only`; `net_egress = ∅` unless declared and attested (WS-H5); `memory_write` only at `scope = run` in the critic's namespace; no `permission_request`, `spawn_process` (except declared panel members), or `spend` domains; its own `BudgetNode{mode = slice}` (ADR-0040) with `budget_share_cap`; its context label computed from its bundle (ADR-0034 P3) so its probes are gated like any proposal. `same_context` critics are not critics (they are `HarnessRule`s) and never produce verdicts.
4. **Evidence admission is a declared mode** enforced by `build_evidence`: `principal_only` (items with `authority ≥ principal` plus kernel-rendered facts; WS-H7's reviewer default), `quarantined_external` (adds `environment`/`external`/`unverified` items rendered at ≤ `role_map(authority)` with their labels; the verdict's `taint` and authority follow the join — Codex Guardian's actual behaviour), `all_labelled` (instrument critics; everything the observability level permits, labelled). A verdict from `quarantined_external` may only *raise* into Π (ADR-0053); omitted items are always listed (`OmissionRecord`), and a critic that needed an omitted item returns `inconclusive{missing_evidence}` rather than `pass` (Codex "do not assume the missing content was benign").
5. **Gate/report separation.** A `use = gate` critic's verdict is never the reported success of the run or arm; inside an evolution arm its calls are `search_budget.judge_calls` (ADR-0046); the arm's `artifact_benefit`/`transfer` rows must be produced by a critic outside the transitive `held_out_from` closure of every gate critic used in the search, or by a deterministic oracle — `JudgeLeakedIntoArtifact` otherwise. Bounded in-loop use records every iteration (`control.critic.gated`) so the Lab can attribute the critic's effect (`critic_experiment`).
6. **Adversarial independence** at C1/C2 is `trusted_weaker` (a monitor from a different or weaker snapshot, calibrated) or `untrusted_monitored`; `untrusted_unmonitored` is admissible only for exploratory reporting. Anti-collusion protocols (paraphrasing, honeypots, audit budgets — AI Control) are deferred to C4 with WS-I5/H1 (OQ-280); until then a monitor sharing the beneficiary's family is recorded as `untrusted_monitored` only if a deterministic or human audit sample (`CalibrationRecord`) exists.
7. **Same-family rule (OQ-123 independence half).** When only one frontier family is available, `different_snapshot_same_family` is admissible for `report` and bounded `gate` uses with a mandatory `self_preference_check` in the calibration record; `same_snapshot` is never admissible for any non-exploratory use; a deterministic oracle or human reviewer is always an admissible reference.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-05 (isolation thresholds, admission modes and same-family allowances are conditioned rules with debt records), T-LCD-08 (the critic process receives its own profile and resource account), T-LCD-12 (attenuation, admission and separation are record checks; out-of-process), T-LCD-14 (critic snapshot, placement and admission mode are matched-budget factors), T-LCD-15 (independence summary stratifies judged metrics; hosted rows lacking `model_io` render `n/a`). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-04/-06/-07/-09/-10/-11/-13. Rung on the ADR-0024 ladder: the vector schema, minimums table, attenuation and separation checks are MUST-code; the chosen levels per critic, admission mode and iteration caps are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** self-preference is causal and grows with self-recognition — S-431 (Tier A); self-enhancement and position bias — S-430 (Tier A); intrinsic self-correction degrades — S-438 (Tier A); leniency of a separate but untuned QA agent and the sprint-contract/negotiation pattern — S-046 (Tier B); evaluator tampering by the beneficiary — S-439 (Tier B); obfuscated reward hacking under monitor optimization — S-434 (Tier C); safety techniques must survive intentional subversion; trusted-weaker monitoring with an audit budget — S-433 (Tier A); sneaky provers fool verifiers unless the verifier is trained against them — S-437 (Tier C); no usable signal between similarly powered peers — S-068 (`provisional`); ~17 % classifier miss rate and 93 % approval rate bound any critic-as-approver — S-053 (Tier B); attenuating delegation, R-TEXT, the join rule and the closed endorsement list — ADR-0016/0033/0034/0035 (ratified). No performance number is load-bearing.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** `codex/codex-rs/core/src/guardian/reviewer_config.rs` @ 0735c51 (`intersect_with_read_only`; approval policy `Never`; MCP servers cleared; memories, skills, hooks, plugins, apps, web search disabled; retries capped; own token budget), `guardian/request_budget.rs` + `input_budget.rs` (`ExhaustedReviewBudget` distinct from backend failure), `guardian/decision.rs` (fail-closed; "no contributor is never an implicit allow"), `codex-rs/core/assets/guardian/policy_template.md` (trusted-content enumeration; read-only probing to establish facts; truncation = omitted), `codex-rs/protocol/src/protocol.rs` (`SubAgentSource::Review` with parent lineage); `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/critic/base.py` + `agent/critic_mixin.py` @ 3fc7b22 (`IterativeRefinementConfig{success_threshold, max_iterations}` — the bounded in-loop shape; negative: same score reported as success); `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/scorer/_model.py` @ 75f4891 (`model_role = "grader"` binding; negative default = evaluated model); `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/approval/_policy.py` (chain on `escalate`; reject when no approver — non-human stages never allow by default). The six-axis declared vector, per-use minimums and `held_out_from` closure are `no-precedent / novel` as a contract.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) full isolation forbids agentic judges — answered by `capability = shared_readonly` with kernel-mediated probes on snapshots; (ii) chain-of-thought monitors are the best detectors and need the beneficiary's reasoning — answered by admitting `reasoning` as `claimed` evidence for `monitor` use (veto-only), never for success certification (S-434's own recommendation); (iii) stripping untrusted content entirely (WS-H7 draft) is simpler — answered by CF-245: the Guardian precedent needs tool outputs to judge egress payloads; `principal_only` remains the authorize default; (iv) one family only — answered by decision 7 and ADR-0047 (c)(iii); (v) held-out judges double judge cost per reported arm — the cost is one instrument-charged invocation and the alternative (RHO-style self-preference gating the artifact number) is the confound ADR-0046 exists to exclude. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** independent of model tier for the vector and the minimums; the *value* of `trusted_weaker` monitoring assumes the weaker snapshot can read the evidence format (S-434 found GPT-4o sufficient for o3-mini — mechanism only, not assumed to transfer); `quarantined_external` assumes WS-D1's role-placement rendering and WS-H1's raise-only Π rows; evolution-time separation assumes WS-I5's pipeline exposes the in-loop critic set; hosted rows supply only what their observability level permits.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C0 / Stage 1** — `IndependenceVector` schema, minimums table as a declaration constraint, `independence_summary` on verdicts; **C1 / Stage 4** — kernel-enforced isolation (attenuated `AgentProcess`, `BudgetNode`, admission modes) for runtime critics with WS-H7/H1 (AC-G3-3/-4/-11); **C2 / Stage 4** — agentic judges with probes on snapshots (B4/B5), same-family allowance with `self_preference_check`; **C4 / Stage 6** — `held_out_from`/`JudgeLeakedIntoArtifact` in WS-I5, untrusted-monitor protocols (OQ-280).

## Consequences

- WS-H1 enforces critic attenuation from the declaration (check 7 at spawn; Π rows for probes); WS-H7's reviewer is a critic with `admission_mode = principal_only` by default and may declare `quarantined_external` under the same label rules; WS-F3 spawns critics with `role = judge` and attenuated leases only.
- WS-I2/J4 stratify judged metrics by `independence_summary`; WS-I5 must name in-loop critics per arm and use a held-out or deterministic artifact-benefit oracle; WS-I6 owns expiry of same-family allowances.
- WS-C3 declares whether a profile exposes reasoning to critics (OQ-281); WS-J6 maps hosted judged rows to `n/a{observability}` without `model_io`.
- The Lab's standard `critic_experiment` gains the factors `critic snapshot × admission mode × placement`.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Axes and minimums are additive by ADR; relaxing a minimum for a use is a policy flip that leaves stored verdicts re-stratifiable because every verdict carries its independence summary. Two rules are load-bearing: `same_context`/`same_snapshot` critics never produce non-exploratory verdicts, and gate critics never produce the reported success — removing either after Stage 4 silently re-admits self-preference and reward-hacking into published numbers.
