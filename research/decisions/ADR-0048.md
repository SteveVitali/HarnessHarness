# ADR-0048 — Phase 1 cross-dossier reconciliation: canonical identifiers and shared-contract rulings (CF-101…CF-115), resolutions of OQ-052/070/073/079/088/126, and scope-register stage notes

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 1 synthesis, 2026-09-10; synthesis-authored)
**Owner workstream(s):** Phase 1 synthesis (parties: WS-A2, A3, A4, A5, B1, B2, L2, L3, L4, I1, I2) · **Phase:** 1 · **Related:** ADR-0012…ADR-0047 (every amended ADR cites the ruling that amended it); CF-101…CF-115, CF-030, CF-043, CF-052, CF-056, CF-060, CF-064–067, CF-077, CF-078, CF-083, CF-086, CF-089–095, CF-099, CF-100; OQ-052, OQ-070, OQ-073, OQ-079, OQ-088, OQ-126; R-2.1.4, R-2.3.3, R-2.4.4, R-2.5.4, R-2.8.2, R-2.10.1, R-2.10.2

## Context

Eleven Phase 1 dossiers were written concurrently. Each honoured the Phase 0 settled list, and their proposed ADRs are individually sound (ADR-0012…ADR-0047), but where two dossiers named the same object they sometimes spelled it differently, placed it in a different plane, or declared it twice (§7.1 "shared-contract convergence"). Doc 3 §7.1 makes the Ontology register the vocabulary tie-breaker and WS-A3 the arbiter of typed IR vocabulary; the WS-A2 ontology arbitrates concepts. This ADR records the rulings that no single workstream's ADR could own, so that Phase 2 inherits one identifier per object and one contract per seam. Every ruling below is also recorded in `registers/conflicts.md` (CF-101…CF-115) and, where it amended an ADR, in that ADR's amendment log.

## Options considered

1. **Leave each dossier's spelling and let Phase 5 reconcile.** Rejected: Phase 2 workstreams (H1/H2/D1/D4/C1/C3/E1/E4/F1/F2/B3/B4/B5/I3/I4) would each pick a side and the reconciliation cost would multiply by thirty-one dossiers.
2. **Pick the first-written dossier's spelling in every case.** Rejected: ordering is accidental; the rule must be the ontology's (concept → WS-A2; typed form → WS-A3; event identifiers → WS-B1's `plane.noun.verb` rule with the home-plane prefix).
3. **Rule case by case under the doc 3 §7.1 arbitration rule and record each ruling with its rationale (chosen).**

## Decision

**A. Identifiers (one spelling per object).**
1. **T-LCD-13 chain (CF-101):** `context.artefact.delivered`, `context.artefact.activated` (family *artefact exposure*, home P1) and `verification.artefact.followed` (family *verification*, home P4); shared keys `artefact_id` + `delivery_id`; `artefact` in identifiers, *artifact* in prose (CF-032).
2. **Structural ids (CF-060):** `run_id ⊃ turn_id ⊃ model_call_id ⊃ tool_call_id`; `step_id`/`call_id` retired; `effect_id = f(run_id, model_call_id, tool_call_id, ordinal)` unchanged.
3. **Effect-phase classes (CF-103):** `action.effect.intended, authorized, refused, prepared, committed, observed, unknown, probed, compensated, reverted, abandoned`; `committed` = commit record durable before dispatch.
4. **Effect domains (CF-102):** `fs_read, fs_write, exec, net_egress, secret_access, spend, message_human, spawn_process, memory_write, permission_request, model_call` — frozen for HIR/1; extension by dialect bump (OQ-055).
5. **Authority of lifted content (CF-082):** `origin = import`, `authority = unverified`; `imported` is never an `AuthorityClass` value.
6. **Security event classes (CF-077):** `security.label.applied/endorsed/declassified`, `security.permission.granted/revoked`.
7. **Configuration identity (CF-083/CF-115, ADR-0036):** `configuration_id` (semantic coordinate, seed excluded) and `configuration_version_id` (exact-bytes manifest key, seed included); `bundle_id` is recorded but never a coordinate (CF-108); the `seed` coordinate of κ is the replicate axis (CF-095).
8. **Cost fields (CF-105/106/109):** `Money{micro_units, currency}`; `provenance: CostProvenance` six-valued with derived `provenance_class ∈ {measured, reported, reconstructed}`; `confidence ∈ {exact, bounded{lo, hi}, estimate, unknown}`; `charged_to ∈ {subject, instrument}`; exclusive token-role counters are primary, `TokenVector` is the view, the profile's `usage_mapping` is what `normalizer_ref` points at.
9. **`ContentAddress{idp, algorithm, digest, media_type, size}`** (CF-086); **`VersionedRef`** is the one reference type, WS-A3's `Ref` its HIR projection (CF-110).
10. **`DecisionPoint = {plan, act, retrieve, compact, verify, delegate, authorize, retry, stop, escalate}`** (CF-107).

**B. Contracts (one declaration per seam).**
11. **Effect risk class is a projection** of the declared `EffectClass.attributes` (`reversibility' = read_only if mutability = read_only else reversibility`; `scope = workspace_local if world = closed else external`; `repeat_safety` verbatim) re-assessed by the kernel; `ToolCapability` carries no second declaration (CF-104).
12. **`AgentProcess.native.slots: map<class_id, SlotBinding | [SlotBinding]>`** with `control_strategy` and `context_policy` mandatory at Stage 1 (CF-052); an `ordered-many` slot is one ordered list whose identity is the `semantic_id` over the ordered element `semantic_id`s, per-element `enabled` is the ablation switch, reorder is a semantic op (OQ-073 shape).
13. **One resolver, two callers** (CF-056): `compose → resolve → validate_assembly → seal` is the only path to a sealed definition; the compiler's `link` refuses unpinned selectors (`LinkError{unbound_slot}`); the alias `ResolvedDefinition` is retired.
14. **`CompensationPlan` is a runtime record** (field group on the `Effect` record + registered `EffectIntentTemplate`), authorised by the reference monitor over the template's `EffectClass` at `prepare`; `compensates`/`reverts`/`parent-of` are ledger relations, never HIR/1 edges (OQ-088, CF-043).
15. **`Budget.dimensions` is the WS-L2 kernel dimension list** (ADR-0039) for HIR/1 and for `eval_budget` equality (CF-067, CF-099).
16. **WS-B1 taxonomy amendments** are those listed in the ADR-0026 amendment log (additions: `control.budget.allocated/reserved/released/amended`, `model.call.attempt.*`, `measurement.export.delivered`, `model.surface.relowered`, `lifecycle.component.bound`, `lifecycle.definition.changed`; payload extensions; renames; `assembly_ms{measured_at}` on `context.assembled` instead of a new class — CF-092; the ADR-0035 mandatory-provenance table). OQ-079 resolved.
17. **Adding a plane or boundary** (OQ-052): ADR + reclassification of every affected kind + re-run of the home-plane totality check; the event `plane` enum change bumps the ledger `schema_version` and the HIR dialect; no `state` value is added to the `plane` enum (CF-030).
18. **Second C0/Stage 3 target** (OQ-070): the MCP tool-surface export; the two minimal profiles cover the provider tool-API dialects.
19. **Equivalence margins** (OQ-126 shape): per scorecard dimension, pre-registered in the `PreRegistration` record, absolute (pp) for rates and relative for cost/latency, signed by the sponsor at Stage 3 pre-registration; values are Stage 3.
20. **Judge Validators** carry `calibration_ref?` and `charged_to` at HIR/1 (CF-114).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** each ruling adopts the mechanism already evidenced in the ADR it reconciles (cited there); the reconciliation itself rests on doc 3 §7.1 (ontology as tie-breaker; WS-A3 arbitrates typed vocabulary) and on WS-B1's `plane.noun.verb` naming rule (ADR-0026) and WS-A2's home-plane rule (ADR-0012).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** the identifiers chosen follow the precedents cited in the owning ADRs — e.g. OpenHands `SystemPromptEvent`/`activated_skills` for the artefact chain (ADR-0014), Codex `SessionMeta`/Pi `EntryBase` for structural ids (ADR-0027), MCP `ToolAnnotations` as a projection of attributes (ADR-0016/0021), goose `CostSource` for cost provenance (ADR-0043). The consolidated cross-dossier ruling set is `no-precedent / novel` as a program artifact.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** several rulings pick one of two defensible spellings (CF-101, CF-103, CF-105); the cost of a wrong pick is a rename before Stage 1 schemas freeze, which is why every ruling is recorded with its rationale and is reversible by a superseding ADR. Ruling 14 (compensation plan as record, not entity) could be reversed if WS-H1 or WS-D5 need typed edges to it — a dialect bump, stated in the ruling.
- **(d) Conditionality:** independent of model tier and task class; rulings 11 and 15 assume ADR-0016 and ADR-0039 stand as amended.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** C0 / Stage 1 — all identifiers and the single-declaration rules land in the Stage 1 schemas; the stage notes in §C are placements, not new items.

## Consequences

- Phase 2 dossiers use these identifiers verbatim; the readiness glossary check (ADR-0008 rule 4) fails on `measurement.artifact.*`, `step_id`, `call_id`, `ResolvedDefinition`, `authority = imported`, `cost_attribution`, `read_fs`/`write_fs`, `action.effect.intent`/`committing`.
- `registers/ontology.md` v1 §5/§6 carry these names; `registers/scope.md` carries the stage notes; the WS-B1 event table is read with the ADR-0026 amendment log until WS-B1's dossier is refreshed at Phase 5 assembly.
- OQ-052, OQ-070, OQ-079 and OQ-088 are resolved; OQ-073 and OQ-126 are resolved in shape with values deferred.

## Reversibility

**High for spellings** (a rename before Stage 1 schemas are cut); **medium for rulings 11–15** (each removes a duplicate declaration or resolver whose reintroduction would need a superseding ADR and a schema migration).
