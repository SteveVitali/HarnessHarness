## Context

Spec §11.4.1 lists the questions that block Stage 2 (mediation, custody, leases, caching, trust) and Stage 3 (interchange, margins, TLS placement, isolation defaults, detectors, autonomous validators, benchmark anchors, shared subject runs, analysis and admission constants, the hot-path ceiling). Each has a ratified placeholder; several are explicitly *measurements* the Stage-3 suite performs (OQ-133's cousin OQ-075/407, OQ-360, OQ-366, OQ-367, OQ-375). Doc 3 §8 requires the deferral ADR; §10.9 already lists the placeholders as MUST-data.

## Options considered

1. **Fix the constants now from the placeholders.** Rejected: the placeholders exist *because* the Stage-3 measurement decides them (ADR-0154 D7; ADR-0158 D2; ADR-0163 D2); fixing them is the S-508 failure mode (an under-covering interval chosen a priori).
2. **Resolve OQ-363 by ruling "share runs".** Rejected: ADR-0156 D2 refuses `compare` across the two `MatchSpec`s; sharing subject runs changes the exemplar's cost model and the engine's S-2 invariant, which WS-L2/J3 own.
3. **Defer with interim rules and the named measurement or ruling (chosen).**
