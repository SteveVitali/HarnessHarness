# ADR-0184 — Scope-register changes at Phase 3: R-2.10.1…R-2.10.6, R-2.11.1…R-2.11.4 and R-2.12.2 → `specified-by-ADR`; stage notes on R-2.10.3, R-2.10.4, R-2.10.5, R-2.10.6, R-2.11.1, R-2.11.2, R-2.11.3 and R-2.12.2; no tier or MoSCoW change

**Status:** ratified (synthesis-authored)
**Owner workstream(s):** Phase 3 synthesis (scope register) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0010 (R-2.10.6 Should), ADR-0048/ADR-0146 (prior stage notes), ADR-0147…ADR-0183; CF-368, CF-390; `registers/scope.md`

## Context

The LEDGER's binding constraint requires every scope change to be logged as an ADR. Phase 3 ratified the eleven laboratory, surface and extensibility items. Several ADRs place C0 slices inside C1/C2 items (the ratified pattern of ADR-0048/0146), and one conflict (CF-368) asked for stage notes on the MCP-server item. No item is added or removed; no MoSCoW or tier value changes.

## Options considered

1. Re-tier the items whose C0 slices are large (e.g. R-2.10.4 → C0, R-2.11.3 → C1). Rejected: the *items* remain the C1/C2 services and surfaces; the C0 slices are schemas, kernels and fixtures already owned by the Phase 1–2 contracts; re-tiering would move MoSCoW without new evidence.
2. Record statuses and stage notes only (chosen).

1. **Status → `specified-by-ADR`** (contracts, data models, acceptance criteria and build stages ratified; Spec text at Phase 5): R-2.10.1 (ADR-0147…0150), R-2.10.2 (ADR-0151…0153), R-2.10.3 (ADR-0154…0156), R-2.10.4 (ADR-0157…0160), R-2.10.5 (ADR-0161…0163), R-2.10.6 (ADR-0164…0166), R-2.11.1 (ADR-0167…0169), R-2.11.2 (ADR-0170…0172), R-2.11.3 (ADR-0173…0175), R-2.11.4 (ADR-0176…0179), R-2.12.2 (ADR-0180…0182).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: the ratified ADRs' (e) fields; ADR-0048/0146 pattern of C0 slices inside C1/C2 items.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `no-precedent / novel` (program artefact); precedents per item are in the cited ADRs.

- (d) Conditionality: none beyond the cited ADRs.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: register bookkeeping; effective immediately.
