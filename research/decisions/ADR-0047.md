# ADR-0047 — Oracle taxonomy and judge governance: nine declared oracle classes; deterministic classes only for C0 headline capability; judged values labelled, calibrated, independent and instrument-charged; veto invariants separate from averaged dimensions; teacher-relative lift admitted as a calibrated C4 oracle

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10); previously: **ratified, amended** (Phase 1 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I2 (taxonomy, admissibility) with WS-A3 (`Validator` kinds), WS-G1 (executable validators), WS-G2 (execution-alignment detectors), WS-G3 (judges), WS-H1–H6 (veto invariant sources), WS-I4 (per-suite oracles), WS-I5/I8 (teacher-relative use) · **Phase:** 1 · **Related:** ADR-0014 (detector provenance, `detector_classes_allowed`), WS-A3 §6.1 item 10 (Validator judge kind, `ProfileRef`), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05 reflexive, T-LCD-13), ADR-0006 non-goal N13; CF-029, CF-097; OQ-018, OQ-053, OQ-059, OQ-123, -05, -07, -08; R-2.9.2, R-2.7.1, R-2.7.3

## Context

Doc 2 §8: "prefer executable/end-state oracles when possible; LLM judges … can be correlated with the same failure modes as the tested agent"; "security and memory need dedicated invariants rather than being folded into average task success". WS-A2 fixed detector provenance on the compliance chain; WS-A3 typed `Validator.kind ∈ {executable, schema, predicate, judge, human}` with `judge ⇒ deterministic = false` and its own `ProfileRef`. What the measurement backbone must fix is the *oracle class* every metric declares, which classes may feed a headline number, what a judged number must carry, and how properties that can fail independently of task success are reported. The audit shows deterministic oracles are the norm for coding/terminal/tool/security suites (Terminal-Bench tests, Harbor reward files, τ-bench end-state equality, AgentDojo `security_from_traces`, SWE-bench F2P/P2P) and that judges are kept in separate scorers/jobs/modules (Inspect `model_graded_*`, Harbor `analyze`, HAL `llm_log_analyzer` vs `compliance_checkers`). Two judge failure modes are documented: correlation with the tested agent (doc 2 §8) and no usable signal between similarly powered models (S-068). AgentDojo already reports utility and security as separate booleans (S-097, Tier A).

## Options considered

1. **One "grader" abstraction with a score.** Rejected: hides determinism and provenance; a judged 0.8 and a test-derived 0.8 become indistinguishable in storage (CF-029's masquerade risk).
2. **Deterministic-only (refuse judges).** Rejected: removes most grounding/compliance/quality signal at Stage 3 (WS-A2 third-link risk); HAL's log analysis found behaviours no verifier caught (S-135).
3. **Nine declared classes with per-class obligations; deterministic-only headline at C0; judges admitted with labelling, independence, calibration and instrument cost attribution; veto invariants as a separate reporting channel (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`OracleDeclaration{oracle_id, class ∈ {executable, end_state, output_check, trace_predicate, protocol_check, judge, human, teacher_relative, reference_relative}, deterministic, requires_observability, verdict_type ∈ {bool, graded, verdict_lattice{C,I,P,N}, vector}, evidence_out, charged_to ∈ {subject, instrument} (CF-109), calibration_ref?, provenance}`** is a C0/Stage 1 registry object; every `MetricValue` references the oracle that produced it; a metric's `oracle_classes_allowed` bounds its inputs.
2. **Headline capability at C0 admits only `{executable, end_state, output_check, trace_predicate, protocol_check}`.** Judged, human and teacher-relative values are reported under distinct metric names with `detector = judged` (or `human`), never merged with deterministic values.
3. **Judge obligations.** A judge oracle is a WS-A3 `Validator{kind: judge}` with its own `ProfileRef` and assumption-debt record (T-LCD-05 reflexively); its model snapshot must differ from every beneficiary snapshot in the configuration (`JudgeNotIndependent` otherwise); it carries a `calibration_ref` recording agreement with a deterministic oracle on a labelled subset (protocol and minimum agreement: OQ-123); its calls are charged to the *instrument*, not the harness under test (OQ-053/OQ-059 answered for the measurement side); its rubric is a typed `Text` leaf with provenance; it may not consume `model_claim` observations as its only evidence.
4. **Oracle failure is an outcome class, not a score.** A verifier crash, empty/non-finite reward, or judge refusal yields `outcome_class = oracle_failure` (Harbor's parse-rejection behaviour generalized), excluded from the metric's denominator and counted beside it (ADR-0045 item 3).
5. **Veto invariants.** Metrics with `veto: true` — secret leak, authority widening, permission violation after deny, irreversible effect outside declared scope, duplicate side effect after retry, inconsistent durable state, action on revoked policy — are `trace_predicate` oracles over the ledger (native) or sandbox/environment evidence (hosted, where observable). A run that trips one is *success-with-veto*: excluded from headline success, counted in a separate column, and reported per invariant. AgentDojo's DoS convention (`security = ¬utility` for denial-of-service attacks) is adopted as a declared rule of the injection design, not an implicit branch.
6. **Teacher-relative lift** is admitted as oracle class `teacher_relative` for label-sparse learning-harness evaluation (C4, WS-I5/I8): `score = convergence of student+harness toward a stronger teacher's sparse corrections` (S-068); it requires a `calibration_ref` against a labelled subset in the target domain before it may gate anything, and is tagged `provisional` in every report until corroborated outside its origin domain (OQ-127).
7. **Reference-relative** (`equivalence_run`, T-LCD-03/-04) is a derived class whose verdict is three-valued `{equivalent, not_equivalent, inconclusive}` against a pre-registered margin.

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-13 (deterministic vs judged stages are distinguishable at the oracle level), T-LCD-05 reflexively (judges carry expiry), T-LCD-15 (oracle `requires_observability` scopes availability). Does not supply the per-suite oracle implementations (WS-I4; N13) nor the judge rubrics (WS-G3).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: deterministic end-state and trace oracles are implementable and standard — S-127 (Tier A: db equality + output check), S-097 (Tier A: utility/security from pre/post environment and traces), S-123/S-124 (SWE-bench F2P/P2P; Terminal-Bench tests), S-062 (`provisional (mech)`: validator outputs per run); judges fail in two documented ways — doc 2 §8 (Tier-B synthesis), S-068 (`provisional`: no signal between similar models), S-135 (Tier A: judged log analysis finds gaming that verifiers miss — the reason judges are admitted at all); security must be an invariant, not an average — doc 2 §8, S-097; validity/compliance detector provenance — ADR-0014 (S-077, S-088 `provisional (mech)`). The C0 rules (declaration, headline restriction, labelling, outcome class) assert no performance number.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `harbor/src/harbor/verifier/verifier.py` (reward file contract; rejects empty/non-numeric/non-finite), `tau-bench/tau_bench/run.py` (reward threshold), `agentdojo/src/agentdojo/base_tasks.py` (`utility/security(model_output, pre_env, post_env)`, `*_from_traces`, `ground_truth`), `agentdojo/src/agentdojo/benchmark.py` (separate utility/security results; DoS rule), `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/scorer/_model.py` (`model_graded_fact/qa` as separate scorers) and `scorer/_metric.py` (`NOANSWER` verdict), `hal-harness/hal/utils/compliance_checkers.py` (deterministic `ComplianceMonitor`) vs `hal/utils/llm_log_analyzer.py` (judged `AnalysisType`), `harbor/src/harbor/analyze/analyzer.py` (judged analysis as a separate job). Judge calibration records, judge independence enforcement, instrument cost attribution and `teacher_relative` as a declared class: `no-precedent / novel`; veto separation: `renamed` (AgentDojo utility/security split generalized).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) many valuable dimensions have no executable oracle — answered by admitting judges everywhere except the headline, with obligations; (ii) calibration subsets may not exist for novel rubrics — answered by allowing `calibration_ref = none` with the metric rendered `n/a{no_detector}` for gating purposes and reported as exploratory; (iii) independence is impossible when only one frontier family is available — answered by allowing a *different snapshot* of the same family with the weaker independence recorded on the declaration; (iv) veto invariants may be over-triggered by benign actions (e.g. an approved irreversible effect) — answered by scoping invariants to *declared* scope/authority so an authorized effect never trips them; (v) teacher-relative lift is single-domain — answered by C4-only, calibration-first, `provisional` labelling.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes the Stage 3 suite is coding/terminal/tool where deterministic oracles exist (WS-I4); for domains without them (research/writing) the headline is `n/a{no_detector}` and judged metrics are the only signal — a limitation the report states rather than hides; judge independence assumes ≥ 2 model snapshots available to the Lab.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0 / Stage 1 — `OracleDeclaration`, `oracle_failure` outcome class, `veto` flag, headline restriction rule; C0 / Stage 3 — deterministic oracles wired for the reference suite, veto invariants over the ledger (with H1/H6/B2 events), AC-I2-5/-9; C2 / Stage 4 — judge oracles with calibration and independence checks (WS-G3), execution-alignment detectors (WS-G2); C4 / Stage 6 — `teacher_relative`.

## Consequences

- WS-A3's `Validator` gains `calibration_ref?` and `cost_attribution` on the judge kind (or references an `OracleDeclaration`); WS-G3 must ship a calibration recipe; WS-L2 charges judge calls to the instrument.
- WS-H1/H2/H3/H6 and WS-B2 must emit the ledger facts the veto predicates read (`security.*`, `action.effect.*{effective_class, scope}`, `verify(run)`); WS-I4 declares, per integrated suite, which oracle classes exist.
- WS-J4's report generator renders the headline from deterministic oracles only, a separate judged block, and a veto column; WS-J5 stores oracle ids per value.
- WS-I5/I8 may use `teacher_relative` only after a calibration record exists; every such use is tagged `provisional` in reports.
- OQ-018's measurement half is closed by ADR-0014 + this ADR; the prediction half stays with WS-C3.

## Reversibility

**High for the taxonomy (classes are an enum with additive growth), medium for the headline restriction.** Admitting judged values into headline capability later is a policy flip, but every stored value already carries its oracle class, so historical scorecards can be re-rendered under either policy without loss — which is precisely why the class is stored per value.
