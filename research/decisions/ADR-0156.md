# ADR-0156 — Built-in experiment kinds and the two canonical exemplars instantiated (`lab/compaction-family-v1`, `lab/control-strategy-family-v1`) at their Stage-3 sizes; `equivalence` / `retirement` / `reproduction` / `exploratory` as data recipes; adaptive validation (`disagreement_weighted`, `successive_halving`) as optional, search-only strategies with `estimated` labelling

**Status:** ratified
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-J3 (with WS-D2/ADR-0077 and WS-F1/ADR-0105 as design owners; WS-I2/J4 for reporting; WS-I5 for search arms; WS-I4 for suites; WS-B4/I7 for the boundary companion; WS-C3/I6 for retirement) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (evolution evidence standard), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-03/-04/-05/-09/-14/-15), ADR-0017 (`HirDiff` classification), ADR-0041 (`MatchSpec` modes), ADR-0045/0046 (design; benefit kinds; adaptive selection inside search), ADR-0077 (compaction design), ADR-0105 (control-strategy design), ADR-0126 (removal test), ADR-0140 (`reproduce`), ADR-0143 (L3 split before search), ADR-0144 (Stage 3 suite); S-082 (`provisional`), S-504 (Hyperband), S-505 (W&B `early_terminate`); OQ-113, OQ-121, OQ-339, OQ-363; R-2.10.3

## Context

ADR-0077 and ADR-0105 ratified two pre-registered designs as the Lab's canonical component-level comparisons and named WS-J3 as their executor; both fix factors, `MatchSpec`, hypotheses, metrics and stage placement but neither is yet an `ExperimentSpec` document the engine can register. Several other Lab obligations are experiments in disguise: T-LCD-03/-04's `equivalence_run`, T-LCD-05's "delete this rule" recipe (ADR-0126's removal test; ADR-0105's β-ablation), ADR-0140's `reproduce` at R2/R3, and ad-hoc exploration. Adaptive evaluation is documented (S-082: ~80 % fewer search evaluations; Hyperband: successive halving as adaptive resource allocation) and already bounded by ADR-0046 §6 (inside `search_budget`; `estimated` rows) and ADR-0143 L3 (split assignment before search), but no operation admits or refuses it.

## Options considered

1. **Ship the exemplars as prose recipes in the spec and let users author specs.** Rejected: the exemplars are the executable proof that the Lab's contracts hold on real components (ADR-0077 decision 8, ADR-0105 decision 6); an unregistered recipe cannot be an acceptance test.
2. **Separate engines or code paths for equivalence, retirement and reproduction.** Rejected: each is a `paired` design with fixed constraints; separate paths would triplicate refusal logic and T-LCD-12 obligations.
3. **Adaptive validation as a default to cut Stage-3 cost.** Rejected: S-082 is `provisional`, rank-grade at low sampling fractions (WS-I2 F8), and admissible only inside search (ADR-0046 §6).
4. **Exemplars and the other obligations as `ExperimentSpec.kind` values with fixed constraints; the two exemplars shipped as registered documents; adaptive strategies optional, search-only, labelled (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`lab/compaction-family-v1` is shipped as an `ExperimentSpec` (ADR-0077 executed, nothing re-decided).** *Stage-3 instance (C0):* `kind: comparative`; `design.kind: paired` on `harness` at `component-level` (class `compaction_strategy`, levels `{evict_oldest, clear_tool_results}`); one model level (roles fixed incl. `summarizer_profile`), one environment level (Terminal-Bench 2.0 family, `fault_profile = none`), suite A held-out split; one `eval_budget` over `{tokens.input.total, tokens.output.total, model_calls, time.wall_ms}` with `MatchSpec{matched_cap, tolerance 0.10, same_snapshot, cache_policy: cold_start}`; `replicates_per_cell: 5`; `search_budget: 0`; pre-registration H2 with primary metrics `{capability.task_success, grounding.reacquisition_rate, reliability.repeated_action_rate}`, `min_n: 5`, margins per OQ-113; `scheduling{order: interleaved_blocked}`; `validation_strategy: full_set`; outputs a `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind: artifact_benefit}` plus the seven ADR-0077 metrics; `bundle(kind = experiment)`. *Full family (C1/Stage 5):* `full_factorial` over five strategies (+ `window_ceiling` as a `reference_only` arm excluded from the primary contrast) × ≥ 2 families × ≥ 2 tiers × three environment families, with `fractional_factorial{resolution: IV}` admitted when the cell count exceeds the experiment ceiling and only main effects plus the model × strategy interaction are pre-registered; the "retire rule R" arm is a `retirement` experiment. *Boundary companion (C2/Stage 4):* fork points (`fork_by_reference` at `context.compaction.started`) are task coordinates with `environment_derivation: fork_snapshot` and `eval_budget` on `turns = 5`.
2. **`lab/control-strategy-family-v1` is shipped as an `ExperimentSpec` (ADR-0105 executed).** *Stage-3 first execution (C0):* `kind: comparative`; `full_factorial` over `control_strategy ∈ {react/minimal, react/steerable}` × `model_snapshot` (2 families, roles fixed); one coding suite (ADR-0144 A/B), held-out split; `MatchSpec{dimensions: {model_calls, tokens.output.visible, time.wall_ms}, matched_cap, cold_start}` plus one `iso_cost` arm carried as a distinct arm with its own `MatchSpec` — the engine refuses a direct `compare` across the two modes (`IncommensurableMatch`); whether such arms may share subject runs when limits coincide is OQ-363; `replicates_per_cell: 5`; `seed_policy{seed_honoured_required: false}`; pre-registration H0–H4 with primary metrics `{capability.task_success, control.reproducibility, efficiency.model_calls}`; unmet `capabilities.requires` yields `n/a{reason: class}` cells at `expand`. *Full family (Stage 4):* adds `plan_execute`, `workflow`, `program`; the β-ablation recipe is a `retirement` experiment over `HirDiff`s on `ControlBoundary`.
3. **Four further kinds are fixed constraints on `paired` designs, not separate engines.** `equivalence` (T-LCD-03/-04): arms = reference participant vs native definition, `equivalence_run` (ADR-0046) with `PreRegistration.equivalence_margin`, three-valued verdict, unequal budgets refused. `retirement` (T-LCD-05; ADR-0126 removal test; ADR-0105 β-ablation): arms = `definition` vs `definition − R` where the `HirDiff` must classify as `{semantic_ops: 1, authority_delta: none, budget_delta: none}` (refusal `NotARetirementDiff`), matched-cap, non-inferiority against the pre-registered margin. `reproduction` (ADR-0140 R2/R3): arms = the bundle's configuration vs the reproducer's re-runs, seeds from the bundle, `reproduce` charged `instrument`, `ReproReport` as the output. `exploratory`: no pre-registration or `MatchSpec`; rows `comparable = false`; never in a `ComparisonReport`; the implicit kind for ad-hoc overrides (ADR-0154, OQ-076).
4. **Adaptive validation is optional, search-only and labelled.** `ValidationStrategy ∈ {full_set (default), disagreement_weighted{lambda, min_inclusion_fraction, estimator ∈ {hajek, anchored_difference}}, successive_halving{eta (default 3), min_budget, brackets}}`. Admissible only when `design.kind = adaptive_search`, on arms with `search_budget > 0`, over `split_labels ⊆ {search, dev}`, after the `SplitAssignmentRecord` exists (ADR-0143 L3); refused otherwise (`AdaptiveOutsideSearch`). `disagreement_weighted` weights tasks by candidate-outcome variance (S-082 mechanism) and records per-task inclusion probabilities as a content-addressed blob cited by every row (`estimated{estimator, inclusion_probabilities_ref}`); `successive_halving` allocates replicates in brackets and promotes the top `1/eta` per rung (S-504; W&B `early_terminate` shape), recording bracket membership and drop rung. Both feed `search_time_benefit` and the `SearchBudgetRecord` only; the frozen artifact's `artifact_benefit` is always a `full_set` experiment on the held-out split (ADR-0046 decisions 4 and 6); neither is ever the sole basis of acceptance (ADR-0046 §7).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies **T-LCD-03/-04** (`equivalence` kind executes `equivalence_run`), **T-LCD-05** (`retirement` kind is the "delete this rule" experiment with a diff-shape refusal), **T-LCD-09** (both exemplars are explicit factorials with interaction hypotheses), **T-LCD-14** (every comparable kind carries `MatchSpec`; adaptive rows labelled and search-scoped; `iso_cost` never compared directly with `matched_cap`), **T-LCD-15** (hosted levels only at product granularity in the exemplars, per ADR-0077/0105; `n/a` for unmet `requires`), **T-LCD-12** (kinds are data constraints over one operation set). Not applicable: T-01/-02/-06/-07/-08/-10/-11/-13. **ADR-0024 rung:** the exemplar documents, kinds' constraint tables and strategy parameters are MUST-data (experiment layer); the strategy samplers/estimators and the kind checks are MUST-code.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: ADR-0077 and ADR-0105 (ratified designs); doc 2 §8 (separate search-time from artifact benefit; freeze the artifact; held-out evaluation), §5.8 (Task-CoEvolve's value-of-information framing); S-082 (`provisional`: disagreement-weighted sampling with inclusion-probability-corrected estimates; ρ = 0.13 at 7 % via WS-I2 F8 — the reason for search-only, labelled use); S-504 (Tier A: successive halving as adaptive resource allocation with early stopping); S-505 (Tier B production: `early_terminate{hyperband, min_iter, max_iter, s, eta, strict}`, `run_cap`); ADR-0046 §6–7 and ADR-0143 L3 (ratified admissibility bounds); ADR-0126/0140 (removal test and reproduction as experiments). No `provisional` number is load-bearing: the exemplars measure; the strategies are optional.
- (b) Source-code precedent: exemplars — the ADR-0077/0105 precedents (gemini-cli `chatCompressionService.ts`, codex `compact_remote_v2.rs`, Inspect `_react.py`, Harbor ATIF) via those ADRs; adaptive — W&B sweep `early_terminate` (documentation, S-505); Hydra sweeper plugins (S-507) as the "strategy is a plugin over a fixed plan" shape; SWE-bench `best@k` disclosure rule (S-488 via WS-I3: all rollouts + selection mechanism must be shown) as the leaderboard form of `search_time_benefit`. **`no-precedent / novel`:** experiment kinds as constraint tables over one engine; the exemplars as registered, engine-checked documents; refusal of adaptive strategies outside search splits.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) full-set Stage-3 designs are expensive (S-135 ≈ $40 k / 21,730 rollouts) — answered by the staged sizes (two arms/two variants first), experiment ceilings and the *optional* strategies for search; (ii) Hyperband's guarantees assume a monotone resource whereas replicates across heteroskedastic tasks are not one — accepted: `successive_halving` is admitted as a search heuristic with bracket records, never as an estimator of effect; (iii) PRISM (S-093 via I2) shows optimizers can select brittle winners — answered by `estimated` labelling and full-set held-out artifact benefit; (iv) `retirement`'s diff-shape rule may block legitimate multi-op removals — accepted: a multi-op removal is several retirement experiments or a `comparative` one, which keeps attribution to one rule. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: exemplars assume the Stage-3 suites and deterministic oracles of ADR-0144, two model families with declared windows/profiles (ADR-0077 (d)), and native participants with `ledger` observability (ADR-0105 (d)); adaptive strategies assume `adaptive_search` designs with a registered split record and enough candidate disagreement to be informative (S-082's own caveat).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 3** — both exemplars at Stage-3 size registered and executed (the Lab's evaluation-first proof); `exploratory` kind; **C1 / Stage 4–5** — `equivalence` (T-LCD-03 anchor run), `retirement` (removal tests; β-ablation), `reproduction`; full compaction family; **C2 / Stage 4** — boundary companion; **C4 / Stage 6** — `adaptive_search` with the two strategies under WS-I5's gates.

## Consequences

- WS-D2/F1 own the designs; WS-J3 owns their registered documents and refuses drift between the two (a document that no longer matches its ADR is a `SpecMismatch` at synthesis review).
- WS-I2 registers `wasted_runs`/`reattempt_rate` (ADR-0155) and confirms that `estimated` rows carry the inclusion-probability member; WS-J4 implements the rank-only reporting for adaptive rows.
- WS-I5 uses `adaptive_search` + `retirement` + matched-total arms as its pipeline steps; WS-C3/I6 run removal tests as `retirement` experiments; WS-I3 receives `reproduction` experiments' `ReproReport`s.
- OQ-363 (shared subject runs across `matched_cap`/`iso_cost` arms) is answered before the Stage-3 control-strategy execution.

## Reversibility

**High.** Every element is data in the experiment layer; revising an exemplar is a new `PreRegistration`; removing a strategy loses nothing stored elsewhere; removing a kind would only move its constraints back into prose.
