# ADR-0141 — Bundle lifecycle and interop: status by supersession, publication policy with contamination defaults, export/import as lowering/lifting with loss reports, results-store linkage as a derived annotation, and bundle-kind composition by reference with containment equality

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I3 (consumers: WS-J5, WS-J3, WS-J6, WS-I4, WS-K2, WS-L6, WS-H6, WS-I1) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0038 (results-store keys; bundle kinds), ADR-0037 (immutability, supersession, `StaleIndex`, annotated-never-hidden), ADR-0026/0027 (row purity; fork lineage; interchange identifiability), ADR-0021/0042 (lowering with loss reports; `measurement.export.delivered`), ADR-0044 (content classes, `SinkPolicy`), ADR-0033/0034 (readers label; lifted authority `unverified`), ADR-0005/0010 (native ledger + interchange; act as a participant), ADR-0045/0046 (design, arms, `UnmatchedBudget`), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-06/-09/-11/-15); ADR-0139/-2; CF-298/-03/-05, CF-087, CF-057; OQ-030, OQ-112, OQ-116, OQ-331/-03/-05; R-2.9.3, R-2.10.5, R-2.9.4, R-2.8.6

## Context

ADR-0038 keyed results rows by `(configuration_version_id, run_id)` and said "every row carries `bundle_ref` and `claimed_level`"; ADR-0026 made rows pure projections of the ledger. Read literally these cycle: a row's hashed bytes would contain a manifest id that lists the row. ADR-0038 also named `bundle_kind ∈ {run, experiment, arm, lineage}` without a composition rule; Harbor's job lock contains trial locks verbatim and requires them to "match each trial's own lock.json exactly". Publication practice diverges: SWE-bench requires public, non-post-hoc trajectories; HAL releases every model call but encrypts the archive with a fixed password to prevent contamination; Terminal-Bench 2.1 reviews trajectories and has closed community submissions. Post-hoc invalidation exists in Inspect (`invalidate_samples` with `ProvenanceData`) but mutates the log in place. Interchange formats churn (ATIF v1.0–v1.8) and drop the white-box members by construction. The instrument must interoperate with Harbor/Inspect/HAL-style planes (ADR-0005/0010, non-goal N13: integrate, never author benchmarks) without letting a foreign artefact's version strings or hints become identity or authority (CF-087, ADR-0033 rule 6).

## Options considered

1. **`bundle_ref` inside the hashed row.** *Rejected:* hash cycle with the manifest's `results.rows[]`; breaks ADR-0026 purity.
2. **Rows outside the bundle, referenced only by the results store.** *Rejected:* R1 needs the exact rows pinned to compare re-derivation; ADR-0038 lists rows as members.
3. **Experiment bundles that copy run manifests verbatim** (Harbor). *Rejected as the only form:* duplicates payload and manifests; kept as an `encode(self_contained)` materialization.
4. **Publish everything (SWE-bench) or encrypt everything with a shared secret (HAL).** *Rejected:* neither is a reader policy; the first leaks solutions/personal data and hosted content without consent, the second is not access control.
5. **In-place status edits** (Inspect). *Rejected:* violates ADR-0037 I1; loses the audit of who changed what.
6. **Derived `bundle_ref` annotation; reference-with-equality composition; status records by supersession; publication as a policy-governed lowering; import as lifting to hosted R0 with foreign claims (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Results-store linkage.** Rows are bundle members by `version_id`; `bundle_ref` and `claimed_level` on rows (ADR-0038 decision 3) are **derived index annotations** maintained by WS-J5 when `bundle` runs, excluded from the row's hashed bytes; a row may be annotated by several bundles (run, arm, experiment). Leaderboard rows = `(results row, name-history entry, bundle_ref, bundle status)`; rows of `retracted` or `depends_on_revoked` bundles are annotated, never hidden. The **bundle catalogue** is a J5 header-only view over manifests keyed by `configuration_id`, level, status and class (CF-298).
2. **Bundle kinds compose by reference with containment equality.** `experiment ⊃ arm ⊃ run` reference contained manifests by `version_id`; `validate_bundle` S9 checks that each contained run manifest's `definition`/`configuration` sections equal the levels the `Design` declares for its cell, that `eval_budget` is equal across compared arms (`UnmatchedBudget`, ADR-0046) and that `pre_registration` predates every subject run (`PreRegistrationLate`); `lineage` bundles reference every fork-prefix bundle; `encode(self_contained)` materializes contained bundles on demand (Harbor's self-contained job as a transport option, OQ-335).
3. **Lifecycle by `BundleStatusRecord{bundle_ref, status ∈ {assembled, validated, attested, published, restricted, reproduced, superseded, retracted}, readers, reason, supersedes?, provenance, created_at}`** — a versioned sidecar/registry record; transitions monotone except `published ↔ restricted` and any → `retracted`; `validated` requires a `valid` report; `attested` a WS-H6 attestation on the manifest; `reproduced` a `ReproReport{verdict = reproduced, independent = true}` (adr-2); `retracted` never deletes; regrade/re-pin/redaction/migration/correction are `supersede_bundle(new, old, reason)` with a `derived-from` edge, and the `StaleIndex` marks bundles whose closure contains a revoked member (ADR-0037).
4. **Publication is a lowering under `PublicationPolicy{readers, content_classes ⊆ {accounting, structural, content, diagnostic}, redaction, payload_protection ∈ {none, restricted_store}, loss_report}`.** Defaults (MUST-data): manifest + L0/L1 to the bundle's readers; L2 content only where every item's `readers` label admits the audience; benchmark task-solution material and `model_io` for suites under a contamination policy (WS-I4) `restricted_store` by default; hosted L2 requires recorded consent (OQ-116); every publication appends `measurement.export.delivered` to each subject run and produces a loss report naming every withheld member so a reader knows which level *they* can verify. A shared fixed secret is not a `payload_protection` value.
5. **Export and import are lowering and lifting with typed loss reports (T-LCD-11).** `export(bundle, target ∈ {ledger_native, interchange_trajectory, harbor_job_dir, inspect_eval_log, swebench_submission, in_toto_bundle, telemetry_trace}, policy) → (artefact, LoweringLossReport{entries[{member, class ∈ {no_slot, hint_only, untyped_slot, narrowed, truncated}}], granularity_ceiling})`; only `ledger_native` is lossless; the interchange carries the ADR-0021 minimum set through its typed extension slot or degrades to `product` granularity (format choice stays OQ-030/WS-J6). `import(artefact, format) → (BundleManifest{participant_class = hosted, producer.origin = import, claimed_level ≤ R0}, ImportRecord{format, source, foreign_manifest: ContentAddress, mapping_loss})`: the foreign manifest (Harbor `lock.json`, Inspect header, SWE-bench `metadata.yaml`) is kept byte-for-byte as a member; digests become `foreign` claims and version strings `claims[]`/`name_bindings[]` (CF-087); events are lifted by `open_run + append` with `provenance = imported` (ADR-0026 decision 7) and authority `unverified` (ADR-0033); Harbor `source_trial{regrade}` lifts to `derived-from{regrade}`; a foreign digest that fails to verify against supplied bytes is a recorded claim conflict, never coerced.
6. **Hosted bundles** use the same manifest and roles (T-LCD-06): lifted events + `hosted.native_record`, model as claim, `OpaqueProcess` declaration `version_id` + `participant_version` claim, supplied context/tools/skills pinned, the rest `unpinned{hosted_mechanism}`; `claimed_level ∈ {R0, R3}`, R2 only with `end_state`.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-11 (every export/import carries a typed loss report; foreign digests kept), T-LCD-06 (hosted bundles add no role; import is lifting into the native shape), T-LCD-15 (hosted levels `n/a` where inapplicable), T-LCD-09 (catalogue and annotations keyed by `configuration_id`, never by name), T-LCD-14 (S9 refuses unmatched arm budgets). Not applicable: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-05/-07/-08/-10/-12/-13. **ADR-0024 rung:** `export`/`import`/`set_status` and the annotation maintenance are MUST-code; the target list and status enum are dialect schema; `PublicationPolicy` defaults, contamination lists and `readers` are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** job/trial containment equality — Harbor `lock.py` L268–272 (S-132); annotation with provenance vs in-place mutation — Inspect `_edit.py` (S-136) against ADR-0037 I1 and the S-106 revocation failure class (via ADR-0037); publication divergence — SWE-bench experiments README/checklist (S-488: public, non-post-hoc trajectories; best@k disclosure), HAL `upload.py` (S-134: fixed-password encryption "to prevent contamination"), HAL site (S-491), Terminal-Bench 2.1 (S-489: review and closed submissions); export as lowering with loss — ADR-0021/0042 (S-154 WORA decay via ADR-0007); lifted content is `unverified` — ADR-0033 rule 6 (in-toto/SLSA signer–builder rule, S-222/S-223); interchange churn — Harbor `trajectories/trajectory.py` (ATIF v1.0–v1.8 in one enum); readers label — ADR-0034; rows as projections — ADR-0026.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** harbor `src/harbor/models/job/lock.py` @7d5285b L200–272, `src/harbor/models/trajectories/trajectory.py` L1–60, `src/harbor/upload/uploader.py` L279–300, L544–620 (upload archive + lock rows + separate trajectory); inspect_ai `src/inspect_ai/log/_edit.py` @75f4891 L13–30, L178–250, `log/_log.py` L1242–1266 (`invalidated`, `log_updates`, `etag`); hal-harness `hal/utils/upload.py` @16bb03e L15, L95–120; SWE-bench/experiments README + `checklist.md`; SWE-bench `swebench/harness/run_evaluation.py` @02e7a74 L233–260 (prediction shape for `swebench_submission` export). **`no-precedent / novel`** for: a derived (non-hashed) row↔bundle annotation; status by supersession with `reproduced` gated on independence; a loss report on every export/import; a reader-labelled publication policy replacing shared-secret encryption.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** a derived annotation can lag or be lost (it is not in the hash); reference composition loses self-containment; restricting L2 by default makes public bundles less useful than SWE-bench's fully public trajectories; import to R0 with `unverified` authority may seem to undervalue carefully produced foreign artefacts; the interchange format is undecided (OQ-030). *Why none overturns:* the annotation is rebuildable from manifests (a `project` view over the catalogue — ADR-0026 discipline); self-containment is available by `encode(self_contained)`; the default is a *policy*, and a suite without a contamination policy publishes L2 to its readers; `unverified` is about *authority* (what an item may command), not about scientific value — foreign bundles still validate, claim R0 and keep their digests; the interchange member is optional and its loss report makes the format's limits explicit whichever is chosen.
- **(d) Conditionality:** contamination defaults depend on WS-I4's suite policies; hosted L2 on OQ-116 consent; `reproduced` on WS-H6 signer distinctness (OQ-334); foreign import fidelity on the foreign format's schema stability (ATIF churn); model- and task-independent otherwise.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C0 / Stage 3** (rows as members; catalogue view; `ledger_native` export with `measurement.export.delivered`); **C1 / Stage 4** (`BundleStatusRecord`, `supersede_bundle`, S9 composition, `PublicationPolicy`, `import` of Harbor/Inspect/SWE-bench artefacts, `export` to submission formats, interchange export once OQ-030 lands, AC-I3-8…12); **C2 / Stage 5** (independent-reproduction status; hosted `end_state` R2; A2A/Agent-Spec-shaped targets per ADR-0021 tiers).

## Consequences

- WS-J5 maintains the annotation index and catalogue view; ADR-0038 decision 3 is read as "every row is annotated with" rather than "carries inside its bytes" (CF-298, wording amendment proposed for synthesis).
- WS-J3 assembles `arm`/`experiment` bundles at sweep close and runs S9; WS-J4 pools only across bundles with equal level and `valid` status unless annotated.
- WS-J6 owns the interchange format (OQ-030) and the hosted adapter that fills `traces[run]`; WS-I4 supplies per-suite contamination policies and the Harbor/Inspect/SWE-bench mapping tables.
- WS-I1 records `measurement.export.delivered` with the loss report for every publication; WS-H6 attests status transitions as ledger-visible events where a run is the subject.
- WS-L6 decides retention interaction (`gc` members cap what a reader can verify — OQ-112) and consent recording (OQ-116).
- WS-K2 renders bundle status and loss reports beside results.

## Reversibility

**Low** for "annotation, never inside the hashed row" and "status by supersession" (stored rows and bundles depend on them); **medium** for the status enum and composition equality rules (extendable by dialect bump); **high** for publication defaults, the target list and contamination policy (MUST-data; per-suite).
