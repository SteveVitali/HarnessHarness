# ADR-0186 — The topology catalogue as data (T0–T7), recursive full-harness delegation as one preset, tree-local messaging, `delegation_reason` on every `delegate` decision, the eight value-of-compute inputs, and the canonical Lab comparison `lab/delegation-v1`

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 4 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-F3 (consumers: WS-F4, WS-F5, WS-L8, WS-J3, WS-J4, WS-I2, WS-F1, WS-F2, WS-D5, WS-G3, WS-C2, WS-E4, WS-J6, WS-B3) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0185 (the spawn contract), ADR-0187 (merge and containment), ADR-0012 decision 6 (β as a factor), ADR-0014 (realized-benefit decomposition), ADR-0015 (closed sums; dialect bump), ADR-0016 (`Procedure` steps; `Delegate`), ADR-0019 (`RuntimePlan/1 delegate` nodes), ADR-0035 (a message never endorses), ADR-0040 (gauges), ADR-0045/0046/0041 (design, matched-total), ADR-0085 (`subagent_task`), ADR-0096 (A2A peers), ADR-0103/0105 (`delegate` decision; "move the boundary only by `ComparisonReport`"), ADR-0106, ADR-0116 (critics), ADR-0123 (ensembles), ADR-0131 (delivery modes; goal inboxes), ADR-0138 D4, ADR-0154/0156 (`ExperimentSpec`), ADR-0161; CF-395; OQ-012, OQ-415/-04/-05; R-2.6.3, R-2.6.4, R-2.6.5

## Context

Doc 3 §5 asks for "topology (orchestrator-worker, recursive full-harness delegation, pipelines)" and for "when multi-agent is NOT justified (doc 2 §5.7)"; doc 2 §11 Stage 4 admits subagents "only for measurable reasons": "parallelizable subtasks, clean-context specialization, or independent verification". Doc 2 §6 rates multi-agent decomposition "medium-high" with the caveat that "coordination overhead and cost can erase gains". The evidence is split and mechanism-grade on the negative side: MAST finds gains "often minimal" with 14 architectural failure modes (S-403, Tier C); Anthropic reports ≈15× token cost, coding as poorly parallelizable and models "not yet great at coordinating and delegating … in real time" (S-036, Tier B); OrchBench finds "the benefits of parallelism diminish as coordination failures accumulate" and information retention across hand-offs to matter more than agent count (S-564, `provisional`); RAH and Prime Agent report large lifts from recursive full-harness delegation (S-064, S-069, `provisional`). The audit finds every shipped shape to be a composition of a few presets over one spawn record: parallel workers with a wait (S-036, S-109, S-111, S-107, S-113), chains passing the last output forward (S-113 `{previous}`, S-079 iterations), recursion bounded only by depth (S-064, S-116, S-107, S-111), read-only reviewers (ADR-0116 precedents), background workers that outlive the parent over shared external state (S-052, S-111, S-108), and family-scoped messaging with runtime-stamped senders and steering delivery (S-116 `agent-message`). No runtime records *why* a spawn happened; the reasons live in prompts ("effort scaling rules", S-036).

## Options considered

1. **Orchestration as opaque code** (a `CompiledPayload` orchestrator per definition; topology invisible to the Lab). *Rejected:* the topology is exactly what the Lab must vary (T-LCD-09) and what ADR-0105 says may move only by `ComparisonReport`; an opaque orchestrator fails the ADR-0015 ceiling test T3.
2. **A fixed topology decided now** (orchestrator–worker only, as S-036). *Rejected:* a C0/C1 commitment resting on split and partly `provisional` evidence (doc 3 §3.1); recursion and pipelines are precedented and cheap to express.
3. **Open topology "framework" with plug-in shapes.** *Rejected:* non-goal N1 (ADR-0006); every audited shape composes from the presets; a genuinely new primitive is a dialect bump (ADR-0015).
4. **Broadcast/global messaging between runs** (a shared bus). *Rejected:* S-036's "distracting each other with excessive updates"; Prime Agent's own daemon restricts reach to the family; a bus is a shared mutable context by another name.
5. **Let the model delegate freely; measure only cost.** *Rejected:* the Lab could not attribute benefit to a reason; F4 would have no labelled signal to learn from; MAST's system-design failures would be invisible.
6. **A closed `TopologyPreset` sum T0–T7 as MUST-data over the one spawn contract, recursion as T3 with depth as the only extra rule, tree-local kernel-stamped messaging as a cue, `delegation_reason` mandatory and typed on every `delegate` decision, the eight ledger-derived inputs fixed for F4, and a pre-registered `lab/delegation-v1` whose null hypothesis is the negative literature (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`TopologyPreset` (closed per dialect; MUST-data bound through the `orchestrator` component class or directly as `Procedure`/`RuntimePlan/1` step graphs):** **T0 `single`** (no delegation; `delegate` refused `DelegationUnavailable`; the control arm of every comparison); **T1 `orchestrator_worker{fan_out ≤ cap, wait ∈ {all, any, quorum(k)}, depth_cap = 1 default}`** (parent-authored goals; children do not spawn); **T2 `pipeline{stages[], handoff: artifacts}`** (stage i+1's `supplies` ∋ stage i's returned artifacts; failure at a stage propagates its `outcome_class`; loops under a `Loop(bound)`); **T3 `recursive_full_harness{depth_cap ≥ 2}`** (child `harness_def.semantic_id` may equal the parent's; children may spawn; every level attenuates and contains per ADR-0185 — no other rule changes); **T4 `independent_verifier`** (= ADR-0116, not re-decided: `role = judge`, grants ∩ read-only, own `slice`, `fork_snapshot`/`download` environment); **T5 `ensemble`** (= ADR-0123, not re-decided); **T6 `background_detached{on_parent_end: detach_to_child}`** (completion arrives as an `occurrence` into the parent's run or goal inbox, ADR-0131; shared external state is the merge layer); **T7 `hosted_peer`** (= ADR-0096/0164: an `OpaqueProcess` at product granularity). Persona role-play over one context is not a topology; it is `Procedure` steps under T0 (doc 2 §5.7 "phases of one policy").
2. **Recursive full-harness delegation is T3, not a second contract.** The delegated unit is a full `AgentProcess.native` with its own definition, control strategy, environment and budget (S-064's "full agent harness"); `depth_cap` is the only additional parameter; `delegation_depth` is the ratified gauge; there is no path by which depth > 1 relaxes SP-1…SP-8.
3. **Messaging (C1 semantics; used by T1/T3/T6).** `send_message(from_run, to ∈ {parent, child(id), sibling(id)}, payload: Text{authority ≤ delegate} | ArtifactRef, delivery_mode ∈ {steer, follow_up}) → receipt{delivered | queued} | MessageRefused{rate | size | queue | reach}` appends `control.subagent.message{sender (kernel-derived; never from payload), receiver, payload_ref, delivery_mode}` on both ledgers (`causes[]`-linked) and is delivered as a cue at a decision point per ADR-0131 (`steer` before the next model call; `follow_up` after the current effect's terminal) — proposed as `Cue.peer_message` or `human_input{kind: message}` (CF-395). Reach is tree-local; grandchildren relay; there is no broadcast beyond the family and no shared mutable context between runs. A message never endorses or widens (ADR-0035); rate/size/queue caps are gauges that refuse, never drop.
4. **`delegation_reason` is mandatory.** Every `control.decision{kind: delegate}` carries `delegation_reason ∈ {parallelism, clean_context, isolation, independent_check, specialization}` and `owner ∈ {code, model}`; the envelope refuses a `delegate` without one (`MissingDelegationReason`); a `model`-owned reason is a `model_claim` at `authority = delegate`; the reason is a factor on every results row that involves a child run, and `realized_benefit_by_reason` (ADR-0014 decomposition) is a class-scoped metric.
5. **The eight inputs F4 estimates over (all ledger-derived, no `Text` read):** (i) `context.occupancy` and distance to `CompactionRequired`; (ii) the candidate `Procedure`'s `B(P)`, `R(P)`, `I(P)` predicates and declared effects (ADR-0085); (iii) `remaining` per dimension, the child `BudgetSpec`, the profile's `subagents` capability and `procedure_inline_budget`; (iv) `IndependenceVector` minimums of any bound judge (ADR-0116); (v) the `context_label`/taint of the material to be processed (isolation is the only label exemption — ADR-0074); (vi) the dependency structure of candidate subtasks (step-graph fan-in/out; a `share` requirement signals coupling); (vii) `realized_benefit_by_reason` history for the `(profile, environment family)` cell from the results store (ADR-0161); (viii) the arm's `MatchSpec` dimensions (wall-time-matched arms value parallelism; spend-matched arms do not). The F4 scheduler is a policy that emits `delegate` decisions with `owner = code` over these inputs and never bypasses `spawn`.
6. **`lab/delegation-v1`** — a registered, pre-registered `ExperimentSpec` (ADR-0154/0156): factors `topology ∈ {T0, T1(fan_out ∈ {2, 4}), T2, T3(depth 2)}` (component-level; the definition supplies a `Goal` and a decomposable `Procedure` so every arm binds) × ≥ 2 model families × {Stage-3 coding suite, research/long-context suite} × budget (`MatchSpec{matched_total on {tokens.blended, spend}}` and a second arm set `MatchSpec{matched_cap on time.wall_ms}`) × ≥ 5 seeds. Hypotheses **H0** (S-403, S-036): no headline difference between T0 and any delegating topology at matched total on the coding suite; **H1** (S-036, S-399): T1 lowers `time.wall_ms` at equal tokens where subtasks are independent; **H2** (S-064 `provisional`; OrchBench retention): T3's benefit grows with task context length and depends on `return_contract` retention; **H3** (T-LCD-09): model × topology interaction ≠ 0; **H4**: `clean_context` delegations reduce `context_exhausted`/compaction counts more than headline. Metrics: headline executable oracle; `time.wall_ms`, `tokens.*`, `spend`; `harness_overhead.subagent`; `merge_conflict_rate`; `return_contract_violation_rate`; `subagent_retention` (T-LCD-13 on the summary artifact); `duplicate_work_rate` (identical `effect_id` derivations across siblings — MAST FM-1.3 recast); `fan_out`/`delegation_depth` distributions; `realized_benefit_by_reason`. Distributions with clustered-by-task intervals; `n/a` never 0. Topology moves in a definition only by `ComparisonReport` (ADR-0105's rule extended from β to topology).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-09 (topology, `fan_out`, `depth`, `delegation_reason` are explicit factors with an interaction contrast), T-LCD-14 (matched-total or wall-matched arms; refusal without `MatchSpec`), T-LCD-13 (retention through the summary artifact's delivery chain), T-LCD-15 (child and topology metrics class-scoped; hosted T7 rows `n/a` at component level), T-LCD-05 (a profile-conditioned topology default — "family X delegates well" — is a conditioned rule with a debt record and a `retirement` recipe), T-LCD-08 (the `orchestrator` class receives `(ModelProfile, ResourceAccount)`), T-LCD-12 (presets are data; the class contract is operations), T-LCD-06/-07 (T7 needs no native change; `subagents` tri-state). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-04/-10/-11. **ADR-0024 rungs:** presets, caps, `delegation_reason` vocabulary and the experiment are MUST-data; the `orchestrator` class contract, messaging delivery and the refusal on a missing reason are MUST-code.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** the three admissible reasons — doc 2 §11 Stage 4 (Tier B); isolation/concurrency as the architectural test — doc 2 §5.7; orchestrator–worker with effort rules, over-spawning and duplicated work as failure modes, filesystem hand-off to avoid "telephone" — S-036 (Tier B); minimal gains and architectural failure modes — S-403 (Tier C); retention over agent count; diminishing parallelism — S-564 (`provisional`); full-harness recursion implementable — S-064 (`provisional`), S-116 `rlm()` (source); family-scoped, kernel-stamped, steering-delivered messaging — S-116 `agent-message`; parallel and chain presets — S-113; background children outliving parents — S-052 (Tier B); tracker-as-shared-state workers — S-108; β as a cheaply variable factor and "move the boundary only by `ComparisonReport`" — ADR-0012/0105 (ratified).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** `pi-mono/packages/coding-agent/examples/extensions/subagent/index.ts` L1–13, L605–645 @ 400d690 (single/parallel/chain modes; caps); `prime-agent/packages/coding-agent/docs/rlm.md` §2 and `skills/agent-message/SKILL.md` @ 427ea4c (recursion depth; family-scoped messaging; steering delivery; daemon-derived sender; limits); `opencode/packages/opencode/src/tool/task.ts` L100–112, L232–262 @ 9f8db11 (depth; background completion injected as a synthetic message); `codex/codex-rs/core/src/tools/handlers/multi_agents_common.rs` @ 0735c51 (spawn/wait/send verbs on one surface); `symphony/SPEC.md` L643–811 @ e0ccc83 (claims and concurrency over a tracker). The closed preset sum, `delegation_reason` as a ledgered decision field, the eight-input list and `lab/delegation-v1` are **`no-precedent / novel`**.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** orchestration research keeps producing new shapes (AOrchestra dynamic sub-agent synthesis, S-565; OrchBench DAG plans) — each is a composition (T1 with per-call `SubagentSpec` synthesis; T2 with `wait ∈ {all, any}` over a step graph), and a new primitive is a dialect bump; a typed reason can be gamed by the model — the reason is a `delegate`-authority claim whose realized benefit is measured, so gaming surfaces as divergence; tree-local messaging forbids swarms — swarms over shared external state are T6 (Symphony) and need no messaging; H0 may be true, making the catalogue dead weight — then T0 wins the comparison and every other preset carries the evidence for its own retirement (T-LCD-05). None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** the value of any preset is model- and task-conditioned (S-036: coding vs research; MAST's per-model differences) and is the experiment's object, not an assumption; messaging assumes ADR-0131 delivery at decision points (C1); T3 assumes ADR-0185 at depth > 1 (Stage 5); F4 inputs (vii) assume a populated results store (Stage 4+); matched-total arms require ADR-0041 M3.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C3 / Stage 4** — the `orchestrator` component class with T1/T2/T6 presets over ADR-0185's C1 slice; `delegation_reason` enforcement; `control.subagent.message` (C1 semantics); `lab/delegation-v1` registered and its first execution on the coding suite. **C3 / Stage 5** — T3 with `depth_cap ≥ 2`; the F4 scheduler as `owner = code`; profile-conditioned topology rules with debt records; the research/long-context suite arm. **C2** — T7 hosted peers. T4/T5 stay at their own ADRs' stages.

## Consequences

- WS-F4 receives a fixed input list and a labelled training signal; its decisions are `delegate` decisions, never a bypass.
- WS-F5 inherits "no shared mutable context between runs" and tree-local messaging as the only channel; its ownership model governs T6's shared external state.
- WS-F1/F2: `delegate` decisions gain `delegation_reason` and `owner`; the envelope refuses a reason-less delegate; the `Cue` sum gains `peer_message` (CF-395).
- WS-J3/J4/I2: `lab/delegation-v1` joins the registered exemplars; `subagent_retention`, `duplicate_work_rate`, `merge_conflict_rate`, `return_contract_violation_rate`, `realized_benefit_by_reason` enter the metric registry as class-scoped declarations.
- WS-L8: the organizational layer's tracker-driven workers are T6 presets.

## Reversibility

**High** for the preset defaults, caps and the experiment design (data); **medium** for the closed sum and `delegation_reason` vocabulary (growing them is a dialect bump; removing the reason field after Stage 4 would strand results rows); **medium** for tree-local messaging (widening reach later is additive; a shared bus would be a superseding ADR against S-036/S-116 evidence).

## Amendment log (Phase 4 synthesis, 2026-09-10)

- Decision 3 (messaging) is realised by ADR-0191 decision 7: `send_message` is `record_occurrence` on the receiver's `peer_message` subscription (`Trigger.peer_message{from}`, ADR-0131 amended), delivered as the existing `Cue.woken{trigger: peer_message, payload_ref, delivery_mode}` — no `Cue.peer_message` and no `human_input{kind: message}` (CF-395, CF-450); events are `control.message.{sent, refused}`. Reach (tree-local; grandchildren relay), `delivery_mode ∈ {steer, follow_up}`, kernel-derived sender, the no-endorsement rule and the caps stand; `MessagingPolicy` (ADR-0191 M-2) is the definition data with defaults `parent_to_child/child_to_parent: true, sibling/broadcast: false`.
- Decision 5 (last sentence) reworded per CF-452: the F4 scheduler **never emits** a `delegate` decision; a `code`-owned `delegate` decision is proposed by a `HarnessRule`/`Procedure` step (ADR-0103 owners) and the scheduler binds its `budget_slice`, `spec_ref` selection and — when left unbound — `delegation_reason` from the declared reason of the `Procedure`/`subagent_task` (ADR-0188 B-1…B-3); `spawn_subagent` is `infeasible{undeclared_parallelism}` without a declared parallel step.
- Decision 6: `merge_conflict_rate` aggregates `MergeReport.conflicts[]` per parent run (ADR-0192; OQ-414).
