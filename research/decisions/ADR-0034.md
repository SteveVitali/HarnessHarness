# ADR-0034 — Label propagation: narrow-or-preserve through derivation, delegation, persistence and lowering; the per-call context label; the role-placement invariant; retrieval filters validity, then authority, then readers, then relevance

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10); previously: **ratified** (Phase 1 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-L3 (consumers: WS-D1, WS-D2, WS-D3, WS-D4, WS-F3, WS-H1, WS-H2, WS-A4, WS-C3, WS-E4, WS-J6, WS-I5) · **Phase:** 1 · **Related:** ADR-0033 (the lattice), ADR-0035 (endorsement points and monitor checks), ADR-0016/ADR-0017 (`derived-from`, `delegated-to`, `AuthorityWidening`), ADR-0014 (three-valued validity), ADR-0026 (`context.assembled`, `context.compaction.completed`), ADR-0002 (iii), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-10/-11); CF-006, CF-008; OQ-020, OQ-101/-02/-04/-05/-08; R-2.1.5, R-2.4.1, R-2.4.3, R-2.4.4, R-2.6.x (subagents)

## Context

A label is only as good as its propagation. The audit found three mechanisms that must be governed: **derivation** (summaries, compactions, extractions and subagent outputs — where an untrusted source is laundered into a fresh, apparently model-authored artifact), **control flow** (a model that has read untrusted content proposes actions — CaMeL's STRICT dependencies and Fides' `current_label` exist for this), and **persistence** (S-103's cross-scope escalation: session content re-entering at project/user scope). Retrieval is the fourth: S-106 shows revoked items with a visible flag still outrank their replacements when the filter is not in the contract; CF-008 already commits the program to authority/validity before relevance. Lowering is the fifth: MCP/A2A/ACP targets carry no authority and MCP annotations are hints.

## Options considered

1. **Propagate origin only; recompute trust at use.** *Rejected:* the deriver's identity replaces the sources' (a summary becomes "model-authored"), which is exactly laundering; CaMeL/Fides both propagate the join, not the last writer.
2. **Per-value dependency tracking at C0** (CaMeL interpreter). *Rejected for C0:* requires a model-programmed interpreter and per-tool policies; utility and authoring cost documented; retained as a C2 option under the same lattice.
3. **No control-flow taint** (labels on data only). *Rejected:* S-104 shows every harness with automatic permission review executed all 13 objectives; a proposal made after reading external content must inherit that fact.
4. **Reset the context label on every new user turn.** *Rejected:* that is the M-CPE escalation in another guise (the injected content is still in the window).
5. **Relevance-first retrieval with a post-filter.** *Rejected:* S-106 — the revoked item outranks its replacement and the post-filter is the part every system skipped; filter order must be the contract.
6. **Join-based propagation with a per-model-call context label, delegation attenuation, scope-ceilinged persistence, contract-ordered retrieval, and declared-lossy lowering (chosen).**

## Decision

- **P1 Derivation.** `label(out) = ⊔ label(inputs) ⊔ label(deriver)`, with `deriver = delegate` for any model-produced summary/compaction/extraction/translation and `kernel` for deterministic projections (truncation, redaction, structured projection). `derived_from` lists the inputs (for compaction: the forgotten ids WS-B1 already records). A summary of `external` content is `external` with the union taint.
- **P2 Delegation.** On `delegated-to`/`control.subagent.spawned`: child `Permission ⊆` parent's and `Budget ≤` parent's (WS-A3 invariants, unchanged); **child authority ceiling ≤ parent's effective authority at spawn**; every child result enters the parent at `≤ delegate`, `taint ⊇` the child's accumulated taint, `derived_from.kind = subagent_result`. Hosted participants are children with `origin = participant`.
- **P3 Context label and effective authority.** For each model call `c`: `context_label(c) = ⊔ label(items delivered in c)`, stamped on `context.assembled`. `effective_authority(p) = min(authority(proposer), context_label(c).authority)` and `taint(p) = taint(context_label) ∪ taint(args)` for any proposal `p` made in `c`. The context label is recomputed per call from what is delivered — compaction that drops external items restores it — and is otherwise lowered only by delivery and raised only by isolation (a fresh context) or a legitimate endorsement; a new principal message never raises it by itself.
- **P4 Persistence.** A write to `scope ∈ {user, project, session, run, definition}` is an effect (`memory_write`/`fs_write` with scope) subject to the ceilings in ADR-0035; the persisted item's label is `⊔(context_label, label(writer))`.
- **P5 Retrieval — authority before relevance.** `retrieve(query, slot_min_authority, validity_required = {valid}, reader, k)` applies, in this order and as part of the contract: (i) `validity(item, now) ∈ validity_required` (revoked/superseded/expired excluded by default; `unknown` admitted only if the slot declares `admit_unknown_validity`); (ii) `authority(item) ≥ slot_min_authority`; (iii) `reader ∈ readers(item)`; (iv) relevance ranking. `NoAuthoritativeItems` is distinguishable from an empty relevance result. **Supersession** requires `authority(new) ≥ authority(old)` (`SupersessionAuthorityInsufficient`); the superseded version is never deleted (WS-A3 `Memory` invariant).
- **P6 Never widen.** Outside the closed endorsement list (ADR-0035) no operation increases `authority`, shrinks `taint` or widens `readers`; the ledger refuses such an append (`IllegitimateEndorsement`); a `HirDiff` with `authority_delta = widening` is rejected in evolution contexts and requires `human` origin otherwise (ADR-0017, unchanged).
- **P7 Lowering and lifting.** Lowering to MCP/A2A/ACP/provider tool APIs carries `hir/provenance{origin_ref, authority, scope, taint_tags}` in the target's typed extension slot (`_meta` for MCP); a target that cannot carry it records the loss (T-LCD-11). Lifting assigns `origin ∈ {import, participant, tool}` and `authority = unverified` (or `external` for open-world results of a tool present in the sealed definition); a class is never read from the lifted payload.
- **RP Role-placement invariant** (compiler/context builder). The Model Profile owns `role_map: AuthorityClass → ProviderRole`; an item is rendered only into a role whose privilege ≤ `role_map(authority(item))`; an item below its slot's `min_authority` is rejected or rendered through a profile-declared demotion wrapper, never promoted; targets with fewer roles collapse classes and the lowering loss report says so. This single invariant forbids the developer-role injection of discovered instruction files and the skill-directory elevation reported by S-103, unless the file was sealed or pinned.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-10 (`role_map` is a profile surface; renaming roles never touches IR identity), T-LCD-11 (declared-lossy lowering; lifting never invents authority), T-LCD-06/-07 (hosted children are `participant`/`unverified`; nothing native changes), T-LCD-12 (contract is operations and an order of application), T-LCD-13 (delivered items carry the label that `context_label` joins). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-05/-08/-09/-14/-15 (T-LCD-14 is engaged by ADR-0035's approval accounting).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** join propagation over dependencies and control flow — CaMeL (S-099: "in the code c = a + b, variable c will depend on both a and b"; STRICT mode "add[s] the conditional test or loop iterable as a dependency to all variables assigned in the control flow block"); conversation-level label joined on every tool result and checked on every action — Fides (S-101: `current_label = label(result).join(current_label)`; tool-result rule ℓ″ = ⊔τ(x) ⊔ ℓ_f ⊔ ⊔ℓ′_a; `Hide` for values above the context label); low-water mark — Biba (S-217); per-issuing-agent capability checks so sub-agent permissions never transfer — CapScope (S-105, `provisional`); cross-scope persistence as an attack — S-103 X-CPE (`provisional (mech)`), corroborated by Claude SDK `PermissionUpdateDestination` and Codex/OpenHands memory-file loading read in source; revoked items outranking replacements unless withheld by a guard — S-106 (`provisional (mech)`); validity deterministic where designed — S-077 (`provisional (mech)`); annotations are hints, `_meta` is the carrier — MCP (S-153); derivation subtypes — PROV-DM (S-221); clean-context subagents as the utility path — doc 2 §5.7 (S-047/S-049 via WS-B1/L7).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** `camel-prompt-injection/src/camel/interpreter/interpreter.py` L116–130 (`MetadataEvalMode.STRICT`), L1667–1750 (`_eval_if`/`_eval_if_exp` add the test to body dependencies), L2047–2068 (`check_policy(tool, kwargs, dependencies)` before every call; Q-LLM args as dependencies), `src/camel/capabilities/utils.py` L15–52 (`get_all_readers` ∩, `get_all_sources` ∪ over the dependency graph); `fides/Tutorial.ipynb` cell 29 (`LabeledPlanningLoop.loop`: join per tool result; policy over the labeled trace), cell 27 (`read_emails` joins per-email labels with `reduce(join)`); `codex/codex-rs/core/src/session/inject.rs` L102 (developer-role instruction items — the counter-example RP forbids); `codex/codex-rs/protocol/src/protocol.rs` L2835–2850 (`SubAgentSource::ThreadSpawn{parent_thread_id, depth}` — delegation lineage); `claude-agent-sdk-python/src/claude_agent_sdk/types.py` L109–112, L296–305 (scope destinations; sub-agent attribution); `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/event/condenser.py` (compaction names forgotten ids — the `derived_from` input list, via WS-A3/WS-B1). Retrieval filter order and the role-placement invariant are `no-precedent / novel` as contracts (S-106's guard is the closest, as an external add-on).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** join propagation over-taints and CaMeL fails tasks "when the actions to take depend on untrusted data" — accepted as the correct semantics; mitigated by per-call recomputation, read-only/closed-world exemption in the taint gate, shape endorsement and subagent isolation, all measured at Stage 3 (AC-L3-10); implicit flows remain (Fides) — stated non-goal at C0; retrieval filters may hide useful-but-unverified memories — absorbed by three-valued validity with `admit_unknown_validity` per slot (CF-028); reference-forks and reference-lowering lose self-containment — the loss report and bundle export (WS-I3) carry it. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** independent of model tier; assumes the context builder can enumerate delivered items per call (true for native participants; hosted participants supply what their observability level permits — `n/a` otherwise); assumes tool capabilities declare `world` (WS-A3); the value of isolation depends on task decomposability (a Lab factor, not an assumption).
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** C0 / Stage 1 (P1, P6, P7 lowering carrier, RP in the compiler); C0 / Stage 2 (P3 context label, P4, P5 filter order in D1/D3's C0 retrieval contract); C1 / Stage 4 (P2 with subagents and hosted children); C2 (taint/readers components of every rule enforced by WS-H2).

## Consequences

- WS-D1 emits `context_label` on `context.assembled` and enforces RP with the profile's `role_map`; WS-D2's compaction variants must call `derive(kind = compaction, …)` so summaries inherit labels; WS-D3/D4 implement `retrieve` with the fixed filter order and the supersession-authority rule; WS-F3 applies P2 at spawn and on result ingestion; WS-H1 reads `effective_authority` and `taint(p)`; WS-A4/E4 lower `hir/provenance` and report role collapse; WS-C3 owns `role_map` and its demotion wrappers (T-LCD-05 applies to them); WS-J6 lifts hosted rows as `participant`; WS-I5 candidates inherit `delegate` and can never widen.
- A Lab experiment "taint gate on/off × isolation on/off under matched budget" becomes a standard Stage 3 recipe.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Each rule is a pure function over records and can be tightened or relaxed by ADR; relaxing P3 (dropping the context label) or P5's order after Stage 2 reopens the two attack classes the acceptance tests encode, so those two rules are the ones to treat as load-bearing.
