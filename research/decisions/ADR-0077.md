# ADR-0077 — The canonical laboratory comparison `lab/compaction-family-v1`: compaction strategy as a component-level factor under matched cap with compaction charged to the subject; ledger-computed process metrics (re-acquisition, repeated action, recall probe, overhead, latency); deterministic-only headline oracles; pre-registered hypotheses; and the boundary-local paired-continuation companion

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-D2 (executed by WS-J3; consumed by WS-J4, WS-J5, WS-I2, WS-G1, WS-G3, WS-I5, WS-I7, WS-B4) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (evidence standard for evolution), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-09/-14/-15), ADR-0012 (model set; β as a factor), ADR-0013/0014 (participant class; validity vs compliance), ADR-0027 (fork by reference), ADR-0031 (idempotency key), ADR-0036 (`configuration_id`), ADR-0039/0041 (`charged_to`; `MatchSpec` modes), ADR-0043/0044 (turn phase profile; cost view attribution classes), ADR-0045 (`MetricDeclaration`, six factors, outcome classes, `n/a`), ADR-0046 (paired protocol, benefit kinds, `ComparisonReport`), ADR-0047 (oracle classes; judge governance), ADR-0075 (contract), ADR-0076 (provenance); OQ-058, OQ-113, OQ-124, OQ-193/-06; R-2.4.2, R-2.10.3, R-2.10.4, R-2.7.x

## Context

Doc 3 §2.4 and §2.10 make the compaction family "a canonical lab comparison target"; doc 2 §6 rates context policy a high-confidence lever with model-conditioned, stale-prone recipes; doc 2 §8 demands factorial designs, process metrics, executable oracles, distributions and robustness across capability changes. Phase 1 fixed the instrument: six factors + replicate axis, `MetricDeclaration`, outcome classes, `MatchSpec` with engine refusal, benefit kinds, nine oracle classes with deterministic-only headline at C0, and class-scoped `n/a`. What the Lab lacks is a *first experiment* that exercises every one of those contracts on a component with real variants. Three 2026 preprints (`provisional`) report that compaction's costs are invisible to task success — re-acquisition of dropped state (S-341), blocked and repeated actions (S-340), tens-of-seconds stalls (S-343) — and two propose validating a compaction at its boundary by continuing from the same pre-compaction state (S-340 TRACE, S-342 Slipstream); gemini-cli already runs a self-verification turn. Every one of those quantities is computable from ratified ledger events without a judge.

## Options considered

1. **Report task success per compaction variant, averaged across models.** Rejected: T-LCD-09 forbids a single "harness score"; doc 2 §6 says the effect is model-conditioned; the preprints say success hides the cost.
2. **Judged summary-fidelity as the headline metric.** Rejected: ADR-0047 admits judges at C0 only as labelled, calibrated, instrument-charged non-headline values; fidelity judges share the tested model's failure modes (doc 2 §8).
3. **Unmatched comparison (summarising arms get extra calls "for free").** Rejected: T-LCD-14/ADR-0046 refuse it; compaction calls are `subject` cost by ADR-0039 R-ACC-3.
4. **Component-level factorial under matched cap with compaction charged to the subject, ledger-computed process metrics with `trace_predicate` oracles as headline companions, pre-registered hypotheses, and a boundary-local paired-continuation companion (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Design record** `lab/compaction-family-v1` (`Design` + `PreRegistration`, immutable once the first run opens — ADR-0045). Granularity `component-level` for native participants; hosted rows admitted only at `product-level`, stratified by `capability_declaration.compaction` (T-LCD-15).
2. **Factors.** `model`: ≥2 families × ≥2 capability tiers (each arm's `configuration_id` carries the model set incl. `summarizer_profile`); `compaction_strategy`: {`evict_oldest` (C0), `clear_tool_results`, `summarize_rolling`, `structured_checkpoint`, `offload_then_summarize`} + a `window_ceiling` reference arm (same model, larger declared window, no compaction) where the profile allows; `environment`: coding/terminal long-horizon, research/browse, multi-episode structured-tool tasks (one benchmark family each, with split labels); `budget`: `MatchSpec{dimensions: [tokens.input.total, tokens.output.total, model_calls, time.wall_ms], mode: matched_cap, tolerance: 0.10, model_scope: per-model}`; `seed`: replicate axis, ≥5. The compaction prompt's `Text` leaf hash is part of each arm's definition `semantic_id`; `search_budget = 0` for every arm (`benefit_kind = artifact_benefit`).
3. **Cost rule.** Summariser and validation calls made by the harness are `charged_to = subject`, attribution class `harness_overhead.compaction` (ADR-0039 R-ACC-3; ADR-0044 cost view); Lab detectors and judges are `instrument`. Cache warmth is controlled per arm or stratified (ADR-0041); `tokens.input.cache_write` after clearing is reported (OQ-196).
4. **Metric declarations** (all `computed_from` ledger events; `applies_to_classes = {native}` unless stated): headline `capability.task_success` (`oracle_classes_allowed = {executable, end_state}`), `reliability.pass^k` + per-task variance; `efficiency.tokens_total`, `efficiency.spend`, **`efficiency.compaction_overhead_share`** (= `harness_overhead.compaction` ÷ subject total), **`efficiency.compaction_latency_ms`** (`turn_phase_profile.compaction`); **`grounding.reacquisition_rate`** = |deliveries (`context.artefact.delivered`) or reads (`fs_read`/`memory_read` effects) after compaction c whose content address or `artefact_id` ∈ forgotten(c)| ÷ |forgotten(c)| (`trace_predicate`); **`reliability.repeated_action_rate`** = |effects after c whose idempotency key (ADR-0031) equals one before c| ÷ |effects after c| (`trace_predicate`); **`grounding.recall_probe@compaction`** = fraction of facts extracted by a deterministic `projection_ref` (paths, ids, error literals — OQ-193) from forgotten(c) that are present in the summary artifact or reachable by a recorded handle (`trace_predicate`; extractable subset only); `security.label_restored` (context label after vs before); `grounding.summary_followed` (T-LCD-13 on the summary artifact, `detector ∈ {deterministic, judged}`); `opacity_dynamic` delta; `compaction.count`, `compaction.ineffective_count`, `compaction.fallback_count`; `applies_to_classes = {native, hosted}` only for `compaction.count` (from lifted events) and product-level headline metrics. Optional C1 `grounding.summary_fidelity_judged` — `Validator{kind: judge}` with `calibration_ref`, independent of every beneficiary snapshot, `charged_to = instrument`, `headline = false` (ADR-0047).
5. **Pre-registered hypotheses and analyses.** H1: at matched cap, `summarize_rolling` ≥ `evict_oldest` on `task_success` for lower-tier models and the gap shrinks for higher tiers — the canned interaction analysis "does variant V help model M more than N" (T-LCD-09). H2: `reacquisition_rate` and `repeated_action_rate` separate variants where `task_success` does not. H3: `offload_then_summarize` lowers cost per re-acquired item without exceeding budget tolerance. H4: `structured_checkpoint` lowers `repeated_action_rate` vs free-text summaries. Equivalence margins per environment pre-registered (WS-I2 R2/R3, OQ-113). Outcome classes per ADR-0045 (`budget_exhausted` and `infrastructure_failure` rows reported, never dropped); intervals clustered by task; tails reported. The report is a `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind: artifact_benefit, budget_match.status, per-configuration distributions, interaction effects}` (ADR-0046).
6. **Robustness across capability change (doc 2 §8).** The design re-runs on every new profile version; a variant or conditioned rule whose benefit disappears is marked conditional and its `AssumptionDebtRecord.expiry_condition` fires (T-LCD-05; "retire rule R" arm included in the design).
7. **Boundary-local companion** `lab/compaction-boundary-v1` (C2): at each `context.compaction.started` event, `fork_by_reference{run_id, at_seq, source_head_hash}` (ADR-0027) and continue k = 5 turns with and without the proposal applied, same seed policy; report per-boundary `reacquisition_rate`, `repeated_action_rate`, next-effect agreement and `recall_probe`; refuse unequal budgets. This is the recipe for ablating compaction prompts (`Text` leaves; OQ-058 placeholder semantics) and, at C4, for guideline optimisation under `SearchBudgetRecord` with matched-total accounting (ADR-0046 §7) — the ACON mechanism (S-337) admitted as a search procedure, never as a design constant.
8. **Stage placement.** A two-arm instance (`evict_oldest` vs `clear_tool_results`, one model, one environment) runs at **C0 / Stage 3** as the evaluation-first proof that the Lab's contracts hold on a real component; the full family at **C1 / Stage 5**; the boundary companion at **C2 / Stage 4** (with B4/I7); judged fidelity with G3 at Stage 4.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: doc 2 §8 (factorial design; process metrics; executable oracles; distributions; robustness across capability changes) and §6 (context policy model-conditioned); ratified ADR-0041/0045/0046/0047 (the instrument); ADR-0031 (idempotency key — makes repeated action detectable), ADR-0027 (fork by reference — makes boundary-local pairing cheap), ADR-0014 (compliance chain); S-038 ("loss of subtle but critical context whose importance only becomes apparent later"; "maximize recall then improve precision"); S-050 ("fewer unnecessary summarizations"); `provisional` mechanism only: S-341 (retrieval calls rise while completion is flat; environment-dependent), S-340 (blocked/repeated actions; boundary-local evaluation), S-342 (validation from continued execution), S-343 (blocking latency; prompt-invariant volume), S-337 (guideline optimisation from failure pairs).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `gemini-cli/packages/core/src/context/chatCompressionService.ts` L382–411 (self-verification turn after summarisation), L462–471 (inflation check as a metric-like guard) (S-119 @ed2ac40); `goose/crates/goose-context-management/src/structured.rs` L5–13 ("experiments that truncate sections can cut from the tail") and goose `usage_ledger.is_compaction` (via WS-L2 S-112 @fae91d0); `codex/codex-rs/core/src/compact_remote_v2.rs` L140–230 (`CompactionAnalyticsAttempt{trigger, reason, implementation, phase}`, `active_context_tokens_before`), `core/src/compact.rs` L399–402 (warning that repeated compaction reduces accuracy) (S-107 @0735c51); `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/event/_compaction.py` L8–27 (`tokens_before/after`, `source`) (S-136 @75f4891); `harbor` (S-132 @7d5285b) and `hal-harness` (S-134) as comparison-plane precedents (via WS-A1). A component-level compaction factorial with ledger-computed re-acquisition/repeated-action metrics and a fork-based boundary companion is `no-precedent / novel` as a design; the pieces are precedented as listed.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: "only a judge can measure fidelity" (answered: re-acquisition and repeated action are behavioural consequences needing no judge; the recall probe covers the extractable subset; judges admitted as labelled non-headline values); "the numbers will not replicate" (answered: no number from S-340/-05/-06/-07 is reused — the design measures them; if the mechanisms do not appear, H2 is falsified and the metrics stay as cheap process metrics); "a `window_ceiling` arm is not budget-matched" (answered: it is a declared *reference* arm reported with `budget_match.status`, never a compared arm in the primary contrast); "environment dependence makes a canonical design meaningless" (S-341 — answered: environment is a factor and interaction effects are the deliverable, T-LCD-09). None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes ≥2 model families with declared windows and profiles, executable/end-state oracles for the three environment families (I4), and deterministic extractors for the recall probe (G1; the probe is reported `n/a{no_detector}` where none exists). Hypotheses are tier-conditional by construction. The boundary companion assumes fork-by-reference and re-execution (ADR-0028: reconstruction + fresh samples; deterministic replay only where declared).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (metric declarations registered; `Design`/`PreRegistration` records); **C0 / Stage 3** (two-arm instance; `trace_predicate` oracles; AC-D2-8/-9 partial); **C1 / Stage 5** (full family; conditioned-rule retirement arm; AC-D2-9); **C2 / Stage 4** (boundary companion AC-D2-12; judged fidelity with G3); **C4** (guideline optimisation under I5 gates).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-09 (explicit factors; interaction analysis is the deliverable), T-LCD-14 (`MatchSpec` mandatory; engine refuses; compaction charged to the subject; `search_budget` recorded), T-LCD-15 (every metric class-scoped; hosted component metrics `n/a`), T-LCD-13 (summary compliance measured), T-LCD-05 (retirement arm), T-LCD-02 (prompt-leaf ablation via the boundary companion), T-LCD-08 (variants enter through the contract). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-06/-07/-10/-11/-12. **Ladder rung (ADR-0024):** the design and metric declarations are Lab data (parameters/entities); the metric definitions are Core declarations by this ADR.

## Consequences

- WS-J3 executes the design as the Lab's first component-level experiment and inherits its `Design`/`PreRegistration` records; WS-J4 implements the interaction analysis and Pareto view; WS-J5 stores rows keyed by `(configuration_version_id, run_id)`.
- WS-G1 supplies deterministic extractors for the recall probe (OQ-193, with OQ-049); WS-G3 supplies the calibrated fidelity judge (C1).
- WS-I2 registers the seven new metric declarations; WS-I1 confirms `turn_phase_profile.compaction` and `harness_overhead.compaction` feed them.
- WS-B4/I7 provide the fork-and-continue driver for the boundary companion; WS-I5 uses it for guideline optimisation under matched-total accounting.
- The design doubles as the executable form of doc 3 §1.3's promise: the same Context contract satisfied by a trivial and a rich variant, compared under one instrument.

## Reversibility

**High.** The design is data; factors, arms and hypotheses can be revised by a new pre-registration without touching contracts. Metric definitions are registry declarations; retiring one is an ADR amendment. The only sunk element is the C0 metric catalogue entries, which are cheap to keep even if unused.
