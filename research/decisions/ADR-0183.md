# ADR-0183 — Phase 3 cross-dossier reconciliation: one embedding contract for every surface (Group L), the CLI's binding, one `run_kind` sum and one experiment event family, registry kinds for hosted participants and adapters, the analysis↔results seam, the hosting-tier placement check, and the vocabulary rulings of Ontology v3 (CF-322…CF-393)

**Status:** ratified (synthesis-authored)
**Owner workstream(s):** Phase 3 synthesis (WS-J1…J6, WS-K1…K4, WS-L5; consumers WS-B1, WS-A2, WS-F1, WS-E1, WS-H5, WS-I2/I3, Phase 4 F3/F4/F5, I5–I8, L8) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0147…ADR-0182 (Phase 3 workstream ADRs), ADR-0050 (D1–D3, §8), ADR-0026, ADR-0045/0046, ADR-0041, ADR-0023/0025, ADR-0042/0043, ADR-0063, ADR-0100, ADR-0103, ADR-0133, ADR-0143; CF-322…CF-393; OQ-004, OQ-076, OQ-128, OQ-130, OQ-314, OQ-340, OQ-346, OQ-348, OQ-359, OQ-365, OQ-369, OQ-371, OQ-385, OQ-391, OQ-393, OQ-403, OQ-404; ADR-0007 (T-LCD-06/-07/-12/-14/-15)

## Context

Eleven Phase 3 dossiers (the laboratory J1–J6, the surfaces K1–K4, the extension contract L5) were written in parallel against the Phase 1–2 contracts and the "settled for Phase 3" list of `synthesis/phase-2.md` §6. Four of them (WS-K1, WS-K2, WS-K3, WS-J4) could not see the dossiers they depend on (WS-K4, WS-J3, WS-J5) and pre-registered the gap (CF-340, CF-353, CF-358, CF-363). The brief's five foci — OQ-004's ABI depth, the lab chain J1↔J2↔J3↔J5↔J4, L5's consistency with J2/H5, every surface as a client of K4's contract, and technology neutrality — each surfaced one or more cross-dossier contradictions that no single workstream could rule on. This ADR records the rulings; the amended texts live in the amendment logs of the ADRs named per section. Doc 3 §7.1: the ontology is the vocabulary tie-breaker; §11.5 step 4: shared-contract convergence before ratification.

## Options considered

1. **Ratify each workstream's proposal as written and leave the seams to Phase 5.** Rejected: three manifest discriminators, two experiment event families and four separately requested boundary-verb extensions would enter the spec as they stand; Phase 5's coherence review would find them, but every Phase 4 dossier (F3–F5, I5–I8) would meanwhile build on divergent spellings.
2. **Re-open the ratified boundary (ADR-0050 D3) and layer split to fit the surfaces' needs.** Rejected: nothing found in Phase 3 narrows Q-L1-14 or fires a revalidation trigger (ADR-0179 decision 4); the gaps are additive operation groups, not a different boundary.
3. **Rule each seam once at synthesis, amend the affected ADRs by logged amendment, fold the vocabulary into Ontology v3, and record every ruling as a conflict row (chosen).**

## Decision

### A. Every surface is a client of one contract — Group L (CF-383; resolves CF-322, CF-356, CF-359, CF-369)

1. `hh-embed/1` (ADR-0176) is the only kernel-facing contract any surface, host or laboratory process uses. Its Group W gains the branch operations (`coherent_fork_points`, `discard`, `promote`, `rollback`, `replay`, `counterfactual`); Group R gains the audit proofs (`audit_view`, `verify`, `prove_inclusion`) and `request_redaction`, with `durability` explicit on every frame; a new **Group L** carries every design-time and Lab operation record ratified in ADR-0147…0166 and ADR-0173 (assembly, registry, experiment, analysis, results, leaderboard, hosting `describe/probe/attach`, `serve`), generated from the one schema source, records-in/records-out, `instrument`-charged where the owning ADR says so.
2. Design-time crossings (assembly, registry, analysis over rows) are exempt from ADR-0050 D2's "per run, never per model call" granularity rule, which governs run-time verbs; the Lab's run-time crossings remain per run.
3. The CLI (ADR-0167), the web surface (ADR-0170–0172), the MCP server's Lab tools (ADR-0173–0175), the ACP artefact (ADR-0098) and third-party SDKs are clients of this contract; AC-K4-2 (identical canonical envelopes over every binding) fails any private verb. No surface bypasses the contract; no surface holds state of record.

### B. The CLI's binding (CF-384; closes CF-353)

ADR-0050 D1 binds: the CLI is kernel-native and drives the kernel over the in-process binding (a) of `hh-embed/1` (ADR-0179), or attaches to a running local service over (b)/(c) in `serve`/`attach` modes — indistinguishably in the ledger. ADR-0167 K-2 is amended accordingly; K-1 (byte-identical ledger effects for every command) and K-3 stand.

### C. One ledger discriminator, one experiment family, one registry (CF-385, CF-386, CF-387, CF-391; OQ-314, OQ-359)

1. The run manifest gains one additive field **`run_kind ∈ {agent, experiment, surface, inbox}`** (ADR-0026 dialect bump). `agent` runs drive a harness (subject vs instrument stays `charged_to`); `experiment` (ADR-0155/0162), `surface` (ADR-0174) and `inbox` (ADR-0131; OQ-314) runs have no `configuration_id`, environment or metric cells, hold their own fenced writer, chain by `continued_from`, and never enter subject aggregates. An `ext` marker is refused (ADR-0016).
2. The experiment ledger has one closed event family (spellings `plane.noun.verb`): `measurement.experiment.{declared, run_planned, run_claimed, claim_expired, run_launched, run_bound, run_settled, run_excluded{reason}, run_replanned, cell_completed, amended, paused, resumed, drift_bracket, closed, bundle_assembled}`, `measurement.analysis.{recorded, superseded}`, `measurement.leaderboard.{published, entry_retracted}`. The sweep engine (WS-J3) is the producer and single fenced writer; the results store (WS-J5) validates appends; `declared` is exactly one and precedes every `run_bound`.
3. `registry/1` includes the kinds `participant` and `adapter`, and `conformance_report` carries `subject_kind ∈ {variant, profile, participant}` — one record kind and one `reconcile` for component variants (ADR-0152), profiles (ADR-0125) and hosted participants (ADR-0166).
4. Every ledger addition requested by a Phase 3 dossier is consolidated in the ADR-0026 Phase 3 amendment log with its producer; Rule P holds because surfaces and the Lab supply records through `hh-embed/1` and the kernel appends.

### D. The lab chain composes; hosting stays secondary (CF-388, CF-389, CF-390, CF-340, CF-365)

1. **J1 → J2:** a sealed definition enters the registry only through `apply/publish` (ADR-0147 M-1/M-2); `resolve` takes one `registry_snapshot_id` (ADR-0151). **J2 → J3:** `slot_choices` supplies factor levels; `register(spec)` refuses on the closed set incl. `DependsOnDriftedCapability` (ADR-0154/0152). **J3 → J5:** every subject run carries `measurement.experiment.bound{cell_id, replicate_index, attempt_no, budget_id}`; rows key `(configuration_version_id, run_id)` and carry `experiment_id, arm_id, cell_id, replicate_index, attempt_no, comparable` (ADR-0155/0161); exclusions are appended, never inferred (ADR-0162). **J5 → J4:** `analyze(spec, QuerySpec @ watermark_set)` is pure over `cells`; its `AnalysisReport` is recorded as `AnalysisRecord.report` through `record_analysis` (ADR-0157/0162; CF-388). **J4 → J5 → K2:** `RankReport` sets feed the leaderboard (ADR-0163 L5); K2 renders from report refs and never recomputes (ADR-0170). **Bundles:** every experiment result links to a bundle by the derived annotation index (`bundle_refs[]`, ADR-0161; CF-298), and every score is class-labelled with a typed `n/a{reason}` (ADR-0157 decision 3; ADR-0163 L2).
2. **OQ-004 (ADR-0164–0166):** the Hosting ABI's verbs map onto ADR-0098 D6's ACP v2 stable core (`open`/`submit`/`cancel`/`resume`/`close`/`stream_events`/`request_permission`/`report_usage` ↔ `session/new`/`prompt`/`cancel`/`resume`/`close`/`update`/`request_permission`/`usage_update`; `set_coordinate` ↔ `set_config_option`; `steer`/`account`/`export`/`elicit` capability-declared) and lift onto ADR-0026 classes through a MUST-data lifting table (tool-plane spellings follow OQ-235; CF-389). Hosting remains strictly secondary (ADR-0001): the C0/Stage-3 items (`HostedEvent` schema, `proj_ABI`, `ConformanceRecord`/`AdapterRecord` schemas, adapter zero) are schemas and a fixture over native runs, add no native field (T-LCD-06) and are removable with the hosting tier (ADR-0182 X4; CF-390); hosting proper is C2/Stage 4 (R-2.10.6 Should).
3. **L5 ↔ J2 ↔ H5:** a plugin is an `ExtensionRecord{kind: plugin}` whose contributions are `registry/1` records with their own trust legs (ADR-0180; ADR-0063); third-party variants bind only through the variant host at `placement ≥ subprocess_confined` (ADR-0181; ADR-0153; CF-141); `host_unconfined` is narrowed to tool executables (CF-380); the extension DAG is stated as invariants X1–X6 with a readiness-gate check (ADR-0182).

### E. Rulings on open questions (recorded here; register rows updated)

OQ-128 veto tiering per WS-J5; OQ-340 `probe_memorization` as instrument diagnostic and stratum evidence; OQ-348 both `component.bound{hosting_adapter}` and `hosted.attached`; OQ-359 chain length as MUST-data; OQ-365 `contrast` through C1, `factorial_glmm` C2 with shrinkage disclosure; OQ-369 sets rendered with a boundary, in-set order by point estimate only with intervals shown; OQ-385 `lifecycle.surface.invoked` as a class; OQ-391 the kernel alone selects `replay_mode`; OQ-393 null profile default for exposure definitions and supply surfaces; OQ-403 experimental fields under `ext hh.experimental/<n>`; OQ-404 `environment_class = host_process`, results `external`.

### F. Vocabulary (Ontology v3 §5d/§5e/§6)

Results plane nouns (results store / experiment ledger / leaderboard definition · snapshot); `conformance_report` with three subjects; `placement` vs binding locality vs isolation class; `hosting_mechanism` spelling; exposure definition as an object-level document calling the instrument level; three bindings, one verb shape (`hh-embed/1`, `plugin_abi/1`, `hh-hosting/1`); Harness Lab as the CLI noun for the laboratory group; the experiment event family.

**T-LCD statement.** The rulings preserve T-LCD-06 (no native field for hosted participants; hosting tier removable — §D.2), T-LCD-07 (declared/probed kept distinct across variants, profiles and participants — §C.3), T-LCD-12 (every seam is an operation over canonical records; one contract, three bindings), T-LCD-14 (matched-budget refusals re-checked at `register`, `launch`, `settle`, `close` and report time — §D.1), T-LCD-15 (typed `n/a` on every class-inadmissible cell). Every closed sum that grew (`run_kind`, `scope_kind`, `interval_method`, `RecordKind`, `environment_class`) grew by dialect bump. **ADR-0024 rung:** the boundary groups, the event family and the `run_kind` check are MUST-code; lifting tables, tiering lists and defaults are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: the contradictions themselves (CF-322…CF-393) and the ratified ADRs they are resolved against; one contract over several bindings with identical envelopes (S-107 `codex-rs/app-server/src/in_process.rs`, `app-server-client/README.md` — Tier B source via ADR-0176/0179); the cost of two drifting localities (S-110 `remote_conversation.py` `reconcile`); one authoritative log with projections (ADR-0026); write-once run records summarized into tables (S-526 HELM); declared-vs-probed as the one conformance shape already ratified for profiles and hosted participants (ADR-0125, ADR-0013). No performance claim is asserted.
- (b) Source-code precedent: per section — S-107 (one `MessageProcessor`, three transports), S-110 (`session_protocol.py` frames; `conversation` factory over two localities), S-136 (`eval_set_manifest.py` declaration-before-running; `task_identifier`), S-132 (`hub/leaderboards.py` — negative precedent), S-112 (`McpClientTrait` over placements by data tag), S-118 (`harness_capabilities.py` tri-state), pinned commits as cited in ADR-0147…0182. The reconciliation as a whole is `no-precedent / novel` (a program artefact).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) Group L widens the public contract before Stage 3 exercises it — accepted: additive, behind `serves_measurement`, and generated (ADR-0178 tiers let items ship `experimental`); (ii) a single `run_kind` sum couples B3's inbox to J3/K3 designs — accepted: one discriminator on the manifest is cheaper than three, and non-`agent` runs share every ledger contract already; (iii) merging J3's and J5's event families forces the sweep engine and the results store to agree on one schema — that is the point (one producer, one validator); (iv) keeping `HostedEvent` at C0/Stage 3 could be read as hosting entering the Core — answered by the removability build (§D.2). None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier and task class; assumes the Phase 1–2 ADRs as amended at this synthesis; assumes OQ-235 rules tool-plane spellings before Stage 3 (the hosted lifting table is re-keyed as data); assumes WS-B1 confirms the ADR-0026 log at Phase 5 (a synthesis obligation).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: §A/§B/§C.1 — C0 / Stage 1 (schema export, `run_kind`, Group L signatures); §C.2/§D.1 — C0 / Stage 3 (experiment family, results rows, analysis kernel); §C.3/§D.3 — C0 / Stage 1–2 (registry kinds, variant host); §D.2 — C0 / Stage 3 schemas, C2 / Stage 4 hosting proper.

## Consequences

- Phase 4 dossiers (F3/F4/F5, I5–I8, L8) build on `synthesis/phase-3.md` §6 and on the amended ADRs listed there; they cite `hh-embed/1` Group L for every Lab operation and never define a second boundary verb set.
- WS-B1 (Phase 5) confirms the consolidated ADR-0026 Phase 3 log; WS-A2 confirms Ontology v3 §5e; WS-F1/A3 close the HIR half of OQ-165 against `GuardVerdict`.
- The readiness report gains rows: AC-K4-2 over three bindings, the experiment family's single-writer proof (KP-E1…E5), the registry `subject_kind` reconcile fixture, the hosting-tier removability build (n = 1).

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Every ruling is additive or a wording choice over ratified decisions; reverting §A (a second contract) or §C.1 (per-consumer discriminators) after Stage 1 would re-key every generated client and manifest fixture — the cost the rulings avoid. The vocabulary rulings are free to change until Spec §2 lands at Phase 5.
