# ADR-0014 — Validity vs compliance as a measured pair: three-valued validity; delivered/activated/followed event chain with detector provenance; realized-benefit decomposition; class-scoped compliance metrics

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 1 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-A2 (semantics) with WS-B1 (event schema), WS-I2/WS-J4 (metrics), WS-G1/WS-G3 (detectors), WS-D4 (memory validity), WS-A4 (compiled identity) · **Phase:** 1 · **Related:** ADR-0007 (T-LCD-13, T-LCD-15), ADR-0002 (evidence standard item "beneficiary-model compliance"), ADR-0004; CF-008, CF-028, CF-029, CF-032; OQ-018 (partly answered), OQ-049, OQ-053, OQ-059; R-2.1.1, R-2.9.2, R-2.4.4

## Context

Doc 2 §1/§5.1 distinguishes harness *validity* (the artifact encodes a correct policy or fact) from harness *compliance* (the beneficiary model notices, activates and follows it) and v0 declared validity "deterministic at the protocol layer". Two independent 2026 preprints supply the mechanism: Invalidation Contracts (S-077) decomposes realized savings into validity × compliance and makes validity deterministic via version stamps (identical across seven models, zero contract failures in ~9,400 episodes) while compliance with *identical wire bytes* varies from 3.7 % to 100 % by model; Harness Updating Is Not Harness Benefit (S-088) splits compliance into *activation* (Skill-Load Rate — deterministic from trajectories) and *following* (Harness-Following Rate — LLM-judged). The LCD battery already requires *delivered / activated / followed* events (T-LCD-13) and class-scoped metrics (T-LCD-15). What the ontology must fix is (i) that validity is not always decidable, (ii) that following is usually judged and judges correlate with the tested agent's failures (doc 2 §8), and (iii) that a compliance metric must never masquerade as a validity fact. WS-B1 has now registered the three event classes and WS-A4's closed-world rule guarantees every delivered artifact has a compiled identity.

## Options considered

1. **Keep v0: validity boolean and "deterministic at the protocol layer".** Rejected: a prose memory or free-text rule has no oracle unless the harness supplies one; the claim is true only for artifacts designed for it (S-077's version stamps).
2. **One "compliance score" per artifact, judge-based.** Rejected: collapses activation and following (S-088 shows they fail separately and at different rates), hides the judge's provenance, and would be `n/a` for participants without `model_io` — but reported as a number anyway.
3. **Deterministic-only compliance (refuse judged detectors).** Rejected: no audited runtime records a `followed` event; refusing judges would leave the third link of T-LCD-13 empty for every artifact kind without an executable validator, which at Stage 3 is most of them.
4. **Chosen: a measured pair** — three-valued validity decided model-free; a three-event compliance chain with detector provenance and confidence; a realized-benefit decomposition as the reporting shape; class-scoped compliance metrics with a `detector_classes_allowed` field.

## Decision

1. **Validity** `validity(α, at: event_id) → {valid, invalid, unknown, check_ref}` is decided by a protocol-layer check that never consults the beneficiary model: version stamp, executable validator (`validates` edge), external oracle, or the entity's declared validity interval (WS-A3 `ValidityState`); `unknown` when none exists. Invariant (testable): identical result for the same artifact version and check across all configurations (the S-077 property). WS-D4 must require a check or interval on memory artifacts (CF-008/CF-028).
2. **Compliance chain** (events `typed-by: WS-B1`, identifiers per B1): `context.artefact.delivered` (P1; one per artifact per model call; requires a compiled identity — WS-A4 closed-world rule; `UndeliverableArtifact` if the artifact lacks identity/provenance) → `context.artefact.activated{detector, signal?, detector_ref, confidence, evidence}` (P1; never emitted for kinds with `activation_observable = false`) → `verification.artefact.followed{detector, detector_ref, verdict, confidence, evidence_ref}` (P4; deterministic only via a validator the artifact references; else judged; `NoDetector` ⇒ metric `n/a`). Every `activated`/`followed` event carries **`detector ∈ {deterministic, judged}`** and a `detector_ref` with its own provenance (judge configuration = a Validator of judge kind with a `ProfileRef`, WS-A3; cost attribution OQ-053/OQ-059).
3. **Realized-benefit decomposition** is the reporting shape for any artifact-level effect: E[Δ] ≈ P(valid) · P(activated | delivered, valid) · P(followed | activated) · E[Δ | followed]; each factor is reported with its detector class and sample size; no single "compliance score" is reported without its stages.
4. **Class-scoped compliance metrics.** `MetricDeclaration` gains `detector_classes_allowed ⊆ {deterministic, judged}`; deterministic stages require `observability = {events}`, judged stages `{model_io}`; unavailable ⇒ `n/a`, never 0 (T-LCD-15). Compliance is keyed by model *snapshot* through the configuration (S-077's sign flip across versions of one family is data, not noise).
5. **Artifact kinds declare `activation_observable`**; kinds without an activation signal (instructions inside a rendered prompt) collapse the chain to delivered → followed and say so in the metric.

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-13 (three events with artifact ids; per-profile compliance computable from events alone for deterministic stages) and T-LCD-15 (class- and detector-scoped metrics). Reflexively applies T-LCD-05 to judged detectors (a judge configuration is a conditioned component with expiry). Does not itself supply detectors for every artifact kind — OQ-049 (WS-B1/G1/D4) is the mitigating owner.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: validity and compliance separate cleanly and compliance is not monotone in capability — S-077 (`provisional (mech)`: algebra, deterministic validity, per-model compliance with identical bytes); compliance decomposes into activation and following with different failure rates — S-088 (`provisional (mech)`: SLR deterministic, HFR judged, "fail to activate" vs "activate but fail to follow"); judges correlate with agent failures — doc 2 §8 (Tier-B synthesis); memory needs lifecycle semantics beyond retrieval — doc 2 §5.1 second-pass ledger (medium-high). C0 content (definitions, event schema, metric fields) rests on Tier-B source precedents for the first two stages and on the LCD battery (ADR-0007, ratified); the preprints corroborate the mechanism and contribute no load-bearing number.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/event/llm_convertible/system.py` (`SystemPromptEvent{system_prompt, tools, dynamic_context}` — the delivered record), `event/llm_convertible/message.py` (`MessageEvent.activated_skills`, `extended_content` — deterministic activation) and `context/agent_context.py` (`get_user_message_suffix`: `skill.match_trigger`, path triggers); `AIOS/aios/memory/context_injector.py` ("Injected %d memories" log line, L491) with `injection_log_parser.py` (`InjectionRecord`, `verify_injection_occurred`) — a kernel-level delivered record parsed back deterministically; `AIOS/aios/memory/write_barrier.py` (`WaitOutcome{DRAINED, TIMEOUT}`) — validity as a protocol-layer ordering property. **`followed` has `no-precedent / novel` in source**: every following signal in the literature is judged or post-hoc; this is the main unproven obligation on WS-B1/G1.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) HFR is judged (S-088) and judges are unreliable (doc 2 §8) — absorbed by detector provenance, `detector_classes_allowed`, `model_io` requirement and OQ-053 (judge independence); (ii) validity is undecidable for many artifacts — absorbed by `unknown` and by WS-D4's requirement; (iii) activation is not observable for some kinds — absorbed by `activation_observable`; (iv) compliance flips sign across versions of one family (S-077: Sonnet 4.6 88.9 % vs Sonnet 5 11.1 %) — this is *why* compliance is keyed by snapshot, not evidence against the pair; (v) the third event has no source precedent — recorded as the risk that drives OQ-049's blocking status.
- (d) Conditionality: the *schema* is unconditional; the *usefulness* of deterministic detectors depends on artifact kind (skills/retrievals/tool surfaces at Stage 3; prose rules only with validators); judged stages require `model_io` observability and a judge snapshot distinct from the beneficiary; magnitudes from S-077/S-088 are not assumed to transfer to any tier.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0 / Stage 1 — event *schema* (delivered/activated/followed with `detector`), `MetricDeclaration.detector_classes_allowed`, `activation_observable` on artifact kinds, `validity()` contract; C0 / Stage 2 — `delivered` emitted by the context builder (WS-B1 places it at Stage 2); C0 / Stage 3 (evaluation-first) — deterministic `activated` detectors and deterministic `followed` via validators on the reference harness; per-profile compliance metric; C2 / Stage 3–4 — judged detectors (WS-G3) with provenance and cost attribution.

## Consequences

- WS-B1 adds `detector`, `detector_ref`, `confidence` to its registered `activated`/`followed` payloads (its `signal` enum is the deterministic case) and keeps identifiers with the *artefact* spelling (CF-032).
- WS-I2/J4 add `detector_classes_allowed` to `MetricDeclaration` and report the decomposition's stages, never a bare compliance score.
- WS-A3 marks `ContextItem.delivery_id` and the artifact `kind`/`activation_observable`/`validity_check_ref` fields; `ValidityState` and `validity()` are documented as two faces of one notion.
- WS-D4 requires a validity check or interval on every memory artifact; WS-A4's closed-world rule becomes a precondition of `deliver`.
- WS-C3's per-profile behavioural test suite (WS-A4 §6 item 5) consumes the chain; ADR-0002's evidence standard item "beneficiary-model compliance" becomes computable.
- OQ-018 is re-scoped to prediction of compliance only.

## Reversibility

**High for the schema, low for the science.** Dropping `detector` or `detector_classes_allowed` is a field removal; the cost is that judged and deterministic numbers become indistinguishable in stored results and every published compliance figure would need re-labelling. Collapsing the three events to two is cheap only for kinds with `activation_observable = false`, which the schema already handles.
