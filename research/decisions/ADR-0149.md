# ADR-0149 — Typed-diff semantics for assemblies: `AssemblyDiff` as a lossless derived projection of `HirDiff`, sealed-only diffs with a plan rendering, snapshot drift as a typed diff with sameness-graded severity and a `freeze` default, and hosted diff restrictions

**Status:** ratified
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-J1 (consumers: WS-J2, WS-J3, WS-J5, WS-J6, WS-I5, WS-I7, WS-K1, WS-K4, WS-L4) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (iii), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05/-10/-14/-15), ADR-0017 (`HirDiff`, classification, invertibility, widening rule; P2 amendment `validity_delta`), ADR-0018, ADR-0025 (`diff` over sealed definitions; `enabled` ablation; AC-6), ADR-0036, ADR-0037 (sameness ladder L0–L4; lineage storage; rule (c) for hosted), ADR-0045 (`Design` immutable once opened), ADR-0064 (`UpdateAvailable` never re-seals), ADR-0088 (refresh as supersession), ADR-0095 (`freeze | adopt | ask` epochs); OQ-350; R-2.10.1

## Context

ADR-0017 fixes `HirDiff` as the unit of edit and ADR-0025 maps assembly edits onto its ops (`Rebind`, `ReplaceField`, `AddNode`, `RemoveNode`); ADR-0037 grades sameness L0–L4 from the classification. What the Lab still lacks is (i) a *readable* view — an operator comparing two arms, or an optimizer proposing a change, reasons about slots, parameters and caps, not about node-level ops — and (ii) a name for the change that occurs when the *same authored source* is resolved twice under different registry snapshots. The audit shows why (ii) matters: OpenHands pins plugin sources to a commit SHA for resume but has no notion of "what changed since" (`plugin/types.py` L119–150); opencode re-merges every load with no memory of the previous result; goose keys recipes by path. Terraform's plan (S-499) is the mature precedent for (i) — four action classes, a three-valued exit, drift surfaced by a refresh-only plan and never applied implicitly — and ADR-0095 already gives the Lab's catalog-drift policy vocabulary.

## Options considered

1. **Render `HirDiff` ops directly** to operators and optimizers. *Rejected:* node-level ops hide the assembly-level meaning (a `Rebind` on `slots.compaction.variant` is "the compaction strategy changed"); the optimizer's proposal space is slots/params/caps.
2. **A second, stored assembly diff object** with its own apply. *Rejected:* two edit units diverge (the ADR-0017 objection to textual diffs, now at a higher level); attribution would key on two identities.
3. **Treat snapshot drift as an ordinary re-resolution** (silently adopt the newest pins on the next run). *Rejected:* violates ADR-0045 (`Design` immutable once opened), ADR-0064 (a run keeps its pins) and T-LCD-14 (arms would differ by an unlogged factor).
4. **A derived, lossless projection of `HirDiff` with a plan rendering; diffs only over sealed definitions; drift as a typed `HirDiff` reported with sameness-graded severity under a `freeze` default; hosted diffs restricted to claims and coordinates (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`AssemblyDiff = project(HirDiff)`** — a total, injective bucketing of every `DiffOp` into `{slot_rebind, slot_toggle, slot_param_change, value_change, space_change, entity_add, entity_remove, entity_change, edge_change, cap_change, profile_constraint_change, surface_only, ext_only, provenance_only}` keyed by the assembly path the op touches, carrying `classification` verbatim (including the ADR-0017 P2 `validity_delta`), `sameness ∈ {L0..L4}` per ADR-0037, and a Terraform-class rendering (`+` add, `-` remove, `~` change, `-/+` rebind, `≈` surface-only, `·` provenance-only). **T-1** `flatten(project(d)) = d.ops` as a multiset; `apply`/`invert` remain `HirDiff`'s; the projection is never stored (registry lineage stores sealed snapshots plus cached `HirDiff`s, ADR-0037 (4)).
2. **T-2 sealed only.** The service diffs sealed definitions; an authored source is diffed by desugaring and resolving both sides under one snapshot; textual diffs of authored files are never produced by the service.
3. **T-3 snapshot drift.** `drift(source, snap_old, snap_new) = project(diff(resolve(source, snap_old), resolve(source, snap_new)))` — by construction `Rebind`/`ReplaceLeaf` on pinned references only. Reported as `C-REF-6 SnapshotDrift{diff_ref, sameness}` with severity `info` at L1, `warning` at L2, `error` at L3 (widening/loosening/dialect change). Policy: **`freeze` is the only default** — an arm whose `Design` has opened keeps its pins (ADR-0045; ADR-0064 "a run keeps its pins"); `adopt` for *new* arms is an explicit `apply` producing a `HirDiff` with `derived-from{hypothesis: "snapshot adoption", …}` under ADR-0017's widening rule (`origin = evolution` may never adopt L3 drift); `ask` is a surface concern (WS-K1/H7). Lineage-level drift policy is OQ-350.
4. **T-4 hosted.** For `AgentProcess.hosted` the projection admits only `value_change`, `space_change`, `entity_change` on `declared_capabilities`/`supplies`/`budget`/`permissions`, and `participant_version_change` (a *claim* — ADR-0037 rule (c)); `sameness ∈ {L0, L4}`; a `declared_capabilities` flip `supported → unknown | unsupported` is a `warning` because it removes admissible factors (ADR-0013 (4)); DRIFT in the ontology sense (observed ≠ declared) is a WS-J6 observation, not an assembly diff.
5. **T-5 ablation.** `enabled = false` on one binding is exactly one `slot_toggle` (ADR-0025 AC-6); WS-A3 `ablate` is one `entity_remove` with `derived-from{hypothesis: "ablation"}`; both are L2 successors filed in an experiment-scoped namespace (ADR-0037 (b)).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: typed change previews with action classes, a three-valued exit and drift surfaced only by an explicit refresh-only plan (S-499 Terraform); SHA-pinned resolved sources with no "what changed" (S-110 `plugin/types.py` L119–150 — the gap); registry refresh as supersession with a classified `HirDiff` and pinned definitions staying pinned (ADR-0088); `freeze | adopt | ask` as the Lab's drift vocabulary (ADR-0095); identity-keyed structural diff over stable ids (ADR-0017; S-174 GumTree via ADR-0017); sameness computed from classification, never from names (ADR-0037; S-171 Unison via ADR-0036); span/ledger projection as the precedent for a derived view that is never a store (ADR-0042).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/plugin/types.py` L119–150, L181–192 (S-110 @3fc7b22); `packages/opencode/src/config/config.ts` L337–368 (re-merge per load; S-111 @9f8db11); `crates/goose/src/recipe/manifest.rs` L18–24 (S-112 @fae91d0) — all as gaps; `FoundationAgents/AFlow` and `ShengranHu/ADAS` round-as-files with rationale (S-025, S-170 via ADR-0017). The lossless assembly-level projection and typed snapshot drift with sameness-graded severity are `no-precedent / novel`; the plan rendering and freeze default are precedented.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) most optimisation gains may be opaque-leaf edits (S-080, S-083 `provisional`) so the projection would mostly show `entity_change{ReplaceLeaf}` — accepted (ADR-0017 (c)(i)): the bucket still localises the leaf and its hash; (ii) drift reports may be noisy under frequent variant releases — answered by sameness grading (L1 is `info`) and `freeze`; (iii) a derived view can diverge from the stored diff — answered by T-1 as a tested equality (dossier AC-J1-6). None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier and task class; assumes ADR-0037's ladder and lineage storage; hosted rules assume WS-J6's `ParticipantRecord` carries `participant_version` as a claim.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C1 / Stage 3** (`project`, rendering, T-1/T-2/T-5, `C-REF-6` detection with `freeze`); **C1 / Stage 3+** (`adopt` for new arms with WS-J3); **C2 / Stage 4** (T-4 with WS-J6).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies: T-LCD-10 (`surface_only` bucket; rename yields no semantic entry and no identity change), T-LCD-05 (`touches_conditioned_rules` carried; an edit on a conditioned rule surfaces with its debt record), T-LCD-14 (drift never silently changes an opened arm; adoption is a logged diff), T-LCD-15 (hosted diffs report `L0 | L4` and coordinate-level changes only; no component-level pretence). Does not address T-LCD-03/-04/-11/-13. **ADR-0024 rung:** `project`, drift detection and rendering are MUST-code; drift policy per lineage is MUST-data (`freeze` default).

## Consequences

- WS-K1/K4 render `plan.diff` and `plan.drift` with the rendering symbols; WS-I5 proposes candidates as `HirDiff`s and reads them back as `AssemblyDiff`s for human review; WS-I7 attributes on `HirDiff` op targets (unchanged); WS-J3 refuses to open a `Design` whose arms carry unresolved L3 drift; WS-J2 exposes `lineage` with cached diffs so `plan` against a lineage head is a lookup.
- WS-J6 confirms T-4's field list against the final `ParticipantRecord`.

## Reversibility

**High.** The projection is derived; changing bucket names regenerates nothing stored. **Medium** for the `freeze` default (switching to `adopt` would change which sealed definition new arms bind and requires a J3 design rule). No stored identity depends on this ADR.
