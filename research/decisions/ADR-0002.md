# ADR-0002 — Evolution and co-evolution are fully in scope (specified, not stubbed)

**Status:** ratified (pre-ratified by sponsor; seeded at Preflight 2026-09-09 per doc 3 §4, §11.2 step 6)
**Owner workstreams:** WS-I5 (evolution service), WS-I8 (model-harness co-evolution & consolidation); consumers WS-I6, WS-I7, WS-H1
**Supersedes:** —
**Related:** ADR-0001; CF-004; OQ-008, OQ-025, OQ-026; R-2.9.5–R-2.9.8

## Context

Doc 2 §5.8–5.9 identifies harness self-improvement and model-harness co-evolution as the most distinctive 2026 research frontier, and §12 places the "safe adaptive optimizer" and "assumption-debt manager" among the highest-leverage novel contributions. At the same time doc 2 §5.8, §6 and §8 record strong counter-evidence: under matched feedback and inference budgets, automatic evolution does not consistently beat simpler test-time search and transfers weakly (S-083); generated harnesses trail mature human references (S-075); gains can be localized (S-092) and unstable (S-091); and changing the model can invalidate a harness tuned around it (S-096).

The program must decide whether the Canonical Spec *fully specifies* the C4 evolution cluster (evolution service, assumption-debt manager, causal attribution, co-evolution/consolidation) or ships it as a stub/placeholder to be designed later.

## Options considered

1. **Stub C4.** Reserve interface names, defer design. *Rejected by sponsor:* the full-vision mandate (doc 3 §0) requires the whole vision in one spec; stubbing would also leave the security kernel (WS-H1) without the concrete threat model of a self-modifying policy layer, which doc 2 §11 says must be designed *before* autonomous self-modification.
2. **Specify C4 as research-only appendix.** *Rejected:* an appendix escapes the interface-contract/acceptance-criteria/build-stage discipline that makes the spec decomposable.
3. **Fully specify C4 as extension tiers, last on the build ladder, under matched-budget conditionality (chosen).**

## Decision

- The evolution service (governed pipeline: propose → failure hypothesis → counterexample set → matched-budget eval → held-out regression → cross-model compatibility → security-invariant check → canary → rollout → retirement test), the assumption-debt manager, causal attribution/counterfactual execution, and model-harness co-evolution/consolidation are **fully specified** in the Canonical Spec with interface contracts, data models, acceptance criteria, test-matrix hooks, and build-stage assignments — **not stubbed**.
- They are **tier C4**, depend only on Core contracts and declared lower tiers (measurement I1–I3, security H1, durable/reversible runtime B3–B4), and sit **last on the staged build ladder** (Stage 6 per doc 2 §11).
- **Governance constraints are part of the scope, not optional:** (i) proposal is separated from deployment; (ii) the same agent that proposes a safety-critical change may not unilaterally deploy it; (iii) the security kernel, credential mediation, egress policy, sandbox boundaries, extension trust, and audit are *outside* the editable policy surface — the evolution service may propose narrower permissions or an approval request, never enlarge its own authority; (iv) every accepted change is reversible and carries expiry/revalidation.
- **Evidence standard for anything the evolution service claims** (doc 2 §5.8 seven-point standard): matched total compute; held-out tasks and ideally held-out model families; persistent artifact separated from search-time computation; component-level attribution; forgetting/regression; security-policy invariance; beneficiary-model compliance.

## Evidence (per doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** that the harness is an editable compound artifact learnable from trajectories is established across many independent methods (AHE S-061, RHO S-063 [accepted EMNLP Findings], Meta-Harness S-080, Harness-R1 S-081, MemoHarness S-066, DGM S-026 [ICLR 2026], GEPA S-028 [ICLR 2026], AFlow S-015 [ICLR 2025], ADAS S-023 [ICLR 2025]). The *mechanism* (editable representation + trajectory-derived candidates + gated validation) is corroborated by peer-reviewed Tier-A lineage (S-015, S-023, S-026, S-028) and is therefore admissible; the *performance* claims of 2026 preprints remain `provisional`.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** DGM (S-027), AFlow (S-025), CaMeL/Fides artifacts for the security-outside-the-policy-surface pattern (S-100, S-102). The *governed deployment pipeline* with retirement tests is `novel` as an integrated contract (doc 2 §11 "treat evolution as a deployment pipeline"; no complete precedent identified at Preflight — WS-I5 to confirm).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** Rethinking Harness Evolution (S-083): no consistent win over matched test-time search, weak held-out transfer. HarnessDev (S-075): generated harnesses trail human references; unstable evolution. EVOHARNESSBENCH (S-091): retention/adaptation tension. Where-does-value-live (S-092): gains localized. On-policy co-evolution (S-096): full-trajectory imitation can *reduce* performance by breaking model-harness compatibility. **Response:** these do not argue against *specifying* the capability; they dictate its *governance* — matched-budget gates and separation of search-time from artifact benefit are mandatory acceptance criteria, not options.
- **(d) Conditionality:** the *value* of C4 is conditional on model tier, task class, and eval budget; the *specification* is not. C4 acceptance criteria must be expressed relative to a matched-budget baseline, never absolute.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** C4 / Stage 6 (doc 2 §11); co-evolution/consolidation (WS-I8) is the final rung and may be marked `research-grade` in the spec with an explicit maturity flag.

## Consequences

- WS-I5/I6/I7/I8 run in Phase 4 (last research phase) so they inherit stable measurement, security, and durability contracts.
- WS-H1 must design the reference monitor with a self-modifying policy layer as an explicit threat actor.
- The spec's build ladder must make Stage 6 unreachable until Stage 3 (evaluation-first) and the security kernel are complete; `decompose-spec` inherits this ordering.
- The Readiness Report must show that no C0 decision depends on any provisional evolution result (§3.1).

## Conditionality summary

Specification unconditional (sponsor mandate). Deployment value conditional on matched-budget evidence per model/task/budget; each C4 mechanism carries a maturity flag and a revalidation cadence (assumption-debt discipline applied to itself).

## Build-stage

C4 / Stage 6 (last).

## Reversibility

High for the *spec* (a tier can be descoped by a later ADR without touching C0–C3 contracts because C4 depends only on declared lower-tier contracts). Low for *deployed* self-modification without the governance constraints above — which is why they are in scope.
