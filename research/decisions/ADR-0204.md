# ADR-0204 — Consolidation and the co-evolution cycle: never/candidate criteria, consolidation proven by a retirement experiment on the post-training snapshot, `CoEvolutionCycleRecord`/`CyclePolicy`, safety co-evolution scope, maturity `research_grade`

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 4 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I8 (with WS-I5, WS-I6, WS-F4, WS-H1, WS-G3) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002, ADR-0017, ADR-0024, ADR-0037, ADR-0041, ADR-0046 §7, ADR-0047, ADR-0053 D-5, ADR-0059, ADR-0080 (memory consolidation — distinct), ADR-0126, ADR-0139, ADR-0147, ADR-0154…0159, ADR-0181/0182; OQ-025, OQ-026, OQ-281; CF-439/-05; R-2.9.8; T-LCD-05/-09/-14

## Context

Doc 2 §10: "Which runtime lessons should remain explicit and reversible, and which should eventually be distilled into the model? How can consolidation avoid breaking the model-harness compatibility that produced the lesson?" (OQ-026). Doc 2 §5.9: weights are slow, opaque and expensive; harness artefacts fast, inspectable and reversible; a system could "use harness adaptation for rapid local learning and periodically consolidate robust lessons into weights". WHALE (S-085, source read) is the reference loop for the two rates: a weight phase under the current harness, a harness-search phase under the new checkpoint, fixed `(E, I)` or a patience rule, resumable from checkpoints and an `accepted_harness.txt`. SafeEvolve (S-086, source read) shows that safety artefacts are *not* consolidated away: they stay explicit, versioned and reversible while the policy is trained to use them. Trainer-side, on-policy distillation is the consolidation operator (tinker-cookbook `distillation/train_on_policy.py`; verl `teacher_loop`). HarnessHarness cannot observe weights; it can only observe whether the explicit lesson is still needed.

## Options considered

1. **Consolidate by training loss / trainer report.** Rejected: unobservable by HarnessHarness, model-specific, and no evidence about behaviour under the harness (S-088: validity ≠ compliance).
2. **Never consolidate; keep everything explicit forever.** Rejected: contradicts the sponsor's scope (ADR-0002, R-2.9.8) and doc 2 §5.9's "remove scaffolding, not only add it"; explicit lessons carry recurring context cost (opacity ratio, tokens per run).
3. **Consolidation = the lesson's ADR-0126 removal test passing on the post-training snapshot, under retention and held-out-family conditions, with the explicit lesson retained as `retired` (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Never consolidate:** (N1) MUST-code / kernel invariants — permissions, Π, budgets, effect model, validators/oracles, idempotency, containment (ADR-0024 d.1; ADR-0053) — distilling a validator into the policy is reward hacking by construction (S-415); (N2) volatile informational content — memories with dependency stamps, task/repository facts, catalog and pricing snapshots (ADR-0037/0081); (N3) safety artefacts — the explicit safety layer is evolved and trained-to-use, never removed by training (S-086 mechanism); (N4) anything with `authority_delta ≠ none` or that endorses; (N5) hosted-participant artefacts.
2. **Candidate** only if all hold: (C1) `active` ≥ `k_cycles` (MUST-data, default 3) with the removal test *failing* on the current snapshot; (C2) `transfer` sign-stable over ≥ 2 task families and ≥ 2 snapshots/families (ADR-0159); (C3) behavioural rung — `Text` instruction leaf, `Procedure`/skill, conditioned rule of an interaction/reminder/description class, control nudge — never a Core invariant; (C4) economics: recurring explicit cost (opacity share × tokens/run × forecast runs over the snapshot's life) exceeds the trainer's claimed consolidation cost by a MUST-data factor, **or** the lesson is valid but under-followed (`followed` rate below threshold; S-088); (C5) no `veto` invariant references the lesson.
3. **Procedure and proof.** `propose_consolidation(lesson_ids[], snapshot_in) → ConsolidationProposal{export (samples under `definition` with the lessons, `search`/`dev` splits only), regression_suite (retention set + target set + ≥ 1 held-out family), removal_designs[] (one `retirement` experiment per lesson: `definition_out` vs `definition_out − R` on `snapshot_out`, ADR-0156 diff-shape refusal inherited), pre_registration}`. After `import_snapshot` and a `verified | drifted` regression verdict (ADR-0203), each lesson's retirement experiment runs. `ConsolidationReport{lesson_id, verdict ∈ {consolidated, partially_consolidated, not_consolidated}, target_report, retention_report, family_report, compliance_delta}`: `consolidated` iff non-inferior within pre-registered margins on target **and** retention **and** held-out family, no veto tripped, and the lesson is not delivered by any other path in the removal arm; then the lesson transitions `expired → retired{reason: consolidated, snapshot_ref, report_ref}` **by ADR** (ADR-0126 d.2 unchanged). `partially_consolidated` ⇒ stays `active`, `snapshot_scope` narrowed, report attached. **Reversibility:** the retired lesson remains a registry record; a later `broken`/`drifted` record on a newer snapshot re-enters it as a candidate for `expired → active` on fresh evidence (ADR-0126); reactivation is one `HirDiff` (`AddNode` of the retired version). Consolidation changes what is delivered, never what is known.
4. **The cycle.** `CoEvolutionCycleRecord{cycle_id, lineage_ref, policy: CyclePolicy, phases[]: {phase ∈ {harness_search, weight_update}, inputs{definition_in, snapshot_in}, outputs{definition_out?, snapshot_out?}, experiment_refs[] | training_run_ref, budgets{search, eval, training: compute_claim}, verdict}, stop_reason ∈ {budget_exhausted, max_cycles, patience, broken_compatibility, operator}}` is a sidecar record (ADR-0139 S-layer shape) on a `lineage` bundle — **no new `run_kind`, registry kind or design kind** (ADR-0183 §C, ADR-0151, ADR-0154 not re-decided). `CyclePolicy{weight_phase_budget (external claim), harness_phase_budget: SearchBudgetRecord, switch_rule ∈ {fixed{E, I}, patience{min_iters, patience, max_iters}}, max_cycles, retention_set_ref, regression_suite_policy}` is MUST-data (WHALE's loop variables, typed). A harness phase is exactly one WS-I5 pipeline invocation with `snapshot_in` fixed and output `apply(base, HirDiff)` (ADR-0147 M-2) stamped `derived-from.snapshot_refs = [snapshot_in]`; a weight phase is opaque (export → trainer → import → regression suite). On `broken`, the policy selects `re_search` (harness phase under `snapshot_out` — WHALE's order) or `rollback` (records retained, never deleted). The C4 claim "co-evolution vs weight-only vs harness-only" is a three-arm `ComparisonReport` at `MatchSpec{matched_total}` over search + evaluation + *claimed* training compute (`confidence = reported`), rendered only within its confidence stratum (ADR-0041/0046).
5. **Safety co-evolution scope.** Co-evolvable safety surface = `Text` leaves in profile-owned slots, safety `Procedure`s/skills, narrowing-only guards (`GuardVerdict` never `allow`), narrowing `HarnessRule`s; Π, handles, flow policies, broker, containment excluded (ADR-0053 D-5, ADR-0182 X6). Safety rewards exported only from deterministic H-track detectors (LT-01…12, injection/egress/permission vetoes) and utility oracles as decomposed `RewardRecord`s; reasoning-reading critic verdicts never (OQ-281). Post-training regression suites include per-rule compliance of safety rules (valid-but-unfollowed is `drifted`). Every accepted safety edit is a sealed version with a cycle phase row; rollback is `apply(base, invert(diff))`.
6. **Maturity and conditionality.** The whole layer is `maturity = research_grade`: every performance result is `provisional` (single-team, ≤ 9B open-weight beneficiaries, frontier proposers, unmatched-total budgets); no study reports post-training regression suites or per-rule compliance; value on frontier snapshots is unknown. Consequently: no co-evolution or consolidation result may become a leaderboard entry without `BundleStatusRecord.status = reproduced` (independent) and a `matched_total` `ComparisonReport`; the layer's operations are excluded from every C0–C3 acceptance suite; the spec marks R-2.9.8 `specified, research-grade, conditional`.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-05 (consolidation is the removal test; retired lessons keep their records; the layer carries its own debt record and removal test), T-LCD-09/-14 (retirement, regression and the three-arm claim are explicit factorials under `MatchSpec`; training compute is a `reported` stratum), T-LCD-13 (compliance delta is part of every verdict), T-LCD-02 (consolidation lowers the definition's opacity ratio measurably — the ADR-0024 ladder's downward move), T-LCD-10/-12/-15 as in ADR-0202/2. Not applicable: T-01/-03/-04/-06/-07/-08/-11. **ADR-0024 rung:** candidacy checks, verdict function, cycle orchestration MUST-code; N/C thresholds, `k_cycles`, economics factor, `CyclePolicy`, margins MUST-data; the never-list is an ADR-level list.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: S-085 (`provisional`; alternation, either coordinate can be the bottleneck, patience rule), S-084 (`provisional`; alternation from the critic side), S-086 (`provisional`; explicit reversible safety layer + trained use), S-096 (`provisional`; compatibility break — why the cycle re-searches after a weight phase), S-091 (`provisional`; forgetting ⇒ retention condition), S-088 (`provisional`; compliance re-measurement), S-047/S-049 (Tier B; deletion of scaffolding as models improve — consolidation's production motive), ADR-0126 (removal test exists), S-614 on-policy distillation as the trainer-side operator.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `krafton-ai/WHALE` @ `fbe125e` `run/lib/alternate.sh` (`run_weight_phase`/`run_harness_phase`, `ROUND_STEPS`, `ROUND_MH_ITERS`, `ADAPTIVE`, `MH_PATIENCE`, `MAX_ROUNDS`, resumability), `domains/search_qa/meta_harness/meta_harness_search_r1.py` L166–177, L664–679; `MaoPopovich/SafeEvolve` @ `94dc369` `core/skillrl/phase3_online_update.py` `cmd_commit_context` (versioned reversible edits beside `policy_checkpoint`), `docs/method.md` (context stack: default policy > safety prompt > skills); `thinking-machines-lab/tinker-cookbook` @ `1f962ed` `tinker_cookbook/distillation/train_on_policy.py`; `volcengine/verl` @ `483512d` `verl/experimental/teacher_loop/`. Consolidation proven by a retirement experiment with retention and family conditions, and the cycle as a sidecar over ordinary experiments: `novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: S-083/S-075/S-092 (evolution gains inconsistent under matched budgets, unstable, localised); the retirement test can pass because the suite stopped exercising the behaviour, not because weights absorbed it; distilled behaviour may not survive the next update. Does not overturn: the layer is specified not promised; the verdict requires retention and a held-out family; the explicit lesson is retained and reactivation is one diff; every claim is matched-total and stratified by cost confidence.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes a trainable beneficiary with `ledger` observability, a WS-I5 pipeline, the ADR-0126 removal test and ADR-0159 transfer profiles; for frontier snapshots only ADR-0203's provider-drift path applies and consolidation is `n/a`. Value conditional on matched-total evidence not yet in the literature.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C4/Stage 6, after Stage 3, AT-H1-05 and WS-I5's pipeline; the null-trainer fixture (AC-I8-8/-9) is the Stage 6 acceptance; `switch_rule` learning deferred (OQ-025/WS-F4).

## Consequences

WS-I6 gains a retirement reason `consolidated` and consolidation as a scheduled removal-test class; WS-I5's pipeline becomes the harness phase of a cycle; the ontology distinguishes *weight consolidation* from ADR-0080's *memory consolidation* (CF-439); the Readiness Report lists R-2.9.8 as specified-but-research-grade with its conditionality; the build ladder places nothing of this layer before Stage 6 except schemas.

## Reversibility

**High** for the spec (descope by ADR without touching C0–C3); **high** operationally by construction — a consolidated lesson is retired, never deleted, and re-enters on evidence.
