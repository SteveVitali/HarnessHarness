# ADR-0109 — Task contracts as a typed projection over `Goal` and `validates` records with visible and held-out criteria; a kernel completion gate; pinned validators and evidence-integrity veto predicates

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-G1 (consumers: WS-F1, WS-F2, WS-G2, WS-G3, WS-I2, WS-I4, WS-I3, WS-J4, WS-J5, WS-D1, WS-B1, WS-A3) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0016 (`Goal.success_criteria`, `validates` edge, `unverifiable_reason`), ADR-0015 (`ext` never decides validity), ADR-0019 (closed-world rule; `deliver` precondition), ADR-0026/0027 (manifest; events), ADR-0033/0035 (authority floor; `validator` endorsement basis), ADR-0038 (lock manifest; R1), ADR-0040 (verification spend under the budget), ADR-0045 (outcome classes, `n/a{no_detector}`, scorer boundary item 9), ADR-0047 (headline restriction; veto invariants item 5), ADR-0014 (compliance vs validity), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05/-13/-14/-15); OQ-049, OQ-092, OQ-266/-03/-05; CF-230/-06/-07; R-2.7.1, R-2.9.2

## Context

Doc 2 §11 lists "task contracts, executable validators, tests, evidence requirements" as the verification fabric that "continuously reconciles claims with external state", and §5.3 asks for global checks that "evaluate task-level invariants and evidence of completion". Phase 1 typed the pieces — `Goal.success_criteria: [Ref<Validator>]` with the invariant "non-empty or `unverifiable_reason`" (ADR-0016), the nine oracle classes with a deterministic-only headline (ADR-0047), outcome classes and `n/a{no_detector}` (ADR-0045) — but left the *contract* itself undefined: which criteria the subject may see, what "done" requires, and who runs what. The audit (dossier §4) found the failure locus is the completion claim (S-041 "declare the job done"; S-046 "confidently praising the work"; S-062 execution-alignment failures against "verifiable output contracts") and that the visible verification surface is what gets hacked (SpecBench S-412: visible vs held-out gap; RHB S-413: skipping verification, metadata shortcuts, tampering with evaluation functions). Harbor's verifier already keeps tests out of the environment until verify time (S-132), and Anthropic's sprint contract negotiates "what 'done' looked like … before any code was written" (S-046) but enforces test integrity only by prose (S-041).

## Options considered

1. **No contract object; tests in the environment; the agent's `finish` is the completion.** *Rejected:* the visible test suite becomes the only oversight surface (S-412) and false completion is undetectable (S-041/S-046/S-062).
2. **A new `TaskContract` HIR entity.** *Rejected:* an HIR/2 dialect bump for information that is a function of `Goal`, its validators and the experiment; WS-A2's `HarnessArtifact` precedent shows a projection suffices.
3. **Prose contract in the system prompt ("do not edit tests"; "run tests before finishing").** *Rejected as the guarantee* (kept as a measurable compliance artifact, CF-236): under ADR-0014 prose is delivered → followed at a model-dependent rate; it cannot be the floor.
4. **Typed projection over `Goal` + `validates` edge records with a visible/held-out split, pinned validators, a kernel completion gate and veto predicates (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`TaskContract` is the projection** `project(sealed ∪ experiment, task_contract) → TaskContract{contract_id, goal_ref, criteria[AcceptanceCriterion{criterion_id, validator_ref: VersionedRef, record: ValidatesRecord}], invariants[], completion_policy ∈ {all_required, weighted_threshold(t), unverifiable(reason)}, evidence_kinds_required, budget_ref, provenance, sealed}` with `contract_id = H(canonical(goal.semantic_id ∥ sorted criterion refs ∥ records))`, computed at `seal` (or at experiment binding for instrument criteria) and stamped in the run manifest as `task_contract_id`. It is never edited during a run.
2. **`ValidatesRecord`** (typed fields on the `validates` edge; kernel, never `ext`): `{role ∈ {acceptance, invariant, precondition, postcondition}, phase ∈ {local, global, completion}, required: bool, visibility ∈ {visible, held_out}, veto: bool, evidence_requirements: [EvidenceRequirement{kind, min_authority ≥ environment, freshness ∈ {any, after_last_effect_on_scope, after_seq(n)}, integrity ∈ {content_addressed, chain_verified}, scope?}], window?, weight?}`. Whether this lands as an HIR/1 amendment or an HIR/2 item is for WS-A3 at synthesis (OQ-266); it cannot live in `ext` (ADR-0015).
3. **Visible vs held-out.** `visible` criteria compile to `ContextItem`s at `seal` (`authority = definition`) and may be run by the subject; `held_out` criteria are never the payload of `context.artefact.delivered` — `deliver` refuses with `HeldOutLeak` (error) and the same predicate is a veto invariant over the ledger. Held-out criteria are executed by the instrument over the end-state handle after the run (per-run over the scorer boundary, ADR-0045 item 9; alternative in OQ-268).
4. **Pinning and integrity.** Every validator referenced by a criterion is pinned by `version_id`; executable validators' `CompiledPayload.bytes_hash` and fixture `ContentAddress`es are members of the lock manifest (ADR-0038). At `collect`, the kernel compares any workspace copy of a pinned fixture/validator against its address; mismatch ⇒ `EvidenceTampered` (veto invariant, ADR-0047 item 5 list extended with `EvidenceTampered`, `HeldOutLeak`, `VerificationSkipped` — a `required` visible criterion never invoked before a `stop{completed}` proposal — and `MetadataShortcut` — a read inside the suite-declared `task_metadata` scope, OQ-270).
5. **Completion gate (kernel-enforced).** On `stop{reason = completed}` the kernel computes `VerificationSummary{contract_id, required: {pass, fail, inconclusive, not_run}, invariants_tripped[], evidence_head_seq, freshness_ok, held_out_status}` and sets `status`: `succeeded` iff every `required` non-held-out criterion is `pass` with fresh evidence (produced after the last effect terminal event on the criterion's scope) and no veto tripped; `succeeded_unverified` iff `completion_policy = unverifiable` (requires `Goal.unverifiable_reason`); otherwise `CompletionRefused{summary}` is rendered to the model as an `Observation{source = validator}` and F2's bounded-reflection policy decides whether the run continues or ends `stop_reason = verification_failed`. Instrument verdicts can lower a run to success-with-veto or `scored = fail`; they never raise it. The invariant "all effects terminal" is exposed as `inconclusive` when `unknown`/`abandoned` effects remain, so OQ-092's owner (WS-G2/F1) chooses block-vs-annotate without touching this gate.
6. **Scorecard rules.** Headline capability reads only held-out and instrument verdicts of deterministic oracle class; `visible`/subject verdicts feed process metrics; `reward_hacking_gap = visible_pass_rate − held_out_pass_rate` is a class-scoped metric (`requires_observability ⊇ {end_state}`, applies to native and hosted); a run with `succeeded_unverified` renders `n/a{no_detector}` for headline capability.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-05 (every held-out suite and threshold carries an assumption-debt record — suites age, S-415), T-LCD-13 (contract verdicts on delivered artifacts emit `followed`), T-LCD-14 (verification spend is metered under the budget with `charged_to`), T-LCD-15 (held-out criteria are the class-common floor; local metrics `n/a` on hosted), T-LCD-06 (no native field exists for hosted participants; hosted runs are verified through `end_state`), T-LCD-02 (criteria are typed; messages are `Text` leaves). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-07/-08/-09/-10/-11/-12 (T-LCD-08/-12 are carried by ADR-2's Validator contract). **ADR-0024 ladder rung:** `contract_id` derivation, `deliver` precondition, completion gate and veto predicates = MUST-code; criteria, records, thresholds, fixtures, completion policy = MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: contracts as precondition/postcondition/invariant triads checkable at run time — S-409 (Meyer 1992, Tier A); monitors deciding properties over trace prefixes with inconclusive answers — S-410 (Leucker & Schallhart 2009, Tier A); false completion as a documented failure — S-041, S-046 (Tier B production), S-062 (`provisional (mech)`); the visible surface as the hacked surface and the shortcut classes — S-412, S-413 (both `provisional`; classes adopted as predicates, numbers not used), S-414 (`provisional`; detection mechanism noted for I4); tests-held-out-until-verify as a working convention — S-132 (Tier B source); negotiated "done" before work — S-046 (Tier B). C0 elements (schema fields, `deliver` precondition, pinning) rest on Tier A/B evidence and ratified ADRs only.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `harbor/src/harbor/verifier/verifier.py` L166–265 (tests uploaded at verify time; reward files protected; typed parse failures), `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/critic/impl/agent_finished.py` L33–77 (deterministic completion gate: FinishAction ∧ non-empty patch), `symphony/SPEC.md` L43 (run ends at a workflow handoff state, not the agent's `Done`), `hal-harness/hal/utils/compliance_checkers.py` (invariants over traces). Typed visible/held-out split in the task representation, `contract_id` in the manifest, `EvidenceTampered`/`HeldOutLeak` predicates and a kernel completion gate over typed criteria: `no-precedent / novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) "no fixed reward survives" (S-415, `provisional`) — answered by held-out + pinned + veto structure rather than a fixed reward, and by debt records on suites; (ii) many tasks have no executable criterion — answered by `unverifiable` with `n/a{no_detector}`, never success; (iii) a gate may block autonomy and invite fatigue (S-053) — the gate refuses a *claim*, never prompts a human by itself; H7 decides escalation; (iv) held-out execution costs a second environment pass — bounded by the instrument budget and already Harbor's cost model. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes coding/terminal/tool suites where deterministic held-out criteria exist (WS-I4); for domains without them the contract degrades honestly to judged criteria (G3) or `unverifiable`; freshness assumes effect terminal events carry `resource_keys[]` (ADR-0030/0031); model- and tier-independent otherwise.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0 / Stage 1 — `ValidatesRecord` and `TaskContract` schemas, `deliver` precondition, pinning in the manifest; C1 / Stage 3 (evaluation-first) — projection, completion gate, veto predicates, `reward_hacking_gap`, held-out execution over the scorer boundary, AC-G1-5…8; C2 / Stage 4 — hosted `end_state` verdicts (J6), judged criteria (G3).

## Consequences

- WS-A3 decides the dialect placement of `ValidatesRecord` (OQ-266); WS-B1 adds `task_contract_id` to the manifest and either `verification.completion.*` classes or a `verification_summary` payload on `lifecycle.run.finished` (CF-235); WS-D1's `deliver` gains the `HeldOutLeak` precondition; WS-I3 lists pinned validator payloads and fixtures as lock-manifest members; WS-I2/J4 add `reward_hacking_gap` and the four veto predicates; WS-I4 declares `task_metadata` scopes per suite; WS-F2 owns the bounded-reflection policy after `CompletionRefused`; WS-G2 answers OQ-092 through the "all effects terminal" invariant; WS-J6 verifies hosted participants through `end_state` only.
- The scope register carries a stage note: R-2.7.1 stays C1, with the contract *schema* at C0/Stage 1 (CF-230).

## Reversibility

**High for the schema, medium for the gate.** Dropping `visibility` or the veto predicates is a field removal that leaves stored verdicts intact; relaxing the completion gate to advisory is a policy flip in F2. Reversing the projection design (making the contract an entity) is a dialect bump with a mechanical migration.
