**D2 — What crosses each boundary (exact contracts, not "JSON").** Kernel↔lab and kernel↔surfaces carry only: (i) the §6.5 verbs above; (ii) `Event` envelopes (ADR-0026/0029) in the **canonical form of `idp/1`** (JCS-class, integers + decimal strings, no doubles — ADR-0029, ADR-0036), hash-equal on both sides (CF-058 wording of I4); (iii) HIR/1 documents, `HirDiff`s and sealed Harness Definitions as data (ADR-0015/0017/0023) — the lab *composes* by calling the kernel's `compose → resolve → validate_assembly → seal` (one resolver, ADR-0025), never by re-implementing the assembler; (iv) `MetricDeclaration`/`MetricValue`/`ComparisonReport`/`MatchSpec` records (ADR-0045/0046/0041); (v) budget and cost records (ADR-0039/0040/0043); (vi) `project(run, view_kind, until_seq)` results with their `derived_from` watermark. Nothing else crosses: no `RuntimePlan` execution, no permission *decisions* (the far side may only `request_permission`; I5), no raw provider credentials (WS-H3), no unhashed blobs (content addresses only, property 4 of l1-inputs §2.4).

**D3 — Boundary contract adopted.** WS-L1 §6.5 (operations, inputs/outputs, invariants I1–I5 with I4 as reworded by CF-058, failure modes) is a **C0 / Stage 1 item owned by WS-K4 with WS-L5** (ADR-0009 "Boundary contract"; CF-023). I1 single source of truth: the kernel's schema export (HIR/1 + ledger + measurement schemas) is the only hand-written type source; every other binding is generated and CI-checked (S-107 `app-server-protocol/src/export.rs` `GENERATED_TS_HEADER`, S-153 `check:schema:json`). I3 pinning: the lab package pins the exact kernel binary version (S-107 `sdk/python-runtime` "wheel-only … exact Codex CLI version" precedent).

**D4 — Third-party variants and hosted participants** use the same verb discipline through the variant-host boundary (ADR-0023 §5) and the Hosting ABI (ADR-0005); they may be in any ecosystem. Nothing in this ADR restricts the ecosystem of a hosted participant or an out-of-process variant.

**D5 — ADR-0009 is ratified** together with this decision (amendment log in ADR-0009).

**Reading.** The *kernel* choice is stable: an E1 kernel wins 97.9 % of corners and every uniform sample, and survives every widened-band and adversarial sweep at the family level (the adversarial case moves the win from E5a to E5e — both E1-kernel + E2-lab). The *lab* choice is stable: E2 wins the lab layer in every E5 winner. Where E1 (single) wins (158 corners) it is exactly the corners with C1 = 10 *and* C12 = 12; where E2 wins (22 corners) it is C1 = 20 *and* C6 = 8 *and* C12 = 12. What is **not** stable is whether a third ecosystem is bound for surfaces now (E5a) or deferred (E5e): E5a's individual win share is 0.746 (< 0.8), so **tie-breakers apply**:

- **T1 fewer hot-path boundaries:** E5a 0, E5e 0, E1 0 → tie.
- **T2 higher C6:** all three score 5 → tie.
- **T3 higher C1:** E5a 4 = E5e 4 > E1 1 → **E1 (single) eliminated**.
- **T4 cheaper reversibility (per-layer, §6):** E5a and E5e share kernel and lab; they differ only in binding the surface ecosystem now. Binding it now costs nothing kernel-side and can be reversed at zero cost until the kernel↔surfaces boundary is first exercised (Stage ≥ 5); deferring costs nothing either. The cheaper-to-reverse commitment is the **late-bound** one: declare E3 as the surface default (the evidence is one-sided — every audited surface, M2) and let WS-K2/K4 bind it at Phase 3 inside the boundary contract. **Decision: E5a with a late-bound surface layer** (D1). This is a T4 outcome, not sponsor judgment; no "judgment call" clause of step 7 was needed.

**Spike revalidation rule.** If the Stage-0 spike (l1-spike-spec.md) yields C5/C7/C12 scores that differ from the assumed ones by more than the ±1 band for any candidate, steps 5–7 are re-run with the measured values (§6). The adversarial sweep shows the *kernel/lab* outcome survives even the worst case; only the surface binding could move, and it is late-bound.
