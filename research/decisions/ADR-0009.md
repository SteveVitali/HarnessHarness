## Context

Doc 3 §0 constraint 1 defers language/ecosystem selection to an explicit, criteria-driven decision inside the program, ratified at the end of Phase 1 once the IR (WS-A3), compilation model (WS-A4), composition model (WS-A5) and event store (WS-B1) contracts are stable (doc 3 §6, §11.5 step 6). Without a pre-registered procedure, the end-of-Phase-1 decision would be made ad hoc, with weights chosen after seeing candidates, and with Phase 2 workstreams tempted to assume a runtime (RK-09). The WS-L1 dossier (research/dossiers/WS-L1.md) establishes from primary source reading that (a) mature harnesses hand-roll their async loops and import no general agent framework, so framework availability is not a criterion; (b) every audited system with a product surface is already polyglot at some boundary, using one of six recurring boundary mechanisms; (c) protocol SDK coverage (MCP, A2A, ACP) is broad enough that no ecosystem class fails outright but tiers differ; (d) sandbox primitives are OS-level and drivable from any ecosystem, with in-process syscall filtering the exception; (e) eval tooling is concentrated in one ecosystem class but bridgeable.

## Options considered

1. **Decide the language now (Phase 0) on current evidence.** Rejected: violates the sponsor mandate and doc 3 §6; the IR type discipline (Q-L1-01/02), canonical encoding (Q-L1-03), and code-vs-config boundary (Q-L1-09) are unknown and each can flip a hard gate.
2. **Defer without a procedure; let Phase 1 synthesis decide informally.** Rejected: unfalsifiable weights, no sensitivity analysis, no protection against post-hoc weight tuning or language leakage; not mechanically executable by a later agent.
3. **Adopt a single-ecosystem-only framing (exclude polyglot).** Rejected: contradicts the corpus — Codex, goose, prime-agent, OpenHands, opencode, Claude SDK, Omnigent all cross an ecosystem boundary in production; excluding the split would hide the real design variable (where the boundary is, and which mechanism).
4. **Pre-register criteria with weight *ranges*, hard gates, a questionnaire to A3/A4/A5/B1, two mandatory spikes, blind weight freezing, a sensitivity sweep, ordered tie-breakers, and revalidation triggers; treat the polyglot split as a first-class candidate (chosen).**

## Decision

- The language/ecosystem decision is made by the procedure in WS-L1 dossier §6.3, with the criteria and weight ranges in §6.1, the candidate classes E1–E5 in §6.2, and the questionnaire in §6.4. The dossier sections are incorporated by reference; the ADR that ratifies the decision must embed the frozen weights, matrix, sweep and spike results using the data model in §6.6 and satisfy acceptance criteria AC1–AC8 in §6.7.
- **Criteria (weight ranges):** C1 research/eval alignment 10–20; C2 protocol-edge SDK maturity 8–12 (hard gate: no Tier-1/official MCP SDK and no official ACP library ⇒ excluded for the kernel); C3 durability fit 8–14; C4 sandboxing/isolation 8–14 (conditional hard gate if WS-B5/E5 place the sandbox helper in-process); C5 concurrency/streaming 6–12; C6 type-system/IR match 8–15 (conditional hard gate if WS-A3 requires statically checked properties); C7 performance/footprint 3–8; C8 FFI to serving stacks 2–6; C9 web-tooling affinity 3–8; C10 community/hiring/agent-codability 5–10; C11 maintenance/supply chain 5–10; C12 boundary cost 5–12 (penalty applied to polyglot candidates).
- **Candidates:** E1 compiled memory-safe systems ecosystem; E2 managed-runtime dynamically-typed research ecosystem; E3 managed-runtime gradually-typed web-native ecosystem; E4 managed-runtime statically-typed enterprise ecosystems; E5 polyglot split with named kernel/lab/surface/sandbox-helper ecosystems and a named boundary mechanism per boundary (subprocess+JSON-RPC stdio; localhost HTTP/WS with generated clients; schema-first codegen; in-process FFI; embedded engine; protobuf/gRPC).
- **Decider:** the sponsor ratifies; the Phase 1 synthesis agent (or a WS-L1 ratification subagent) executes and recommends; WS-A3, WS-B1 and WS-H1 (via B5/E5) hold hard-gate vetoes limited to C6, C3, C4 respectively.
- **Procedure invariants:** weights frozen and logged before candidate evidence is examined; every score carries a citation; two time-boxed throwaway spikes (kernel slice; boundary slice) supply C5/C7/C12 numbers; a sensitivity sweep over the weight hull reports win share; win share ≥ 0.8 decides, otherwise tie-breakers T1 fewer hot-path boundaries → T2 higher C6 → T3 higher C1 → T4 cheaper reversibility → sponsor judgment.
- **Neutrality enforcement:** until the ratifying ADR lands, every dossier/ADR is grep-audited for language commitments (RK-09) and violations are logged in `conflicts.md`; WS-L1 is the only workstream permitted to name languages, and only as evidence.
- **Boundary contract:** if the decision yields any ecosystem boundary (E5, or a sandbox helper/surface in another ecosystem), the spec adopts the boundary contract in dossier §6.5 (same verb set as the ADR-0001 thin ABI; single source of truth + generated bindings + CI check; version handshake; exact pinning; byte-identical events; no authority widening) as a C0/Stage 1 item owned by WS-K4 with WS-L5.
- **Revalidation triggers:** re-run the procedure if WS-A3 amends the IR type discipline; if WS-B5/E5 move the sandbox helper across the process boundary; when WASI 1.0 ships; if a Tier-1 protocol SDK relied upon is deprecated.

- **(b) Source-code precedent:** boundary mechanisms and their mitigations — `openai/codex` `codex-cli/bin/codex.js`, `sdk/typescript/src/exec.ts`, `sdk/python-runtime/README.md`, `codex-rs/app-server-protocol/src/export.rs`, `codex-rs/app-server-transport/src/lib.rs`, `codex-rs/exec-server/README.md`, `codex-rs/linux-sandbox/src/landlock.rs`, `codex-rs/code-mode-runtime/Cargo.toml`; `aaif-goose/goose` `crates/goose-sdk/Cargo.toml` (uniffi), `vendor/v8/Cargo.toml`, `ui/desktop/src/gooseServe.ts`; `badlogic/pi-mono` `packages/protocol/README.md`; `anomalyco/opencode` `packages/sdk/js/package.json`, `packages/opencode/src/acp/agent.ts`; `OpenHands/software-agent-sdk` `openhands-workspace/openhands/workspace/docker/workspace.py`, `clients/typescript/package.json`, `openhands-agent-server/openhands/agent_server/conversation_lease.py`; `omnigent-ai/omnigent` `omnigent/acp_cli_harnesses.py`; `PrimeIntellect-ai/prime-agent` `prime-agent-runtime/src/rlm/`; `anthropics/claude-agent-sdk-python` `_internal/transport/subprocess_cli.py`; `microsoft/autogen` `protos/agent_worker.proto`; `agentclientprotocol/agent-client-protocol` `schema-generator/src/main.rs`; `modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol` `schema/2026-07-28/schema.ts`. The *pre-registered, sensitivity-swept decision procedure* itself is `no-precedent / novel` in this domain (no audited project documents how it chose its ecosystem).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) S-074 (`provisional`): loop sophistication does not predict benchmark outcomes — implies the choice is a cost, not capability, decision; addressed by the sensitivity sweep, which reveals low stakes as a high win share. (ii) Eval tools bridge to external agents over containers/stdio (Inspect, Harbor), weakening C1 — addressed by a wide C1 range and Q-L1-13. (iii) The AIOS `aios-rs` scaffold stalled ("No async runtime yet… No FFI bridge") and OpenHands pins ACP after a minor-version break — polyglot cost is real even for large teams; addressed by C12 as an explicit penalty and by the boundary contract's codegen/pinning invariants. (iv) Sandbox primitives are reachable from any ecosystem (Claude Code drives Seatbelt/bubblewrap without in-process bindings) — addressed by splitting C4 into "drive" (any) vs "host in-process" (conditional gate). None overturns the need for a pre-registered procedure; each shaped a range or a gate.
- **(d) Conditionality:** the procedure is unconditional; individual weights are conditional on Phase 1 answers (Q-L1-01…14). The decision it produces assumes the coding/terminal task domain chosen by the sponsor (doc 3 §2.9) and the two-participant-class positioning (ADR-0001); if the program's primary environment class changes (e.g. browser/computer-use first), C4 and C8 ranges must be re-derived.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** program-level (outside C-tiers); a prerequisite for Stage 0 of the build ladder and for `decompose-spec`; the boundary contract, if triggered, is C0 / Stage 1.

## Consequences

- Phase 1 workstreams A3, A4, A5, B1 must answer their questionnaire items in their dossiers, language-neutrally; WS-I2 answers Q-L1-13; WS-A1 answers Q-L1-11.
- Phase 1 synthesis schedules the two spikes and the ratification pass after the four contracts are ratified; if any precondition fails, Phase 2 starts language-neutral and the decision is retried at the Phase 2 gate.
- WS-L6 (packaging/licensing) remains blocked on the ratifying ADR; WS-K1–K4 and WS-L5 may proceed on the boundary contract shape regardless of the outcome.
- The readiness report must include the frozen weights, the citations spot-check, and the language-leak audit result.

## Reversibility

**High for this ADR** (it is a procedure; amending weights or gates before ratification costs a synthesis pass). **Medium-to-low for the decision it produces**: reversing a kernel-ecosystem choice after Stage 1 costs a rewrite of the event store, control envelope, reference monitor and protocol edges; reversing a *lab* or *surface* ecosystem choice is cheap if the boundary contract (§6.5) is honored, because those layers only consume generated bindings. The procedure therefore records reversibility per layer (data model §6.6) and uses it as tie-breaker T4.
