# ADR-0087 — The capability record: `ToolCapability` semantic/surface field discipline, closed precondition domains and scope bindings, observation contract, and no second risk declaration

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-E1 (consumers: WS-E2, WS-E3, WS-E4, WS-E5, WS-H1, WS-H5, WS-H7, WS-D1, WS-C3, WS-A4, WS-B5, WS-J2, WS-I3) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0015 (leaves, `ext`), ADR-0016 (item 6 `ToolCapability`; `EffectClass`; V-EFF), ADR-0017 (`SurfaceEdit`), ADR-0019 (closed-world rule; tool table), ADR-0020 (profile-owned surface fields), ADR-0021 (MCP target), ADR-0022 (E1–E7; `SurfaceArgMap`), ADR-0024 (MUST-data), ADR-0029 (payload offload), ADR-0030/0031 (effect lifecycle; derived risk class — CF-104), ADR-0033/0034/0035 (authority minting; P7 lifting; R-TEXT), ADR-0039 (kernel dimensions), ADR-0045 (`n/a` never 0), ADR-0048 (spellings), ADR-0050 §8; CF-104, CF-041, CF-047; CF-191, CF-193, CF-194; OQ-055, OQ-101, OQ-106, OQ-219, OQ-223; R-2.5.1

## Context

ADR-0016 ratified `ToolCapability` as the plane-2 entity with a sketch field list (`purpose, input_schema, output_schema, effects, preconditions, resources, cost_model, risk_class, observation_kind` + profile-owned surface fields) and delegated its completion to WS-E1; phase-1 §6.9 adds "E1 carries `preconditions` as checkable domains". Since then CF-104 made the effect risk class a *derived projection* of `EffectAttributes`, WS-H1 fixed that authority handles match `ResourcePattern`s against canonical capability parameters (H-5) without saying which parameters are scope-bearing, and ADR-0033 fixed how a tool result's authority is minted. The source audit (dossier §4) shows every production harness keys metadata off the model-facing name, stores effect hints that are untrusted for authority, rewrites schemas and descriptions per model outside the tool, and handles truncation/offload/error typing ad hoc per tool. This ADR fixes the record so that one capability record lowers to many surfaces (T-LCD-01/-10), its declarations are checkable (V-EFF, E1–E3, H-5) and its results are governed (ADR-0029/0033).

## Options considered

1. **The provider tool schema is the capability (Codex `ToolSpec`, opencode `Tool.Def`, MCP `Tool`).** *Rejected:* identity == name (T-LCD-10 failure); no effect domain, scope or precondition the kernel can check; one record per provider dialect (T-LCD-01 failure); descriptions become authority-bearing (tool-poisoning surface).
2. **MCP `Tool` + `ToolAnnotations` as the canonical record, with `_meta` for extras.** *Rejected:* annotations are hints by the protocol's own rule and lack the `domain` axis a `Permission` grants on (CF-041); `_meta` is an extension slot, not a kernel field; adopted instead as the *lowering target* (ADR-0021).
3. **A free-form metadata map (`meta: dict`, OpenHands; `ext` only).** *Rejected:* `ext` never decides authority, budget or validity (ADR-0015); an open map cannot be validated, diffed by class or ablated.
4. **Closed-kernel record with a semantic/surface split, closed sums for preconditions/scope kinds/error classes, derived risk class, and provenance-stamped declarations (chosen).** Everything the kernel, compiler or Lab reads is a typed semantic field; everything the model reads is a profile-owned surface field.
5. **Option 4 plus a second, tool-authored risk field (WS-A3's `risk_class`).** *Rejected:* CF-104 ruled one declaration; a second field invites disagreement between the two and gives a lifted tool a lever to lower risk.

## Decision

1. **Field discipline.** `ToolCapability.semantic = {purpose: Text, input_schema: SchemaDoc, output_schema?: SchemaDoc, effects: set<EffectClass> | pure, preconditions: [PreconditionDomain], scope_bindings: [ScopeBinding] | scope_bindings_unknown, resources: {declared, keys}, observation_contract, cost_model?, execution_requirement, source: CapabilitySource, exposure_hint}`; `ToolCapability.surface = {name, namespace, description_template: Text, argument_names_and_order, examples, error_format, result_renderer, strictness, schema_dialect_narrowing, exposure_mode, display_title, icon_ref}`. `semantic_id` hashes `semantic` minus `exposure_hint` and minus `cost_model.measured_ref`; `version_id` hashes everything. There is **no** `risk_class`, `idempotent`, `reversible` or `read_only` field: the effect risk class is `project_risk(effects)` per CF-104 and re-assessed by the kernel (ADR-0031). CF-191 asks synthesis to reword ADR-0016 item 6 accordingly (no dialect bump).
2. **Precondition domains are a closed sum:** `param_domain(schema_fragment) | path_within(param_path, ResourcePattern | writable_roots | environment_root) | host_within(param_path, HostPattern | definition_allowlist) | env_requires(EnvironmentRequirement) | state_requires(Ref<Validator>) | capability_requires(Ref<ToolCapability>)`. Static kinds are checked by the compiler (E3 inclusion) and by the kernel's argument analysis (WS-H1 check 3); `env_requires` at `bind`; `state_requires` at `prepare`; `capability_requires` at `link`. Violation is a refusal (`PreconditionViolated` → `action.effect.refused`), never a warning.
3. **Scope bindings.** `ScopeBinding = {param_path, scope_kind ∈ {fs_path, host, recipient, spend_amount, secret_ref, process_target, memory_scope, resource_key}, canonicalization}`. Totality: every scoped effect domain in `effects` has ≥ 1 binding, or the record declares `scope_bindings_unknown` and can be covered only by unscoped grants. The kernel matches `Permission.grants[].scope` and authority-handle scopes against the canonical values these bindings select (through `SurfaceArgMap`, ADR-0022 E2) — resolving CF-193.
4. **Observation contract.** `{content_class ∈ {public, sensitive, secret_bearing}, size_class, offload_policy (default = ADR-0029 threshold), streaming, error_classes ⊆ closed ErrorClass sum}`; `error_classes` makes ADR-0022 E5 checkable; `partial_application` is mandatory for any capability record that may return `observed(partial)` (ADR-0030). The result's `Observation.authority` is derived by the kernel — `environment` iff every effect has `world = closed` ∧ `output_schema` is closed ∧ `source ∈ {native_variant, pinned mcp_listing}`; else `external`; lifted/unpinned sources `unverified` — resolving CF-194; the capability record never sets it.
5. **Declarations carry provenance and cannot self-elevate.** `effects`, `preconditions`, `scope_bindings`, `cost_model` are `definition`-authority for `native_variant` sources at `seal`; `unverified` for `mcp_listing` (ADR-0034 P7) and `participant_supplied`; `unverified` declarations project to `unknown_domain` / risk `{irreversible, non_idempotent, external}` and can only be raised by a WS-H5 `pin` over the `listing_hash` (ADR-0035 basis `pin`). `purpose` and `description_template` are `Text` leaves under R-TEXT (ADR-0033): lifted descriptions are `external` and render only into roles ≤ `role_map(external)`.
6. **Invariants V-E1-1…10** (dossier §6.2) are `validate`/`register` errors: non-empty effects or `pure`; closed sums only; scope totality; parameter paths exist; no model identity outside `surface`; `Text` leaves with provenance; lifted ⇒ `unverified`; `exact` cost only with `measured_ref`; identity excludes surfaces/hints/provenance; composite effects equal member effects.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: hints are untrusted for authority and defaults are conservative — S-153 (`schema.ts` L1904–1910; `tools.mdx` L305–306), S-110 (`tool.py` L215–249 defaults `destructiveHint=True`); a closed harness-owned effect kind drives permissions and parallelism — S-119 (`tools.ts` L1104–1131); tri-state resource declaration — S-110 (`tool.py` L251–282); model-specific surfaces are selected outside the tool — S-107 (`spec_plan.rs` L1255–1258; `models.json`), S-111 (`registry.ts` L340–350), S-119 (`getSchema(modelId)`), S-049 ("we provision each model with the tool format it had during training"); descriptions are a learned performance surface — S-367 (documentation vs demonstrations), S-037; schema narrowing is target-conditioned and lossy — S-112 (`tool_schema_normalize.rs` L1–80), S-107 (`responses_api.rs` L35–38 strict rule); result truncation/offload and typed errors — S-111 (`truncate.ts` L11–40; `tool.ts` L24–37), S-110 (`tool.py` L560–640), S-037 (`response_format`, actionable errors), S-049 (error taxonomy); descriptions as an injection surface — S-310 (mechanism; S-370/-06 `provisional`, corroborating). Ratified basis: ADR-0016 (`EffectClass`), CF-104 (derived risk), ADR-0033/0034/0035 (minting, P7, R-TEXT, `pin`), ADR-0022 (E2/E3/E5), ADR-0029 (offload), ADR-0031 (monotone risk).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `openai/codex` `codex-rs/tools/src/{tool_executor.rs L51–130, responses_api.rs L16–44}`, `core/src/tools/spec_plan.rs L1255–1258`; `OpenHands/software-agent-sdk` `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/tool/tool.py L215–282, L347–470, L660–800`; `anomalyco/opencode` `packages/opencode/src/tool/{tool.ts, truncate.ts, registry.ts L340–390}`; `google-gemini/gemini-cli` `packages/core/src/tools/tools.ts L390–480, L1094–1135`; `aaif-goose/goose` `crates/goose/src/agents/{platform_tools.rs, tool_schema_normalize.rs}`; `modelcontextprotocol` `schema/2026-07-28/schema.ts L1900–2016`. **`no-precedent / novel`** for `scope_bindings`, the closed `PreconditionDomain` sum, the derived result-authority rule, and semantic identity of a tool excluding its name.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: "the schema is the tool" (every audited harness) — does not overturn because the record *contains* the canonical schema and the audit shows the rendering is already rewritten per model outside the tool; misdeclaration is the norm (S-110 defaults, S-112 `destructive(true)` on a scheduler) — does not overturn because the kernel's assessment is monotone and lifted declarations cannot lower anything (ADR-0031), and E4 tests native declarations at Stage 3; arbitrary real-world preconditions exceed any closed sum — does not overturn because `state_requires(Validator)` covers the remainder as a declared runtime check, and E3 marks it `n/a (runtime_validator)`; a lifted tool could game `scope_bindings` — does not overturn because lifted bindings are `unverified` and match only unscoped grants. S-369/-05/-06 and S-076 are `provisional` and not load-bearing.
- (d) Conditionality: model- and task-independent; the `ScopeBinding.scope_kind` list assumes the ADR-0016 initial `EffectDomain` list (coding/computer-use); other domains extend both by dialect bump (OQ-055); `content_class` enforcement beyond carriage is C2 (WS-H2).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (record schema, invariants, `project_risk`, `required_grants`); Stage 2 (`observation_contract` offload, `env_requires` at `bind`); Stage 3 (E4/E5 fixtures, MCP lift with `unverified`); C2 (`capability_requires`, `procedure` sources, taint enforcement).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-01 (surface fields profile-owned; AC-E1-4), T-LCD-02 (`purpose`/`description_template` are `Text` leaves), T-LCD-06 (`participant_supplied` is a source kind of the IR's own record; no field exists for hosting), T-LCD-07 (lifted declarations `unverified`, never coerced), T-LCD-10 (AC-E1-2), T-LCD-11 (fields lower through `_meta`; loss reported by ADR-0021), T-LCD-12 (record is data; checks are operations), T-LCD-13 (tool surfaces are delivered artifacts with a surface hash), T-LCD-15 (`estimate_error` class-scoped). Not applicable: T-LCD-03/-04 (executable; owned with WS-A4/E2/I2), T-LCD-05/-08/-09/-14. ADR-0024 rung: every field is MUST-data; `validate`/`project_risk` are MUST-code.

## Consequences

- ADR-0016 item 6 is reworded (CF-191): drop `risk_class`, `observation_kind`; add `scope_bindings`, `observation_contract`, `execution_requirement`, `source`, `exposure_hint`; `preconditions` becomes the closed sum. HIR/1 wording only.
- WS-H1 adds `UnscopedParameter` beside `UnmappedArgument` (CF-193); WS-A4's hint projection is restated over `attributes` (CF-192); WS-D1 stamps result authority from the §6.2 derivation (CF-194).
- WS-E2 owns everything in `surface`; WS-C3 profile rules are the only writers; a `SurfaceEdit` on a tool is a profile experiment.
- Every tool in the Stage-0 baseline is authored as a capability record; the reference runtime's built-ins (`read_file`, `edit_file`, `execute`, `search`) are the first records and the T-LCD-01 fixture.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Adding sums (scope kinds, error classes, precondition kinds) is a dialect bump (cheap). Re-admitting a tool-authored risk field or moving the name into identity would unpick CF-104, T-LCD-10 and the H-5 evaluation path — expensive in program terms, trivial in bytes.
