# ADR-0188 — The value-of-compute scheduler is a bind-only `compute_policy` component inside the ratified control seam: closed `ComputeOption` sum, `ComputeDecisionRecord` and `control.compute.decided`, invariants B-1…B-6 (never proposes, refuses, grants, widens or owns β), `static` as the packaged null, hosted participants `n/a`

**Status:** ratified
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-F4 (consumers: WS-F3, WS-F1/F2, WS-C2, WS-G3, WS-B4, WS-B1, WS-I1, WS-I2, WS-J3/J4, WS-K2, WS-I5, WS-I6) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0103 (control seam; driver loop — amendment requested), ADR-0106/0107 (envelope; reservation sizing hand-off), ADR-0040 (budget nodes; "F4 as a policy over `allocate/reserve`"), ADR-0039/0043 (`harness_overhead.*`), ADR-0089 (`estimate`), ADR-0115…0117 (critic placements; the four inputs), ADR-0121…0123 (router guard; roles; ensembles), ADR-0133/0134 (branches; `SpeculationPolicy`), ADR-0109 (task contract), ADR-0053 D-5 (no widening), ADR-0012 decision 6 (β), ADR-0026 (event table — CF-401), ADR-0007 (T-LCD); OQ-012, OQ-025, OQ-098, OQ-292, OQ-419/-03/-06/-07/-08; CF-399/-02/-03/-05/-06/-07; R-2.6.4 (primary), R-2.6.3, R-2.3.2, R-2.7.3

## Context

Doc 2 §10 and §12 (direction 6) name value-of-compute orchestration as an open problem — "when is a subagent, alternate model, evaluator, or longer search worth its marginal tokens, latency, and coordination risk?" — and observe that multi-agent use "currently relies heavily on heuristics". The audit confirms this at the source level: allocation is prose (Anthropic's orchestrator "effort scaling rules", S-036), static configuration (Claude SDK `AgentDefinition{model, effort, maxTurns}`, S-109 `types.py` L87–104; Codex `multi_agent_reasoning_effort`, S-107 `models.json` L23) or a capacity gauge (Codex `AgentExecutionLimiter`, `execution.rs` L14–98); no audited harness estimates marginal value before spending, and none records the options it did not take. The mechanism evidence that allocation matters is strong and two-sided: the compute-optimal allocation varies with input difficulty (S-566), and more calls can *lower* accuracy (S-453, Tier A); HAL finds more effort often hurts (S-135). Every lever the scheduler could pull already exists as a field of a ratified record — `delegate.budget_slice` (ADR-0103/0040), a model call's role and effort (ADR-0121; vendor effort ladder S-577), `verify.validator_refs` and critic placements (ADR-0117), `EnsembleProcedure.members[].count` (ADR-0123), a branch's `budget_fraction` (ADR-0134), `retry.attempt` (ADR-0107) — and ADR-0103 fixes that control has exactly three parties (strategy proposes, envelope checks, driver executes) with closed decision and decision-point sums. ADR-0040 (e) already places "WS-F4 value-of-compute as a policy over `allocate/reserve`" at Stage 4–6; ADR-0117 decision 7 says "WS-F4 owns the estimator"; ADR-0121 says WS-F4 "may call `select` but never bypasses the guard". The Replay Gap (S-454, `provisional (mech)`) shows an allocation decision rewrites the trajectory, so decisions must be recorded live with their alternatives or they are unattributable.

## Options considered

1. **Fold value-of-compute logic into each control-strategy variant.** Rejected: duplicates the estimator across `react/plan_execute/workflow/program`, makes the allocation policy unablatable as a factor (T-LCD-09), and lets a variant own β-adjacent spend decisions invisibly.
2. **Make the scheduler an envelope guard (a "value guard") that refuses low-value spend.** Rejected: ADR-0106 decision 2 — the envelope "never chooses the next action"; refusing a spawn on value grounds *is* choosing; it would also make a `provisional`-evidence heuristic part of Core.
3. **Make the scheduler a fourth control party with its own decision kinds (`spawn`, `sample`, `escalate_effort`).** Rejected: grows the closed `ControlDecision`/`DecisionPoint` sums (CF-107 dialect bump) for levers that are already parameters of existing decisions; creates a second owner of β.
4. **Scheduler as a gateway/serving-side feature (best-of-n, effort by the provider).** Rejected: unattributable (one `model_call_id` hides k charges — ADR-0039 R-ACC-2, ADR-0123 option 2).
5. **A bind-only policy component inserted between `decide` and `envelope.check` that binds unbound parameters, tightens bound ones, advises model-owned points through a measured artifact, records every option considered, and defaults to a `static` null (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Component.** `compute_policy` is a registered component class (`home = control plane`, cardinality `exactly-one`, `required_inputs ⊇ {ModelProfile, ResourceAccount}` — T-LCD-08) bound in `AgentProcess.native.slots["compute_policy"]`; the packaged default variant is `static`, which binds nothing, runs no estimator and appends no event (AC-F4-1: byte-identical ledger modulo ids). Registration is under ADR-0023/0151 with a `VariantRecord`; `capabilities(variant) → {options_supported, decision_points ⊆ {propose, delegate, verify, retry, stop}, makes_model_calls, estimator_ref, requires{task_value, priors}, deterministic}`.
2. **Placement — the bind step.** ADR-0103 decision 4's driver loop becomes `(state, d) ← decide; d′ ← compute_policy.bind(d, ctx); verdict ← envelope.check(d′); append control.decision{…}; append control.compute.decided{…} (when d′ ≠ d or options were considered)`. The envelope, `authorize`, the stop protocol and every ADR-0106 enforcement point are unchanged (CF-400).
3. **Closed option sum.** `ComputeOption = {continue_as_configured, spawn_subagent{spec_ref, slice}, extend_search{kind ∈ {additional_sample, additional_branch, retry_task, continue_generation}}, evaluator{placement_ref}, effort_level{level}, alternate_role{role, quality_target?}, stop_now{proposed_reason}}`; extension by dialect bump. `stop_now` is advice to the β-owner of `stop`, or a binding of `stop_rule` parameters a strategy exposes; it is never an owner value (ADR-0103 I5/I6).
4. **Invariants.** B-1 `d′.kind = d.kind ∧ d′.decision_point = d.decision_point ∧ d′.owner = d.owner` (β untouched; `boundary_drift` must stay ∅). B-2 every bound parameter was unbound, or is tightened (`slice′ ≤ slice`, `k′ ≤ k`, `effort′ ≤ effort`; a step-up in effort is admissible only as a declared rule on a recorded failure streak and only within the profile's declared ladder). B-3 `bind` projects `reserve` (ADR-0040) for `d′` and returns `Unchanged` rather than a binding the envelope would refuse; `bind` never calls `amend` (ADR-0040 decision 6) and never touches `authorize` or converts `ask → allow` (ADR-0053). B-4 every option in `options_supported` appears in `options_considered[]` with a `MarginalValueEstimate` or a typed infeasibility `{gauge_at_cap, insufficient_budget, undeclared_parallelism, no_placement, calibration_inactive, capability_unmet, prior_unknown, policy_excluded}`. B-5 `bind` reads no `Text` leaf and no `model_claim` except as a labelled feature of a C4 estimator (companion ADR-0189); lifted preferences are `authority ≤ external` and may only exclude. B-6 `bind` is pure over `ctx` (equal inputs ⇒ equal `d′` and record minus ids), so `deterministic_replay` variants stay replayable (ADR-0028).
5. **Advice for model-owned points.** `advise(decision_point, ctx) → ContextItem{kind: compute_advice}` is rendered through the Model Profile by a `HarnessRule` and measured through `context.artefact.delivered/activated` + `verification.artefact.followed{kind: compute_advice}` (T-LCD-13); it never affects `bind`.
6. **Record and events.** `ComputeDecisionRecord{record_id, decision_id, decision_point, options_considered[{option, estimate | infeasible{reason}}], chosen, binding: map<param, value>, estimator_ref{variant_ref, version_id}, rules_fired[], priors_used[{cell, n, mean, interval, valid_until}], inputs_read[] + inputs_digest, rationale: RationaleRecord{objective_value_per_option, lambda, task_value_ref}, cost_of_estimation: ResourceVector}` (content-addressed) is the payload of the new ledger class `control.compute.decided` (plane `control`, `durability = ledger`, min observability `ledger`, hosted lowering `none`); `control.compute.prior_reset{cell_family, reason}` is the second class; the run manifest gains `compute_policy_ref` and `compute_decisions` (CF-401). `compute_decision_outcome(run)` is a `project()` view joining each record to the outcome class, verdicts and consumption that followed — never a stored score (ADR-0026/0042). `explain(record_ref)` is a projection for WS-K2/J4.
7. **Levers and their owners.** `spawn_subagent` binds `delegate.budget_slice` (F3 executes; ADR-0040 `allocate(mode = slice)`); `effort_level`/`alternate_role` set the call's effort and role/quality target and the router's `select` decides admissibility under G-1…G-7 (ADR-0121; a cross-profile change is a `relower` with `routing.deviation = true`, ADR-0122); `evaluator` binds `verify.validator_refs` only to placements declared in the sealed definition with an `active` `CalibrationRecord` (ADR-0117 decisions 2/7); `extend_search{additional_sample}` binds an `EnsembleProcedure` member count `≤ k_max` (ADR-0123); `extend_search{additional_branch}` binds a branch's `budget_fraction` within `SpeculationPolicy` (ADR-0134); `retry_task` binds `retry.attempt` continuation within `RetryPolicy` (ADR-0107). When a non-`static` policy is bound, its estimate replaces ADR-0107 decision 6's reservation-sizing term under `pool` (permitted there; CF-404).
8. **Task value.** The task contract (ADR-0109) gains `TaskValue{success_value, failure_cost | from_reversibility, currency}` defaulted at `seal` (CF-405; OQ-419); the policy's objective is `ΔP̂(success) × success_value − λ·cost − P̂(harm)·failure_cost` with λ MUST-data on the arm.
9. **Hosted participants.** No native scheduling inside a hosted participant; only the ADR-0040 boundary budget; `scheduling.*` metrics `n/a{class}`; no Hosting ABI verb changes (T-LCD-06/-07/-15).
10. **Governance.** Every `compute_rule` threshold is MUST-data with an `AssumptionDebtRecord`; an evolution-authored `HirDiff` raising `child_floor`, `k_max` or `budget_share_cap` above the sealed value classifies `budget_delta = loosening` and is rejected (ADR-0053 D-5); adopting a non-`static` default requires the companion ADR-0190 `ComparisonReport`.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-01 (no per-model branch; model-keyed thresholds are conditioned rules), T-LCD-02 (advice is a typed `ContextItem`; no `Text` read), T-LCD-05 (every rule carries a debt record; `retirement` experiments delete rules), T-LCD-06/-07/-15 (hosted untouched; `n/a{class}`), T-LCD-08, T-LCD-09 (`compute_policy` is a component-level factor; budget × policy interaction estimable), T-LCD-10 (variant identity content-addressed; option names are surface), T-LCD-11 (preferences lower to MCP `ModelPreferences` with declared loss via ADR-0121), T-LCD-12 (operations over canonical records; out-of-process implementable), T-LCD-13 (`compute_advice` chain), T-LCD-14 (adoption only by matched-budget `ComparisonReport` — ADR-0190). Does not itself satisfy T-LCD-03/-04 (the `static` null preserves the Stage-3 obligations of the reference harness). **ADR-0024 rung:** the bind step, B-1…B-6, the record schema and the event classes are MUST-code; option thresholds, λ, `TaskValue` defaults and slot choice are MUST-data; policies are registered variants.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** compute-optimal allocation is input-dependent (S-566, Tier C) and non-monotone in calls (S-453, Tier A; S-135, Tier A; vendor doc S-577 "`max` … adds significant cost for relatively small quality gains"); spawning is the dominant multiplier and is budgeted by prose or caps in production (S-036; S-107 `execution.rs`, `models.json` L23; S-109 `types.py`); effort is a closed, role-shaped, profile-conditioned ladder with a cache side effect (S-577; S-107); a decision must be recorded with its alternatives because replay-scored allocation rankings ≈ 0 similarity to live (S-454, `provisional (mech)`; ADR-0122 already refuses `replayed_trajectory`); the seam and the levers exist as ratified records (ADR-0103/0040/0107/0117/0121/0123/0134). No `provisional` claim is load-bearing: the contract asserts a shape and invariants, not a lift.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** codex `codex-rs/core/src/agent/control/execution.rs` L14–98 (fan-out capacity gauge, `AgentLimitReached`), `control/spawn.rs` L104 (child inherits context, not usage), `control/service_tier.rs` (tree-wide tier), `models-manager/models.json` L23 (`multi_agent_reasoning_effort`) @ 0735c51; claude-agent-sdk-python `src/claude_agent_sdk/types.py` L37, L87–104 @ ad77094 (static per-subagent effort/model/turns — the `static` null); litellm `litellm/router_strategy/adaptive_router/bandit.py` L112–137 @ c1a83fc (allocator returning one choice per call; served-model-only attribution — the negative precedent for recording options); dspy `dspy/predict/best_of_n.py` L50–83 @ ca54a85 (threshold stop as a bound parameter of a search loop). **A bind-only policy that records every option considered with a typed estimate or infeasibility, inside a three-party control seam, is `no-precedent / novel as integrated`.**
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) most adaptive allocators do not beat simple baselines (S-449 `provisional`; S-083 `provisional (counter)`) — answered by the `static` null default, the component-level factor status and ADR-0190's acceptance rule; (ii) a bind-only policy can starve a strategy (bind `k = 1` everywhere) — answered by the recipe's `static` arm (a starving policy loses) and by B-2's ladder rules; (iii) the seam insertion complicates ADR-0103 — answered by monotonicity, purity and removability (AC-F4-1/-2/-11); (iv) estimator cost can exceed savings (S-046's "unnecessary overhead") — answered by `harness_overhead.scheduling` as a primary metric and the `rules` variant making no model calls (ADR-0189). None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** assumes the ratified budget contract (reservation, slices, gauges) and F3 delegation at Stage 4; assumes deterministic oracles or validators exist where `extend_search` is to be estimated (else `unknown`, the policy is inert); `TaskValue` defaults assume `EffectAttributes.reversibility` is declared on affected effects (ADR-0031); holds for any model tier — every model-keyed threshold is a conditioned rule; **any benefit claim is conditional on ADR-0190's matched-budget protocol.**
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C3 / Stage 4** — class contract, `static` + `rules` variants, `ComputeDecisionRecord`, `control.compute.decided`, the bind step, `TaskValue`, AC-F4-1…8/10/11; **C3 / Stage 5** — online priors and `prior_reset` (ADR-0189), `explain` in K2; **C4 / Stage 6** — `predictor` variants and scheduling rules as evolution targets through WS-I5. Never C0/C1.

## Consequences

- WS-F1's ADR-0103 decision 4 gains the bind step (CF-400); WS-F2 is unchanged and receives already-bound decisions; WS-F3's `delegate` executes a bound `budget_slice`; WS-C2's `select` receives `effort`/`role`/`quality_target` from the binding and still decides admissibility; WS-G3 placements are consumed as declared; WS-B4 branches receive `budget_fraction`.
- WS-B1 adds two event classes and two manifest fields (CF-401); WS-I1 attributes estimator cost under `harness_overhead.scheduling`; WS-I2 registers the `scheduling.*` declarations; WS-J4/K2 render `explain` and `compute_decision_outcome`.
- WS-G1's task contract gains `TaskValue` (CF-405); WS-I6 owns the debt records of every `compute_rule` and prior family.
- Doc 3 §2.6's "predict whether to spawn a subagent / alt model / evaluator / longer search" reads, in the spec, as "bind or advise at the ratified `delegate` / `propose` / `verify` / `retry` / `stop` points" (CF-403).

## Reversibility

**High** for the component (binding `static` removes every effect; no ledger data is lost) and for the option sum (additive by dialect bump). **Medium** for the seam insertion: removing the bind step after Stage 4 is a driver change but loses nothing; folding the scheduler *into* strategies later would be the expensive direction (four implementations, no factor), which this ADR forecloses. Records already written remain valid projections.
