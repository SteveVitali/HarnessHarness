# ADR-0197 — The assumption-debt manager contract: a closed debt-home inventory `DebtHomes/1` bound to IR entities and registry kinds; additive `AssumptionDebtRecord/1` fields (typed hypothesis with `DeficiencyClass/1`, typed evidence refs, scope, runway, revalidation, typed `RemovalTest`); the derived `evidence_grade` with probation; current status as a ledger fold and the `DebtIndex` view; one trigger model over all homes; `validate_removal_test` at `seal`/`register`; retirement as a human-sealed supersession with a `RetirementRecord`; `DebtReport`, `AssumptionDebtHealth`, `DebtPolicy`, notices; the manager's reflexive record

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 4 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I6 (consumers: WS-C3, WS-E2, WS-C1, WS-C2, WS-G3, WS-J3, WS-J4, WS-J5, WS-J2, WS-K1, WS-K2, WS-I2, WS-I5, WS-I7, WS-I8, WS-L5) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (retirement test in the evolution pipeline; governance), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05; `AssumptionDebtRecord` C0 schema), ADR-0016/0017 (`HarnessRule.conditioned_on`; `touches_conditioned_rules`; `derived-from`), ADR-0020 §4/§7 (fields, enums, states, refuse/warn/retire), ADR-0024 (MUST-data; migration ladder retirement), ADR-0025 (`validate_assembly`/`seal`), ADR-0035 (`seal` basis), ADR-0037 (expiry as supersession; `StaleIndex`), ADR-0040 (instrument budgets), ADR-0045/0046 (`MetricDeclaration`, `ComparisonReport`), ADR-0053 D-5, ADR-0091, ADR-0117, ADR-0119, ADR-0121, ADR-0124/0125/**0126** (profile expiry contract — consumed verbatim), ADR-0127, ADR-0151 (R5 status axes; `register`), ADR-0154/0155/**0156** (`retirement` kind), ADR-0157/0158, ADR-0160, ADR-0165/0166, ADR-0170, ADR-0176, ADR-0178, ADR-0181; CF-049, CF-327; CF-423…08; OQ-009, OQ-242, OQ-285, OQ-301, OQ-304, OQ-368, OQ-441…07; R-2.9.6, R-2.9.5, R-2.9.8

## Context

T-LCD-05 requires that every conditioned rule carry hypothesis, evidence, owner, expiry condition and removal test, that the compiler refuse a rule without them, and that the Lab can run "delete this rule" on demand — reflexively for the compiler and the Hosting ABI. Phases 1–3 ratified the record (ADR-0007/0020), its two homes (ADR-0016; CF-049), the profile-side state machine, observables, events and removal verdict (ADR-0126), the `retirement` experiment kind (ADR-0156), expiry as supersession (ADR-0037) and registry refusal of incomplete records (ADR-0151), and attached the record to at least fourteen further record kinds under six spellings (ADR-0091/0117/0119/0121/0160/0165/0166/0178/0181/0127; WS-I6 dossier §4a F3). What no ADR fixes is the *manager* R-2.9.6 names: which kinds carry the record (so "no rule without a hypothesis" is one schema rule), how the brief's *hypothesized → evidenced* lifecycle composes with the ratified four states, how a current status is derived when records are immutable, one trigger model across homes (profile change and evidence supersession are ratified only in pieces), what makes a removal test *executable* rather than a `TODO`, what "retired by ADR" means inside the product, how debt is reported (K2/I2) and what the manager's own removal test is. The evidence is unambiguous on the failure mode: production teams find dead weight by inspection (S-047 context resets "had become dead weight" on the next model; S-049 five guardrails "mostly long gone" with no retirement process), no audited harness records a reason, owner or test for any model-conditioned rule (S-107 codex `models.json` at 0735c51), and the disciplines that work elsewhere each have at most two of the four parts (Chromium owners + milestone expiry; OpenHands `warn_cleanup` deadline + runway + CI gate; litellm dates + status buckets + alerts; Piranha owner + expected treatment + automated removal diff).

## Options considered

1. **Leave the manager as prose: each home's ADR governs its own record; retirement by human review.** *Rejected:* six spellings already exist; "no rule without a hypothesis" is unenforceable as a fleet rule; Chromium/Piranha show metadata without an actuator accumulates debt.
2. **Expiry by date only (OpenHands/Chromium regime) with automatic removal at the deadline.** *Rejected:* removes rules a subset still needs (S-461 mechanism); ADR-0126 already rejected automatic deletion (ADR-0002 governance); version strings and dates are not observables of the beneficiary.
3. **A fifth status vocabulary `hypothesized | evidenced | …` replacing ADR-0020's states.** *Rejected:* contradicts a ratified enum and ADR-0151 R5; the evidence question is orthogonal to the status question.
4. **A separate debt store with its own mutable status field.** *Rejected:* a second store of record (ADR-0026/0151 R1); mutable status contradicts ADR-0037 I1.
5. **A closed inventory over the ratified refusal points; additive record fields; a derived evidence grade with probation; status as a ledger fold with a derived index; one trigger model delegating to ADR-0126 for profiles; a closed `RemovalTest` sum validated by dry-run expansion at `seal`/`register`; retirement as a human-sealed supersession with a `RetirementRecord`; `DebtReport`/health metrics/`DebtPolicy`/notices as records and data; reflexive record (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Debt-home inventory `DebtHomes/1`** (closed; growth by dialect bump): the seventeen homes of the WS-I6 dossier §6.1 — `HarnessRule.assumption_debt` (conditioned), `ProfileRule.debt`, `ModelProfile.expiry`, `ModelProfile.ext[].debt`, the `fallback_profile`, `WireDialect` rules/document, `RoutingPolicy.conditioned_rules[]`, `HarnessRule{kind: critic_gate}`, `CalibrationRecord.expiry`, admitting rules of synthesized surfaces, `FittedSurfaceReport.debt_record_ref`, `AdapterRecord` and the `hh-hosting/1` version, the `budget_enforcement` derivation table, `experimental`/`deprecated` contract items and protocol eras/compatibility profiles, the `hot_path` ceiling and cache retention class, the Profile Compiler and the manager, and spec decisions (ADR-0198). A debt record on any other kind is `UnknownDebtHome`; a listed home without a complete record is refused at that home's ratified point (`ConditionedRuleIncomplete`, `LinkError{missing_debt_record}`, `SurfaceSynthesisRefused`, `ExperimentalRequired`/schema). Kernel-derived constraints carry no record (ADR-0124 §2); there is no never-expire list.
2. **`AssumptionDebtRecord/1` additive fields** (ratified fields and enums unchanged): `debt_class ∈ {model_conditioned, provider_conditioned, task_conditioned, instrument_reflexive, spec_decision}`; `hypothesis_typed{subject, deficiency_class ∈ DeficiencyClass/1 (closed: premature_stop, format_noncompliance, tool_shape_mismatch, name_misalignment, schema_dialect_gap, context_overflow_handling, loop_or_repetition, instruction_following_gap, claim_grounding_gap, reasoning_replay_incompatibility, latency_or_cost_profile, unknown{Text}), predicted_effect{metric_ref, direction, magnitude?}}`; `scope{model_selectors?, task_classes?, roles?}`; `evidence_refs[]` as `EvidenceRef{kind ∈ {source, inspection, probe_run, compliance_report, conformance_report, comparison_report, calibration_record, fitted_surface_report, experiment_report}, ref, observed_at, tier?, provisional?}`; `expiry{condition (ratified enum), params{until?, evidence_max_age?, dependency_capabilities?, design_ref?}}`; `runway`; `revalidation{on, action}`; `removal_test: RemovalTest`; `owner: PrincipalRef | TeamRef` (resolvable and reachable by a declared sink — `OwnerUnreachable`); `created_by`, `created_at`, `supersedes?`. `DebtPolicy.required_fields_by_home` says which are mandatory per home.
3. **Evidence grade is derived, never stored:** `hypothesized` (no instrument-produced ref), `evidenced` (≥ 1 probe/compliance/conformance/calibration ref newer than creation), `confirmed` (≥ 1 matched `comparison_report` with `benefit_kind = artifact_benefit` over the rule and its removal on a beneficiary in `scope`, interval excluding 0). Refs to superseded/revoked/`expired` records do not count. **Probation and runway:** a `hypothesized` record older than `DebtPolicy.hypothesized_max_age` becomes `expiring`; a record whose earliest time expiry is nearer than `DebtPolicy.min_runway` is refused `InsufficientRunway` at `seal`/`register`. Defaults are OQ-441 (with OQ-304).
4. **Status derivation and `DebtIndex`.** The stored `status` is the value at seal/registration; the current status is the fold of `status.changed` events after that version (ADR-0126 §2 generalised); ADR-0151's three axes are untouched. `debt_index(registry_snapshot_id, watermark) → [DebtIndexRow{debt_ref, debt_class, owner, stored_status, current_status, evidence_grade, deficiency_class, last_trigger?, next_time_expiry?, removal_test{kind, executable, last_report_ref?, last_verdict?}, used_by[], expired_used_runs, staleness_reasons[]}]` is a derived registry projection (`StaleIndex` family), served by `query`. Events: ratified classes stand for their homes; for the others `lifecycle.debt.status.changed{debt_ref, home, from, to, trigger, evidence_ref?, causes[]}`, `lifecycle.debt.removal_test.{scheduled, settled}`, `lifecycle.debt.expired_used{debt_ref, intent_ref}` (family vs generic + instances: CF-425).
5. **Trigger model.** `evaluate_debt(record, home, observables, registry_snapshot, watermark, now, policy) → [Transition]` is pure and delegates to ADR-0126's `evaluate_expiry` for profile homes. Families: *beneficiary observables* (ADR-0126 §1 unchanged); *profile change* — a definition-level rule whose `conditioned_on`/`scope.model_selectors` profile is superseded at sameness ≥ L2, retired, or past `retirement_at`, or whose role is re-bound to a different profile `semantic_id`, becomes `expiring` (`expired` after `grace_period` when the profile is retired); *evidence supersession* — any evidence ref resolving to a superseded, revoked or `expired` record (ADR-0160 §6 generalised) makes the record `expiring`; *time* — `until`, `evidence_max_age` over instrument evidence only (OQ-304 answered: Tier-A `source` refs never age out by themselves), probation. `revalidated` for a `model_conditioned` record requires grade ≥ `evidenced` evidence newer than the trigger. A consolidated/fine-tuned beneficiary fires *as* `model_version_change` (WS-I8 hook; no enum growth). Version strings fire nothing.
6. **`RemovalTest`** (closed sum): `retirement_experiment{template_ref, primary_metric (default = predicted_effect.metric_ref), margin_ref?, beneficiaries ⊆ scope}` instantiating `ExperimentSpec{kind: retirement}` with arms `A` / `A − R` under `MatchSpec{matched_cap}`, equal `eval_budget`, held-out split (ADR-0126 §5 verdict); `critic_experiment` (ADR-0117); `successor_chain_experiment`; `probe_pass{probe_spec_refs}`; `recalibrate`; `refit`; `zero_uses{scope}`; `null_profile_compile`; `remeasure{ac_ref}`; `no_dead_weight_found{window}`; `revalidation_review`; `profile_registered`. Operations: `validate_removal_test(record, context) → ok | UnexecutableRemovalTest{reason}` runs **at `seal` (inside `validate_assembly`) and at `register`** and instantiates the kind without running it — for `retirement_experiment` a dry-run `expand` that must not refuse (`NotARetirementDiff`, `UnbudgetedArm`, `SplitUnassigned`, `UnsealedArtifact`, unresolvable template); `schedule_removal_test(debt_ref, policy) → experiment_id | Deferred{reason}` registers through WS-J3 with `charged_to = instrument` under `DebtPolicy.priority` and `max_open_removal_tests`; `settle_removal_test(debt_ref, report_ref) → [Transition]` records an immutable `RemovalVerdict{debt_ref, kind, verdict ∈ {pass, fail, inconclusive{reason}}, report_ref, settled_at}` — `fail` revalidates with the report as `comparison_report` evidence (grade `confirmed`), `inconclusive` reschedules; `propose_retirement(debt_ref, verdict_ref) → HirDiff | ProfileDiff` with `derived-from` provenance and retirement-diff classification.
7. **Retirement** is `retire(debt_ref, diff, RetirementRecord{removal_test_report_ref, verdict_ref, decided_by: ProvenanceRecord{origin: human}, rationale}) → new version`: `apply` + `seal` (or `publish`) of the version without the rule, carrying `supersedes{reason: expiry}` and the `RetirementRecord` ref, appending `status.changed{to: retired}`; refuses `RetirementNotEvidenced` without a `pass` verdict on every beneficiary in `scope`; an `origin = evolution` diff is admissible only as a proposal (ADR-0053 D-5). "Retired by ADR" (ADR-0020 §7, ADR-0126 §2) means this record inside the product; program ADRs govern only home 17 (CF-427).
8. **Reporting and policy.** `DebtReport{scope, watermark, rows, counts_by, buckets{overdue, imminent, scheduled, awaiting_seal}, health, generated_from}` is a persisted provenance-bearing record rendered identically by CLI and web (ADR-0170 §4; placement CF-426). `AssumptionDebtHealth` = `MetricDeclaration`s `debt.{records_total, complete_rate, hypothesized_share, confirmed_share, expired_count, expired_used_runs, overdue_count, removal_tests_executed_rate, retirement_pass_rate, median_age, owner_unreachable_count}` (+ C3's `profile.ext_field_count`), `applies_to_classes = {native}`, hosted `n/a{class}`, reported in the evolvability dimension (ADR-0045; WS-I2 `assumption_debt_health`). `DebtPolicy{required_fields_by_home, min_runway, hypothesized_max_age, evidence_max_age, warn_within, grace_period, max_open_removal_tests, priority, notice_sinks, schedule, dead_weight_designs_allowed}` is MUST-data layered with `authority_cap`. `DebtNotice{debt_ref, owner, bucket, reason, next_action, report_ref}` goes only to declared sinks, never into a model context.
9. **Invariants.** D-1 no conditioned rule sealed/registered without a complete record whose removal test validates; D-2 the manager never opens a run or applies a diff (it authors specs and proposals, reads reports); D-3 every executable removal test is an ordinary J3 experiment, instrument-charged, matched-cap, held-out; D-4 `retired` only through a human-sealed supersession referencing a `pass` verdict; D-5 a `fail` verdict is evidence; D-6 `expired_used` runs are excluded from headline reports unless the `Design` declares dead-weight purpose (ADR-0126 §3), read from the results-row annotation.
10. **Reflexivity.** The manager carries `{hypothesis: "conditioned rules go stale faster than teams notice them", deficiency_class: unknown{"human review cadence"}, predicted_effect: debt.retirement_pass_rate ↑, evidence_refs: [S-047, S-049 (inspection); Piranha (source)], expiry: evidence_refresh_due, removal_test: no_dead_weight_found{window: N model-version changes}}` — if no scheduled removal test ever passes across the window, the manager is dead weight and `retired` is its honest end state.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: dead weight discovered by inspection after a model change (S-047 Tier B: context resets "had become dead weight"; S-049 Tier B: five guardrails "mostly long gone", no retirement process); behaviour changes under a stable id (S-460 Tier B); aggregate removal verdicts hide per-item regressions (S-461 `provisional`, mechanism only, already in ADR-0126's verdict); dead experimental codepaths and configuration debt as named debt classes (S-591 Sculley et al. 2015, Tier A); stale flags accumulate despite metadata and are removable as typed diffs with owner + expected treatment (S-590 Piranha, Tier A industry track); beneficiary change voids a rule's evidence (S-096 `provisional`, mechanism only — shapes the consolidation hook, C4); matched-budget and paired comparison ratified (ADR-0041/0045/0046; S-083).
- (b) Source-code precedent: OpenHands `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/utils/deprecation.py` L115–159 `warn_cleanup(workaround, cleanup_by, details)` and `.github/scripts/check_deprecations.py` L560–590 (`_runway_error`, ≥ 5 minor releases), L633–711 (CI fails on overdue `cleanup_call`) (S-110 @3fc7b22) — runway, reason and static gate; litellm `litellm/proxy/common_utils/model_deprecation.py` L45–104, L145–200 and `types/proxy/model_deprecation.py` L14–35 (S-457 @c1a83fc) — date-derived status buckets and alerts; Chromium `docs/flag_expiry.md` / `flag_ownership.md`, `chrome/browser/flag-metadata.json`, `flag-never-expire-list.json`, `tools/flags/generate_unexpire_flags.py`, generated `expired_flags_list.h` (S-589) — mandatory reachable owners, graded milestone expiry with bounded un-expire, generated enforcement; codex `codex-rs/models-manager/models.json` (`comp_hash`, `upgrade.retirement_at`), `core/src/session/turn.rs` L1234–1340, `core/src/compact_model_fallback.rs` L8–34 (S-107 @0735c51) — compatibility token and retirement date (consumed via ADR-0124/0126); inspect_ai `src/inspect_ai/_util/deprecation.py` L10–160 (S-136 @75f4891) — `remove_in`; Piranha (S-590) — automated removal diff from `{flag, treatment, author}`. The closed inventory, derived evidence grade with probation, `validate_removal_test` by dry-run expansion, `RetirementRecord`, `DebtIndex`/`DebtReport` and the reflexive test are `no-precedent / novel` as integrated.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) bureaucracy — Chromium still accumulates flags with mandatory metadata; Piranha needed an automated refactorer; Cursor and Anthropic shipped by deleting on observation — answered: fields that rot are made checkable (derived grade, probation, owner reachability, expansion at seal), defaults per home make the complete record the cheap path, and the instrument's novelty claim (c) and T-LCD-05 require the recipe; (ii) removal tests are too expensive for a fleet — answered: rules without a design never reach `retired` but never go silently stale either; static kinds cost nothing; batch designs and priority-by-policy bound the spend (OQ-442); the date-based alternative retires rules a subset needs (S-461); (iii) co-evolution says re-tune, not delete (S-096, S-084/0085 `provisional`) — answered: `fail` revalidates, re-tuning is a superseding version, the manager never deletes; (iv) a generic event/record over all homes duplicates ratified classes — answered: one record schema with one spelling is a normalisation synthesis needs; family vs instances is CF-425. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes the ratified observables exist at the gateway (ADR-0126 (d): `served_model` per call; `unknown` fires nothing) and that the Lab can afford paired `retirement` designs for the rules it cares about (C1/Stage 5; otherwise `expired`-with-intent is the floor); independent of model tier and task class; the record and static gate hold for a single-family deployment (where the inventory is small and the manager is nearly idle — its reflexive test then retires it). No `provisional` claim is load-bearing: S-096/S-461/S-462 shape C1/C4 behaviour only.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (`DebtHomes/1`; additive fields; `DeficiencyClass/1`; `RemovalTest`/`RemovalVerdict`/`RetirementRecord`; `validate_removal_test` in `validate_assembly`/`register`; `InsufficientRunway`/`OwnerUnreachable`; `lifecycle.debt.*`; `DebtPolicy` schema; health `MetricDeclaration`s); **C0 / Stage 3** (`retirement` experiments for the two minimal profiles and the Stage-0 fixture; `settle`/`retire` on fixtures; `expired_used` exclusion; minimal `DebtReport`; `evaluate_debt` with injected observables); **C1 / Stage 5** (live triggers; `DebtIndex` projection; profile-change/evidence-supersession triggers; notices; K2 rendering); **C4 / Stage 6** (manager service: scheduling under `DebtPolicy` and instrument budgets, batch designs, fleet reports, reflexive test, WS-I5 retirement gate, WS-I8 hook).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-05 (schema-level across a closed inventory; refuse/warn/retire; executable "delete this rule" at seal; reflexive), T-LCD-14 (every executable removal test is a matched `retirement` experiment), T-LCD-09 (per-beneficiary removal tests; rule × model as a `contrast`), T-LCD-13 (`evidenced` requires compliance/probe evidence), T-LCD-10 (renames trigger nothing; retirement is a semantic diff), T-LCD-15 (native-only health metrics with typed `n/a`), T-LCD-12 (operations over records; out of process), T-LCD-06 (adapter records without IR change), T-LCD-01 (observables, no model literals). ## Consequences

- WS-C3's contract is consumed unchanged; the manager calls `evaluate_expiry`, `retire_rule`, `probe_profile`. WS-J3 receives the `retirement_batch` request (OQ-442) and the dead-weight purpose field in `PreRegistration`. WS-J4/I2 own margins (OQ-301) and confirm the health metric family (CF-430).
- WS-B1 adds `lifecycle.debt.*` classes (or folds them, CF-425); WS-J2 serves `DebtIndex` through `query`; WS-K1/K2 render `DebtReport` (placement CF-426).
- WS-I5's candidate gate: a candidate adding a conditioned rule ships its record and passes `validate_removal_test`; one removing a rule carries a `pass` verdict and is sealed by a human. WS-I8's consolidation fires `model_version_change`.
- ADR-0117's free-text expiry and ADR-0178/0160's field spellings are normalised at synthesis (CF-428/07).
- ADR-0007's `AssumptionDebtRecord` line gains the additive fields; the `lcd_report.conditioned_rules` entry gains `evidence_grade` and `removal_test.executable`.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Additive fields, enums and policy defaults are cheap to change by dialect bump. Removing the inventory constraint or the `seal`-time removal-test validation would silently return the fleet to inspection-based dead-weight discovery and make T-LCD-05 unenforceable outside profiles — the costly direction. The manager *service* (C4) can be absent without any C0/C1 contract change: records, gates and metrics stand alone.
