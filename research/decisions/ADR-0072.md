# ADR-0072 — The context builder is kernel admission plus a pluggable selection policy over a content-addressed context plan; admission invariants are C0 and the policy can never widen authority

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-D1 (consumers: WS-D2, WS-D3, WS-D4, WS-C3, WS-C1, WS-E3, WS-H1, WS-H2, WS-F2, WS-F3, WS-G2, WS-B1, WS-I2, WS-J3) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0033, ADR-0034 (RP, P3, P5), ADR-0035 (mandatory-provenance table; option 4 rejected), ADR-0016 (`ContextItem`, `slots` with `context_policy` mandatory — CF-052), ADR-0019 (closed-world rule; `ModelSurface.layout`; `render_transcript` hook), ADR-0020 (`ProfileRule` kinds), ADR-0026 (context view as `project()`), ADR-0029 (content addresses), ADR-0014 (compliance chain), ADR-0023/0025 (variant registration), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-02/-05/-08/-10/-12/-13); CF-006 (resolved at scheme level — this ADR designs against it), CF-092, CF-158…CF-163; OQ-063, OQ-084, OQ-080, OQ-058, OQ-049; R-2.4.1

## Context

Doc 2 §11 asks for a context builder that is "a policy engine for working set, retrieval, compaction, tool-result offload, skills, and provenance" treating tokens as "budgeted working memory, not a transcript dump", and doc 2 §5.6 diagnoses context-privilege escalation as "partly an assembly bug in the harness". Phase 1 ratified the scheme the builder must consume (ADR-0033/0034/0035: one provenance record, seven authority classes, the per-call `context_label`, the role-placement invariant RP, retrieval order validity → authority → readers → relevance, and "the monitor reads records only") and the compilation contract it must fit (ADR-0019: compile time decides existence/identity/shape/layout; run time decides selection, filling, ordering within declared layout and transcript rendering; no run-time operation may deliver an artefact without a compiled identity). The WS-D1 audit (dossier §4) found that every production builder — Codex, Pi, OpenHands, browser-use, AIOS — is already a slot-and-projection machine with kind, tier, cache tier and truncation on items, that **none** carries authority on an item or checks placement against one, and that content gains model-facing privilege by *location* (project skills authoritative by path; memories at system position) or by *role* (AGENTS.md and memory instructions as `developer` items) in each of them; the only structural defences found were Codex's untrusted-project gate and its refusal to retain raw `system` items. Three of five systems expose the builder to extension as a single whole-list transform, which is exactly where the escalation bug lives.

## Options considered

1. **One whole-list policy hook** (Pi `transformContext`, OpenHands condenser returning a `View`, browser-use rebuilding everything in one class). *Rejected:* nothing prevents the transform from moving a tool result into the system block; the monitor has no record to read; nothing is ablatable per item (T-LCD-02); D2 cannot learn legal cut points.
2. **Text-labelled admission** (OpenHands `<UNTRUSTED_CONTENT>`, browser-use "treat as unverified"): let the policy place anything anywhere and annotate untrusted content in prose. *Rejected:* ADR-0035 option 4 ("monitor reads the model-facing text") is already rejected; S-103/S-104 (`provisional (mech)`) show harnesses using banners were compromised end-to-end; a banner is at most a profile-owned demotion wrapper.
3. **Kernel-only builder, no policy variants** (a fixed algorithm at C0 and forever). *Rejected:* every mechanism the audit found valuable — priority eviction, world-state diffs, handle-only catalogs, recency-plus-pins — is model-conditioned (S-049, S-047) and must be a component variant the Lab can vary under matched budgets (T-LCD-08/-14).
4. **Policy owns slots and authority thresholds** (the variant declares which items are "trusted"). *Rejected:* it recreates three schemes (CF-006) and lets an evolution candidate (`delegate` origin) widen authority through the builder (ADR-0002 iii, ADR-0034 P6).
5. **Kernel admission (fixed invariants over ratified records) + pluggable selection policy (chooses among admitted candidates, admissible slots, order within slot, eviction order, by-reference and expansion) + a content-addressed `ContextPlan` as the seam that the renderer, the monitor, the ledger and the Lab all read (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Component class and placement.** The context builder is the `context_policy` slot of `AgentProcess.native` (Stage-1 mandatory, ADR-0016/CF-052). Its *kernel* — operations `assemble`, `render`, `offload`, `emit` and the invariants below — is MUST-code (ADR-0024); its *policy* is a registered `ContextPolicy` variant with the operation-shaped contract `select(candidates, layout, budget_remaining, profile, account, params) → Selection`, `priority(candidate) → PriorityClass`, `declare()`, receiving `(ModelProfile, ResourceAccount)` (T-LCD-08/-12). It runs once per model call after E3's `select_surfaces` and D3's `retrieve` and before any proposal is evaluated by the monitor.
2. **Inputs and outputs.** `assemble(AssemblyRequest{model_call_id, view: ContextView = project(run, context_view, until_seq), candidates: [Candidate], layout: Layout, budget: ContextBudget, profile, account, policy_params, estimator_ref}) → ContextPlan | AssemblyError`. `Candidate = {candidate_id, context_item: Ref<ContextItem>, kind: CandidateKind (closed + registered ext; never a role), state ∈ {handle_only, expanded}, retention ∈ {required, optional(PriorityClass)}, estimate{tokens, estimator_ref}, source_event, slot_hint?, readers?}`. `ContextPlan = {plan_id = H(canonical(plan − plan_id)), model_call_id, derived_from (view watermark), slots: [SlotFill{slot_id, items: [PlannedItem{candidate_id, context_item_id, artefact_id, delivery_id, authority, label, tokens, state, delivered_by_reference, derived_from?}]}], omitted: [Omission{candidate_id, reason ∈ {authority, validity, readers, budget, dedup, media_unsupported, cardinality, unidentified}, evidence}], context_label, occupancy_estimate, legal_cut_points[], reserved}`. The plan carries **no provider role** and **no rendered text**; roles and bytes are produced by `render` under the compiled renderer spec.
3. **Kernel admission invariants** (errors, never warnings): **I-ID** every planned item has a compiled identity (trace-map locator) or a ledger content address (`UndeliverableArtifact` when a `required` candidate has neither); **I-RP** (ADR-0034 RP verbatim) an item is placed only in a slot with `slot.min_authority ≤ authority(item)`, `role_map(slot.min_authority)` bounds the provider role, an item admissible nowhere is `Omission{authority}` or goes through a profile-declared demotion wrapper into the `external` slot — the builder never promotes; **I-ORDER** (ADR-0034 P5) validity, then authority, then readers are checked at admission before any policy selection, re-checking whatever D3 already applied; **I-LABEL** (ADR-0034 P3) `context_label = ⊔ label(admitted items)` recomputed from the plan, by-reference items included; **I-NOWIDEN** the policy cannot set or change `authority`, `label`, `validity`, `retention = required`, or any slot's `min_authority` — an attempt is `PolicyViolation` and no plan is emitted; **I-PAIR/I-ATOM/I-MEDIA** every tool call in `transcript` has exactly one paired observation and vice versa, batches and tool loops are atomic, unsupported media is replaced by a typed kernel placeholder; **I-NOSYS** raw provider `system`-role items from previous responses are never candidates; **I-DET** `assemble` is a pure function of its request and two implementations agree on `plan_id` (canonical form per ADR-0015/0029).
4. **Fixed kernel step order:** `admit` → `reserve_mandatory` → `policy.select` → `enforce_budget` → `check_invariants` → `plan` (budget semantics in WS-D1 ADR-2). The policy sees every admitted candidate annotated with `admissible_slots[]` and decides only membership, admissible slot, within-slot order, eviction order, by-reference delivery and expansion requests.
5. **Ledger obligations.** `emit` appends `context.assembled{model_call_id, plan_id, view_hash, items[{context_item_id, delivery_id, slot_id, provenance, authority, tokens, kind, state, delivered_by_reference}], omitted[], context_label, occupancy_estimate, reserved, assembly_ms{measured_at}, compaction_state, estimator_ref}` (payload extension to ADR-0026's row, CF-159; items inline when ≤ the class threshold, else by content address with counts inline — OQ-084) and one `context.artefact.delivered{…, by_reference}` per admitted harness artifact (ADR-0014). Provenance is mandatory on every item (ADR-0035).
6. **The C0 `default` policy** is the trivial builder: admit all, layout precedence, ledger order in `transcript`, `optional` = observations older than N turns and commentary, no by-reference, no expansion — the Stage 0/1 builder and the T-LCD-03 anchor's builder. The C1 family (`priority_eviction`, `world_state_diff`, `jit_handles`, `recency_plus_pins`, `retrieval_augmented`, D2's trigger policies) are variants of the same class with debt records on every model-conditioned rule.
7. **Answers.** OQ-063: `render` executes the compiled transcript-renderer spec inside the builder and hands `RenderedRequest{messages[], surfaces_revealed[], trace[]}` to the C1 gateway as data (CF-044); OQ-080: the builder reads inline observations from the view; OQ-058: `Text`-leaf ablation replaces the leaf with a kernel placeholder of matched estimated length.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-02 (typed candidates; `opacity_dynamic` from `context.assembled`; per-item ablation), T-LCD-08 (policy receives profile and account), T-LCD-10 (roles and order are profile surfaces; the plan has no role), T-LCD-12 (operations, out-of-process implementable), T-LCD-13 (delivered events with `delivery_id`, by-reference marked), T-LCD-05 (every ordering/threshold rule is a conditioned rule), T-LCD-15 (hosted participants have no plan; `n/a{observability}`). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-06/-07/-09/-11/-14 (T-LCD-14 engaged through WS-D1 ADR-2's budget accounting). **ADR-0024 ladder rung:** kernel = MUST-code; policy choice, slot declarations, priority classes and thresholds = MUST-data (variant refs, parameters, conditioned rules).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** context assembly elevates lower-level content to higher instruction levels — S-104, S-103 (`provisional (mech)`), corroborated in source by Codex `core/src/context/memory.rs` (memory instructions at `developer` role), OpenHands `context/agent_context.py` L104–150 (project skills "authoritative" by location) and AIOS `aios/memory/context_injector.py` L23–170 (relevance-filtered memories prepended as a system message); per-call context label and low-water mark — ADR-0033/0034 (Fides S-101, Biba S-217, ratified); the plan-as-record principle — ADR-0035 ("the monitor reads records only"; DLM/Flume); typed fragments with a stable kind classification — Codex `context-fragments/src/fragment.rs` L64–119; declared view invariants enforced to a fixpoint — OpenHands `context/view/view.py` L1–160; the compile/run-time split and closed-world rule — ADR-0019 (S-107/S-111/S-112/S-113 audited by WS-A4); context as a finite attention budget, just-in-time context, tool-result clearing — S-038 (vendor); dynamic context discovery — S-050 (vendor); position effects as model-conditioned surfaces — S-335 (Tier A), S-336 (vendor research).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** `codex/codex-rs/context-fragments/src/fragment.rs` L64–119 and `core/src/context/mod.rs` (typed fragments, 54 kinds); `codex/codex-rs/core/src/context_manager/history.rs` L700–800 (`normalize_history` pairing and media stripping; `is_api_message` never retains raw `system`); `codex/codex-rs/core/src/agents_md.rs` L55–75 (untrusted-project gate), L382–400 (`InstructionProvenance::Project`); `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/context/view/view.py` L1–160 and `view/properties/tool_call_matching.py` L1–100 (properties, `manipulation_indices`); `software-agent-sdk/.../context/prompts/section.py` L1–143 (`PromptSection{name, cache_tier, guard, render}`, `PromptBlocks`); `pi-mono/packages/agent/src/harness/session/context.ts` L10–70 (view = last compaction + tail; aborted items excluded); `browser-use/browser_use/agent/message_manager/views.py` L1–101 (three fixed slots rebuilt per step). The `ContextPlan` with `min_authority` slots, omissions as data, `context_label` on the plan and a no-widen policy boundary is `no-precedent / novel` as an integrated contract.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** a whole-list hook is simpler and is what three audited systems ship — overturned because it is precisely the escalation surface and yields no record for the monitor or the Lab; `min_authority` slots may lower compliance with legitimate project conventions — accepted as a measured trade (AC-D1-12) with the legitimate paths up (`seal`/`pin`/`promotion`) already ratified, and Codex already ships a stricter rule (skip untrusted project docs); determinism is undermined by host-dependent token estimates — absorbed by making the estimate and `estimator_ref` recorded inputs of the plan hash; the closed-world rule seems to exclude run-time content — absorbed because observations, memories and model messages all carry ledger identities (ADR-0027/0029). None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** independent of model tier and task class; assumes the native participant can enumerate delivered items per call (hosted participants render `n/a`, ADR-0034 (d)); assumes the profile declares a context window and a tokenizer or proxy (ADR-0020 capability declaration); the utility cost of RP is a Stage 3 measurement, not an assumption.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C0 / Stage 1** (schemas; I-ID, I-RP, I-ORDER authority part, I-NOWIDEN, I-NOSYS, I-DET; `default` policy; `context.assembled` payload; reserved-slot check at `link`); **C0 / Stage 2** (`context_label` to H1; I-PAIR/I-ATOM with `legal_cut_points` to D2; validity part of I-ORDER with D3/D4; `render`; delivered/activated events); **C0 / Stage 3** (executable privilege-preservation fixtures AC-D1-1/-2; `opacity_dynamic`; re-lowering test); **C1 / Stage 5** (policy family); **C2** (readers enforcement at the slot boundary, WS-H2).

## Consequences

- WS-D2 receives `legal_cut_points` and `CompactionRequired`; its summaries enter as candidates whose label is `⊔ label(inputs) ⊔ delegate` (ADR-0034 P1) and are subject to the same admission; it may never produce a plan directly.
- WS-D3 proposes candidates and may apply relevance ranking only after the three filters; the builder re-checks them (defence in depth) and exposes `view.derived_from` for read-your-writes checks.
- WS-C3 owns slot declarations beyond the six reserved ones, comparators, cache tiers, demotion wrappers and reminder rules — each with an assumption-debt record; WS-A4's `link` refuses a layout lacking a reserved slot or declaring `min_authority` below `unverified`.
- WS-C1 consumes `RenderedRequest` as data and may not re-order, merge or re-role; a gateway that needs to is reporting a profile bug (CF-044).
- WS-H1 reads `context_label` from `context.assembled`; the read-only/closed exemption (OQ-101) is decided on the effect, never by the plan.
- WS-B1 extends the `context.assembled` and `context.artefact.delivered` payloads (CF-159); WS-I2 computes `opacity_dynamic` and omission-rate process metrics from them.
- The AIOS-shaped injection (relevance-first, top role) becomes inexpressible; text banners become optional surface decorations.

## Reversibility

**Low-to-medium.** Relaxing I-NOWIDEN or I-RP after Stage 2 reopens the M-CPE/X-CPE fixtures and silently changes what the monitor's `context_label` means, so those two are constitutional alongside ADR-0034 P3/P5. Adding candidate kinds, priority classes or slots is a dialect/profile-schema bump. Moving `render` to the gateway (if C1 needs it) is a medium change because the rules are data and the plan is unchanged.
