# ADR-0146 — Scope-register changes at Phase 2: R-2.7.2 split into R-2.7.2a/R-2.7.2b, stage notes placing C0 slices inside C1/C2 items (R-2.2.3, R-2.3.3, R-2.4.2, R-2.4.5, R-2.5.2, R-2.7.1, R-2.8.5, R-2.9.3), one recorded deviation from the brief's anchor list (SWE-bench Verified demoted), and status → specified-by-ADR for the 31 Phase 2 items

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10; synthesis-authored)
**Owner workstream(s):** Phase 2 synthesis · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0007 (stage-note precedent), ADR-0048 (Phase 1 stage notes), ADR-0145; CF-285 (R-2.2.3), CF-271 (R-2.3.3), CF-166 (R-2.4.2), CF-188 (R-2.4.5), CF-200 (R-2.5.2), CF-230 (R-2.7.1), CF-238 (R-2.7.2), CF-144 (R-2.8.5), CF-301 (R-2.9.3), CF-304 (SWE-bench Verified); `registers/scope.md`

## Context

`registers/scope.md` requires every addition, removal or tier/MoSCoW change to be logged as an ADR. Phase 2 produced one structural change (WS-G2's request to split R-2.7.2 so that the claim-ledger substrate and gate floors needed at C0/Stage 3 are not filed under a C2 "Could" item) and eight stage notes of the ADR-0007/ADR-0048 kind (a C0 slice placed inside a C1/C2 item without changing its tier or MoSCoW). WS-I4 also recorded a deviation from the brief's literal anchor list: SWE-bench Verified is retired for headline numbers because it is memorised (CF-304).

## Options considered

1. **Re-tier the parent items to C0.** Rejected: the C1/C2 bodies (reconcilers, healing, probes, publication, code-mode) are genuinely later-stage; re-tiering would drag them into the Stage 1–3 build ladder (RK-01, RK-04).
2. **Leave the C0 slices implicit in the ADRs.** Rejected: the scope register is the sponsor-facing scope contract; a C0 obligation invisible there is a planning defect (Phase 1 ADR-0048 rationale).
3. **Split where the slice is a separately deliverable item (R-2.7.2), stage-note everywhere else (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **R-2.7.2 is split** (CF-238; ADR-0112…0114): **R-2.7.2a** *Claim ledger substrate, completion-gate floors and deterministic divergence detectors (D2/D3/D5/D6)* — C0, ⊕ NEW, WS-G2, Stage 1–3, **Should**; **R-2.7.2b** *Belief-state reconciler component class and judged detectors* — C2, ⊕ NEW, WS-G2, Stage 4, Could. Nothing is added to or removed from the program's scope; the item's content is partitioned.
2. **Stage notes (no tier or MoSCoW change):** R-2.2.3 (C0 slice: recovery procedure, writer takeover, `retry_due` recomputation, Stage 3 kill-point battery — ADR-0130/0132; CF-285); R-2.3.3 (C0/Stage 2 schema slice of `compaction_reminder` params; profile schema, two minimal profiles, golden surfaces and refusal semantics C0 — ADR-0124; CF-271, CF-185); R-2.4.2 (C0 trigger is the kernel gauge cap; `evict_oldest` compactor C0/Stage 2 — ADR-0075; CF-166); R-2.4.5 (C0/Stage 2 slice: `ProcedureProfile/1`, `lift_skill`, `instruction` target, index/body candidates, deterministic detectors, `RenderTimeExecution`, claims → grants at seal — ADR-0084…0086; CF-188); R-2.5.2 (C0/Stage 1 schema slice: `SurfaceBinding`, transforms, `SurfaceFailure`, render/error specs; static composites C1/Stage 5; freeform/code-mode/shim C2 — ADR-0090…0092; CF-200); R-2.7.1 (Validator contract, verdict schema and kernel local checks C0/Stage 1; task contracts, completion gate, held-out execution, veto predicates C1/Stage 3 — ADR-0109…0111; CF-230); R-2.8.5 (C0/Stage 1 slice: `ExtensionRecord`/`ExtensionTrustRecord`, pins at `resolve`, `default_text_authority`, `security.extension.*`; attestation, surface pins, revocation propagation, model-install path C1/Stage 4 — ADR-0063…0065; CF-144); R-2.9.3 (C0/Stage 3 slice: manifest schema, `check_completeness`, `bundle(kind = run)`, R0/R1 for native runs; kinds, import/export, lifecycle, publication, `diff`, `fetch` C1/Stage 4; R2 C2/Stage 5 — ADR-0139…0141; CF-301).
3. **Recorded deviation:** SWE-bench Verified (mini + full) stays as the T-LCD-03 round-trip fixture and the `contaminated_public` control stratum with `retired_for_headline = true`; headline coding capability comes from Terminal-Bench 2.0 and a fresh SWE-style pool (ADR-0144; CF-304). No scope item changes.
4. **Status → specified-by-ADR** for the 31 Phase 2 items (R-2.2.3, R-2.2.4, R-2.2.5, R-2.3.1–R-2.3.4, R-2.4.1–R-2.4.5, R-2.5.1–R-2.5.5, R-2.6.1, R-2.6.2, R-2.7.1, R-2.7.2a/b, R-2.7.3, R-2.8.1–R-2.8.7, R-2.9.3, R-2.9.4) with their ADR ids; existing stage notes on R-2.2.5, R-2.3.3, R-2.4.1, R-2.4.4, R-2.5.4, R-2.5.5, R-2.8.1, R-2.8.2 are marked satisfied.

## T-LCD statement

No abstraction is introduced; the split and the notes make the C0 obligations of T-LCD-03/-04/-13 visible in the scope contract (T-LCD-14: every C0 slice names its budget and stage).

## Evidence

- **(a) Mechanism:** stage placement follows doc 2 §11 and ADR-0016's population-by-stage table; the split follows ADR-0026 §6.7 (claim events at C0/Stage 3) and ADR-0047's grounding metrics, which need G2's substrate.
- **(b) Precedent-or-novel:** ADR-0007 (R-2.9.6) and ADR-0048 (R-2.5.4, R-2.3.3, …) are the program's precedents for stage notes; the split mirrors Phase 0's ADR-0010 practice of changing one attribute with a logged reason.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence:** if Stage 3 shows the R-2.7.2a substrate cannot be delivered without the reconciler (e.g. deterministic detectors D2/D3/D5/D6 have insufficient coverage), the split is undone by merging the rows back under C2.
- **(d) Conditionality:** all notes assume the Stage ladder of doc 2 §11 unchanged.
- **(e) Build-stage:** register change only; effective immediately.

## Consequences

`registers/scope.md` rows and its scope-change log carry these entries with this ADR id; Phase 3 briefs read the C0 slices from the register.

## Reversibility

A single ADR can merge R-2.7.2a/b or strike a stage note; no downstream identifier depends on the row ids.
