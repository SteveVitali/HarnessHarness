# ADR-0147 — The assembly service pipeline: `AssemblySource` desugaring, `assemble / plan / apply / validate_batch / explain` as a semantics-free orchestration over the kernel's one resolver, dry-run parity, and the "meta" invariants

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 3 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-J1 (consumers: WS-J2, WS-J3, WS-J5, WS-J6, WS-K1, WS-K4, WS-I5, WS-I7, WS-L5) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0001, ADR-0002 (iii), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-10/-12/-14), ADR-0017, ADR-0019, ADR-0023, ADR-0024, ADR-0025 (CF-056 one resolver), ADR-0033, ADR-0036, ADR-0037, ADR-0045 (`Design` immutability), ADR-0048 (CF-053 split), ADR-0050 (D1 layers, D2 boundary objects, D3), ADR-0057, ADR-0139; CF-322, CF-323; OQ-076, OQ-346, OQ-347, OQ-349; R-2.10.1, R-2.1.4

## Context

CF-053 (ADR-0048) split "harness assembly" into the C0 grammar and kernel operations (WS-A5: `load/compose/resolve/validate_assembly/identity/instantiate/diff/verify_resume/space/enumerate`, ADR-0023…0025) and a C1 assembly *service* (R-2.10.1, this workstream). ADR-0050 D2(iii) fixes that the laboratory "composes by calling the kernel's `compose → resolve → validate_assembly → seal` (one resolver, ADR-0025), never by re-implementing the assembler", and D1 places the assembler in the kernel layer and the sweep driver in the laboratory layer. What is undecided is what an operator or optimizer *writes* (every audited system has an authoring form richer than its sealed form — OpenHands `AgentProfile` vs `AgentSettingsConfig`, goose recipe template vs rendered `Recipe`, opencode's eight-layer merge), what the service may add beyond calling the kernel in order, and how the sponsor's central "meta" capability is stated as an invariant rather than a feature. The audit (dossier §4) found dry-run parity implemented once (OpenHands `resolve_agent_profile_dry_run`, verdict "match[es] exactly what a real resolve produces") and required by one protocol (Kubernetes `dryRun=All` under the same authorization); it found no system that combines dry-run, content identity and layer provenance.

## Options considered

1. **No service; the Lab calls kernel operations directly from each consumer (sweep engine, CLI, SDK).** *Rejected:* every consumer re-implements sequencing, snapshot selection, caching and publication; ad-hoc overrides would be materialised differently by each (OQ-076 unanswerable); no single place enforces "no run without a sealed registry record".
2. **A service with its own resolver/validator (a Lab-side implementation for speed, kernel re-validation at run time).** *Rejected:* the two-resolver divergence CF-056 closed; contradicts ADR-0050 D2(iii).
3. **Authoring forms admitted into the sealed grammar (selectors, overrides, `base` kept in the sealed document).** *Rejected:* breaks ADR-0025's closed/idempotent `resolve` and ADR-0036's "sealed forms contain only pinned ids"; two authoring paths would fork `semantic_id` (fails ADR-0025 AC-3).
4. **A semantics-free orchestration: an `AssemblySource` authoring document whose every production desugars to ADR-0023/0024 constructs before `resolve`; `assemble/plan/apply/validate_batch/explain` over the kernel's operations with dry-run parity, content-keyed caching and registry publication; two "meta" invariants (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`AssemblySource`** = `{dialect, root_kind ∈ {native, hosted}, base?: DefinitionRef, layers: [Layer], overrides?: [Override], imports?: [Import], ext}`. Desugaring is pure, total and order-defined: **D1** `base` becomes the lowest-precedence layer (`source_kind = packaged-default`, `id = base.version_id`); **D2** `overrides` become exactly one `experiment` layer with `id = H(idp ∥ "override-layer" ∥ canonical(overrides))` — always materialised, content-addressed (answers OQ-076 by recommendation; identity is unaffected because ADR-0025 excludes `layers[]`); **D3** prose/skill imports become `ContextItem`/`Procedure` entities with `Text{authority = external, origin = import}` leaves and typed frontmatter (ADR-0023 (3), ADR-0086), never above `external`; **D4** selectors and deny-lists are legal only inside layers and are pinned by the kernel's `resolve`; **D5** `root_kind = hosted` selects `AgentProcess.hosted` and refuses `slots` (ADR-0150). Invariant A-2: the desugared output validates against the `hir/1` schema alone — no authoring construct survives desugaring.
2. **Operations** (pure over declared inputs; out-of-process over the canonical encoding, T-LCD-12): `assemble(source, snapshot, mode ∈ {plan, seal}) → AssemblyResult{status, sealed?, identity?, report, plan, snapshot, derivation_key}` sequencing `desugar → compose → resolve → validate_assembly` and retaining the sealed document only in `seal` mode; `plan(source | (sealed_a, sealed_b), snapshot') → AssemblyPlan{diff: AssemblyDiff, defaulted_paths[], authored_paths[], layer_map, drift?, exit ∈ {no_changes, changes, error}, sameness}`; `apply(source, snapshot, publish?) → {sealed, identity, report, published?}` calling the registry's `publish` (ADR-0037) and refusing when `plan.exit = error`; `validate_batch(points, snapshot) → [ValidationReport]` as the pre-spend gate for `enumerate` output; `explain(sealed, path | node_id) → {value, layer, defaulted, diagnostics[]}`. `derivation_key = H(idp ∥ "assemble" ∥ canonical(desugared layers ∥ snapshot ∥ class_catalog.version ∥ assembler.version))` is the cache key (ADR-0019 decision 4 shape).
3. **Invariants.** S-1 dry-run parity: `plan` and `seal` modes yield identical `report`, `plan`, `identity`, `snapshot`. S-2 one resolver: the service never pins a reference itself. S-3 no semantics: every returned `SealedDefinition`, `ValidationReport` and `HirDiff` is byte-identical to the kernel's output on the same inputs (conformance test re-runs the kernel). S-4 sealed outputs are closed and secret-free (channel names only; ADR-0057). S-5 `apply` refuses on error status. S-6 a publish whose diff vs `supersedes` classifies `authority_delta = widening` / `budget_delta = loosening` / `validity_delta = loosening` requires `origin = human` with attestation — forwarded from ADR-0017/0037, never decided here. S-7 the sealed definition, `ValidationReport`, `registry_snapshot_id`, `layers[]` and `derivation_key` are stored together as registry records (ADR-0025 consequences; ADR-0139 `definition` member). S-8 defaulting fills values only (Kubernetes convention, S-186); a required slot is never added by defaulting (`SlotUnbound` error); every defaulted path is listed in `plan.defaulted_paths`.
4. **The "meta" invariants.** M-1: no run manifest references a definition that is not a sealed registry record produced through this pipeline (`configuration_version_id` keys on its `version_id`, ADR-0036). M-2: every Lab operation that changes a harness — sweep point, ablation, evolution candidate, ladder promotion, hosted re-declaration — is a `HirDiff` between sealed definitions applied through `apply(base, diff)` and re-validated, never a mutation of an authored file. The sealed definition is the sole exchange object between J1, J2, J3, J5, I5/I7 and the surfaces, crossing the ADR-0050 D2 boundary as data.
5. **Placement (ADR-0050, by layer only).** `desugar/compose/resolve/validate_assembly/seal/identity/diff` execute in the kernel / reference-runtime layer (D1 row 1); orchestration, caching, batch validation, plan rendering and publication live in the laboratory layer (D1 row 3) and reach the kernel over the D2 boundary. The D3 verb set must gain assembly verbs generated from the kernel's schema source (CF-322, WS-K4/L5).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: authoring vs sealed document separation in every mature system (S-110 `profiles/agent_profile.py` L1–7; S-112 `recipe/validate_recipe.rs` L25–32; S-111 `config/config.ts` L413–547 — Tier B source); dry-run parity as an implemented property (S-110 `profiles/resolver.py` L325–336) and as a protocol rule (S-501 Kubernetes dry-run, same authorization, generated values returned); typed change previews before apply with a three-valued exit (S-499 Terraform plan); defaulting that never rewrites user values and validation that never silently corrects (S-186), with goose's `ensure_analyze_for_developer` as the counter-example that motivates S-8 (S-112 `recipe/mod.rs` L216–245); SHA-pinned resolved sources for deterministic resume (S-110 `plugin/types.py` L119–150); content-addressed cache keys (ADR-0019, S-225 Nix input addressing via ADR-0038); a hosted agent as one data document with typed `params` (S-118 `docs/AGENT_YAML_SPEC.md` L37–62). Modular recombination requires uniform slot interfaces (S-024 AgentSquare — mechanism only).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/profiles/resolver.py` L293–400 (resolve + dry-run twin), `profiles/agent_profile.py` L67–76, L85–91 (S-110 @3fc7b22); `packages/opencode/src/config/config.ts` L41–50, L337–368, L413–423, L529–547 and `config/agent.ts` L11–31 (S-111 @9f8db11); `crates/goose/src/recipe/build_recipe/mod.rs` L20–43, `recipe/validate_recipe.rs` L25–32 (S-112 @fae91d0); `omnigent/docs/AGENT_YAML_SPEC.md` L13–62 (S-118 @6a069da). The semantics-free service with dry-run parity, content-keyed caching, always-materialised override layers and the M-1/M-2 invariants is `no-precedent / novel` as an integration; each mechanism is precedented.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) a service layer risks becoming a second implementation (the CF-056 failure) — answered by S-3 as a tested invariant; (ii) materialising ad-hoc overrides bloats provenance — answered by content addressing (identical overrides are one record) and identity exclusion of `layers[]`; (iii) S-074 finds every configuration schema bespoke, so an authoring grammar may be adopted by nobody — accepted per ADR-0003/0006: the source format serves native participants of an instrument, and hosted participants keep their own configuration (ADR-0004), desugared at product granularity; (iv) authoring sugar (`base`, imports) could hide what is sealed — answered by `plan.defaulted_paths`/`authored_paths`/`layer_map` and `explain`. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier and task class; assumes definitions are small (≤10⁴ nodes) so parity checks are cheap; assumes WS-J2 exposes ADR-0037 `publish/resolve/snapshot`; the placement clause assumes ADR-0050 D1 stands (a revalidation trigger there would move the orchestration, not the contracts); the OQ-076 answer is a recommendation WS-J3/K1/K4 confirm.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C1 / Stage 3** (`AssemblySource` + desugaring D1–D5; `assemble/plan/apply/validate_batch/explain`; S-1…S-8; M-1/M-2 as gates of the sweep engine; Stage-0 anchor authored as a source — dossier AC-J1-11); **C1 / Stage 5** (stage 6b `UnexpressibleSurface` at plan time with WS-C3); **C2 / Stage 4** (hosted desugaring populated with WS-J6).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies: T-LCD-12 (pure operations over canonical documents; AC-J1-10 out of process), T-LCD-10 (identity is the kernel's; two authoring paths → one `semantic_id`, AC-J1-2), T-LCD-14 (defaulted budget-relevant values are visible; `authority_cap` widening is refused at `compose`), T-LCD-06 (hosted sources desugar onto `OpaqueProcess` without new native fields), T-LCD-02 (imports enter only as `Text` leaves). Does not address T-LCD-03/-04/-11/-13 (executable; the sealed definition is their input) — no mitigating test needed beyond `plan` exposing `lcd_report`. **ADR-0024 rung:** operations and desugaring are MUST-code; `AssemblySource`, layers, overrides, imports, `PublishSpec` are MUST-data.

## Consequences

- WS-K1/K4 expose `assemble/plan/apply/explain/diff` and add assembly verbs to the boundary contract (CF-322); WS-J3 consumes `validate_batch` before spend and materialises overrides through D2; WS-J2 stores the S-7 record set and serves `publish`; WS-I5/I7 read `plan.diff` and key attribution on `HirDiff` ops; WS-J6 supplies the `ParticipantRecord` D5 resolves against.
- Spec §6.1 carries the pipeline diagram and M-1/M-2 as the section's lead invariants.

## Reversibility

**High** for the service surface (it composes ratified operations; renaming or splitting operations touches no stored object). **Medium** for D2 (changing how overrides are materialised changes stored `layers[]` provenance, not identities). **Low** for M-1/M-2: allowing a run without a sealed registry record, or a harness change that is not a `HirDiff`, would re-open the divergence and attribution gaps every upstream ADR closed and would require superseding ADR-0025/0036.
