# ADR-0190 — The evaluation protocol `lab/value-of-compute-v1` and Lab-side value-of-information: `static` / `uniform` / `oracle_allocation` baselines, matched-cap primary contrast with an `iso_cost` frontier, matched-total mandatory for any arm with `search_budget > 0`, replay refused, adoption only by `ComparisonReport`; `voi_weighted` as a search-only `ValidationStrategy` — **every benefit claim is conditional on matched budget**

**Status:** ratified
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-F4 (consumers: WS-J3, WS-J4, WS-J5, WS-I2, WS-I5, WS-I7, WS-K2) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** companion ADR-0188/ADR-0189, ADR-0002 (matched-budget conditionality on C4), ADR-0041 (M1/M2/M3; refusals), ADR-0046 (protocol; §7 gates; adaptive selection inside search), ADR-0045 (design; pre-registration), ADR-0105 (the boundary moves only by `ComparisonReport`), ADR-0122 (`replayed_trajectory` refused), ADR-0154/0155/0156 (`ExperimentSpec`; scheduler; `adaptive_search`, `ValidationStrategy`), ADR-0157/0158/0159 (analysis; `contrast`; frontier; `oracle_best_of_n`), ADR-0143 (splits), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-09/-14/-15); phase-3 §6.1 (WS-F4 allocates within experiment/instrument nodes; `OrderPlan` re-ordering under a recorded seed); OQ-025, OQ-121, OQ-124, OQ-423; CF-402; R-2.6.4, R-2.10.3, R-2.10.4, R-2.9.5

## Context

The brief requires "an evaluation protocol proving the scheduler beats matched-budget baselines" and an ADR "flagged with matched-budget conditionality". The disconfirming evidence is the reason: under matched feedback and inference budgets adaptive harness methods do not consistently beat test-time scaling and transfer weakly (S-083, `provisional (counter)`); routers and allocators fail to reliably beat simple baselines (S-449, `provisional`); more calls can hurt (S-453, Tier A); the only quantitative "subagents help" claims opened (S-036, S-064) carry `search_budget`/matched status `unknown`; and replay-scored allocation rankings have ≈ 0 similarity to live outcomes (S-454, `provisional (mech)`). The program already owns the machinery: `MatchSpec` with three modes and refusal semantics (ADR-0041), `eval_budget` equality with three benefit kinds and C4 gates (ADR-0046), the experiment as a run with four enforcement points (ADR-0155), `adaptive_search` with `disagreement_weighted`/`successive_halving` search-only (ADR-0156 decision 4), frontiers with `cost_of_pass` and synthesized `oracle_best_of_n` baselines (ADR-0159), and the rule that a default moves only on a `ComparisonReport` (ADR-0105). Task-CoEvolve (S-082) and HarnessLens (S-089), both `provisional`, motivate spending evaluation budget where candidates disagree; phase-3 §6.1 allows WS-F4 to allocate within the experiment and instrument nodes and to re-order `OrderPlan` only under a recorded permutation seed.

## Options considered

1. **Report scheduler benefit as a cost-adjusted Pareto view only.** Rejected: cannot answer "did the scheduler help at the same budget?" (ADR-0046 option 1 reasoning; T-LCD-14).
2. **Evaluate scheduler variants offline by replaying logged trajectories under alternative allocations.** Rejected: Replay Gap mechanism; ADR-0122 already refuses `replayed_trajectory`.
3. **Compare only against `static` (no scheduler).** Rejected: a scheduler that merely spends *more* would "win" on success while losing on cost; `uniform` (maximal allocation) and a hindsight `oracle_allocation` bound are needed to locate the variant on the frontier (S-453; ADR-0159 upper-bound pattern).
4. **Let learned variants be compared under `matched_cap` like heuristics.** Rejected: their warm-up/training is search spend; ADR-0041 M3 and ADR-0046 §7 require matched total for anything with a `SearchBudgetRecord`.
5. **A new Lab engine for evaluation-budget allocation.** Rejected: ADR-0156's `ValidationStrategy` already admits adaptive selection search-only; a variant suffices (T-LCD-12 contracts, no second engine).
6. **A pre-registered factorial recipe with `static`/`uniform`/`oracle_allocation` baselines, matched-cap primary contrast plus `iso_cost` frontier, matched-total for any searched arm, replay refused, adoption only by `ComparisonReport`; and `voi_weighted` as a `ValidationStrategy` variant (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`lab/value-of-compute-v1` is shipped as an `ExperimentSpec`** (ADR-0154; `kind: comparative`, `design.kind: full_factorial`). Factors: `compute_policy ∈ {static, uniform, rules, bandit [, predictor at C4]}` at component level × `model_snapshot` (≥ 2 families, roles fixed) × `environment` (one coding, one terminal suite, ADR-0144) × `budget` (two `eval_budget` levels, tight and wide) × replicate (≥ 5; `seed_honoured` declared). The definition supplies a `Goal`, a `Procedure` with ≥ 1 declared `parallel` step, one calibrated critic placement and an `EnsembleProcedure{sample_k_vote, k_max}` so every `ComputeOption` is reachable; `uniform` binds every option at its configured maximum (k_max samples, spawn wherever declared, evaluator always on).
2. **Matching.** Primary contrast: `MatchSpec{mode: matched_cap, dimensions: {model_calls, tokens.output.visible, time.wall_ms, spend}, tolerance: 0.10, model_scope: same_snapshot, cache_policy: cold_start}` with `budget_utilization` reported for every arm (an under-spending arm is not "cheaper", ADR-0041 M1). A separate `iso_cost` arm yields the frontier with `cost_of_pass` (ADR-0159). **Any arm with `search_budget > 0`** (`bandit` warm-up, `predictor` training, any evolution-produced scheduling rule) **is comparable only under `matched_total`** (ADR-0041 M3; ADR-0046 §7); the engine refuses `IncommensurableMatch` for such an arm under `matched_cap` (AC-F4-12). This is the matched-budget conditionality of every benefit claim about a scheduler.
3. **Baselines.** `static` (the null), `uniform` (maximal allocation — S-453 predicts it can lose), and an `oracle_allocation` row synthesized by WS-J4 from the `uniform` arm's replicates (per task, the cheapest option set that succeeded; a hindsight **upper bound**, labelled as such — ADR-0159 decision 5 pattern) to compute `scheduling.regret_vs_oracle`. Hosted participants enter only as configuration-level effort/model coordinates when `SUPPORTED`, never at component level (`InadmissibleFactor`).
4. **Pre-registered hypotheses.** H0: no headline difference `rules` vs `static` at matched cap; H1: `rules` spends less than `uniform` at equal `task_success`; H2: the `rules` effect is larger at the tight budget (budget × policy `contrast`, T-LCD-09; S-072 mechanism); H3: `bandit` ≥ `rules` only after `min_n` per cell and only under `matched_total`; H4: `scheduling.overhead` is zero model calls for `rules` and ≤ 1 % of subject spend for `bandit`; H5: `spawn_subagent` share tracks declared parallel steps. Primary metrics `{capability.task_success, efficiency.cost_of_pass, scheduling.overhead}` (Holm-controlled, ADR-0158); secondaries exploratory.
5. **Metrics.** Headline `task_success` from deterministic held-out/instrument oracles with per-task (c, n); `cost_of_pass`; `scheduling.decision_count`, `scheduling.option_share`, `scheduling.overhead`, `scheduling.regret_vs_oracle`, `scheduling.prior_coverage`, `scheduling.advice_compliance` (T-LCD-13); `reliability.runaway_rate`, `budget_exhausted` rate; `control.boundary_drift` (must be ∅); tails at P95. All `applies_to_classes = {native}`; hosted rows `n/a{class}`.
6. **Acceptance rule for adopting a non-`static` default.** A `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind: artifact_benefit, budget_match.status: matched, label: confirmatory}` in which `task_success` Δ's interval excludes 0, **or** a TOST non-inferiority verdict on `task_success` (ADR-0158) together with a cost Δ interval below 0; no veto metric regresses; `transfer` on ≥ 1 held-out model family reported with sign and interval (ADR-0046 §7); `outcome_bounds.verdict = robust` (ADR-0157). A variant that fails is kept as a registered variant and never becomes a default (ADR-0105 rule). Replay-scored inputs are refused (`replayed_trajectory`).
7. **Lab-side value-of-information.** `ValidationStrategy` gains `voi_weighted{estimator ∈ {disagreement, expected_information_gain}, lambda, min_inclusion_fraction, min_replicates}` (dialect bump of ADR-0156 decision 4 — CF-402): weights `w_t = max(p̄_t(1 − p̄_t), ℓ_t) + λ/√n_t` over candidate-outcome variance on the `search`/`dev` split (S-082 mechanism), or preference for cells whose outcome most changes the ranking of surviving candidates under successive-halving rungs (S-504); operation `voi_next(experiment_state, capacity, strategy) → [(cell_id, inclusion_p)]` called from ADR-0155 `next`. Invariants: V-1 admissible only in `adaptive_search` over `split_labels ⊆ {search, dev}` after a `SplitAssignmentRecord` (`AdaptiveOutsideSearch` otherwise); V-2 rows `estimated{estimator = hajek, inclusion_probabilities_ref}` and never the sole basis of acceptance (ADR-0046 decision 6); V-3 spend only from the experiment's search/instrument nodes, `charged_to = instrument`; V-4 `OrderPlan` re-ordering only as a recorded permutation under the experiment's `permutation_seed` with the drift bracket intact; V-5 the allocator's own estimation cost is `harness_overhead.scheduling` on the experiment run; V-6 confirmatory `full_set` arms are untouchable. Allocation *across* designs stays with WS-I5 (OQ-423).
8. **Refusals.** `MissingMatchSpec`, `UnmatchedBudget`, `IncommensurableMatch` (searched arm under `matched_cap`; cross-model token match), `InadmissibleFactor`, `AdaptiveOutsideSearch`, `replayed_trajectory`.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies **T-LCD-14** (every scheduler comparison under a `MatchSpec`; matched-total for searched arms; search vs artifact separated; replay refused), **T-LCD-09** (policy, budget and their interaction are explicit factors; `contrast` over probed cells), **T-LCD-15** (class-scoped metrics; hosted `n/a`), T-LCD-05 (a losing variant's `retirement` arm is the removal test for its rules), T-LCD-07 (hosted coordinates only when `SUPPORTED`), T-LCD-12 (`voi_next` is an operation of an existing contract). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-04/-06/-08/-10/-11/-13. **ADR-0024 rung:** the recipe document, hypotheses, tolerances, weights and strategy parameters are MUST-data (experiment layer); the refusal checks, the matched-total rule for searched arms, `voi_next` and the `oracle_allocation` synthesis are MUST-code.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** unmatched budgets reverse conclusions (S-083 `provisional (counter)`; doc 2 §8; ADR-0002/0041/0046 ratified); more calls can hurt (S-453, Tier A) — the reason `uniform` is a baseline; replay-scored allocation is invalid (S-454 `provisional (mech)`; ADR-0122); budget × harness interaction exists (S-072 `provisional`; ADR-0046 context); evaluation budget has value-of-information but cheap estimates are noisy (S-082 `provisional`, ρ = 0.13 at 7 %; S-089 `provisional`); successive halving as the promotion rule (S-504, Tier A); hindsight best-of-N is an upper bound (S-567; ADR-0159). The C3/C4 protocol rests on ratified ADRs and Tier-A statistics; the `provisional` sources motivate hypotheses and the search-only VOI variant, never a default.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** hal-harness `hal/agent_runner.py` + `hal/utils/weave_utils.py` @ 16bb03e and harbor `src/harbor/models/job/config.py` @ 7d5285b (cost-controlled runs; per-attempt cost — via ADR-0041/0046); inspect_ai `util/_limit.py` `apply_limits` @ 75f4891 (uniform per-sample limits across solvers); tau-bench `tau_bench/run.py` L181–203 @ 59a200c (per-task replicates from which hindsight baselines are computable); W&B sweep `early_terminate{hyperband}` shape (S-505 via ADR-0156). **An allocation-policy experiment with `static`/`uniform`/`oracle_allocation` baselines and matched-total gating for searched arms is `no-precedent / novel`; `voi_weighted` is a variant of a ratified contract.**
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) the recipe is expensive (five policies × two families × two suites × two budgets × ≥ 5 replicates) — accepted; `predictor` is C4 and the Stage-4 first execution is `{static, uniform, rules}` × 1 family × 1 suite × 2 budgets, the full factorial at Stage 5; power via ADR-0157 A13 (OQ-121); (ii) `oracle_allocation` is undeployable — labelled upper bound, used only for regret; (iii) VOI estimates are noisy at low sampling fractions — search-only, `estimated` rows, never acceptance; (iv) matched-total can still be unfair if allocation differs inside the search budget (S-092) — `SearchBudgetRecord.allocation` is recorded so the critique is checkable (ADR-0046 (c)(iii)); (v) hosted products cannot be scheduled natively — configuration-level coordinates only, stratified. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** **every benefit claim about a scheduler is conditional on `budget_match.status = matched` under the declared `MatchSpec`; learned variants additionally on matched total with a complete `SearchBudgetRecord`; results hold per model family, suite and budget level probed — unprobed cells are `unknown`, never interpolated (ADR-0012);** assumes deterministic oracles for the Stage-4/5 suites (ADR-0144) and F3 delegation plus the ADR-0123 ensemble at Stage 4.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C3 / Stage 4** — recipe registered and first executed with `{static, uniform, rules}`; refusals AC-F4-12; **C3 / Stage 5** — `bandit` arm under `matched_total`; `voi_weighted` and AC-F4-13; **C4 / Stage 6** — `predictor` arms and scheduling rules as evolution candidates through ADR-0046 §7 gates consumed by WS-I5.

## Consequences

- WS-J3 grows `ValidationStrategy` by `voi_weighted` (dialect bump, CF-402) and calls `voi_next` from `next`; WS-J4 synthesizes `oracle_allocation` and computes `scheduling.regret_vs_oracle`; WS-J5 stores the reports; WS-I2 registers the `scheduling.*` declarations; WS-K2 renders `explain` beside the frontier.
- WS-I5 inherits the rule that a scheduling policy or rule proposed by evolution is admissible only under matched total with the recipe's baselines; WS-I7 may use `compute_decision_outcome` rows as designed-ablation inputs, never as counterfactuals without a live arm.
- The packaged default stays `static` until a report satisfies decision 6.

## Reversibility

**High** for the recipe (MUST-data; re-registering with new levels is a new spec with `derived-from{amendment}`), **medium** for the matched-total rule on searched arms (relaxing it would re-admit the S-083 class of confounded claims into stored results). Rows recorded without the recipe's `MatchSpec` can never be made comparable after the fact, which is why the spec lands with the Stage-4 contract.
