# ADR-0171 — The time-travel viewer contract over the run ledger and the branch model: scrub vs fork, tree-with-HEAD and rewind, replay-mode badges, deep links, and the requirements R-TT-1…12 on WS-B1/WS-B4

**Status:** ratified
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-K2 (requirements on WS-B1, WS-B4; consumed by WS-K1, WS-I7) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0026 (`read/project/subscribe`, view kinds, `durability`), ADR-0027 (`head.moved`, `forked_from`, `ir_refs[]`, `causes[]`), ADR-0028 (reconstruction; declared deterministic replay), ADR-0042 (`trace_view`, `open_scopes`), ADR-0133 (branch model, coherent fork points, rollback with restore), ADR-0134 (speculative containment, `deferred`), ADR-0135 (replay validity, intervention record), ADR-0066 (`audit_ref`), ADR-0072/0074 (D1 `context_view`), ADR-0050 D2; CF-359/03; OQ-388/04; OQ-082, OQ-320; R-2.11.2, R-2.2.4; T-LCD-06/-07/-13/-15

## Context

Every audited harness branches, forks or rolls back its session (WS-B1 F4; WS-B4 F1–F3) and every surface that renders history must therefore handle a *tree with a HEAD*: Cursor's clients "rewind [their] stream" on retried steps (S-052); Codex's `thread/rollback` rewrites history but "does not revert local file changes … Clients are responsible" (S-107); LangGraph Studio offers time travel and its docs must warn that "Replay re-executes nodes—it doesn't just read from cache" (S-542). ADR-0027/0133/0135 already make rollback and branch moves appended events, couple forks to environment snapshots, and label every branch with a `replay_mode` and a `ReplayValidityReport`. The omniscient-debugging line (S-543, Tier A) fixes what a time-travel viewer needs from its store: indexed random access over the whole trace, filtered partial views, and bounded query time. The 2026 viewer preprints (S-544…11, `provisional`) are view compilers over one record; none forks or shows validity. The decision is the contract between the viewer and B1/B4: which affordances are pure projections, which are operations, what must be shown before an operation runs, and which additive requests go to ADR-0026.

## Options considered

1. **Scrub = `project(run, view, until_seq)` (pure); fork/rollback/discard/promote/replay = ADR-0133–0135 operations with human provenance; tree-with-HEAD rendering with mandatory rewind handling and replay-mode/validity badges; additive view/index requests to B1** — chosen.
2. *Viewer-side replay engine* (the surface reconstructs context or re-drives the loop from events) — rejected: duplicates D1's `context_view` and B4's `replay`, cannot produce a matching `view_hash`, and would let the UI claim determinism the kernel does not (ADR-0028/0135).
3. *Linear transcript with rollback as truncation* (Codex/opencode client model) — rejected: hides discarded attempts that attribution and audit need (ADR-0027 never deletes; WS-B4 F3); the SF3/SF5 failure signature when the environment is not restored.
4. *Fork points chosen freely by the user at any seq* — rejected: incoherent forks are refused or coerced by ADR-0133 (`ForkPointNotCoherent`, `coerce_to_boundary`); the viewer renders `coherent_fork_points` and offers the nearest coherent seq.
5. *Snapshot-per-message time travel* (gemini-cli/opencode shadow-repo shape) — rejected as the contract (B4 already chose `EnvSnapshot` with declared coverage); admitted as what the viewer shows when `EnvSnapshot.kind = workspace_tree`.

## Decision

1. **Two affordances, never conflated.** *Scrub* is `project(run, view_kind, until_seq = s)` for `view_kind ∈ {context_view, trace_view, effect_ledger, cost_view, branch_tree}` at any durable `s` (coherent or not) — nothing runs, nothing is written; "what did the model see at this call" is the D1 `context_view` whose `view_hash` equals `context.assembled.view_hash` at that call (AC-K2-3). *Fork/rollback/discard/promote/replay/counterfactual* are the ADR-0133–0135 operations invoked with `provenance.origin = human(author_ref, role)` and a surface-session reference; the confirmation renders `fork_point`, coherence (or the nearest coherent points), `env_binding` with `EnvSnapshot.coverage/uncaptured[]`, the budget slice, the `SpeculationPolicy`, the predicted `replay_mode`, and — for `discard`/`rollback` — the effects that will be compensated and those that cannot be.
2. **Tree with a HEAD.** The viewer's model is the `parent_event_id` tree plus inter-run `forked_from` lineage, rendered as the HEAD path by default with discarded/abandoned subtrees folded at their `lifecycle.branch.disposed` point and always reachable; `lifecycle.head.moved`, `lifecycle.run.rolled_back` and `branch.disposed` are **rewind frames**: the rendered path switches, nothing is removed, and the rewind note (ADR-0133) is shown at the next assembled context (AC-K2-4).
3. **Replay-mode and validity badges are mandatory.** Every branch and every branch-derived results row renders `replay_mode ∈ {reconstruct_reexecute, deterministic}` and `ReplayValidityReport.mode ∈ {deterministic, re_executed, degraded, invalid}` with `reasons[]`; the surface never offers `deterministic` unless `variant_declares_deterministic_replay ∧ observability_level ⊇ model_io` (whether the surface may request it at all is OQ-391; the kernel downgrades regardless). Candidate-fork-point rankers (S-547 shape) are advisory views labelled as such, never a replay.
4. **Deep links.** Every rendered event is an `EventRef`; every citation is an `audit_ref{run_id, seq, hash, checkpoint_ref, inclusion_proof?}` (ADR-0066) that the viewer verifies on open; a link re-opens the exact view at the same `until_seq`, filter and branch; `ir_refs[]` on events link to the definition node (capability, rule, validator) that produced them.
5. **Requirements on WS-B1/WS-B4 (R-TT-1…12, WS-K2 §6.2).** Satisfied as ratified: random access and reverse scan by `seq` (ADR-0026 `read`), filtered reads by plane/class/scope, projection at arbitrary `until_seq`, inclusive-cursor `subscribe` with per-class `durability`, rewind semantics, `coherent_fork_points`, content-addressed validity/intervention records, blobs with redaction tombstones, fork-from-viewer with lineage, `audit_ref` citations. **Additive requests** (CF-359/03): (a) `branch_tree` listed as a C0 view kind in ADR-0026's view list and crossing the boundary; (b) `ir_refs` as a `read.filter` key backed by a rebuildable index; (c) `durability` carried explicitly on every frame that crosses the kernel↔surfaces boundary; (d) `coherent_fork_points`, `project`, `read`, `subscribe` and the branch operations in WS-K4's surface verb group; (e) a query-latency acceptance criterion on B1's derived index — `read(filter)` and `project(until_seq)` bounded by result size, not run length (R-TT-9; bound in OQ-388).
6. **Hosted participants.** A hosted run renders its branch tree only if `fork = supported` in its capability declaration; otherwise the time-travel affordances render `n/a{class}` and the viewer degrades to scrub over the events the participant delivered (T-LCD-06/-07/-15).
7. **Staging.** Scrub, tree rendering, badges and deep links: C1/Stage 4 (read-only, over inter-run forks and rollbacks from B4's C0/Stage-2 slice). Operations from the viewer (`fork/discard/promote/rollback/replay/counterfactual`): C2/Stage 5, with intra-run branches when B4's C2/Stage 4 lands. Counterfactual arms rendered as `ComparisonReport`s with `replay_mode` on every row: C2/Stage 6 with WS-I7.

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-06/-07 (no native field for hosted participants; `fork` is a declared capability, `n/a` when absent), T-LCD-15 (branch/replay cells class-scoped), T-LCD-13 (V2/V3 render the delivered/activated/followed chain per artifact at any `until_seq`), T-LCD-10 (branch and snapshot identity by content, never by name), T-LCD-12 (every affordance is an operation over the canonical encoding). Not addressed: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-05/-08/-09/-11/-14 (owned elsewhere). **ADR-0024 rung:** the viewer is MUST-code on the surface side; no MUST-data record is introduced (the `SpeculationPolicy` it displays is B4's).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** stream rewind on retry (S-052, production); history-only rollback as a client burden (S-107 `ThreadRollbackParams` docs) and the trace-only checkpoint failure class (S-476 via WS-B4, `provisional`, failure signatures only); "replay re-executes" (S-542 LangGraph docs); indexed, partial, query-bounded traces as the requirement for interactive time travel (S-543 TOD, Tier A; abstract via search summaries); view compilation from one record with back-pointers (S-546, `provisional`); branch-point ranking without replay (S-547, `provisional`, advisory only); durable/transient framing and replay-then-go-live (S-110 `session_socket.py`); Shepherd's trace as an inspectable first-class object (S-078, `provisional (mech)`).
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** software-agent-sdk `openhands-agent-server/openhands/agent_server/session_socket.py` (`DurableFrame{seq}`, `_replay`, `go_live`) @3fc7b22; codex `codex-rs/app-server-protocol/src/protocol/v2/thread.rs` (`ThreadRollbackParams`, `ThreadForkParams`), `thread_history_projection.rs` @0735c51; langgraph `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py` (`parent_config` chain) @e539ac1 with the time-travel docs; pi-mono `packages/agent/src/harness/session/fork.ts` and software-agent-sdk `conversation/state.py` (`navigate_to`) via WS-B4 §4c. An integrated viewer that renders `replay_mode`/validity and `ir_refs` links is `no-precedent / novel` (WS-K2 §5 negative results).
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) linear transcripts are simpler and what most products show — accepted as the *default rendering* (HEAD path), not the model; (ii) users want to fork anywhere — refused/coerced by ADR-0133 for the reasons WS-B4 §5 gives (Claude SDK `resume_drops_turn` refusal precedent); (iii) a viewer-side replay would be faster than round-tripping `project` — rejected because it cannot match `view_hash` and would misreport determinism (ADR-0028); (iv) indexing every run for interactive queries costs storage — the index is a rebuildable derived view (ADR-0026) and TOD shows the cost is the price of interactivity; the bound is measured at Stage 4 (OQ-388). None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** assumes native participants (`ledger` observability) for full trees; hosted participants only with `fork = supported`; assumes B4's C0/Stage-2 slice (inter-run forks, workspace-tree snapshots, rollback with restore) for the read-only viewer and B4's C2/Stage-4 slice for intra-run operations; deterministic replay only for declaring control-strategy variants with `model_io` recorded; budget-independent (forks are budget slices the kernel enforces).
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** C1/Stage 4 (scrub, tree, badges, deep links); C2/Stage 5 (operations from the viewer); C2/Stage 6 (counterfactual arms with WS-I7); prerequisite additive amendments to ADR-0026 (`branch_tree` view kind, `ir_refs` index) and the WS-K4 surface verb group at C0/Stage 1.

## Consequences

- ADR-0026's view list grows by `branch_tree` and its `read.filter` by `ir_refs` (additive, CF-360); B1's derived index gains a latency acceptance criterion.
- WS-K4's boundary carries `durability` on frames and the branch/projection verbs (CF-359).
- WS-D1's `context_view` at arbitrary `until_seq` becomes an externally exercised contract (AC-K2-3 is a D1 test too).
- The viewer can never show a "deterministic replay" the kernel did not certify; product pressure to hide `re_executed` is a rejected option here.
- WS-I7 gets its human-in-the-loop entry point (choose a fork point, author an `InterventionRecord`) with the pairing design enforced by the kernel, not the UI.

## Reversibility

**High for the viewer; medium for the additive B1 requests.** Dropping the viewer changes nothing upstream; `branch_tree`/`ir_refs` index are derived views that can be removed by deleting a projection, but the CLI (WS-K1) and WS-I7 would want them regardless.
