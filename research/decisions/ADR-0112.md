# ADR-0112 — Claims are typed `model_claim` Observations reconciled against authoritative handles: the claim model, the claim-ledger substrate, four-valued agreement, and the divergence taxonomy D1–D10 with deterministic-first detectors

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-G2 (consumers: WS-F1, WS-F2, WS-G1, WS-G3, WS-B2, WS-B3, WS-D1, WS-D2, WS-E5, WS-H1, WS-I1, WS-I2, WS-J4, WS-J6, WS-C3) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0016 (`Observation.source = model_claim`; "`model_claim` never satisfies a Validator alone"; `Goal.success_criteria`), ADR-0026 (`verification.claim.recorded/reconciled` reserved at C0/Stage 3), ADR-0030/0031 (effect states, `probe`, read-only exemption), ADR-0033/0034 (authority `delegate`; P3/P6; evidence class ≠ authority class, CF-080), ADR-0014/0047 (detector provenance; headline oracle classes; judge obligations), ADR-0024 (MUST-code vs MUST-data), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-01/-02/-05/-08/-10/-12/-13/-15); OQ-011, OQ-102, OQ-125; CF-237…CF-240, -07; R-2.7.2, R-2.7.1, R-2.2.2, R-2.9.2

## Context

Doc 2 §5.3 defines execution alignment as continual belief-state reconciliation — "the model emits claims about what it has read, changed, tested, or achieved; the harness owns authoritative handles … verification checks whether the narrative and external state still agree" — and §12 lists it as contribution direction 4. Harness-Bench defines the property as "the degree to which a harness preserves correspondence among the agent's reasoning, the observed workspace state, the actions taken through tools, and the conditions checked by the evaluator" and finds Evidence/Grounding and Artifact Commitment failures at 14.6 % and 11.1 % of failures (S-062, `provisional (mech)`). Independent 2026 work characterizes *false success* — a final message asserting completion while environment state shows otherwise — at 45–48 % of failures in single-control τ²-bench domains and 75.8 % among self-assessing AppWorld architectures, labelled *without reading text* from a status field the agent writes versus a programmatic check; the same study shows LLM judges never exceed 0.65 AUROC on the task and shift by +0.27–0.36 on confident language alone (S-416, `provisional`). Real issue trackers contribute 86 "deception" and 53 "fabrication" incidents (S-425). A source audit of seven harnesses finds no typed completion claim, no comparison of a claimed edit/test/read against its event, contract-shaped checks only (OpenHands `AgentFinishedCritic`: FinishAction ∧ non-empty patch), loop detectors, and one side-by-side self-report/judge log (browser-use). Phase 1 already fixed the primitives: a model claim is an `Observation{source: model_claim}` that never satisfies a Validator alone (ADR-0016), authority `delegate` is command authority and evidence class is orthogonal (ADR-0033/CF-080), the ledger reserves `verification.claim.recorded/reconciled` with producer G2 (ADR-0026), and every effect has a typed terminal or an explicit `unknown` (ADR-0030).

## Options considered

1. **No reconciliation layer; rely on G1 validators and G3 judges at the end.** Rejected: validators check correctness of outputs, not correspondence of the narrative; judges anchor on confident closing language (S-416 §4.3) and localize trace errors at 11 % (S-418); the compaction study shows the false result becomes durable *before* any end-of-run check (S-417).
2. **A model-based critic scoring "success likelihood" that gates retries** (OpenHands `critic/base.py`). Rejected: a judged score as a gate violates ADR-0047's headline rule; the prose nudge carries a number at implicit authority; nothing binds the score to a handle.
3. **Lexical false-success detector as the primary monitor** (S-416's TF-IDF detectors). Rejected as primary: 50 % precision at a 10 % flag rate, 0.69 zero-shot cross-domain, 0.73 across model generations, 20 % fooled by confident rewrites; admitted as a C2 *triage* oracle with calibration and expiry.
4. **Free-text claim parsing only (FActScore-style decomposition of the final message).** Rejected as sole channel: the false-success study's text-independent AppWorld label exists only because the claim was structured; production precedents (browser-use `is_done/success`, OpenHands task tracker, AppWorld status) show structured channels are cheap and deterministic to extract.
5. **Typed claims with a compiled claim surface as primary channel, parsed/judged channels as lower-confidence fallbacks, per-claim binding to authoritative handles, a four-valued agreement, and a closed divergence taxonomy with deterministic-first detectors over ratified ledger records (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Claim model.** `Claim = {claim_id, run_id, model_call_id, at_seq, kind: ClaimKind, subject: SubjectRef, predicate, asserted, evidence_refs: [EventRef | ContentAddress], extracted_by ∈ {structured(surface_field_id), parsed(grammar_ref), judged(detector_ref)}, extraction_confidence, provenance: ProvenanceRecord{origin ∈ {model, participant, subagent}, authority = delegate, …}, evidence_class = claimed}`. `ClaimKind` is a closed sum per HIR dialect: `observed | effected | verified | achieved | unachievable | progress | pending | assumption`. `SubjectRef` is a closed sum over file, effect, validator/target, criterion, artifact, detached process, environment-state selector, progress item. A claim is an `Observation{source: model_claim}` (ADR-0016) and never evidence; `validate`/`append` reject a claim with `authority > delegate` (`AuthorityExceedsOrigin`).
2. **Claim surface.** The compiled model surface of every native harness carries structured claim fields on the completion capability (`finish{criteria_status: [{criterion_ref, status ∈ {met, unmet, unverifiable}, evidence_refs[]}], artifacts_claimed[], open_items[]}`) and on progress artifacts (`{item_id, status}`); optional per-step belief-probe fields are profile-owned `ProfileRule`s with assumption-debt records (T-LCD-01/-05). Structured claims extract with confidence 1.0; parsed claims use a profile-declared grammar over `Text` leaves; judged extraction is a `Validator{kind: judge}` under WS-G3. Extraction never executes effects.
3. **Authoritative handles.** `AuthoritativeHandle{kind ∈ {ledger_event, effect_state, validator_verdict, world_state, environment_probe, task_contract, progress_artifact_version}, ref, at_seq, authority ∈ {kernel, environment}, freshness}`. `bind(claim) → [HandleRef]` by `kind × subject`; a handle is never the model's own text; `Unbindable ⇒ unverifiable`, never `agree`. Execution alignment therefore needs the `environment` authority class for closed-schema handle values (OQ-102 census: keep).
4. **Reconciliation record and agreement.** `reconcile(claim, handles, mode ∈ {ledger_only, probe}, until_seq) → ReconciliationRecord{agreement ∈ {agree, diverge(class), unverifiable, stale}, severity, detector ∈ {deterministic, judged}, detector_ref, confidence, evidence_refs[], probe_effect_ids[], charged_to}`; the record never mutates the claim; the claim's evidence class becomes `reconciled(agreement)` by projection. `ledger_only` is pure in `(claim, records ≤ until_seq, detector version)`; `probe` dispatches only `read_only ∧ closed-world` effects through the ordinary effect lifecycle and monitor (ADR-0030/0031), charged to `subject` when the reconciler is a bound component of the harness under test and to `instrument` when a Lab detector invokes it (ADR-0039). Deterministic detectors read closed-schema fields only; judged detectors satisfy ADR-0047 §3.
5. **Divergence taxonomy (closed per dialect).** D1 `phantom_observation`, D2 `phantom_effect`, D3 `unverified_verification`, D4 `stale_belief`, D5 `contract_gap`, D6 `open_effect_at_completion`, D7 `censored_evidence`, D8 `progress_regression`, D9 `evidence_inversion`, D10 `no_progress_loop` — definitions and detectors per WS-G2 dossier §6.3. D2/D3/D5/D6 are ledger-only and C0; D1/D4/D7/D8/D9/D10 are C2. `unachievable` claims are the honest-failure channel and never a divergence by themselves.
6. **Ledger obligations.** `verification.claim.recorded{claim | ref, kind, subject, extracted_by, extraction_confidence, evidence_refs[]}` and `verification.claim.reconciled{claim_ref, agreement, divergence_class?, severity, detector, detector_ref, confidence, evidence_refs[], probe_effect_ids[], mode, charged_to}` extend ADR-0026's reserved rows (CF-239); both carry mandatory provenance (ADR-0035 table); `project(run, claim_ledger, until_seq)` is a derived view with a `derived_from` watermark.
7. **Invariants.** No operation of the reconciler raises a label, widens a grant or budget, executes a non-read-only effect, or edits history; a `parsed` or `judged` record alone never produces an intervention above `annotate` (WS-G2 ADR-2); every `diverge` record cites at least one handle record in `evidence_refs`.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-01 (no per-model branch in claim schema or detectors; the surface is profile-owned), T-LCD-02 (parsed claims and rubrics are typed `Text` leaves with provenance), T-LCD-05 (claim-surface and belief-probe rules carry debt records), T-LCD-08, T-LCD-10 (claims bind to `semantic_id`s, never surface names), T-LCD-12, T-LCD-15 (declarations are class-scoped; hosted rows `n/a`). Not applicable: T-LCD-03/-04/-06/-07/-09/-11/-13/-14 (no lowering target or hosting edge; the notice chain and budget matching are WS-G2 ADR-2/-3).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: execution-alignment failures exist as a class and are defined by correspondence among reasoning, workspace state, tool actions and evaluator conditions — S-062 (`provisional (mech)`); false completion is frequent, environment-dependent and invisible to judges — S-416 (`provisional`); real-incident deception/fabrication — S-425 (`provisional`); compaction converts observed-but-unpersisted output into confirmed claims — S-417 (`provisional`, single researcher); harness mediation changes beliefs while preserving success — S-148 (`provisional (mech)`); deterministic checks over tool results catch injected and provoked fabrications with zero false positives while statistical monitors miss organic ones — S-420 (`provisional`); task-verification failures as a category — S-403 (2025); claim verification requires cited evidence and a three-way label — S-428 FEVER (Tier A), S-429 FActScore (Tier A/B); level-triggered reconciliation against observed state — S-426 (Tier B). The C0 content (claim schema, `model_claim` stance, agreement values, two event payloads, D2/D3/D5/D6 as ledger lookups) rests on ratified ADR-0016/0026/0030/0033 and Tier-B source precedents; the 2026 preprints corroborate mechanism only and contribute no load-bearing number.
- (b) Source-code precedent: browser-use `browser_use/agent/views.py` L307–347, L381–415 @ 50f2055 (structured `is_done/success`, `validate_success_requires_done`, `AgentBrain` fields) and `agent/service.py` L1628–1660 (self-report and judge recorded side by side, never overwritten); OpenHands `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/critic/impl/agent_finished.py` @ 3fc7b22 (contract check at finish), `conversation/stuck_detector.py` (D10 patterns), `openhands-tools/openhands/tools/task_tracker/definition.py` (progress claims); gemini-cli `packages/core/src/services/loopDetectionService.ts` @ ed2ac40 (hashed `(name, args)` loop key; non-loop list) and `utils/nextSpeakerChecker.ts` (narrative announces a call that never ran); Codex `codex-rs/core/src/turn_diff_tracker.rs` @ 0735c51 (belief-side diff "without rereading the workspace filesystem" — the expected side of D2/D4) and `core/src/context_manager/normalize.rs` (synthetic terminals); SWE-agent `sweagent/agent/agents.py` L824–845 @ 3ea751c (harness-derived submission); HAL `hal/utils/compliance_checkers.py` @ 16bb03e (severity on deterministic findings). The typed `Claim` with per-claim handle binding, the four-valued agreement, and the taxonomy D1–D10 as detectors over ADR-0030 states and ADR-0033 provenance are `no-precedent / novel` **as an integrated contract**; each element has the partial precedent named.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: capability moves the boundary and evaluators become overhead (S-046) — does not overturn: the substrate is model-independent and needed for measurement; interventions are conditioned rules with removal tests (WS-G2 ADR-2/-3). Deterministic checks verify correspondence, not correctness (S-420 limits; MAST's superficial-check chess program) — does not overturn: correctness is G1/G3's; the scope is stated as correspondence. Structured surfaces cost tokens and compliance (browser-use `flash_mode`; S-088 via ADR-0014) — does not overturn: completion fields are minimal and always-on, per-step probes are C2 and ablatable, compliance is measured by the ADR-0014 chain. Prior art (Kubernetes, runtime verification, FEVER, tool receipts) — narrows the novelty claim to the integrated contract (CF-244).
- (d) Conditionality: schema and ledger events are model- and task-independent. Deterministic detectors assume native observability (`ledger`); hosted participants get D5-shape checks over `end_state` only (`n/a` otherwise). Assumes WS-B2's effect states and WS-E1's `observation_contract.error_classes` exist (ratified/Phase 2). The *usefulness* of the parsed channel is profile-conditioned and its grammar expires (OQ-275). Assumes at most one frontier snapshot may be shared between beneficiary and judged extractor with the weaker independence recorded (ADR-0047 (c)(iii)).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — `Claim`, `ReconciliationRecord`, `AuthoritativeHandle` schemas; event payloads; structured `finish` fields on the Stage-0 reference harness. **C0 / Stage 3** — ledger-only D2/D3/D5/D6; AC-G2-1/-2 (C0 subset)/-3. **C2 / Stage 4** — the `execution_alignment` component class with D1/D4/D7/D8/D9/D10, `probe` mode, judged extraction; AC-G2-2 (rest)/-6/-7/-10. Rung on the ADR-0024 ladder: claim schemas and detectors are Core (MUST-code); the claim surface, grammar, belief probes and Γ rows are MUST-data (`ProfileRule`s and `HarnessRule`s).

## Consequences

- WS-B1/ADR-0026 gain the two payload extensions and (via WS-G2 ADR-2) one new class `verification.gate.evaluated`; WS-I1/I2's grounding rows compute from them (WS-G2 ADR-3).
- WS-C3 owns the claim-surface and belief-probe `ProfileRule`s with debt records; WS-A4 compiles the `finish` fields as part of the completion capability's surface (E1–E7 equivalence applies).
- WS-D2 receives CF-243: `Summarize` outputs that state an outcome carry `effect_refs[]`/`observation_refs[]` or are treated as D7 evidence.
- WS-G1's validators are consumed by `verified`/`achieved` binding; WS-G3 governs judged extractors and D9 rubrics; WS-E5 executors expose read-only postconditions for `probe`.
- WS-J6 must say what the Hosting ABI can supply for hosted claims (OQ-276); until then hosted rows are `n/a` for D1–D4/D6–D10.
- The `environment` authority class is retained (OQ-102 census contribution).

## Reversibility

**High for the schema and events (additive over ADR-0026), medium for the taxonomy.** Dropping a divergence class is a dialect migration; collapsing the four-valued agreement to a boolean would erase the honest-failure and unverifiable distinctions that WS-G2 ADR-2's gate and ADR-3's metrics depend on, and would have to be re-derived from records — the direction is costly. Removing the claim surface reverts the primary channel to parsed text with the precision loss S-416 measures.
