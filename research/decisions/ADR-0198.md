# ADR-0198 — The spec-debt register: the assumption-debt record applied to every ratified ADR (RK-06) — conditionality as hypothesis, the (a)/(c) sources as typed evidence, the owning workstream as owner, revalidation triggers in the ADR-0050 style, `revalidation_review` as the removal test; a readiness-gate rule for C0 ADRs resting on `provisional` sources; retirement of a decision only by a superseding ADR

**Status:** ratified
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I6 (consumers: Phase 5 synthesis, every ADR owner, WS-L6 maintenance) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (every C4 mechanism "carries a maturity flag and a revalidation cadence (assumption-debt discipline applied to itself)"), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05 reflexive), ADR-0037 (supersession; names as histories), ADR-0050 (revalidation triggers 1–6 as the precedent inside the program), ADR-0197 (home 17), doc 3 §3.1/§3.3/§7.3/§9 RK-06; OQ-445; R-2.9.6, R-2.12.x

## Context

Doc 3 §9 lists "fast-moving model/evidence landscape invalidating decisions" (RK-06) and names its mitigation: "assumption-debt discipline (WS-I6) applied to the *design decisions themselves*, with revalidation cadence". Every ratified ADR already carries the raw material — (a) mechanism evidence with S-ids and tiers, (c) disconfirming evidence and why it does not overturn, (d) conditionality — and ADR-0050 already carries explicit revalidation triggers that later phases checked (ADR-0179: "revalidation trigger 6 does not fire"). What is missing is the *record*: a place where the conditionality is a hypothesis with an owner and a trigger, where a `provisional` source that is later replicated, refuted or retracted changes a decision's evidence grade, and where the program's own rule — no C0 decision may rest on `provisional` evidence (§3.1) — is checkable as a table rather than as a synthesis-pass promise. 184 ADRs exist; 17 more are expected from Phase 4; the Source Registry is versioned and already flags `provisional?` per row.

## Options considered

1. **No register; rely on each ADR's (d) field and periodic re-reading.** *Rejected:* RK-06 names a cadence; re-reading 200 ADRs has no trigger, no owner and no gate — the program-level analogue of "dead weight found by inspection".
2. **A free-form "revalidation notes" section per ADR.** *Rejected:* unqueryable; no link to the Source Registry's `provisional?` flag; no readiness-gate rule.
3. **One register row per ratified ADR, generated from fields the ADR already carries, shaped as `AssumptionDebtRecord` with `debt_class = spec_decision`, `revalidation_review` as removal test, revalidation triggers in the ADR-0050 style, and a readiness-gate rule (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`research/registers/spec-debt.md`** is created at Phase 5 synthesis with one `SpecDebtRecord` per ratified ADR: `AssumptionDebtRecord{rule_id: ADR-NNNN, debt_class: spec_decision, hypothesis: (d) conditionality verbatim, hypothesis_typed{subject: adr_id, deficiency_class: unknown{"decision conditionality"}, predicted_effect: none}, evidence_refs: (a) and (c) S-ids as EvidenceRef{kind: source, tier, provisional?}, owner: owning workstream → Phase-5 section owner, expiry{condition: evidence_refresh_due, params{evidence_max_age: OQ-445}}, revalidation{on: [named triggers], action: review}, removal_test{kind: revalidation_review, trigger}, status: active}`. Rows are derived from the ADR text by the synthesis pass; an ADR whose (a)/(c)/(d) cannot be lifted into a row is returned to its owner.
2. **Revalidation triggers** (the ADR-0050 form; each names an observable of the program, never a calendar alone): a source in `evidence_refs` is superseded, refuted or retracted in the Source Registry; a `provisional` source is replicated by an independent team or a Tier-A venue (grade rises) or fails replication (row `expiring`); a build-stage measurement named in (e) contradicts (d) (e.g., an AC's Stage-3 number outside the ADR's assumed range); a ratified ADR is amended or superseded (dependants `expiring`, ADR-0037 `StaleIndex` shape); a model generation change among the families (d) names.
3. **Evidence grade for spec decisions:** `hypothesized` iff every (a) ref is `provisional`; `evidenced` iff ≥ 1 Tier-A/B ref or a source-code precedent at a pinned commit; `confirmed` iff a Stage-3+ acceptance test named in (e) has passed. **Readiness-gate rule (doc 3 §7.3 addition):** no C0 ADR may be `hypothesized`; every C0 ADR with any `provisional` ref carries a non-empty trigger list; every C4 ADR carries matched-budget conditionality (already required by phase-3 §6.4 rule 3) *and* a trigger naming the model generation it assumes.
4. **Lifecycle and retirement.** Status transitions are rows in the register's log (no ledger — the program is not a run); `expiring` schedules a `revalidation_review` at the next synthesis or maintenance pass (WS-L6); the review's outcome is `revalidated{evidence}` or a superseding/amending ADR; a decision is `retired` only by a superseding ADR (ADR-0037 applied to the program), never by deleting the row.
5. **Reflexivity.** ADR-0197 and this ADR carry rows; the register's own removal test is "over two revalidation passes no trigger fired on any ADR that a review then amended" — if the register never finds a stale decision, it is retired as a table nobody needed.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: the program's own evidence discipline (doc 3 §3.1: `provisional` tagging; §3.3 fields (a)–(e)) and ADR-0050's revalidation triggers, one of which was checked and found not to fire at Phase 3 (ADR-0179) — the mechanism is already in use once; the 2026 literature the spec rests on is explicitly pre-paradigmatic and fast-moving (doc 2 §6; S-083 counter-evidence arriving after the positive results); configuration/decision debt as a debt class (S-591 Sculley et al., Tier A); expiry with owner and reason as the working regime elsewhere (S-589 Chromium; S-110 OpenHands `warn_cleanup`).
- (b) Source-code precedent: OpenHands `.github/scripts/check_deprecations.py` L1–20 (static analysis over declared deadlines with a runway rule and a failing exit) (S-110 @3fc7b22) — the shape of a register check run at a gate; Chromium `flag-metadata.json` (owners + expiry per entry) (S-589); the program's own `research/registers/sources.md` `provisional?` column and ADR-0050 §"revalidation triggers". The register as a derived table over ADR fields with a grade and a gate rule is `no-precedent / novel` inside this program.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) a 200-row table nobody maintains — answered: rows are generated from fields the ADRs must already carry, the only new content is a trigger list, and the reflexive test retires the register if it never fires; (ii) ADR amendment already handles staleness — answered: amendment is the *outcome*; the register supplies the *trigger* and the owner, which amendments today lack; (iii) "provisional" is a property of sources, not decisions — answered: the grade is derived from the refs, exactly as for harness rules, and the §3.1 rule already couples them. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes the Source Registry keeps its `provisional?` and tier columns current (a maintenance obligation, WS-L6) and that synthesis passes continue after Phase 5 at least at model-generation cadence; independent of any model tier or budget; no `provisional` claim is load-bearing.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **Phase 5 synthesis** (register created; readiness-gate rule applied; every C0 ADR row checked); **program maintenance (WS-L6)** thereafter; no software stage — the register is a program artifact, though its rows use the C0/Stage-1 record schema so the same tooling can validate them.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-05 reflexively (the spec's own conditioned decisions carry hypothesis, evidence, owner, expiry and a removal test; the register expires). Not applicable: every other test (no model-facing surface, target or participant). **ADR-0024 rung:** the register is MUST-data of the program; the gate rule is a synthesis-procedure invariant.

## Consequences

- Phase 5 synthesis gains a generation step and a gate rule; every ADR owner gains a trigger-list obligation when an ADR ratifies from Phase 4 onward (Phase 1–3 rows are generated retroactively from (a)/(c)/(d)).
- WS-L6 (maintenance) owns revalidation passes after Phase 5; OQ-445 fixes cadence and `evidence_max_age`.
- `registers/risks.md` RK-06 moves from "mitigation named" to "mitigation specified".

## Reversibility

**High.** The register is a table over existing fields; dropping it costs nothing in software and returns RK-06 to an unenforced promise. Keeping it costs one generated row per ADR and a trigger list per new ADR.
