# ADR-0202 — The training-stack boundary and interface: HarnessHarness never trains; `training_export/1` lowering, `SnapshotClaim` lifting, two modes, admissible reward sources

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 4 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I8 (with WS-I5, WS-C1, WS-I3, WS-K4) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002, ADR-0006 (N1/N12), ADR-0021, ADR-0033, ADR-0047, ADR-0050 (D2/D4/§8), ADR-0053, ADR-0118, ADR-0120, ADR-0139…0141, ADR-0143, ADR-0176/0178; OQ-026, OQ-281, OQ-127; CF-438; R-2.9.8; T-LCD-06/-11/-12/-15

## Context

Doc 2 §5.9 asks for "the interface HarnessHarness exposes to an external training stack (it does not train models itself — confirm/define this boundary)". Co-Harness (S-084), WHALE (S-085) and SafeEvolve (S-086) each vendor a trainer beside the harness and pass the harness to it as files, environment variables and scraped constants (WHALE `run/lib/alternate.sh`, `scripts/resolve_harness_max_turns.py`); SafeEvolve's checkpoint provenance records the harness artifact hash it was trained under (`core/skillrl/validate_sft_rl_compat.py`). Independently, four trainer codebases converge on the same boundary: the harness owns the interaction loop and the trainer sees only LLM request–response pairs plus rewards plus a policy version (Agent Lightning v1.0 S-610; `agentlightning/schemas.py`; ART `Trajectory{initial/final_policy_version}`; verifiers `PolicySpan`; verl `AgentLoopOutput` for what stays trainer-side). HarnessHarness must expose that boundary as a typed lowering/lifting, not as a co-located trainer.

## Options considered

1. **Vendor or embed a trainer (WHALE/HASE shape).** Rejected: ADR-0006 N1/N12 and ADR-0050 §8 forbid a training runtime inside the instrument; weights and optimiser state would enter the stores with no identity model; token-level material (masks, logprobs) is provider- and tokenizer-specific and would leak into the IR.
2. **No interface — leave training entirely outside and import snapshots by hand.** Rejected: the compatibility failure (S-096, S-047) is undetectable without a typed record of what a snapshot was trained under; exports without loss reports and leakage checks would violate ADR-0143 and T-LCD-11.
3. **Typed lowering target for exports + snapshot claim lifting, two modes (export/attached), no new IR/ledger/registry kinds (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Boundary.** HarnessHarness never trains, loads, merges, quantises or serves weights and never stores checkpoints, datasets or optimiser state as members. A trained model is only ever a **model snapshot** (`ModelSnapshotRecord{provenance = reported, pinned = false until probed}`) behind a `serving_route` (ADR-0118/0120). The training stack is an external participant at the model boundary — not a component class, plugin or hosted participant; it may be in any ecosystem (ADR-0050 D4). Nothing crosses in either direction beyond the records below; no permission decision, handle, credential or unhashed blob crosses (ADR-0050 D2 holds).
2. **Exports.** `export_training(bundle_refs[] | experiment_id, TrainingExportPolicy) → (TrainingExport, LoweringLossReport, measurement.export.delivered)` is a lowering target `training_export/1` proposed for ADR-0141's target list. `TrainingSample{run_id, model_call_id, role, snapshot_ref, snapshot_span, definition{semantic_id, version_id}, profile{…}, bundle_id, plan_hash (+bytes if policy), response: ModelMessage (redacted per policy), usage, context_label, rewards[]: RewardRecord, outcome_class, task{task_id, split_label, contamination.stratum}, compliance[], attribution_refs[]?}`; `TrainingExport` also carries `harness_constraints` (typed from the sealed definition/compiled bundle) and appends a `TrainingExposureRecord{export_id, task_ids[], split_hash, snapshot_lineage}`. Invariants E-1…E-7 (dossier §6.2): export = `project(run, training_export)`; no held-out task or surface (`HeldOutInExport`); rewards only from admissible oracle classes with `veto_tripped` explicit; typed loss report; nothing token-level is fabricated; exports only from `valid` bundles. `export_regression_suite` and `export_compatibility_tags` accompany every export (ADR-0203).
3. **Imports.** `import_snapshot(SnapshotClaim{provider, model_id, snapshot_id, serving_route?, base_snapshot_ref, training_lineage{training_run_ref, step, data_refs[], method, compute_claim{confidence = reported}}, trained_under[], training_cutoff_claim?, weights_digest?: ForeignRef}) → (ModelSnapshotRecord, CompatibilityRecord[], registry lifecycle event)`. I-1 a claim never pins; I-2 weights are foreign claims; I-3 every `(snapshot, definition)` pair gets a `CompatibilityRecord` (`trained_under` or `unknown`); I-4 exposure links are appended. `SnapshotClaim` is proposed as an additive extension of ADR-0120's `ModelDiscoveryClaim`, with a new transport capability `policy_version_exposed`.
4. **Two modes.** *Export mode:* offline; only the records above cross. *Attached mode:* the trainer's endpoint is a `serving_route` whose discovery claim declares `policy_version_exposed = supported`; every `model.call.completed` stamps the exposed policy version as `snapshot_id`; reward records per run are published over an `hh-embed/1` Group R read/subscription (any missing operation is proposed `experimental` under ADR-0178 — never a new boundary); runs with `snapshot_span.drift > 0` are annotated `policy_drift` and excluded from `artifact_benefit` claims.
5. **Admissible reward sources.** `RewardRecord{value, oracle_class, validator_ref, verdict_ref, veto_tripped, charged_to = instrument, provenance}` derives only from ADR-0047 oracle classes admitted for the metric; a judge verdict is exportable only with a valid `CalibrationRecord` and `reasoning_reading = false` — **verdicts of reasoning-reading critics are never rewards** (OQ-281, I8 half). Blended scalars are trainer-side.
6. **Maturity.** Every operation above is `maturity = research_grade`; the interface's own version carries an `AssumptionDebtRecord` whose removal test is "the null-trainer conformance suite passes with the interface absent" (T-LCD-05 reflexively).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-06 (no IR/ledger/registry/`run_kind` change; removable build AC-I8-11), T-LCD-11 (export/import are lowering/lifting with typed loss reports; foreign digests as claims), T-LCD-12 (operations over canonical records, out of process), T-LCD-15 (hosted rows `n/a`; nothing unobserved is exported), T-LCD-10 (snapshots are claims + fingerprints), T-LCD-14 (training compute is a `reported`-confidence dimension, never pooled). Not applicable: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-05 (beyond reflexive)/-07/-08/-09/-13. **ADR-0024 rung:** export/import/loss-report logic MUST-code; `TrainingExportPolicy`, readers, redaction, reward-source allowlist MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: S-610 (harness owns the loop; trainer sees request–response pairs), S-096 and S-047/S-049 (a snapshot change invalidates harness assumptions — the record of what a snapshot was trained under is the only way to notice), S-088 (compliance must be re-measured per snapshot), S-434/S-415 (never optimise against monitors/fixed verifiers — reward-source allowlist), ADR-0143 (held-out never leaves). All 2026 preprints `provisional`; the boundary is corroborated by four codebases and one Tier-B account.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `microsoft/agent-lightning` @ `218f1f7` `agentlightning/schemas.py` (`ModelRequestData{model_version}`, `RewardData{value, source}`, `Model{model, endpoint, version}`), `server/proxy.py` L223–229; `OpenPipe/ART` @ `66f644d` `src/art/trajectories/__init__.py` (`initial/final_policy_version`), `src/art/adapter_leases.py` (`InferenceTarget{step}`), `src/art/types.py` (`TrainResult{step}`); `PrimeIntellect-ai/verifiers` @ `3bbac38` `verifiers/v1/episode.py` (`PolicySpan`, train/eval work); `MaoPopovich/SafeEvolve` @ `94dc369` `core/skillrl/validate_sft_rl_compat.py` (checkpoint provenance carries harness hash + compiler/renderer versions); `krafton-ai/WHALE` @ `fbe125e` `run/lib/alternate.sh`, `scripts/resolve_harness_max_turns.py` (the untyped coupling). The typed lowering with loss report + snapshot claim lifting as one contract: `novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: HASE (S-618) and WHALE vendor the trainer and could exploit token-level signals; verl's `AgentLoopOutput` shows trainers want masks/logprobs. Does not overturn: those signals are produced by the trainer's own serving/proxy path in attached mode (S-610 demonstrates this works end to end); embedding a trainer violates N1/N12 and the identity model.
- (d) Conditionality: applies only to snapshots a trainer can produce (open-weight or provider fine-tuning APIs); for frontier snapshots only the provider-drift path of ADR-0203 applies. Value is conditional on matched-total evidence never yet reported; the specification is unconditional (ADR-0002).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0/Stage 1 schemas only (`SnapshotClaim` extension, `policy_version_exposed`); C4/Stage 6 operations, null-trainer fixture, attached mode (`experimental`).

## Consequences

WS-I5's pipeline emits exports through this contract and never calls a trainer directly; WS-C1 gains a transport capability and a claim extension; WS-I3/J6 gain a lowering target; WS-J4 gains `policy_drift` and `trained_on` annotations (OQ-455); the Readiness Report must show no C0 element depends on any training result.

## Reversibility

**High** for the spec (the interface is removable without touching C0–C3; AC-I8-11 proves it); **medium** once exports have been consumed by external trainers (produced snapshots remain claims; nothing in HarnessHarness must change to drop the interface, but lineage records would become dangling `foreign` refs, annotated never deleted).
