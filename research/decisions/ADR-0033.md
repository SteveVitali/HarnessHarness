# ADR-0033 — One provenance/authority scheme for security, context and memory: the provenance record, the seven authority classes, and the product label lattice (answers OQ-054; resolves CF-006 at the scheme level)

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 1 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-L3 (consumers that must cite this ADR: WS-H1, WS-H2, WS-D1, WS-D4; also WS-A3, WS-B1, WS-A4/C3, WS-H5, WS-H6, WS-E1, WS-F3, WS-J6, WS-I5) · **Phase:** 1 · **Related:** ADR-0001, ADR-0002 (iii: no self-widening), ADR-0004/0006 (novelty claim a: "provenance/authority on every entity"), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-02/-06/-07/-10/-11/-12/-13), ADR-0008, ADR-0011; ADR-0015/ADR-0016 (slot shape), ADR-0014 (three-valued validity), ADR-0026/ADR-0029 (envelope, chain); CF-006, CF-008, CF-074…09; OQ-014, OQ-020, OQ-021, OQ-054, OQ-101…08; R-2.1.5, R-2.8.1, R-2.8.2, R-2.4.1, R-2.4.4

## Context

Doc 2 §4 names provenance a cross-cutting property and diagnoses one shared root cause behind context-privilege escalation and revoked-memory failures: heterogeneous sources flattened into an undifferentiated token stream. Doc 3 §7.1 and CF-006 require that the security kernel, IFC, context builder and memory lifecycle converge on **one** authority/label scheme before any of their ADRs ratifies. WS-A3 fixed the *shape* — a closed `AuthorityClass` sum on every `Observation`, `ContextItem`, `Memory`, `Permission` issuer and `Text` leaf, and a `ProvenanceRecord{origin, authority, created_at, attestation?}` on every node and edge — and left the values open (OQ-054). The WS-L3 audit found: (i) CaMeL and Fides, read in source, use the same label shape — an integrity component joined toward *less* trust, a confidentiality component joined toward *fewer* readers, combined as a product lattice, checked deterministically at the tool boundary; (ii) the 2026 escalation studies (S-103, S-104, `provisional (mech)`) show that production harnesses carry *origin* and *scope* but no *authority* on content and elevate content by location (memory files, skill directories, developer-role injection); (iii) the revoked-memory study (S-106) shows validity must be a separate filter, not a label; (iv) PROV, in-toto/SLSA, MCP and A2A already supply the derivation vocabulary and the rule that authority is derived from a verified origin, never read from an item's own claim.

## Options considered

1. **Origin only** (OpenHands `source`, Codex `SessionSource`; roles as the model-facing hierarchy). *Rejected:* origin is a fact, not an ordering; every audited system that stops here was compromised by re-serializing low-origin content into high-privilege roles (S-103/S-104).
2. **Binary trusted/untrusted** (Fides two-point integrity; Codex project `TrustLevel`). *Rejected:* cannot distinguish the kernel from the harness author from the principal from the model, so "the model may propose but never confer" and "definition policy binds the principal's task" are inexpressible; S-103's five roles × three scopes need finer structure.
3. **Three separate schemes** — capability classes for H1, IFC labels for H2, trust scores for D1/D4. *Rejected by CF-006:* the escalation bug is precisely that the context builder and the monitor disagree about what an item is.
4. **Full decentralized labels everywhere at C0** (DLM owners/readers on every value, CaMeL-style per-value dependency tracking). *Rejected for C0:* proven utility and authoring cost (CaMeL 77% vs 84%; per-tool policies), implicit flows still open (Fides); kept as the C2 extension of the same lattice, not a different one.
5. **Numeric trust scores.** *Rejected:* not a lattice (no sound join), not auditable, invites models to argue thresholds.
6. **One product lattice — a closed seven-class command-authority chain × taint set × reader set — carried on one provenance record by every consumer; authority axis enforced at C0, taint/readers at C2; validity kept orthogonal (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Provenance record** (supersedes nothing; extends WS-A3's slot): `ProvenanceRecord = {origin: Origin, authority: AuthorityClass, taint: set<TaintTag> = ∅, readers: ReaderSet = Public, scope: PersistenceScope, derived_from: [Derivation] = [], created_at, attestation?}`. `Origin` is WS-A3's sum plus `kernel(component_ref)` and `participant(participant_ref, hosting_mechanism)`, and `human` carries `role ∈ {author, principal, reviewer}` (CF-074). `PersistenceScope ∈ {definition, user, project, session, run, turn}`. `Derivation = {kind ∈ {summary, compaction, extraction, quotation, revision, translation, subagent_result, projection}, inputs, deriver: Origin, deterministic}` (PROV `wasDerivedFrom` subtypes). `Attestation = {kind ∈ {seal, hash_chain, signature, pin}, subject_hash, anchor, verified_by: kernel(ref), verified_at}` — a *verified* fact, never self-declared.
2. **`AuthorityClass`** is the closed total order **`kernel > definition > principal > delegate > environment > external > unverified`** — "how much may this item command the harness." Minting: `kernel` only by the reference runtime (ledger facts, monitor decisions, deterministic validator verdicts); `definition` only at `seal` or by a `pin` endorsement (WS-H5); `principal` only for the run's human principal (task, replies, approvals, promoted memories); `delegate` for outputs of any AgentProcess — model claims, plans, subagent results, evolution candidates, hosted-participant claims; `environment` only for closed-schema structured values from closed-world tools declared in the sealed definition; `external` for open-world content and for **all free text** not from kernel/definition/principal (rule R-TEXT); `unverified` for imported, lifted, migrated or attestation-failed content and for hosted content the ABI cannot vouch for. `unverified` is a distinct bottom, never coerced into `external` (T-LCD-07 in the authority direction).
3. **Label lattice.** `Label = (authority, taint, readers)`; `L₁ ⊑ L₂ ⇔ authority₁ ≥ authority₂ ∧ taint₁ ⊆ taint₂ ∧ readers₁ ⊇ readers₂`; **join** `= (min authority, taint ∪, readers ∩)` (Public is the identity of ∩); **meet** `= (max, ∩, ∪)`; top `(kernel, ∅, Public)`, bottom `(unverified, U, ∅)`. This is Fides' `ProductLabel[Integrity, Inverse[Powerset]]` with CaMeL's `sources_set` as the taint component and the seven-class chain in place of the two-point integrity lattice.
4. **Tiering.** C0 enforces the `authority` component (one enum, one join per delivered item); `taint` and `readers` are carried with defaults and joined at C0 but enforced only at C2 (WS-H2). Same record, same lattice, two enforcement depths — never two schemes.
5. **Validity is orthogonal.** The label never encodes validity; `validity ∈ {valid, invalid, unknown}` (WS-A2) is a separate filter applied *before* authority in retrieval (ADR-0034 P5). A revoked item keeps its label and loses its validity — the S-106 failure is closed by the contract, not by a flag.
6. **Derived, never declared.** `authority ≤ default_authority(origin, scope, attestation)` unless a legitimate endorsement event (ADR-0035) names the item; imported/lifted items never carry a class read from their payload (MCP hints rule; in-toto/SLSA signer–builder rule).
7. **Invariants** (validation and append errors, never warnings): `MissingProvenance`, `AuthorityExceedsOrigin`, `TextAboveExternal`, `TaintedAboveExternal` (`taint ≠ ∅ ⇒ authority ≤ external`), `IllegitimateEndorsement`, `ScopeCeilingExceeded`. Provenance is excluded from `semantic_id` and included in `version_id` (WS-A3 §6.1, unchanged). Adding or collapsing a class is an HIR dialect bump with migration.
8. **Vocabulary.** Authority class is *command* authority; it is never used as *evidence* weight (WS-A2 detector provenance, WS-G2 reconciliation own that). `Permission.principal` (WS-A3) is renamed `Permission.holder` to free the word (CF-076).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-02 (every `Text` leaf has a class; opacity by class), T-LCD-06 (hosted content maps to the IR-defined `participant` origin; no IR→ABI edge), T-LCD-07 (`unverified` never coerced), T-LCD-10 (provenance outside `semantic_id`), T-LCD-11 (provenance lowered through typed extension slots; loss reported — mechanics in ADR-0034), T-LCD-12 (operations only). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-05/-08/-09/-13/-14/-15.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** lattice model of information flow and join as least upper bound — Denning 1976 (S-216, Tier A); integrity as a join toward lower trust, low-water mark — Biba (S-217, Tier A); decentralized owner/reader labels, "only the owner can declassify," dual integrity model — DLM (S-218, Tier A); small trusted reference monitor with declassification at endpoints — Flume (S-219, Tier A); labels as a product of confidentiality and integrity, joined conservatively into the planner's context label, explicit secrecy + integrity non-interference — Fides (S-101, 2025, code); capabilities = sources ∪ / readers ∩ on every value, deterministic policy at the tool boundary — CaMeL (S-099, 2025, code); origin ≠ authority and location-based elevation as the harness bug — S-103, S-104 (`provisional (mech)`, corroborated by the source audit of Codex/OpenHands/Claude SDK); revoked facts retained authority — S-106 (`provisional (mech)`, corroborated by the audited absence of any validity filter); authority derived from verified signer, not predicate — in-toto/SLSA (S-222/-08), MCP annotations-are-hints (S-153), A2A JWS over JCS (S-156); derivation/attribution/delegation vocabulary — PROV-DM (S-221); model-side hierarchy as complement — S-220.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** `camel-prompt-injection/src/camel/capabilities/{capabilities.py L23–38, sources.py L22–42, readers.py L21–45, utils.py L15–73}` (label shape, ∪/∩ join, trusted-set predicate); `fides/Tutorial.ipynb` cells 17–20 (`Lattice`, `IntegrityLabel`, `PowersetLattice`, `ProductLabel`, `InverseLattice`), 23–27 (`MetaValue` on typed leaves, `readers_label`); `software-agent-sdk/openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/event/types.py` L5 (`SourceType`) and `security/risk.py` (risk ≠ authority); `codex/codex-rs/protocol/src/protocol.rs` L2751–2850 (`SessionSource`, `SubAgentSource`), `config_types.rs` L641 (`TrustLevel`); `claude-agent-sdk-python/src/claude_agent_sdk/types.py` L33, L109–112 (`SettingSource`, `PermissionUpdateDestination` = persistence scopes); `modelcontextprotocol/schema/2026-07-28/schema.ts` L35–60, L1903–1910. The seven-class chain, the free-text rule and the single record shared by four consumers are `no-precedent / novel` as an integrated scheme; each component is precedented as cited.
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** model-side instruction hierarchy may suffice (S-220) — overturned for the harness layer by S-103/S-104 (assembly defeats it) and by the need for a ledger-readable record; IFC costs utility and authoring effort and leaves implicit flows (S-099 77% vs 84%, S-101 explicit secrecy only) — absorbed by enforcing only the authority axis at C0, making taint/readers C2, and treating isolation as the utility path; total orders are too coarse (DLM) — absorbed by keeping ownership/audience in the set-valued components; label creep (Biba) — accepted as correct semantics with per-call recomputation and isolation as mitigations, measured at Stage 3; `environment` is weakly evidenced — restricted to closed-schema values and reversible by dialect bump (OQ-102); no audited runtime carries authority on content (WS-B1 §5) — the cost at C0 is one enum and one join per item. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** independent of model tier and task class. Assumes a human principal per run (unattended/scheduled runs bind a `definition`-class policy as the ceiling — S-104's persistent-goal vector); assumes tool capabilities declare `world ∈ {closed, open}` (WS-A3 `EffectAttributes`); assumes reader sets are small (per-run principals and named recipients). The utility cost of the taint gate is conditional on task distribution and is a Stage 3 measurement (AC-L3-10), not an assumption.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C0 / Stage 1** (record, classes, `default_authority`, lattice operations, IR validation, ledger stamping); C0 / Stage 2 (context label, memory labels, retrieval filter); **C2** (taint/readers enforcement, WS-H2; lifecycle richness, WS-D4).

## Consequences

- **WS-H1** implements the seven-check minimal monitor (ADR-0035) over this record; `Permission.issuer.authority ≥ principal` is the conferral rule; the policy table Π is H1/H7's (OQ-101).
- **WS-H2** enforces `taint`/`readers` and owns declassification policy at C2 without introducing a second label type.
- **WS-D1** stamps `context_label` on `context.assembled`, enforces the role-placement invariant through the profile's `role_map`, and gives every slot a `min_authority`.
- **WS-D3/D4** apply validity → authority → readers → relevance and the supersession-authority rule; memory labels are joins of writer context and sources.
- **WS-A3** adopts the `Origin` additions, `taint: set<TaintTag>`, and `Permission.holder` (CF-074/-02/-03) at HIR/1 freeze; `AuthorityClass` values are these seven.
- **WS-B1** makes provenance mandatory on the classes listed in ADR-0035 and renames `security.taint.*` / `security.authority.*` (CF-077).
- **WS-A4/C3** own `role_map` as a profile surface and report role collapse in the lowering loss report; **WS-E1** derives a tool's `observation_authority` ceiling from its effects' `world` attribute; **WS-J6** maps hosted content to `participant`/`unverified`; **WS-I5** stamps candidates `evolution`/`delegate`.
- Every later dossier that says "trusted" must say which of the three components it means.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Adding classes or collapsing `environment` is a dialect migration over stored records (cheap while the corpus is small). Reverting to origin-only or binary trust after Stage 2 would invalidate the monitor checks, the retrieval contract and the M-CPE/X-CPE acceptance tests simultaneously — which is why the scheme is fixed before H1/H2/D1/D4 design against it.
