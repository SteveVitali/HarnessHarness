# ADR-0165 — Mediation and accounting for hosted participants: policy-first boundary, credential/egress/environment channels, per-dimension `budget_enforcement` feeding `MatchSpec`, cost provenance per mechanism, scorecard applicability with `requires_capabilities`/`requires_mediation`, and the hosted-specific metrics (resolves OQ-038)

**Status:** ratified
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-J6 (consumers: WS-J3, WS-J4, WS-I2, WS-L2, WS-H1, WS-H3, WS-H4, WS-H7, WS-B5, WS-C1, WS-K4) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0013, ADR-0018, ADR-0039, ADR-0040, ADR-0041, ADR-0043, ADR-0045, ADR-0046, ADR-0047, ADR-0053 (D-4), ADR-0057, ADR-0058 (SV-7), ADR-0059, ADR-0061, ADR-0069, ADR-0070, ADR-0071, ADR-0098 (D3, D6), ADR-0120 (served-model coordinate), ADR-0136…0138; ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05/-07/-14/-15); CF-073, CF-105, CF-349, CF-350; OQ-033, OQ-038, OQ-048, OQ-116, OQ-246; R-2.10.6, R-2.10.4

## Context

ADR-0001 promises hosted participants "the same environments, evals, scorecards, cost/latency/audit" as native ones. The Phase 2 contracts made the kernel's enforcement points explicit (effect gate, helper, broker, EP3 egress, approvals) and ADR-0053 D-4 placed hosted participants as attenuated children at the boundary. What is unspecified is what the Lab can actually *enforce* on a process whose loop it does not own, what it can only *observe* it report, and how the scorecard and the matched-budget engine must treat the difference. The audit (WS-J6 §4 F4–F6) shows: policy is evaluated outside the loop in every serious host (Omnigent policy-first; Databricks forbids custom policy code and forces a gateway; Inspect overrides generation config); cost provenance differs per mechanism and one adapter often has two (OpenHands: reported tokens, estimated cost); and no session protocol offers a token cap, so a `matched_cap` on tokens over a non-intercepted hosted arm is a matched-*report* comparison that ADR-0041's engine would currently accept. ADR-0071 already had to invent a pseudo-class (`hosted-with-permission-elicitation`) because `MetricDeclaration` cannot express a capability precondition.

## Options considered

1. **Trust participant self-report for budgets and security** (Harbor/HAL practice: cost from `AgentContext`, no permission mediation). *Rejected:* self-report cannot enforce; `permission_violation_rate = 0` from an unmediated participant is the T-LCD-15 failure.
2. **Require interception for every hosted participant** so tokens are always enforceable. *Rejected:* excludes container-installed and many session-ABI CLIs; ADR-0005 forbids privileging a mechanism; interception is a declared upgrade, not a requirement.
3. **Deliver the policy to the participant** (vendor permission modes, allowlists). *Rejected:* the participant's mode is a *coordinate* the Lab may set, never the Lab's policy; the kernel's Π is the decider (ADR-0053; Omnigent/Databricks precedent).
4. **Chosen:** enforce at kernel-owned channels only; declare per-dimension enforceability in the descriptor; make the engine refuse unenforceable caps; record every cost provenance; add capability and mediation preconditions to `MetricDeclaration`; add hosted-specific metrics instead of zeros.

## Decision

1. **Boundary rule.** The Lab never delivers policy, authority, credential values or budgets to a hosted participant. It enforces through: the sealed MCP surface (Lab-supplied tools), the environment helper and containment floor (Lab-provisioned environments), the credential broker (bindings only for channels in `credential_supply`, proxy-injection primary — ADR-0058 SV-7), EP3 egress with attribution tokens (ADR-0061), the model gateway/proxy when `model_io_intercept ≠ none`, and the verbs `cancel` and `submit` refusal. A hosted participant's `request_permission` is evaluated by Π first; a human is asked only on `ask`; adapter auto-approval fails admission (static check); `allow_always` lowers to the H7 pattern cache only until OQ-246 is answered. `elicit` becomes a `message_human` effect proposal.
2. **Placement.** `process_placement ∈ {in_environment, lab_host, remote_service}` is a descriptor field (via `ext hh.hosting/1`); the participant receives `connection_info`, never a handle (R-NOSIDE); its own fs/exec effects are `observed`/`unobserved`, never `action.effect.*` rows; `end_state` comes from the kernel's own snapshot at `close`.
3. **`budget_enforcement: map<dimension, {enforced, advisory, unenforceable}>`** is derived by the adapter from mechanism × placement × interception and stored in the descriptor. Derivation floor: `time.wall_ms`, `turns`, `approvals.requested` — `enforced` for every mechanism with a live session (`unenforceable` turns/approvals for container-installed); `network.calls`, `env.active_ms` — `enforced` when the environment is Lab-provisioned; `spend` — `enforced` iff the model path is gateway-mediated, else `advisory`; `tokens.*` — `enforced` iff `model_io_intercept ≠ none`, else `advisory` (CF-073) or `unenforceable` (no instruction delivery). Hard ceilings fire at boundary decision points (before `submit`, at `request_permission`, at the proxy before a model call, at EP3, at wall-clock via `cancel`) and end the run `budget_exhausted{dimension}` after `session.closed`.
4. **Engine consequence (amends ADR-0041/0046 by synthesis; CF-350).** `MatchSpec` validation adds: for every arm and every dimension in `MatchSpec.dimensions`, `budget_enforcement[dimension] = enforced` is required for `matched_cap`; otherwise `IncommensurableMatch{arm, dimension, enforceability}`. `iso_cost` over `spend` requires every arm's spend `confidence ∈ {exact, bounded}`; `estimate`/`unknown` rows render Pareto bands (CF-105) and never a point. `matched_total` for C4 claims is refused if any arm has an `unenforceable` dimension in the match set.
5. **Cost provenance per mechanism (OQ-033 refined).** Session-ABI `usage.reported` → `participant_reported` (`confidence: estimate`; `bounded` with reported cache buckets and a pinned pricing table); interception → `gateway_reported`/`provider_reported` (`exact`); container-installed → `reconstructed_from_native_log` (`estimate`) or `unknown` (`NoPrice`, never zero). When measured and reported coexist, both `measurement.cost.attributed` rows are appended; measured is primary for totals; `usage_report_agreement` is computed. Adapter/probe/reconstruction work is `charged_to = instrument`. A participant's `usage_mapping` (token-role convention) is declared; undeclared ⇒ `hh-inclusive/1` assumed and `confidence` capped at `bounded`.
6. **`MetricDeclaration` gains `requires_capabilities ⊆ capability names` and `requires_mediation ∈ {any, observed, mediated(effects | egress | model_calls)}`** (proposed amendment to ADR-0045; retires ADR-0071's pseudo-class in favour of `applies_to_classes = {native, hosted}` + `requires_capabilities = {permission_surface}`); `n/a.reason` gains `capability` and `mediation`. Applicability = class ∧ observability ∧ capabilities ∧ mediation.
7. **Applicability by dimension** (WS-J6 §6.5): capability, efficiency (with `confidence` stratum), portability, end-state security and reliability tail metrics apply to hosted rows; `crash/recovery_rate` requires `resume`; autonomy metrics require `permission_surface`; `steer_rate` requires `steer`; `secret_leak` (veto) applies through broker + EP3 canaries; `permission_violation_rate` (veto) requires `mediated(effects)`; `duplicate_effect_rate`, `audit_completeness`, `blast_radius`, `claim_state_agreement`, `evidence_traceability`, `execution_alignment_failure_rate` (deterministic) and every evolvability metric are `n/a{class}`.
8. **Hosted-specific declarations** (`applies_to_classes = {hosted}`, non-veto): `hosted_observation_completeness`, `mediation_coverage`, `usage_report_agreement`, `conformance_drift_count`, `synthesized_terminal_rate`, `observed_duplicate_action_rate`, `observed_blast_radius`.
9. **OQ-038.** Model-boundary interception is an optional capability of any participant (`model_io_intercept ∈ {base_url, client_patch, none, unknown}`); when SUPPORTED and enabled it upgrades `observability_level` to include `model_io`, makes `tokens.*` enforceable, yields `exact` cost, and lets the Lab set the `model` coordinate for the intercepted role (OQ-048/ADR-0013 consequence: class unchanged). The interception path is the C1 gateway (ADR-0118), so a hosted participant's calls pass the same normalizer and price tables as native calls.

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-14 (unenforceable caps are refusable; utilisation and confidence reported), T-LCD-15 (typed applicability; hosted-specific metrics instead of zeros), T-LCD-07 (enforceability and interception are declared and probed, never assumed), T-LCD-05 (the `budget_enforcement` derivation table is a conditioned rule with a debt record), T-LCD-06 (no native contract changes; `MetricDeclaration` fields are additive and default to `any`), T-LCD-12 (channels are operations at kernel-owned boundaries). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-04/-08/-09/-10/-11/-13. ADR-0024 rung: enforcement points, the `MatchSpec` check and provenance stamping are MUST-code; the derivation table, applicability table and metric declarations are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: policy outside the loop — S-118 `inner/acp_executor.py` L775–800 ("NOT a blind approve"), S-060 ("custom policy functions … are not supported"; "always routes model access through AI Gateway"), S-137 (generation config dropped by default), ADR-0053 D-4/decision 5 (raise-only reviewers; S-104 `provisional` shows automatic review stopping nothing); two provenances in one adapter — S-110 `acp_agent.py` L912–958; usage via vendor hooks — S-118 `docs/cursor-native-cost-tracking.md`; usage groups by model/effort — S-107 `thread_usage.rs`; post-hoc cost — S-132 `AgentContext`; no token cap in any session protocol — S-035 `meta.json` (no such method), S-107 (`turn/interrupt` only); interception yields complete model I/O — S-137, S-136 `_bridge/sandbox/service.py`, S-140 (`provisional`, mechanism only); matched-budget necessity — S-083 via ADR-0041/0046; cost provenance vocabulary — ADR-0039/0043; canary channels — ADR-0059.
- (b) Source-code precedent: omnigent `omnigent/inner/acp_executor.py` @6a069da; software-agent-sdk `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/agent/acp_agent.py` L912–958 @3fc7b22; inspect_ai `src/inspect_ai/agent/_bridge/sandbox/service.py` L70–160 @75f4891; codex `codex-rs/app-server-protocol/src/protocol/v2/thread_usage.rs` @0735c51; harbor `src/harbor/models/agent/context.py` @7d5285b. Per-dimension `budget_enforcement` consumed by a matched-budget engine, and capability/mediation preconditions on metric declarations, are `no-precedent / novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) HAL/Harbor compare black boxes on self-reported cost without any of this and are widely used (S-135 Tier A) — accepted as the precedent for *product-level* rows; the instrument's claim is class-aware attribution, which their single-run, self-reported rows cannot make (WS-I2 F2/F3); (ii) requiring `enforced` for `matched_cap` will refuse many hosted comparisons — accepted: they remain expressible as `iso_cost`/wall-clock matches or with interception, and a refused false comparison is the T-LCD-14 intent; (iii) `requires_capabilities` couples the metric registry to the capability vocabulary — mitigated: names are strings validated against the registered `ext hh.hosting/n` schema, default `∅`; (iv) interception changes participant behaviour (dropped generation parameters) — accepted and declared as an adapter parameter with a debt record. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier; assumes Lab-provisioned environments for in-environment placements (remote-service peers get `end_state = unknown` and most enforcement `unenforceable`); assumes WS-C1's gateway can serve provider dialects as a proxy (interception adapters); `allow_always` semantics wait on OQ-246; L2 content capture for hosted participants waits on OQ-116 consent rules.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 3** — `MetricDeclaration.requires_capabilities/requires_mediation` and the two `n/a` reasons (additive schema); `MatchSpec` enforceability check (engine precondition; native arms are trivially `enforced`); **C2 / Stage 4** — boundary rule, placement, `budget_enforcement` derivation for session-ABI, provenance stamping, hosted-specific metrics; **C2 / Stage 4–5** — interception (`model_io_intercept`) and container-installed provenance paths.

## Consequences

- WS-J3 validates `MatchSpec` against every arm's `budget_enforcement`; WS-J4 stratifies Pareto reports by provenance/confidence and renders bands; WS-J5 stores both cost rows.
- WS-I2 amends `MetricDeclaration` and re-declares `approval_*`, `steer_rate`, `crash/recovery_rate` with `requires_capabilities`; ADR-0071's `applies_to_classes` value is retired (CF-349).
- WS-L2 records the per-mechanism defaults in its provenance table; WS-C1 exposes the gateway as the interception proxy's model path; WS-H3/H4 treat hosted processes as ordinary bound principals of their channels; WS-H7 answers OQ-246 before `allow_always` is offered to any hosted participant.
- Spec §6 states plainly: hosted comparisons are class-aware and mediation-aware; a hosted row never claims a security or autonomy property the Lab did not mediate.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Relaxing the enforceability check is a one-line engine change but re-admits matched-report comparisons already refused; removing `requires_capabilities/requires_mediation` would orphan the hosted-specific declarations and re-create ADR-0071's pseudo-class; the boundary rule inherits ADR-0053's low reversibility.
