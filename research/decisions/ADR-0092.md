# ADR-0092 — Result and failure rendering are compiled, validator-aware surface records: `ResultRenderSpec` with declared loss and offload-to-artifact (compression never a default; token-optimized notations inadmissible below C2), a closed `SurfaceFailure` taxonomy rendered by `ErrorFormatSpec`, the `action.tool.surface_rejected` event and `surface_rejection_rate` as a class-scoped metric

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-E2 (consumers: WS-C3, WS-D1, WS-D3, WS-E1, WS-E5, WS-B1, WS-G1, WS-I1, WS-I2) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0007 (T-LCD-02/-13/-15), ADR-0015/0016 (`Artifact`, `ContextItem`, `derived-from`), ADR-0019 (closed-world rule; run-time rendering under a compiled spec; OQ-063), ADR-0020 (`result_render`, `error_format` rule kinds), ADR-0022 (E5, E6), ADR-0026/0029 (payload offload; ledger events), ADR-0033/0034 (labels on derived items), ADR-0044/0045 (`MetricDeclaration`; `n/a`), ADR-0047; ADR-0090; OQ-064; R-2.5.2, R-2.4.1

## Context

ADR-0022 fixes E5 (every failure class of a capability has a distinguishable rendering) and E6 (a result renderer preserves every field a bound Validator reads; truncation declared `truncated` with retained fields) but leaves their data model and ownership to Phase 2. The audit found three compression mechanisms and two failure taxonomies in production, mostly undeclared: opencode and pi truncate at 2,000 lines / 50 KiB and offload the full text to a file with a hint (pi reports `truncatedBy/totalLines/totalBytes`); codex keys a `truncation_policy{mode: tokens, limit: 10000}` per model slug and its `ToolOutput` contract states that the lossy `log_output` "is not the authoritative tool result"; Anthropic recommends a `ResponseFormat{detailed, concise}` enum (≈⅓ tokens) and, in programmatic tool calling, keeps results in the execution environment rather than the context; Cloudflare's Code Mode "only read[s] back the final results it needs". Notation Matters (`provisional`, S-376) reports that token-optimized notations save 18–27% tokens at a 9–14 pp accuracy cost and "collapse parallel tool-call output for most models". On failures, SWE-agent's parsers raise typed codes (`missing, multiple, invalid_command, invalid_json, missing_arg, unexpected_arg`) with per-parser templates, Cursor separates `InvalidArguments/UnexpectedEnvironment` (model mistakes) from `ProviderError/UserAborted/Timeout`, and WS-H1 denies `UnmappedArgument` at the monitor — but no audited system persists surface failures as typed events a compliance metric can count. No audited system ties a truncation policy to what a downstream validator reads.

## Options considered

1. **Rendering and truncation stay runtime constants of the tool executor** (all audited systems). *Rejected:* model-conditioned policy hidden in code (T-LCD-01 signature); E6 uncheckable; loss undeclared.
2. **Rendering is a context-builder (WS-D1) concern only.** *Rejected:* the *policy* is profile-owned data (ADR-0020 `result_render`) and E6 needs retained fields at compile time against validator bindings; execution at run time under the compiled spec is the ADR-0019 split already used for the transcript renderer.
3. **Aggressive compression by default (token-optimized notations, concise-first).** *Rejected:* model-conditioned accuracy cost and parallel-call collapse (S-376, counter-evidence); validators may read dropped fields; contradicts doc 2 §5.2 ("not just lower token count").
4. **Compiled `ResultRenderSpec`/`ErrorFormatSpec` surface records with declared loss, offload to a content-addressed `Artifact` reachable through a compiled surface, `full` as the reference mode, a closed `SurfaceFailure` taxonomy with its own ledger event and class-scoped metric (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`ResultRenderSpec{mode ∈ {full, truncate{max_lines, max_bytes, max_tokens, direction ∈ {head, tail}}, concise{format_param?, retained_fields[]}, offload{threshold, preview_lines, artifact_kind}}, declared_loss: truncated{retained_fields[]}?, validator_reads: [field]}`** is a profile-owned surface record written by `result_render` rules. E6 is the invariant `validator_reads ⊆ retained_fields` for every Validator bound to the capability's results; `full` is the family's reference mode; every non-`full` mode is a conditioned rule with a debt record — **compression is never a default**.
2. **Authoritative result always exists.** Whenever any loss occurs the untruncated result is stored as a content-addressed `Artifact` (ADR-0026/0029 offload) and the delivered `ContextItem` carries `payload: Ref<Artifact>` or the preview with a `derived-from` edge and the artifact reference; `rendering_ref` names the surface and mode. `offload` is admissible only if the bundle contains a compiled `read_artifact` surface (closed-world rule), else `UnexpressibleSurface`.
3. **Results that never enter context** (code-mode `result_policy = return_only`) are admissible only because nested calls are ledgered individually (ADR-0091 S2); the returned value is an `Observation` with `authority = delegate`.
4. **Token-optimized notations** (non-JSON encodings of schemas, calls or results) are inadmissible at C0/C1; at C2 they are a `schema_dialect`/`result_render` rule requiring their own E3/E6 evidence and a debt record.
5. **`SurfaceFailure ∈ {unparseable, unknown_surface, unmapped_argument, domain_violation, missing_required, multiple_calls_unsupported, grammar_violation, session_state_invalid}`** (closed; extended by ADR) is detected by the parser or the reference monitor before dispatch; no executor runs and no `Effect` record exists. It is ledgered as **`action.tool.surface_rejected{surface_id, binding_ref, failure_class, raw_call_hash, model_call_id, rendering_ref}`** (WS-B1 to place in the `action` category beside `action.effect.*`).
6. **`ErrorFormatSpec{renderings: map<SurfaceFailure ∪ CapabilityFailureClass, Text>, distinguishability: verified}`** is the `error_format` rule's record; E5 ranges over both failure sets; renderings are `Text` leaves (opacity-counted under `profile_prose`, OQ-064) and must be pairwise distinguishable under the profile (static test).
7. **Metric.** `surface_rejection_rate{dimension: grounding/compliance, level: run, applies_to_classes: {native}, requires_observability: {ledger}, computed_from: action.tool.surface_rejected ÷ model-emitted calls per (profile, family)}` enters the ADR-0044 catalogue; hosted participants report `n/a{class}`. It is the per-profile, per-shape measurement of "the model cannot use this surface" that keep-rate only captures indirectly.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: truncation/offload as model-keyed policy (S-107 `truncation_policy` per slug; `ToolOutput` authoritative-vs-lossy distinction); reported truncation (S-113 `truncatedBy/totalLines`); concision enums and their token effect (S-037); results kept out of context (S-040; S-372); compression is not free and is model-conditioned (S-376, `provisional`, counter-evidence); typed surface-failure codes (S-114 parsers; S-049 error taxonomy); the E5/E6 obligations and `n/a` rule are ADR-0022/0045 (ratified); MCP `outputSchema`/`structuredContent` as the typed-result carrier (S-153).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `opencode/packages/opencode/src/tool/truncate.ts` (limits; offload file + hint) and `tool/code-mode.ts` `projectMcpResult` (prefers `structuredContent`) (S-111 @9f8db11); `pi-mono/packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/truncate.ts` (reported loss) (S-113 @400d690); `codex-rs/tools/src/tool_output.rs` (`log_output` "not the authoritative tool result"; `fallback_token_limit_override`; `code_mode_result`) and `codex-rs/models-manager/models.json` `truncation_policy` (S-107 @0735c51); `SWE-agent/sweagent/tools/parsing.py` L371–460 (`FunctionCallingParser` error codes and template) (S-114 @3ea751c). The validator-aware declared loss, the offload-with-compiled-reader rule, the surface-failure event and the metric are `no-precedent / novel` relative to these (which truncate without knowing the consumer and do not persist surface failures as typed events).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) truncation limits are universal runtime constants and work — answered: the *policy* is data keyed per model in codex already; making it compiled changes where it lives, not what it does, and adds the E6 check. (ii) Programmatic tool calling shows the best compression is "never render" — answered: admitted as `return_only` with per-nested-call ledgering; the authoritative results still exist as ledger records. (iii) Persisting every malformed call as an event bloats the ledger — answered: the event carries a hash of the raw call, not the call; blobs offload per ADR-0029; the count is what the compliance metric needs. (iv) A closed `SurfaceFailure` set will lag provider quirks — answered: extended by ADR; unknown parser failures map to `unparseable` with the parser's detail in the payload. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes WS-E1 supplies capability failure classes; assumes WS-G1 validators declare the result fields they read (`validator_reads` derivable from Validator `inputs`); assumes WS-B1 accepts the event in the `action` category; independent of model tier; the only `provisional` source (S-376) is counter-evidence, not load-bearing.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (`ResultRenderSpec{full | truncate}`, `ErrorFormatSpec`, `SurfaceFailure`, `action.tool.surface_rejected`, `surface_rejection_rate` declaration); **C0 / Stage 3** (E5 distinguishability and E6 static checks for the two minimal profiles); **C1 / Stage 5** (`concise`, `offload` with `read_artifact`); **C2** (`return_only` with code-mode; token-optimized notations if ever).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies: T-LCD-01 (rendering policy is profile-owned data), T-LCD-02 (renderings and templates are `Text` leaves; opacity reported), T-LCD-13 (`surface_rejected` is the *activated-but-unusable* signal that separates compliance from validity per profile), T-LCD-15 (`applies_to_classes = {native}`; `n/a` for hosted), T-LCD-11 (retained fields travel with `outputSchema`; loss declared). Does not by itself satisfy T-LCD-04 (executable at Stage 3 via AC-6/AC-7 of the dossier). **ADR-0024 ladder rung:** render/error specs are *typed parameters* of a profile rule (rung 3); the renderer and parser are *Core* (rung 5).

## Consequences

- WS-C3's `result_render`/`error_format` rules write these records and nothing else; WS-D1 executes `ResultRenderSpec` at run time beside the transcript renderer (OQ-063 ownership extended).
- WS-B1 adds `action.tool.surface_rejected`; WS-I1 projects it into the tool-call span as a terminal without an effect.
- WS-E5/D3 provide the `read_artifact` executor for offloaded results (OQ raised on ownership).
- WS-I2 registers `surface_rejection_rate` and may pair it with keep-rate-class outcome metrics in surface-variant experiments.
- WS-E4/K3 lower `retained_fields` alongside `outputSchema` and list `concise`/`truncate` as `truncated` loss entries.

## Reversibility

**High for modes and thresholds** (rule parameters). **Medium for the offload-with-compiled-reader rule** (relaxing it re-admits surfaces without compiled identity). **Low for "compression never a default" and the authoritative-artifact rule** — reversing them makes E6 unverifiable and re-introduces silent loss.
