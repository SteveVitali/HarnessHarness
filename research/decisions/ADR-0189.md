# ADR-0189 — The estimator interface: `compute_estimator` with `rules` (C3 heuristic baseline, no model calls), `bandit` (windowed/discounted, drift-reset), `surface_prior` (read-only evidence refs) and `predictor` (C4 via WS-I5); `MarginalValueEstimate` with oracle conditionality; typed inputs only; estimation cost charged and capped; every non-static variant a conditioned rule with expiry

**Status:** ratified
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-F4 (consumers: WS-F3, WS-C2, WS-G3, WS-I5, WS-I6, WS-J4, WS-C3, WS-D3, WS-B1) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** companion ADR-0188 (the `compute_policy` contract), ADR-0117 (the four recorded inputs and the critic decision rule), ADR-0047 (oracle classes; judged values labelled/calibrated), ADR-0115/0112 (`claimed` evidence class; `model_claim` never sufficient), ADR-0120/0126/0155 (fingerprint drift; profile supersession; drift bracket), ADR-0160 (`FittedSurfaceReport` with expiry), ADR-0121 (`QualityPrior`; `trajectory_digest`), ADR-0020/0124 (profile capabilities; conditioned rules with debt records), ADR-0039/0043 (`harness_overhead.*`; `evaluator_calls`), ADR-0078 (materialized views vs derived memories), ADR-0012 decision 7 (no interpolation), ADR-0046 §7 (C4 gates), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05); OQ-012, OQ-098, OQ-296, OQ-420/-04; CF-404; R-2.6.4, R-2.7.3, R-2.9.5

## Context

The scheduler's value depends entirely on its estimates, and the literature gives four families of estimator with different evidence status. Rule-based stopping on agreement among samples is accepted and cheap (Adaptive-Consistency, S-570, EMNLP 2023: up to 7.9× fewer samples within 0.1 % accuracy); answer-stabilisation probes (Certaindex, S-568; Dynasor `cot.py` L36–150) stop reasoning early but each probe is a real model call every 32–64 tokens and the hedge detector reads free text; bandit allocation over (task class × model) cells runs in production (litellm `adaptive_router`, S-457) with informative priors but, by its own README, "no rescaling — drift is a v1 concern", regex-only rewards and no latency term; learned predictors of reward-given-budget (S-572) and of self-predicted improvement (S-571) deliver the largest reported gains but require training and, in the second case, consume a `model_claim`. Non-stationary bandits have a standard remedy — discounted or sliding-window statistics with regret matching the lower bound up to a log factor (Garivier & Moulines, S-573, Tier A) — and the program already emits the reset triggers: `provider_drift = observed` (ADR-0120/0155) and profile supersession (ADR-0126). Coverage from repeated sampling is log-linear only when a verifier selects among samples; majority and reward-model selection plateau (S-567), so an estimate for "one more sample" is meaningless without knowing the oracle class. ADR-0117 has already fixed the evaluator decision rule and its four recorded inputs and left "the estimator" to WS-F4; ADR-0107 decision 6 permits WS-F4 to replace the learned-p95 reservation term.

## Options considered

1. **One learned estimator from the start (bandit or predictor as the C3 default).** Rejected: routers and adaptive allocators mostly fail to beat simple baselines under unified evaluation (S-449 `provisional`; S-083); a learned default would put `provisional` evidence into a default; cold cells have no data.
2. **Model self-estimates as the estimator ("ask the model whether more compute would help").** Rejected as sole basis: a `model_claim` (`authority = delegate`, evidence class `claimed`, ADR-0112/0115) can never alone decide spend or stop (ADR-0016 invariant); admitted only as a labelled feature of a C4 predictor.
3. **Free-text stability heuristics (hedge-word lists, regex rewards).** Rejected: T-LCD-02 and ADR-0106 decision 3 (guards never read `Text`); litellm's own README calls its mapping "unvalidated".
4. **Stationary posteriors with a sample cap (litellm shape).** Rejected: model churn makes priors assumption debt; a cap without forgetting freezes a stale estimate.
5. **A registry class with a deterministic rule baseline and three learned variants, typed inputs only, windowed/discounted statistics keyed by snapshot fingerprint with hard resets, oracle-conditioned estimates, estimation cost charged and capped, every non-static variant a conditioned rule with expiry (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Class.** `compute_estimator` is a registered component class (`required_inputs ⊇ {ModelProfile, ResourceAccount}`) with one operation `estimate(option, ctx: ComputeContext, priors) → MarginalValueEstimate | EstimatorUnavailable{reason}` and an `observe(events[])` fold; variants `{rules, bandit, surface_prior, predictor}`, closed per dialect. A `compute_policy` variant names its estimator by `estimator_ref`.
2. **Estimate record.** `MarginalValueEstimate{delta_p_success{point, interval, source ∈ {rule, prior, online, unknown}}, expected_cost: ResourceVector (reserve-sized: profile `max_output`, capability `cost_model` via ADR-0089 `estimate`, child slice), expected_latency_ms, risk: RiskEstimate{effect_classes (derived from the option's capabilities, ADR-0031), coordination ∈ {none, fan_out, merge}, starvation_p (from `reservation_refusal_rate`), cache_invalidation: bool (from the profile's effort declaration)}, oracle_conditionality ∈ ADR-0047 oracle classes ∪ {none}, evidence_refs[]}`. Rules: `delta_p_success` for `extend_search` is `unknown` when `oracle_conditionality = none` (no reducer or validator — S-567 plateau); an `unknown` estimate is never ranked above `continue_as_configured`.
3. **`rules` — the C3 baseline; makes no model calls.** Deterministic rule table over ledger views: (a) additional sample while agreement over canonical output hashes or validator verdicts among samples so far < θ_agree and one more member is reservable; stop at agreement ≥ θ_agree or `k_max` (Adaptive-Consistency); (b) spawn only on declared parallel steps or `subagent_task` targets with `fan_out < cap` and a reservable slice ≥ `child_floor`; (c) evaluator iff ADR-0117 decision 7's rule is positive over `missed_failure_rate`, `false_stop_rate`, `harness_overhead.verification` and `cost(uncaught)` from `reversibility`, and the placement's calibration is `active`; (d) effort by `ModelRole` at the profile's declared ladder, step-up only on a recorded failure streak ≥ n and only when the profile declares per-message effort without cache invalidation (S-577); (e) alternate role/quality target only on a validator-fail streak with a `QualityPrior` whose `valid_until` holds and whose level is not `unknown`. Thresholds are MUST-data with debt records, pre-registered per experiment (Stage-4 placeholders, review items: θ_agree 0.8 over ≥ 3 samples, k_max 5, child_floor 10 % of `remaining`, streak n 2).
4. **`bandit` — C3/Stage 5.** Cells keyed by `(profile version_id, snapshot fingerprint, task_class, option)`; reward = `task_success` (Lab, deterministic oracle) or validator verdict/outcome class (in-loop), minus λ·cost from the pinned pricing table; selection by Thompson sampling or UCB; informative cold-start prior from registry declarations (litellm shape); statistics **windowed or discounted** (Garivier–Moulines) with declared `window | discount` (OQ-420); `min_n` below which a cell is `unknown` and the `rules` fallback fires; **hard reset** (`control.compute.prior_reset`) on `provider_drift = observed` or profile supersession; no cross-cell pooling unless declared as a debt-recorded rule (ADR-0012 decision 7). Credit is assigned by causal precedence (`causes[] ∋ decision_id`, ADR-0027) and rewards are ledger facts only. The `PriorCell` store is a kernel-derived **materialized view** rebuildable from `compute_decision_outcome` rows (ADR-0078), never a derived memory.
5. **`surface_prior` — C3/Stage 5.** Reads `QualityPrior` (ADR-0121) and `FittedSurfaceReport.conditionality_region` (ADR-0160) as *evidence refs* with `valid_until`; `unknown` levels refused; the engine never emits a rule (ADR-0160 consumer rule).
6. **`predictor` — C4/Stage 6.** A trained model over the typed feature set (`trajectory_digest` per OQ-296, stability signals over hashes/verdicts, prior cells, budget views) that may include `claimed` self-estimates (S-571) only as labelled columns; may issue model calls (probe completions, Certaindex-style) each ledgered with attribution `harness_overhead.scheduling` and capped by a per-run `budget_share_cap` (ADR-0117 pattern); exceeding the cap yields `EstimatorUnavailable{budget}` → `unknown`, never a stop of the beneficiary; deployable only through WS-I5 with a complete `SearchBudgetRecord` and ADR-0046 §7 gates; whether a probe completion is a `Validator{kind: judge}` is OQ-422.
7. **Inputs are typed and provenance-bounded.** No variant reads a `Text` leaf; no variant consumes a `model_claim` as its sole basis for `stop_now` or for any spend increase; lifted preferences (`authority ≤ external`) may only exclude options; stability is computed over canonical output hashes and typed verdicts.
8. **Expiry.** Every non-`rules` variant and every prior family is a conditioned rule with `AssumptionDebtRecord{expiry_condition ⊇ {profile superseded, provider_drift = observed, suite validity superseded, age > window}, removal_test_ref: lab/value-of-compute-v1 retirement arm}`; `rules` thresholds carry the same record (T-LCD-05).
9. **Reservation sizing (OQ-098; CF-404).** When a non-`static` policy is bound, `expected_cost` replaces the learned-p95 term of ADR-0107 decision 6 under `pool`; conservation is unaffected (over-consumption is charged and flagged, ADR-0040 decision 3).

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-01 (no per-model branch; model-keyed cells and thresholds are conditioned rules), T-LCD-02 (no `Text` read), T-LCD-05 (expiry and removal test on every variant and prior family), T-LCD-07 (`unknown` never coerced; cold cells are `unknown`), T-LCD-08, T-LCD-09 (estimator variant is a factor), T-LCD-10 (variant identity content-addressed), T-LCD-12, T-LCD-14 (estimation cost budgeted and reported; learned variants gated matched-total — ADR-0190), T-LCD-15 (`scheduling.prior_coverage` and judged rewards class-scoped; `n/a` for hosted). Not applicable: T-LCD-03/-04/-06/-11/-13. **ADR-0024 rung:** the estimate schema, reset rules, `min_n` gate, causal credit assignment and the input-provenance checks are MUST-code; thresholds, λ, window/discount, `budget_share_cap` and cell keys are MUST-data; estimators are registered variants.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- **(a) Mechanism evidence:** adaptive stopping on agreement (S-570, Tier A); non-stationary bandit remedies (S-573, Tier A); coverage log-linear only with verifiers, plateau otherwise (S-567, Tier C); difficulty-conditioned allocation (S-566, Tier C); reward-distribution predictors (S-572, Tier C); stability probes cost real calls (S-568; Dynasor source); drift and supersession triggers exist (ADR-0120/0126/0155); the four critic inputs are recorded (ADR-0117); model claims are `claimed` evidence (ADR-0112/0115). No `provisional` number is load-bearing; the 2026 preprints (S-574/-10, S-082, S-089) contribute mechanism only.
- **(b) Source-code precedent:** litellm `litellm/router_strategy/adaptive_router/bandit.py` L28–137 (Beta cells, informative priors, Thompson sampling, weighted quality/cost score) and `signals.py` L31–120 (per-turn credit from cheap detectors) @ c1a83fc — adopted shape, forgetting and typed rewards added; dynasor `dynasor/core/cot.py` L36–150 and `core/entropy.py` L32–54 @ 0d2f1b9 — stability window adopted over hashes/verdicts, hedge-word regex rejected, probes charged; dspy `dspy/predict/best_of_n.py` L50–83 @ ca54a85 — threshold stop over a declared reward. **A drift-reset, ledger-fed, oracle-conditioned estimator with typed-only inputs is `no-precedent / novel as integrated`.**
- **(c) Disconfirming evidence considered:** (i) learned allocators often do not beat baselines (S-449 `provisional`; S-083) — answered by `rules` as the default and matched-total gating for learned variants; (ii) estimation overhead (Dynasor probes; predictor training) — answered by charging, capping and the no-model-call baseline; (iii) agreement ≠ correctness (S-453 hard regime) — answered by oracle conditionality and the ordering below validator verdicts; (iv) discount/window constants are arbitrary — answered by pre-registration as MUST-data and the fact that a reset degrades only to the baseline; (v) fingerprint drift has false positives (S-441 via ADR-0120) — a spurious reset costs regret, not correctness. None overturns.
- **(d) Conditionality:** `rules` assumes a reducer or validator exists for sampling options (else inert) and calibrated placements for evaluator options; `bandit` assumes ≥ `min_n` observations per cell within one snapshot's lifetime — short-lived snapshots will keep cells `unknown` (which is correct); `predictor` assumes a search budget and WS-I5's pipeline; all model-keyed values are conditioned rules; benefits are conditional on ADR-0190's protocol.
- **(e) Build-stage assignment:** **C3 / Stage 4** — `compute_estimator` class, `MarginalValueEstimate`, `rules`; **C3 / Stage 5** — `bandit`, `surface_prior`, `PriorCell` view, `prior_reset`; **C4 / Stage 6** — `predictor` through WS-I5.

## Consequences

- WS-C3 is asked to confirm effort as a profile capability/rule kind with `per_message_effort` and cache-invalidation declared (OQ-421); WS-C2's `QualityPrior` and OQ-296 `trajectory_digest` become estimator inputs; WS-G3's inputs are consumed unchanged; WS-I6 owns the expiry of every prior family; WS-J4 computes `scheduling.regret_vs_oracle` from replicates; WS-I1 adds `harness_overhead.scheduling`; WS-D3 hosts the `PriorCell` materialized view.
- ADR-0107 decision 6's sizing rule is superseded only while a non-`static` policy is bound (additive).

## Reversibility

**High** for `bandit`/`surface_prior`/`predictor` (registered variants; dropping them loses no ledger data — the `PriorCell` view is rebuildable). **Medium** for `rules` thresholds as MUST-data (changing them is a `HirDiff`; loosening is rejected in evolution contexts). The typed-input rule is the one hard to reverse: admitting free-text signals later would re-open the T-LCD-02 escape hatch.
