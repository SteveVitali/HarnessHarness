**Status:** ratified (synthesis-authored)
**Owner workstream(s):** Phase 4 synthesis (WS-F3, F4, F5, I5, I6, I7, I8, L8; consumers WS-B1, WS-A3, WS-H1, WS-B3, WS-B4, WS-C1, WS-C3, WS-G1, WS-I2, WS-I3, WS-J2…J5, WS-K2, Phase 5 spec assembly) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0185…ADR-0207 (Phase 4 workstream ADRs), ADR-0002 (i)–(iv) and (d), ADR-0053 D-5, ADR-0041/0046, ADR-0103, ADR-0131, ADR-0026, ADR-0135, ADR-0152/0183, ADR-0141, ADR-0197; CF-394…CF-467; OQ-414, OQ-419, OQ-421, OQ-427, OQ-437, OQ-442, OQ-452, OQ-454, OQ-455, OQ-460, OQ-463, OQ-464, OQ-465; ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05/-06/-09/-13/-14)

## Context

Eight Phase 4 dossiers were written in parallel against the Phase 1–3 contracts and `synthesis/phase-3.md` §6. Three pairs could not see each other and pre-registered the gap — WS-F4 and WS-F5 wrote without WS-F3 (CF-399, CF-406), WS-I8 reconciled itself against WS-I5/I6 mid-flight (CF-436, CF-437) and WS-L8 pre-registered its seam with WS-F3 (CF-448). The brief's six foci — evidence discipline on the highest-uncertainty cluster, composition of F3/F4/F5 with the security kernel and the budget model, the evolution loop as one evidence-bearing chain over the lab substrate, L8's boundaries with B3/H7, the deferral list for Phase 5, and the settled-for-Phase-5 list — each surfaced contradictions that no single workstream could rule on. This ADR records the rulings; the amended texts live in the amendment logs of the ADRs named per section. Doc 3 §7.1: the ontology is the vocabulary tie-breaker; §11.5 step 4: shared-contract convergence before ratification.

## Options considered

1. **Ratify each workstream's proposal as written and leave the seams to Phase 5.** Rejected: two `MergePolicy` sums, two peer-message mechanisms, two spawn procedures, a scheduler that both binds and emits decisions, two maturity vocabularies and a debt-record field its own dossier withdrew would enter the spec as they stand; Phase 5 would find them, but the Canonical Spec's control and evolution sections would be assembled from divergent spellings.
2. **Defer the contested seams by rejecting the later ADR of each pair.** Rejected: every pair is complementary (F3 owns *when/what*, F5 *who writes/how merged*; I5 owns the stages, I7 the label, I8 the snapshot; L8 the inter-activation object, F3 the intra-activation one); rejecting one loses a ratified-quality contract to save a rename.
3. **Rule each seam once here, amend the parties by logged amendment, and fix the vocabulary in Ontology v4 (chosen).**

## Decision

### A. One coordination substrate (F3 ↔ F5; CF-449, CF-450, CF-451, CF-458, CF-466; OQ-414, OQ-427)

1. **One `spawn`.** ADR-0185's kernel `spawn` is the only child-creating act; ADR-0193 D4's reservation order is realised inside it as steps (4b) `grant_ownership` and (4c) all-or-nothing `acquire(resource(key))` for `share` mode; `control.subagent.spawned` carries the union payload; `SubagentResult` carries `outcome: ChildOutcome` beside `outcome_class` and folds ADR-0193's `ChildResult` (CF-451).
2. **One `MergePolicy`.** ADR-0192 D1's closed sum is canonical; ADR-0187's `refuse`/`ask`/`three_way` are aliases (`single_writer`/`parent_decides`/`three_way_text | validator_selected`); `parent_wins`/`child_wins` are `MergeResolution.choose{side}` values on a surfaced conflict, never policies; `MergeConflictRecord` is the `workspace`-kind `MergeConflict`; one event family `control.merge.{started, completed, resolved}` (CF-449). The C1/Stage-4 workspace default is `single_writer` (conflict on any overlap, `escalate`); `three_way_text{line}` for disjoint hunks is the second-slice default; evidence from `lab/coordination-topology-v1` (OQ-427).
3. **One peer-message mechanism.** A peer message is a wakeup occurrence on `Trigger.peer_message{from}` (ADR-0131 amended) delivered as `Cue.woken` (no `Cue.peer_message`), events `control.message.{sent, refused}`, reach tree-local under `MessagingPolicy` (defaults `sibling/broadcast: false`), `delivery_mode ∈ {steer, follow_up}`, label `⊔(ctx(from), delegate)`, never an endorsement (CF-395, CF-450).
4. **Names.** ADR-0193's invariants are FD-1…FD-4 (CF-458); `on_owner_end ∈ {return_to_grantor, detach_to_child}` (CF-466); kill-point rows KP-16…KP-21 and KP-F1…KP-F5 in ADR-0132's sequence (CF-459); `coordination_delta` joins `HirDiff.classification` (ADR-0017; CF-414); check 8 (ownership) joins ADR-0035's monitor set (CF-408).
5. **Composition with the kernel and the budget model (brief focus 2).** Delegation narrows-or-preserves at one seam — `delegate` (ADR-0053 D-1…D-3) is step (3) of `spawn`, `attenuation_delta` is recorded, ownership is attenuated like authority (ADR-0191 O-2) and never *is* authority (O-6); budget is conserved across fan-out because every child budget is an `allocate` against `remaining` with `slice` remainders released at the child's terminal and `pool` children only tightening (SP-2; ADR-0040); the scheduler cannot widen (B-2/B-3; `amend` absent from its verb set; evolution-authored loosening refused, ADR-0053 D-5); a peer message cannot endorse, grant or move ownership (M-1).

### B. The control seam (F4 ↔ F1/F3/G1/C3; CF-399, CF-400, CF-403, CF-405, CF-452; OQ-419, OQ-421)

1. ADR-0103's driver loop gains the bind step `d′ ← compute_policy.bind(d, ctx)` between `decide` and `envelope.check` (ADR-0188 D2); the scheduler binds unbound parameters, tightens bound ones, or advises through a measured artifact — it never proposes, refuses, grants or owns β, and the packaged `static` variant leaves every earlier stage byte-identical.
2. The scheduler **never emits** a `delegate` decision (CF-452): a `code`-owned `delegate` is proposed by a `HarnessRule`/`Procedure` step; the scheduler binds its `budget_slice`, `spec_ref` and an unbound `delegation_reason` from the declared reason; `spawn_subagent` is `infeasible{undeclared_parallelism}` otherwise. ADR-0186 decision 5 reworded.
3. `TaskValue{success_value, failure_cost | from_reversibility, currency}` is an optional field of the ADR-0109 task-contract projection defaulted at `seal`; money-valued success only in Lab arms (OQ-419; CF-405).
4. `effort{ladder, per_message, cache_invalidation_on_change}` is a `ModelProfile/1` capability and a `ProfileRuleInventory/1` rule kind by dialect bump (ADR-0124; OQ-421); a profile declaring none yields `capability_unmet`.
5. Every benefit claim about a scheduler is conditional on `budget_match.status = matched` under the declared `MatchSpec`, learned variants on `matched_total` with a complete `SearchBudgetRecord` (ADR-0190); adoption of a non-`static` default only by `ComparisonReport` (ADR-0105 rule).

### C. The evidence-bearing evolution loop (I5 ↔ I6 ↔ I7 ↔ I8 over J2/J3/J4/J5; CF-415…CF-421, CF-436…CF-438, CF-453…CF-457, CF-460, CF-461, CF-462; OQ-437, OQ-442, OQ-452, OQ-454, OQ-455, OQ-460)

1. **The loop.** *edit* — a candidate is a `HirDiff` with a falsifiable `FailureHypothesis` (ADR-0195) applied through `apply(base, diff)` and published only into `exp/` (ADR-0147/0153); *attribute* — S2 consumes `attribution_ref → AttributionHypothesis | AttributionReport/1` and refuses `TargetMismatch`; the S5 paired `full_set` comparison is an M1 designed ablation over the diff, so every survivor carries `attribution_label ≥ designed_ablation`; `causal_*` labels license `locality_claim` (ADR-0201; CF-455); *evaluate matched* — S4 `matched_total` against `oracle_best_of_n`/`selected_best_of_n` is normative, S5 `artifact_benefit` + `retention` on held-out, S6 `transfer` + beneficiary compliance emitting `CompatibilityRecord{verified}` per pair (ADR-0194; ADR-0041 M3; ADR-0046 §7; ADR-0159; ADR-0163 L4); *expire/retire* — every accepted edit is a conditioned rule with a typed `removal_test: RemovalTest{kind: retirement_experiment}` (ADR-0197 D6), `expiry_condition ∋ model_version_change` and a snapshot scope in `scope.model_selectors`; the retirement experiment (ADR-0156 as amended: candidate-bound diffs, `retirement_batch`) yields a `RemovalVerdict`; `retired` is a human-sealed supersession with a `RetirementRecord` (ADR-0197 D7); *consolidate* — a lesson is `consolidated` only when its retirement experiment passes on the post-training snapshot under retention and held-out-family conditions (ADR-0204), recorded in `RetirementRecord.rationale`/`report_ref` (CF-457), and the imported snapshot fires `model_version_change` on every scoped rule (ADR-0197 D5; ADR-0203 D5).
2. **Substrate.** Every evaluation is an `ExperimentSpec` of a ratified kind run as `run_kind = experiment` (J3); every score is a `ResultsRow/1` version — counterfactual and regrade scorings are overlay rows with `arm_role`/`intervention_ref` (J5; CF-431); every claim is a `ComparisonReport` with `label`, `budget_match`, `benefit_kind` and `EstimatorSelection` (J4); every artefact a candidate needs is a registry record (J2) — the evolution service reads registry records never as a model-facing surface (R-NM) and proposers run under `plugin_abi/1`.
3. **Record homes (CF-460; OQ-454).** `CompatibilityRecord` = `conformance_report{subject_kind: snapshot_pair}` (ADR-0152/0183); `TrainingExposureRecord` = `measurement.export.delivered` payload of the `training_export/1` target (ADR-0141); `ConsolidationReport` = `AnalysisRecord` (ADR-0162); `CoEvolutionCycleRecord` = bundle S-layer sidecar (ADR-0139). `trained_on` is a WS-J4 row annotation, not an ADR-0143 stratum (OQ-455). `SnapshotClaim` extends `ModelDiscoveryClaim` (ADR-0120).
4. **Governance invariants as acceptance criteria (CF-462).** ADR-0002 (i)–(iv) are G1 (`SealRefused{not_human}`; AT-H1-05 (iv)), G1/G7 (judges never gate), G2/G5 (`classify` at S1 and S7; `SelfModificationRefused` joins ADR-0053 D-5 and ADR-0182 X6), G3/G4 (auto-revert only to a human-sealed ancestor; reflexive debt), each with an AC-I5 fixture and each one-to-one with the H1 monitor's refusals. `split` canaries are `exploratory` at Stage 6a (CF-421).
5. **Maturity (CF-453).** One vocabulary `maturity ∈ {instrument-grade, research-grade}`; Stage 6a (human-proposed) is `instrument-grade`; every automated family, hosted-coordinate campaign and the whole co-evolution layer is `research-grade`, `preview`-labelled, excluded from C0–C3 acceptance and from leaderboards without independent reproduction; promotion test OQ-440.

### D. The organizational layer's boundaries (L8 ↔ B3/H7/F3/H6/L2; CF-441…CF-448, CF-464; OQ-463, OQ-464, OQ-465)

1. L8 adds no trigger, lease, approval verb or Π row: it consumes ADR-0130/0131 (leases, restore, triggers, occurrences, inbox runs, `continue_goal`), ADR-0069…0071 (Π-12, `UnattendedPolicy`, `ApproverGrant`, `defer`, `ActionPattern`) and ADR-0066 (`lifecycle.escalation.*`) verbatim and adds the `WorkItem` records, the reconciler over them, the state map as Π narrowing data, escalation *routing*, and projections (CF-464).
2. `run_kind` gains `fleet` (ADR-0183 §C.1 rule; CF-441); `control.work_item.*` enters the ledger with the audit-grade subset `{dispatched, stopped, blocked, handoff, owner_changed, owner_acknowledged, cancelled}` and the fleet-anchor obligation (ADR-0066; CF-444; OQ-465).
3. `effects.external_irreversible` is a registered `ext.*` dimension until D-3 closes (OQ-463; CF-445).
4. The F3 seam: `WorkItem` is inter-activation and never spawns; `SubagentSpec`/child runs are intra-activation; `causes[]` + `goal_ref` suffice (CF-448; OQ-464).
5. **One attendance/default table (CF-447):** `interactive` → `ask` resolves to the human; `async` → `defer` (H7 default); `unattended` headless CLI → `deny` (ADR-0168, no sink); `unattended`/`async` fleet → `defer` + `EscalationPolicy` chain (ADR-0206, a sink exists by construction); every default a conditioned rule with `lab/org-policy-v1` as removal test.
