# ADR-0046 — Matched-budget paired comparison protocol: `eval_budget` equality as an engine precondition; `search_budget` as a separate record; artifact / search-time / transfer as three benefit kinds; adaptive selection admissible only inside search; C4 admissibility gates

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 1 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I2 (protocol) with WS-J3 (enforcement), WS-J4 (estimation), WS-I5 (C4 gates), WS-L2 (budget vector, cost confidence) · **Phase:** 1 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (evidence standard: matched compute, held-out, artifact vs search-time), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-03/-14), ADR-0005 (cost provenance), ADR-0012 (paired Δ at matched budget), ADR-0013 (admissible granularities); CF-004, CF-099; OQ-016, OQ-025, OQ-033, OQ-124, OQ-082; R-2.9.2, R-2.9.5, R-2.10.3, R-2.10.4

## Context

WS-A2 defines the harness effect as the *paired* Δ = J(θ) − J(θ₀) at matched budget and T-LCD-14 (ratified) requires the engine to refuse un-budgeted comparisons with `search_budget`/`eval_budget` per arm. What remains undefined is the *protocol*: which budget must match (limits vs consumption), what tolerance applies, how search spend is recorded, and how artifact benefit, search-time benefit and transfer are distinguished as reportable rows. The motivating mechanism is documented from several directions: S-072 shows a harness effect that exists at a 20 k window and vanishes at wide windows (budget × harness interaction); S-083 shows that under matched feedback and inference budgets harness evolution does not consistently beat test-time scaling and transfers weakly; S-092 shows allocation *within* a search budget changes outcomes; PRISM (S-093) reports lift as a function of budget with repeatability. No audited evaluation plane (Harbor, HAL, Inspect, τ-bench, AgentDojo) enforces budget matching; HAL and Harbor *measure* cost per attempt with pinned pricing.

## Options considered

1. **Cost-adjusted comparison only (Pareto view; no precondition).** Rejected: a Pareto plot cannot answer "did the component help at the same budget?", which is the Lab's core question (T-LCD-09/-14); it stays as a *view*.
2. **Strict equality of actual consumption.** Rejected: arms rarely consume identical amounts; would refuse most legitimate comparisons and invite gaming by padding.
3. **Equality of declared hard limits as precondition; consumption measured and flagged beyond a tolerance; search spend recorded separately; three benefit kinds (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Arm budgets.** Every `Arm` carries `eval_budget` (per-run hard limits over the WS-L2 budget vector — the WS-L2 kernel dimension list ratified in ADR-0039 (CF-099)) and a `SearchBudgetRecord{rollouts, model_calls, tokens, feedback_labels_used, judge_calls, wall_clock, allocation: map<component|slot, share>, adaptive_selection?}` covering *everything* spent to produce the arm's frozen artifact. `search_budget = unknown` is legal only for product-level comparisons and is flagged.
2. **Precondition.** `compare(arm_a, arm_b, …)` refuses (`UnmatchedBudget`) unless `eval_budget` is equal dimension-wise, the task set/split, environment image and fault/perturbation profile are identical unless they are the varied factor, and model snapshots per role are identical unless model is the varied factor. Refusal applies to *running and reporting*.
3. **Consumption.** Actual consumption per run is measured (`control.budget.consumed`, `measurement.cost.attributed{provenance, confidence}`) and reported as a distribution; `budget_match.status = imbalanced` when medians differ by more than the declared tolerance (default 10 % per dimension, OQ-124) — reported with the cost-adjusted (Pareto-with-origin) view attached, never refused. For hosted rows cost confidence (measured / reported / reconstructed, WS-L2) is carried into the comparison.
4. **Three benefit kinds, three rows.** `artifact_benefit` = paired Δ of the *frozen* artifact on a held-out split whose hash predates the search (`LeakedSplit` otherwise), with evaluation-phase search spend = 0; `search_time_benefit` = performance during search against a baseline given the *same* `SearchBudgetRecord` (test-time-scaling/discovery baseline per S-083, e.g. best-of-N with equal rollouts; `UnmatchedSearchBudget` otherwise); `transfer` = paired Δ on a held-out model family, environment or task family, with `transfer_ratio`. A claim that reports only one kind is labelled as such.
5. **Pairing and estimation.** Effects are paired by task; by replicate only where all participants declare `seed_honoured`; intervals by the metric's `interval_method` (clustered-by-task CLT or bootstrap by default). Equivalence (T-LCD-03/-04) is the same operation with a pre-registered margin and a three-valued verdict.
6. **Adaptive/reduced evaluation.** Disagreement-weighted or difficulty-filtered task selection (S-082, S-237) is admissible inside `search_budget`; an `artifact_benefit` row computed from a sampled set must carry `estimated{estimator, inclusion_probabilities_ref}` and may not be the sole basis for acceptance.
7. **C4 admissibility gates (inherited by WS-I5).** A proposal is acceptable only if: `artifact_benefit` on held-out tasks has an interval excluding zero at the pre-registered level; `search_time_benefit` is reported; `transfer` on ≥ 1 held-out model family is reported with sign and interval; no veto metric regresses; `retention` Δ ≥ −margin (S-091 pair); `search_budget` is complete. These are the operational form of ADR-0002's seven-point standard items 1–3 and 5–7.

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-14 (precondition; `search_budget`/`eval_budget` per arm; search vs artifact separated in the results schema) and supplies the operation T-LCD-03/-04 use. Does not decide the budget-vector dimensions (WS-L2, CF-099) nor the statistical model for interaction/surface estimation (WS-J4).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: budget is an interacting factor — S-072 (`provisional`); evolution must be compared to matched test-time search with held-out transfer — S-083 (`provisional (counter)`), doc 2 §8 and §5.8 seven-point standard (Tier-B synthesis), ADR-0002 (ratified evidence standard); allocation within a search budget matters — S-092 (`provisional`); lift should be reported under budget with repeatability — S-093 (accepted venue, single team); adaptive selection estimates rank, not effect — S-082 (`provisional`: ρ = 0.13 at 7 %), S-237 (`provisional`); measured consumption with pinned pricing — S-135 (Tier A), S-132 (Tier B). The C0 rule (refuse unmatched limits; record search spend; three rows) asserts no magnitude and rests on the ratified T-LCD-14 plus Tier-A/B measurement precedents.
- (b) Source-code precedent: measurement — `harbor/src/harbor/models/trial/result.py` (`compute_token_cost_totals`), `hal-harness/hal/benchmarks/base_benchmark.py` (`get_total_cost`, wall-clock file), `hal/utils/weave_utils.py` (unified cost); attempts — `harbor/src/harbor/models/job/config.py` (`n_attempts`, per-phase timeout multipliers = the only budget-like limits in an audited plane). **Enforced budget matching, a search-budget record and benefit-kind separation: `no-precedent / novel`.**
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) multi-dimensional budgets rarely balance — answered by limits-vs-consumption split with tolerance and a flagged, not refused, imbalance; (ii) cost-efficiency is a legitimate claim — answered by the Pareto view on the same runs (HAL precedent, origin included); (iii) "matched total" can still be unfair if allocation differs (S-092) — answered by recording `allocation` in the search record so the critique is checkable; (iv) held-out splits shrink already-small suites — answered by admitting estimated rows inside search and by leaving suite composition to WS-I4; (v) counterfactual attribution without deterministic replay may be invalid (OQ-082) — this ADR pairs by task, not by replay, so it is unaffected; WS-I7 must resolve replay separately.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier; tolerance default is a placeholder pending OQ-124; the protocol assumes the Lab controls hard limits for native runs and can *enforce* them at the environment boundary for hosted runs (wall-clock, env-minutes) while token/spend limits for hosted runs may be *reported* rather than enforced — such arms are flagged `limits_enforced = partial`.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0 / Stage 1 — `Arm{eval_budget, search_budget}` schema and the precondition as a schema constraint; C0 / Stage 3 — `compare`, `artifact_benefit`, `search_time_benefit`, `transfer`, `equivalence_run`, consumption imbalance reporting (AC-I2-2, -6); C4 / Stage 6 — the admissibility gates consumed by WS-I5.

## Consequences

- WS-J3 must enforce hard limits per run and record consumption; WS-L2 must fix the budget-vector dimensions and cost confidence before Stage 3 (CF-099).
- WS-I5's pipeline steps "matched-budget eval → held-out regression → cross-model compatibility" are `artifact_benefit`, `retention`, `transfer` rows from this protocol; WS-I5 adds no separate estimator.
- WS-I3's bundle pins the `PreRegistration.task_split_hash`, price-table version and the search record so a published effect is auditable.
- WS-J4's Pareto and surface views consume `budget_match.status` and cost confidence; mixed-confidence rows are stratified (OQ-033).
- Every published "X beats baseline" in HarnessHarness reports is a `ComparisonReport` with `benefit_kind` and `budget_match.status`; prose claims without one fail the readiness glossary/claims check.

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Relaxing the precondition to a warning is a one-line policy change but would re-admit the S-083 class of confounded claims into stored results; tightening the tolerance or adding budget dimensions is additive. The three-row separation is cheap to keep and expensive to reconstruct after the fact (search spend is unrecoverable if not logged).

## Amendment log (Phase 3 synthesis, 2026-09-10)

- `ComparisonReport` gains (additive) `test{kind ∈ {permutation_signflip, wilcoxon_signed_rank, paired_bayes}}`, `sign_profile{helped, hurt, unchanged}`, `tail_effects{P50, P95, max}`, `outcome_bounds{lower, upper, verdict ∈ {robust, sensitive}}`, `multiplicity{holm | bh}`, `label ∈ {confirmatory, exploratory, preview}` and an `EstimatorSelection` per interval (CF-337; ADR-0157/0158). The readiness glossary rule gains: every "X beats Y" is a `ComparisonReport` *with a `label`*; unlabelled or `preview` claims fail the check.
- Decision 4's search baseline is realized by ADR-0159 decision 5 (`oracle_best_of_n` = pass@N of the baseline arm's replicates, labelled upper bound; `selected_best_of_n` when a selector was recorded); the realized-benefit decomposition (ADR-0014) is reported as four stages with `n/a{observability}` for judged stages.
