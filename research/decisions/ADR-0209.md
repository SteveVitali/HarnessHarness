# ADR-0209 — Scope-register changes at Phase 4: R-2.6.3, R-2.6.4, R-2.6.5, R-2.9.5, R-2.9.6, R-2.9.7, R-2.9.8, R-2.12.6 → specified-by-ADR; stage and maturity notes on seven items; R-2.9.8 marked research-grade and conditional; novelty annotations confirmed; no tier or MoSCoW change

**Status:** ratified (synthesis-authored)
**Owner workstream(s):** Phase 4 synthesis (WS-F3, F4, F5, I5, I6, I7, I8, L8) · **Phase:** 4 · **Related:** ADR-0002 (C4 fully specified; (e) research-grade flags), ADR-0007 (R-2.9.6 stage note), ADR-0010, ADR-0146, ADR-0184 (prior scope ADRs), ADR-0185…ADR-0207, ADR-0208; CF-397, CF-403, CF-434, CF-453, CF-464; R-2.6.3, R-2.6.4, R-2.6.5, R-2.9.5, R-2.9.6, R-2.9.7, R-2.9.8, R-2.12.6

## Context

Doc 3 §4 and the LEDGER binding constraints require every scope change to be an ADR, never a silent register edit. Phase 4 delivered contracts, data models, acceptance criteria and build-stage assignments for the eight remaining `open` items of the §2 catalogue (the C3 control/orchestration items and the five C4 items). Four dossiers also proposed stage placements that put C0/C1/C2 *slices* inside C3/C4 items (the CF-285 pattern used at Phases 2 and 3), and ADR-0002 (e) requires the co-evolution layer to carry an explicit maturity flag.

## Options considered

1. **Leave the eight items `open` until Spec text lands at Phase 5.** Rejected: the readiness gate audits `specified-by-ADR` status per item, and the Phase 5 briefs need to know which C0/C1 slices the C3/C4 items carry.
2. **Re-tier the items whose slices are C0/C1 (e.g. R-2.6.3 → C1).** Rejected: the items' *services* remain C3/C4; the slices are already scheduled by ratified ADRs (ADR-0053/0116/0085 for `spawn`; ADR-0007 for the debt record) and a stage note preserves the ladder without changing the catalogue.
3. **Status change to `specified-by-ADR` for all eight with stage/maturity notes, R-2.9.8 marked `research-grade, conditional`, novelty annotations confirmed; no tier or MoSCoW change (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Status → `specified-by-ADR`** for R-2.6.3 (ADR-0185…0187), R-2.6.4 (ADR-0188…0190), R-2.6.5 (ADR-0191…0193), R-2.9.5 (ADR-0194…0196), R-2.9.6 (ADR-0197/0198), R-2.9.7 (ADR-0199…0201), R-2.9.8 (ADR-0202…0204), R-2.12.6 (ADR-0205…0207). Spec text lands at Phase 5. No item added or removed.
2. **Stage notes (no tier or MoSCoW change):** R-2.6.3 — the C1/Stage 4 kernel slice (`spawn`/`merge`, `SubagentSpec`/`SubagentResult`, SP-1…SP-8, C-1…C-8, payloads, AC-F3-01…09/12) is required by ADR-0053/0116/0085 regardless of the C3 orchestrator (CF-397); R-2.6.4 — the scheduler binds/advises at ratified points and is never a second owner of model choice, ensembles or critic placement; `static` is the packaged default; `predictor` variants are C4 via WS-I5 (CF-403); R-2.9.5 — Stage-6 sub-ladder 6a (human-proposed, `instrument-grade`) → 6b → 6c → 6d, with AT-H1-05 and Stage 3 as preconditions; R-2.9.6 — ADR-0007's note refined: `DebtHomes/1`, the additive record fields, `RemovalTest`/`RemovalVerdict`/`RetirementRecord`, `validate_removal_test` and the health declarations are C0/Stage 1; retirement experiments on the two minimal profiles C0/Stage 3; live triggers and `DebtIndex` C1/Stage 5; only the manager *service* is C4/Stage 6; R-2.9.7 — M1 designed ablation over typed targets and `AttributionReport/1` are C2/Stage 5, M2–M5 C4/Stage 6; Spec §7.2-5 text uses "interventional re-execution with declared estimands" (CF-434); R-2.9.8 — schemas C0/Stage 1, the provider-drift half of the compatibility guard C1/Stage 5, everything else C4/Stage 6; R-2.12.6 — records/reconciler/state map/ownership/escalation C4/Stage 4 over the fixture adapter, adapters/`FleetView`/metrics Stage 5, `lab/org-policy-v1` Stage 6, deferrals D-1…D-6 outside the ladder with entry tests (CF-464).
3. **Maturity:** R-2.9.8 is marked `specified-by-ADR, research-grade, conditional` per ADR-0002 (e) and CF-453; every automated proposer family under R-2.9.5 is `research-grade` until promoted (OQ-440); the flags are data on the registry records and on the spec items (ADR-0182 X-check).
4. **Novelty annotations confirmed:** R-2.6.5 ⊕ NEW (`OwnershipRecord`, labelled peer message, per-object `ConsistencyLevel` are `no-precedent`); R-2.9.6 ⊕ NEW (`novel as integrated`; every piece precedented — Chromium, OpenHands, litellm, Piranha); R-2.9.7 ⊕ NEW (attribution over typed IR components with a declared estimand per effect; partial precedents CAR over steps, S-092 over slots); R-2.6.3 ↑ EXT and R-2.9.5 ↑ EXT unchanged (integrated contracts `novel`, elements precedented); R-2.6.4 ⊕ NEW (a bind-only policy recording every option considered is `novel as integrated`); R-2.9.8 ⊕ NEW (typed lowering + snapshot claim + retirement-proven consolidation as one contract); R-2.12.6 ⊕ NEW (work item with desired/observed halves, owner-as-principal, accountability projection). The Phase 5 `precedent | renamed | novel` obligation (ADR-0004) applies per item.
5. **Scope-change log rows** are appended to `registers/scope.md`; the summary table (60 items; tier counts) is unchanged.

**T-LCD statement.** Placing C0/C1/C2 slices inside C3/C4 items preserves T-LCD-06 (every slice is removable with its tier — ADR-0182 X2/X4 removability builds; AC-I8-11; the hosting-tier pattern of CF-390) and T-LCD-05 (the C0 debt schema is the enforcement point for every conditioned rule regardless of whether the C4 manager is built). **ADR-0024 rung:** register statuses and notes are MUST-data of the program.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: the ratified Phase 4 ADRs themselves (each with (a)–(e) and a T-LCD statement); the CF-285 stage-note pattern applied at Phases 2–3 (ADR-0146, ADR-0184); ADR-0002 (e) on maturity flags.
- (b) Source-code precedent: not applicable to a register change; the per-item novelty confirmations cite the precedents named in ADR-0185…0207 (b).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: re-tiering would make the catalogue honest about where code lands but would let a Stage-4 build claim C1 items it does not need — the ladder already states them; marking the entire evolution cluster `research-grade` (rather than only R-2.9.8 and the automated families) would over-state uncertainty about the human-proposed pipeline, whose gates are ratified operations. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier and task class; R-2.9.8's status is conditional on trainable beneficiaries and matched-total evidence not yet in the literature (ADR-0204 (d)).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: per decision 2; the register itself is a Phase 4 program artifact consumed by Phase 5.

## Consequences

- Phase 5 spec assembly reads the eight items as `specified-by-ADR` with their stage/maturity notes; the readiness report lists R-2.9.8 as specified-but-research-grade with its conditionality and every automated proposer family with its flag.
- Every item of the §2 catalogue is now `specified-by-ADR` or `specified` except R-2.12.4 (WS-L6, Phase 5); no item is `open` for a Phase 4 workstream.

## Reversibility

**High.** Register statuses and notes; reverting is a register edit logged as an ADR.
