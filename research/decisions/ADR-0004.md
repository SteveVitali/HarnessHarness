# ADR-0004 — Amend ADR-0001 evidence: the two-participant-class comparison plane has precedent; novelty is narrowed to the component-level white-box axis, metric class-declarations, and declared/observed conformance

**Status:** ratified (Phase 0 synthesis, 2026-09-09) — amendment to ADR-0001 evidence field (b) and consequences; ADR-0001's decision is unchanged. **Applied to ADR-0001 on 2026-09-09** (see its *Amendment log*).
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-A1 · **Phase:** 0 · **Related:** ADR-0001, CF-003, CF-010, CF-011, OQ-004, OQ-034, R-2.10.6, R-2.10.4

## Context

ADR-0001 (b) records: "The **two-participant-class comparison plane** is `novel` in combination (no precedent found at Preflight; WS-A1 to confirm or refute)." WS-A1 audited the landscape and found four independent systems that already run participants they control alongside participants they do not, on one environment/verifier/cost plane:

- **Harbor** (S-132/S-133; `src/harbor/agents/base.py`, `agents/installed/` 47 adapters, `agents/terminus_2/`, `agents/dspy_rlm.py`, `models/job/config.py`, `models/trial/result.py`, `models/trajectories/` ATIF) — native reference agents and installed/external black-box agents in one job; shared cost fields; interchange trajectory format; Harness-Bench (S-062) runs on this style of plane while "keeping each harness's native execution behavior".
- **HAL** (S-134/S-135, ICLR 2026 — Tier A) — model × scaffold × benchmark, cost-controlled, `agent_function` contract, "without … constraining agent developers to follow a certain agent framework".
- **Inspect AI** (S-136/S-137) — bridged black-box agents and native solvers share one transcript/log and scorer plane via model-boundary interception.
- **Omnigent** (S-118; `omnigent/inner/executor.py`, `harness_capabilities.py`, `tests/harness_bench/`) — 12+ hosted harnesses under one session/policy/cost plane with a declared-vs-observed conformance bench.

None of them, however, has a participant class that is **component-decomposable through a typed IR** with **component-level attribution over an event-sourced ledger**, nor metrics that declare which participant class/observability level they apply to, nor conformance verdicts fed into the analysis as data.

## Options considered

1. **Keep ADR-0001 (b) as `novel` unchanged.** Rejected: contradicted by four precedents, one Tier-A; would fail the §3.3 evidence contract at the coherence review.
2. **Reverse ADR-0001 (drop the hosted class; native-only).** Rejected: the decision itself is sound and precedent *strengthens* it (hosting is cheap and well-understood); ADR-0001 is ratified and its decision is not contradicted by this evidence.
3. **Amend (b) to cite precedent and narrow the novelty flag (chosen).**

## Decision

- ADR-0001 evidence (b) is amended to read: *Precedent exists for a comparison plane spanning controlled and uncontrolled participants (Harbor, HAL, Inspect AI, Omnigent; Harness-Bench methodology). What is `novel` is (i) that the white-box class is component-decomposable via the Harness IR so that component-level ablation, causal attribution and evolution apply to it on the same plane; (ii) that every metric carries a class/observability declaration so cross-class results are never silently conflated; (iii) that a participant's declared capabilities are reconciled against observed conformance (DRIFT) and treated as experimental data.*
- The ontology (WS-A2) adds `comparison granularity ∈ {component, configuration, product}` and `observability level` alongside the two participant classes; IR-native participants admit all granularities, hosted-external only `configuration` and `product`.
- WS-J4 must implement `MetricDeclaration{requires_observability, applies_to_classes}`; results for inapplicable class/metric pairs render `n/a`, never zero.
- WS-J6 must define the thin observational ABI as a *minimum observable event set* satisfiable by each of the three hosting mechanisms (session-ABI, model-boundary interception, container-installed), with richer observability declared per participant — mirroring Omnigent `HarnessCapabilities` and Harbor `AgentCapabilities`.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: S-062 (harness effects are configuration-level; native execution preserved) `provisional (mech)`; S-135 HAL (Tier A) — scaffold as an explicit factor; S-072 `provisional (mech)` — controlled component-level comparison is only possible when the harness is under the experimenter's control (both arms were configurations of one harness), which is exactly the white-box class's justification; S-140 ClawGym II `provisional (mech)` — model-boundary interception yields complete model I/O but no internal state, establishing the observability-level distinction.
- (b) Source-code precedent: the plane — Harbor `models/job/config.py:396 JobConfig{agents[], tasks[], n_attempts}` + `models/trial/result.py:70 TrialResult{cost…}`; HAL `hal/agent_runner.py`; Inspect `agent/_bridge/bridge.py`, `_bridge/sandbox/bridge.py`. Declared-vs-observed conformance — Omnigent `tests/harness_bench/verdict.py:112 reconcile` → `DRIFT`. The component-level white-box axis with attribution — `no-precedent / novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) Pi's `packages/evals/src/vitest-evals/harness-table.ts` compares baseline vs candidate *configurations* of one runtime with repetitions — close to component-level comparison, but within a single non-IR runtime and without attribution; it does not span a black-box class. (ii) Open Agent Spec (S-138) compares the *same spec* across runtimes — that is runtime-as-factor, not component-as-factor, and its entity set omits permissions/effects/validators/provenance. Neither overturns the narrowed claim.
- (d) Conditionality: the narrowed novelty depends on WS-A3 delivering the seven-plane entity set and WS-B1 a ledger that supports counterfactual attribution (OQ-007/OQ-024). If attribution proves infeasible under nondeterminism, the white-box class still offers ablation but the "causal attribution" part of the claim must be softened.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: ontology terms and `MetricDeclaration` — C0, Stage 1; conformance probes and cross-class joins — C2, Stage 3–4 (with WS-J6 hosting).

## Consequences

- ADR-0001's (b) text **was updated** at the Phase 0 synthesis pass (2026-09-09); its decision, (d), (e), consequences and reversibility stand. The unreachable S-055 citation in (b) was replaced by the S-107 source paths actually read (CF-024).
- The spec's laboratory section states plainly that black-box comparison is precedented and cites Harbor/HAL/Inspect; MetaHarness's differentiation is the white-box axis and the joint analysis.
- WS-L7's novelty thesis may not use "compare everyone's harness" as a headline differentiator.

## Reversibility

High for the text; low for the underlying fact (precedent cannot be un-found).
