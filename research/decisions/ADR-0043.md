# ADR-0043 — Cost and latency measurement contract: named measurement points, integer units, monotonic clocks, cost provenance, one token-accounting convention

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10); previously: **ratified, amended** (Phase 1 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I1 (with WS-L2, WS-C1, WS-B1, WS-B2, WS-J6) · **Phase:** 1 · **Related:** ADR-0029 (canonical numbers: no IEEE doubles), WS-B2 §6.4 (Effect `cost{…}`), ADR-0005 (cost attribution per hosting mechanism with provenance + confidence), OQ-033, OQ-041, CF-089, CF-090, CF-091, CF-093, OQ-117, OQ-118, R-2.9.1, T-LCD-14

## Context

"Per-call cost + latency" is a sponsor requirement, and the scorecard's efficiency dimension (tokens, cache hit, tool calls, latency, dollars — doc 2 §8) and every capability-cost Pareto analysis depend on numbers that are comparable across providers, harnesses, hosting mechanisms and ecosystem boundaries. The source audit shows the numbers are *not* comparable today: latency is measured at different points (Codex: e2e/TTFT/TTFM/API attempt/SSE/engine; OpenHands: one wall latency per retry attempt), in different units (OTel seconds vs harness milliseconds); cost is a float USD from four different provenances (OpenHands gateway header → pricing library → custom rates; goose `ProviderReported | Estimated`; HAL static table; Harbor optional `cost_usd`) with only goose and OpenHands recording *which*; and token accounting contradicts (OTel/Harbor: `input_tokens` includes cached; Inspect: excludes; OpenHands guesses at runtime). WS-B1 forbids IEEE doubles in hashed payloads (OQ-041), so float USD cannot enter the ledger.

## Options considered

1. **Adopt OTel GenAI units and attributes as the native measurement model** (seconds as float, `gen_ai.usage.*`, no cost). *Rejected:* floats violate the canonical form; no cost attribute; single-point latency; Development stability.
2. **Record whatever each provider/participant reports, with a free-form `source` string.** *Rejected:* incomparable across arms; "cache hit rate" and "cost" become silently wrong by the cached fraction; the matched-budget rule (T-LCD-14) is unenforceable.
3. **Named measurement points, integer units, monotonic-clock durations with `measured_at`, `Money{micro_units,currency}` with a closed provenance enum + confidence + pricing snapshot ref, one declared token convention with a stamped normalizer (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Measurement points M1–M18** (dossier §6.3) are the closed list of places where cost or latency is measured; each names its scope kind, opening/terminal events, measured quantities, units and producer. Adding a point is a spec change, not a producer's choice.
2. **Durations** are integers in milliseconds (`*_ms`; `*_us` permitted where measurable), taken from the *measuring component's monotonic clock*, stamped `measured_at ∈ {adapter, gateway, interceptor, executor, runtime, participant}`. Wall-clock `ts` orders nothing (ordering is `seq`) and is used for durations only as a cross-check (`clock_skew_flag` beyond `clock_tolerance_ms`).
3. **Latency is multi-point per logical call**: a logical `model_call` span carries `latency_ms` = request-sent → last-byte of the successful attempt and `attempts`; each attempt is a child `model_attempt` span with `Timing{request_sent_ms, first_byte_ms?, first_token_ms?, last_byte_ms}`; turns carry `ttft_ms`, `ttfm_ms`, `turn_e2e_ms` and a `turn_phase_profile` partition `{before_first_sampling, sampling, compaction, tool_blocking, permission_wait, between_sampling_overhead, idle_after_sampling}`; tool calls separate `tool_ms` (proposal→completion, includes permission wait) from `executor_ms` and per-attempt `isolation_mode`.
4. **Money** is `Money{micro_units: integer, currency: ISO-4217}`; never a float; never summed across currencies (`MixedCurrency` failure). Every `Money` carries `provenance ∈ {provider_reported, gateway_reported, participant_reported, estimated_from_pricing, reconstructed_from_native_log, unknown}`, `confidence ∈ {exact, bounded{lo, hi}, estimate, unknown}` (CF-105; `high` → `bounded`), and, for estimated values, `pricing_ref: ContentAddress` of the pricing snapshot pinned in the bundle. `cost_view` reports the provenance mix and minimum confidence; it never averages provenance away. Mapping to WS-B1's `{measured, reported, reconstructed}`: `measured ⊇ {provider_reported, gateway_reported}`, `reported = participant_reported`, `reconstructed ⊇ {estimated_from_pricing, reconstructed_from_native_log}`.
5. **Tokens** are `TokenVector{input_total, input_uncached, cache_read, cache_write, output_total, output_reasoning, output_text?}` (integers) under the **inclusive convention** `input_total = input_uncached + cache_read + cache_write`, `output_total ⊇ output_reasoning`, tagged `convention = "hh-inclusive/1"`. The model adapter (WS-C1) normalizes provider shapes *once* and stamps `normalizer_ref`; each per-provider normalization rule is a conditioned rule with an assumption-debt record (T-LCD-05).
6. **Attribution.** A child run's `ResourceVector` is counted exactly once under the parent's `subagent` scope; compaction, verification, routing and retry costs are attributed `harness_overhead.{…}`; search-time and evaluation-time consumption are attributed to the arm's `search_budget` / `eval_budget` (T-LCD-14). WS-L2 owns the attribution *policy* applied by `cost_view` and the resource taxonomy; this ADR fixes the measurement points and the stamps.
7. **Export/interchange conversion** (ms → s, micro-units → decimal, inclusive → exclusive tokens) happens only in lowering and is listed in the telemetry loss report (ADR-0042).

**T-LCD statement:** satisfies T-LCD-14 (budgets measurable per arm with separated search/eval attribution), T-LCD-15 (every measured quantity declares the observability level at which it exists; hosted rows carry `participant_reported`/`reconstructed` provenance rather than zeros), T-LCD-05 (normalization rules carry assumption-debt records). Does not address T-LCD-07 (probing whether a hosted participant's reported usage is trustworthy — WS-J6 conformance probes; mitigated by `confidence`).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: dossier F4 (multi-point latency: Codex `core/src/turn_timing.rs` `TurnProfileState`, `otel/src/metrics/names.rs` TTFT/TTFM/e2e/attempt/SSE; OpenHands per-attempt span `telemetry.py: on_request/on_response`; Codex `sandbox_outcome{initial, escalated}`), F5 (cost provenance: goose `CostSource`, OpenHands `_compute_cost` precedence, HAL static table, Harbor optional `cost_usd`, Codex micro-USD), F6 (token-convention contradiction: S-228 note [23], Harbor `metrics.py`, Inspect `ModelUsage`, OpenHands `cache_hit_rate` heuristic), F8 (tree rollup and compaction attribution: goose `get_session_usage_totals`, `usage_ledger.is_compaction`; Codex `TokenUsageRecord{root_turn_id}`). WS-B1 canonical-number rule (F-B1 §6.3). HAL (S-135, Tier A) demonstrates cost-controlled comparison requires a single pricing basis.
- (b) Source-code precedent: Codex `codex-rs/otel/src/events/session_telemetry.rs: record_turn_cost` (decimal → integer micro-USD with rounding), `core/src/turn_timing.rs` (phase profile); goose `crates/goose-provider-types/src/conversation/token_usage.rs: CostSource`, `crates/goose/src/session/session_manager.rs` (`usage_ledger`, recursive totals); OpenHands `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/llm/utils/telemetry.py: _cache_buckets` ("single source of truth for both metrics and the span"), `_compute_cost`; OTel GenAI `model/gen-ai/registry.yaml` (`gen_ai.usage.*` inclusive rule, `time_to_first_chunk`). The closed measurement-point list, `measured_at`, `pricing_ref`, and the stamped normalizer are `no-precedent / novel` as a contract.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: every interchange and pricing precedent uses float USD and OTel histograms use seconds — conversion is a lossy extra step; provider usage shapes change faster than any normalization table (assumption debt). Not overturning: floats are excluded by WS-B1's canonical form regardless; Codex already performs the exact conversion; the normalization table is the *only* alternative to a runtime guess and is made expirable/testable by T-LCD-05; conversions are centralized in lowering and tested (AC-I1-5/-6).
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier; assumes providers report usage per call (if not, `provenance = estimated_from_pricing` with `confidence = estimated`); for hosted participants assumes the Hosting ABI supplies `usage_update`-class reports or a native log to reconstruct from (else `unknown`, never 0); micro-unit precision assumes pricing granularity ≥ 10⁻⁶ per currency unit (true of every table audited).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0 · Stage 0 (M1/M3/M7 with `Money`, `TokenVector`, `latency_ms`) → Stage 1 (M1–M12, attempt spans, `measured_at`, normalizer stamping, phase profile) → Stage 2 (M5/M6/M13/M14) → Stage 3 (budget attribution per arm; AC-I1-3/-4/-5/-9/-13) → C1 Stage 4 (M12 rollup, M18 hosted provenance, interchange conversion declared lossy).

## Consequences

- WS-C1's adapter contract gains `emit_call_timing`, `emit_usage(normalizer_ref)`, `emit_cost(provenance, pricing_ref, confidence)` obligations; WS-C4 consumes the token buckets.
- WS-B1 extends `model.call.completed` with `timing` and attempt classes (CF-089) and adopts the six-valued provenance on `measurement.cost.attributed` (CF-090); WS-B2 aligns the Effect record's `cost{…}` to `ResourceVector` (CF-093).
- WS-I3 pins pricing snapshots and normalization-rule versions in the bundle; WS-L2 writes the attribution policy `cost_view` applies (OQ-033); WS-J6/I3 declare the float conversion lossy in interchange.
- Scorecards show provenance mix and confidence beside every cost figure.

## Reversibility

High for units (a pure conversion) and for the provenance vocabulary (extensible enum); medium for the inclusive token convention once bundles and metric histories exist (a convention flip invalidates cache-hit-rate comparisons across bundle versions unless re-normalized — possible because raw provider usage is stored by reference).
