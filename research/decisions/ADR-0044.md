# ADR-0044 — Instrumentation levels, telemetry sink classification, and the process-metric catalogue as ledger-computed declarations

**Status:** **ratified, amended** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10); previously: **ratified, amended** (Phase 1 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-I1 (with WS-H6, WS-I2, WS-J4, WS-L6) · **Phase:** 1 · **Related:** ADR-0042, ADR-0026 (persistence policy; `ephemeral`), ADR-0029 (payload offload; redaction tombstones), ADR-0004 (`MetricDeclaration`), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-02, -09, -13, -14, -15), OQ-036, OQ-037, OQ-046, OQ-116, OQ-120, R-2.9.1, R-2.8.6, R-2.9.2

## Context

"Deep audit at all levels" conflicts with the industry default of *not* capturing model content in telemetry (OTel GenAI default; goose/Codex opt-in gates; OpenHands' analytics contract that admits no bare string) and with privacy, storage and access-control concerns. At the same time the instrument's purpose — component attribution, opacity measurement (T-LCD-02), compliance measurement (T-LCD-13), replay for declared variants (WS-B1 AC-B1-4) — is impossible without full model I/O for native participants. The audited systems resolve the tension not by sampling but by *sink classification*: Codex routes each telemetry event to a `log_only` target (may carry arguments, output, account identity) and a `trace_safe` target (lengths, counts, booleans); goose records tool results only on success and only under an opt-in; OpenHands buckets magnitudes, pseudonymizes identifiers, fingerprints errors and resolves consent with a kill switch. Separately, the scorecard needs a process-metric catalogue (doc 2 §8; Cursor's four online metrics and error taxonomy; Harness-Bench's completion/process/efficiency/failure families) whose numbers must be class-scoped (`n/a`, never 0 — T-LCD-15) and reported as distributions per configuration (T-LCD-09).

## Options considered

1. **Follow the OTel default: no content anywhere unless opted in; metrics from exported spans.** *Rejected:* defeats attribution, opacity, compliance and replay; metrics from a sampled export are invalid for heavy-tailed runs.
2. **Everything everywhere: full content in the ledger and in every sink.** *Rejected:* leaks secrets/personal data to analytics and third-party trace backends; no access-control separation; contradicts every audited sensitivity practice.
3. **Four instrumentation levels with content classes; L0–L2 always-on in the ledger (content by reference) for native participants; sinks declare classes, redaction, sampling; metrics are declared computations over the ledger only (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Instrumentation levels / content classes:** `L0 accounting` (counts, `TokenVector`, `Money`, durations, bucketed magnitudes, error classes, ids — never free text), `L1 structural` (every `durability = ledger` event: scopes, decisions, effects, permissions, verdicts, artefact exposures, budgets, lineage), `L2 content` (model requests/responses, tool arguments/observations, context items, judged evidence — stored only in the content-addressed blob store above WS-B1's offload threshold and referenced by `ContentAddress`), `L3 diagnostic` (streaming deltas, permission *requests*, previews, process stats — `ephemeral`).
2. **Always-on rule:** L0–L2 are recorded for every native-participant run (the ledger is never sampled — ADR-0042); L2 for hosted participants is recorded only as their capability declaration and consent permit (OQ-116); L3 is sampled, rate-limited or off. Redaction is WS-B1's tombstone; retention is experiment-scoped (WS-B1 §6.5).
3. **Sink policy:** every export sink is declared as `SinkPolicy{sink_id, content_classes ⊆ {accounting, structural, content, diagnostic}, redaction ∈ {none, allowlist, pseudonymize}, sampling{mode ∈ {all, ratio, parent_based}, ratio?}, rate_limit?, max_field_bytes, requires_consent}`. A sink without `content` never receives dereferenced blob bytes — only addresses, lengths and digests; an `accounting`-only sink accepts constrained scalars and buckets (OpenHands contract shape). Every delivery is recorded as `measurement.export.delivered{sink_id, view_kind, seq_range, content_classes, loss_report_ref}` so `audit_view` can prove what left the system.
4. **Audit completeness** is a computable per-run metric: `chain_ok ∧ open_scopes_past_deadline = ∅ ∧ content_refs_missing_without_tombstone = ∅`; WS-H6 attests `audit_view`; this ADR fixes what audit must be able to see.
5. **Process-metric catalogue:** the metrics in dossier §6.4 (efficiency, reliability, grounding incl. compliance, autonomy, security, evolvability, budget discipline) enter WS-I2's registry as `MetricDeclaration{name, requires_observability, applies_to_classes, computed_from: event classes, unit}`; a metric is a *computation over ledger events* with `computed_from: [EventRef]` on every value; no metric is computed from an exported sample; inapplicable metric × class/observability pairs render `n/a`. Thresholds and targets are out of scope (model-conditional; doc 2 §8).
6. **Compliance metrics (T-LCD-13, co-owned with WS-B1):** `artifact_activation_rate`, `artifact_follow_rate`, `delivered_but_never_activated` are computed from `ArtifactExposure` records built from `context.artefact.delivered/activated` + `verification.artefact.followed`, stratified by `detector ∈ {deterministic, judged}`, per Model Profile; validity is `verification.validator.verdict` and is never inferred from compliance.

**T-LCD statement:** satisfies T-LCD-13 (compliance computable from delivered/activated/followed with artefact ids), T-LCD-15 (declarations with `requires_observability`/`applies_to_classes`; `n/a` semantics), T-LCD-09 (stratification keys `configuration_id`, `model_profile_ref`, `environment_ref`, `budget`, `seed` are carried on every value), T-LCD-14 (budget metrics per arm), T-LCD-02 (`opacity_ratio` recorded at M5 once OQ-036 fixes its definition). Does not decide gate-vs-checklist (OQ-037 — Phase 1 synthesis with WS-I2).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: dossier F3 (sink classification in Codex `otel/src/targets.rs` + `events/shared.rs`; goose `gen_ai_telemetry.rs` opt-in and success-only results; OpenHands `agent_server/telemetry/{models,policy,sanitizer}.py`; OTel GenAI three content modes with the upload hook independent of sampling — S-228), F9/F10 (no compliance instrumentation anywhere; no self-sampling of session records), F1 (metrics derived from the same events as the loop). Cursor (S-049) supplies the operationally load-bearing metric set (latency, token efficiency, tool-call count, cache-hit rate) and the tool-error taxonomy with per-tool × per-model baselines; Harness-Bench (S-062, `provisional (mech)`) supplies the completion/process/efficiency/failure families; S-088 (`provisional (mech)`) supplies validity-vs-compliance. Managed Agents (S-047) shows dead-weight guardrails are found observationally today — the gap compliance metrics close.
- (b) Source-code precedent: Codex `codex-rs/otel/src/targets.rs` (`OTEL_LOG_ONLY_TARGET` / `OTEL_TRACE_SAFE_TARGET`), `otel/src/tool_result.rs` (`telemetry_preview{max_bytes, truncated}`), `otel/src/metrics/names.rs` (declared metric registry); OpenHands `openhands-agent-server/openhands/agent_server/telemetry/models.py` (`SafeToken`, `SafeIdentifier`, `Bucket`, `Digest`, "no bare str"), `sanitizer.py` (`bucket`, `pseudonymize`, `normalize_exception`), `policy.py` (consent precedence, `DO_NOT_TRACK`); goose `crates/goose/src/agents/gen_ai_telemetry.rs` (`capture_message_content`, `record_tool_result` success-only); OTel GenAI `docs/gen-ai/gen-ai-spans.md` §Capturing instructions, inputs, and outputs. `measurement.export.delivered`, `audit_completeness` and the compliance metrics are `no-precedent / novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: OTel default-off content capture and regulatory/storage arguments; OpenHands' stance that any string field is a leak; doc 2 §8 warning that harness metrics go stale as models change; S-083 showing apparent gains reverse under matched budgets. Not overturning: content lives only in the blob store behind addresses, sinks default to `accounting`+`structural`, redaction/retention are structural; the catalogue fixes computations, not thresholds, and every value is stratified and budget-attributed. Residual risk — the blob store becomes a sensitive asset — is carried to WS-H6/H3 (access control on blobs) and WS-L6 (consent policy).
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier and task class. Assumes the deployment context is a research instrument where operators consent to L2 capture for native runs; production embeddings (WS-K4) may run with L2 disabled, at the declared cost of `n/a` on every `model_io`/`ledger`-requiring metric (T-LCD-15 makes this visible rather than silent).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: C0 · Stage 1 (levels, `SinkPolicy`, `measurement.export.delivered`, registry populated with names/requirements) → Stage 2 (`audit_view`, H6 attestation) → Stage 3 (catalogue computed as distributions; compliance metrics over the reference harness; AC-I1-8/-10/-11/-12). Gate-vs-checklist per OQ-037.

## Consequences

- WS-I2 imports the catalogue rows as its initial `MetricDeclaration` set and owns statistics, factors and `n/a` rendering; WS-J4 shows provenance/confidence and observability level beside every number.
- WS-H6 attests `audit_view`; WS-H3/H4 apply access control and egress policy to the blob store and to `content`-class sinks.
- WS-L6 owns the consent/kill-switch policy surface (OpenHands `DO_NOT_TRACK`-style) for any sink with `requires_consent`.
- WS-K2's viewer reads `content` only through the blob store under the same access control.

## Reversibility

High for sink policy (declarative); medium for the always-on L2 rule (turning it off is one manifest flag, but Stage 3 acceptance and T-LCD-02/-13 tests then render `n/a`); high for the catalogue (rows are declarations; adding/removing a metric does not touch the ledger).
