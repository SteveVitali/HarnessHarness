# ADR-0166 — Version/extension negotiation and conformance for the Hosting ABI: the `hh-hosting/1` handshake, `ext`-first extension with dialect promotion, `AdapterRecord` as a conditioned artefact, the probe catalogue P-01…P-16, `ConformanceRecord`/DRIFT rules, three abstract adapters plus adapter zero, and acceptance criteria AC-J6-1…10 (resolves OQ-034)

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 3 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-J6 (consumers: WS-J2 registry, WS-J3, WS-J4, WS-K4, WS-I3, WS-I4, WS-I6, WS-E4, WS-C1) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0005 (mechanism ordering; act as a participant), ADR-0013 (capability vector; DRIFT symmetric), ADR-0015 (`ext` registered, never decides), ADR-0018 (tri-state record; `ext` or dialect bump), ADR-0020/0124…0126 (profile debt/expiry pattern), ADR-0024, ADR-0037 (stale annotated never hidden), ADR-0088 (registry `quarantined` status), ADR-0098 (D1 v1 profile, D5 `_hh/*`, D6 baseline), ADR-0099 (`ProtocolBinding` probe-first), ADR-0141 (hosted bundles), ADR-0142 (Harbor/Inspect environments); ADR-0007 (T-LCD-05/-06/-07/-11); CF-027, CF-087, CF-351; OQ-032, OQ-034, OQ-242, OQ-379/-03/-04; R-2.10.6

## Context

doc 2 §5.11 requires "extension/version negotiation and typed behavioral capabilities, not just a chat endpoint". The audit finds drift is the normal condition: every ACP host speaks v1 against a v2 spec; Codex namespaces `v1`/`v2` and filters experimental APIs at export; OpenHands keeps a CI drift check; Omnigent's bench seam reports that only 4 of 23 declarations are "verified live" and that a version bump must re-trigger probes; Harbor refuses a bridge kind the adapter class did not declare. T-LCD-05 applies reflexively ("the Hosting ABI carr[ies] an expiry and a removal test") and T-LCD-07 forbids coercing probe results. ADR-0018 fixed the kernel-owned tri-state record and said WS-J6 "may add fields only through `ext` or an HIR dialect bump"; ADR-0099 fixed probe-first pinning for protocol edges. OQ-034 asks whether DRIFT excludes or annotates.

## Options considered

1. **Trust declared capabilities; no probes** (Harbor/HAL practice). *Rejected:* Omnigent's own data shows 19/23 declarations unverified; the analysis would stratify on fiction.
2. **Probe everything, declare nothing** (pure runtime discovery). *Rejected:* probes are per fixture and environment (`skipped` is common); without a declaration DRIFT is undefined and the engine could never pre-plan admissible factors.
3. **Hard-code adapters per product inside the Lab with version `if`s.** *Rejected:* the T-LCD-01 failure shape at the instrument level; Omnigent's history ("scattered across `if harness == 'x'` branches") is the counter-example its capability model replaced.
4. **Chosen:** append-only declarations pinned by `version_identity`; an ABI major/minor handshake; `ext`-first extension; adapters as registered, version-selected, debt-carrying artefacts; a dimension-keyed probe catalogue with Omnigent's verdict lattice; DRIFT excludes on P0 dimensions and annotates on others; adapter zero (`proj_ABI`) as the validation fixture.

## Decision

1. **Handshake.** `describe` returns `abi_versions[]`; the Lab attaches at the highest mutually supported major of `hh-hosting/n`; a participant advertising none attaches at `hh-hosting/1` with every declaration `unknown` (silence is never refusal); unknown majors → `AbiVersionUnsupported`; unknown minors attach with unknown fields preserved. Protocol negotiation (`ProtocolBinding`, ADR-0099) is orthogonal.
2. **Extension.** New capability fields live in the registered `ext` block `hh.hosting/n` on `CapabilityDeclarationRecord` (this ADR registers: `credential_supply`, `receipts`, `account_exact`, `elicitation`, `model_io_intercept`, `process_placement`, `tool_supply_channel`, `context_supply_mode`, `coordinates`, `budget_enforcement`, `abi_versions`, `event_channels`, `usage_mapping`); `ext` never decides authority, budget or validity (ADR-0015); promotion into the kernel record is a HIR dialect bump. Vendor-specific methods are reachable only from an adapter that declares them as a capability with a debt record; the ABI has no raw pass-through verb. `OpaqueProcess.hosting_mechanism` never carries `none` (CF-351).
3. **`AdapterRecord{adapter_id, version_id, hosting_mechanism, participant_selector (a claim), declaration_defaults, placement_supported, lowering_table_ref, loss_report_ref, debt: AssumptionDebtRecord}`** is a registry record (WS-J2) under the ADR-0036/0037 identity model. The debt record's hypothesis is "participant versions matching `participant_selector` behave per `declaration_defaults`", its evidence is the set of `ConformanceRecord`s, its expiry condition is "selector matches no registered participant version, or DRIFT on a P0 dimension", and its removal test is the "retire adapter" recipe. `link` refuses an adapter without a complete record (T-LCD-05 reflexive). An adapter refuses to attach a mechanism or placement the declaration does not claim.
4. **Probe catalogue** (Lab-side, fixture sessions, `charged_to = instrument`, each with `applies_to_mechanisms`): P-01 `basic_turn` (always included), P-02 `streaming`, P-03 `interrupt`, P-04 `tool_calling_supplied` (mediated), P-05 `tool_calling_own` (observed), P-06 `policy_deny`, P-07 `policy_ask`, P-08 `context_delivery`, P-09 `coordinate_model`, P-10 `coordinate_other` (per declared coordinate), P-11 `usage_reporting` (+ agreement when intercepted), P-12 `resume_cold`, P-13 `resume_warm`, P-14 `steer`, P-15 `credential_channel` (declared channel bound; undeclared canary refused at EP3), P-16 `end_state`. **P0** = {P-01, P-03, P-04, P-06, P-09, P-16}. Verdicts `{supported, unsupported, partial, not_applicable, unknown, skipped}`; `partial` is probe-only, never declared; `skipped` when the environment cannot exercise the probe.
5. **Conformance and DRIFT (OQ-034).** `ConformanceRecord{participant_version_identity, adapter_version_id, dimension, declared, observed, verdict, observed_in ∈ {probe, run}, evidence_ref, at}` is immutable; `verdict = drift` iff declared and observed are both concrete and differ (symmetric); `unknown`/`skipped` never establish DRIFT. `capability_vector = reconcile(declaration, latest records)`; records older than the current `version_identity` are `stale` (annotated, never hidden). DRIFT on a P0 dimension sets the participant version `quarantined` for experiments until a new declaration is registered (**excludes**); DRIFT elsewhere is a stratum on every results row (**annotates**). A run-time contradiction of a SUPPORTED entry appends a record with `observed_in = run`.
6. **Adapters (abstract).** A — session-ABI adapter for protocol-speaking coding CLIs (v2 baseline; v1 through ADR-0098's compatibility profile; `in_environment`; tools via the sealed MCP surface; optional interception); B — model-boundary interception adapter for hook-bearing CLIs without a session protocol (proxy inside the environment; hooks as `observed` channel; transcript reconstruction fallback; `model` coordinate by proxy override); C — container-installed adapter reconstructing from native logs (install/run/kill/snapshot; per-format lifters with the last-compaction-boundary rule; `end_state` only). **Adapter zero** — the reference runtime attached through its own ACP artefact (ADR-0005 "act as a participant", ADR-0098): the fixture on which probes and lifting are validated; it is `hosted` for a Lab that does not own the definition and never yields `ledger`. Implementation order A → B → C (ADR-0005).
7. **Acceptance criteria** AC-J6-1…10 as written in WS-J6 §6.7 are adopted; AC-J6-1 (a hosted participant joins a mixed-class `Design` and produces a class-labelled scorecard) is the readiness line for R-2.10.6; AC-J6-2 (adapter-zero self-consistency) is the executable form of ADR-0013 decision 5.

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-05 (reflexive expiry on ABI version, adapters and `_hh/*`; removal tests), T-LCD-07 (declare/verify/stratify; `skipped ≠ unknown`; `unknown` never coerced; features never removed), T-LCD-11 (adapter loss reports in the bundle; adapter zero round-trips events), T-LCD-06 (extension via `ext`; dependency direction ABI → IR), T-LCD-12 (adapters are out-of-process artefacts), T-LCD-15 (quarantine and strata, never zeros). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-02/-03/-04/-08/-09/-10/-13/-14. ADR-0024 rung: handshake, reconcile and probe drivers are MUST-code; `AdapterRecord` tables, the probe catalogue and P0 set are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: declared-vs-probed layers and symmetric DRIFT — S-118 `designs/harness-capabilities-bench-seam.md`, `tests/harness_bench/verdict.py`, `docs/harness-bench-design.md` ("earns each cell by running a live turn"; "DRIFT produces a non-zero exit status"; confidence caveat 4/23 verified); frozen declarations and undeclared-bridge refusal — S-132 `agents/capabilities.py`, `bridges/base.py` L25–33; negotiation with omission-as-unknown — S-035 `initialization.mdx` (via ADR-0013), S-152 LSP; version drift is the norm — ADR-0098 context (all hosts v1), S-107 `export.rs` (`v1`/`v2`, experimental filter), S-110 `check-acp-drift.py`; versions are claims — ADR-0037/CF-087; profile expiry pattern — ADR-0126; registry quarantine — ADR-0088; LCD antidote — ADR-0007.
- (b) Source-code precedent: omnigent `omnigent/harness_capabilities.py`, `tests/harness_bench/{verdict.py, manifest.py, transport.py}`, `tests/harness_bench/probes/*.py` (14 modules), `designs/harness-plugin-interface.md` (namespace rule; no overriding builtin names) @6a069da; harbor `src/harbor/agents/{capabilities.py, base.py}`, `bridges/base.py` @7d5285b; codex `codex-rs/app-server-protocol/src/export.rs` @0735c51; agent-client-protocol `schema/v2/meta.json` (`version: 2`) @9b00c27. The version-pinned `ConformanceRecord`, the adapter-as-debt-carrying-artefact and adapter zero are `no-precedent / novel`.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: (i) probes pass on fixtures and fail on tasks — mitigated by run-time `ConformanceRecord{observed_in: run}` and by P0 quarantine; (ii) quarantine on P0 DRIFT may exclude a widely-used product over a transient bug — accepted: re-declaration is cheap (a registry entry), and admitting a participant whose `interrupt` claim is false would corrupt every budget-enforced arm; (iii) `ext`-first extension risks a second capability vocabulary drifting from the kernel record — mitigated by registration of `hh.hosting/n` and by promotion through dialect bumps only; (iv) Omnigent is one team's design — corroborated by Harbor's independent frozen-capability model and by LSP/ACP negotiation; the verdict lattice is adopted for its *distinctions*, not its exact glyphs. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier; assumes WS-J2's registry accepts `AdapterRecord`/`ConformanceRecord` kinds; assumes the Stage-3 suite provides fixtures for P-01…P-16 (coding/terminal first); v1 ACP hosts depend on ADR-0098's compatibility profile until OQ-242's removal test passes.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 3** — `ConformanceRecord`/`AdapterRecord` schemas and adapter zero (AC-J6-2, run against the native reference harness); **C2 / Stage 4** — handshake, Adapter A, probe catalogue, quarantine rule, AC-J6-1/-3/-4/-8/-10; **C2 / Stage 4–5** — Adapters B and C, AC-J6-6/-7/-9; **C2 later** — A2A remote peers.

## Consequences

- WS-J2 hosts `AdapterRecord`, `ConformanceRecord` and participant version records with `quarantined` status; WS-J4 reads `capability_vector` strata and `drift_observed`; WS-J3 refuses factors outside the SUPPORTED coordinate set.
- WS-K4 implements Adapter A's transport and the `_hh/participant/describe` inbound path; WS-I4 may register Harbor-installed agents as Adapter-C participants once its environment providers land (ADR-0142); WS-I6 inherits adapter debt records into the assumption-debt manager.
- WS-I3 stores adapter loss reports and referenced `ConformanceRecord`s in hosted bundles (ADR-0141 D6).
- The readiness report lists AC-J6-1 under R-2.10.6 (Should) and AC-J6-2 under the T-LCD-11 executable row.

## Reversibility

**High** for the probe catalogue and P0 set (registry data); **high** for individual adapters (retire by removal test); **medium** for the `ext hh.hosting/1` field set (promotion or removal is a dialect bump with a migration of stored declarations); **medium** for the quarantine rule (relaxing it re-admits participants whose stored rows would need re-stratification).

P0 DRIFT sets the `participant` record's admission to `quarantined` for experiments.
