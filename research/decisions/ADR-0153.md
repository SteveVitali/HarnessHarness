# ADR-0153 — Third-party variants, namespaces and foreign registries: variants as `component_variant` extensions, namespace ownership and publish authority (OQ-110 partial), and foreign registries as quarantined lifts

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 3 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-J2 (with WS-H5, WS-L5; consumers WS-J6, WS-K1, WS-I3, WS-L6) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0151/-2, ADR-0063/0064/0065 (extension trust, lifecycle, model-install), ADR-0033/0034/0035 (authority; never widen; endorsement bases), ADR-0036/0037 (name histories, revocation), ADR-0024 (`authority_cap` layering), ADR-0021/0141 (lifting/lowering with loss reports), ADR-0018 (hosted participants), ADR-0002 (iii), ADR-0050 §8 and D3 (variant-host boundary), CF-141 (no in-process third-party code), CF-330; OQ-110, OQ-162, OQ-212, OQ-352/-06; R-2.10.2, R-2.12.2, R-2.8.5

## Context

ADR-0063 already lists `component_variant` as an extension kind and ADR-0023 decision 5 makes out-of-process binding mandatory for third-party variants, but neither says what a third-party `VariantRecord` is in the registry, who may publish, deprecate or revoke under a name (OQ-110, assigned to WS-J2), or how entries from the registries that now exist — the MCP registry with verified reverse-DNS namespaces and a closed set of package registries (S-502), the ACP agent registry with CI-probed manifests but digest-less `latest` archive URLs (S-503), plugin marketplaces with optional pins and daily refresh (S-313) — enter the instrument. The audit's failure evidence is uniform: registries with weak ownership host squatted names (S-303), every plugin system auto-updates or resolves `@latest` (goose, opencode, pi ranges, Claude marketplaces), every in-process plugin loader inherits ambient authority (CF-141), and description text alone moves selection (S-301, `provisional`). Omnigent's contribution validation (reserved prefix; override refusal) and pi's package identity are the constructive precedents.

## Options considered

1. **Third-party variants as first-class `VariantRecord`s with no extension view** (trust via a boolean `trusted`). *Rejected:* a single-approval persistent trust model is the structural cause the skills literature names (S-300); ADR-0063's three legs exist to avoid it.
2. **A separate "marketplace" registry for third-party variants.** *Rejected:* two stores, two snapshot rules (ADR-0151 option 1); the sealed definition must pin one closure.
3. **Namespaces owned by whoever publishes first** (npm-style, no verification). *Rejected:* squatting (S-303); the MCP registry moved to platform/DNS-verified namespaces for this reason.
4. **Foreign registries resolved live at run time** (Harbor's `acp:<id>@<version>` walk, S-132). *Rejected for run time:* the closed-world rule (ADR-0019) and pinned sealed forms (ADR-0036 N5); admissible only as an `import` at assembly time with a recorded resolution.
5. **Third-party variants as `ExtensionRecord{kind: component_variant}` views of the same record (out-of-process, three-leg trust, declarations as claims); `NamespaceRecord` with reserved namespaces and layered publish authority; foreign registries imported as quarantined lifts with loss reports (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **A third-party variant is an extension.** A `VariantRecord` whose `registrar.origin ∉ {kernel, definition}` is simultaneously an `ExtensionRecord{kind: component_variant}` (ADR-0063): the envelope's `trust_record_ref` is mandatory; `content` is the tree address of the package; `code_identity` lists every executable body; `implementation.locality ∈ {out-of-process, remote}` (`in-process` ⇒ `LocalityInadmissible`; CF-141); `isolation ≥ subprocess_confined`; `grants` are conferred only by a `Permission` at `seal`; the variant's own `capability_declaration` is a *claim* (`declared_claims`) that enters no conformance vector until a `registry_ci`/`lab` report exists (ADR-0152) and reaches no monitor decision (ADR-0063 L3); `summary` and any `Text` in `param_schema` receive the ADR-0063 §6.2 default text authority (`external` for hash-pinned unsigned packages; `definition` only through a `pin` endorsement under `TrustRootPolicy`). The WS-L5 variant host's `hello` carries `contract_version`, the variant `version_id` and the `registry_snapshot_id` so `lifecycle.component.bound` can be checked against the sealed definition. First-party kernel-ecosystem variants are the only `in-process` implementations (ADR-0050 §8 clause (a) is the only permitted reference).
2. **`NamespaceRecord{namespace, owners: [SignerRef | PrincipalRef], policy{who_may_publish, who_may_deprecate, who_may_yank, who_may_revoke, require_signature: bool}, provenance}`** is a registry record layered per ADR-0024 (`authority_cap`: organisation > user > project may only narrow). Reserved namespaces: `hh/` (kernel-owned; the instrument's release process is the only publisher; `ClassRecord`s live here), `local/` (the run's principal; never exported without re-namespacing), `exp/<experiment_id>/` (ablation and evolution lineages, ADR-0037 §6.4 rule b; owner = the experiment's principal; the evolution service publishes only here at `origin = model` — ADR-0002 (iii)). Shared namespaces (C1/Stage 4) are reverse-DNS, owned by an accepted signer (H5 `TrustRootPolicy.accepted_signers`) or by the registry's administering principal; `publish` by a non-owner is `NamespaceForbidden`; `revoke` of another publisher's version is admissible only to the namespace owner or the registry administrator at `authority ≥ principal`, is always a `RevocationRecord` (never deletion), and propagates per ADR-0064/ADR-0037 S4. Community packages may not publish into `hh/` or override an existing `(namespace, name)` (Omnigent rule). This is the answer to OQ-110 for one registry; cross-registry and memory-registry (OQ-212) revocation authority remain OQ-352.
3. **Freshness and rollback (the TUF shape without a TUF repository; ADR-0063 §6.3):** the `registry_snapshot_id` plays the snapshot role (one consistent closure per sealed definition — mix-and-match impossible); `NameHistoryEntry.published_at` with `RegistryPolicy.max_age` plays the timestamp role (stale pins refused at `resolve`); a new head whose `version_label` regresses is `LabelRegression` at `review`; auto-update never touches a sealed definition — an update is a new record plus supersession (ADR-0064). Every audited plugin system's `@latest`/auto-update default is a rejected precedent (CF-330).
4. **Foreign registries are lifting sources, never live resolvers.** `import(ForeignRegistryRef{system ∈ {mcp_registry, acp_registry, marketplace, git, archive}, locator}, policy) → [RegistryEnvelope]` runs only at assembly time through ADR-0064 `discover`/`resolve` over a *declared source*; each result is a `foreign_import` record (`admission = quarantined` by default; `resolved` only by attestation under `TrustRootPolicy`) carrying `ForeignRef{system, digest?, label}` (ADR-0141), the lifted kind-specific record (an MCP `server.json` → `ExtensionRecord{kind: mcp_server}` with `packages[]`/`remotes[]` as `SourceLocator`s and `fileSha256` as a `ForeignRef` digest where present; an ACP `agent.json` → a hosted `ParticipantDescriptor` candidate for WS-J6 — **never** a `VariantRecord`, T-LCD-06), text legs `unverified` (ADR-0034 P7), and a `LossReport` naming what the foreign manifest cannot express (capabilities and conformance for both; digests for ACP archives and for all but one MCP package kind; trust legs everywhere). Foreign `status` labels (`active | deprecated | deleted`) are recorded as claims and mapped to name-history statuses only by the importing principal. `export(refs[], target)` lowers a first-party `VariantRecord` to a WS-L5 plugin manifest with a loss report; round-trip recovers everything not declared lost (T-LCD-11).
5. **The registry is not model-facing for third-party content either** (ADR-0151 R6): a third-party variant's `summary` is never rendered into a model context; the model-install path (ADR-0065) may add a `component_variant` extension only through a non-widening `HirDiff` into `exp/` or `local/`, at `text_authority ≤ external`, out-of-process, and it enters no conformance vector.

**T-LCD statement:** satisfies T-LCD-06 (foreign hosted-agent manifests become participant descriptors, never variants; no registry field exists for hosted participants' sake), T-LCD-10 (foreign names and labels are surfaces; identity is content), T-LCD-11 (import/export with loss reports; foreign digests as claims), T-LCD-12 (out-of-process third-party variants), T-LCD-07 (foreign `status`/capability claims recorded, never coerced), T-LCD-05 (a third-party variant's conditioned rules need debt records like any other). Not applicable: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-08/-09/-13/-14/-15 beyond ADR-0151/-2. **ADR-0024 rung:** namespace enforcement, attestation verification, import lifting and the out-of-process rule are MUST-code; `NamespaceRecord`, `TrustRootPolicy`, declared sources and `foreign_import` records are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: verified reverse-DNS namespaces and a closed public-registry set — MCP registry requirements (S-502); squatting under weak ownership — S-303 (Tier C); CI-probed manifests with digest-less `latest` archives — ACP registry (S-503); contribution validation with reserved prefixes and override refusal — Omnigent (S-118); package identity ignoring version — pi (S-113); pinned-vs-range bit — pi (S-113); auto-update/`@latest` defaults — goose (S-112), opencode (S-111), Claude marketplaces (S-313); in-process plugin loading as the ambient-authority vector — CF-141 (S-111/S-113); single-approval persistent trust as a structural cause — S-300 (`provisional`); description-only selection attacks — S-301 (`provisional`); TUF roles and attacks — S-306; signer-anchored trust — S-223; three-leg trust record — ADR-0063.
- (b) Source-code precedent: `omnigent/omnigent/harness_plugins.py` L932–990, L1010–1040 @6a069da; `pi-mono/packages/coding-agent/src/core/package-manager.ts` L59–64, L1101–1105, L1682–1700 @400d690; `harbor/src/harbor/agents/installed/acp_registry.py` L17–130 @7d5285b (foreign-registry version walk — adopted as an assembly-time `import`, rejected at run time); `goose/crates/goose/src/plugins/mod.rs` L18–60, L345–392 @fae91d0; `opencode/packages/opencode/src/plugin/shared.ts` L194–210 @9f8db11; `agent-client-protocol/docs/rfds/acp-agent-registry.mdx` L22–76, L154–185 @9b00c27. Namespace records with layered publish authority, quarantined foreign lifts with loss reports, and the variant-as-extension dual view are `no-precedent / novel` as an integration.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: "no ecosystem signs; requiring owners/signers makes third-party variants unusable" — answered by principal-owned namespaces at C0, hash-only publication with `external` text ceilings at C1, and signatures that raise rather than gate unless policy says otherwise; "out-of-process third-party hot-path variants distort latency" — answered by attributed cost and `locality`/`hot_path` strata (OQ-075); "live foreign resolution is what users expect (Harbor `acp:` shorthand)" — answered by the closed-world rule and by keeping the user experience at assembly time (declared source + recorded resolution). None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: assumes ADR-0063/0064 C1 machinery (attestation, `TrustRootPolicy`, revocation propagation) at Stage 4, the WS-L5 variant host at Stage 2 for any out-of-process variant, and that the MCP/ACP registry formats read on 2026-09-10 remain the reference (both are young; loss reports must be revisited on format change). Independent of model tier and task class.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (reserved namespaces `hh/`, `local/`; `NamespaceRecord` shape; `LocalityInadmissible` at `register`); **C0 / Stage 2** (first out-of-process variant through the L5 host; extension view of `VariantRecord`; `exp/` namespaces); **C1 / Stage 4** (shared namespaces with signer ownership; third-party publish/deprecate/yank/revoke with propagation; `import` from MCP/ACP registries and marketplaces; `export` to the L5 manifest; `publisher_claim` reports at `review`); **C2** (federation across registries, OQ-357).

## Consequences

WS-L5's plugin manifest must lower to and lift from a `VariantRecord` + `ExtensionTrustRecord` without loss beyond the declared report; WS-H5's `TrustRootPolicy.accepted_signers` becomes the source of namespace ownership; WS-J6 receives ACP-registry imports as participant-descriptor candidates; WS-K1 gains `import`/`publish` commands that always show the loss report and the resulting text authority (SEP-1024 shape via ADR-0064 `review`); WS-L6 owns the signing-infrastructure choice (OQ-162).

## Reversibility

**Low** for out-of-process-only third-party variants and declarations-as-claims (ADR-0063 L3/L5 and CF-141 already bind them); **medium** for the reserved-namespace set and publish-authority rules (policy records; changing them is a `NamespaceRecord` supersession); **medium-high** for foreign-import lifting tables (data that tracks external formats).
