# ADR-0089 — Cost, risk, observability and exposure metadata on capabilities: declared cost is advisory and never accounting; risk is derived only; observability is a content-class declaration lowered to standard tool spans; exposure is a hint selected at run time

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-E1 (consumers: WS-E3, WS-E5, WS-F4, WS-D1, WS-L2, WS-I1, WS-I2, WS-H1, WS-H2, WS-H7, WS-J4) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** ADR-0019 (closed-world rule; run-time *selection*), ADR-0022 (E5/E6/E7), ADR-0026 (views derived), ADR-0029 (offload threshold), ADR-0031 (derived risk class; CF-104), ADR-0039 (kernel dimensions; measured usage; `NoPrice` never zero), ADR-0040 (reserve-before-spend), ADR-0042 (spans are ledger projections; export is lowering), ADR-0043 (M-points; integer units), ADR-0044 (`SinkPolicy.content_classes`; process-metric catalogue), ADR-0045 (`n/a{reason}`; class-scoped metrics), ADR-0047 (judges are raise-only inputs); OQ-101, OQ-221; R-2.5.1, R-2.5.3, R-2.9.1

## Context

Doc 2 §11 lists "cost/risk metadata" on the registry row and §5.2 asks the harness to "choose how much of the catalog to reveal … how to compress outputs, how errors are typed". The audit shows cost effects come mainly from *exposure policy* (deferred loading, tool search, programmatic calling — S-040; HEART S-076 `provisional`), that observability of tool calls has a shared span vocabulary with explicit sensitivity warnings (S-228), that every harness caches or defers listings, and that a resource-aware planner benefits from measured per-tool profiles (S-369, `provisional`). Phase 1 fixed that all accounting is measured on the producing event (ADR-0039), that risk class is derived from declared attributes and re-assessed monotonically (ADR-0031/CF-104), that spans are ledger projections (ADR-0042) and that sinks declare content classes (ADR-0044). This ADR fixes what the capability record may say about cost, risk, observability and exposure so that none of it becomes a second accounting, a second risk declaration, a telemetry store or a run-time surface without a compiled identity.

## Options considered

1. **Cost as budget-bearing metadata (the registry charges `control.budget.consumed` from declared costs; pre-charge on dispatch).** *Rejected:* ADR-0039 mandates measured usage on the producing event; declared costs drift with prices and tokenizers; a wrong declaration would corrupt the ledger's totals.
2. **No cost metadata; planners infer from the ledger only.** *Rejected:* WS-E3/F4/D1 need a prior before a capability record has ever run (cold-start catalog selection, result-size planning); PeakBench's mechanism (`provisional`) and Anthropic's deferral figures show exposure/selection decisions benefit from a resource expectation.
3. **Risk as a tool-authored field alongside effects (WS-A3's `risk_class`; OpenHands `security_risk` on the action).** *Rejected:* CF-104 ruled one declaration; model-supplied risk is an input to Π at most (ADR-0031, WS-H1 check 3 raise-only).
4. **Observability as a per-tool telemetry schema (OTel attributes on the record).** *Rejected:* ADR-0042 — no telemetry store; exporters are subscribers; the record must declare *content classes* the sink policy consumes, and the exporter lowers to the OTel vocabulary with a loss report.
5. **Exposure as a semantic field (a tool "is" deferred).** *Rejected:* exposure is a run-time selection among compiled identities (ADR-0019 closed-world rule; Codex keeps deferred tools registered); making it semantic would change identity when a profile changes what it reveals (T-LCD-10).
6. **Declared-but-advisory cost model with provenance/confidence and a measured-profile pointer; risk derived only; observability as content-class + error-class declaration bound to ADR-0044 and lowered to OTel tool spans; exposure as a hint (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Cost.** `cost_model? = {declared: partial map<KernelDimension, Estimate{kind ∈ {constant, per_unit(param_path, unit), distribution(p50, p95)}, value, unit, confidence ∈ {exact, bounded, estimate, unknown}, provenance}>, measured_ref?: ContentAddress}` over ADR-0039 kernel dimensions. `estimate(version_id, canonical_args) → CostEstimate` is pure, returns `unknown` for undeclared dimensions (never 0; ADR-0045), and is consumed by WS-E3 (reveal/defer), WS-F4 (value of compute), WS-D1 (result-size planning) and WS-F2 (`reserve` sizing hint only). Invariants: never read by `control.budget.consumed` or by `MatchSpec`; `exact` requires `measured_ref`; `measured_ref` is a ledger projection `project(runs, tool_cost_profile)` with `computed_from` (ADR-0044) recomputed by the Lab (cadence OQ-221); `estimate_error = |estimate − measured|` is a class-scoped process metric (`requires_observability ⊇ {events}`); `measured_ref` is excluded from `semantic_id`.
2. **Risk.** The record carries no risk field. `project_risk(effects)` yields the CF-104 projection per effect; the kernel's `effective_risk_class = max_by_danger(kernel_assessed, projected(declared))` (ADR-0031) with `unverified` declarations ⇒ `unknown` ⇒ `{irreversible, non_idempotent, external}`. The read-only∧closed exemption of Π (OQ-101) is decidable from `effects` alone (`∀e: mutability = read_only ∧ world = closed`) — no extra field. Judge or model-supplied risk (goose `permission_judge`, OpenHands `security_risk`) enters only as a raise-only Π input.
3. **Observability.** `observation_contract.content_class ∈ {public, sensitive, secret_bearing}` for arguments and results (default `sensitive`; `secret_bearing` for any capability record with `secret_access` effects or a `secret_ref` scope binding) binds to `SinkPolicy.content_classes` and redaction (ADR-0044); `error_classes` (closed sum) binds to E5 and to the `reliability` metric family; `execution_requirement.environment_class`/`source` lower to OTel `gen_ai.tool.type ∈ {function, extension, datastore}` (S-228) in the exporter with a telemetry loss report entry for the collapse; the `tool_call`/`tool_attempt`/`effect` spans remain ADR-0042 projections whose `ir_refs[]` carry `{semantic_id, version_id}`; nothing on the record is a sink, sampler or exporter setting. Tool arguments/results reach an exporter only under a sink whose `content_classes` admit the declared class (ADR-0044 requires consent for `sensitive`; never for `secret_bearing`).
4. **Exposure.** `exposure_hint.default ∈ {direct, deferred, on_demand_only}` and the surface field `exposure_mode ∈ {primitive, composite_member, hidden}` are inputs to WS-E3's per-turn *selection* among compiled identities (ADR-0019); selection is ledgered as `context.artefact.delivered` for the tool surface (T-LCD-13) and never creates a surface without a compiled identity. `exposure_hint` is excluded from `semantic_id`.
5. **Measurement.** Tool cost and latency are observed only at ADR-0043 M-points on `action.tool.*`/`action.effect.*` events (integer ms, monotonic clock) and posted as `control.budget.consumed`; a capability record's `measured_ref` is a view over those events and nothing else.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: exposure policy drives token cost — S-040 (`defer_loading`, tool search "85% reduction", programmatic calling 37 %; vendor numbers not load-bearing), S-107 (`ToolExposure` keeps deferred tools registered, `tool_executor.rs` L51–71), S-076 (`provisional`; catalog-scale pressure); resource-aware selection benefits from exposed resource profiles — S-369 (`provisional`, mechanism only); result-size control as a tool parameter and truncation defaults — S-037 (`response_format` 206 vs 72 tokens; pagination/truncation), S-111 (`truncate.ts` limits and offload); typed error taxonomy as the reliability lever — S-049 (`InvalidArguments`, `UnexpectedEnvironment`, `ProviderError`); standard tool-span vocabulary with sensitivity warnings — S-228 (`gen-ai-spans.md` L1135–1215: `gen_ai.tool.call.arguments/result` "may contain sensitive information", opt-in), S-111 (`Tool.execute` span attributes); judge/model risk as raise-only — S-112 (`permission_judge.rs` L41–181), S-110 (`security_risk` field added at export, `tool.py` L699–717, L878–882). Ratified basis: ADR-0039/0040 (measured usage; reserve), ADR-0031/CF-104 (derived risk), ADR-0042/0043/0044 (projection, M-points, sinks), ADR-0019 (closed-world rule).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `openai/codex` `codex-rs/tools/src/tool_executor.rs L51–130`, `tool_search.rs L18–32`; `anomalyco/opencode` `packages/opencode/src/tool/{truncate.ts L11–40, tool.ts L84–125}`; `OpenHands/software-agent-sdk` `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/tool/tool.py L699–717, L878–882`; `aaif-goose/goose` `crates/goose/src/permission/permission_judge.rs L41–181`; `open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-genai` `docs/gen-ai/gen-ai-spans.md L1135–1215`. **`no-precedent / novel`** for a declared-vs-measured cost model with confidence/provenance on a capability record, and for content-class declarations bound to sink policy.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: declared cost models are never right (prices, tokenizers, provider drift) — does not overturn because the model is optional, non-authoritative, carries `confidence`, and its error is itself measured; a registry without cost metadata is simpler — does not overturn because cold-start selection has no other prior and the field costs nothing when absent (`unknown`); OTel attributes could simply be stored on the record — does not overturn because ADR-0042 forbids a telemetry store and the exporter mapping is a lowering with a loss report; making exposure semantic would let the Lab compare "deferred vs direct" as an IR diff — does not overturn because it is already comparable as a *profile/E3 policy* diff (`SurfaceEdit`/policy parameter) without changing tool identity. `provisional` sources (S-369, S-076) and vendor figures (S-040, S-049) are used for mechanism only.
- (d) Conditionality: independent of model tier; `distribution` estimates assume a stationary environment and are stratified by `environment_ref` and profile in `measured_ref`; `content_class` enforcement beyond sink admission (taint) is C2 (WS-H2); `gen_ai.tool.type` mapping assumes S-228's Development-status values and is revalidated when the convention stabilises.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (`cost_model` schema; `project_risk`; `content_class`/`error_classes` fields; `estimate` returning `unknown`); Stage 2 (offload policy; sink binding); **Stage 3** (`measured_ref` views; `estimate_error` metric; AC-E1-9/-10); C1/Stage 3–4 (`exposure_hint` consumed by WS-E3).

**T-LCD statement.** Satisfies T-LCD-10 (`cost_model.measured_ref` and `exposure_hint` excluded from identity), T-LCD-13 (exposure selection ledgered as artefact delivery), T-LCD-14 (cost metadata never substitutes for matched budgets — `MatchSpec` reads measured events only), T-LCD-15 (`estimate_error` and reliability metrics are class-scoped; `n/a` for hosted participants), T-LCD-11 (`gen_ai.tool.type` collapse recorded in the telemetry loss report), T-LCD-02 (no new free-text field). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-05/-06/-07/-08/-09/-12 (no surface, comparison, hosting or contract change beyond the record fields of ADR-1). ADR-0024 rung: MUST-data declarations; `estimate`/`project_risk`/exporter mapping MUST-code.

## Consequences

- WS-L2/I1 add `tool_cost_profile` to the view catalogue with `computed_from` and a refresh cadence (OQ-221); WS-I2 registers `estimate_error` and the tool `reliability` family as `MetricDeclaration`s.
- WS-E3's selection policy reads `estimate`, `exposure_hint` and `search_projection`; every reveal is a `context.artefact.delivered` with the surface hash.
- WS-H1/H7 read no risk field; Π's read-only∧closed exemption is computed from `effects`.
- WS-I1's OTel exporter carries a fixed `source/environment_class → gen_ai.tool.type` table and records the collapse as loss.

## Reversibility

**High.** Removing `cost_model` or `exposure_hint` loses planning priors only; the derived-risk and content-class rules restate ratified decisions and would revert only with them.
