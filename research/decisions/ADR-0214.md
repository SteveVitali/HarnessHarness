## Context

Spec §11.4.1 lists the questions that block Stages 4–6 with MUST-data placeholders in force. They are of three kinds: security-relevant rulings that Phase 3/4 deliberately left to the owning workstreams (OQ-246 approval persistence across an edge and its dependants OQ-386/405; OQ-177 reviewer authority; OQ-418 child attendance; OQ-116 hosted consent; OQ-392 sink defaults; OQ-401 non-local hosts), placements and lifting tables the Hosting ABI needs at Stage 4 (OQ-242, OQ-378…381, OQ-400, OQ-434, OQ-453), and constants measured at their stage (OQ-388, OQ-413, OQ-417, OQ-424, OQ-430, OQ-432, OQ-433, OQ-368, OQ-420, OQ-441, OQ-446, OQ-435/436, OQ-438, OQ-448, OQ-450, OQ-457/458). Doc 3 §8 requires the deferral ADR.

## Options considered

1. **Close OQ-246 by admitting `allow_always` as a run-scoped lease.** Rejected: ADR-0070/0071 and ADR-0174's consequences constrain the shape (basis `approval`, scope ≤ run, principal seal) but the representation across three surfaces is WS-H7's with K1/K2/K4; ruling it here would bind the ACP artefact, the web inbox and the MCP `human_principal` channel without their owners.
2. **Fix the Stage-5/6 constants from the placeholders.** Rejected for the same reason as ADR-0213 option 1.
3. **Defer with interim rules and entry tests (chosen).**
