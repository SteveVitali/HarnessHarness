# ADR-0152 — The conformance contract per component class: suites pinned to contract versions, reports as records, and the declared-vs-probed conformance vector

**Status:** ratified, amended (Phase 3 synthesis)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-J2 (consumers WS-J3, WS-J4, WS-J5, WS-L5, WS-H5; every Phase 2 class owner as suite author) · **Phase:** 3 · **Related:** ADR-0151 (record model), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-07/-08/-12), ADR-0013 (`capability_vector = reconcile(declaration, probes)`), ADR-0023 (`ClassRecord.contract`), ADR-0028 (`deterministic_replay` as declared capability), ADR-0039 (`charged_to = instrument`), ADR-0045/0047 (metric declarations; oracle classes), ADR-0075/0103/0110/0121/0124–0126 (Phase 2 class contracts and `declare()`), ADR-0094 (offline retrieval suite), ADR-0125 (profile conformance probes), ADR-0063 (claims ≠ grants); OQ-034, OQ-191, OQ-354/-04/-05; CF-325; R-2.10.2, R-2.10.3

## Context

Every Phase 2 class contract ends with a `declare()` operation returning a class-specific declaration (D1 §6.3, D2 §6.5, D3 §6.5, E5 §6.2, F1 §6.1, G1 §6.3, G2 §6.4, I4 §6.4, B5, C1) and an acceptance criterion that says "every registered variant passes a shared battery … implementable out-of-process" (AC-D2-1, AC-F1-1, AC-B5-10, C1 §6.7 golden streams, ADR-0094 §5 retrieval suite). None of them says what *record* the battery is, who runs it, where the result lives, or how the Lab reads it. The audit shows the two ways a registry gets this wrong: trusting declarations it never probes (Harbor `AgentCapabilities`, S-132) or deleting declarations it cannot verify (the ACP registry removed `capabilities` from its manifest and instead runs the `initialize` handshake in CI, S-503). HarnessHarness already has the right pattern twice — hosted participants (`capability_declaration` vs `capability_vector`, ADR-0013; DRIFT as data) and profiles (`ConformanceRecord{declared, probed, verdict}`, ADR-0125) — and a third copy for component variants would be a third vocabulary unless fixed now.

## Options considered

1. **Declarations only** (register `capability_declaration`, trust it; the Lab finds out). *Rejected:* Harbor's shape; DRIFT invisible; third-party claims would decide admissibility (ADR-0063 L3 forbids claims reaching decisions).
2. **Probes only** (drop declarations; a variant is what its suite says). *Rejected:* the ACP registry's shape; suites cannot cover every field (`unknown` must be representable — T-LCD-07); declarations are what the sweep engine filters on before spending budget.
3. **Per-class ad-hoc test suites with no registry record** (status quo of the Phase 2 ACs). *Rejected:* no uniform floor for `resolve`, no bundle member, no way for WS-J3 to refuse an arm that depends on a drifted capability.
4. **Suites as registry records pinned to a contract version; reports as immutable records from ledgered instrument-charged runs; a derived vector with one verdict vocabulary shared with profiles and hosted participants; admission floors as policy (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **`ConformanceSuite{suite_id, class_ref: VersionedRef<ClassRecord> (pinned to contract_version), tests[{test_id, kind ∈ {static, contract, executable, property}, fixture_ref: ContentAddress, driver ∈ {in-process, out-of-process}, oracle_class (ADR-0047; deterministic-only at C0), budget: BudgetNode, verdict_rule}], required_for_status: set<test_id>, version_id}`** is a registry record; every `ClassRecord` carries `conformance_suite_ref`. A class without a suite is `SuiteMissing` and its variants resolve only under `require_conformance = none`. Minimum at Stage 1: `control_strategy` and `context_policy` each have ≥1 test of each kind with an out-of-process driver.
2. **Four test kinds (the form; classes fill the content from their ACs):** *static* — record schema, `declaration_schema` conformance, complete debt records (T-LCD-05), `required_inputs` (T-LCD-08); *contract* — operations driven over canonical documents out-of-process, every closed-sum input exercised (unhandled inputs fail here, never at run time), purity where the contract demands it, typed failures returned; *executable* — fixtures with deterministic oracles (golden streams, `apply(base, diff) = target`, retrieval floors, cut-point fuzzing, capability-honesty probes: a declared-`supported` field is exercised and an undeclared operation returning success fails); *property* — kernel-relied invariants (never widens authority; never returns a permission/label/lower risk class; reads only its declared feature vocabulary; `deterministic = true ⇒` replay-equal).
3. **`ConformanceReport{report_id, variant_ref, suite_ref, host{locality, isolation, instrument: InstrumentRecord}, produced_by ∈ {registry_ci, lab, publisher_claim}, results[{test_id, verdict, evidence_ref}], probed_declaration, run_id, stale: bool, version_id}`** is an immutable registry record. `registry_ci` and `lab` reports must reference a ledgered run with `charged_to = instrument` (ADR-0039); `publisher_claim` reports are recorded and shown at `review` (ADR-0064) and never count toward `probed`. A report against a suite that is not the head suite of the class's current `contract_version` is accepted with `stale = true` and satisfies no floor (`SuiteStale`). Reports never overwrite.
4. **One verdict vocabulary.** `results[].verdict` and the per-field `probed` value use Ontology v1 *observed conformance*: `SUPPORTED, UNSUPPORTED, PARTIAL, NOT_APPLICABLE, UNKNOWN, SKIPPED, DRIFT` (plus `pass/fail/inconclusive/n/a{reason}` as the test-level projection). `ConformanceRecord` (profiles, ADR-0125; hosted participants, ADR-0013) and `ConformanceReport.results[]` share this vocabulary with kind-specific subject fields (CF-325 for synthesis to confirm one shape).
5. **Derived vector.** `variant_conformance_vector(variant_ref, policy) = reconcile(capability_declaration, {reports : produced_by ∈ policy.admissible_report_producers ∧ ¬stale})` → per declaration field `{declared, probed, unknown, drift}`; `unknown` is never coerced (T-LCD-07); DRIFT is data — a stratification coordinate and a results-row annotation, never a silent exclusion (OQ-034's shape). The vector is a view (ADR-0026), never stored primary.
6. **Floors.** `RegistryPolicy.require_conformance ∈ {none, declared, probed}` (default `declared` at C0; `probed` in Lab strict mode) is checked at `publish` (`ConformanceRequired`) and at `resolve(mode = execute)` (`ConformanceBelowFloor{vector}`); a `resolve` without a floor succeeds with the vector attached.
7. **Lab consumption.** `slot_choices(class_id, constraints{conformance_floor, …}, snapshot)` and `substitutable(a, b, constraints)` are deterministic registry operations; WS-J3 pins one snapshot per `Design`, refuses an arm binding a variant with `DRIFT` on a capability the design depends on (`DependsOnDriftedCapability`, mirroring ADR-0125's dependency-scoped DRIFT refusal), and records the vector on every arm; component-level conformance metrics are `n/a{reason: class}` for hosted rows (T-LCD-15).
8. **Ordered-many classes.** Per-element reports are mandatory; a pipeline-level test kind for `ordered-many` classes is OQ-356 (with OQ-191) — until answered, a pipeline's vector is the field-wise meet of its elements' vectors.

**T-LCD statement:** satisfies T-LCD-07 (reflexive: declared vs probed, `unknown` never coerced, comparisons stratify), T-LCD-08 (static test), T-LCD-12 (out-of-process drivers mandatory), T-LCD-05 (static test), T-LCD-14 (reports charged `instrument`; never a subject's budget), T-LCD-15 (`n/a` for hosted rows), T-LCD-09 (`slot_choices` per class). Not applicable: T-01/-02/-03/-04/-06/-10/-11/-13. **ADR-0024 rung:** drivers and reconciliation are MUST-code; suites, reports and `require_conformance` are MUST-data.

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: probe-at-registration and capabilities-out-of-the-manifest — ACP registry RFD and CI (S-503); declared capabilities validated but never probed — Harbor (S-132); declared-vs-observed with DRIFT as data — Omnigent bench (S-118 via WS-A1, Ontology v1); the same split ratified for profiles (ADR-0125) and hosted participants (ADR-0013); every Phase 2 class shipping a battery (AC-D2-1, AC-F1-1, AC-B5-10, C1 golden streams, ADR-0094 §5); claims never decide (ADR-0063 L3; MCP hints rule, S-153); description-only manipulation of registries (S-301, `provisional`).
- (b) Source-code precedent: `agent-client-protocol/docs/rfds/acp-agent-registry.mdx` L163–185 @9b00c27 (CI runs `initialize`, checks `authMethods`; revision 2026-02-04 removed `capabilities`); `harbor/src/harbor/agents/base.py` L83–130, `agents/capabilities.py` @7d5285b (static declaration with conflict refusal); `omnigent/omnigent/harness_capabilities.py` L107–182 @6a069da (tri-state declaration read by the bench); `inspect_ai/src/inspect_ai/_util/registry.py` L613–650 @75f4891 (versioned registry consumers). The uniform suite/report records and the derived vector for component variants are `no-precedent / novel` (ADR-0125's `ProfileTestReport` is the nearest internal precedent).
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: "suites give false confidence and cost budget" — answered by scoping suites to the *contract* (not quality), charging `instrument`, and making the floor a policy knob; "we control our own code, declared/probed is ceremony" — answered by third-party variants (ADR-0063) and by first-party drift the audit found unobservable in Harbor; "a uniform IO interface is the LCD trap" (S-024) — answered by tri-state declarations and profile-receiving contracts recorded, not intersected. None overturns.
- (d) Conditionality: model-independent; assumes ADR-0047's deterministic oracle classes for C0 suites (judged tests are C1 and inherit judge governance), the WS-L5 variant host for out-of-process drivers at Stage 2 (Stage 1 drivers may run in the reference runtime's test harness), and Phase 2 owners authoring suite content at Phase 5 (OQ-355).
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** (suite/report record shapes; suites for the two Stage-1 classes; `require_conformance = declared`); **C0 / Stage 2** (`record_conformance`, vector, floors at `resolve`, remaining Phase 2 class suites, out-of-process drivers via the L5 host); **C0 / Stage 3** (`slot_choices` with floors, `DependsOnDriftedCapability` in WS-J3, vector on results rows, reports as bundle members); **C1 / Stage 4** (`publisher_claim` reports at `review`; `probed` strict mode); **C1 / Stage 5** (profile reports unified under the shared vocabulary).

## Consequences

Every Phase 2 class owner must express its acceptance battery as a `ConformanceSuite` record (a Phase 5 obligation, OQ-355); WS-J3 gains a design-time refusal; WS-J5 rows gain a conformance annotation; WS-I3's bundle carries report refs (CF-328); WS-L5's host must expose the out-of-process driver seam; third-party declarations are inert until probed.

## Reversibility

**Low** for declared ≠ probed and the shared verdict vocabulary (Lab stratification and T-LCD-07 depend on them); **medium** for the four test kinds and the floor semantics (additive test kinds are a suite version; floors are policy); **high** for Stage-1 minimum cardinality.
