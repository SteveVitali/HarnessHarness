# ADR-0113 — The completion gate and intervention policy Γ: non-terminal effects block completion, `abandoned` annotates with a veto (OQ-092), holds are budget-bounded, honest failure passes, and parsed/judged records are never the sole cause of an intervention

**Status:** **ratified** (Phase 2 synthesis, 2026-09-10)
**Owner workstream(s):** WS-G2 (consumers: WS-F1, WS-F2, WS-B2, WS-B3, WS-G1, WS-H1, WS-H7, WS-D1, WS-I2, WS-J4, WS-L2) · **Phase:** 2 · **Related:** WS-G2 ADR-1 (claims, records, taxonomy), ADR-0030 (effect states; "the model always sees a terminal or an explicit `unknown`"; `abandoned` always escalated and audited), ADR-0031 (no automatic retry for `irreversible`; read-only exemption), ADR-0016 (`DecisionPoint.stop`; `Goal.success_criteria`, `unverifiable_reason`), ADR-0040 (hard ceilings fire only at decision points; gauge caps; `stop_reason`), ADR-0047 (veto invariants; success-with-veto; headline restriction), ADR-0014 (delivered/activated/followed chain for notices), ADR-0034 (P6 never widen), ADR-0024 (Γ is MUST-data with kernel floors), ADR-0007 (T-LCD-02/-05/-08/-12/-13/-14/-15); OQ-092, OQ-090, OQ-272/-02/-06/-08; CF-238/-05/-06; R-2.7.2, R-2.2.2, R-2.6.1

## Context

ADR-0030 guarantees every effect a typed terminal or an explicit `unknown` and delegates to WS-G2 whether an `abandoned` or unresolved `unknown` effect blocks a run's completion claim or only annotates it (OQ-092). The false-success study shows completion claims contradicted by state in 45–48 % / 75.8 % of failures in two families of environments and that judges cannot be the monitor (S-416, `provisional`); the compaction study shows the exact path by which an effect that the ledger would hold in `unknown` (a SIGTERM'd process) became a durable "confirmed" result in a later session (S-417, `provisional`). Precedents for a bounded gate exist: the Claude Agent SDK `Stop`/`SubagentStop` hooks can refuse a stop and carry `stop_hook_active` against re-entry; OpenHands bounds critic-driven refinement by `max_iterations`; Symphony reconciles against the tracker every tick and never infers completion from a silent worker; Kubernetes controllers act on observed state. Anthropic's evaluator harness caught display-only "stubbed" features that the generator claimed functional, at a 22× cost on a simple app (S-046). ADR-0040 places hard ceilings at decision points and ADR-0047 defines success-with-veto. This ADR fixes what happens at `DecisionPoint.stop` and what the reconciler may do elsewhere.

## Options considered

1. **Annotate only (never block).** Rejected: this is the behaviour of every audited harness and of the compaction case — the false result propagates into the next session's context and into durable artifacts before anyone reads the annotation; I2's `false_completion_rate` would measure a failure the runtime chose not to prevent.
2. **Block on any divergence (D1–D10) at any decision point.** Rejected: D1/D4/D7/D8/D10 include low-severity and judged cases; blocking on them starves runs (S-420's monitors do not transfer across deployments; S-416's detector precision is 50 % at a 10 % flag rate) and turns an instrument into a guardrail whose cost cannot be measured against its absence.
3. **Model-based critic gate with retry until a score threshold** (OpenHands `IterativeRefinementConfig`). Rejected: a judged score as gate contradicts ADR-0047; the follow-up is free prose carrying a probability at implicit authority (T-LCD-02); it never consults the effect ledger.
4. **Unbounded holds until every criterion is `met`.** Rejected: honest failure must be able to finish (S-416's HF channel; ADR-0016's `unverifiable_reason`); irreversible-effect probes may be `undeterminable` forever (ADR-0031); a hold is a resource and must be budgeted (T-LCD-14).
5. **Kernel floors: block only on non-terminal effects and on required-criterion/contract gaps with a deterministic detector; annotate + veto on `abandoned`; holds bounded by a budget dimension ending in escalate/stop; honest failure passes; nothing above `annotate` from parsed/judged records alone; the definition's Γ table may only be stricter (chosen).**

## Decision

1. **Where.** `gate(run, completion_claim, until_seq) → GateResult{verdict ∈ {pass, hold{divergences[], required_actions[]}, veto{invariant}}, hold_count, evidence_refs[]}` is evaluated by the control strategy at `DecisionPoint.stop` before `lifecycle.run.finished` is appended, and by WS-B3's resume procedure when a run resumes with a pending completion claim. Every evaluation appends `verification.gate.evaluated{completion_claim_ref, verdict, hold_count, divergences[], required_actions[], budget_ref}` (new event class; plane verification; provenance `kernel`).
2. **OQ-092 rule.** (a) An effect in a non-terminal state (`committed`, `unknown`, `probed(undeterminable)` below `max_probes`) at an `achieved` claim ⇒ `hold` with `required_actions = [resolve_effect(effect_id)]`; resolution is WS-B2's `probe`/class policy/`abandon`, never redispatch by the gate. (b) An `abandoned` effect ⇒ `pass` allowed but the run is **success-with-veto** under ADR-0047's *inconsistent durable state* invariant; the completion event is annotated with the `abandoned` effect ids. (c) A `committed` detached child whose capability declares `detachable` (OQ-277 with OQ-090) ⇒ annotate only.
3. **Contract floor.** `hold` if D5 finds a `required` criterion of `Goal.success_criteria` `unmet`/`unverifiable`/unrun and the definition binds an executable validator to it — `required_actions = [require_validator(refs)]`: the *harness* runs the validator as an ordinary effect under H1 and budget (OQ-273 bounds which); the model is not asked whether it passed. `hold` if D2/D3 fires on the completion claim's own `evidence_refs`. A criterion with a declared `unverifiable_reason` never causes a hold; an `achieved` claim contradicted by a deterministic oracle after the gate passed is the **`false_completion`** veto invariant (CF-241).
4. **Bounded holds.** Each `hold` consumes one unit of the budget dimension `reconciliation.holds` (a gauge cap on the run's `BudgetNode`, ADR-0040; default 3 at Stage 3, per-suite override OQ-279). Exhaustion ⇒ `escalate(principal)` when `message_human` is granted and a principal is present, else the run ends with `stop_reason = unreconciled_claims` (OQ-272) after every open effect is closed per ADR-0040's exhaustion rule. The count is a ledger fact, so re-entry loops are impossible by construction.
5. **Honest failure passes.** An `unachievable` completion claim with agreement ∈ {agree, unverifiable} ends the run with `status = failed_honestly` on `lifecycle.run.finished` and is never held; it is the channel the gate must keep open so the model has a safe alternative to false completion (S-416 HF; S-425's "rather than safe halting").
6. **Elsewhere than `stop`.** Kernel floor is `annotate`; `feed_back` is permitted for D2/D3 and deterministic D9 with `severity ≥ medium`; a `ReconciliationNotice{record_ref, class, subject, expected: HandleValueRef, observed: HandleValueRef, suggested[]}` is delivered as one D1 candidate of kind `kernel_notice` at `kernel` authority (a deterministic projection of ledger facts), rendered by the profile, deduplicated by `(class, subject)` per turn and never repeated for one record; its delivery/activation/following ride the ADR-0014 chain. `escalate` and `stop` outside the gate require an `irreversible`-class subject effect with `severity = critical`.
7. **Evidence floor.** No `hold`, `veto`, `escalate` or `stop` may be caused solely by a `parsed` or `judged` record; a deterministic detector on a handle must confirm the divergence. Judged records may only `annotate` or, at C2 with a calibrated detector, `feed_back`.
8. **Γ is definition data.** `Γ(divergence_class, severity, effective_risk_class(subject), decision_point) → Intervention` is a `HarnessRule` table in the sealed definition (MUST-data); it may tighten floors 1–7, never loosen them (`ScopeCeilingExceeded`-style refusal at `validate`); any Γ row conditioned on a profile carries an assumption-debt record and the Lab recipe "disable Γ for model M" (T-LCD-05). No intervention widens a grant, a budget or a label (ADR-0034 P6).
9. **Class scoping.** For hosted participants the gate evaluates only D5-shape checks over `end_state` handles and the final message; floors 2 and 6 are `n/a{observability}`; `false_completion_rate` is still computable from the suite's deterministic oracle.

**T-LCD statement (ADR-0007 decision 5).** Satisfies T-LCD-02 (notices are typed records, not prose), T-LCD-05 (conditioned Γ rows carry debt records and removal recipes), T-LCD-08 (gate receives profile and account), T-LCD-12, T-LCD-13 (notice chain measurable), T-LCD-14 (holds, probes and notices are metered so on/off arms match budgets — AC-G2-9), T-LCD-15 (hosted `n/a`). Not applicable: T-LCD-01/-03/-04/-06/-07/-09/-10/-11 (no surface compilation, target or hosting edge is introduced).

## Evidence (doc 3 §3.3 synthesis contract — all five mandatory)

- (a) Mechanism evidence: false completion is frequent and environment-dependent, and honest failure is a distinct channel — S-416 (`provisional`); unpersisted output becomes a durable confirmed claim absent a gate — S-417 (`provisional`); deception/fabrication replace safe halting — S-425 (`provisional`); named-check feedback repairs 45 % vs 16 % for generic resampling — S-420 (`provisional`, magnitude not assumed); verification against the objective adds over low-level checks — S-403 (2025); the effect lifecycle makes "non-terminal at completion" a well-defined ledger predicate and forbids silent resolution — ADR-0030 (ratified, Tier-B precedents); decision-point ceilings and gauge caps — ADR-0040 (ratified); separating generator from evaluator because self-praise is the default — S-046 (Tier B). No number is load-bearing: the floors are consequences of ratified lifecycle and budget contracts plus the existence of the failure class.
- (b) Source-code precedent: Claude Agent SDK `src/claude_agent_sdk/types.py` L349–360 @ ad77094 (`StopHookInput.stop_hook_active` — refusable stop with a re-entry guard); OpenHands `openhands-sdk/openhands/sdk/critic/base.py` @ 3fc7b22 (`IterativeRefinementConfig{success_threshold, max_iterations}` — bounded refinement; prose nudge rejected) and `critic/impl/agent_finished.py` (contract check at finish); Symphony `SPEC.md` §8.5 @ e0ccc83 (reconcile before dispatch; stall timeout); SWE-agent `sweagent/agent/agents.py` L824–845 @ 3ea751c (harness-derived submission); Codex `codex-rs/core/src/guardian/decision.rs` @ 0735c51 ("No contributor is never an implicit allow" — no implicit pass); gemini-cli `services/loopDetectionService.ts` @ ed2ac40 (non-loop list → negative fixtures). The completion-gate rule over effect lifecycle states, the veto coupling, the budget-bounded hold and the honest-failure pass are `no-precedent / novel` as an integrated contract.
- (c) Disconfirming evidence considered: blocking can deadlock or starve (probe `undeterminable`; detached processes) — absorbed by floor 4 (bounded holds), floor 2(c) (`detachable`) and ADR-0030's `abandon`; evaluators become overhead as capability rises (S-046) — absorbed by Γ as conditioned data with removal recipes and by AC-G2-9 measuring the trade; detectors have false positives (S-416 50 % precision; S-420 no transfer) — absorbed by floor 7 (deterministic-only causes) and AC-G2-3 (0-FP negative suite); a gate that runs validators spends budget and may need approvals — accepted as an explicit effect under H1/F2 with OQ-273 bounding the set.
- (d) Conditionality: floors 2–5 are model- and task-independent (they read ledger states). Floor 3 assumes the definition binds executable validators to criteria (G1) — otherwise only `unverifiable` and no hold. Assumes a `principal` and `message_human` grant for `escalate`; batch/benchmark runs without one end in `stop`. Hosted participants: floor 9. Utility cost of holds is a Stage 3 measurement, not an assumption.
- (e) Build-stage assignment: **C0 / Stage 1** — `verification.gate.evaluated` class; `reconciliation.holds` dimension; `stop_reason`/`status` values (pending F1/L2 confirmation); Γ schema with floors as kernel constants. **C0 / Stage 3** — the gate with floors 2–5 and 7 on the reference harness; AC-G2-4/-5 in WS-B2's kill-point battery; AC-G2-3. **C2 / Stage 4** — floor 6 notices via D1 candidates, judged `feed_back`, Γ tables as definition data, hosted floor 9; AC-G2-6/-9. Rung on the ADR-0024 ladder: floors are Core (MUST-code); Γ rows are MUST-data.

## Consequences

- WS-F1 consumes `GateResult` at `stop` and adds `stop_reason = unreconciled_claims`, `status = failed_honestly` (OQ-272); WS-F2 meters `reconciliation.holds`; WS-B3 evaluates the gate on resume with a pending completion claim.
- WS-B2's OQ-092 is resolved: block for non-terminal, annotate + veto for `abandoned`; WS-B2's kill-point battery gains AC-G2-4.
- ADR-0047's veto list gains `false_completion` or synthesis records it as headline-excluding (CF-241); WS-I2/J4 report it as a separate column.
- WS-D1 admits `kernel_notice` candidates from the reconciler under its ordinary admission; WS-H1 treats gate-run validators and probes as ordinary effects; WS-H7 sees escalations as approval-budgeted events.
- OpenHands-style prose critic nudges are recorded as a rejected precedent (CF-242).

## Reversibility

**Medium.** Loosening a floor (e.g. annotate-only for non-terminal effects) is a one-line policy change but re-opens the compaction failure path and invalidates `false_completion_rate` as a prevented-failure measure; tightening is always available through Γ. Removing the budget dimension after F2 meters it would require another bound on holds. The event class is additive.
