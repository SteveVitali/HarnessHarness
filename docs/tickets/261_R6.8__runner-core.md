## Goal

Create the one committed runner of [§3.2](../experiments-spec.md#s3): a new workspace crate `hharness-runner` with binary `hh-runner`. It drives `hh-experiment`'s `ExperimentEngine` and keeps no scheduler, retry counter or authoritative store of its own. It adds no K-2 edge, emits one `hh-experiment-slot/1` record per slot (with the closed `withheld{…}` member set), and maps slot facts to engine outcome classes and dispositions by the §3.3 table (AR-04). Stage 2 proves the process topology: a spike on cross-process reads of a root another process is writing, process-level concurrency, a two-process kill-point battery, and resume derived only from durable records.

## In scope — deliverables

**Stage 1 — RUNNER-CORE (a)** (commit checkpoint `R6.8 stage 1/2: runner core` before Stage 2).
1. `crates/hh-runner/` package `hharness-runner` (lib + `hh-runner` bin), a workspace member depending only downward (hh-experiment, hh-lab, hh-eval, hh-bench, hh-env, hh-helper, hh-ledger, hh-budget); no external dependency. [REQ-XB-101]
2. The coordinator drives `next`/`claim`/`launch`/`settle` with `ReattemptPolicy`; the runner holds no scheduler, retry counter or authoritative store. [REQ-XB-102]
3. A K-2 edge check (`cargo tree` based) that fails when the runner reaches hh-embed, hh-cli or hharness-hosting, wired per ADR-0362 path modes. [REQ-XB-103]
4. No absolute paths; inputs from committed fixtures or declared run inputs; every packet records `driver_commit` and the runner binary digest; an absolute-path scan test over the runner sources. [REQ-XB-101, REQ-XA-010]
5. `hh-experiment-slot/1` with every §3.3 section, reusing `hh-s4-slot/1` spellings where they carry over. `costs` references `hh-slot-costs/1` (`model` is the proxy fold and `compute` is the window reference; both are owned by R6.14) and never duplicates them. It carries the closed `withheld{…}` member set enumerated in Notes (spec Appendix D). [REQ-XB-108]
6. The outcome map: every §3.3 slot-fact row in table order, with the premature-refusal precedence; only `infrastructure_failure` replans; Harbor `error_policy` causes map one-to-one through a pinned cause map. [REQ-XB-120, REQ-XB-012]
7. A fixture executor plus a fake verifier drive a 2-arm × 3-task × 2-replicate experiment end to end.
8. The ADR for AR-04 (crate, placement, slot record). The Harbor tool-admission part stays open for R6.17 as a dated addendum.

**Stage 2 — RUNNER-CORE (b).**
9. Process-topology spike: a cross-process read of a store root that another process is writing. The outcome is recorded in the run ledger and the ADR. [REQ-XB-105; contributes REQ-XB-104]
10. Process-level slot concurrency: a coordinator process plus slot-worker processes, and no store root opened by two writing processes. [contributes REQ-XB-104]
11. Two-process kill-point battery: worker death before seal, worker death after seal and before bind, and coordinator death mid-claim. Each recovers to the correct disposition with no duplicate attempt; an interrupted attempt closes as `inconclusive{infra:runner_crash}` and replans within bounds; a paid agent phase is never resumed mid-run. [REQ-XB-105]
12. Resume derives only from durable records; a scan test finds no file-existence skip. [REQ-XB-105]
