## In scope — deliverables

Stage (a) — FP-RESULTS (a), committed before stage (b) starts:

1. **Seal opening** — one durable, recorded seal-open act over W2/W4 agent-slot outcome fields through R6.30's projections; no outcome field was read before it (firewall check over every prior readout). Consumes REQ-XH-121.
2. **Integrity scan** — `answer-scan/1` over every scored trial's integrity-input bundle; a flagged-pass list for HUMAN-H16 with excerpt hashes and harness identity masked where feasible; a missing scan is unresolved, never clean. Consumes REQ-XD-070; feeds REQ-XD-071.
3. **P2-FP packet** — `hh-comparative-results/1`: Δ(Pi − mini), Δ(Claude Code − mini), FM credible and task bootstrap, sharp-null sign-flip p under `slot_schedule_randomization`, Holm α = .05 over 2 members with any held arm retained as an unavailable member (REQ-XH-127(a)); every §6.11 quantity with interval and label (REQ-XD-090); secondary populations `raw` and the all-planned lower bound beside the primary (REQ-XD-005); intended-vs-served counts (REQ-XA-006); expected precision quoted from the pre-registration; labels per REQ-XH-119.
4. **P4-FP packet** — Δ(V5 − baseline), family of one, §8.8 first-pass form; per-level cost, $/solve, tokens and calls descriptive only (REQ-XD-224); under `fallback`, a typed `withheld{l1_not_passed}` packet (REQ-XH-123).

Stage (b) — FP-RESULTS (b), K9:

5. **P3-L2 packet** — the landed `parity-admission/1` packet plus the directional-KL report over the N = 50 W4 pairs at ε = ±.20, typed unknowns and veto layer as wired by R6.46; under `fallback`, `withheld{l1_not_passed}`.
6. **L1-on-L2 audit** — seeded sample of W4 O recordings only (never the W2 corpus) replayed to the frozen H under L1.1; a failure fails the parity claim and the L2 verdict stays recorded (REQ-XD-119). Withheld under `fallback`.
7. **L3 report** — §7.6 per-arm distributions, per-pair differences and every discordant pair, with no equivalence wording (REQ-XD-120). Withheld under `fallback`.
8. **Recompute battery** — every packet recomputes byte-equal from committed records (REQ-XD-090); golden fixtures for each disposition branch.
