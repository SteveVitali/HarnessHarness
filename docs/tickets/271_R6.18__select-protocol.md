## Goal

Make the selection and protocol halves of spec §3.1, §6.1–§6.3, §6.7, §6.12 and §4.6 exist as code. Stage 1 (SELECT) lands one `task-selection/1` materializer in hh-eval: strata from declared `task.toml` fields, the seeded band × difficulty split that reserves the development pool, a seeded stratified draw with within-cell replacement orders, the TB 2.1 three-part pin with `latest` refused, and outcome-field input refusals. Stage 2 (D1-a) lands `comparative_protocol/1` (H ≥ 2 arms, declared T, k ≥ 5, the schedule-randomization record, declared-unsupported support members under AR-12) and the single quote artifact `comparative-quote/1` with model and compute lines, with compute priced only by R6.14's §3.10 formula and the envelopes recorded by AR-13. Both are siblings of `central_protocol` / `exposure_quote`, never edits (D1 F2, C-11). Nothing for the real first pass is committed here; R6.30 commits it.

## In scope — deliverables

**Stage 1 — SELECT** (own AC block, tests and commit):

1. `task-selection/1` module in hh-eval: declared-field census in; exclusion, split, draw and replacement-order records out, content-addressed with `idp_digest` under the domain `comparative_protocol.split` (REQ-XB-122, REQ-XD-011).

3. Seeded difficulty-stratified draw by largest remainder with a pre-committed seeded within-cell replacement order (§10.1.2's shape) — functions only (REQ-XD-011; contributes REQ-XH-116).
4. TB 2.1 pin (git commit, Hub dataset ref, per-task `dataset.toml` digest) carried by every selection record and required on the run record; `ref=latest` refuses typed (REQ-XB-122 + REQ-XB-001's literal predicate).
5. Input-manifest refusal: an outcome field, or another phase's held-out outcome, in any selection input refuses typed (REQ-XH-012).

**Stage 2 — D1-a:**

6. `comparative_protocol/1` generalizing `central_protocol` to H ≥ 2 arms, declared T and k, consuming `task-selection/1`, reusing exclusion, digest-ranked selection, reattempt and phase-identity machinery; owns arms, k, the schedule-randomization record and declared-unsupported members; byte-stable materialization and refusal matrix (REQ-XD-020).
7. k < 5 refuses a confirmatory identity (REQ-XD-002); a pooled cross-tranche estimand refuses unless declared at the TR1 freeze with unchanged versions, effort, rewrite version and catalog identity (REQ-XD-004).
8. Schedule-randomization record over R6.16's seeded k-round kind, recorded before execution; `slot_schedule_randomization` is the protocol's only admitted exchangeability argument; an unrandomized order refuses typed (REQ-XD-021).
9. Support declarations for glm-5.3-flash on Ollama (`support_fingerprint = false`, `support_cache = false`, `support_seed = unverified` treated false); `hh.fingerprint/v1` never an identity gate; the gate is amended by ADR **AR-12**, never satisfied by flipping booleans (REQ-XC-010).
10. `comparative-quote/1`: the §6.7 menu at N = H·T·k with every column REQ-XD-050 lists, deterministic recompute from measurement records, model and compute lines, compute lines only through R6.14's formula; simulated half-width/power cells are inputs read from battery records (R6.27) (REQ-XD-050, REQ-XH-030).
11. ADR **AR-13** (experiments envelopes and quote ceilings; provisioned `exposure_quote` quantities; round-5 §9.2 constants unchanged; recipe pre-quote stays labelled until AR-13 lands) (REQ-XD-051 + REQ-XB-221's literal predicate).
