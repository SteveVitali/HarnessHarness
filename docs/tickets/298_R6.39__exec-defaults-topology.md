## Goal

Make the helper's exec match `subprocess` semantics where the spec requires it (G17, REQ-XE-119): exec capabilities declare `exec_defaults{timeout_ms, stdio, cwd, env}`, the helper supports merged stdio and an in-cell process-tree kill, and a timeout renders as `returncode = -1` plus the declared exception text. Then run the HH-native participant topology spike (E2-T8 (a), REQ-XE-120): `hh-kernel` as a child participant process over binding (b), sole writer of the slot store during the agent phase, with cell custody passed to the slot worker by `env.close{detach}` and re-attach — and **land the C-05 rung record at the rung actually reached, in every outcome**. GATE-G26 (FP-S) waits for that record (Q-X62).

## In scope — deliverables

**Stage 1 — E2-T6 (b) (own commit).**
1. Exec capabilities declare `exec_defaults{timeout_ms, stdio, cwd, env}` in the AR-22 spelling; declared `env` constants merge into the exec request; `cwd` defaults to the image WORKDIR from R6.9's cell spec. *(REQ-XE-119)*
2. Helper exec member `stdio = merged` (one ordered stream) and an in-cell process-tree kill on deadline; the helper protocol is internal (no `hh-embed` schema change). *(REQ-XE-119)*
3. A timeout renders as `returncode = -1` plus the declared exception text through R6.36's result renderer. *(REQ-XE-119)*

**Stage 2 — E2-T8 (a) spike + rung record (own commit).**
4. The two-process ownership spike: the slot worker spawns `hh-kernel` over binding (b) with the slot store as its root (sole writer during the agent phase), opens the session with the `exec` member, provisions the cell with `env.open{connection_info}` (reusing the helper cell-spec and egress admissions — no second provisioning path, CC1), drives, calls `env.close{detach}`, and the worker re-attaches the cell for seal, verify and teardown. *(REQ-XE-120)*
5. A fixture slot end to end against R6.12's replay proxy, when rung 1 or rung 2 is reached. *(REQ-XE-120)*
6. **The C-05 rung record**, committed in every outcome: rung reached (1, 2 or 3), the evidence for it, and the failure detail for any rung not reached. On rung 3, the row drafts the AR-04-amending ADR **for the operator** and raises it through a scoped `decompose-spec` insert request; it does not adopt it, and HH-native slots refuse typed until it is admitted. *(REQ-XE-120)*
