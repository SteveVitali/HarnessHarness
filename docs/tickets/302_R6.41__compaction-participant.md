## Goal

Wire the registered-but-ignored `compaction_strategy` slot into the kernel compaction ladder, with an absent slot byte-identical to today (G16a, REQ-XE-122). Then complete the HH-native participant on top of R6.39's topology at the rung its record reached (E2-T8 (b)): HH-native model lanes route through the proxy's token-gated loopback listener with the same pins and capture points as O (REQ-XE-121); every HH-native slot record carries `participant.hir{…}` (REQ-XE-126); and the runner refuses to schedule a paid L2 pair without a matching `hh-l1-conformance/1` pass id for the exact H (REQ-XD-102). Needed before W3/W4 only, so it sits after W2 (plan tradeoff 3).
