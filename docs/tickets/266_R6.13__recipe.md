## Goal

Make the spec's single committed compute backend exist and be provably safe offline: `scripts/gce-backend.sh` (bash 3.2) with its startup script, `hh-compute-decl/1` declarations and `hh-compute-receipt/1` receipts, the nine-step unskippable teardown, `scripts/compute-open.sh`, and the deterministic fake-shim battery `scripts/check-gce-backend.sh` that proves every refusal, receipt shape and ordering of §3.9 and the secret path of §3.11 — without a single real GCP call. `quote` emits declaration quantities only, labelled `pre-quote`; pricing is R6.14's.

Stage B — RECIPE-b:
7. `prepull` (admitted images by digest; receipt with digest, size, arch) and the runner diskwatch unit test. [REQ-XB-207]
