Make phase 1 recomputable records-in (spec §2.2–§2.3). The 71 scratch drivers and the raw-record set are committed verbatim as unmaintained provenance with digests. ## In scope — deliverables

**Stage 1 — P1-A provenance** (commit checkpoint before stage 2).

1. **Presence check first.** Verify the operator-disk archive against its manifest (71/71) and the manifest's own sha256. If either is absent or differs, stop typed (`blockedOn: operator-disk input absent|digest mismatch`). Never reconstruct, edit or substitute. *(REQ-XA-101)*
2. **Archive** under `docs/build/reports/provenance/2026-11-06-scratch-drivers/`: `files/` byte-identical, `SHA256SUMS`, `index.json` (`hh-scratch-driver-archive/1`), and a README. The README states that the files are historical and unmaintained, why they are neither built nor workspace members, that the §3.2 runner supersedes them, and the manifest's sha256. *(REQ-XA-101, REQ-XA-102)*
3. **Historical re-run recipe** in the README: prerequisites, per-epoch HH commit, the declared non-verbatim path-dep edit, the provisioning gate. It states that a re-run is fresh execution, never replay. *(REQ-XA-104)*
4. **Raw-record set** `raw/` + `raw/SHA256SUMS`. Bulk artifacts and the dataset are content-addressed only and labelled not committed. Run a secret scan before commit (X-05). *(REQ-XA-103)*
5. **Content digests** of the 30-task and 80-task sets, recorded beside the FNV `catalogue_identity` and never replacing it. When the dataset is absent, record `skipped(dataset_absent)`. Task content is never committed. *(REQ-XA-105)*
6. **`docs/build/tools/p1_records.py`** (stdlib, read-only, `hh-p1-records-check/1`, exit 0/1/2; no absolute paths; committed inputs only) with checks RC-1…RC-6. Battery under `docs/build/fixtures/p1-records/`. *(REQ-XA-106)*

**Stage 2 — P1-B hardening run.**

7. **`hh-env` test** `crates/hh-env/tests/<id>.rs` over byte-copy fixtures (`crates/hh-env/tests/fixtures/p1/`), emitting `docs/build/reports/<date>-p1-surface-xcheck.json` and `<date>-p1-estimators.json` through `HH_P1_ESTIMATORS_EMIT` (plus an equivalent emit variable for the cross-check). The packets are emitted by the test, never hand-written. *(REQ-XA-110)*
8. **Cross-check** on basis A (inclusion rule REQ-XH-032), basis B and basis C. O build-stage tasks are reported separately. Slot counts are never treated as pairs. Agreement table, per-side discordance evidence, and a confound list naming `hf-model-inference` `inconclusive_with_receipt`. *(REQ-XA-107, REQ-XA-108, REQ-XH-032)*
9. **Estimator ladders** — `parity_admission` on the strict records-only and labelled design-probe rungs, for tranche 3 and for the cross-check. `kl_equivalence` with `margins_source: "pre-registration:none"`. Descriptive endpoints come from `stats::kl_directional_bound` with `inference: descriptive` and no `verdict` member. The margins slice is empty, and the §4.4 floors are stated. Each outcome is recorded with variant, Display text and rung; an outcome other than §2.3's prediction is recorded as emitted. *(REQ-XA-109, REQ-XA-111, REQ-XA-112, REQ-XA-113)*
10. **Intended vs served policy counts** per arm, with the analysis basis named, on every arm-bearing packet emitted here. This defines the field convention later rows reuse. *(REQ-XA-006)*
11. Wire RC-5 (fixture↔docs byte equality) and RC-6 (re-emission byte equality) in `p1_records.py` over the stage-2 outputs.
