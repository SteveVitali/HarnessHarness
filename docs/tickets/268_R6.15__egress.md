## Goal

Make the spec's single paid-cell network posture real: a TB 2.1 cell stays on podman `--network none` for its whole life with `net.mode = mediated`, reaching the host only through the two bind-mounted unix sockets `{model, egress}` that a statically linked `hh-cell-relay` (PID 1) bridges from in-cell loopback ports; per-phase host-side policy switches under the sealed `set_phase` schedule; the egress mediator decides every request with `decide_egress` and records it before any byte flows, including CONNECT tunnels; and the first-pass allowlist (Q-X55: apt, PyPI, npm, crates; no `github.com` in the agent phase; grading may reach its verifier's hosts) is frozen with a digest before any paid slot.
