## Goal

Stage 1, REPLAY ([§4.9](../experiments-spec.md#s4)), gives the proxy `record` and `replay` modes over one recording format: the ordered raw upstream exchanges of a slot plus the downstream request bytes. `replay` has no upstream port and no budget charge, and it has:
- a `RequestCanonicalizer` hook with built-ins `identity`, `json_canonical` (`Json::to_canonical_string`) and R6.10's `hh-l1-canon`;
- per-call match verdicts, strict and lenient modes, synthetic-sequence ingestion, and a typed end-of-sequence refusal;
- both capture points and a declared divergence policy.

A gateway `RecordedTransport` serves the same recordings to native runs. Every proxy-machinery acceptance test runs on fixture or replay upstreams (REQ-XC-125).

Stage 2, ARCHIVE (a) ([§4.10](../experiments-spec.md#s4), AR-20), defines the committed record of the archive: the `hh-receipt-index/1` schema (per-attempt `files`, pass-level `artifacts`, `scans`, supersession and tombstone entries) and a deterministic condenser that writes one `transcript.jsonl` per trial. A fixture proves that a fold recompute from archived store segments equals the committed packet, with no archive byte in git.

## In scope — deliverables

**Stage 1 — REPLAY** (commit checkpoint `R6.12 stage 1/2: replay` before Stage 2).
1. Modes `record` (live plus a sealed recording manifest) and `replay` (no upstream port and no budget charge by construction, receipts `mode: replay`), over one recording format. [REQ-XC-122, REQ-XD-108]
2. `RequestCanonicalizer` with built-ins `identity`, `json_canonical` (`Json::to_canonical_string`, never a second canonicalizer, CC1) and `hh-l1-canon`; per-call match verdict `{exact | canonical_equal | diverged{first_diff_path}}`. [REQ-XC-122]
3. Strict and lenient modes; synthetic-sequence ingestion (`provenance: synthetic`); a typed end-of-sequence refusal; both capture points (downstream, and upstream-bound with lane transforms applied in replay); a declared divergence policy (e.g. stop with a typed 409 `replay_diverged`); deterministic re-chunking when a harness requests streaming (P7). [REQ-XC-122]
4. A gateway `RecordedTransport` implementing `Transport` that decodes the same recorded bytes through the existing translators for native runs. [REQ-XC-122]
5. An offline-only check over the proxy machinery's tests: none reaches a live upstream; any live test is `#[ignore = "live — ADR-0363: scripts/live-verify.sh <leg>"]`. [REQ-XC-125]

**Stage 2 — ARCHIVE (a).**
6. The `hh-receipt-index/1` schema and validator: dataset repo, revision and visibility; per slot attempt, `files[{role ∈ raw_receipts|transcript|atif|egress_log|native_log|store_segment, path, sha256, bytes}]`; per pass, `artifacts[{role ∈ layer_export|bundle|binary_set, name, window, image_id or digest, path, sha256, bytes, upload ∈ archived|digest_only}]`; `scans{secret_scan_ref, contamination_scan_ref}`; supersession and tombstone entries. [REQ-XF-124]
7. A deterministic condenser producing one `transcript.jsonl` per trial from the receipts, with a tombstone hook the R6.23 scan uses for slot tokens. [REQ-XF-124]
8. Archive-outside-git machinery: index entries for every archived attempt and store segment; a fixture fold recompute from archived segments that equals the committed packet; a check that `git ls-files` holds no archive bytes; build-memory validator size caps unchanged. [REQ-XH-101]
9. The ADR for AR-20 (index, condenser, private-then-public dataset, per-window upload design). The upload-tool identity and version are left for R6.23's dated addendum.
