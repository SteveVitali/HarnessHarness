## Goal

Make §9.4–§9.6 true for enforcement and deployment: one shared `scripts/claims-lexicon.json` that both `check-position.sh` (behaviour byte-identical, C-25) and a new `scripts/check-site.sh` read; the fourteen `check-site.sh` legs and a `test-check-site.sh` battery in which each mutation (a)–(v) fails its named leg; the `ci-mode: both` CI step; and a separate, SHA-pinned, minimally-permissioned `pages.yml` that builds the public profile and deploys **only** pages pinned by a committed `hh-site-publication/1` record — so that until GATE-G22 nothing is public. It also lands the publication-gate checklist that GATE-G22 and GATE-G31 present.
