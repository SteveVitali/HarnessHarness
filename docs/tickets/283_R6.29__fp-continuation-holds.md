Make spec §10.1.4's first-pass runner rules mechanical and fixture-verified, reading cost and infrastructure fields only. ## In scope — deliverables

**Stage 1 — FP runner rules** (own AC block, tests and commit):

1. Projection: p̂_h = 1.2 × max(1.5 × mean_h, P90_h) over ≥ 10 measured trials in scope, else the binding prior; the step-fit test against root − consumed − Σ orphaned at `bounded.hi` − C × s; integer µ$ (REQ-XH-112).
2. Cost-only input manifest: any outcome field in a continuation input refuses typed (REQ-XH-112).
3. Checkpoint after round 2 and at each later round boundary: re-project, withhold the remaining slots of the lowest-priority arms (V5, then Claude Code, then Pi) as `withheld{budget}`; never a protected arm while an unprotected arm remains; a protected-pair breach withholds every remaining slot and P2-FP/P3-L2/P4-FP refuse primary inference with typed reasons; readout cost fields only; run rounds stay; every drop labelled "cost-triggered; cost correlates with failure length" (REQ-XH-112).
4. Per-arm caps for Pi, Claude Code and V5 as a µ$ formula table (planned slots × p̂_h), with expected-cost admission per REQ-XH-117; a failing slot and the arm's rest are `withheld{budget}`, no cell and never a scored refusal; the slice is reserved against the root only; protected arms bounded by root and checkpoint only; a dropped arm stays in its Holm family as unavailable (REQ-XH-117).
5. Spend enforcement by proxy reservation under the root cap with pre-declared caps; a capped harness's cost reported as a censored lower bound (REQ-XD-040 + REQ-XC-211's literal predicate).
6. Wall time: W_p with retry allowance and load, t̂_h = 1.1 × measured mean (≥ 3 trials) else the 1,040 s prior, HH-native and V5 using original mini-swe-agent's t̂; the D_max function over R6.14's ceilings (billed where reconciled, else ceiling; unauthorized Wc released except one restart-overflow reservation); drops in the same order at FP-E and round boundaries; O/H pairs admitted or `withheld{window_deadline}` together (REQ-XH-126).
7. Stop rules: budget, infrastructure, integrity (stops everything, keeps partial rows) and calendar only, with §6.6 thresholds; no outcome-based trigger exists (REQ-XD-042).

**Stage 2 — FP holds:**

8. Fallback: a trigger record (Q-X62) or a G-L1 both-partition failure record yields the hosted-only W4 schedule and `withheld{l1_not_passed}` for P3-L2 and P4-FP; never decided from an outcome field (REQ-XH-123).
9. Lane probe: a ≤ $0.05 driver exercising the HH-native lane through the proxy's loopback listener with one receipted call per pinned effort path, its `w4-lane-probe` leg registered in `scripts/live-verify.sh`; a failed probe, or ≥ 1 infra-failed slot among the first 3 HH-native or V5 slots, holds that arm as `withheld{admission:native_lane_hold}` while the hosted arms continue (REQ-XH-124; contributes REQ-XH-027).
10. PP0 holds: hold detection from infra members and AG3/AG5/AG6/AG8 evidence only; dispositions (a), (b) and (c) of REQ-XH-127, including the FP-E scope-decision record whose default ends the pass after W2, and adjacent-cell development replacement with a disclosure flag for R6.33 (REQ-XH-127).
11. Stage-tagged tests in the runner crate, run ledger, PR body.
