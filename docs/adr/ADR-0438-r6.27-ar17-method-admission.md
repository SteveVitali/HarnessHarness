- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** an explicit non-contract note — this ADR governs experiments-program method admission, not the canonical kernel contract ladder.

## Context

Experiments spec §6.4 lands five new method pieces beside the admitted §4.3 machinery: the single-arm finite-manifest rate (route (b)), the `slot_schedule_randomization` exchangeability spelling, the rerun/harness-swap flip estimators, $/solve, and the tied-with-leader descriptive route. §6.4 REQ-XD-023 requires an independent numerical and routing review and an operator method-admission gate before any confirmatory or first-pass use. §6.5 adds one explicit departure: **one Holm family per tranche**, departing from evidence-spec §4.3's single cross-strata family — needing a method-admission decision (Q-X40, recorded default: accept).

## Decision (proposed — presented to GATE-G23)

- **Admit** `comparative_protocol/1`, `comparative-quote/1`, the five new pieces and the per-tranche family, subject to the gate's review of: the independent numerical and routing review record (`docs/build/reports/2026-11-08-r6.27-method-review.md`), the known-answer test runs (`crates/hh-eval/tests/r6_19.rs`), the `comparative-battery/1` records (`docs/build/reports/2026-11-08-r6.27-comparative-battery.json` — stopped scenarios never counted), the first-pass T = 10 grid, and the method dossier index (`docs/build/reports/2026-11-08-r6.27-method-dossier.md`).
- **One Holm family per tranche** (α = .05, `{Δ(h − ref) : h ≠ ref}`, reference pre-registered, recorded default mini-swe-agent): each tranche is an independent cohort — later tranches may never be funded, so a program-wide family would hold unavailable members at worst-case mass forever (design/35 F8). Other pairs stay an exploratory BH family; unavailable members stay in the family.
- **Descriptive routes stay descriptive:** flips, $/solve and tied-with-leader never carry a confirmatory label (REQ-XD-022/031).
- Until a GATE-G23 PASSED record exists, every gated piece refuses `MethodNotAdmitted` — the refusal stays in force here (tested in `crates/hh-eval/tests/r6_27.rs`).

## Alternatives considered

- **Program-wide Holm family (the §4.3 default)** — binds every later tranche's members, including never-funded ones, into the first family; rejected (F8's recorded rationale).
- **Admit without the battery** — violates REQ-XD-024's simulation-before-confirmation rule; rejected.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit when GATE-G23 returns its decision (this proposal is admitted, refused, or amended there), or when a later-pass tranche proposes a cross-tranche pooled estimand (its contrasts form their own family per F8).
