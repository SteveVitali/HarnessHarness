# ADR-0224: S0.3 Stage-0 revalidation verdict — the ADR-0009/ADR-0050 ecosystem decision holds; no trigger fires (feeds GATE-G1)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Ticket:** S0.3
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.12.3 (measurement); OQ-131 (agentic/measurement acceptance); OQ-402 (connection bounds)
- **Spec:** §9.1 (Stage-0 gating acceptance + revalidation rule), §10.6; `research/synthesis/l1-spike-spec.md` §5; ADR-0050 §3/§4/§6 (trigger 5), ADR-0009 steps 5–8

## Context
Stage-0 acceptance requires evaluating ADR-0050 §6 trigger 5: *"any candidate's measured C5, C7 or
C12 score falls outside the assumed value ± 1, or any gate G1–G4 / M-S2-6 / M-S2-7 fails for the
winning candidate."* If it fires, the phase-synthesis agent (at GATE-G1) re-runs ADR-0009 steps
5–7 with the measured scores. The S0.3 spikes produced the numbers
(`docs/build/reports/S0.3-measurement-sheet.md`, method ADR-0223). This ADR records the verdict that
GATE-G1 consumes. It is the owned decision the ticket `## Notes` flags (OQ-131 acceptance).

## Decision
**The ADR-0009 / ADR-0050 ecosystem decision HOLDS. No revalidation trigger fires** (winning
candidate E5a = E1 kernel + E2 lab; measured offline/hermetic, N = 5 median):

- **Gates (winning candidate):** G1–G4 pass; M-S2-6 pass; M-S2-7 = 100 % → no gate-failure trigger.
- **C5** = 4 (CPU fan-out 1.58× → 4; cancel 4.25 ms → 5; tail p95 23 µs → 5; min governs) vs assumed
  5 → |Δ| = 1 → **within band**.
- **C7** = 4 strict (throughput 3 791 e/s → 4) / 5 per the `l1-spike-spec §5` note (C7 discriminates
  only footprint 3.74 MiB → 5 and start-up 10.6 ms → 5) vs assumed 5 → |Δ| ≤ 1 → **within band**.
- **C12** (E5a) — transport components favorable (per-run overhead ≈ 0.056 %, codegen 26 ms,
  hash-equality 100 %); this **does not contradict** the assumed value 2, whose polyglot penalty is
  deferred (DF-S0.3-2, re-scored at Stage 3). No C12 score is asserted from a transport-only
  measurement → **no C12 trigger**.

**Disposition:** the `unmeasured-by-spike` (`u`) marks on **C5** and **C7** for the E1 kernel are
**cleared** in the ADR-0050 amendment log; the **C12 `u` mark stays** (polyglot conjunct deferred to
Stage 3). Connection bounds (OQ-402) are set as MUST-data from M-S2-1/-2 and carried to S1.25.

## Consequences
- GATE-G1 opens with "decision holds, no re-run" as its input; it still evaluates whether the
  deferred cross-candidate / polyglot / official-SDK measurements (DF-S0.3-1/-2/-3) warrant an
  online spike budget before Stage 1. The compensating control is the ADR-0050 §4 kernel-family
  robustness (E1-kernel 0.979 of corners; survives every adversarial sweep) — the decision does not
  turn on the deferred non-winning candidates.
- Because trigger 5 does not fire, ADR-0009 steps 5–7 are **not** re-run; the frozen weights and the
  E5a-with-late-bound-surface decision stand unchanged.
- No claim about any harness is made at Stage 0 (§9.1); these are internal tier-B measurements (R5).

## Alternatives considered
- **Declare the trigger fired on the raw/durable fan-out (C5 → 3) or the favorable transport (C12 →
  5).** Rejected: both are measurement artifacts (host cores / fsync serialization; transport-only)
  — see ADR-0223 — and firing on them would misdirect a steps-5–7 re-run on false evidence.
- **Defer the whole verdict to GATE-G1.** Rejected: the ticket owns the trigger-5 evaluation
  (OQ-131); GATE-G1 reads out the verdict and decides the online-budget question, it does not
  perform the evaluation.

## Revisit trigger
The GATE-G1 phase-synthesis pass, or any close of DF-S0.3-1/-2/-3 (online spike budget, or the
Stage-3 E2-lab crossing), that yields a cross-candidate or polyglot C5/C7/C12 score differing from
ADR-0050 §3 by more than 1 — then re-run ADR-0009 steps 5–7 and amend ADR-0050.
