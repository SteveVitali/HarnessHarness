# ADR-0228: S1.1 delegates the per-kind IR-entity `classify_home` values to S1.4/§3.1.3 under the ADR-0216 interim ruling

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Ticket:** S1.1
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.1.1 (delegates the §3.1.3 slice of R-2.1.2 to S1.4)
- **Spec:** §2.2.3 (the IR-entity-kind `home` row, marked `[[DEFERRED ADR-0216: OQ-467]]`);
  §2.9.1/§2.9.4 (`classify_home`; the `home` table row canonical at §3.1.3); §2.9.8 (totality
  check); manifest §Requirement-ID index (R-2.1.2 → S0.2, **S1.4**, S3.2); ADR-0012 D2, ADR-0016,
  ADR-0216

## Context
AC-A2-1 requires "a `home` assignment for every entity kind, component class and event family
named anywhere in the spec," discharged by "§2.1–§2.4; §2.2.3; **§3.1.3 `classify_home` table**."
§2.2.3 lists the thirteen IR entity kinds (`Goal, Observation, ContextItem, Memory, Procedure,
ToolCapability, Permission, Effect, Artifact, Validator, AgentProcess, Budget, HarnessRule`) plus
the two leaves (`Text`, `CompiledPayload`) and states their home is "derived by
`classify_home(kind)`" with "the per-kind value table is **§3.1.3** (thirteen rows, one plane
each)" — and marks that table `[[DEFERRED ADR-0216: OQ-467 — no ratified ADR publishes the
per-kind `classify_home` table; ADR-0216 fixes the §3.1.3 rows as MUST-data placeholders until
WS-A2/WS-A3 ratify them]]`. §3.1.3 is part of **R-2.1.2 (the Harness IR)**, which the manifest
requirement index and chain assign to ticket **S1.4** (`008_S1.4__harness-ir-dialect.md`), not
S1.1. S1.1's Load block names §2 only; §2 crisply homes just two of the thirteen entity kinds in
prose (`ToolCapability`=P2, `Permission`=P6, glossary CF-037). The manifest rule is explicit:
"tickets inherit; they do not re-decide" deferred data.

## Decision
S1.1 lands the home-plane **rule** and the home **table** for the categories §2 fixes directly
(event families, plane operators, §2-named component classes, boundary components,
`run_lifecycle`) — over which `classify_home` is total (ADR-0227). It **does not publish** the
thirteen per-kind IR-entity plane values: those are the §3.1.3 MUST-data owned by **S1.4** and
held interim under **ADR-0216** (OQ-467). The fifteen entity kinds are registered as the
`IR_ENTITY_KINDS` roster (so the AC-A2-1 roster is present and an unregistered kind still errors),
with their plane assignment explicitly delegated. A DEFERRALS row records the obligation and its
compensating control.

## Consequences
- Good: S1.1 does not fabricate or re-decide ADR-0216-deferred data (respects the manifest
  inherit-don't-re-decide rule and CC10 ownership — the §3.1.3 per-kind table is S1.4's to own);
  `classify_home` is genuinely total over the domain S1.1 owns; the entity roster is present for
  AC-A2-1; the S1.1 spec-DAG and totality checks are green now.
- Cost: the *full* AC-A2-1 "every entity kind homed" is completed only when S1.4 lands §3.1.3;
  until then the per-kind entity planes are not machine-checkable here. Compensating control: the
  `IR_ENTITY_KINDS` roster + the totality check over the §2-fixed domain + the DEFERRALS row that
  S1.4 must close by publishing and testing the thirteen-row table.

## Alternatives considered
- **Hardcode the thirteen per-kind planes in S1.1.** Rejected: it re-decides ADR-0216-deferred
  MUST-data from §3.1.3 (out of S1.1's Load and out of scope; violates "tickets inherit, do not
  re-decide" and CC10 ownership), and §2 alone fixes only 2 of 13, so 11 would be guesses.
- **Make `classify_home` fail (`UnclassifiedKind`) for every entity kind.** Rejected: that would
  make the totality check red and misrepresent the entity kinds as unclassifiable rather than
  classified-elsewhere-later.

## Revisit trigger
When **S1.4** lands the HIR/1 §3.1.3 entity-and-edge catalogue: publish the thirteen-row per-kind
`classify_home` table, extend the ontology's totality check to cover every entity kind, flip the
S1.1 DEFERRALS row to `DONE` with evidence, and — when WS-A2/WS-A3 ratify OQ-467 by an
ADR-0048-class ruling — lift the ADR-0216 interim marker.
