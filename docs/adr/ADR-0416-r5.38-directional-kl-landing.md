## Context

REQ-D-304/305/306/307 bind §4.4: the finite-manifest H/O
equivalence test is a directional Bernoulli KL concentration bound,
never CP/binomial inversion or a credible interval; planned N is
preserved through the missingness/veto completion envelope; bounded
absolute and relative-mean contrasts use declared physical ranges
with outward rounding; equivalence is strict (.90 TOST) and an
inferential `not_equivalent` needs the separate .95 reporting
interval wholly outside the band under selected-claim multiplicity
(S11); one preregistered all-endpoints conjunction uses the IUT and
can never remove a required unknown or suppress the D2 efficacy
Holm. Before this ticket `hh-eval` had no KL inversion, no
completion envelope, no relative-mean branch, no IUT conjunction;
`hh-analysis` had no kind routing to them; `IntervalMethod` had no
spelling to declare the method.

## Decision

1. **`kl_directional_bound(x_ppm, n, eta_ppm)` is the math owner's
   one inversion.** In `stats.rs` — `ln` via sqrt-reduction plus the
   atanh series, deterministic under `+ − × ÷ √` only (the module
   contract: no libm transcendentals); continuous endpoint
   conventions `x=0 ⇒ [0, 1−η^{1/N}]`, `x=1 ⇒ [η^{1/N}, 1]`; ppm
   endpoints floored/ceiled **outward** so a stored endpoint never
   overstates coverage (S.2/S.3). Concentration, never CP.
2. **`directional_kl_equivalence` consumes declared pair records.**
   `filters.endpoints[]` carry `endpoint/stratum/required/kind/
   ranges/zero_reference/tasks/repeats/pairs[]`; each pair carries
   `task_id, repeat, pair_id, subject, phase, stratum, h, o`
   (absent = uncertain). Planned `N = T·R` equality, unique
   pair/subject identity, per-task equal repeats and declared
   stratum are validated; synthetic pairs are admissible inputs —
   they are not authentic H/O evidence (Packet E capture stays owed
   to R5.39/R5.47).
3. **Margins come only from the pre-registration.** The kernel arm
   decodes `PreRegistration.equivalence_margin` through the
   existing `benefits::registered_margins` (one decode, CC1); every
   endpoint binds one `dimension` — absolute for `binary`/`bounded`,
   relative for `relative`; absent/mismatched margins refuse.
4. **Binary endpoints use the c+/c− directional construction.** The
   TOST combines `L_Δ = L+−U−`, `U_Δ = U+−L−` at `η=.025` per
   endpoint (joint ≥.90); `u` uncertain pairs preserve N via
   `[K(c/N).L, K((c+u)/N).U]`; equivalence is **strict**
   `L_Δ > −ε ∧ U_Δ < ε`; the .95 reporting interval at `η=.0125`
   must lie wholly outside the band for `not_equivalent`, and a
   disjoint .90 alone lands a descriptive note, never the inference.
5. **Bounded endpoints normalize `(D−a)/(b−a)` and transform back**;
   `a=b` is exact; zero sample variance never proves zero range.
   **Relative endpoints** build `C− = E_H−(1−ρ)E_O` and
   `C+ = E_H−(1+ρ)E_O` per pair under the declared physical ranges,
   equivalence iff `L(C−)>0 ∧ U(C+)<0` at `η=.025`; the S11
   negative is `U(C−)<0` or `L(C+)>0` at `.025/m` per branch. A
   reference range containing zero requires the frozen `"absolute"`
   convention — a separately registered `{endpoint}:absolute`
   margin — or `ZeroReference` refuses.
6. **Selected-claim allocation divides the frozen levels by `m`.**
   Non-conjunction endpoints run at `η/m` (Bonferroni over the
   declared selected family); a declared `m` below the actual
   selected count is a family-identity refusal. The one declared
   IUT conjunction runs members at base levels; a member never
   supplied is `unknown_members[]` and blocks the conjunction, and
   a `required` endpoint absent from the member set refuses — a
   required unknown can never be removed or averaged away.
7. **Every refused inference is typed.** `KlEquivalenceError` —
   `MissingKey`, `PairIdentity`, `UnequalDesign`, `ReusedSubject`,
   `UnsupportedDependence`, `InvalidCounts`,
   `OutsideDeclaredRange`, `UnknownRange`, `UnboundedMeasure`,
   `ZeroReference`, `MissingMargin`, `MarginKindMismatch`,
   `MarginAuthority`, `UnsupportedVerdict`, `UnsupportedLevel`,
   `NonFiniteInput`, `UnknownMethod`, `MetadataOnly`,
   `FamilyIdentity`. `AnalysisError::KlEquivalence` surfaces it;
   `IntervalMethod::KlDirectional` ("kl_directional") is the
   additive D9 enum member declared on the kind — a cell
   declaration reports `estimator_undefined` like Packet D's.
8. **The routed kind reuses the R5.16/16b/37 records-in path**:
   `analyze` → `analyze_and_record` → persisted
   `analysis_report_body/1`; `record_kind` maps
   `directional_kl_equivalence`; a top-level `MetadataOnly` gate
   refuses the method on non-computing kinds. The S.1 metadata
   labels the interval `concentration_bound` at .90 — never
   confidence/credible.

## Consequences

- Spec §4.5's pinned widths reproduce exactly: zero-discordance
  N={10,30,150} → {.308497,.115703,.024293}; N=150 equal counts
  {1,3,8,38} → {.042944,.065355,.100601,.190899} (±≤3 ppm outward
  rounding), asserted in `crates/hh-analysis/tests/r5_38.rs`.
- The strict/outward-rounded boundary means a margin-touching
  interval is `inconclusive` at encoded resolution.
- Authentic H/O pair capture (Packet E), the signed dimension map,
  live-parity execution and the study report keep their owners —
  this ticket discharges engineered D/E only.

## Alternatives considered

- **Reading pairs from `ExperimentView` plans** — rejected: plans
  carry no H/O `pair_id`/subject identity (Packet E is unlanded,
  R5.39); fabricating the binding from arm cells would re-pair
  after observation.
- **Caller-supplied margins via filters** — rejected: F.4 and
  OQ-113 bind margins to `PreRegistration` only.
- **CP/score/MOVER or ratio-interval shortcuts** — rejected
  (F.10): concentration only; no observed-reference division,
  mean-of-task-ratios, clipping or invented bound.
- **A new report store or event class** — rejected: additive
  `analysis_report_body/1` member reuses the existing route.
