## Context

The first pass (Q-X47…Q-X55) departs from the general protocol in enumerated ways. AR-33 is the register item that records every such departure and presents it at the method-admission gate.

## Decision (proposed — presented to GATE-G23)

The first-pass departures, each declared in the pre-registration packet before any paid subject:

1. **One interleaved window with coupled O/H pairs** (Q-X52): every arm on the same 10 held-out band-A tasks at k = 5 in W4's one seeded interleaved schedule; each (task, replicate) O/H pair launched together (REQ-XH-111). At most 5 arms × 10 × 5 = 250 main-window trials.
2. **The shared phase-4 baseline and its concurrency with L2** (FP-6; Q-X57 pending): P4-FP departs from Q-X15, REQ-XD-215 and REQ-XD-220; both the shared-baseline design and the L2 concurrency are declared in the packets.
3. **The family-of-one P4 contrast**: Δ(V5 − baseline) alone forms its confirmatory family.
4. **A T = 10, H = 3 family below the §4.5 precision target**: P2-FP is a Holm family of two members at T = 10 — a pipeline demonstration whose confirmatory test is pre-stated as near-zero power (§10.1.3's table quoted before data; the REQ-XH-119 label).
5. **ε = ±.20 with the scoped label** (Q-X53, REQ-XH-122): the first-pass L2 margin is fixed in the pre-registration with the N = 50 P(`equivalent`) table; an `equivalent` earns only "first-pass L2 equivalent at a ±.20 margin (N = 50; gross-infidelity screen; not parity-verified)"; `inconclusive` yields "L1-conformant baseline".
6. **The cost-only checkpoint and arm priority** (REQ-XH-112, Q-X61 recorded default): no continuation rule reads an outcome field; the protected pair is original and HH-native mini-swe-agent; truncation lands on V5, then Claude Code, then Pi; cost-triggered drops carry the "cost correlates with failure length" label.
7. **Dependent claims, no cross-claim control** (RB-25): P2-FP, P3-L2 and P4-FP share trials; the dependency is declared, never corrected.
