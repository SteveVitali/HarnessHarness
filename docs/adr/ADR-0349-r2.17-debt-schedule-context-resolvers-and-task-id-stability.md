# ADR-0349: R2.17 bench/measurement legs — schedule-point `validate_removal_test` resolvers, `dead_weight_purpose` on `PreRegistration`, the derived grace leg, and `task_id` as the import-identity contract

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Ticket:** R2.17
- **Requirement ids:** DF-S1.24-1 · DF-S1.24-2 (machine cells) · BL-26 ·
  R-2.9.4 · R-2.9.8 · R-2.10.3 · AC-R-2.9.6-11/-12 · CC9 · T-LCD-07
- **Spec:** §5h.6 (assumption-debt manager: `DebtPolicy/1`, the
  `schedule_removal_test` row, `Deferred{reason}`) · §5h.4 (suite
  admission, `task_id`) · §6.2/§6.3 (the removal match shape) ·
  ADR-0214 (proposed policy defaults) · ADR-0126 (dead-weight design
  admissibility) · ADR-0321 (the manager's settled rulings).
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** C0 — every deliverable runs at the
  offline/fixture ceiling: the schedule context checks execute over the
  production `LabDocs`/`records-in` resolvers and the recorded
  `benchset/stage3_v1` corpus; the live foreign-manifest import stays
  environment-gated under R2.21 (HUMAN-H3).

## Context

R2.17 discharges the two rowed DF-S1.24 residuals. Five rulings were
the ticket's to make:

1. **Where the resolver-backed `validate_removal_test` context checks
   bind.** The member-level battery landed at Stage 1
   (`RemovalTestContext::member_level()` at HIR `validate` and registry
   admission); the context-parameterized members — template resolution,
   match-spec presence, split assignment, artifact sealing, runway and
   owner-reachability under the operative `DebtPolicy` — had no
   production call site. The schedule point is where a removal test
   becomes a real `ExperimentSpec`, so that is where the checks must
   run; the registry admission stays member-level by design (the
   registry holds no experiment specs — its comment already says the
   resolution-dependent refusals land with the experiment registry).
2. **How `dead_weight_designs_allowed` binds.** `DebtPolicy/1` carries
   the admissibility flag (ADR-0126), but nothing declared dead-weight
   *purpose* on the design side — the policy had no datum to gate on.
3. **How `grace_period_ms` becomes load-bearing.** `evaluate_debt`'s
   `retired`-profile-change hard leg trusted a caller-projected
   `grace_elapsed` flag — a policy member a caller could assert without
   the policy ever executing.
4. **How `template_ref` resolves at the boundary.** `lab.debt.sweep`
   entries are records-in; a caller who only carries the record's
   `template_ref` had no resolution path — the sweep deferred
   `template_unresolved` even when the spec was registered.
5. **What `task_id` round-trip stability means.** `Text::to_json` is
   deliberately hash-addressed (raw `content` never emitted), so a
   naive whole-record `to_json → from_json ==` equality is not the
   contract; and `from_json` decodes strictly but does *not* verify the
   id — `check_id()` is the importer's identity check.

## Decision

- **D1 — the schedule point runs the context battery under the
  resolvers it honestly has.** `hh_debt::schedule::instantiate_check`
  (invoked inside `schedule_removal_test` and per-member inside
  `schedule_batch`) builds the `RemovalTestContext` from production
  inputs: `policy`/`declared_sinks` from the operative `DebtPolicy`
  (`InsufficientRunway`, `OwnerUnreachable`), `resolve_template` from
  the caller-resolved `ExperimentSpec` (records-in — the boundary
  resolves `template_ref` through `LabDocs`'s `put_spec`/`put_named`
  deposit, per D4), `split_assigned` from the resolved template's
  `suite.split_assignment_ref` member, `artifact_sealed` from every
  arm's `artifact_ref.version_id` under the one §5h.2 §2 pin spelling
  (`hh_ontology::eval::is_pinned_version_id`, now public — CC1).
  `probe_ref_known`/`zero_use_scope_resolved` stay absent at schedule:
  those kinds defer `static_kind` before ever reaching the context
  check; `diff_removed_rules` is the settle/propose-side check.
  `validate_removal_test_at(record, Option<&DebtHome>, …)` is the
  home-free entry point — `SweepEntry.home` is caller-projected and may
  be absent; the *verdict* never degrades, only the refusal detail text.
- **D2 — `PreRegistration.dead_weight_purpose` declares the design's
  purpose.** Additive optional member on `PreRegistration/1` (default
  `false`; emitted only when true — `/1` bodies untouched). The
  schedule point gates on it: a resolved template whose design declares
  dead-weight purpose under `dead_weight_designs_allowed = false`
  defers `dead_weight_design_disallowed`. The policy flag now has the
  datum ADR-0126's admissibility rule requires.
- **D3 — `ProfileChange.changed_at_ms` makes the grace leg a policy
  fact.** `evaluate_debt` derives
  `changed_at_ms + policy.grace_period_ms <= now_ms`, OR-ed with the
  caller's `grace_elapsed` flag (records-in compatibility); the live
  seam (`hh-embed::evaluate_debt_on_lifecycle`) stamps `changed_at_ms`
  at the transaction time so a later sweep answers under the operative
  policy, never the caller's claim alone.
- **D4 — `lab.debt.sweep` resolves `template_ref` through `LabDocs`;
  records-in stays authoritative.** `resolve_entry_templates` fills
  `entries[].template` from `LabDocs` only when the caller did not
  project it — `docs.spec(template_ref)` (the content-addressed
  `lab.experiment.*` deposit) then `docs.get_named(SPEC, template_ref)`
  (the `spec:`-named deposit). A miss stays `template_unresolved`; a
  store entry that fails decode is a schema violation, never a
  fabricated spec.
- **D5 — `task_id` is the import-identity contract; the canonical form
  is the round-trip.** `check_id()` (`task_id == semantic_id()`) is the
  admission predicate — a forged id decodes under the strict codec but
  never verifies. The corpus legs assert canonical-JSON round-trip +
  semantic-id recomputation + annotation-invariance (foreign identity,
  `validity`/`contamination` audit members, provenance, `ext` never
  feed `task_id`) — the partition a real import relies on (CF-450). The
  *live* foreign-manifest import stays the R2.21 environment-gated
  residual; nothing here fakes it.

## Consequences

- Every `DebtPolicy` member now has a runtime consumer:
  `required_fields_by_home` (`validate_for_home`), `min_runway_ms`
  (schedule-point `insufficient_runway`), `hypothesized_max_age_ms`
  (probation + evaluate), `evidence_max_age_ms` (stale-evidence warn),
  `warn_within_ms` (the `expiring` window + debt index), `grace_period_ms`
  (the derived hard leg), `max_open_removal_tests` (the bound),
  `priority` (closed-set at register *and* schedule), `notice_sinks`
  (the declared sink table at schedule + `route_notices`), `schedule`
  (non-empty at register + the `sweep.completed` `cadence` attribution),
  `dead_weight_designs_allowed` (the D2 gate).
- Context misses are `Deferred{reason}` data — new `DeferredReason`
  spellings `unexecutable:<reason>`, `insufficient_runway`,
  `owner_unreachable`, `dead_weight_design_disallowed` — never silent
  drops. A batch member failing the context check makes the batch
  `BatchIncompatible` (the caller re-batches without it).
- The `DebtManagerRecord::validate` admission now refuses an empty
  `policy.schedule` and a `priority` spelling outside
  `PRIORITY_SPELLINGS` — fail fast at register, never at the sweep.
- Fixtures whose `owner.reach_via` declared sinks the operative policy
  did not declare had to declare `sink:ops` — the correct tightening,
  not a regression.
- `PreRegistration` gains a member — additive, decode-tolerant; old
  bodies decode, new bodies omit it when `false`.

## Alternatives considered

- **Registry admission resolves templates.** Rejected — the component
  registry does not hold experiment specs (records-in boundary); its
  member-level admission already documents that the
  resolution-dependent refusals land with the experiment registry and,
  now, the manager's schedule point.
- **`grace_elapsed` flag only (no `changed_at_ms`).** Rejected — the
  operative policy would never execute; a caller could assert grace
  elapsed under any policy. The derived leg makes the member
  load-bearing while the OR keeps records-in callers working.
- **Drop-or-error on a context check failure.** Rejected — §5h.6 §5's
  failure row requires a `Deferred{reason}` the sweep reports; a silent
  skip or a hard error would either hide the record or fail the whole
  sweep for one bad member.
- **Verify `task_id` inside `from_json`.** Considered — but `from_json`
  is the strict codec (parse, not admit); the importer's `check_id` is
  the admission predicate. Faking verification in the codec would hide
  the compute-vs-trust distinction the spec draws.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen when R2.21 lands the live foreign-manifest import (the corpus
legs generalise to real manifests) or when a `DebtPolicy` member gains
a second legitimate runtime binding and the schedule-point context
needs re-anchoring; likewise if `PreRegistration/2` re-scopes
`dead_weight_purpose` into a first-class design member.
