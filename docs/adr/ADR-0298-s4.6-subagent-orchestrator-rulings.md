# ADR-0298: S4.6 subagent and orchestrator rulings

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Ticket:** S4.6
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.6.3, R-2.6.3¹, R-2.1.6¹
- **Spec:** §05e.3; §08.2; ADR-0185…0193; ADR-0067; ADR-0138

## Context

S4.6 had to land the kernel-owned child-spawn path and the C3 orchestrator
without introducing a second delegation path, copying child context into the
parent, or hiding merge and crash-recovery ambiguity. The same ticket also
owned the producer halves deferred by DF-S2.5-2: parent/child spawn anchors
and child terminal heads that the checkpoint verifier can independently
recompute.

## Decision

- `hh-subagent::spawn` is the only spawn implementation. The C3
  `hh-orchestrator` plans and stages topology runs, but every admitted stage
  is lowered to an ordinary `SubagentSpec` and admitted through the same
  kernel `spawn` steps 1–8.
- The child run id is deterministic over `(decision, H(spec))`. A
  `control.subagent.spawned` row is appended before `open_run_with_id`, and
  replay adopts durable budget, ownership, environment, subscription and
  child-run effects instead of minting duplicates.
- `EnvDeriver::derive_for` accepts `derived_for = child_run_id` so a crash
  and retry after environment derivation adopts the durable child handle.
- A child manifest carries `parent_run_id`, `spawn_event`, inherited
  attendance/envelope/audit policy fields and `parent_anchor`. The spawned
  event stamps `scope.child_run_id`; `result` and `cancelled` close that
  scope. A child inherits `signer_key_ids`, and its `parent_anchor` names the
  parent's `parent_head_at_spawn` citation. When a final checkpoint exists,
  `child_head` on `result`/`cancelled` cites the checkpoint-covered tip and
  its `checkpoint_ref`; otherwise it cites the child's current durable head.
- Child results re-enter only as a typed `subagent_result` payload carrying
  refs, artifacts, claims, usage and provenance. There is no transcript or
  raw model-I/O member.
- Merge is explicit and audited. `single_writer` and `parent_decides` are
  the implemented policies; conflicts, absent children and lost writes are
  retained on the `MergeReport`, and unowned or unaccounted writes veto
  rather than being resolved silently.
- Peer traffic is parent-mediated. Sends produce `control.message.sent` or
  `control.message.refused`, mediated delivery scans durable inboxes, and
  receiver-side wakeups use `peer_message` occurrences.
- The topology catalogue declares T0–T7 as a closed set. This slice admits
  T0, T1, T2 and T6; T3, T4, T5 and T7 parse but refuse as unsupported.
- `lab/delegation-v1` executes topology arms through the same spawn path and
  emits conditional comparison reports only when the match/budget
  specification is complete and commensurable.

## Consequences

Crash recovery can reconstruct or terminate an interrupted spawn without
double effects, while audit consumers can connect the parent's spawned edge
to the child's run and terminal checkpoint. The C1 kernel slice remains
usable without depending on the orchestrator crate, and the orchestrator
remains the single normal consumer of the C1 surface. Unsupported topology
presets fail typed rather than silently approximating another topology.

The child-run producer has to maintain the durable `scope.child_run_id` and
anchor members on every terminal emitter. Any future topology that changes
the wait, messaging, ownership or merge semantics must still enter through
`SubagentSpec` and the kernel spawn path.

## Alternatives considered

- **Let the orchestrator open child runs directly.** Rejected: it would
  create a second authority/budget/recovery path and weaken the one-spawn
  invariant.
- **Adopt only child-run ids on replay.** Rejected: it leaves a crash window
  in which durable budget, ownership or environment effects exist without a
  child run or subscription.
- **Cite the unsigned child tip unconditionally.** Rejected for runs with a
  final checkpoint: the reciprocal audit obligation is to cite the signed
  checkpoint-covered head.
- **Implement all topology presets with internal aliases.** Rejected:
  recursive, judge-panel, ensemble and hosted-peer presets have semantics
  outside this slice; typed refusal is safer than silently degrading them.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen when any of T3, T4, T5 or T7 is implemented, when hosted children
need process-side checkpoint custody distinct from the parent signer set,
or when a new merge policy changes conflict or lost-write accounting.
