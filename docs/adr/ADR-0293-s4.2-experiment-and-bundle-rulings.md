# ADR-0293: S4.2 experiment & bundle rulings — `inference_budget` on the arm, S9's arm-only `results.arm`, reachable `superseded`, pool-backed `fetch`, embed-side scoped assembly, foreign import at `kernel.import`

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Ticket:** S4.2 (`063_S4.2__experiment-service.md`)
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.10.3 (the §6.3 C1 service delta) · R-2.9.3 (the
  §5h.3 C1 bundle/lifecycle delta)
- **Spec:** §6.3 (experiment engine: claims, pools, hosted participants,
  pause/resume, close) · §5h.3 (bundle kinds, S1–S9, status ladder,
  import/export, reproduce, diff/fetch/lineage); owning ADRs ADR-0184
  (experiment routing) · ADR-0191 (bundle S-stages) · ADR-0204 (the
  matched-budget M1–M3 ladder)

## Context

Stage 4's second ticket lands the multi-worker experiment engine and the
reproducible-bundle C1 surface at once: pool limits, hosted arm launches,
scoped `arm`/`experiment`/`lineage` bundles, the S9 cross-section stage,
the eight-status lifecycle, and foreign/native import. Each ruling below
is the owned call the ticket made where the spec's machine text left the
shape open — none of them is a spec deviation.

## Decision

1. **`ArmSpec.inference_budget: Option<ContentAddress>`** — the arm pins
   its model-plane budget as a content-addressed budget document, added
   as a trailing optional field (additive; every prior initializer
   compiles with `None`). `matched_total` resolves M3 as the three-term
   intersection `{declared, consumed, inference_budget}`; an arm that
   omits the field is unconstrained on that term, never silently zero.
   The field rides `ArmSpec` (not `Factor`) because the matched-budget
   check reads it per arm at close and per comparand group at compare
   time — the factor levels stay pure configuration.

2. **S9 `results.arm` is required on `kind = arm` bundles only.** R-2.9.3
   binds arm-level result invariants ("every arm's rows land under the
   bundle's declared arm") to the arm bundle — the scope that *has* arms.
   A `run` bundle is checked against `declaration.members[]` and its
   `run_id` capture only; a bare `run_id` on an arm bundle (no
   `results.arm`) is `MissingArmResult` — the containment check is
   scoped, not weakened.

3. **`may_transition_to`: `superseded` is reachable from every other
   state.** §5h.3's status vocabulary text marks `superseded` terminal —
   the text describes the *destination*, not the sources; blocking it
   would make `kernel.supersede` unreachable and orphan the
   `superseded_by` chain. Retraction stays reachable from every state
   except `superseded` (nothing supersedes a void claim, and a
   superseded claim is retracted on the successor).

4. **`kernel.fetch` is pool-backed; remote transports are deferred.**
   `manifest.locations[]` entries carrying a `pool` blob path materialise
   `bundle/` + `pool/`; `fetch_by_digest` treats the pool alias as a
   location too (the transport marker prefix the location grammar
   accepts). Remote transports (`http:`, `ipfs:`, signed-URL carriers —
   the WS-L6 trust-field half the spec parks) refuse `Refused{fetch =
   transport_pending}` rather than fabricate bytes — **DF-S4.2-1**.

5. **Scoped bundles assemble at the boundary.** `kernel.bundle{kind,
   contains[]}` validates `kind ∈ {arm, experiment, lineage}`, expands
   `contains[]` version ids against the blob pool + manifest store at the
   embed layer (the embed service owns the fs layout — same embedded-
   authority pattern as `kernel.import` over `lift_foreign`), re-validates
   S9 against the *assembled* manifest, then persists under
   `bundles/scoped/<version_id>/`. The pool stays keyed by content
   address so `fetch`/`export`/`import` need no scoped-aware branches.
   `kernel.bundle` with no `kind` is the unchanged run path.

6. **`lab.experiment.close` returns `bundle_id`.** The engine's `close`
   stays pure `ExperimentReport`; the embed boundary composes the
   experiment-bundle assembly on success and stamps `bundle_id` on the
   wire result. Composition at the boundary — not inside the engine —
   keeps `hh-experiment` free of the fs/blob-pool dependency (CC6) and
   makes the assembled-artifact link a ledger-visible consequence of the
   op that produced it.

7. **`kernel.import` is the general import head.** `format =
   ledger_native` takes the prior decoded-bundle `lift()` arm;
   `format ∈ FOREIGN_FORMATS` collects `{path|files[]}` inputs, runs
   `lift_foreign` (hosted participant class, `unverified` authority,
   `import(...)` origin, foreign digest claims + explicit conflicts),
   persists members/blobs, and carries the `ImportRecord`/`MappingReport`
   coordinates on the receipt row. Unknown formats refuse typed; the
   remaining declared foreign vocabularies (`swebench_submission`,
   `inspect_eval_log`, `in_toto_bundle`, `telemetry_trace`) stay
   unlifted — **DF-S4.2-2**.

## Consequences

- The M3 `inference_budget` term lets a `matched_total` arm bound the
  model plane without a harness-level budget — required for
  `value-of-compute-v1`'s iso-cost companion to refuse honestly rather
  than compare unbounded arms.
- A scoped bundle's S9 runs twice (assemble-time against the embedded
  manifest, validate-time against bytes) — duplicated work, but the
  assemble-time check is what keeps an invalid scope out of the pool
  under a real `version_id`.
- Embed-side scoped assembly means `hh-bundle` stays fs-free; the embed
  service is the only crate that knows the store layout — one place for
  the authority seam.
- `superseded`-from-any-state means the gate is evidence (the successor
  pointer), not provenance — a retracted bundle can still be named as a
  predecessor, which is the honest lineage.
- `fetch` covers exactly what the local store can prove; a remote
  `locations[]` member on an imported foreign bundle refuses typed until
  DF-S4.2-1 lands.

## Alternatives considered

- **`ArmSpec.inference_budget` as a `Factor` member** — rejected: budget
  is a per-arm constraint consumed by matched accounting, not a
  varied dimension; a factor would admit `Factor{budget, levels}` grids
  the engine would have to refuse as meaningless.
- **`results.arm` required on every scoped kind** — rejected: a
  `run`-kind scoped assembly (multi-run experiment) legitimately has no
  single declared arm; the requirement is arm-scoped.
- **`fetch` refusing `stage_pending` wholesale** — rejected: the
  blob-pool half is implementable now and `import`→`fetch` on the local
  store is the round-trip the AC exercises; a blanket refusal would
  park a landed capability on a transport half it doesn't need.
- **Engine-side bundle assembly at `ExperimentEngine::close`** —
  rejected: injects the fs/blob-pool seam into `hh-experiment`, coupling
  the pure scheduling engine to store layout; the embed boundary already
  owns that seam.

## Revisit trigger

DF-S4.2-1/2 landing (remote fetch transports or the remaining foreign
vocabularies) reopens ruling 4/7's deferral rows; a spec amendment
widening `results.arm` to non-arm kinds or hardening `superseded`'s
source set reopens rulings 2/3; a second caller needing scoped assembly
inside `hh-experiment` itself reopens ruling 6.
