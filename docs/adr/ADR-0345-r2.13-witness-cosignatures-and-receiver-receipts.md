# ADR-0345: R2.13 audit residual — the witness-cosignature construction, `witness_policy` on the manifest, and the receiver-receipt lift rule

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Ticket:** R2.13
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.8.6 · DF-S1.15-3 · DF-S1.15-1 · BL-21
- **Spec:** §5g.6 §2 (the audit checkpoint contract and its C2 extensions —
  witness cosignatures, receiver-attested receipts) · §5a.2 (the effect
  lifecycle rows a receipt binds to) · ADR-0067 (the C2 witness/receipt
  rulings this ticket lands) · ADR-0068 (GC/redaction tombstoning) ·
  ADR-0307 (the rotation claim `rehash`/`bridge_record_ref` members the
  witnessed rotation covers) · ADR-0331 D3 / OQ-170 (signer custody stays
  external)
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** C2 — the witness-cosignature and
  receiver-receipt halves are the spec's named C2 extensions; everything
  here runs at the offline/fixture ceiling with custody external.

## Context

R2.13 discharges DF-S1.15-3's two remaining C2 members — witness
cosignatures over audit checkpoints and receiver-attested receipts —
leaving per-event blob encryption honestly deferred (no conforming
pure-std AEAD mechanism exists in the workspace; inventing one would
fabricate cryptography the same way a live-verified claim would
fabricate evidence). The landed machinery already had most of the
substrate: `unsigned_checkpoint` already excluded `witness_cosignatures`
from the canonical preimage (the C2 shape was anticipated at R-2.8.6's
first landing), the `AuditSigner`/`AuditKeyResolver` custody seams
existed, and `Store::gc`/`Store::redact` were already durable-before-
delete. What the ticket owned was four design rulings:

1. **The cosignature's byte construction.** The spec says witnesses
   produce *timestamped signatures over the same note* — the exact byte
   string was an owned choice (the C2SP tlog-cosignature shape is the
   cited construction, S-320, but it does not pin the serialization).
2. **Where the witness quorum is declared.** §5g.6 §2 puts
   `witness_policy` inside `AuditPolicy`; the build has no standalone
   `AuditPolicy` record — `audit_policy_ref` names an external one the
   kernel cannot parse. The declaration had to land somewhere durable
   and kernel-readable.
3. **The verification posture for cosignatures.** Primary signatures
   already take the honest posture: shape-check always, value-check
   when a resolver is held, never a fabricated pass. Cosignatures need
   the same lattice, plus the quorum rule — and the mint must not emit
   a claim its own declared policy cannot substantiate.
4. **The receipt lift rule.** Receipts are external evidence: the
   kernel must record them durably and bind them to the run's folds
   without ever claiming the receiver's attestation was verified.

## Decision

**D1 — the cosignature construction.** A witness cosignature is
`hmac-sha256(witness_key, timestamp_ms ‖ checkpoint_sig_preimage)` —
the witness's timestamp (big-endian milliseconds) prepended to the
*same* canonical unsigned-claim bytes the primary signer signs
(`tree::witness_sig_preimage` is the one construction, mint and verify
share it — CC1). The durable entry is the C2SP tlog-cosignature shape
`witness_cosignatures[{witness_name, key_id, timestamp, sig}]` on the
checkpoint payload. Reusing the existing `hmac-sha256` construction
keeps custody symmetric with the primary signer: the manifest declares
the admissible pairs, the resolver seam supplies key bytes, and no key
material ever enters the ledger. `unsigned_checkpoint` already dropped
`witness_cosignatures`, so witnesses sign exactly the note the kernel
signed — including the rotation claim's `identity_profile`/`rehash`
members, which makes witnessed rotation a non-special case.

**D2 — `witness_policy` is a `RunManifest` member.**
`witness_policy{required, witnesses[{witness_name, key_id}]}` rides the
manifest: durable from seq 0, inside every checkpoint's covered prefix,
parsed by the same `RunManifest` codec — the honest stand-in for the
spec's `AuditPolicy.witness_policy` while `audit_policy_ref` remains an
external reference the kernel does not dereference. `required` counts
*distinct* `witnesses[].key_id`s whose cosignature must verify on every
`security.audit.checkpoint`. The member is additive/optional: absent
declares no requirement and pre-C2 manifests are untouched.

**D3 — one shared check, three honest surfaces.**
`audit::check_witness_cosignatures(claim, policy, keys)` is the single
check: every entry carries `{witness_name, key_id, timestamp, sig}`;
each `(witness_name, key_id)` pair must be declared; with a resolver
each `sig` is value-checked against `timestamp ‖ note` (an unresolvable
declared witness key is a custody gap, not a pass); the distinct-key
count must reach `required`. Its `WitnessDefect` vocabulary maps onto
each surface's own reporting — `verify_run` → `Tampered`
(`BadSignature`; `CheckpointInvalid` on quorum-unmet;
`SignerUnavailable` on an unresolvable declared key),
`Auditor::check_claim` → `AuditFault` (the policy comes from the
*durable* manifest the auditor itself folded — a caller-supplied
policy would let verification weaken the run's own requirement),
`audit_view` → the per-checkpoint `witness_status` lattice
(`verified`/`unverified`/`failed`/`n/a{no witness_policy declared}`,
folded into `verification_status` the same way primary signatures are).
The mint side refuses a sub-quorum or undeclared supply with
`SignerUnavailable` — the same "never sign a head the kernel cannot
substantiate" posture as `InconsistentHead`. The public ops are
`checkpoint_witnessed` / `rotation_checkpoint_witnessed`; the pre-C2
ops keep their signatures and pass an empty witness slice.

**D4 — the receipt lift rule.** `lifecycle.ledger.receipt` is a
kernel-origin audit-grade row
`{effect_id, observed_ref?, receiver_ref, receipt_id, attested_at,
attestation{key_id, alg_ref, sig}, status, supplied_by}` + the optional
`claim_ref` content ref (the receiver's claim document lives
blob-side). `Store::record_receipt` lifts the record *as supplied*:
`status` is fixed `unverified` by the codec — no receipt row can carry
a verification claim the kernel did not make (T-LCD-07 — receiver
authority never exceeds `external`). `effect_id` must name an effect in
the run's durable fold (`UnknownEffect`); `observed_ref`, when carried,
must name this run's `action.effect.observed` row for that effect;
`receipt_id` is the idempotence key (identical re-record returns the
existing row; same-id/different-members refuses). The boundary op is
`lab.receipt.record` — receipts are a capability lift over the one
`hh-embed/1` boundary, not a native-contract change (ADR-0067's
capability ruling).

## Consequences

- A run that declares `witness_policy` cannot mint a checkpoint
  (ordinary *or* rotation) without quorum — the refusal is mint-time,
  so no sub-quorum claim ever reaches the durable prefix. Existing
  manifests declare no policy and are unaffected (CC8).
- `verify_run`, `audit_with`, and `audit_view` all recompute witness
  status against the *durable* declaration — the quorum is covered by
  the checkpoints' own hash chain; a post-hoc policy edit is a manifest
  mutation, which the store does not admit.
- Receipt rows are evidence records, not verdicts: their `status` is
  `unverified` forever, `audit_view` reports each row's *binding*
  (`bound`/`unbound`) and never upgrades the receipt itself. Receiver
  key custody and receipt verification semantics stay external — the
  same open obligation as signer custody (OQ-170).
- Per-event blob encryption is unchanged: DF-S1.15-3 keeps it OPEN with
  a dated note — the honest gap, not a fabricated mechanism.

## Alternatives considered

- **A standalone `AuditPolicy` durable record** carrying
  `witness_policy`: rejected — the manifest is already the durable,
  seq-0, checkpoint-covered declaration vehicle; a second record would
  need its own custody/amendment story for zero extra integrity (the
  manifest is inside the covered prefix already).
- **Witness signatures over only `tree_head`** (a smaller note):
  rejected — cosigning the full unsigned note binds the witness to
  *what* was claimed (size, anchors, prev link, rotation rehash), not
  just a digest; that is the C2SP cosignature semantics and costs one
  canonicalization the mint already computes.
- **Receipts carrying caller-asserted `status`:** rejected — a
  boundary-supplied `verified` would be a fabricated kernel claim; the
  fixed `unverified` member is the honest lift.
- **Deferring receipts to a plugin-side record only:** rejected — the
  spec wants the lift durable in the run ledger where audit
  completeness can account for it; `audit_view`'s `receipts` fold is
  exactly that accounting.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen if (a) a conforming pure-std AEAD lands and per-event encryption
graduates (the blob-confidentiality half may interact with what
receipts' `claim_ref` may name), (b) real signer/witness key custody
lands and the `hmac-sha256` construction is replaced (OQ-170 closure —
cosignature verification is construction-agnostic at the codec level,
but `CHECKPOINT_ALG` spelling would change), or (c) a standalone
`AuditPolicy` record is introduced and `witness_policy` should migrate
to it with a manifest-compat story.
