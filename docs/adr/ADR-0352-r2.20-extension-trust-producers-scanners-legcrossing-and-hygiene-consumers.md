# ADR-0352: R2.20 extension-trust producers — declared-source scanners, the LegCrossing runtime checks, and the hygiene consumers

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Ticket:** R2.20
- **Requirement ids:** DF-S1.23-2 · BL-28 · R-2.8.5 · R-2.12.2 · CC1 ·
  CC2 · CC5 · CC10
- **Spec:** §5g.5 (extension supply-chain trust — the `discover →
  resolve → review → seal → activate → update → revoke` lifecycle, the
  three-leg `ExtensionTrustRecord`, L1–L6, the `security.extension.*`
  audit family) · §5g.2 (audit-grade payload rules — events are
  refs-and-spellings, never content).
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** C1 — every leg executes at the
  offline/fixture ceiling: source listings are caller-supplied
  `ScanEntry` rows, resolve/install/seal/activate are pure folds over
  the caller's inputs, and the minted `security.extension.*` payloads
  ride results for the caller's fenced writer. No filesystem walk,
  marketplace listing or endpoint inventory is performed by the pure
  layer — and none is claimed.

## Context

DF-S1.23-2 (BL-28) owed the producer half of §5g.5: the audit-grade
classes `security.extension.{resolved, quarantined, sealed, loaded}`
were declared in `hh-ledger::classes` since S1.23 but had **no
producers** anywhere (the S4.14a emitter slice covered
`install_requested`, `install_completed`, `drift` and `revoked`); no
`DeclaredSource` scanner produced `ExtensionRef`s (discovery was
declaration — refs arrived authored in the assembly); the
`LegCrossing`/L3 claims-vs-grants checks ran only at record-validation
time, not at resolve/install runtime; and the `TextHygieneReport` the
resolver minted was advisory — nothing consumed it. Five rulings were
the ticket's to make:

1. **What a "scanner" is at this layer.** A real filesystem walk or a
   marketplace/endpoint listing is I/O the pure crate never performs
   (CC5 — the same seam `FetchOutcome` occupies at resolve). The owed
   behaviour is the *trust leg* of enumeration: source allowlist,
   containment under declared roots, credential-free locators, and the
   merge-policy fold — not the walk itself.
2. **Where `MergePolicy` lives.** It was declared in
   `hh-assembly::grammar` (the only consumer at S1.23). The scanner —
   a `hh-registry` type — now applies it when folding listings into
   refs. Two spellings of one enum is a CC1 defect; one owner had to
   be chosen.
3. **Which manifest members lift to `declared_claims`, and what a
   leg crossing is.** §5g.5 §3 names `allowed-tools`, tool annotations
   and permission manifests as the claim sources; L3 says a claim
   dressed as a conferral (a pin/`grant`/`version_id`-shaped value)
   is the crossing. Whether that check runs only at
   `validate_extension_record` or also at mint time decides whether a
   hostile manifest can ever produce a record.
4. **How the `TextHygieneReport` is consumed.** `TrustRootPolicy`
   has carried a declared-but-unwired `scanner_policy` member since
   S4.1. The consumption postures and their meet were the open
   ruling — including what `unavailable` means (it must never be
   `clean`).
5. **What `seal`/`activate` produce.** The spec's lifecycle table
   wants `security.extension.sealed{extension_id,
   trust_snapshot_ref}` and `security.extension.loaded{extension_id,
   isolation, pin_check, pin attestation}`; `activate` additionally
   re-runs the surface pin-vs-live check for `surface_pin`ned records
   under the landed `SurfaceDriftPolicy` vocabulary (S4.14a's
   `notify|pause_surface|terminate` — the spec table's
   `refuse|quarantine_changed|accept_schema_only` spellings predate
   the landed enum; the landed spellings stay — append-only).

## Decision

- **D1 — the scanner is a pure fold over caller-supplied listings.**
  `lifecycle::scan_declared_sources(&[(DeclaredSource, Vec<ScanEntry>)],
  &TrustView, MergePolicy) → Result<Vec<ExtensionRef>, RegistryError>`
  takes the enumeration as input (the walk/listing/inventory is the
  caller's I/O — CC5) and performs the trust legs: the live
  `allowed_sources` union gates each *source* (`SourceNotAllowed`,
  fail-open only when no live policy declares the member — the landed
  `discover`/`resolve` convention); filesystem-shaped members are
  contained under their declared roots (`directory_scan` members are
  relative under `root`; `instruction_files` members must live under a
  declared `roots` entry — `..`/absolute/foreign paths are
  `ScanDenied`); every produced locator is credential-free
  (`locator.validate_credential_free`); and `merge_policy` resolves
  `(name, kind)` collisions — `exact_only` refuses `NameCollision`,
  never silent last-wins; `disjoint` admits the disjoint namespaces.
  Produced refs carry `locator.scheme = source.kind_str()` so the
  assembly's declared-source coverage check (`ExtUndeclaredSource`)
  sees them by construction; `resolved`/`content` stay unset — resolve
  pins, the scanner only enumerates. The new `RegistryError::ScanDenied`
  is the typed refusal (§5g.5 §2 error column).
- **D2 — `MergePolicy`'s home is `hh_registry::extension`;
  `hh_assembly::grammar` re-exports it.** The enum is the §5g.5
  vocabulary both the assembly (collision diagnostics) and the scanner
  (the fold) consume; the lower crate owns it, the grammar keeps its
  public spelling via `pub use` (CC1 — one enum, one owner; no caller
  changes).
- **D3 — claims lift at mint time; a grant-shaped claim is a runtime
  `LegCrossing`.** `extension::lift_declared_claims(manifest)` lifts
  the recognised claim members — `allowed_tools`/`allowed-tools`/
  `allowedTools`, `tool_annotations`/`tool-annotations`/`annotations`,
  `permissions`/`permission_manifest`/`permission-manifest`/`requests`,
  `claims` — into `trust.declared_claims`, the only legal home
  (unrecognised members stay in `record.manifest` verbatim, never
  lift). A claim value carrying `version_id`/`version-id`/`grant`/
  `grants`/`pin` refuses `LegCrossing` (`SchemaViolation` with the
  typed detail — the landed claim-validation spelling) **inside
  `resolve_extension`/`install_completed`**, before the record exists —
  the record-validation check alone left a hostile-manifest gap
  between mint and register. `enforce_leg_boundaries` re-runs
  L1–L3 plus the L2 conferral check (a delegate-origin or
  delegate-`installed_by` record carrying conferred `grants` is a
  `LegCrossing` — grants confer only through a `Permission` with
  `issuer.authority ≥ principal`) at resolve, install, seal and
  activate — the runtime enforcement DF-S1.23-2 owed.
- **D4 — `scanner_policy` consumes the minted `TextHygieneReport`;
  strictest-wins; `unavailable` is never `clean`.** The declared
  `TrustRootPolicy.scanner_policy` member now drives consumption at
  resolve/install: `advisory` mints + surfaces the report (the
  pre-R2.20 posture, and the default when no live policy declares the
  member); `quarantine` lands a `flagged`/`unavailable` report
  `TrustStatus::Quarantined` with the durable
  `security.extension.quarantined` row naming `scanner_flagged` /
  `scanner_unavailable`; `deny` refuses `ScanDenied` — nothing mints.
  `TrustView::scanner_policy` projects the strictest posture over the
  live policy set (`deny > quarantine > advisory` — `strictest_scan`,
  same rule as `strictest_install`); an unrecognised *declared*
  spelling parses `Deny` — a malformed trust rule fails closed, never
  silently advisory.
- **D5 — `seal_extension`/`activate_extension` mint the
  `sealed`/`loaded` producers; payloads ride results.** `seal_extension`
  gates `status = resolved`, a pinned locator (L4 —
  `UnpinnedInSealedForm`), the leg boundaries, and
  `grants ⊆ authority_cap` (`AuthorityWidening`) before lifting
  `status → sealed` and minting `security.extension.sealed`.
  `activate_extension` gates `status ∈ {resolved, sealed}` (`revoked` →
  `Revoked`, `quarantined` → typed refusal), the L4 pin check, and —
  for `surface_pin`ned records whose caller supplies both listings —
  re-runs `check_surface` under the declared `SurfaceDriftPolicy`
  (`notify` loads with changed/added tools suppressed; `pause_surface`/
  `terminate` mint no `loaded` row — the drift row + decision are the
  evidence). The minted `loaded` carries `{extension_id, isolation,
  pin_check, content + resolved (the pin attestation — Rule O),
  surface_check, checked_by}`; `surface_check` is honest —
  `clean`/`drifted`/`not_supplied`/`no_surface_pin`, never a check
  that did not run. All payloads ride the result structs
  (`ResolveReport.events`, `InstallOutcome.events`,
  `SealOutcome.sealed_event`, `Activation.loaded_event`) — the landed
  `check_surface` convention: the boundary mints evidence, the
  caller's fenced writer appends (durable-before-visible, CC2).
- **D6 — three boundary ops expose the legs.** `lab.extension.scan`,
  `lab.extension.seal`, `lab.extension.activate` join the
  `lab.extension.*` family (group `L`, `json`/`json` — one schema
  source, `ops.rs` + regen, CC7); `resolve`/`install` accept the
  optional `payload` (the bytes the hygiene scan reads) and `manifest`
  (the claim source) members and return `events[]` alongside the
  landed members.

## Consequences

- DF-S1.23-2's residual closes: every declared
  `security.extension.*` class now has a producer, declared sources
  enumerate into refs under policy, claims-vs-grants enforces at
  runtime, and the hygiene report is consumed, not just minted.
- `resolve_extension`/`install_completed` gained a `manifest`
  parameter and richer results — the S4.14a call sites and the
  `s6_3a` `TrustView` literal were updated; the signatures are the
  crate's internal surface, not the boundary contract.
- The produced refs are *declared* into `extensions.refs[]` by the
  author — scanning never grows the surface implicitly (CF-079:
  sources are declared, never implicit).
- `TrustRootPolicy` documents written before R2.20 that declare a
  `scanner_policy` spelling outside `{advisory, off, quarantine, deny}`
  now project `Deny` — fail-closed, and the projection is visible in
  `trust_view()`.
- Budget honesty: the production diff is ~1,039 changed lines against
  the ticket's 900 — the overage (~15%) is the `seal`/`activate`/
  `scan` producers plus the boundary ops the closeout required; the
  test battery (~933 lines) rides the AC matrix per the R2.6–R2.19
  precedent and is not counted.

## Alternatives considered

- **Filesystem/endpoint enumeration inside `hh-registry`** — rejected:
  I/O in the pure layer breaks hermeticity (CC5) and could never be
  fixture-verified; the caller-supplied `ScanEntry` listing keeps the
  trust legs deterministic and offline.
- **`MergePolicy` stays in `hh-assembly` and the scanner takes the
  assembly type** — rejected: `hh-registry` cannot depend upward; a
  second enum in `hh-registry` would be two spellings of one contract
  (CC1).
- **Claims stay manifest-only until registration validation** —
  rejected: `validate_extension_record` runs at register time; a minted
  record carrying a grant-shaped claim would exist (and could be
  passed around) before validation ever saw it. L3 must hold at mint.
- **A new `RegistryError::LegCrossing` variant** — rejected: the
  landed codebase spells the crossing `SchemaViolation` with a
  `LegCrossing:`-prefixed detail (`l3_claim_with_grant_shape_refused`
  predates this slice); one refusal spelling, one owner (CC1).
- **Renaming `SurfaceDriftPolicy` to the spec table's spellings** —
  rejected: `notify|pause_surface|terminate` landed at S4.14a and is
  referenced by tests and docs; the spec table is the older spelling
  of the same lattice. A rename is a separate dialect question, not
  this ticket's.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen when (a) a real scanner backend lands (a filesystem walker or a
marketplace/endpoint client) — the `ScanEntry` seam is designed for
it; (b) `scanner_policy` needs a declared vocabulary tighter than the
three postures (e.g. per-finding-kind rules); (c) the
`SurfaceDriftPolicy` spellings are reconciled with the spec table as
part of a dialect bump; or (d) seal gains HIR-document semantics
(sealing a definition that *contains* the extension) beyond the
record-level gate this slice lands.
