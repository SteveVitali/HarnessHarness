# ADR-0220: S0.1 AC-R-2.11.4-9 first measurement and the retained boundary-spike harness

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Ticket:** S0.1
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.11.4⁰ᵃ (AC-R-2.11.4-9), R-2.12.3 (program)
- **Spec:** §7.4 (AC-R-2.11.4-9), §10.6 (S2 boundary slice, rules R1–R6); ADR-0050 (e)

## Context
AC-R-2.11.4-9: "The S2 boundary slice reports per-crossing overhead, durable-frame delivery
latency (p50/p95) and ephemeral-drop rates at the spike's fan-out; results append to ADR-0050's
amendment log (OQ-131) and set the connection bounds as MUST-data (Stage 0–3)." The S0.1
acceptance requires this "first measured at S0". §10.6 rules R1–R6 for spikes say the measurement
sheet is the only durable output and "the code is deleted". Two tensions must be resolved:
1. Durable-frame latency and ephemeral-drop presuppose the Group R **event stream**, which lands
   at Stage 1 — there is nothing to time at Stage 0.
2. The full comparative S1/S2 battery (E5a/E5b/E5c splits, gates G1–G4, and the ADR-0050
   amendment-log append) is ticket **S0.3**, a throwaway spike per R1–R6.

## Decision
- **First measurement (S0.1):** measure **per-crossing overhead** of the `hello` round trip over
  stdio (binding (b)) using the persistent kernel and the generated client, plus the codegen
  round-trip wall time (§10.6 M-S2-1 / M-S2-3) and far-side hash-equality (M-S2-7). The runner is
  `scripts/spike-s2-boundary.sh`; the stamped sheet is
  `docs/build/measurements/S0.1-boundary-spike.md` (host, commit, date, numbers).
- **Deferred columns:** durable-frame delivery latency (p50/p95) and ephemeral-drop rate are
  emitted as typed `n/a{stage_0_no_stream}` and tracked in a DEFERRALS row (V); they are completed
  at S0.3 / once the Group R stream exists (Stage 1), whose sheet is what appends to the ADR-0050
  amendment log.
- **Retained harness (deviation from R1–R6 "code deleted"):** the boundary-measurement harness
  (`spikes/s2-boundary-spike/`) is **retained**, not deleted, because S0.1's boundary skeleton is
  the *persistent* Stage-0 output and its acceptance check must stay reproducible (and run in CI).
  The harness is kept outside the Cargo workspace so it is not a build dependency of the kernel.
  The *throwaway* comparative spike (rivals deleted after measurement) remains S0.3's job.

## Consequences
- Good: AC-R-2.11.4-9 has a real first measurement now; the spike is reproducible and CI-runnable
  (non-gating); the deferred columns are honestly typed, not fabricated.
- Cost: a deviation from R1–R6's "delete the code" — bounded to the persistent pipeline's own
  acceptance harness, recorded here; S0.3's comparative spike still follows R1–R6.
- The numbers are internal tier-B measurements (ADR-0050 R1–R6): they support no claim outside the
  ADR-0009 matrix and are not a gate threshold.

## Alternatives considered
- **Fabricate durable-frame/ephemeral numbers.** Refused — there is no stream at Stage 0; a
  fabricated pass violates the ticket's "never a fabricated pass" rule.
- **Delete the harness per R1–R6.** Rejected: S0.1's boundary skeleton persists (unlike S0.2/S0.3
  outputs), so its acceptance check must persist and re-run; deleting it would make the Stage-0
  drift/round-trip acceptance non-reproducible.

## Revisit trigger
When the Group R event stream lands (Stage 1) and/or S0.3 runs the comparative S1/S2 battery:
complete the durable-frame/ephemeral-drop columns, append the comparative sheet to the ADR-0050
amendment log, set the connection bounds (OQ-402) as MUST-data, and close the DEFERRALS row.
