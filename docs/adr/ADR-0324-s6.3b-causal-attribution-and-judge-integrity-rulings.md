## Context

S6.3a landed the multi-family
code-search + hosted-coordinate legs;
S6.3b lands sub-stage 6c's remaining
research-grade machinery: the designed
causal-attribution arms (M2 paired
counterfactuals under CRN, M3 direct/
Shapley effects, M4 mediation, M5),
the label gate that keeps attribution
claims honest, the judge-integrity
closures, and attribution over the
evolution lineage DAG. The run had to
rule on where the designed-attribution
link binds (S2 vs S5), how a monitor
selector stays veto-only, what a
value-of-compute row's shape is, and
how the lineage DAG attributes without
fabricating decomposition claims.

## Decision

- **D1 — the S2 `attribution_ref`
  requirement gates on the hypothesis's
  *designed claim*, not the raw diff.**
  A `FailureHypothesis` whose
  `semantic_op_targets` is non-empty
  must name `attribution_ref` → a
  deposited `hh-attribution-design/1`
  whose `targets[].ref` cover every
  semantic op (`AttributionMissing` /
  `AttributionReportInvalid` /
  `TargetMismatch`); a hypothesis
  claiming no designed targets keeps
  the mechanical path. Gating on raw
  `diff.classification.semantic_ops`
  would have retroactively refused
  every landed mechanical-diff
  hypothesis — the claim, not the op
  count, is what the design binds.
- **D2 — monitor selectors are
  veto-only and deterministic-first.**
  `SelectorDeclaration.kind ∈
  {judge, monitor}`; a monitor must
  declare `adversarial ∈
  {trusted_weaker, untrusted_monitored}`
  (`untrusted_unmonitored` and a
  missing declaration refuse
  `MonitorSelectorInadmissible` at
  `spec.validate`). `monitor_assign`
  mints
  `measurement.evolution.monitor.assigned`
  and `monitor_veto` mints
  `…monitor.veto` + `rejected
  {monitor_vetoed}` — a verdict with
  no prior assignment row never counts
  (deterministic-first), and a monitor
  is never a vote toward acceptance.
- **D3 — the judge's held-out closure
  is declared, and its leak refuses at
  S5.** `JudgePolicy.artifact_
  benefit_selector` carries
  `held_out_from` (the producer refs
  the selector is held out from);
  naming that selector on an S5
  `artifact_benefit` produced inside
  the closure is
  `JudgeLeakedIntoArtifact` — the
  durable mirror of hh-verification's
  boundary check, never a warning.
- **D4 — the S5 label gate binds the
  report to the named design.**
  A hypothesis that named
  `attribution_ref` lands its
  `hh-attribution/1` report at S5:
  `attribution_report_ref` must
  resolve, `schema` must be
  `hh-attribution/1`, the report's
  `design_ref` must equal the
  hypothesis's `attribution_ref`, and
  `attribution_label` must sit in the
  closed ladder (`designed_ablation <
  causal_interventional <
  causal_coupled`); a `locality` claim
  requires ≥ `causal_interventional`
  (M1's `designed_ablation` carries no
  locus). Member-level reads, no new
  crate edge — the hh-analysis codec
  stays authoritative at its own
  boundary (CC10).
- **D5 — the value-of-compute row is
  the `PlacementFact` member set,
  produced only by the critic
  experiment.**
  `hh_eval::critic_experiment::
  placement_row` folds the {on,off}
  outcome into
  `hh-critic-placement-row/1` — rates
  from the critic-on arm's absolute
  cells, `verification_overhead` the
  on−off delta (the critic's marginal
  cost, never the off arm's total),
  `calibration_status`/`uncaught_cost`
  declared inputs, `removal_test` the
  experiment's own handle. The
  boundary's placement decision
  decodes it records-in — the row
  never fabricates a rate the
  comparison did not measure.
- **D6 — the lineage DAG's parent edge
  is the base↔target link, and
  attribution over it reports edge
  shares, never decompositions.**
  `hh_evolution::lineage` folds the
  view's candidate records into the
  DAG: a candidate's `base_ref`
  resolves to the earlier-registered
  candidate whose `target_ref` it
  equals (the campaign base is the
  root); generations BFS from roots.
  `attribute_lineage` composes the
  deposited `hh-attribution/1` reports:
  a node's edge effect is the *signed
  sum* of its report's
  `effects[].point`, `edge_share_ppm`
  its share of the lineage-wide
  |effect| mass; nodes without a
  report carry `reported = false`,
  never a dropped row.
- **D7 — M2's effects are
  per-(target, fork-point), and CRN
  couples at the exogenous draw.**
  The M2 estimator emits one
  `effects[]` row per (target,
  fork-point) — locus needs every
  decision point, not a pooled per-
  target estimate — and the counter-
  factual arm under
  `noise_coupling = crn` shares the
  factual arm's exogenous draw (the
  paired difference is the honest
  causal contrast); uncoupled sources
  are typed refusals, never silently
  dropped (KA-I7-2).
- **D8 — the label ceiling is an
  admissibility floor, never an
  upgrade.** `label = confirmatory`
  is a ceiling the estimator may only
  downgrade (`exploratory`/`preview`
  on degraded validity, truncation,
  or uncoupled evidence); authority-
  widening interventions refuse, and
  hosted/non-native rows render
  `n/a{class}` — never a native causal
  observation (KA-I7-13/-14).

## Consequences

- The designed-attribution link is now
  durable end-to-end: hypothesis →
  design → report → label, with the
  `attribution_ref` member on the
  `hypothesized` transition row and
  the design/report bodies pinned
  under LabDocs kinds
  `attribution_design`/
  `attribution_report`.
- Mechanical-diff campaigns (every
  landed fixture) are unaffected —
  D1's gate reads the claim, not the
  diff.
- Judge integrity has a durable
  boundary: monitor rows mint
  audit-grade under their own classes;
  the held-out closure refuses at S5
  the same way hh-verification refuses
  at the critic boundary.
- The lineage DAG is a record, not a
  winner's history — rejected
  candidates stay nodes, and
  attribution over it reports shares
  of measured mass only.

## Alternatives considered

- **Gating `attribution_ref` on raw
  semantic ops** (any semantic diff
  requires a design): rejected — it
  silently breaks every mechanical-
  diff hypothesis the pipeline has
  landed; the *claim* is the honest
  contract (D1).
- **Monitor verdicts as votes** (a
  weighted acceptance input):
  rejected — §8.1's protocols are
  veto-only; an untrusted monitor
  that can vote *for* is a judge with
  weaker integrity (D2).
- **An hh-evolution → hh-analysis
  crate edge** for design/report
  decode: rejected — member-level
  reads at the campaign boundary keep
  the tier graph clean; the
  hh-analysis codec stays the single
  authoritative decoder at its own
  boundary (D4, CC10).
- **Lineage attribution as
  counterfactual decomposition**
  ("which ancestor caused the head's
  outcome"): rejected — the honest
  composition is per-edge measured
  effects summed into shares; a
  counterfactual-over-ancestors claim
  is a further experiment, not a fold
  (D6).

## Revisit trigger

- Reopen D1 if a spec amendment
  requires *every* semantic diff to
  carry a design (the mechanical path
  disappears) — the existing fixtures
  would need migration rows, not a
  silent tightening.
- Reopen D2 if monitors gain a
  graded (non-veto) verdict channel,
  or if `untrusted_unmonitored` is
  admitted under any protocol.
- Reopen D4/D5 if the
  `hh-attribution/1` report or
  `PlacementFact` member set grows —
  the member-level reads and the
  placement row must track it.
- Reopen D6 if lineage attribution
  is required to compose
  counterfactuals over ancestors —
  that is a new estimator, not an
  extension of `attribute_lineage`.
