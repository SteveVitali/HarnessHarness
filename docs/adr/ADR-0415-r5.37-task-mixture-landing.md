## Context

REQ-D-008 binds §3/§4: task-aware estimators must carry the actual
declared seed/draws, honor estimator floors, keep the complete frozen
primary/stratum families across split reports (unavailable members
retained), separate Holm-corrected primary multiplicity from
exploratory BH, and report signed/absolute tails — while one task,
missing clusters, unsupported dependence and degenerate resampling
refuse inference rather than invent an interval. The sharp-null
sign-flip test is admissible only under a registered exchangeability
argument; no practical-margin/weak-mean/omnibus certificate and no
observed max as a population bound is licensed. Before this ticket
`hh-eval` had the R5.15 `population::contrast_bounds` per-task cells
and a paired bootstrap frozen at `BOOTSTRAP_RESAMPLES` (4,000) — but
no estimator that resampled whole tasks at a declared draw count, no
sharp-null sign-flip leg, no frozen-family Holm/BH members, and no
tail report; `hh-analysis` had no kind that routed to them.

## Decision

1. **`task_mixture(pop, decl)` is one estimator over the R5.15 planned
   population.** It consumes `PlannedPopulation`/`contrast_bounds`
   unchanged — the per-task paired deltas come from the same counting
   rule, never a second resolution. `TaskMixtureDecl` declares the
   population kind, test/control arms, stratum, bootstrap method,
   null, exchangeability, dependence, verdict, bounds, level, draws,
   seed and the frozen primary/exploratory families; `task-mixture/1`
   is its version label.
2. **The bootstrap resamples whole tasks at the declared draws and
   seed.** Resampling is over tasks — the pairing unit — never over
   flattened runs or replicate n. `stats::bootstrap_paired_with`
   carries the additive declared-`resamples` parameter and
   `bootstrap_paired` delegates at the frozen 4,000, so the existing
   signature is untouched while the report's `draws.used` is the
   declared count, never a silent constant: changed draws change the
   resample stream and the report identity. Method selection is a
   floor, not a request: `BCa` iff `T ≥ 30`, `Percentile` below
   (`BCA_TASK_FLOOR`), and a declared method contradicting the floor
   is `BootstrapFloorViolation`. Draws below the 2,000 confirmatory
   floor are `DrawsBelowFloor`; `T < 2` is `OneTask` (raw
   observations only); fewer than two distinct deltas is
   `DegenerateBootstrap`; unresolved cell members and short cells
   (`R < 5`) are `UnresolvedCell`/`ShortCell`.
3. **The sharp-null sign flip runs only under the registered
   exchangeability argument.** `filters.null = sharp_no_effect` plus
   `exchangeability = lane_randomization` is the sole admission:
   `T ≤ 20` enumerates the sign assignments exactly; above 20 a
   seeded 8,000-draw Monte Carlo with the +1 correction applies
   (`SIGNFLIP_DRAWS`). Any other null is `UnsupportedNull`
   (weak-mean and practical-margin declarations alike); a missing or
   unregistered exchangeability is `ExchangeabilityNotAdmitted`; a
   dependence declaration other than independent task clusters is
   `UnsupportedDependence`.
4. **The frozen primary family carries every member through split
   reports; exploratory BH stays separate.** `FamilyDecl` names the
   family id and its declared members; a missing family identity,
   an empty member set, or a report member absent from the family is
   `FamilyIdentity`. Holm adjusts at the frozen α = .05
   (`ALPHA_PPM = 50_000`, level 95% — `LEVEL_PPM = 950_000`) over
   exactly the declared member set: an unavailable member is retained
   as `unavailable`/nonrejecting and can never improve another
   member's multiplicity, and two split reports whose self-member
   assignments differ recompute identical adjustments from the one
   frozen family. The named exploratory family adjusts by BH under
   its own `family` member — it cannot replace, widen or relabel the
   primary Holm correction.
5. **Signed and absolute tails are separate members; the observed
   max is never a bound.** `tail_signed` retains direction;
   `tail_absolute` is its own member — nearest-rank P99 may equal the
   observed max with `n` stated, and the report labels that max
   explicitly not-a-population-bound. Tail uncertainty is a
   clustered-resampling interval where valid and a typed limit where
   the resample degenerates. `signs` counts the per-task delta
   directions; a declared verdict that would fuse the legs into an
   omnibus/combined/TOST/practical-margin certificate is
   `UnsupportedVerdict`.
6. **Every refused inference is typed.** `TaskMixtureError` is the
   closed vocabulary — `Population(..)` passthrough, `OneTask`,
   `UnresolvedCell`, `ShortCell`, `DegenerateBootstrap`,
   `BootstrapFloorViolation`, `DrawsBelowFloor`,
   `UnsupportedDependence`, `UnsupportedNull`,
   `ExchangeabilityNotAdmitted`, `UnsupportedVerdict`,
   `FamilyIdentity`, `UnsupportedLevel`, `NonFiniteInput`,
   `UnknownMethod`, `MetadataOnly` (a method switch declared on a
   kind that does not compute it), `StrataSelectionRequired`
   (candidate slots spanning strata without a declared `stratum` —
   strata are never pooled). No silent default, no invented
   interval, no fabricated universe.
7. **The routed kind reuses the R5.16/16b records-in path.**
   `task_mixture_sensitivity` routes `analyze` →
   `analyze_and_record` → persisted `analysis_report_body/1` through
   the same bound-`ExperimentView` fold and strict-integer param gate
   `hh-embed` already enforces (`view` required — `MissingSpecRef`;
   non-integer `confidence_ppm`/`resampling_draws`/`seed` →
   `SchemaViolation`). `AnalysisError::TaskMixture` surfaces the
   typed vocabulary and `record_kind` maps the kind to its persisted
   member name; the report carries the S.1 method metadata —
   estimator/version/target, keys, seed, declared and used draws,
   floors, refusals, interval type/level — on every row.
8. **Later contributor evidence stays owed.** The full-target
   predicates of REQ-D-008 and the four contributing-only targets
   keep their R/P and actual-execution legs with their named owners
   (R5.16, R5.38, R5.40 — evidence dependency, never dispatch); this
   ticket records engineered D/E only (I-07).

## Consequences

- One scope sentence lands one reviewable unit: estimator, refusal
  vocabulary, routed kind and battery share the task-cluster/family
  decisions the M-D packet binds together.
- Declared draws are a computation input and a report fact — a
  metadata-only re-declaration cannot stand in for a changed
  resample (`changed_draws_change_computation` re-derives a
  different body and `report_id` at 2,000 vs 4,000).
- The frozen family is machine-checkable: split-report
  self-assignments recompute identical Holm adjustments, and an
  unavailable member is visible instead of laundered into a dropped
  row.
- The +1 Monte Carlo correction and the exact-enumeration boundary
  make the sign-flip p-values rational/reproducible; a
  non-exchangeable or shared-shock design is refused rather than
  approximated.

## Alternatives considered

- **Resampling flattened runs or replicate n** — rejected: §3/§4 and
  REQ-D-008 bind the pairing unit to whole tasks; flattened
  resampling breaks pairing and understates cluster-level variance.
- **Splitting the estimator from the route/battery** — rejected at
  the scoped split review (ruling `cec2367f`,
  `planning/2026-11-03_R5.37-split-review.md`): `task_mixture()` is
  one function emitting one report member — the cut is
  mid-predicate, both legs stay over the ≤950 trigger, and
  `task_mixture.rs` has zero `#[cfg(test)]` so an estimator-first
  leg lands with zero fails-without regression (the R5.32/R5.14b-
  rejected impl-vs-battery cut). Recorded Deviation at 2,058 lines.
- **An always-BCa or always-percentile interval** — rejected: the
  floor is T-dependent by contract (BCa at ≥30, percentile below);
  a declared method contradicting the selection is a refusal, not a
  silently honored request.
- **Dropping unavailable members from the frozen family** —
  rejected: §4 binds the complete frozen primary/stratum family
  across split reports; a retained `unavailable` member is the
  honest disposition and can never improve another member's
  multiplicity.
- **A separate tail-report store or a pooled-strata convenience** —
  rejected (M-D names both): tails are members of the one report,
  and `StrataSelectionRequired` keeps strata un-pooled absent a
  declared selection.

## Revisit triggers

- If the registered exchangeability set grows beyond
  `lane_randomization` (a second admitted argument, a protocol
  amendment), re-read the sharp-null gate — the admission is a
  string equality on a registered name by design.
- If any frozen floor is amended (BCa `T ≥ 30`, confirmatory draws
  `≥ 2,000`, sign-flip `8,000` +1, exact-enumeration `T ≤ 20`, level
  `95%`, α `.05`, cell floor `R ≥ 5`), re-read the constants and the
  `BootstrapFloorViolation`/`DrawsBelowFloor` legs — floors are
  contract constants, never parameters.
- If `PlannedPopulation`/`contrast_bounds` or the `ledger_facts/1`
  projections change, re-read the cell derivation — the estimator
  consumes the R5.15 counting rule; a second resolution would fork
  the contract.
- If `analysis_report_body/1` is versioned or the embed view fold /
  strict-param gate is re-scoped, re-read the routed-kind decision —
  the kind rides the R5.16/16b records-in contract unchanged.
