# ADR-0427: The committed runner is a workspace crate below hh-embed (AR-04)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Ticket:** R6.8 (manifest 261)
- **Requirement ids:** REQ-XB-101, REQ-XB-102, REQ-XB-103, REQ-XB-104, REQ-XB-105, REQ-XB-108, REQ-XB-120; alias REQ-XA-010, REQ-XB-012; contributing REQ-XB-104 (R6.16-owned)
- **Spec:** experiments supplement §3.2/§3.3; Appendix D
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** C1 — durable-record topology over the existing engine/store contracts; no schema addition

## Context

R6.8 adds the committed offline experiment runner — the first
architecturally placed participant that *drives* `hh-experiment`'s
`ExperimentEngine` rather than embedding another scheduler. Three forces
pinned the ruling:

1. **AR-04 / K-2.** A runner crate must not introduce a K-2 edge: it stays
   below `hh-embed` and never depends on `hh-embed`, `hh-cli`,
   `hharness-hosting`, `hh-kernel`, `hh-orchestrator` or `hh-fleet`. An
   enforceable gate, not a convention, had to land with the crate.
2. **The slot record's home.** `hh-experiment-slot/1` is a runner-crate
   record (the ticket's Notes) — a codec over `hharness-wire` canonical
   JSON, not an `hh-embed-schema` addition and not a ledger event class
   (the class table gains no row; the record is blob-pool payload bound
   by `slot_record_ref`).
3. **Recovery authority.** REQ-XB-105 requires resume derived only from
   durable records, and the supplement forbids resuming a paid agent
   phase mid-run — a dead attempt is a *new* attempt under the engine's
   `ReattemptPolicy`.

## Decision

1. `crates/hh-runner/` ships package `hharness-runner` (lib + `hh-runner`
   bin) with three process legs: `coordinator` (sole writer of the
   experiment store root, driving `next → claim → launch → settle` under
   the spec's `ReattemptPolicy`), `slot-worker` (one process per
   in-flight attempt, sole writer of its own slot store root, emitting
   exactly one `hh-experiment-slot/1` record per slot), and `store-probe`
   (the topology spike's cross-process read + second-writer legs).
2. The runner keeps **no** scheduler, retry counter or authoritative
   store of its own: plan eligibility, replan, claim lifecycle and
   settlement are the engine's durable records. A claim `WouldBlock` is
   waited out, never worked around — the claim fence *is* the durable
   re-claim gate (a live claim blocks re-claim until its TTL lapses or
   the writer generation advances; no runner-side release row).
3. The `hh-experiment-slot/1` codec lives in the crate with the closed
   `withheld{…}` member set (`admission:<r>` for `r ∈ AG1–AG9` plus the
   §3.3 ST0/named-hold reasons, `budget`, `window_deadline`,
   `image_drift`, `dataset_private`, `no_candidate`, `inert_predicted`,
   `inert_at_admissible_window`, `infra`, `l1_not_passed`,
   `sealed_until_results`) — gate dispositions and outcomes
   (`fallback`, `end_after_w2`, `declined`) are not members. `costs` is
   `{schema:"hh-slot-costs/1", ref}` references only; a value member is a
   duplicated fold and is refused.
4. Recovery is durable-record-only: a launched-but-unsettled attempt
   binds the worker's sealed record (subject `lifecycle.run.finished`
   copies `stop_reason`/`outcome_class`/`disposition` verbatim and names
   `slot_store{root_digest, run_id, head_hash}` at the post-seal head),
   or closes `inconclusive{infra:runner_crash}` and replans — never a
   mid-agent-phase resume. The runner performs no `Path::exists`-style
   existence skips; `has_run`/`sealed_attempt` read durable facts.
5. `scripts/check-runner-edges.sh` + `scripts/ci/test-check-runner-edges.sh`
   + a ci.yml step (`Runner K-2 edge check (AR-04)`, non-fast paths)
   enforce ruling 1 on `cargo tree`.
6. **Dated addendum — Harbor O-surface admission.** The Harbor adapter /
   tool-admission leg (live `harbor_reward_file/1` channel, O-surface
   subprocess ingest, real model-proxy fold) is R6.17's; R6.8 ships the
   fixture executor channel (`hh-runner-fixture-verdict/1`, explicitly
   labelled so it is never confused with the live channel) and the
   pinned Harbor `error_policy` cause map used when the O-surface record
   lands.

## Consequences

- A second writer on a held root is refused at the store's own lease
  fence; the runner adds no liveness logic of its own.
- Process-level concurrency is `max_parallel_slots` slot-worker
  processes, each sole-writing a distinct slot root.
- The premature-refusal precedence (a premature slot budget refusal is
  `infrastructure_failure` even when a valid reward exists) is one
  mapping table in `facts.rs` — the engine re-derives the class from the
  subject run's `stop_reason`, so the runner never carries a parallel
  verdict (X-04/CC1).
- The crate's test battery (`tests/r6_8.rs`) exercises real child
  processes (`env!("CARGO_BIN_EXE_hh-runner")`), SIGKILL kill points,
  and the cross-process read/second-writer legs — no in-process fakes
  for the topology claims.
- The crate lands above its ≤1700 changed-line split trigger (the
  §3.3/ST0–ST7 surface plus the two-process battery is not compressible
  into the budget without dropping acceptance legs); recorded here as a
  deviation, not hidden in the diff.

## Alternatives considered

- **In-process slot execution.** Rejected: REQ-XB-104's topology proof
  is process-level — a thread cannot carry the single-writer/kill-point
  evidence.
- **`hh-embed-schema` record kind.** Rejected per the ticket Notes: the
  codec is a runner-crate record over canonical JSON; a schema row would
  buy no boundary it does not already own.
- **Runner-owned retry/claim bookkeeping.** Rejected outright (CC1/K-2):
  the contract's words are "keeps NO scheduler, retry counter, or
  authoritative store of its own".
- **Releasing the claim lease on settle.** Rejected: claim lifecycle is
  engine authority; the fence is the re-claim gate and the coordinator
  waits it out.
