## Context

REQ-D-007/101/102/103 bind §4.1/F1–F3: the central study needs a
frozen protocol *record* — TB2 catalogue identity, outcome-blind
eligible/excluded sets, dev/held-out separation fixed before pilot
access, a seeded 30-task held-out selection without replacement
under frozen metadata/contamination quotas and equal within-stratum
weights, a fair task-level lane coin assigning arms to identical
lanes interleaved over five rounds, first-slot bindings, the six
phase identities and the frozen analysis-family/stop identities.
`protocol_id` must predate every dependent subject; a material
change after subjects forks a cohort. Retries are packaged: ≤2 per
plan, ≤10 % of plans, frozen infrastructure classes/backoff, and a
wrong answer never retries (`oracle_failure` regrades, never
re-runs — AC-R-2.10.3-6). Before this ticket the spine owned every
downstream consumer (population R5.15, mechanism joins R5.36,
task-mixture R5.37, directional-KL R5.38, parity admission R5.39,
numerical validation R5.40) but no owner produced the deterministic
selection/schedule artifact the consumers and later stages quote.

## Decision

1. **Home and shape.** `hharness_eval::central_protocol`
   (`crates/hh-eval/src/central_protocol.rs`) —
   `materialize_protocol(&ProtocolDecl) ->
   Result<MaterializedProtocol, ProtocolError>`, pure and total,
   records-in/record-out. hh-eval is the only cycle-free home that
   consumes the M-A plan/metadata contract
   (`population::MethodMetadata`, the `(task, split, stratum, arm,
   replicate)` slot vocabulary) *and* the owning §6.3 scheduling
   records (`hharness_lab::experiment::{SchedulingPolicy,
   ReattemptPolicy}`) unchanged; hh-lab cannot consume hh-eval.
   The module emits no analysis-kind arm: materialization is not an
   `AnalysisSpec.kind` — the record travels by `protocol_id`
   reference to R5.42's readiness packet and later stages.
2. **Deterministic derivation.** Seeded ranks via the single
   content-addressing primitive (`idp_digest`, CC1) under
   `central_protocol.{select,order,coin,slot}` — the same scheme
   `expand::order_key` uses for seeded task/arm ranks. Selection
   takes the lowest seeded ranks per stratum (equal weights, no
   replacement, quotas exact); a second seed reproduces a different
   selection deterministically.
3. **Identity.** `protocol_id = idp_digest("central_protocol",
   canonical(record minus protocol_id))` — a derived commitment
   under an existing domain idiom; no new `RecordKind`, event class
   or wire contract is created (I-01/I-02). `check_id()` re-derives
   it; `first_slots` is derived from the stored schedule.
4. **Schedule semantics.** `TASKS=30`, `ROUNDS=5`, two arms
   (`evict_oldest`/`clear_tool_results` — arm A, arm B), two lanes;
   replicate index = round, so R = 5/task/arm and 300 planned first
   subjects. The fair coin is one seeded bit per task; both
   orientations must occur in any honest draw.
5. **Frozen validation.** `SchedulingPolicy.max_concurrent_runs ≤ 2`
   and `order ∈ {interleaved, interleaved_blocked}`; lanes exactly
   two distinct ids under one spec ref (one per arm).
   `ReattemptPolicy` requires `error_classes_included =
   Some(⊆ {infrastructure_failure})` — `None` is unfrozen, any
   other class is a wrong-answer retry — with `max_per_plan ≤ 2` and
   `max_fraction_of_plans_ppm ≤ 100_000`.
6. **Refusal vocabulary (closed `ProtocolError`).** outcome-selected
   exclusion bases (only the six `OUTCOME_BLIND_EXCLUSION_BASES`
   admitted) and unpublished exclusions; `DevOverlap`,
   `ConflictingTask`, `ExclusionEligible`, `LateSplit`;
   `UndeclaredStratum`, `QuotaMismatch`, `InsufficientHeldOut`
   (never replacement or a silent smaller manifest); `ArmSet`
   (≠ exactly the two frozen strategies — no third arm),
   `PolicySearch`, `TaskConditioned`, `ArtificialPressure`;
   `ConcurrencyCap`, `ArmConcurrency`, `OrderPolicy`;
   `RetryCeiling`, `UnfrozenRetryClasses`, `WrongAnswerRetry`;
   `UnknownWarmth`, `Drift`, `SupportMismatch` (route/cache/seed/
   fingerprint), `RolesChanged`; `PhaseIdentity`, `FamilyMismatch`
   (primaries must equal the frozen three), `StopIdentity`;
   `ChangedAfterSubjects` (`amendment_subjects_bound > 0` — a new
   cohort, never a rewrite; `0` is the admitted pre-subject path),
   `PilotRelabel`, `MissingKey`.

## Consequences

- Same supplied manifest/split/quotas/seed yields a byte-identical
  record and `protocol_id` — replayable offline, no clock/store
  reads. Selection, order and coin change together under a
  different committed seed.
- The record names phase/family/stop *identities* — materializing
  them is not executing them; pilot/parity/confirmation/replication
  stages keep their own gates. No participant data, no live
  execution, no scientific claim.
- Actual tasks/seeds/quotas/margins/tariffs stay external gated
  inputs (H2/Q-3/Q-4); this machinery validates their shape and
  freezes their binding — it never invents them.
- `InsufficientHeldOut` is a stop signal for pre-confirmation
  revision — it never silently shrinks the manifest or replaces
  tasks.
- R predicates: the actual protocol hash predating subjects and the
  executed selection/schedule remain owed (R5.46+ stages, R5.49
  report) — this ticket discharges the engineered slice only.

## Alternatives considered

- **hh-lab home** (the memo's "hh-lab/unit" anchor): rejected —
  hh-lab cannot consume the M-A `MethodMetadata`/population
  contract (hh-eval already depends on hh-lab; a reverse edge is a
  CC1 violation).
- **New `RecordKind`/wire kind for the protocol record:** rejected —
  a derived `idp_digest` commitment under the existing
  `central_protocol` domain idiom (the `expand` precedent) needs no
  class-table change; admitted scope stays implementation-local.
- **Kernel `analyze` kind arm:** rejected — materialization is not
  an analysis kind; routing it through `analyze` would invent a
  report-shaped surface the contract never asked for.
- **Reuse `expand()` for the schedule:** rejected — `expand`
  produces `arm × task` cells from a registered `ExperimentSpec`;
  the protocol's quota-constrained seeded selection, lane coin and
  five-round/first-slot identities are a different closed predicate,
  and duplicating an `ExperimentSpec` façade would create a second
  schedule contract.

## Revisit triggers

- The actual gated inputs arrive (tasks/seeds/quotas/margins/
  tariffs — R5.42 quote or a later stage): re-materialize under the
  real decl and re-run the battery.
- A `RecordKind`/event-class admission for protocol records lands:
  migrate `protocol_id` to the admitted kind.
- `ReattemptPolicy`/`SchedulingPolicy` dialect gains members the
  frozen predicates must inspect (e.g. a lane/pool binding):
  re-validate the one-per-arm and retry-class checks.
