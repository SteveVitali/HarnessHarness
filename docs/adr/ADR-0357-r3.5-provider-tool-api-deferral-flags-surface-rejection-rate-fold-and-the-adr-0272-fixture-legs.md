## Context

DF-S1.17-1's R2.8 progress note named exactly two members the row had
never assigned to a ticket: the `provider_tool_api` deferral flags on the
two minimal profiles, and the `surface_rejection_rate` *computation*
(AC-R-2.5.2-12's C0/S3 half — the catalogue row was declaration-only,
`fold: N(Capability)`). R2.26's backlog reconciliation re-homed
ADR-0272's two unmet revisit legs to BL-16: `sync_source`/`adopt` over a
live catalog delta (the trigger matrix landed at S3.9 but pinned the
`freeze` half and empty deltas), and `retrieval_eval` real baselines over
the committed corpus with the `exact_name` floor verdict re-checked.

Three decisions were owned by this ticket:

1. **How the minimal profiles spell `unsupported`.** The deferral flags
   are capability-record members (`deferred_tools`, `tool_search`).
   `CapabilityState` is the tri-state `Declared | Probed | Unknown`;
   `unknown` is the fail-closed C0 spelling — a consumer meeting the flag
   against a tool's deferral admission refuses the deferred leg. Spelling
   them `declared` would guess a capability the reference profiles never
   established.
2. **How the provider artefact carries the flags.** The
   `hh-provider-request/1` artefact is the run-side surface the
   provider-tool selection consumes; the flags had to reach it verbatim
   (never coerced) and a deferred-admitting surface lowered under an
   unestablished `deferred_tools` had to declare its loss rather than
   silently narrow to `direct`-only.
3. **What `surface_rejection_rate`'s denominator and strata are.**
   AC-R-2.5.2-12 fixes `surface_rejected ÷ model-emitted calls` per
   `(profile, family)`. Neither stratum axis was on the emitted rows: the
   payloads carried no `family`, and `profile` had to join through the
   producing model call.

## Decision

1. **The deferral flags are `unknown` on both minimal profiles.**
   `minimal_patch` and `minimal_string_replace` declare
   `deferred_tools: Unknown` and `tool_search: Unknown` on their
   `ProfileCapabilities` record — named record members, not generic
   registry-row fields; `unknown`, never a guessed `declared`. The codec
   round-trips both members (the capability-state table already owned the
   spelling).
2. **The provider lowering stamps the flags and refuses the deferred leg
   honestly.** `lower_provider` emits `deferral{deferred_tools,
   tool_search}` on the `hh-provider-request/1` artefact — the leaf
   profile's capability states verbatim (`declared | probed | unknown`),
   and each tool entry carries `deferrable` (the surface's `admitted_modes
   ∋ deferred`). A `deferrable` surface under an unestablished
   `deferred_tools` records a declared `no_slot`/`narrowed` loss naming
   the unestablished capability — the refusal shape §3.2.5 owns, never a
   silent direct-only narrowing. A declared capability expresses the leg
   clean — no loss entry.
3. **`surface_rejection_rate` computes in the one fold (CC1).** The
   catalogue row's `fold` flipped `N(Capability) → C`; `compute_metric`
   gains the branch: calls are deduped on the `tool_call` scope
   (scope-then-payload), the env pattern — `proposed` opens the scope,
   the arg-eval refusal mints `surface_rejected` on the same
   `tool_call_id` — counts once; `profile` joins the producing
   `model.call.requested`'s `profile_ref` (`model_ref.profile_ref`
   second); `family` is the payload member the emitters stamp from the
   binding's `family_id`. Both read `unknown` absent — never fabricated.
   An empty denominator renders `n/a{estimator_undefined}`; hosted
   participants read `n/a{class}` through the existing class gate.
4. **`family` rides the emitted rows.** `action.tool.proposed` and
   `action.tool.surface_rejected` payloads gain an optional `family`
   member (the class-table members tolerate it — no new classes). The
   env dispatcher stamps `binding.family_id`; the control plane carries
   it on `SurfaceSpec.family` (`Option<String>` — `None` on host-cap
   surfaces and unshaped primitives, the `leaf.arm` record round-trips it
   verbatim); the driver's proposal mint and the guard's parser-reject
   row stamp it off the surface record, `null` when the attempted name
   never bound.
5. **ADR-0272's legs are dated fired-answered.** Leg (a): `sync_source`
   computes the real `CatalogDelta` off a fixture listing (one drop, one
   lift) and runs the existing `adopt` path — epoch advances, the removed
   surface leaves the adopted catalog, the lifted surface joins with
   `authority = unverified`, the source row records the new snapshot, and
   both `action.tool.catalog.*` rows emit (`r3_5_sync_source_adopts_catalog_delta`).
   Leg (b): `retrieval_eval` over `benchset.stage3.v1` (the ≥200-entry
   catalogue, 46 labelled queries over 23 recorded tasks) reports —
   `exact_name` recall@8 = nDCG@8 = 1.0000 (the gated floor re-verified);
   `bm25` and `hierarchical` meet the floor on the same exact-name set
   (1.0000/1.0000 each) and `bm25`/NL, `hierarchical`/paths,
   `lexical_regex`/anchored-name all record 1.0000/1.0000. No variant
   underperforms `exact_name` on exact-name queries — the floor's scope
   stands; the recorded baselines are pinned in the battery
   (`hh-bench/tests/r3_5.rs`) so a regression trips. Legs (c) and (d)
   re-checked at the same sweep and answer fired: (c) DF-S2.9-3's
   automatic snapshot cadence landed at R2.4 — the cadence takes ride
   `KernelSink` inside the armed run, never the CLI takeover session, so
   D6's keep-open posture is unaffected and stands verbatim; (d)
   AC-R-2.11.1-16 landed at S2.12 — `emit_result` gates every emitted
   record including `hh compact`, so a withheld item renders the typed
   `withheld{reason}` tombstone in place and `loss_report.dropped[]`
   enumerates every omitted class. ADR-0272 discharges to BL-43
   (fired-answered).

## Consequences

- DF-S1.17-1's two unassigned members land; the row discharges dated
  DONE. BL-16 closes — R-2.5.3's last PARTIAL cell resolves to MET.
- The `hh-provider-request/1` artefact gains `deferral{…}` and
  per-tool `deferrable` — additive members; consumers that ignore them
  behave exactly as before.
- `action.tool.proposed`/`surface_rejected` payloads gain `family` —
  optional, absent-or-null on unshaped/host-declared surfaces. The
  metric's `unknown` stratum is where those rows honestly land.
- `SurfaceSpec` gains `family: Option<String>` — every constructor names
  it explicitly; `None` stays honest for `supplies.host_capabilities[]`
  (the schema carries no `family_id`, so host-declared surfaces are
  unshaped by declaration).
- `surface_rejection_rate` is `n/a{estimator_undefined}` on any run that
  emits no tool-call rows — a measured answer, never a silent zero.
- **Size flag (the R3.3 precedent).** ~1,528 changed source lines vs the
  ticket's 1,000 budget — ~289 production edits across seven crates plus
  three new acceptance batteries (compiler 540, telemetry 377, bench 306 —
  new `tests/r3_5.rs` files count the same way prior rounds counted).
  Recorded here rather than split post-hoc: the batteries are the ticket's
  acceptance evidence and a split would orphan them from the legs they
  pin; flagged for operator adjudication.

## Alternatives considered

- **Spell the flags `declared` on the minimal profiles.** Rejected —
  the reference profiles never established provider tool-API deferral;
  `declared` would fabricate a capability. `unknown` is the fail-closed
  C0 spelling and meets every consumer's admission check the honest way.
- **A second rejection-rate fold or a dedicated stratum table.**
  Rejected — CC1: the computation lives in the one `compute_metric`
  fold beside its siblings; the strata are a member of the metric's
  cell, not a second metric.
- **Guess `family` from the surface name on unresolved calls.**
  Rejected — a parser reject that never bound has no family; `null` →
  `unknown` is the honest stratum, never a fabricated bucketing.
- **Gate the C1 retrieval variants at the `exact_name` floor.** Rejected —
  AC-R-2.5.3-11 gates the baseline only; the variants' numbers are
  recorded baselines (pinned at observed values), not a silently widened
  floor.

## Revisit trigger

- A provider model capability probe that establishes `deferred_tools` /
  `tool_search` on a real profile — re-lower the artefact and re-check
  the `deferrable`/`no_slot` gating under `probed`.
- A hosted participant gaining the native ledger observability contract —
  re-check the `n/a{class}` boundary.
- A corpus or index change that drops a recorded baseline below 1.0 —
  record the scope issue in a new ADR; never re-shape the `exact_name`
  floor to fit a variant.
- A new ledger event shape for tool proposals/rejections — the fold's
  scope-then-payload `tool_call_id` join and `model_call_id → profile`
  join must be re-verified.
