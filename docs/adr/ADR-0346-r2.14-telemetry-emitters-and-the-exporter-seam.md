# ADR-0346: R2.14 telemetry emitters + the exporter seam — member-stamped durations, the `hh` propagation carrier, lift-back coordinates, and the egress-mediated subscriber

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Ticket:** R2.14
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.9.1 · DF-S1.14-1 · DF-S1.14-2 · DF-S1.14-4 ·
  BL-24
- **Spec:** §5h.1 (the measurement points, propagation contract §2.3,
  export contract §6/§7, process-metric catalogue §8) · §5h.2 (the
  metric registry / `applies_to` gate) · §03 (the MCP/ACP `_meta`
  lowering slots) · ADR-0042 (spans as projections) · ADR-0043
  (provenance/confidence; participant classes) · ADR-0044
  (`n/a{reason}` honesty) · ADR-0061 (EP3 egress mediation — a sink is
  a `net_egress` destination) · ADR-0245/0246 (the §5h.1 landings this
  completes).
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** C0 — every deliverable runs at the
  offline/fixture ceiling; the only wire is the egress-mediator's
  loopback fixture transport.

## Context

R2.14 discharges the telemetry residual — DF-S1.14-1's Stage-2 emitter
classes (M5/M6/M13/M14/M15 minted from real producing paths), the
`turn_phase_profile` view, MCP/ACP propagation carriers, DF-S1.14-2's
convention-shaped sink fixtures with lift-back plus the hosted `n/a`
sweep and per-configuration catalogue distributions, and DF-S1.14-4's
live exporter seam. The substrate was mostly landed: `MEASUREMENT_POINTS`
with stage stamps, `Timing`/payload builders, the subprocess-env
propagation seam, `export_view`/`export_events`/`deliver`,
`sink_deliveries`, the `EgressMediator` (R2.9), and the
`commit_kernel_row_for` append site (S3.1). What the ticket owned was
five design rulings:

1. **How measured durations are stamped.** §2.6 requires member stamps,
   never `ts` differences — but the landed producers carried a mix of
   bare integers and nothing at all on the residual emitter rows.
2. **The carrier spelling on the MCP/ACP surfaces.** §5h.1 §2.3 fixes
   the W3C `traceparent`/`tracestate` pair and the `hh` tracestate
   member; the slot it rides on each surface (`_meta`) was an owned
   choice inside §03's additive-member rules.
3. **How a lowered convention span lifts back.** AC-R-2.9.1's sink
   round-trip needs `span → EventRef`; the conventions have no
   ledger-identity member.
4. **Where the subscriber's wire lives.** The exporter is a
   participant, not a trusted side channel — but it also must not
   mint delivery rows the mediator refused.
5. **The `turn_phase_profile` partition order.** §5h.1 §2.2 names the
   seven members and `Σ members = turn wall`; the attribution rule for
   nested intervals (a permission wait inside a tool call) was an
   owned ruling.

## Decision

**D1 — member-stamped durations, both spellings.** Producing paths
stamp `at_ms`, `started_at_ms`, `turn_e2e_ms`, `wall_ms`,
`duration_ms`, `env_ms`, `snapshot_ms`, `sleep_ms` as measured
members; where a member is a duration the stamp is the §2.6
`{value, measured_at}` shape, and `measured_value` (hh-telemetry
`views.rs`) reads *both* the bare-integer and the stamped spelling —
pre-existing bare-integer rows fold identically (CC8). A new
`DurationSource::TimingMember(f)` resolves a `timing.<f>` member the
same way. A boundary missing its stamp is *unbounded* — counted,
never fabricated (T-LCD-15). Scope coordinates were corrected, not
renamed: `EnvironmentOp` resolves `env_handle` (the spelling the rows
carry) beside the registered `env_id`, and `Wakeup` resolves
`subscription_id` — top-level on the lifecycle terminals, nested under
`subscription` on `scheduled`.

**D2 — the `hh` carrier.** `outbound_context(root, run_id, event_id)`
renders `traceparent = 00-H(root_run_id)[0:16]-H(event_id)[0:8]-01`,
`tracestate = hh=run:<run_id>;ev:<event_id>`, optional
`baggage{hh.configuration_id}` — the provisional derivation
(ADR-0042/OQ-115) the Stage-1 subprocess seam already used. MCP rides
`_meta` with the three flat members beside `requestState`/`target`;
ACP rides `_meta.hh` with the same flat spellings joined into the
existing metadata (`source_seq`, `agent_semantic_id`, `hh_methods`,
`update_kinds` — preserved, never replaced). `inbound_context` parses
strictly: malformed/absent members answer `InvalidInboundContext` or a
`carrier_loss` entry naming the dropped member — a foreign context is
never grafted onto a ledger event it does not name (the same honesty
rule as the sink lift).

**D3 — lift-back via namespaced extension attributes.** Liftable
lowerings stamp `hh.run_id`/`hh.event_id` on the emitted span (the
same namespaced-extension convention as the `hh` tracestate member —
context, never authority; any reader confirms the coordinate against
the ledger itself). `lift_span` resolves `Some(EventRef)` only when
both members are present; a foreign convention span lifts `None`,
never a fabricated parent. Both sink fixtures — the landed
`gen_ai.*` lowering (now with a `lower_run_refs` liftable half) and
the new OpenInference lowering (`openinference.span.kind=LLM`,
`llm.token_count.*`) — emit explicit `LossEntry` reports for every
member the convention cannot spell.

**D4 — the subscriber is an egress participant.** `ExportSubscriber`
(hh-embed `subscriber.rs`) tails the durable prefix through `read` at
`watermark + 1` (the R2.2 tail over compacted prefixes), lowers the
delta under the declared `SinkPolicy` — consent, rate cap and
content-class gates run *before* any wire — and hands one `net_egress`
request to the caller's `EgressMediator`. Only a `Forwarded` outcome
appends `measurement.export.delivered` through
`commit_kernel_row_for` on the run's persisted writer-lease
generation (closed runs still export — the S3.1 append site). A
refused/asked/credential-refused outcome leaves the mediator's own
`security.egress.*` rows as the record and appends *nothing* — an
unlisted sink is an egress fact, not a telemetry event.

**D5 — the partition is innermost-owns.** `turn_phase_profile`
(hh-telemetry `phase.rs`) claims wall in the contract's nesting
order — permission waits innermost, then sampling, then
tool-blocking, then compaction; the unclaimed wall splits by position
against the sampling span into `before_first_sampling_ms`,
`between_sampling_overhead_ms`, `idle_after_sampling_ms`. `Σ members =
turn wall` holds by construction; a stamped boundary escaping
`[started_at_ms, finish]` past `clock_tolerance_ms` sets
`clock_skew_flag` — carried, never corrected. Without the boundary
stamps every `*_ms` member renders `n/a{observability}` while the
request/retry counts stay honest.

**D6 — hosted `n/a` is a scoped fold, not a new view.**
`metric_view_scoped` applies the catalogue's `applies_to` gate per
participant class: a native-only metric over hosted rows renders the
typed `n/a{class}` cell — never a fabricated zero (T-LCD-15;
ADR-0044). The pre-R2.14 `metric_view` delegates with
`ParticipantClass::Native` and is byte-identical. Catalogue
distributions fold per `configuration_id` — `n`/`min`/`p50`/`p95`/`max`
over each configuration's own prefix, so two configurations never
pool silently.

## Consequences

- Every residual emitter class (M5/M6/M13/M14/M15) now mints its
  durable row from the producing path — `context.assembled`,
  `context.compaction.*`, `action.environment.*`,
  `context.memory.*`, `control.wakeup.*` — with measured members
  stamped, so the view folds have honest duration sources.
- A malformed `traceparent`/`tracestate`/`_meta` carrier can never
  parent a span; the `carrier_loss` vocabulary names exactly what was
  dropped (preservation without authority).
- The only `measurement.export.delivered` append sites remain the
  canonical ones: `deliver`/`commit_kernel_row_for`. Egress refusals
  cannot mint false delivery rows — the battery pins this
  (`unlisted_sink_is_an_egress_refusal_never_a_delivered_row`).
- `turn_phase_profile` is a pure fold: reopening the run replays it
  byte-identically (rebuild equality, AC-R-2.9.1-1's rule).
- Live sinks stay operator-gated: the fixture-verified ceiling is
  unchanged — the subscriber's only observed wire is the mediator's
  loopback transport.

## Alternatives considered

- **A dedicated exporter transport beside the mediator** (the
  subscriber opens its own socket): rejected — §5h.1 §6 rules a sink
  a `net_egress` destination under EP3; a second wire would bypass
  attribution, leak-scan and the durable `security.egress.*` record,
  and would mint deliveries with no mediation evidence.
- **`measurement.export.delivered` appended by the mediator itself:**
  rejected — the mediator knows egress, not export semantics (sink
  policy, seq ranges, loss reports); the subscriber owns the export
  fold and appends only after `Forwarded`, keeping the row's meaning
  ("this batch was delivered") exact.
- **Renaming the env/wakeup scope members to one spelling:**
  rejected (CC8) — landed rows already carry `env_handle` and both
  `subscription_id` spellings; resolving both keeps the pre-R2.14
  durable prefix foldable.
- **Synthesizing missing boundary stamps from `ts`:** rejected — the
  §2.6 contract forbids `ts`-derived durations (mixed clocks);
  unbounded intervals are honest, fabricated stamps are not.
- **A separate hosted metric view:** rejected — one fold with a
  participant-class parameter keeps the native path byte-identical
  and makes the `n/a{class}` rendering a gate, not a fork (CC1).

## Revisit trigger

Reopen if (a) a real external observability backend lands and the
subscriber's loopback fixture graduates to a live sink (the egress
mediation contract stays — only the transport widens), (b) OQ-115
closes on a different trace-id derivation and the carrier rendering
changes (the `hh` member spelling is unaffected — it carries ledger
coordinates verbatim), or (c) new scope kinds or emitter classes
extend `MEASUREMENT_POINTS` and the `turn_phase_profile` claim order
needs a deeper nesting level than the current four.
