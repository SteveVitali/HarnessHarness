# ADR-0423: Retroactive — TB 2.0 → `terminal-bench-core==0.1.1` substitution (89 → 80 tasks) and the `catalogue_identity` label correction (AR-01)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-07 (`retro:` recorded after the fact — see Context)
- **Ticket:** R6.3 (manifest row 255; experiments supplement §10.5 AR-01)
- **Requirement ids:** REQ-XA-116 (absorbs REQ-XA-013)
- **Spec:** experiments supplement §4.1, §9.3, §10.5 AR-01; CANONICAL_SPEC unchanged
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** non-contract note — a retroactive record of an execution-time substitution; no runtime contract moves rung

## Context

The frozen experiments supplement §4.1 names the TB 2.0 edition and
imports "the 89-task TB2 catalogue identity". The phase-1 executions
(tranches t1–t3, the four-epoch S4 cohort) actually ran against
`terminal-bench-core==0.1.1` — **80 tasks** — a substitution made at
execution time with no ADR, readout, or ticket record (research/24 F13,
VERIFIED). Two further record defects ride with it:

- the manifests' `catalogue_identity` is a 64-bit FNV-1a over the
  sorted task-id list **printed with a `sha256:` prefix**
  (`sha256:bd18094423eaca3c`) — it pins names, not task content, and
  the label is misapplied;
- no content identity was recorded for either the 30-task selection or
  the 80-task catalogue until R6.2.

BM-CLOCK-01 `retro:` evidence: this ADR is written 2026-11-07 about
execution-time facts of 2026-11-05/06. The evidence for what ran is
committed — every S3/S4 manifest declares `terminal-bench-core==0.1.1`
(`suite_ref`/`dataset` members in the committed packets, e.g.
`docs/build/reports/2026-11-05-s4-confirmation.json`); R6.2's
`hh-dataset-digests/1` (`docs/build/reports/provenance/
2026-11-06-scratch-drivers/dataset-digests.json`) records the
sha256tree/1 content digests `60d47248…` (30-task set, 294 files) and
`f419eda1…` (80-task catalogue, 795 files), committed beside the FNV
`catalogue_identity`, never replacing it.

## Decision

Record the edition substitution retroactively: the executed benchmark
edition is `terminal-bench-core==0.1.1` (80 tasks), substituted for the
TB 2.0 (89 tasks) the frozen supplement names. The recorded reason
(the operator's session choice — the brief's "TB 1.0" identification of
core 0.1.1 was taken on the brief's word and not verified externally,
research/24 F13) and the mislabelled `catalogue_identity` are both
recorded here. The frozen supplement §4.1 stays **untouched** — the
frozen record is corrected by this ADR, never rewritten. Content
digests under `sha256tree/1` are the content identity going forward;
the FNV value stays a name-list identity with its mislabel disclosed.
Future manifests name their edition and carry a correctly labelled
content digest (REQ-XA-105; the committed runner's packets).

## Consequences

- The phase-1 record (`docs/results/phase1-execution-surface-replay.md`)
  cites this ADR in its bounds statement 11.
- Phase-2+ manifests and packets use `sha256tree/1` content digests
  with a correct label; nothing may rely on `catalogue_identity` as a
  content identity.
- A phase-1b tranche, if run, binds its own edition explicitly (R ≥ 5,
  ε pre-registered — REQ-XA-126).
- The substitution is recorded, not endorsed as an equivalent: 80 ≠
  89 tasks; the executed population is the recorded one.

## Alternatives considered

- **Amend the frozen supplement §4.1** — rejected: frozen records are
  corrected by ADR/append, never rewritten (§9.3, REQ-H-004).
- **Leave the substitution unrecorded** — rejected: research F13 marked
  it a record defect; an unrecorded edition makes every count cite the
  wrong population.

## Revisit trigger

A phase-1b tranche or any later pass that executes a different TB
edition (e.g. TB 2.1 under the §3.8 re-proof) must bind its edition,
content digest and R in a pre-registration before data, and cite this
ADR as the precedent for edition corrections.
