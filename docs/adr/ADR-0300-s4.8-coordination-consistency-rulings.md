# ADR-0300: S4.8 multi-agent coordination & consistency rulings

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Ticket:** S4.8
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.6.5, R-2.6.2¹
- **Spec:** §05e.5; §05e.2 (`R-2.6.2¹` — AC-R-2.6.2-11); ADR-0191…0193;
  ADR-0106…0108; ADR-0211 (D-6)

## Context

S4.8 lands the C3 coordination slice: `scoped_subtree`/`share`
environment modes with `resource(key)` leases, the
`three_way_text{line}` and `validator_selected` merge arms, the crash
matrix, HLC stamping under `share`, the `judged` loop-detector arm,
`model_fallback`, and the hosted boundary envelope. The ticket had to
decide where `resource(key)` locking lives (the ledger's existing
scoped-lease fold vs a new table), how `share` provisions a workspace
(copied view vs shared roots), where detach refusal is enforced, what
`merge_hash` is computed over, how an unrankable consistency-level swap
classifies under K-4, how a judged detector acts without a bound judge,
and how a hosted participant's unknown budget dimension lifts into the
native stop-reason vocabulary.

## Decision

- **`resource(key)` is a scoped lease, not a new table.** The
  `resource:<key>` scope string flows through the existing
  `lifecycle.lease.acquired/closed` fold (`hh-ledger/src/leases.rs`); the
  only new surface is `scoped_lease_holders(scope)` — the *cross-run*
  live-holder scan — plus `scoped_leases_for(run, holder)` for the
  terminal drain. Spawn step 4c acquires every `reserved_keys[]` lock
  all-or-nothing in canonical sorted order on the parent's ledger under
  the parent's writer lease, with `holder = subagent:{child_run_id}`. A
  live record in *any* run contends (the local fold alone would let a
  sibling `share` writer race); a record this spawn minted is adopted on
  retry (KP-21's nothing-new rule); failure releases the prefix and
  refuses `ResourceLockTimeout` — a refused spawn holds no key
  (AC-R-2.6.5-8). `record_child_result`/`record_child_cancelled`/
  `cancel_unresponsive` release the child's locks in the same breath as
  the ledgered terminal (O-5).
- **`share` derives the parent's roots verbatim.** `EnvDriver::derive`
  for `DeriveMode::Share` clones `parent.roots` unmodified — one shared
  mutable view (§5e.5). Write coordination is the ownership +
  `resource(key)` layer *above* the fs, never a copied or narrowed view
  (`scoped_subtree` keeps its narrowed-roots semantics). The old
  `Unsupported{derive.share}` refusal is replaced by the real arm;
  `fresh_from_image`/`fork_snapshot` behaviour is unchanged.
- **`share` activates HLC on the parent.** `Store::require_hlc(run_id)`
  seeds `hlc_node`/`hlc_last` at first `share` spawn (K-2 — the parent's
  coordination record must causally order against shared children). It
  is a no-op when stamping is already on (the clock's `hlc_last`
  continuity is preserved); events before the call stay `hlc`-free —
  they predate the shared coordination the clock orders. Non-`share`
  modes never activate.
- **Detach refusal is a *spawn-time* refusal.** `on_parent_end =
  detach_to_child` under `share`/`scoped_subtree` is refused
  `ModeUnsupported` before any reservation (O-2): a detached child could
  keep writing shared territory with nobody holding its lease — the
  spec's closed answer is refusing the spawn, never a late fence.
- **Ownership writes are `check_write`-gated with a three-way verdict.**
  `WriteVerdict::{Ok, NotOwner, Fenced}` distinguishes a missing grant
  from a stale-generation write after transfer; denials append
  `security.policy.evaluated` rows (`record_write_refusal`).
  `control.ownership.{granted, returned}` join `transferred` in the
  class table (the granted conferral row cites the per-object
  `transferred` rows it summarizes); the projection folds roots +
  transfer/return rows across every run in the store, so sibling relays
  see the same table.
- **`three_way_text{line}` merges disjoint hunks, conflicts overlaps.**
  The line engine (`merge.rs`) is a diff3 over
  baseline/parent-current/child-after: identical edits merge, one-sided
  edits take the edited side, disjoint hunks merge both sides, and
  *overlapping* hunks produce a `MergeConflictRecord{detector:
  three_way_text{line}}` — never a silent pick (G-2). The merged content
  is stored as a content-addressed blob the report refs.
- **`validator_selected` requires a bound `MergeValidator` port.** The
  port's `select` returns `choose_parent | choose_child |
  supersede{new_ref} | coexist | abandon | abstain`; abstain leaves the
  conflict open in the report. No bound validator is the typed
  `ValidatorUnavailable` veto — never a resolved-by-default. Validator
  observation rows and the `validator_ref` are recorded on the
  resolution/`control.merge.proposed` rows.
- **`merge_hash` is content-addressed over the canonical report
  projection** — sorted merged refs, conflict ids, absent set — so it is
  independent of child completion order and identical when the report is
  re-folded from the durable prefix after a crash (AC-R-2.6.5-2/-4/-9).
  Every input lands in exactly one of `merged[]`/`conflicts[]`/`absent[]`;
  a hole vetoes `LostWrite{count}` (G-1). `on_absent_child =
  block_completion` leaves `absent[]` children unmerged and the
  completion gate blocked; `annotate` is admitted but classifies as
  loosening under K-4.
- **K-4 direction is per-diff and never guessed.** `coordination_dir` in
  `hh-hir::diff` classifies loosening on: consistency level moving down
  the strength lattice, `messaging.sibling`/`broadcast` enabling,
  `max_pending`/`depth_cap`/`fan_out_cap` raising, `default_isolation`
  widening toward `share`, `on_absent_child` → `annotate`, or a merge
  policy moving toward a weaker resolver. `causal_cross_run` and
  `strong_eventual_union` rank *equal* — a swap between them is `none`,
  never an invented direction; per ADR-0211 (D-6) ownership stays per
  coordination object within one activation.
- **The `judged` loop detector is a `JudgePort`, cadence-bound,
  clamped.** `LoopPolicy.judged{validator_ref, interval_turns,
  confidence_threshold_ppm, charged_to}` declares the arm;
  `detect_with_judge` runs it at the declared cadence (a prior hit
  suppresses re-fire inside the interval) on the same global
  `[nudge→deny→stop]` ladder, stamps `validator_ref` + observed
  confidence on the `control.loop.detected` row, and clamps a judged
  `stop` rung to `deny` (ADR-0108: a judged detector is never sole
  grounds for a stop). `judge: None` with a declared spec emits nothing —
  the detector does not fire unbound, and below-threshold confidence
  emits nothing either. The four deterministic detectors are untouched.
- **`model_fallback` is a recorded `attempt_delta`, executed by the
  driver.** `DeltaKind::ModelFallback{profile_ref}` is admitted only when
  `attempt_delta_allowed` names it (`schedule_retry` gives up otherwise —
  never a silent alteration). On the retry's dispatch the driver finds
  the `control.retry.scheduled` basis row, appends
  `model.rerouted{model_call_id, to_profile_ref, cause: model_fallback,
  basis, charged_to}` and stamps `profile_override` on the re-assembled
  request — the call keeps its `model_call_id` and the attempt number
  advances (AC-R-2.6.2-6's identity rules unchanged).
- **The hosted boundary lifts unknown dimensions honestly.** The member
  path `lift_stop_reason_json` maps hosted `max_tokens`/
  `max_turn_requests` terminals to `budget_exhausted{dimension:
  "unknown"}` — the participant's word names no kernel `DimensionId`, so
  none is fabricated (the raw spelling survives on
  `stop_reason_raw`/`dimension` members; the typed `lift_stop_reason`
  table stays for callers that *know* the dimension). Boundary-budget
  exhaustion cancels the participant through the Hosting ABI's own
  `session/cancel` verb — `cancel_via`/`cancel_ok` record the attempt on
  the exhaustion row, never a synthetic drop. `loop_stop_rate`/
  `loop_nudge_recovery_rate`/`format_failure_rate` are declared metrics
  folding over `control.loop.detected`/`control.output.rejected`/
  `model.call.requested`; an empty denominator renders typed
  `n/a{estimator_undefined}` — a hosted run without observable tool calls
  never fabricates a zero (AC-R-2.6.2-11's metric half).
- **`lab/coordination-topology-v1` binds the closed four-member
  `topology` factor.** `{single_agent, orchestrator_worker_isolated,
  orchestrator_worker_share, peer_messaging}` × `merge_policy` ×
  `default_isolation` over the existing T0/T1 presets under
  `MatchSpec{matched_total}`; child usage folds `charged_to = subject`
  through `fold_arm_usage`, and the arm report's
  `lost_write_count`/`conflicts_open_at_completion`/`absent_children`
  are folded from durable `control.merge.completed` rows — never
  remembered counters. `coordination_match_report` re-runs the shared
  `match_report` gate (missing `MatchSpec` or unaccounted dims refuse)
  and stamps `kind: coordination_topology_v1_arm_report` the
  `ComparisonReport` cites (the `benefit_kind` member is enforced at the
  experiment-engine claim gate).

## Consequences

The spawn path's refusal-before-side-effect ordering (K-1 declaration
check → reservations → ownership grants → step-4c locks → HLC → derive →
durable rows) makes every crashed or refused spawn replayable from the
prefix, and the cross-run holder scan makes `share` contention a ledger
fact rather than a runtime race. Merge reports are pure projections —
re-folding after a crash reproduces the same `merge_hash` — and the
`absent[]` accounting means a missing child can never be silently
merged. The judged detector inherits the common ladder so its hits are
indistinguishable in shape from deterministic ones while its `stop`
clamp keeps ADR-0108's never-sole-grounds invariant structural. Hosted
boundaries fail closed: an unknown dimension is `unknown`, an
unobservable rate is `n/a`, and cancellation travels the adapter's real
verb.

## Alternatives considered

- **A dedicated `resource_lock` table beside scoped leases.** Rejected:
  it would be a second lease mechanism with its own fencing and rebuild
  rules; the scoped-lease fold already carries holder/expiry/closed and
  the cross-run scan is a projection over the same durable rows (CC1 —
  one scheme per concern).
- **`share` as a copy-on-write or narrowed view.** Rejected: a copied
  view cannot express the shared-mutable invariant `share` exists to
  test, and narrowing re-implements `scoped_subtree`; the spec's split
  is verbatim roots + above-fs coordination.
- **Detach refusal at the drain call site.** Rejected: the child would
  already hold shared locks and workspace access when the refusal lands;
  refusing at spawn (before reservations) is the spec's closed answer
  and keeps refused spawns side-effect-free.
- **`merge_hash` over event ids or append order.** Rejected: both depend
  on completion order; the canonical `{merged, conflicts, absent}`
  projection is the only input whose identity the AC requires.
- **A forced ranking between `causal_cross_run` and
  `strong_eventual_union`.** Rejected: the classic hierarchy leaves them
  incomparable; inventing a direction would classify admissible swaps as
  loosening (or vice versa). Equal rank → `none`.
- **Judged `stop` admitted.** Rejected by the spec — ADR-0108's clamp
  keeps a probabilistic detector from being sole grounds for a stop;
  `deny` still escalates the pattern honestly.
- **Guessing the hosted dimension (`model_calls` for `max_tokens`).**
  Rejected: the participant does not know the kernel's dimension
  registry; `dimension: unknown` + the preserved raw spelling is the
  T-LCD-07 lift.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen when a Stage-5/6 slice lands the `escalate` resolution channel
(H7) or additional merge resolvers; when the critic tickets bind real
`Validator{kind: judge}` implementations to `JudgePort`/
`MergeValidator`; when hosted adapters can name kernel `DimensionId`s
(the `unknown` lift narrows); or when `lattice` membership for
`causal_cross_run`/`strong_eventual_union` is ratified one way (the
equal-rank `none` becomes a decided direction).
