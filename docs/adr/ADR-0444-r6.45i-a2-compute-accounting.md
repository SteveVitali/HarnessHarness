## Context

FP-9 / REQ-XH-118 capped first-pass compute at **$20 as the Σ of the windows' declared ceilings**, each ceiling carrying residual-disk and pre-authorized-restart terms. Through the Wc2–Wc8 scope amendments the Σ of declared ceilings reached ≈ **$38.38** — every gate recorded the exceed honestly — while recorded `cost_lib` estimates (PHASE_SPEND) total ≈ **$13.85** and the billed figures are lower still (windows consistently land ~44–65% under ceiling; the ceiling is a dead-man bound, not a price). Under the old rule D_max at GATE-G30 could not fit W4 even after a clean re-proof: REQ-XH-126(b) counts unbilled completed windows at their ceilings, and $38.38 + W4's $7.99 ceiling exhausts $20 twice over. The choice was a $20 accounting artifact, not a spend reality.

## Decision

R-5 (amended, verbatim in the GATE DECISIONS row) — first-pass compute accounting:

- first-pass compute ≤ **$30**;
- **completed windows count at HUMAN-H18 billed figures where a reconcile read exists, else at their recorded `cost_lib` estimate (PHASE_SPEND) — never at their ceilings**;
- authorized-but-unrun windows count at their ceilings;
- the model authorization stays ≤ **$70**; the **$10** unauthorized reserve is unchanged;
- the first-pass total ceiling is **$110** (Σ authorized ≤ $100).

The recorded Σ-of-declared-ceilings exceed history (≈ $38.38 across Wc–Wc8, each exceed recorded at its gate) is **superseded by this accounting, never erased** — the GATE DECISIONS rows, run ledgers and manifest notes keep their bytes. Amends FP-9 / REQ-XH-110 / REQ-XH-118's application / REQ-XH-126(b) / CAP6.2's recompute / the X-02 invariant ($100 → $110), each by register row and dated note — no spec text or landed record is rewritten.

## Consequences

- R6.46 computes D_max under the amended rule: completed windows at billed else `cost_lib`, Wc's reservation released except one restart-overflow reservation only when unauthorized (unchanged), authorized-but-unrun at ceilings.
- GATE-G28j's readout carries the A2 budget table; GATE-G30's does the same at FP-E.
- HUMAN-H18's reconcile list gains Wc8, smoke1–3 and Wc9; reads for Wc2–Wc7 may be appended (each ≥ 24 h past teardown) and supersede their estimates — billed figures always win where they exist.
- CAP6.2's closure recompute reads: Σ authorized ≤ $100, Σ spent ≤ $110, billed figure wherever a bill exists.
- Planning view (UNVERIFIED until billed): W1…Wc7 ≈ $13.85 + Wc8 ≈ $0.5–2.0 + smokes ≤ $3.50 + Wc9 ≈ $2.5–3.2 + W4 $7.99 ≈ $29.5 ≤ $30.

## Alternatives considered

- **Hold the Σ-of-ceilings rule and stop the pass** — wastes ≈ $13.85 of completed work on an accounting artifact; the ceilings were engineered headroom, not spend. Rejected by the ruling.
- **Top up the $20** — explicitly rejected: "no top-up" stands inside the amended totals; the amendment changes *what is counted*, not the discipline (dead-man ceilings per window stay exactly as declared).
- **Count completed windows at ceilings anyway** — the superseded rule; it made the safe bound the binding one. Rejected.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit if a billed figure lands materially above its `cost_lib` estimate (the accounting then under-counts — surface it at once), if the $30 compute or $110 total bound needs to move (a new ruling, never a silent stretch), or at CAP6.2 when all billed reads exist.
