# ADR-0267: S2.5 audit-checkpoint rulings — the C0 HMAC construction, the claim shape, `final`-after-`finished`, the consistency-verified-extension gate, derived tombstones, the signer-custody seam, watermark truncation, the independent auditor fold, the pin set, and the ops surface

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
- **Ticket:** S2.5
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.8.6 (AC-R-2.8.6-{1,2}; removability(0…3))
- **Spec:** §5g.6 (the audit trail: tree heads, `security.audit.checkpoint`,
  proofs, cross-run anchors, `lifecycle.ledger.{redacted,gc}`,
  `AuditCompleteness`) · ADR-0066…0068 (the owning rulings) ·
  ADR-0026…0029 (events/ids) · ADR-0058 D2 (`kernel_use`) ·
  ADR-0213 (OQ-170, kernel signer key custody — OPEN)
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** **C0** — compact-range heads, signed
  checkpoints, proofs, anchors, auditors and ledger GC are Stage-2/C0
  machinery (witness cosigning/receiver receipts stay C2; the anchor
  *reciprocal* producer rows land with their owning tickets).

## Context

S2.5 lands the signed-checkpoint layer of the audit trail. The spec fixes
*what* must hold (signed tree heads, logarithmic proofs, anchors, honest
completeness, durable-before-delete retirement); the mechanism choices
below were left to the ticket:

1. **The signature construction.** §5g.6 names `security.audit.checkpoint`
   and `signatures[]` but the C0 construction is not fixed, and ADR-0213
   (OQ-170) leaves kernel signer key custody **open** — "the key is an
   R-2.8.3 kernel-internal channel with `readers = {kernel}` used through
   `kernel_use(purpose = ledger_signing)`" (ADR-0058 D2).
2. **The claim shape.** Which members the checkpoint binds (tree head vs
   chain tip vs self-id vs prior claim vs anchors) and how `final` relates
   to `lifecycle.run.finished`.
3. **Who may sign.** Whether the kernel signs any presented head or only a
   head it can substantiate against the durable log.
4. **Tombstone persistence.** `get_blob`/`audit_view` must answer
   `redacted`/`gc` after a restart, but no second authoritative store may
   exist (ADR-0066 D1).
5. **Truncation semantics.** When a short committed prefix is a verdict
   versus a valid log.
6. **The auditor's independence.** What "the writer cannot overwrite what
   the auditor already holds" means mechanically.
7. **The pin set.** Which references hold a blob against `gc`/`redact`.
8. **The embed surface.** Which of the staged Group-R ops go live and what
   stays honestly staged.

## Decisions

**D1 — the C0 signature is `hmac-sha256` over the canonical unsigned
claim.** `hh-wire` gains `hmac_sha256` (RFC 4231-tested) built on the one
SHA-256 primitive (CC1). `alg_ref` spells `hmac-sha256`; the signed
preimage is `checkpoint_sig_preimage` — the canonical bytes of the claim
minus `signatures`/`idp` (`crates/hh-ledger/src/tree.rs`). Symmetric keying
matches the ADR-0058 D2 custody seam (`kernel_use` hands the kernel key
bytes; the ledger never stores them). Witness cosignatures and asymmetric
schemes are C2 and compose as additional `signatures[]` members — the
claim shape does not change.

**D2 — the claim binds `(tree_size, tree_head, chain_hash, idp,
prev_checkpoint, cross_run_anchors, signatures)`.** `tree_size` equals the
checkpoint row's own seq — the checkpoint covers the durable prefix that
ends just before itself, so coverage is derivable, never asserted.
`tree_head` is the RFC-6962-style MTH of the covered leaf hashes;
`chain_hash` is the event-hash chain tip at coverage; `idp` is the
self-id over the unsigned claim; `prev_checkpoint` links the previous
claim's `{event_id, tree_size, tree_head}` (first claim carries `null`);
`cross_run_anchors` lists `{other_run, other_head{tree_size, tree_head},
relation}` for every `lifecycle.run.forked`/`control.subagent.spawned`
row in range whose child this store holds. `verify_run` re-derives every
member — a wrong size/head/chain/idp/link is `CheckpointInvalid`, a head
the log cannot substantiate is `ForkEquivocation`, an empty `signatures`
is `SigMissing`, a bad entry is `BadSignature`.

**D3 — `final` lands after `lifecycle.run.finished` and covers it.**
`kind = final` requires `finished` committed; non-final kinds are fenced
post-finish (`RunFinished`); `final` is idempotent (a second call returns
the landed row) and must close the stream — any row after it is
`CheckpointInvalid`. A `finished` run that declared `signer_key_ids` with
no `final` is `Truncate` — the terminal signed head is absent-with-later-
head.

**D4 — the kernel signs only heads it can substantiate.** `checkpoint`
recomputes the previous claim's head over the current leaves and requires
a verifiable consistency proof from the held head to the new head before
signing; divergence is `InconsistentHead`, never a signed fork.

**D5 — tombstones are derived state.** `Store.tombstones` is rebuilt at
`load_all` from the durable `lifecycle.ledger.{redacted,gc}` rows — the
ledger remains the only authoritative store (ADR-0066 D1). Both ops are
durable-before-delete: the record commits to the WAL first, then blob
bytes are removed, then the tombstone is recorded. `get_blob` reads the
map so a retired address reports `Missing{redacted|gc}`, never a bare
absence (ADR-0068 R3).

**D6 — custody is a trait seam, never ledger state.** `AuditSigner`
(`key_id`/`sign`) and `AuditKeyResolver` (`verify_key`) are the ledger's
custody boundary (`crates/hh-ledger/src/audit.rs`); `FixedSigner`/
`KeyTable` are the test implementations. Production wiring resolves the
key through `CredentialBroker::kernel_use(KernelPurpose::LedgerSigning)`
when ADR-0213 lands custody — DF-S2.5-1. `verify`/`audit_view` take an
optional resolver: absent, signatures report `unverified` (shape-checked
against `signer_key_ids`/`alg_ref`), never fabricated `verified`.

**D7 — truncation is a watermark verdict.** `verify_run(run, from, to)`
treats the caller's watermarks as claims: a committed prefix shorter than
either bound is `Tampered{truncate}` (absent-with-later-head). A bare
shorter prefix with no watermark still verifies — the honest split the
spec requires between "the log is internally consistent" and "the log
holds what a witness saw".

**D8 — the auditor holds its own fold.** `Auditor` keeps its own leaf
list, compact range, held heads and observed claims; `observe` replays
`EventFrame::Durable` rows re-checking seq density (`Gap`), envelope-hash
recomputation and run identity (`Equivocation`/`Malformed`), and on a
checkpoint row recomputes the signed claim against its own head — a
verified claim becomes a held head the writer cannot overwrite.
`Store::audit_with` feeds the durable prefix; the auditor never reads the
store.

**D9 — the pin set is `refs`/`content_refs` across other runs plus
`audit_fields` members.** `pin_reason` scans every *other* run's envelope
`refs` and declared `content_refs` members for the address (a live fork
prefix member pins), and any `audit_fields` member naming the address
pins it unconditionally — structural audit data is never collectable.
Own-run `content_refs` are the very things `redact`/`gc` retire — not a
pin. `redact` on an `audit_fields` member is `NotRedactable`
(AC-R-2.8.6-2); endorsement bases are `approval` (principal-or-above
endorser) and `policy_rule` (kernel scanner); anything else is
`IllegitimateEndorsement`.

**D10 — the embed surface goes live for reads only.** `audit_view`,
`verify`, `prove_inclusion` and the new `prove_consistency` are
implemented stable ops (`verify` returns the `Tampered` taxonomy as data
— `{verdict, at_seq, kind}` — a corrupted ledger still answers).
`request_redaction` stays `staged_experimental` (the store-level op is
landed and tested; the endorsement-ingress surface belongs to S2.6's
approvals item) and no `gc` op is added — ledger GC is a store call, not
a session op. Checkpoint *emission* is a `Store::checkpoint` call; the
embed layer does not mint checkpoints until the custody wiring lands
(DF-S2.5-1), so `open_session` runs declare no `signer_key_ids` and
`audit_view` honestly reports `checkpoints: n/a{no signer_key_ids
declared}`.

## Consequences

- `verify` now covers the checkpoint layer: signed heads the log cannot
  substantiate, stripped/malformed/mismatched signatures, broken claim
  links and absent-with-later-head truncation are all typed verdicts.
- `audit_view` is a real dossier: `checkpoints[]` with per-claim
  `verification_status`, `cross_run[]` with `verified|failed|
  unresolvable`, tombstone-aware `content_refs` accounting
  (`present`/`redacted`/`gc`/`missing`), and the component-vector
  `completeness` with honest `n/a` reasons.
- Removability: everything is unconditional — `hh-ledger` still builds
  and refuses C1 surfaces under `--no-default-features`; no new feature
  surface is introduced (removability(0…3) unchanged).
- Deferrals opened: DF-S2.5-1 (custody wiring + embed checkpoint emission
  + `request_redaction` op), DF-S2.5-2 (the I-A6 reciprocal anchors —
  `control.subagent.spawned.parent_head_at_spawn` ↔ child
  `lifecycle.run.created.parent_anchor` and `result/cancelled.child_head`
  ↔ the child's `final` checkpoint — are producer-side and land with the
  subagent-lifecycle ticket).

## Revisit trigger

Reopen when ADR-0213 lands kernel signer custody (DF-S2.5-1 — D1's
symmetric `hmac-sha256` construction and D6's trait seam must be
re-argued against the ruled custody model, and embed-side emission
becomes mandatory), when the subagent-lifecycle ticket lands the
reciprocal anchor producers (DF-S2.5-2 — the `cross_run_anchors`
member set must then cover every anchor kind), or if a witness/
cosignature scheme enters scope (C2 — the claim's `signatures[]` list
is the composition point, and `alg_ref` becomes a real negotiation).
