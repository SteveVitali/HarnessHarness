## Context

§7.1 names three entry choices — run a useful task, inspect a recorded
result, learn offline — with prerequisites and readiness visible first,
and requires evidence cards exposing exact dated scope,
admission/limitations/access and one of four public states. §1 selects
the one primary job (the technically prepared harness evaluator) and
the 22-family/nine-later-group/six-scenario portfolio with per-family
dispositions and revisit conditions. REQ-C-008/REQ-C-101 bind the
honest-release rule: in a partial release the S1 useful-task and S3
compaction-study objectives stay unmet — a fixture or setup success is
never a substitute — and the operator's rescope is recorded. Before
this ticket no reader-facing surface carried the entry choices, the
gate-state vocabulary, the cards, or the maintained coverage table;
the dispositions lived only inside the frozen supplement.

## Decision

1. **`docs/evidence.md` is the claim-index page** — the spec-proposed
   name, landed as a living doc. It carries (a) the closed four-state
   vocabulary (`Available` / `Not yet available` / `Restricted` /
   `Unsupported`, each with its qualifier), (b) the three entry
   choices in a marked `<!-- spine -->` block, (c) eleven
   `### Card —` records in the exact State/Scope/Evidence/Limits/
   Access field shape — useful-task, study and operations are
   `Not yet available` with reasons; component, interchange and
   evidence-access are `Restricted`; setup/EX-01/EX-02/receipt/
   contribute are `Available` with scoped citations, (d) the selected
   job with its inferred-demand label, (e) the maintained capability
   portfolio — all 22 families with entry/contract/limit/disposition,
   nine later groups, six scenarios — and (f) the
   benefit/overlap/exclusion/revisit claim→source map.
2. **The README carries the reader spine.** A marked
   `<!-- spine -->` block lists the same three choices, readiness
   first, plus a failure/report exit; the committed quickstart blocks
   are untouched. `docs/README.md` gains the navigation + living-mode
   rows; `docs/status.md` and `docs/getting-started.md` point at the
   page.
3. **Unmet objectives are preserved mechanically.** The
   `<!-- unmet -->` partial-release clause names S1/S3 unmet, cites
   the recorded operator dispositions (GATE-G8/G9/G10 + the round-5
   partition), and forbids fixture substitution — REQ-C-008/C-101.
   No scientific claim, favorable-treatment language, or
   external-demand assertion is permitted on the surface.
4. **`scripts/check-position.sh` is the static both-modes check.**
   Thirteen legs: canon (the 22/9/6 tables parsed from the frozen
   supplement — never mirrored by hand), entries, spine_page, cards,
   labels, gates, portfolio, unmet, sources, honesty, links, nav.
   Work and `--head` (committed-HEAD clean clone) modes;
   `scripts/ci/test-check-position.sh` mutates seeded trees for every
   named failure; the ci.yml step is `ci-mode: both`.
5. **Later contributor evidence stays owed.** The formative
   participant records (R5.53) and the publication-record legs
   (R5.54) own the contributing-only ids' R/P predicates; this ticket
   records engineered D/E only (I-07).

## Consequences

- Readers see readiness before investment; a docs-only head that
  drops an entry choice, invents a state, marks a gated surface
  available, deletes a family, or weakens the unmet clause fails CI.
- The portfolio table is a living maintained copy whose canon is the
  frozen spec — the checker derives names/dispositions from the
  supplement, so drift between them is a defect, not a quiet fork.
- The claim→source map keeps benefit/overlap/exclusion/revisit claims
  cited to dated research/design sources.

## Alternatives considered

- **A separate `docs/portfolio.md`** — rejected: §7.1's proposed page
  set names `evidence.md` as the claim index; the coverage table is a
  section of it, keeping one new surface and one nav row.
- **Editing `status.md` to carry the cards** — rejected: status.md is
  the availability/evidence-class account; the cards are the §7.1
  gate-state surface with a different update cadence. status.md links
  to the page.
- **A gated (`mode != 'fast'`) CI step** — rejected: the checker reads
  committed content a docs-only head can drift; `ci-mode: both` is
  the landed precedent.
- **JSON `evidence-card/1` records** — rejected: the spec's "exact
  cards" are reader-facing documents with dated scope, not a new wire
  contract; a second card schema would duplicate example-card/1.

## Revisit triggers

- If the entry-choice set or gate-state vocabulary changes (a new
  state, a renamed route, EX-03/EX-04 landing at R5.45), re-read the
  `entries`/`labels`/`gates` legs — the closed sets assert the current
  vocabulary verbatim.
- If a card's state legitimately changes (S1/S3/S4 gated evidence
  lands, a component/interchange is selected), the owning ticket
  updates the card and the checker's `REQUIRED` map together —
  flipping `Not yet available` without its named evidence is the
  fixture-substitution defect this check exists to catch.
- If the supplement's §1 tables are amended, re-read the `canon`
  extractor — the portfolio derives names and dispositions from the
  frozen spec, never a second table.
- If docs/README.md or the navigation shape is restructured, re-read
  the `nav` leg — it pins the page's presence in both tables.
