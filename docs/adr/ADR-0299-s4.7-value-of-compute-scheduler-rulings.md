# ADR-0299: S4.7 value-of-compute scheduler rulings

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Ticket:** S4.7
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.6.4
- **Spec:** §05e.4; ADR-0188…0190; ADR-0041 M3; ADR-0046 §7

## Context

S4.7 lands the C3 value-of-compute scheduler — `compute_policy` with
`static` + `rules`, the `compute_estimator`, `ComputeDecisionRecord`,
`TaskValue`, and the `lab/value-of-compute-v1` recipe over
`{static, uniform, rules}` — under §5e.4's seam: the driver invokes the
bound policy **between `decide` and `envelope.check`**. The ticket had to
decide where the contract lives, how `advise` travels without breaking
crate-layer discipline, where the `matched_cap` searched-arm refusal is
enforced, and how the registry learns the two new classes without implying
a plugin-corpus resolution path the kernel does not use.

## Decision

- The scheduler contract lives in `hh-control::compute`, next to its only
  caller. `bind` is a driver-hot-path call on every `decide` output; a C3
  leaf crate would either be a dependency of the C0 driver (forbidden — a
  lower-tier driver must not depend on a C3 crate) or an orphan.
  `static` keeps the packaged-default invariant: the driver short-circuits
  on an empty `options_supported` capability set, so a `static`-bound run
  emits zero `control.compute.*` rows and stays byte-identical modulo ids
  (AC-R-2.6.4-1).
- `advise` is a `ComputePolicy` trait member returning a typed
  `ComputeAdvice` (`{decision_point, option, estimate, rules_fired}`,
  `KIND = "compute_advice"`). hh-control does not depend on hh-context and
  the `compute_advice` candidate kind is a dialect-bump member of the
  closed `CandidateKind` sum, so the advice record — not a `ContextItem` —
  crosses the seam; the context builder lowers it through a
  `definition`-authority `HarnessRule` when an advising variant lands.
  Every C3 variant advises nothing: the default returns `None`, never an
  error (the contract row's error column is `—`), and advice never
  affects `bind`.
- The `matched_cap` searched-arm rule (AC-R-2.6.4-12) is enforced inside
  `hh_budget::matchspec::validate_match`, not by a lab-side blanket rule.
  `matched_cap` equality now spans the union of the declared match
  dimensions **and every hard-cap dimension appearing in any arm's
  `search_budget`**: equal undeclared search caps (including all-zero)
  admit, unequal caps — a positive search budget against a zero-cap or
  absent-cap comparand — refuse `UnequalCaps`, surfaced at `register` as
  `IncommensurableMatch`. A positive `search_budget` under `matched_total`
  remains legal; only `matched_cap` comparability is gated.
- `compute_policy` and `compute_estimator` are `ClassRecord`s registered
  **floors-only** in the hh-registry corpus (no suite, no variants —
  the `validator`/`execution_alignment`/`compaction_strategy` pattern).
  The packaged `static`/`uniform`/`rules` implementations are first-party
  in-crate variants resolved by `hh_control::compute::policy_for`; the
  corpus records the class contract so `slots["compute_policy"]` bindings
  have a resolvable class and the staged `bandit`/`surface_prior`/
  `predictor` variants have a registration target at Stage 5/6.
- Scheduling telemetry uses §5e.4's canonical six names —
  `scheduling.overhead`, `scheduling.decision_count`,
  `scheduling.option_share`, `scheduling.regret_vs_oracle`,
  `scheduling.prior_coverage`, `scheduling.advice_compliance` — declared
  `applies_to = {native}` so hosted participants render typed
  `n/a{class}` (T-LCD-06/-07/-15).
- `TaskValue` is a `TaskContract` member projected at seal from the goal
  node's extension (`from_reversibility` default); a missing task value
  yields `unknown` estimates or `TaskValueMissing` → `Unchanged`, never a
  guessed value.
- A spawned child binds its own `compute_policy` from its sealed
  `harness_def` — the child manifest stamps `compute_policy_ref: None` at
  spawn; inheritance from the parent's binding would attribute a slot the
  child's process never declared.

## Consequences

The driver gains the decide → bind → envelope.check ordering without
widening the C0 dependency graph, `static` removability is a structural
short-circuit rather than a testable accident, and every bound decision is
re-checked by the envelope before persistence (B-3 enforced at the seam,
not by convention). The searched-arm guard is commensurability, not
presence: `matched_cap` arms that all declare equal search caps still
compare, while an arm whose search spend escapes the declared dimensions
is refused at registration. Registry consumers see the two classes with
their real contracts; nothing about variant resolution changes at C3.

## Alternatives considered

- **A new `hh-compute` leaf crate.** Rejected: the driver calls `bind` on
  the hot path; the leaf would either be depended on by hh-control
  (tier-violating) or house a re-export façade no consumer uses.
- **A blanket `hh-lab` rule rejecting `search_budget > 0` under
  `matched_cap`.** Rejected: it would refuse symmetric declarations the
  spec admits — the invariant is cap *equality* over the union of capped
  dimensions (ADR-0046 §7 commensurability), not the absence of search.
- **Emit `ContextItem` directly from `advise`.** Rejected: it inverts the
  hh-control → hh-context dependency direction and mints a candidate kind
  the closed `CandidateKind` sum does not yet declare.
- **Registry corpus variants for `static`/`uniform`/`rules`.** Rejected:
  first-party in-crate variants resolve through `policy_for` like other
  kernel classes' packaged defaults; publishing plugin-corpus variants
  would fabricate a resolution path nobody executes.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen when a Stage-5/6 variant lands (`bandit`/`surface_prior`/
`predictor` — prior cells, `control.compute.prior_reset`, estimator model
calls and the `advise` arm), when `matched_total` joined comparison across
searched and unsearched arms needs a second rule, or when `compute_advice`
enters the `CandidateKind` sum by dialect bump.
