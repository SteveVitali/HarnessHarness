# ADR-0343: R2.11 approval-UX + monitor-acceptance residuals — the `modify` terminal, the durable intent anchor + `label.endorsed{basis: approval}` mint, `check_write` at `prepare` in `hh-monitor`, and the remedy-ingress seam R2.12 consumes

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Ticket:** R2.11
- **Requirement ids:** DF-S1.23-1 · DF-S1.11-2 · BL-22 · BL-23 ·
  R-2.8.1 · R-2.8.5 · R-2.8.7
- **Spec:** §5g.7 §3 (the approval lifecycle — `pending`/`decided`,
  `ApprovalOption`/`ResponseChoice`), §5g.1 §9 (the `approval` basis +
  `label.endorsed`), §5g.2 §3 (the closed `Remedy` sum), §5e.5 (check 8
  `check_write` at `prepare`) · ADR-0070 D1 (the approval-option sum —
  `modify` is its C2 member) · ADR-0191 D5/O-1…O-6 (the ownership
  records `check_write` folds)
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** C0 — the landed semantics run at the
  offline/fixture ceiling; the option sum now spans the declared C0–C2
  members.

## Context

R2.11 closes the approval-surface and Π-acceptance residuals that share
the `hh-monitor` decide path. Four owned decisions:

1. **`ApprovalOption.modify` (DF-S1.23-1's C2 member).** ADR-0070 D1's
   closed option sum carried `modify` as a declared-but-unimplemented
   member — the response enum, the monitor's `Decision` sum, the
   durable `decided` payload, and the dispatcher's recorded-decision
   arm all lacked it. The shape question is whether an amendment is an
   in-place allow (a mutating answer) or a terminal decision that
   re-enters as a fresh request.
2. **`label.endorsed{basis: approval}` (DF-S1.23-1's C1 member).** The
   legitimacy *rule* was landed (non-`human(principal)`/`ApproverGrant`
   endorsers refuse `IllegitimateEndorsement`) but no call site minted
   the row — an allow ended at `security.permission.decided` +
   `granted`. The endorsement needs a durable subject whose provenance
   is the *intent's own* label — an approval raises a participant's
   proposed effect intent to the responder's authority; there was no
   such subject in the store.
3. **`check_write` at `prepare` (DF-S1.11-2).** The C3 ownership
   machinery (declared roots, `control.ownership.{granted,transferred,
   returned}` rows, `OwnedObject`/`WriteVerdict`) landed with
   `hh-subagent`'s merge path, but the §5e.5 check — the dispatcher
   refusing a write that lands under another holder's record or a
   superseded writer generation — had no call site. `hh-env` cannot
   depend upward on `hh-subagent`, so the pure machinery's home needed
   a ruling.
4. **The remedy *ingress* surface (the R2.12 seam).** §5g.2 §3's closed
   `Remedy` sum had no durable home on the approval lifecycle: an ask
   that offers remedies must carry them on the `pending` row and echo
   them on `decided`, or R2.12's consume path (`remedies.taken`
   re-dispatch) would read a session cache — a second seam R2.12 is
   forbidden to build.

## Decision

**D1 — `modify` is a *terminal* decision, never an in-place edit.**
`ResponseChoice::Modify{amended_args}` (mirrored in the boundary's
`PermissionOutcome::Modified`) records `Decision::Modified` on the owed
`security.permission.decided` row — `amended_args` verbatim
(`DECIDED_FIELDS` admits it as bounded bytes). The as-proposed effect
refuses `action.effect.refused{reason: modified}` +
`action.tool.rejected`; the amendment re-enters as a *fresh* request
that re-runs `authorize` — narrowing-only by construction, since the
decision itself confers nothing and the re-proposal is a new intent.
Three consequences held load-bearing: (a) the legitimacy gate covers
`Modify` exactly as `allow*` — a delegate-voiced `modify` would burn
the exactly-one owed decision, so principal/`ApproverGrant` is required
to *decide*, not just to confer; (b) `modified` is admissible only when
`modify` was *offered* — the same `OptionNotOffered` gate `selected`
answers, read off the durable `request.options`; (c) a recorded
`modified` serves idempotently — the monitor fold restores
`Decision::Modified` and the dispatcher's recorded-decision arm
produces the same terminal `KernelDecision`.

**D2 — the endorsement subject is a durable `action.intent.anchored`
row, minted *before* `pending`; the mint happens at the produce-time
respond call site.** The egress ask path appends
`action.intent.anchored{permission_id, effect_id, subject_ref,
capability_ref, args_canonical_hash}` stamped with the *participant's*
provenance (`Origin::participant` at delegate authority,
`ContentKind::EffectIntent`, run scope) — the intent is the
participant's proposal, never a kernel claim (the class is durable but
deliberately non-audit-grade: its provenance must stay below kernel
authority). The respond path — one shared `approval_endorsement`
helper used by `respond_permission` and `surface_respond_permission`
(CC1) — resolves the anchor by `permission_id`, raises its label to
the responder's authority through `hh_provenance::endorse`, and appends
`security.label.endorsed{basis: approval, basis_ref: permission_id,
endorser, subject_ref: anchor.event_id, from, to}` in the same batch as
`granted`. `NoLabelIncrease` mints nothing (`Ok(None)` — an allow that
cannot rise is not a lie to record); every other illegitimate
endorsement refuses typed. No anchor ⇒ no endorsement — never
fabricated.

**D3 — the ownership machinery's home is `hh-monitor`; check 8 runs at
`prepare` over `FsPathPrefix` + `Resources::Declared` keys.**
`OwnedObject`/`OwnershipTable`/`WriteVerdict`/`check_write` moved to
`crates/hh-monitor/src/ownership.rs` (the monitor is the TCB that owns
checks; `hh-subagent` re-exports so its merge-path producers keep one
home — CC1, and the dep direction stays downward). The dispatcher's
`set_ownership_roots(roots)` arms the regime: armed, stage `prepare`
projects the roots plus every `control.ownership.*` row *across the
store's run tree* (a child sees the parent's grants to it — the
depth-3 chain the DF row names) and runs `check_write` over the
effect's write objects — the canonical `path` of an `fs_write` as
`FsPathPrefix`, every `Resources::Declared` key as `ResourceKey`. A
non-`Ok` verdict mints `security.policy.evaluated{check: ownership,
verdict: deny, object, detail}` (the typed observation),
`action.effect.refused`, `action.tool.rejected`, and the dispatch
returns `Refused` — the same close-out shape `hh-subagent`'s merge
refusal records. Unarmed (no roots) ⇒ the ownership regime is not in
play and the check does not run — an armed regime is the honest
precondition, never a check that pretends to have run.

**D4 — the remedy-ingress surface is `ApprovalRequest.remedies`,
durable under `pending.request.remedies`, echoed on `decided` —
R2.12's only seam.** The ask's offered `Remedy` set rides
`ApprovalRequest.remedies`; `pending_payload` serializes it inside the
declared `request` audit member, the fold restores it into the pending
`ApprovalRequest`, and the respond paths echo the folded set verbatim
onto `security.permission.decided.remedies`. A malformed member folds
to nothing — a row that offered no remedy, never a fabricated one.
R2.12's consume path (`remedies.taken`, remedy re-dispatch,
`shape_endorsed` stamping) reads this durable record; R2.11 lands the
ingress and deliberately does **not** land the consume leg (out of
scope, R2.12's).

## Consequences

- The `ApprovalOptionId`/`ResponseChoice`/`Decision` sums each gained a
  member — `OptionNotOffered`/`UnknownOption`-adjacent decoders updated
  together; `Json` has no `Eq`, so `PermissionOutcome`/`RespondPermission`
  params are `PartialEq`-only.
- `security.permission.pending` now carries `request.options` +
  `request.remedies` — the offered set is durable, so a folded pending
  (surface answer path, `pending_ask_from_row`) applies the same
  `OptionNotOffered` gate with no session cache. Absent `options` on a
  pre-R2.11 row decodes to the canonical pre-R2.11 set — conservative:
  never implies `modify` was offered.
- A `modified` decision mints **no** grant and **no** endorsement —
  both are `allow`-family emissions.
- The monitor's served-decision path treats recorded `Modified` as a
  terminal fail (`recorded_modified:<pid>` check row), matching the
  dispatch arm.
- `hh-subagent` loses ~200 lines of ownership machinery to the re-export
  shim; `hh-monitor` gains the module. No dependency edges changed
  direction.
- DF-S1.23-1 → DONE (the row's three remaining members all landed);
  DF-S1.11-2 → DONE (check 8 + the depth-3 cell were its last open
  members); BL-22/BL-23 → closed; R-2.8.7 → MET.
- R2.12 (ticket 118) consumes `decided.remedies` — the ingress shape is
  frozen by this ADR; a consume-side need to change it amends by a new
  ADR.

## Alternatives considered

- **`modify` as an in-place allow** (rewrite `canonical` args and
  proceed) — rejected outright: an allow under args the monitor never
  saw evaluated is an authority conferral the human did not voice; the
  terminal+re-enter shape keeps the recorded decision honest and the
  re-proposal inside `authorize`'s own gate.
- **Endorsement subject = the `pending` row itself** — rejected: the
  pending is the monitor's owed-decision record, not the participant's
  intent; the endorsement's `from` must be the intent's own label or
  the rise is fabricated.
- **Anchor minted at respond time** — rejected: the endorsement's
  subject must exist in the committed prefix before the answer (the
  append-time subject lookup + the audit story both require it); late
  anchoring also lets a refused-then-re-asked path mint subjects after
  the fact.
- **Ownership stays in `hh-subagent`, `hh-env` gains an upward dep** —
  rejected: deps point downward; `hh-monitor` is the TCB home for
  checks, and `hh-subagent` re-exporting keeps the merge path on the
  same definitions (CC1).
- **Check 8 runs at `authorize` or `commit`** — rejected: §5e.5 names
  `prepare`; earlier is premature (canonical args aren't final),
  later is post-reservation (the write may already be staged).
- **Remedies as a session-cache side channel** — rejected: durable-or-
  nothing (the whole point of the seam is that R2.12 reads the record,
  not a runtime cache that dies with the session).

## Revisit trigger

- A spec amendment admits a *widening* modify semantic (the amendment
  itself authorizing without re-running `authorize`) → D1's
  terminal/re-enter shape and the legitimacy gate are re-examined; the
  recorded `amended_args` member stays the durable carrier either way.
- A second endorsement basis needing a *different* subject kind than
  `action.intent.anchored` → the anchor class generalizes or a sibling
  class lands; the produce-time mint seam is unchanged.
- The ownership regime gains non-dispatch writers (a host-side mutation
  path that bypasses `dispatch`) → `check_write` needs a call site on
  that path too; the arming contract (`set_ownership_roots`) is
  unchanged.
- R2.12's consume path discovers the `decided.remedies` echo is
  insufficient (e.g. it needs per-remedy binding of the taker) → the
  ingress member gains a recorded `remedies_taken` companion member by
  a new ADR, never a silent reshape.
