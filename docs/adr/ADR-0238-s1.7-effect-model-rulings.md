# ADR-0238: S1.7 effect-model rulings — attempt-vs-effect terminality of `not_applied`, conditional scope closing, `probed` as the `observed` transition, conservative unknowns, read_only E1 exemption

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Ticket:** S1.7
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.2.2, R-2.2.3⁰ᵃ
- **Spec:** §5a.2 (R-2.2.2) · §5a.3⁰ᵃ (R-2.2.3 Stage-1 slice) · ADR-0030 §1/§3,
  ADR-0031, ADR-0032 §1, ADR-0100 I-4, ADR-0130, ADR-0237 (DF-S1.6-1)

## Context
§5a.2 names the effect lifecycle, its terminal set and the write-ahead barrier;
ADR-0030 §1 states invariant 4 (an `unknown` effect redispatches only when
`repeat_safety = idempotent` or a probe returned `not_applied`; `irreversible`
never). Four readings needed concrete rulings because the texts pull in different
directions:

1. **`not_applied` terminality.** The terminal set lists `observed(applied |
   not_applied)`, yet the `compensable` delivery row reads "probe first;
   `not_applied` ⇒ retry **same key**" and the idempotency key is a pure function
   of `(run_id, effect_id, canonical(args), capability_version)` — a retry on the
   same key is necessarily the *same effect* at `attempt_no + 1`. If
   `observed{not_applied}` were unconditionally effect-terminal the compensable
   retry would be unreachable.
2. **`probed` vs `observed`.** ADR-0100 I-4: a late executor report after
   `unknown` is `probed`, never a second `observed`; ADR-0030 §3 says
   `probed{applied|not_applied}` *transitions to* `observed`. The fold and the
   scope bookkeeping need one interpretation (CC1).
3. **Recovery of a non-terminal `observed`.** `observed{partial}` (settlement
   pending) and a retryable `observed{not_applied}` are unresolved at crash; the
   §5a.3⁰ᵃ recovery table must say whether restore fences them `unknown`.
4. **Absent risk data.** Legacy/torn rows may lack a parseable
   `effective_risk_class`; the projection must pick a value, and the choice must
   be the conservative one.
5. **E1's carve-out (DF-S1.6-1).** `exhaust` may proceed while only `read_only`
   effects are open; the risk class is now a typed payload field, so the
   exemption can land for real.

## Decision
- **§1 — `not_applied` is attempt-terminal; effect-terminal only when the class
  forecloses retry.** `observed{not_applied}` and `probed{not_applied}` settle
  the *attempt*; the *effect* is terminal iff `reversibility = irreversible`,
  or `repeat_safety ≠ idempotent` and no probe returned `not_applied`
  (`EffectFold::retryable()` is the one predicate — CC1). A retryable
  `not_applied` leaves the effect scope open and admits
  `committed{attempt_no + 1}` under the unchanged idempotency key; `applied`
  always terminates. `observed{partial}` is never terminal. For
  `probed{not_applied}` the probe itself is the `not_applied` proof, so the fold
  records `last_probe_verdict` *before* evaluating terminality.
- **§2 — `probed{applied|not_applied}` IS the `observed` transition.** The fold
  lands the effect in `Observed{outcome}` directly; no second `observed` row is
  required or admitted for that attempt. `probed{undeterminable}` returns to
  `Unknown` with `probe_count + 1` and leaves the scope open. A direct
  `observed` on a `Committed` attempt is the fast path; after `Unknown` the only
  observation channel is `probed` (I-4).
- **§3 — scope closing is payload- *and* fold-conditional.** One predicate,
  `effect::scope_close_fires(class, payload, fold)`, is consulted by the append
  validator (`check_scopes`), the commit fold (`apply_scope_marks`) and the
  checkpoint view, so the three paths can never disagree (CC1):
  `observed{partial}` and `probed{undeterminable}` never close;
  `…{not_applied}` closes iff the effect is terminal under §1; every other
  `closes_scope = effect` row closes unconditionally.
- **§4 — restore fences every *unresolved* effect `unknown`.** `committed`
  without a terminal and any non-terminal `observed` (`partial`, or a retryable
  `not_applied` whose retry was lost) are fenced `unknown{cause: worker_lost}`
  at takeover; the probe decides the rest. Terminal effects are reported, not
  re-marked. `retry_due` is a pure fold of the durable prefix — a rebuilt store
  and a live one agree byte-for-byte.
- **§5 — missing risk is the most dangerous class.** An absent or unreadable
  `effective_risk_class` (or HIR `EffectAttributes` at projection) maps to
  `RiskClass::UNKNOWN` = `irreversible + non_idempotent + external`; effective
  risk only ever rises via `max_by_danger` — model hints can never lower the
  kernel assessment (AC-R-2.2.2-5).
- **§6 — the E1 `read_only` exemption is typed, closing DF-S1.6-1.**
  `BudgetTree.open_effects` maps `effect_id → RiskClass`; `exhaust` refuses
  `EffectsInFlight` only while an unresolved **non-`read_only`** effect exists.
  Unresolved means every non-terminal phase including `observed{partial}` and
  `probed{undeterminable}`; a missing risk class resolves to `UNKNOWN` and still
  blocks — the conservative reading for unknown effects is preserved.
- **§7 — fencing is per-row, inherited from the writer lease.** Every
  post-`prepared` effect row (`committed`, `observed`, `unknown`, `probed`,
  `compensated`, `reverted`, `abandoned`) carries
  `payload.fencing_token == current writer generation`; stale-generation rows
  are refused `Fenced` at append and at restore-time system writes.
- **§8 — `deferred` and the marker forms.** `deferred` leaves only through
  `committed` (post-promotion/re-authorization) or `refused` (branch discard);
  `compensated`/`reverted` have an in-scope form (open effect) and a marker form
  (`payload.original_effect_id`) that settles an already-terminal effect's
  compensation without reopening its scope.

## Consequences
- The lifecycle is total and rebuildable: `RunState.effects`, `open_scopes` and
  `retry_due` are all pure folds of the durable prefix; live and replayed stores
  agree (CC1).
- A compensable `not_applied` can genuinely retry under the same idempotency key
  — the delivery table's row is reachable, not decorative.
- `unknown` never silently resolves: every `committed` either reaches a terminal
  or an explicit `unknown` (AC-R-2.2.2-3), and restore escalates anything left
  unresolved.
- E1 is exact, not conservative: `read_only` effects no longer pin a run open at
  exhaustion; everything else still drains first.

## Alternatives considered
- **`not_applied` unconditionally terminal; retries are new effects.** Rejected:
  the key is a function of `effect_id` — "retry same key" would be unreachable,
  and a fresh `effect_id` fabricates a new key (a second exactly-once claim for
  the same work — precisely what invariant 4 forbids).
- **A separate `observed` row after `probed{applied|not_applied}`.** Rejected:
  two rows record one fact (CC1); I-4 already names `probed` as the only
  post-`unknown` observation channel.
- **Leave non-terminal `observed` effects untouched at restore.** Rejected: a
  lost settlement or lost retry would leave the scope open forever with no
  durable obligation; fencing `unknown` keeps AC-R-2.2.2-3's "terminal or
  explicit `unknown`" true after every crash.
- **Keep the conservative E1 (any open effect blocks).** Rejected: the typed
  risk class exists now; holding `read_only` intents open against exhaustion has
  no safety value and blocks the drain the spec carves out.

## Revisit trigger
Stage-2 effect/resource leases land (`suspend`/wakeups may give `deferred` and
non-terminal `observed` a parked phase that is not `unknown`); the compensation
saga's Stage-3 execution may give `compensated` an in-flight phase; a spec
amendment making `not_applied` unconditionally effect-terminal supersedes §1.
