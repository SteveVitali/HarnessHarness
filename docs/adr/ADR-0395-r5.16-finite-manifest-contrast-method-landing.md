## Context

Packet D (PADR-R5-4*) admits one finite-manifest efficacy method: per `(task, arm)` cell `c_at | p_at ~ Binomial(n_at, p_at)`, `p_at ~ Beta(1,1)` independent across cells, posterior `Beta(c+1, n−c+1)`; equal-task contrast `Δ^(b) = Σ_t w_t [p_Bt − p_At]`, `w_t = 1/T`; integer-Beta inverse-CDF draws, canonical-input-seeded xorshift64*, 8,000-draw battery with the frozen doubling check through 64,000; labelled 95% equal-tail `credible` interval + analytic posterior mean + observed equal-task contrast; the D.2 owner map lands the estimator in eval math, the `interval_method` member in the owning codec, and the `AnalysisSpec`/persisted-report routing in hh-analysis (seq 185a).

The `aa196851` scoped review ruled SPLIT at that owner map: this leg carries the estimator and the codec member; the routed kind, the persisted-report member and the boundary wiring land at 185a.

## Decision

1. **Contract home: `hharness_eval::finite_manifest`** — `finite_manifest_contrast(pop, decl)` over the R5.15 `PlannedPopulation` (consumed unchanged; the one `(s,f,u)` counting rule `contrib` goes `pub(crate)` — never a second cell rule). `ContrastDecl` carries every declared input records-in; `ContrastError` is the closed refusal set (one task, missing/short/unresolved cells, declared dependence, pooled/hierarchical substitute, unknown method, unsupported prior/level, nonfinite input, credible-as-TOST, metadata-only, unannotated multi-strata).
2. **Frozen ladder interpretation (S.3).** The reported interval is the declared **8,000-draw** estimate ("8,000 posterior draws" is the method's declared count); the doubling check recomputes endpoints at 16,000/32,000/64,000 over the same seeded stream and records each level's max endpoint delta; `draws.used` = 64,000 (actual draws consumed) and `numerical_error` = max delta — a recorded fact, never a silent retry/redraw, never a new tolerance.
3. **`IntervalMethod::FiniteManifestContrast(prior)` dialect bump in `hh-ontology`** — the owning codec (`{finite_manifest_contrast: "beta{a,b}"}`); no generated-schema member (CC7 — the enum never crosses `hh-embed-schema`). A `MetricDeclaration`/cell naming it reports `n/a{estimator_undefined}` — a contrast method is never a per-cell interval (metadata-only support fails, D.2/S.5).
4. **Floors and bounds.** `R ≥ 5` resolved first-slot members per `(task, arm)` cell (§4.1; floor is not power), `T ≥ 2` tasks, unit-rate `BoundsDecl` only (the R5.15 bound contract's own refusal vocabulary reused), strata never silently pooled (`filters.stratum` selects within one declared stratum at the routed leg).
5. **Names land as admitted.** `finite_manifest_contrast` (kind + estimator + `finite-manifest-contrast/1` version), `beta{1,1}` registered / `beta{2,2}` predeclared-exploratory prior names — the Packet D spellings; no second public contract.

## Consequences

- The method computes deterministically under `XorShift64` (seed material = canonical estimator/population/arms/stratum/prior/level/draws/seed/cells record); identical inputs give byte-identical draws and endpoints.
- EX-02 known answers pin the contract: observed equal-task contrast 200,000 ppm (.20) and analytic posterior mean 142,857 ppm (1/7); constant binary cells retain nonzero-width credible intervals.
- Refusals are typed through `ContrastError` — leg (b) maps them onto the persisted-report path unchanged; no warning channel exists.
- Pre-ruled size deviation recorded: leg (a) measured ~1,244 source/test lines against the ≤850 contract trigger — inside the ruling's ≤~1.6× band; recorded here per the ruling, never a precedent for unmeasured growth.
- REQ-D-107 remains **unmet** at full-target scope: this leg proves the method contract; the eval→analysis→persisted-report routed predicate and the R/P/evidence legs are owed to 185a and R5.49/R5.40 per the coverage register. No study or numerical-validation claim is made (GATE-G9 exclusion stands — earned at R5.17/GATE-G10/R5.40).

## Alternatives considered

- **Interval at the doubling cap (64,000)** — rejected: the declared method is the 8,000-draw estimate; the ladder exists to *measure* its numerical error, not to silently upgrade the headline (S.3 "a fact, never a silent redraw").
- **Per-cell rounding before the task delta** — rejected: one-shot rational→ppm rounding per task delta (single rounding rule) keeps the EX-02 known answers exact.
- **Refuse at declaration-validate for cell-level use** — rejected: `n/a{estimator_undefined}` is the established typed refusal for an inapplicable method; a validate-time refusal would break decoding of spec-side declarations the routed leg must read.
