# ADR-0277: S3.3 eval-kernel and benchmark-adapter rulings

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-22
- **Ticket:** S3.3 (`046_S3.3__metric-catalogue-and-compare.md`)
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.9.2 (Stage-3 half) · R-2.2.9.4⁰ᵇ
- **Spec:** §5h.2 Stage 3 (the metric catalogue as distributions,
  compliance metrics, the loss-report export, `compare`, the three
  benefit kinds, `equivalence_run`, `render_scorecard`, deterministic
  oracles, pass^k/interval/tails, fault and perturbation profiles) ·
  §5h.4 Stage 3 (`benchmark_adapter` variants A/C/D/E out of process
  with `materialize`/`expose`/`collect_submission`/`grade`, `separate`
  verifiers, the infra detector, parity reports, L4)

## Context

S3.3 lands the Stage-3 evaluation kernel (`hh-eval`) and the benchmark
adapter boundary (`hh-bench`). The owning ADRs (ADR-0042…0047
telemetry/eval, ADR-0142…0144 bench integration, ADR-0158 estimator
selection, ADR-0213 interim rules, ADR-0209 D2 staging) fix the
contracts; the mechanism choices below were left to the ticket:

1. **Which `hh-embed/1` ops carry the eval surface.** §5h.2 names the
   ops (`compare`, `equivalence_run`, `render_scorecard`) but the Group
   L table already declares `lab.analysis.*`/`lab.results.*` as the
   §06 store-backed surface staged to S3.4a–c. Whether the Stage-3
   halves ride the declared-but-unimplemented `lab.analysis.*` names or
   a records-in/records-out `lab.eval.*` group needed a ruling — the
   summary-level scope tension (older comments homed the analysis
   service at S3.4c; the canonical S3.3 spec assigns Stage-3 `compare`
   and `render_scorecard` to S3.3).
2. **Where the report records live (CC7).** `ComparisonReport`,
   `ScorecardReport`, `MetricCell`, `ConfigurationSummary`,
   `ParityReport` are schema; `hh-eval` is the computation crate.
   A second schema home would violate CC7.
3. **The `arm_specs` param form at the embed boundary.** `compare`
   consumes `hh_budget::validate_match`, which wants `ArmSpec`s with
   budgets and `MatchSpec`s; the records-in surface needed a param
   shape that stays records (no second budget DSL).
4. **The out-of-process adapter transport.** `ClassRecord.locality =
   out_of_process` is fixed; the wire form for
   `materialize`/`expose`/`collect_submission`/`grade` was mechanism.
5. **The deterministic oracle boundary.** Deterministic (C0) oracles
   must produce identical verdicts in-process and out-of-process; the
   request/response shape and the subprocess binary were mechanism.
6. **The interim estimator constants.** ADR-0158/ADR-0213 fix
   `T_clt = 100`, `T_bca = 30`, bootstrap draws 2000/200, and the
   deterministic seeding rule; the concrete quantile/interval formulas
   (nearest-rank, Wilson, integer-beta bisection) needed pinning to
   known-answer tests.
7. **Veto-metric catalogue names.** Veto predicates are also scorecard
   metrics; two rows named `attribution_completeness` (compliance +
   veto) collided under `check_catalogue`'s duplicate-name finding.
8. **Equivalence margins.** OQ-113 (ADR-0213) rules margins come from
   pre-registration data only; the refusal shape for a caller-supplied
   margin needed pinning.

## Decision

1. **`lab.eval.*` is the records-in/records-out Group L surface** —
   `lab.eval.catalogue`, `lab.eval.compare`,
   `lab.eval.render_scorecard`, `lab.eval.equivalence_run`,
   `lab.eval.loss_report`. The params carry canonical-JSON records
   (`eval_run/1` rows, `task_context`/`suite_context` members,
   `Design`/`PreRegistration`/`MatchSpec` records, catalogue metric
   names) and the results are the report records — pure functions over
   lab records, no store reads. The §06 store-backed surface
   (`lab.analysis.*`, `lab.results.*`, `lab.experiment.*`) remains
   S3.4a–c's and stays declared-but-unimplemented; the CLI
   `compare report|scorecard` verbs continue to target those staged
   ops, while `eval catalogue|compare|scorecard|equivalence|
   loss-report <params-file>` drive the S3.3 ops. The scope tension is
   resolved by this boundary: S3.3 owns the *computation*; S3.4 owns
   the *store and engine*.
2. **Schema home is `hh-lab::analysis`; computation is `hh-eval`.**
   `ComparisonReport`/`ScorecardReport`/`MetricCell`/
   `ConfigurationSummary`/`ParityReport` live in
   `crates/hh-lab/src/analysis.rs` with canonical JSON codecs;
   `hh-eval` consumes them. `MetricCell` carries `stratum` — the L5
   capability render is per stratum.
3. **`arm_specs[]` members decode `{match_spec, caps{dim→int},
   native?}`** into `ArmSpec`s whose search/eval budgets are the same
   hard-cap `BudgetSpec` — the eval boundary's form (the comparison
   precondition is equal eval budgets anyway). Richer budget forms
   remain records inside `hh-budget`; no second DSL at the boundary.
4. **`hh-bench-adapter` is a newline-JSON stdio binary** (the
   `hh-mcp-serve` precedent): one request per line
   (`{op, adapter, params}`), one response per line. In-process calls
   and subprocess calls share the one dispatch so parity is by
   construction; the conformance battery asserts response equality.
5. **`hh-eval-oracle` is the deterministic-oracle subprocess** sharing
   `hh_eval::oracle`'s request/response codec with the in-process path;
   the acceptance test asserts verdict equality across both
   (`oracle_parity` — AC-R-2.9.2 deterministic-oracle half).
6. **Estimator conventions pinned to known answers:** nearest-rank
   quantiles on a sorted copy (`median` = `quantile(·, 500_000)` — the
   lower element on even n); Wilson score intervals; Bayesian beta
   intervals by deterministic bisection over an integer-parameter
   regularized incomplete beta; `pass^k = C(c,k)/C(n,k)`;
   `pass@k = 1 − C(n−c,k)/C(n,k)`; clustered CLT over per-task cluster
   means; bootstrap via xorshift64* seeded from the canonical-input
   SHA-256. Interim constants stay ADR-0158/0213's.
7. **Veto metrics are namespaced in the catalogue** (`veto.<id>`
   spelling) so `check_catalogue`'s duplicate-name finding stays live;
   the canonical veto ids are unchanged.
8. **`equivalence_run` refuses a caller-supplied margin** — margins
   come from the pre-registration record only (OQ-113); the verdict is
   the three-valued `equivalent | not_equivalent | inconclusive`.

## Consequences

- The `lab.eval.*` ops are pure — they need no store, engine, or
  results projection, so they land now without borrowing S3.4a–c scope.
  The records-in shape also makes the ops trivially replayable (the
  same params file reproduces the same report, hash-for-hash).
- `hh-embed` takes `hh-eval`, `hh-budget`, and `hh-lab` deps; the
  dispatch arms are thin decoders into `hh_eval` calls — no eval maths
  in the boundary crate (CC7).
- Adapter verifiers run `separate` by construction (subprocess), and
  infrastructure failures are typed `InfraFailure`s, never confused
  with task failures.
- The split-hash rule is strict: a run whose `split_hash` differs from
  the pre-registered hash refuses with `SplitHashMismatch` — presence
  in the observed set is not sufficient.
- Known residual: `arm_specs`' hard-cap form does not express every
  `BudgetSpec` variant (ceilings/tokens_blended) — the records-in path
  accepts the budget records directly when richer forms are needed;
  widening is additive (CC8).

## Alternatives considered

- **Route the Stage-3 halves through `lab.analysis.*` now.** Rejected:
  those ops are declared as the §06 store-backed surface (S3.4a–c);
  implementing them as records-in shims would land the wrong contract
  under the right name and block the store-backed halves behind a
  breaking change.
- **A bespoke eval DSL for arm specs.** Rejected: a second budget
  language at the boundary violates CC1/CC7; the `caps` object is a
  projection of the canonical records, not a new grammar.
- **In-process-only oracles/adapters.** Rejected: `locality =
  out_of_process` and the separate-verifier rule are spec-fixed; the
  shared-codec subprocess gives parity by construction.

## Revisit trigger

S3.4a–c lands the store-backed `lab.analysis.*`/`lab.results.*`
surfaces; if a caller needs eval over persisted rows rather than
records-in params, the projection ops belong to those tickets — this
ADR's `lab.eval.*` group then remains the pure-kernel surface, and any
rename of the staged ops is a new ADR.
