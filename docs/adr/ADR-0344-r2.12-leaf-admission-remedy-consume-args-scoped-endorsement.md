# ADR-0344: R2.12 IFC residual — the `labels_leaves` walk's observation-plane refusal, the `action.param.anchored` endorsement subject, the args-scoped `shape_endorsed` fold, and the remedy-consume terminal shape

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Ticket:** R2.12
- **Requirement ids:** DF-S2.7-1 · BL-20 · R-2.8.2
- **Spec:** §5g.2 §2 (the IFC admission walk, the `validator` basis's
  `external → environment` rise, D-ROBUST) · §5g.2 §3 (the closed
  `Remedy` sum, `decided{remedy_taken}`) · §5d.5 (observe's second
  output — `context.observation.recorded`) · §5g.1 I-H7 (one final
  decided per attempt) · ADR-0054 D1 (`L(r) = L⁺(p) ⊔
  default_authority`) · ADR-0269 (the S2.7 taint-labeling rulings) ·
  ADR-0343 D1/D2/D4 (the `modify` terminal shape this consume arm
  mirrors, the intent-anchor subject pattern, the remedy-ingress seam
  this ticket consumes)
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** C0 — the landed semantics run at the
  offline/fixture ceiling; the consume path exercises the durable
  approval-ingress surface end-to-end in-process.

## Context

R2.12 discharges DF-S2.7-1's three open members — the declared halves
landed at R-2.8.2/S3.11a (the closed `Remedy` codec, `remedy_taken` on
`DecisionRecord`/`DECIDED_FIELDS`, `shape_endorse`'s bounded emitter,
D-ROBUST's `shape_endorsed` exemption, R2.11's durable
`pending.request.remedies` offer). What remained was produce-time
machinery with real decisions in it:

1. **Where a leaf refusal lives.** A `labels_leaves` contract makes
   admission *per-leaf*: the shaped `structured_result` must sit under
   the declared `output_schema`'s leaf positions. But the walk runs
   *after* the effect committed and observed — `action.effect.refused`
   mints only from `Intended`/`Authorized`/`Deferred` (the leaf
   lifecycle rule); a post-`committed` refusal is lifecycle-illegal.
   The refusal still must be typed — never silent.
2. **What a `shape_endorse` take endorses.** `security.label.endorsed`
   re-validates `subject_ref` over the committed prefix at append —
   the subject must be a durable, provenance-bearing record whose
   label is honestly the param's `from`. `FlowInputs.param_labels`
   carry the label but are produce-time inputs, not records; a
   fabricated param reference fails `check_endorsement`.
3. **What `shape_endorsed` binds.** The fold stamps param *names* for
   D-ROBUST — but an endorsement minted for one dispatch's args must
   not launder across a later dispatch's re-supplied args (the
   endorsed value may differ entirely).
4. **What the endorsed label does to the live proposal.** A
   `shape_endorse` take's *effect* is a label rise — if the proposal's
   `param_labels` keep the pre-endorsement `external`, the remedy's
   purchased fix never reaches `authorize` (the joined `eff` still
   drags to `external` and the Π gate re-asks — the consume would be
   dead code).
5. **What a remedy answer *decides*.** §5g.2 §3's sum spans six kinds;
   the respond path needs a `Decision` for each. `approval{effect_id}`
   is the human's allow; the other five refuse the as-proposed args
   (the remedy re-shapes the *request* — re-dispatch, re-supply,
   endorse — the `modified` arm's terminal/re-enter shape, ADR-0343
   D1). And a take must be consume-once: a second effect claiming the
   same take is a replay.

## Decision

**D1 — a non-admissible leaf refuses typed on the observation plane.**
`observed{status: error{class: conflict, kind: admission_leaf:*}}`
carries the refusal — `conflict` is the class the observation channel
already owns for shaping disagreements; `kind` names the failing
position (`uncovered:/path`, `unchecked_keyword:<kw>`,
`no_shaped_result`, `no_leaf_grammar`, `malformed_result`, `budget`).
`context.observation.recorded` mints `admission: leaf_refused` with
the `leaf` member naming the failure and `label` honestly absent —
the result carries no admitted label. `action.effect.refused` is never
minted post-`committed`. The walk itself is schema-guided over the
OQ-219 admitted keyword subset (`CONFORMANCE_ADMITTED_KEYWORDS` — CC1,
one admitted list shared with `conforms`), recursing
`properties`/`items`/`additionalProperties:<schema>`, bounded at 1024
leaves / depth 64, and every leaf records `{path, admission, label}`
on the recorded row — structural positions only, never result bytes.

**D2 — the endorsement's subject is `action.param.anchored`.** A new
durable class (provenance-mandatory, non-audit — an audit-grade stamp
would force `authority = kernel` over the param's `external` label, a
fabricated `from`; hosted-lowering `hint` like its
`action.intent.anchored` sibling — ADR-0343's revisit trigger named
this case). The anchor carries `{effect_id, args_canonical_hash,
param, anchor_kind: shape_endorse}` with the param's own label as its
provenance; it appends first so the endorsed row's `subject_ref`
resolves over the committed prefix.

**D3 — `shape_endorsed` binds args-scoped, never param-name-global.**
`shape_endorsed_params(events, args_canonical_hash)` resolves each
`validator`-basis endorsed row's `subject_ref` to its
`action.param.anchored` and discharges the `robustness_inputs[]` names
only when the anchor's `args_canonical_hash` equals this dispatch's —
an endorsement for one argument shape never exempts another. The fold
re-checks `capacity_bits ≤ cap_max` (the durable record is the
authority; the emitter's word is never trusted).

**D4 — the take's flow effect lifts the proposal's param label.** On
a successful consume, `param_labels[param]` is replaced by the
endorsed `to` label and `param` enters `shape_endorsed` on the
*stamped* inputs `authorize` sees — the durable endorsed row attests
the rise, so the lift is kernel-derived, never a caller claim. The
observation's `L(r)` reads the same stamped inputs
(`execute_capture_observe` takes the proposal's `flow`, not
`input.flow`); the resume path passes `input.flow` (its stamps landed
on the original dispatch — the re-entry mints none). A `shape_endorse`
take that cannot mint its endorsement — unbounded schema, over
`cap_max`, a non-`external` subject, `NoLabelIncrease` — refuses
`remedy_inapplicable:*`: the remedy's attested fix didn't happen, so
the consume is never recorded as a take-with-no-effect.

**D5 — `PermissionOutcome::Remedy`/`ResponseChoice::Remedy` consume is
durable-or-nothing and exactly-once.** The boundary's `remedy` member
decodes through the closed `Remedy` sum (`malformed_remedy:*` on a bad
decode) and must be a member of the durable `pending.request.remedies`
offer (`OptionNotOffered` — the same discipline `selected`/`modified`
answer). `Remedy::Approval{effect_id}` decides `allow` — the approval
endorses only that effect (I-F6 R4) under the same `scope = once` rule
`allow_once` answers. Every other kind decides `deny{reason:
remedy:<kind>}` — terminal, re-enter under the attestation (ADR-0343
D1's shape). The egress respond path applies the same arms.
`decided{remedy_taken}` is the consume attestation; `DispatchInput.
remedy_taken` is admitted only when a durable `decided` row carries
the canonically-identical take (`remedy_not_attested:*` otherwise) and
no *other* effect's `authorized{remedy_taken}` already consumed it
(`remedy_replayed:*`); the authorized row restates the take — durable
rows both sides.

## Consequences

- `context.observation.recorded` gains `leaves[]` (absent-not-null —
  a `labels_leaves = false` contract mints the pre-R2.12 shape
  byte-identically) and the `leaf_refused` refusal spelling; both are
  non-audit member additions on a non-audit row (no partition entry
  owed).
- `EFFECT_FIELDS` admits `remedy_taken`; `DECIDED_FIELDS` already
  carried it (S3.11a's carry half).
- The consume path is monotonic in one direction: a remedy burned is
  burned (`replay` refuses even when the first consume's effect was
  later refused downstream — the take is consumed at `authorized`).
- A suspended dispatch that consumed `shape_endorse` leaves the
  anchor+endorsed pair durable; a re-dispatch under the same take
  re-endorses a *fresh* anchor (the `subject_ref` differs) — honest
  duplication, never reuse.
- DF-S2.7-1 → DONE: (a) the per-leaf walk mints/verifies leaf records,
  (b) the consume producer exists end-to-end, (c) the produce-time
  caller stamps `FlowInputs.shape_endorsed` from durable rows.
- Schema change: `PermissionOutcome` gains the `remedy` variant —
  `schema/hh-embed-1.schema.json`, `schema/compat-1.matrix.json`, and
  the generated client regenerated (CC7), additive at contract-major 1.

## Alternatives considered

- **Mint `action.effect.refused` post-commit anyway** — rejected: the
  leaf lifecycle rule admits `refused` only from
  `Intended`/`Authorized`/`Deferred`; the observation-plane error is
  the honest channel and `conflict{kind}` already carries typed
  detail.
- **A param-name-global `shape_endorsed` fold** — rejected: it would
  launder an endorsement across re-supplied args; the anchor's
  `args_canonical_hash` is the recorded scope and costs one lookup.
- **The caller re-supplies the endorsed label** — rejected: a caller
  claim of `environment` without the durable row is unverifiable at
  `authorize`; the kernel stamps the rise from the row it just minted.
- **`NoLabelIncrease` proceeds silently** — rejected: the authorized
  row would record `remedy_taken` against a fix that never happened;
  refusal keeps take ⇒ effect honest.
- **Non-`approval` remedies decide `modified`-style re-entry** —
  rejected: `deny` is already terminal + re-enter; a second terminal
  spelling adds a decode arm for no new semantics.
- **A bespoke leaf grammar independent of `conforms`** — rejected:
  CC1 — one admitted keyword list; an unchecked keyword refuses rather
  than a second spelling of the schema subset.

## Revisit trigger

- A spec amendment admits per-leaf *label* annotations (a leaf-specific
  bound distinct from `L(r)`) → the walk's `label` member splits; the
  walk and the refusal channel are unchanged.
- A `shape_endorse` take on args that must bind tighter than
  `args_canonical_hash` (e.g. per-leaf arg slices) → the anchor gains a
  finer `args` scope member; the fold's equality check generalizes.
- A second consume-carrier for remedies (a surface that takes without
  an `ask` cycle) → `remedy_taken_for`'s attestation rule is reviewed;
  the `decided`-row contract is unchanged.
- `labels_leaves` on a contract whose `output_schema` is delivered
  out-of-band (not on the capability record) →
  `walk_shaped_leaves`' schema source re-points; the walk and refusal
  kinds are unchanged.
