## Context

The dossier's S.1 spine admits **one** planned-slot population and
method-metadata contract that every method ticket (R5.16, R5.36–41,
receipt and study reports) consumes: reconcile planned slots, first
subjects, all attempts and historical (pinned) selection; keep the
three named populations, the six outcome classes, the veto axis and the
availability axis distinct; report equal-task contrast bounds on the
unit-rate target — never a divergent per-consumer predicate.

The run-time scoped review (`4d13371f`, record
`docs/build/planning/2026-11-02_R5.15b-split-review.md`) ruled SPLIT on
the ≤1000-line trigger (measured 1,676) at the documented M-A
contingency + decomposition line-150 "population contract vs consumer
wiring", and **corrected the worker's proposed seam**: the worker's
(a) records+assemble / (b) bounds+metadata+adapter cut misassigned
contract members — `contrast_bounds`, `BoundsDecl` and `MethodMetadata`
are part of the M-A contract scope, not consumer wiring. The whole
eval-side contract stays in leg (a); only the hh-analysis
`planned_population` adapter + its battery move to seq 184a.

## Decision

1. **Contract home: `hharness_eval::population`, sole contract owner.**
   The whole contract lands in one module — records (`Availability`,
   `SlotSubject`, `PlannedSlot`, `PopulationInput`), `assemble`, the
   three `PopulationKind` sets, `contrast_bounds`, `BoundsDecl`,
   `MethodMetadata`, and the closed `PopulationError` refusal set —
   per the M-A contingency's "sole contract owner" requirement and the
   corrected seam. Consumers wire to this surface unchanged (seq 184a);
   no consumer defines a second population predicate.

2. **Records-in, never a store.** `assemble(input)` reconciles
   presented records once: slots keyed `(task, split, stratum, arm,
   replicate, plan, attempt, subject)`; a declared required member that
   is absent refuses `IncompleteUniverse`; duplicate subject keys,
   empty subject/task/arm members and conflicting `(plan, attempt_no)`
   ordinals refuse. Short cells keep their planned/not-run rows — M
   counts *slots*, never attempts.

3. **First-subject / recovery / all-attempts semantics.** The first
   bound subject is primary; the only recovery is the *first
   admissible completed retry* (flagged `recovered`, never best-of; a
   wrong answer never recovers). All bound subjects are retained and
   counted on the attempt-level axes without inflating n.

4. **Pinned-watermark historical selection.** Every resolved subject
   reads at a declared pin: a record above its pin is `HistoryLeak`; a
   resolved record with no declared pin is `WatermarkMissing` — the
   latest-history-leakage arm. Pins republish as `watermarks` so a
   historical analysis reproduces the read.

5. **Three named populations, beside — never folded.** `raw`,
   `canonical`, `joint_task_pass_without_observed_veto` are distinct
   named sets with their own `{s,f,u}` plus beside-counts (`vetoed`,
   `vetoed_passes`, `missing_checks`); the six outcome classes, the
   observed-veto axis and the availability axis stay separate.
   `unknown` is never clean; a vetoed pass leaves `canonical` and is
   reported beside (never a silently improved denominator); missing
   required checks read `unknown` in the joint set.

6. **Contrast bounds on the unit-rate target only.** `contrast_bounds`
   computes `[⌊s/M⌋, ⌈(s+u)/M⌉]` per `(task, arm)` and the equal-task
   contrast `[Σw(L_test−U_control), Σw(U_test−L_control)]` with
   `w_t = 1/T` in outward ppm rounding; whole unrun tasks retain weight
   (a fully unrun task contributes its full-width `[0,1]` cell). The
   target is an explicit `test_minus_control` direction (the
   protocol's B−A); the legacy `compare` a−b path is untouched.
   `BoundsDecl` admits `Unit` (or a `Bounded` that *is* `[0,1]`) only:
   `Unbounded` → `UnboundedMetric`, `RightCensored` → `CensoredMetric`,
   non-unit `Bounded` → `UnsupportedBounds` — no invented finite
   bound, no cap-valued completion.

7. **`MethodMetadata` is the S.1 spine, verbatim.** Every declared
   member lands (`estimator`, `version`, `target`, `assumptions`,
   `prior`, `keys`, `stratum_membership`, `seed`,
   `draws_requested`/`draws_used`, `floors`, `interval`, `refusal`);
   `interval` uses S.6's fourth label kind `descriptive` — the bounds
   are descriptive, never confidence/credible/concentration.

8. **Availability is a separate axis.** `grade_pending` composes as an
   availability/admission state (ADR-0393), never a seventh outcome
   class; pending members count as `u` + `pending_slots`, never as
   resolved failures.

## Consequences

- The contract is exercised directly by its 10-check `hh-eval` battery
  (reconcile/recovery/all-attempts, three populations + veto/checks,
  D1 golden bounds `[−.4,.5]` at M=50, unrun-task weight, explicit
  B−A, and the full refusal set); the hh-analysis adapter lands at seq
  184a consuming this surface unchanged.
- REQ-D-001/002/003/104 and REQ-A-004/102 receive contract-scope
  stamps here; consumer-scope stamps land at seq 184a; no requirement
  is discharged — R/P legs stay owed to R5.49 (D-*) and R5.43 (A-*).
- Zero new event classes (CC10), zero new dependencies, no schema or
  wire change: pure records module.

## Alternatives considered

- **The worker's proposed internal cut** ((a) records+assemble+battery,
  (b) `contrast_bounds`/`BoundsDecl`/`MethodMetadata`+adapter) —
  rejected by the scoped review: bounds and metadata are contract per
  the M-A scope, not consumer wiring; the cut would have landed the
  contract's golden-bounds machinery in the consumer leg.
- **AMEND (land everything in seq 184)** — rejected by the same
  ruling: the M-A packet's documented contingency names the
  contract-vs-consumer split and requires naming a sole contract
  owner; the hh-analysis `planned_population` adapter is consumer
  wiring (ExperimentView × `EvalRun.disposition` reconcile), the
  second leg's independent battery keeps fails-without evidence.
- **`Availability` folded into `OutcomeClass`** — rejected: the
  admitted admission-state model (ADR-0393) keeps pending off the
  six-class axis; a seventh class would corrupt every consumer's class
  accounting.
- **A finite `Bounded` target admitted for other intervals** —
  rejected: a non-unit bound is a different, separately owned
  estimand; admitting it here would silently widen the §3.2 target.

## Revisit triggers

- If seq 184a or a later method ticket needs additional slot members
  (e.g., split weighting, further availability spellings), revisit the
  record shape — additive members land by amendment, never as a second
  predicate.
- If an admitted non-unit finite-bound measure needs contrast bounds,
  revisit `BoundsDecl` — today a non-unit `Bounded` refuses
  `UnsupportedBounds` as a separately owned target.
- If a fourth named population is admitted, revisit `PopulationKind` —
  the three spellings are the S.1 contract.
- If the contrast direction convention changes (control-minus-test or
  normalized contrasts), revisit the explicit `test_minus_control`
  direction member — today the target is never implied.
