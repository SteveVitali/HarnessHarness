## Context

§6.1 binds the all-planned reconcile to one closure predicate: the
set-equality chain `slots→plans→first/all attempts→settlements/
selected grades→rows→analysis` plus four verdict axes per slot —
prerequisite, invocation, task, veto — that never fold into one
another. Retention is the contract: not-run, refused, pending,
orphaned and deviating members stay in the universe; a dropped unrun
slot is a failure, never a smaller denominator. The R5.15/R5.15b
population contract already landed the member-resolution and
counting rule (`eval::population::assemble` + the
`population::planned_population` adapter — X7: never a second rule),
and R5.22 landed the recipient-closure walk over the same owner
views. R5.23 lands the analysis/report owner's reconcile that
consumes them; the R0 lifecycle repair (R5.24), recompute (R5.25),
replay (R5.26), rights (R5.27), usefulness (R5.43) and the outside
reviewer (R5.52) remain their named owners.

## Decision

1. **`crates/hh-analysis/src/receipt.rs` owns the interface.** One
   public entry — `assemble_receipt(&ExperimentView, &[EvalRun],
   &[TaskContext], metric_ref, pins, &DeclaredReceipt) →
   Result<PopulationReceipt, AnalysisError>` — with `pub mod
   receipt` + re-exports in `lib.rs` (+6 lines); nothing else in the
   crate's surface changed. Records pass in — `DeclaredReceipt`
   carries the frozen slot set, per-plan attempt sets, settlement/
   grade/row declarations and the analysis declaration; no crate
   edge is added to hh-bundle or any producer (C.1 item 6 / C.10).
2. **The expected side is derived, never declared.** The receipt
   calls `population::planned_population` (the landed R5.15b
   adapter over the R5.15 contract — same `ExperimentView`/
   `EvalRun`/`TaskContext` views, same `pins` watermarks, same
   verbatim `PopulationError` propagation via
   `AnalysisError::Population`) and folds `PlanState.attempts` +
   `launch_binding.subject_run_id` into the `run_id → plan_ref`
   binding the contract itself uses. No second member-resolution,
   counting or missingness rule exists in the module.
3. **Six reconcile legs, each a set-equality check carrying
   expected/joined/failures.** `slots_to_plans` (a frozen/universe
   slot absent from the declared set is `dropped_slot` — not-run/
   refused/pending members are still required members; an extra is
   `extra_slot`); `plans_to_attempts` (an attempt run binding no
   plan is `orphan_launch`; a bound attempt absent is
   `dropped_attempt`; an attempt declared under a different plan is
   `wrong_member_join`); `attempts_to_settlements` (a settled
   attempt without a settlement record is `missing_settlement`; a
   second record is `duplicate_record`; a `cost_version` ≠ the
   pinned cost-view version is `stale_cost_version`; a settlement
   on an unbound run is `orphan_launch`); `attempts_to_grades` (a
   repeated `grade_ref` or a second counting grade is
   `duplicate_grade`; `counts_subject` on an `ordinal > 1` retry is
   `grade_retry_adds_n` — a grade retry never adds subject n, I-03;
   `counts_subject`/`Selected` on a skipped/format-failure/
   unavailable/pending grade is `grade_status_violation`; a
   selected grade under a non-current `selection_ref` is
   `stale_selection`); `attempts_to_rows` (a settled subject
   without a row is `missing_row`; a duplicate row is
   `duplicate_record`; a row whose declared `plan_ref` differs from
   the run's bound plan is `wrong_member_join`; a bound-not-run
   subject reads as a typed `not_run` row loss, never a pass);
   `rows_to_analysis` (an absent required pin — `prices`, `method`,
   `seed`, `draws`, `weights` — is `missing_analysis_pin`;
   `protocol_ref`/`spec_ref`/`record_ref`/`report_ref` may be
   honestly absent but are bound exactly when present). The closed
   fourteen-code set is exported under `receipt::code`.
4. **The four verdict axes stay separate per slot.** Each universe
   slot carries `SlotVerdicts{plan_ref, member, recovered,
   all_attempts, prerequisite, invocation, task, veto, veto_ids}`:
   `prerequisite` ∈ {available, unavailable, unknown};
   `invocation` ∈ {not_run, started, finished, unknown}; `task` ∈
   {pass, fail, refused, unsupported, skipped, unknown}; `veto` ∈
   {none, observed(+ids), unknown}. A finished invocation never
   erases a skipped/format failure and never writes to the task
   axis; the veto axis never folds into the outcome class.
5. **A reconcile mismatch is a recorded finding, not a thrown
   error.** Every leg mismatch lands a typed `ReceiptFailure{leg,
   code, detail}` on both the leg and the receipt's flat `failures`
   list; `verdict` is `closed` only when `failures` is empty, else
   `failed`. Contract refusals (`PopulationError` —
   `HistoryLeak`/`WatermarkMissing`/member-resolution refusals)
   propagate verbatim — a refusal is never laundered into a
   reconcile finding.
6. **`population-receipt/1` is a canonical derived-index record,
   never a promoted class.** `to_json` emits `{schema, version,
   pins, legs[], slots[], analysis, population{…}, verdict,
   failures[]}` over `hh_wire::Json`; `EVIDENCE_CLASS` is the fixed
   `derived_index` label — a hash match or a finished process axis
   never raises it (AC-2's no-promotion clause). The analysis
   declaration is republished verbatim (`protocol_ref`, `prices`,
   `method`, `seed`, `draws`, `weights`, `spec_ref`, `record_ref`,
   `report_ref`) — bound exactly, never implied or re-derived.

## Consequences

- `tests/r5_23.rs` (9 checks) exercises every AC leg in scratch
  fixtures — compile-red at base `d5f6810a` (the module does not
  exist there): full-chain `closed` reconcile with scored/failed/
  unrun slots and retries, dropped/extra slot, orphan launch,
  duplicate grade and retry-counts-subject refusal, skipped/
  format-failure status violations, stale cost/selection versions,
  wrong member join + missing row, pending/not-run axis truth, and
  the extras-make-no-decision/no-class-promotion leg.
- REQ-E-208, REQ-F-003, REQ-F-004, REQ-F-008 and REQ-C-002 keep
  `planned; unmet` — this ticket records achieved engineered D/E
  separately from the owed legs: the named contributors R5.22
  (landed), R5.24 and R5.43, and every R/P/actual-execution
  predicate. REQ-A-101 is stamped contributing-only.
- No crate edge added (records-in; producer wiring stays the
  hh-embed `assemble` callsite's `evidence_profile: None` seam — a
  later owner's decision), no event class, no schema/wire change,
  no new dependency, no second counting rule (X7), no reverse
  dependency.

## Alternatives considered

- **A contract-layer home in hh-eval (the landed R5.15/R5.15b
  seam)** — rejected: the receipt's joins bind `ExperimentView`,
  `EvalRun`, grade-selection and results-row records hh-eval cannot
  name without a reverse edge; the shared population contract
  already landed and is consumed unchanged (X7), and Packet C C.1
  item 6 / C.8 / C.10 forbid the crate edge a contract-layer cut
  would need.
- **Splitting legs 1–5 from leg 6 + verdicts + codec** — rejected
  at the scoped ruling (`40c095aa`): AC-1 binds the four axes to
  the chain members conjunctively — a legs-only receipt drops the
  named axes, a verdicts-only receipt has no chain to join — and
  the legs leg alone remains over trigger.
- **Counting `skipped`/`format_failure` grades as valid
  non-counting members** — rejected: §6.1's task axis reads them
  skipped/unknown forever; a `Selected`/`counts_subject`
  presentation is `grade_status_violation`, never a process pass
  laundering a semantic loss.
- **Refusing extras by throwing** — rejected: the declared side is
  evidence, and an unbound declared record is an `orphan_launch`
  finding on the record — the receipt retains deviations (§6.1) and
  reports them rather than making a producer-side keep/drop
  decision in extras.
- **Deriving the analysis pins from durable analysis state** —
  rejected: the `rows→analysis` leg binds the declared pins exactly
  (`missing_analysis_pin` on absence); re-deriving them would make
  the receipt a second authority over the analysis declaration.

## Revisit triggers

- If a successor receipt schema (`population-receipt/2`) is
  admitted — e.g. a different declared-side shape once a producer
  export binding lands (the C.1-item-6 seam this ticket leaves to
  its named owner) — amend this record's Decision 1/6; the record
  evolves by version, never silently.
- If R5.24's R0 lifecycle repair or the R5.25/R5.26 recompute/
  replay consumers need a different leg partition, verdict-axis
  vocabulary or failure-code set, re-read Decisions 3–4 — the
  fourteen-code set and the four axes are the contract the
  downstream consumers read.
- If the pinned cost-view/selection identity stops being a single
  comparable version/ref (multi-version settlement or selection
  histories), revisit the `stale_cost_version`/`stale_selection`
  single-pin rule — the pin is the frozen read the declaration
  asserts, not a ceiling.
- If `population::planned_population` (the X7 contract fold)
  changes its member-resolution or counting semantics, re-run the
  whole r5_23 battery — the expected side is that fold verbatim
  and this record's reconcile inherits its refusals.
