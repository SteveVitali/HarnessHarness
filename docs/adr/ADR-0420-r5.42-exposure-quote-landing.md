## Context

REQ-D-105/110/309 + REQ-C-004/105/106 bind §4.5/§9.2/M-I: every
activation, pilot, parity, confirmation and replication stage needs
a per-stage readiness and full-exposure quote *before* its gate can
authorize anything — numeric currency ceiling/prices, all roles/
attempts/regrades/cancellation, CPU/RAM/time/full storage/backups/
retention, both runners/policies/endpoints/strata, `N_subject =
2TR ≥ 10T`, and attainable-precision diagnosis. Unset funds are $0;
missing finite exposure means zero dispatch; there is no automatic
top-up, pooled capacity, guessed margin, cloud fallback or
favorable cohort selection. Before this ticket the spine owned the
method/protocol surfaces the quote consumes (R5.38 directional KL,
R5.39 parity admission, R5.40 numerical validation, R5.41 central
protocol, R5.34 ops cells, R5.27 rights/handoff) but no owner
produced the reusable readiness/quote packet the binding gates
reference.

## Decision

1. **Home and shape.** `hharness_eval::exposure_quote`
   (`crates/hh-eval/src/exposure_quote.rs`) —
   `build_quote(&QuoteDecl) -> Result<ExposureQuote, QuoteError>`,
   pure and total, records-in/record-out. Same cycle-free home as
   the R5.36–R5.41 spine: it consumes the parity `DIMENSIONS`
   vocabulary, `directional_kl::ETA_TOST_PPM`,
   `stats::kl_directional_bound` and the S.1 `MethodMetadata`
   contract unchanged; no new crate dependency — callers resolve
   R5.34 ops/R5.27 rights records and pass evidence refs. The
   module emits no analysis-kind arm: a quote packet is not an
   `AnalysisSpec.kind` — the record travels by `quote_id`
   reference to the binding gates, exactly as `protocol_id`
   does (ADR-0419).
2. **Vocabularies are the spec's own.** `STAGES` (5 — diagnostic is
   not a quoted stage), `PREREQUISITES` (7 — the M-I item-1
   treatment/cap/created→finished/oracle/detector/parity/method
   set), `PINS` (18 — the §9.2 binding-input rows incl.
   `endpoint_map`/`backend`/`rights` mapping onto AC-2's
   `map`/`backend`/`rights` blocker words), `COMPONENTS` (23 — the
   §9.2 resource worksheet) and `ENVELOPE_COMPONENTS` (11 — the
   per-subject/tranche capability bounds). Declaring a member
   outside a closed set is a typed vocabulary refusal, never a
   guess.
3. **Readiness, not authorization.** Each stage computes
   `ready_for_gate | blocked{blockers} | infeasible`; blockers are
   drawn from AC-2's vocabulary (`pins`/`map`/`backend`/`rights`/
   `exposure`/`precision`/`prerequisite:<name>`/`funding`). Unbound
   inputs stay `unresolved`/`unknown` and are echoed verbatim into
   the record — never fabricated. Dispatch is recorded as
   `not_requested | refused{first blocker} | gate_pending{gate}`;
   `gate_pending` names the owed gate (GATE-G12/13/14,
   `unscheduled` for replication) and is never an authorization.
4. **Counts are structurally distinct.** `ab_subjects = 2·T·R`
   (subject floor `2TR ≥ 10T` ⇔ R ≥ 5, confirmation ≤300) versus
   `ho_pairs = T·R` — 300 A/B subjects are not 300 H/O pairs; a
   pairs count on a subject stage or a subjects line on the pairs
   stage refuses `CountConflation`, a declared count disagreeing
   with the computed one refuses `CountMismatch`, and activation is
   the separate one-task quote (`ActivationShape`).
5. **Exposure is priced-or-blocked.** Every required worksheet
   component must be declared; `qty × unit_price_micros` sums per
   stage. Any unpriced/unquoted cell, an unset capability bound, or
   `provider_unknown_exposure` ≠ explicit zero is an `exposure`
   blocker on that stage alone — blocking only the dependent
   action. `currency: None` reports $0 (`total_micros = 0`) with a
   `funding` blocker; totals are integer micros, saturating.
6. **Precision is diagnostic.** The parity stage computes
   `kl_directional_bound` at η = .025 over `{0} ∪` declared
   equal-directional discordances; each endpoint's bound margin
   yields `attainable | infeasible | unresolved` (the stop/requote
   posture — `infeasible` is a stage state, never a waiver). These
   are attainable widths, never power or expected agreement.
7. **Forbidden constructs refuse at construction.** `AutomaticTopUp`
   (`top_up`), `PooledCapacity` (`pool_from`), `GuessedMargin`
   (`assumed_margin`), `CloudFallback`, `FavorableShopping`
   (`outcome_conditioned`), `EnvelopeExceeded` (frozen §9.2
   ceilings: pilot ≤60 subjects/≤200 core-h/≤50 GiB + the eight
   per-subject caps; confirmation ≤300/≤900/≤200 GiB/≤14 days under
   the same per-subject caps; parity/replication quote-only — no
   allocated tranche), plus the malformed-declaration set
   (vocabulary/duplicates/`MissingKey`/`ExposureMalformed`).

> Amended 2026-11-03 — pass-2 self-review repairs (close stint, same
> closed predicate; the refusal surface only grew): the parity
> precision verdict is now a sticky severity lattice
> `infeasible > unresolved > attainable` — a `None` margin declared
> after an infeasible endpoint no longer downgrades the proven
> infeasible (order-independent); `2·(T·R)` and the `subjects_first`

## Consequences

- Same supplied declaration yields a byte-identical record and
  `quote_id` — replayable offline; USD stays $0 absent a bound
  currency; no provider/backend call, no spend, no scientific
  claim.
- The packet is *informative when incomplete*: a blocked stage
  names its blockers and echoes every input state, so the gates can
  bind exactly what is missing (HUMAN-H8) rather than re-deriving
  it.
- An infeasible precision diagnosis preserves the unmet study —
  the stage records `infeasible` with owed stop/requote; nothing
  substitutes a guessed margin or a smaller silent design.
- R predicates: actual bound inputs, actual signed maps, actual
  stage funding and the executed quote evidence remain owed
  (HUMAN-H8, GATE-G18/R5.42a, GATE-G11–G14, R5.47 parity-live,
  R5.49 study report) — this ticket discharges the engineered
  quote machinery only.

## Alternatives considered

- **hh-analysis routed kind:** rejected — a readiness/quote packet
  is not an estimator and has no result rows; `analyze` routing
  would invent a report-shaped surface (same reasoning as
  ADR-0419's non-arm for `central_protocol`).
- **hh-env/hh-bundle home or dependency edges:** rejected — the
  quote *references* ops-cell and rights/handoff evidence by ref;
  consuming crates would add dependency scope for no computational
  need (records-in, CC1/I-01).
- **Blocking the whole packet on one stage's missing inputs:**
  rejected — AC-2 scopes each block to the dependent action;
  per-stage blockers preserve partial readiness (M-I item 5:
  infeasible parity leaves independent S1/receipt work free to
  advance).
- **Free-form component/pin names:** rejected — a closed vocabulary
  is what makes "unquoted" checkable; open spellings would let a
  required line hide under a synonym.

## Revisit triggers

- The actual gated inputs arrive (HUMAN-H8 bindings, GATE-G18
  backend, stage-gate quotes): rebuild under the real decl and
  re-run the battery.
- A `RecordKind`/event-class admission for quote records lands:
  migrate `quote_id` to the admitted kind.
- A §9.2 envelope amendment (new ceilings/stage vocabulary) or a
  second admitted precision method lands: re-scope the ceiling
  table or the diagnostic leg.
