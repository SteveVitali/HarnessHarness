# ADR-0226: S0.3b cross-candidate revalidation disposition — winning decision holds; trigger 5 ARMS on the non-winning candidate E2, routed to GATE-G1 (does not move the decision)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Ticket:** S0.3b
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.12.3 (measurement); OQ-131 (agentic/measurement acceptance)
- **Spec:** §9.1 (Stage-0 gating + revalidation rule), §10.6; `research/synthesis/l1-spike-spec.md` §5; ADR-0050 §3/§4/§6 (trigger 5), ADR-0009 steps 5–8, ADR-0224 (S0.3 verdict), ADR-0225 (S0.3b method)

## Context
S0.3 measured the **winning** candidate offline and found the ADR-0009/ADR-0050 decision holds
(ADR-0224). The operator then released an **online** budget (GATE-G1 row) to measure the
cross-candidate cells S0.3 deferred (DF-S0.3-1, DF-S0.3-3 machine cells). Trigger 5 (ADR-0050 §6):
*"if **any** candidate's measured C5/C7/C12 differs from the assumed value by more than 1, or a gate
fails for the winning candidate, the phase-synthesis agent re-runs ADR-0009 steps 5–7 with the
measured scores substituted (weights frozen)."* S0.3b produced the cross-candidate scores
(`docs/build/reports/S0.3-measurement-sheet.md`, S0.3b extension). This ADR records the disposition
GATE-G1 consumes. It is the owned decision the ticket `## Notes` flags (OQ-131).

## Decision
**The winning decision E5a (E1 kernel + E2 lab) HOLDS / is re-confirmed; trigger 5 ARMS on the
non-winning candidate E2 and is ROUTED to GATE-G1 — the arm does not move the decision.**

- **Winning candidate — HOLDS.** S0.3/ADR-0224 stands unchanged: G1–G4 pass, M-S2-6 pass, M-S2-7
  100 %; E1/E5a C5/C7/C12 within band. S0.3b adds two winning-side confirmations: **gate G1
  cross-candidate byte-identity PASSES** (E2/E3 reproduce the E1 head `f0b9620d…` byte-for-byte), and
  **M-S1-7** (official-SDK MCP/ACP round-trip) is within band. No winning-candidate gate fails.
- **Trigger 5 ARMS on E2 (non-winning).** Measured vs assumed (median, `l1-spike-spec §5`):
  **E2 C5 = 1 vs 4** (|Δ| = 3 — GIL-bounded pure-CPU fan-out 12.2× core-normalized, achieved
  parallelism 0.98) and **E2 C7 = 5 vs 2** (|Δ| = 3 under the §5 note; the matched in-process model
  amortizes the runtime the "interpreter-per-session" citation penalized). **E3 is within band**
  (C5 = 3 vs 4; C7 = 4 vs 3). **C12** for E5b/E5c is transport-favorable and does not contradict the
  assumed 3 (polyglot conjunct deferred, DF-S0.3-2) → no C12 arm. Per trigger 5, the E2 outside-band
  cells require the phase-synthesis agent at GATE-G1 to re-run ADR-0009 steps 5–7 with the measured
  E2 scores substituted.
- **The re-run does not move the decision (compensating analysis).** E2's C5/C7 are *kernel*
  concurrency/footprint criteria; substituting them changes only E2-as-*kernel* candidates (E2
  single, E5c), which already lost. In the winning split E5a the kernel is **E1** (C5 = 5, C7 = 5
  unchanged) and E2 is the **lab** (per-run / I/O-bound crossing — CPU fan-out is irrelevant to the
  lab role). ADR-0050 §4 kernel-family robustness: an E1 kernel wins 0.979 of corners and survives
  every adversarial sweep. So E2 C5 4→1 (worse) and C7 2→5 (better) cannot promote any loser past
  E5a. The arm is a formality that GATE-G1 discharges; the frozen-weight E5a-with-late-bound-surface
  decision is expected to stand.
- **S0.3b arms and routes; it does not perform the steps-5–7 re-run** (that is GATE-G1's owned work,
  §10.6/l1-spike-spec §5) and fabricates no "within band." The measured-outside-band E2 result is
  recorded truthfully and handed to the gate with the substituted scores + this analysis.

## Consequences
- GATE-G1 opens with: winning decision re-confirmed; **an armed trigger 5 on E2** to discharge by
  re-running steps 5–7 with the substituted E2 scores; the compensating analysis showing the winner
  is invariant; and the OPEN R2 human-signature residual to arrange.
- The honesty rules hold: no fabricated "within band," no artifact scored as a decision (the CPU
  fan-out reading is disclosed against the I/O-bound counter-reading), the matched shared corpus +
  formulas (ADR-0225, CC9), and the R2 signature left OPEN.
- No claim about any harness at Stage 0 (§9.1); internal tier-B (R5).

## Alternatives considered
- **Declare the whole verdict HOLDS (no arm).** Rejected: E2 measured outside ±1 on C5 and C7;
  trigger 5 fires on *any* candidate, and reporting "within band" would fabricate green.
- **Declare the decision changed.** Rejected: the winning kernel/lab scores are unchanged; the E2
  cross-candidate shifts cannot move E5a (kernel-family robustness). Overstating a reversal is as
  dishonest as hiding the arm.
- **Score E2 C5 from cancel/tail only (→ 5, within band) and suppress the fan-out.** Rejected: the
  S0.3 method (ADR-0223) fixed the CPU/concurrency core-normalized fan-out as the C5 fan-out input;
  honoring it for E1 and dropping it for E2 would be an unmatched, results-driven choice. The
  fan-out is reported and the I/O-bound counter-reading disclosed, but the arm is honored.
- **Perform the steps-5–7 re-run here.** Rejected: GATE-G1's phase-synthesis owns it (§10.6); the
  ticket arms and routes with the substituted scores.

## Revisit trigger
The GATE-G1 phase-synthesis pass performs the steps-5–7 re-run with the substituted E2 scores; or an
R2 cross-camp reviewer is arranged; or Stage 3 closes DF-S0.3-2 (polyglot C12) — any of which yields
a cross-candidate/polyglot score that, unlike here, would move the winner → then amend ADR-0050 and
this disposition.
