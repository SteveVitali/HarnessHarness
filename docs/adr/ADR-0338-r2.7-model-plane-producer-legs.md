# ADR-0338: R2.7 model-plane producer legs — the driver-owned `RoutingPort`/`ResponseCachePort` seam, durable-prefix routing state, and the offline-decidable arm cut

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Ticket:** R2.7
- **Requirement ids:** DF-S1.18-1 (machine cells) · BL-31 (partial — the
  live members stay open) · R-2.3.1 · R-2.3.2 · R-2.3.3 · R-2.3.4
- **Spec:** §5b.2 (routing policies, `model.route.decided`,
  `model.rerouted`, the ADR-0122 consult), §5b.3 (profiles, probes,
  `model.profile.*`), §5b.4 (K4/K5, `model.cache.resolved`) · ADR-0121
  d.3–d.5 · ADR-0122 d.1–d.3 · ADR-0128 d.3 · ADR-0119 d.5
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** C0 — every landed member is
  deterministic machinery over declared records; the live-signal arms
  answer typed refusals, never fabricated reads.

## Context

DF-S1.18-1 parked the §5b halves that need a *real run*: the Stage-1
payload builders, the closed `RoutingRefusal` sum and every arm's guard
shape landed (S1.18/S3.7/S4.16a/S5.1), but no producer emitted
`model.route.decided`/`model.call.attempt.*`/`model.rerouted`/
`model.surface.relowered`/`model.cache.resolved`/`model.profile.*` in
the spec'd order, the routing-policy document had no decode half (the
sealed `router` slot's policy could be written but never read back —
a lane that can't decode its own durable decision can never resume
one), `probe_profile` had no fixture-driven arm, and the K5 store had
no call-site leg.

The ticket's honesty ceiling is fixture-verified: live transports and
provider probes stay BL-31. The owned question is *where* the model-
plane sequencing lives and *which* routing arms are offline-decidable —
an arm that needs a live signal must refuse typed, never default a
missing view to zero/healthy/available.

Two mechanical forces shaped the seam: (a) the T-LCD-01 HIR identity
validator rejects semantic-projection members (`model_ref`, `version`)
inside the sealed document's slot params — the policy's internal
`target` representation needed a slot-safe spelling; (b) the driver's
reroute fold must be reconstructible from the durable prefix alone —
in-process session memory is exactly what takeover discards.

## Decision

**D1 — the `router` slot arms a driver-owned `RoutingPort`; the driver
owns ordering and every append.** `Driver` gains `set_routing_port`/
`set_cache_port`. With a port wired, the call path runs the §5b.2
select *before* the call scope opens (`model.route.decided` lands
durable ahead of the `model.call.requested` it binds), emits
`model.call.attempt.{started,completed,failed}` per attempt, and on a
failed attempt runs the ADR-0122 consult: `error_actions`-driven
`Retry`/`Reroute`/`GiveUp`; a cross-profile `Reroute` emits the port's
`model.surface.relowered` payload, then `model.rerouted`, then the
restarted `attempt.started` — the `check_reroute_order` sequence —
with the consult's `control.budget.{reserved,released}`/
`model.profile.status.changed` side rows drained through
`RoutingPort::take_rows` into the same fenced append (CC3 — the
producer computes, the writer lands). Select refusals land typed
`control.guard.fired` rows and close the call `select_refused`.
With **no** port wired the legacy scripted lane is byte-identical — no
model-plane rows land; the asymmetry is declared, not silent.

**D2 — routing state is a fold over the durable prefix, never session
memory.** `route_decisions(prefix, mc)` replays
`model.route.decided`/`model.rerouted` for the call; attempt counts,
the tried-target `attempted` set, the reroute count and the
compaction-already-fired flag are rebuilt the same way. A pending
reroute's fresh decision is minted at the failure consult and re-read
off the prefix at the next call step — the durable tail is the cursor,
so `resume_from`/takeover continue the lane without a second source of
truth (the R2.6 alloc-watermark defect class: session memory is not a
durable record).

**D3 — `RoutingPolicy`/`RoutingDecision` get strict inverse codecs;
the conditioned-rule debt half uses the schema codec, not the event
helper.** `RoutingPolicy::from_json` decodes the sealed `router` slot's
policy document (required members enforced, `content_hash` computed
when absent, unknown `kind`/`error_actions` spellings refused);
`RoutingDecision::from_json` reads a `model.route.decided` payload back
off the durable tail. The conditioned-rule `debt` member decodes via
`hh_compiler::schema::debt_from_json` — now `pub` — because the
event-payload `debt_json` helper drops the additive members
(`removal_test`, `reach_via`, `debt_class`, `hypothesis_typed`,
`scope`, `expiry`, `runway_ms`, `revalidation`, `created_at`,
`supersedes`); routing a sealed document through the lossy helper
silently amputated them (CC1 — one codec per record).

**D4 — the offline-decidable arm cut.** Enumerated per policy `kind`:
`static`, `role_table`, `fallback_chain`, `capability_filter` select
over the realized `ModelRoleTable` alone; `cost_cap` selects under a
declared pricing view and *refuses typed* when the pricing view is
absent (`cost_cap_without_pricing_refuses`); `latency_cap`,
`quality_target`, `bandit`, `health_aware` evaluate the members whose
views are declared and refuse the members that need live signal;
`learned` refuses unless its evolution-evidence binding is present.
Arms needing a provider-side read (live health, live price discovery)
produce the closed `RoutingRefusal` members — a missing view is never
coerced to healthy/zero/available (R-8's honesty rule, G-3/G-5/G-6).

**D5 — declared probes mint rows at arm; K5 binds at the boundary
seam.** The `router` slot's `probes[]` declarations mint
`model.profile.probed{profile_ref, probe_run_id, records[]}` durable
before the first call; a probe record contradicting the declared
status additionally mints `model.profile.status.changed{trigger:
probe}`. The `response_cache` slot binds `KernelK5` over
`hh_context::k5`'s store — one `model.cache.resolved` per lookup scoped
to the call it serves; a hit serves the recorded document under the
entry ref, never a re-derived one. `probe_profile` against a live
provider stays BL-31 — the machinery executes declared probes only.

**D6 — `model_fail` stages call outcomes; a drained plan means
success.** The router slot's `model_fail[]` script — `{class,
retry_after_ms?}` steps plus `{}` pass markers — is process staging,
not ledger state: the scripted model pops one step per `call` and a
`Fail` step yields the declared `ModelErrorClass` for the consult to
exercise. An empty plan never fabricates a failure; the durable
attempt rows are the only record a resume re-reads (a durable resume
re-arms the script whole — `leaf.arm` data, not session memory).

**D7 — slot params use the identity-safe `candidate` spelling.** The
router slot's policy-candidate params spell their target member
`candidate` (decoded to the policy's internal `target`) and carry the
policy version under the slot-safe member — `model_ref`/`version`
inside a semantic projection trip T-LCD-01's identity validator.
Sealed-document grammar is the boundary's decode, not a schema change.

**D8 — size deviation recorded, not hidden.** ~4,660 changed lines
(2,373 tracked code + 1,834 acceptance battery + ~460 build-memory
records) vs the ticket's 1,200-line budget: the mandated acceptance
batteries plus the driver consult/reroute machinery dominate; the alternative — a
split ticket — would strand the emitter call sites from the codecs
they exercise.

## Consequences

- A bound `router` slot's real run emits the full AC-R-2.3.2-4 order
  under the writer lease; `check_reroute_order` is the oracle the
  batteries pin (`r2_7_*` gateway cells: 11/11; embed cells: 6/6).
- `learned` and live-signal arms stay typed refusals — the honesty
  ceiling is enforced by the arm cut, not by tests alone.
- `DF-S1.18-1` keeps OPEN status: the machine cells landed, the live
  `Transport`/`CredentialPort`/`probe_profile`-live members stay BL-31.
- The unbound lane's byte-identity is a tested cell — no retrofit risk
  to the pre-slot driver path.

## Alternatives considered

- **Session-carried routing state** (attempt counts / tried targets in
  `SessionState`): rejected — takeover discards it; the durable fold is
  the only honest cursor and it already exists.
- **A second codec for conditioned-rule debt**: rejected — CC1; the
  event helper was promoted consumers' convenience, not a schema
  spelling, so the fix was widening `debt_from_json`, not duplicating.
- **Emitting model.* rows from the boundary service directly**:
  rejected — ordering against `model.call.*`/guard verdicts is the
  driver's; a boundary-side mint could not place `route.decided` ahead
  of the scope it binds.
- **Softer budget handling** (land a stub, split the battery):
  rejected — the ticket mandates the failure/removal cells; the
  deviation is recorded (D8), consistent with R2.5/R2.6 precedent.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen if: (a) BL-31 lands a live `Transport`/`CredentialPort` — the
port surface is the seam a real provider binds; the live-arm refusal
cut (D4) should be re-checked against the live views it unlocks;
(b) `probe_profile` gains a live executor — the declared-probe mint
rows (D5) must agree with the executor's own `model.profile.probed`
spelling; (c) a `router` slot param grows a member the `candidate`
spelling cannot express — the slot grammar then moves to the schema
source, not another decode-side alias.
