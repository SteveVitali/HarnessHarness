# ADR-0372: R4.15a — the ADR-0211 deferral set (D-1…D-6) descoped at launch; OQ-461's interim ratified; OQ-462 mooted; OQ-463 resolved on the consumer count

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Ticket:** R4.15a (164b)
- **Requirement ids:** BL-34; ADR-0211; D-1…D-6; OQ-461, OQ-462, OQ-463; R-2.12.6, R-2.6.4, R-2.6.5
- **Spec:** §5i, §11.3, §11.4, §08
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** program record — the descoped items were MUST-data deferral records outside the build ladder; this ADR changes no contract

## Context

ADR-0207 D7 partitioned the organizational layer into a C4 specification and six
deferrals (D-1…D-6), ratified by ADR-0211 with an entry test per item — "the entry
tests are unconditional" and each is a `spec-debt.md` row's removal test. At R4.10
the BL-34 package evaluated every entry test verbatim against landed state
(`docs/build/planning/round4-bl34-adr-0211-briefs.md` §entry-test readings): **all
six FAIL** — no `PrincipalDirectory` contract proposed by any ADR; no tenant
`readers` partition and no sharding/migration ruling; no pricing rule with a debt
record and a `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind = artifact_benefit}`; no
`compute_policy` variant binding `SchedulingPolicy.order` and no `matched_total`
evaluation; the `work_source_adapter` ABI read-only with no principal-issued
writer; no `OwnershipRecord` outliving an activation and `lab/coordination-topology-v1`
intra-run only. The R4.10 contract names a deferral whose entry test fails a
natural `descope-by-ADR`: permanently out of launch scope, the re-entry test
preserved as the future trigger.

At GATE-G7 (GATE DECISIONS `### Round 4`, `docs/build/LEDGER.md`) the operator's
answer to the 146 brief proposals across the 7 packages was, verbatim:

> `| 2026-10-22 | GATE-G7 | round-4 rulings | the 146 brief proposals across the 7 packages | "Adopt all" (Devin CLI structured question, received 2026-10-22T12:15Z) | every proposed disposition in all 7 brief files is adopted as proposed (146 ids: 21 resolve-with-evidence, 82 adopt-interim-as-final, 12 descope-by-ADR, 27 needs-code-leg, 4 pointer-only — readout counts): …`

The adopted BL-34 dispositions: `descope-by-ADR` for D-1…D-6 and OQ-462;
`adopt-interim-as-final` for OQ-461; `resolve-with-evidence` for OQ-463. The brief
drafted this record's text and named "Adopt all" as the authorising act; R4.15a
lands it unchanged (the conform never re-designs).

## Decision

**The ADR-0211 deferral set (D-1…D-6) is descoped at launch** — every entry test
was evaluated and is unmet at the R4.10 readings; each ratified interim rule
stands as the launch mechanism:

- **D-1 Human directory and rotations** — owner-issued `ApproverGrant`s and
  static `EscalationTarget`s (ADR-0207 D3; CF-446); the adapter never mints
  `principal` authority.
- **D-2 Multi-tenant fleets** — one principal per session (ADR-0174 D6); one
  fleet per definition + source set.
- **D-3 Value-at-risk pricing of irreversibility** — the registered
  `ext.effects.external_irreversible` counter and its optional ceiling
  (ADR-0207 D5 as amended; CF-445).
- **D-4 Dispatch prioritization by expected value** — `SchedulingPolicy.order`
  is data with `declared(ref)` as the slot; dispatch order is the reconciler's
  ratified order.
- **D-5 Reconciler-side source mirroring** — mediated tracker writes with
  `ActionPattern`s (ADR-0206 D3); the adapter ABI is read-only.
- **D-6 Cross-item shared-state coordination** — ownership is per coordination
  object within one activation (ADR-0191 D1/D5); no cross-item object exists.

**Every entry test is preserved verbatim as the re-entry trigger** — re-entry is
a new ADR passing its ADR-0211 test:

| Deferral | Re-entry test (preserved verbatim from ADR-0211's Decision table) |
|---|---|
| **D-1** | a `PrincipalDirectory` component-class contract proposed by ADR with two out-of-process precedents; the adapter never mints `principal` authority; rotation resolution time (`raise` vs `after_ms`) fixed in the same ADR (OQ-462; joins OQ-446 on `PrincipalRef` vs `TeamRef`) |
| **D-2** | a tenant boundary expressible as a `readers` partition (ADR-0033) with no new authority class and no change to `CallerBinding`; fleet sharding and item migration ruled in the same ADR (OQ-461) |
| **D-3** | a pricing rule with an assumption-debt record and a matched-budget `ComparisonReport{benefit_kind = artifact_benefit}` against the unit counter under `lab/org-policy-v1`; a second consumer named at the same time promotes the dimension by ADR-0039 dialect bump |
| **D-4** | a `compute_policy` variant that binds `SchedulingPolicy.order` at the ratified `delegate`/`dispatch` seam without becoming a second owner of choice (ADR-0188 D1; ADR-0208 §B), evaluated by a `lab/value-of-compute-v1`-shaped recipe at `matched_total` with `static` order as the null arm and `TaskValue` (ADR-0188 D8) as the value input; adoption only by a `ComparisonReport` at the pre-registered margin |
| **D-5** | AC-L8-06 passing with the mirroring procedure's effects recorded as ordinary `action.effect.*` rows under a principal-issued `Permission` (ADR-0053 D-5 forbids a principal-less writer) |
| **D-6** | an `OwnershipRecord` whose owner may outlive an activation, conferred and fenced through the existing `resource(key)` lease scope (ADR-0130; ADR-0193 D3) with escrow expiry ruled by the OQ-431 ADR, and a `lab/coordination-topology-v1` arm at `matched_total` showing `lost_write_count = 0` and no `silent_overwrite` across activations (ADR-0192 vetoes); no new authority class and no change to `spawn` |

**OQ-462 is mooted by this act.** The question exists solely as D-1's entry test;
its substance (assignee → `PrincipalRef` mapping, rotation resolution time)
presupposes a directory the D-1 descope removes from launch. The question is
preserved verbatim inside D-1's re-entry test above; a future D-1 re-entry ADR
answers it.

**OQ-461's answer is ratified as the interim default** (`adopt-interim-as-final`).
The interim rule in force, verbatim from its register cell: *"no (C4/Stage 4
default: one fleet per definition + source set)"* — as the brief's proposed
ratified answer phrases it: *"no — one fleet per definition + source set; a work
item does not migrate between fleets."* The landed `FleetSpec` shape is exactly
the interim: item `owner`s resolve inside one declared agent set (`cross_fleet`
refused at `set_owner`); no migration path exists. A future D-2 re-entry ADR
would amend it.

**OQ-463 is recorded resolved (`ext.*` — one consumer).** The promotion trigger —
two consumers named — is mechanically unmet: `grep -rln "external_irreversible"
crates/*/src` yields `hh-fleet` (the one consumer — `WorkItem` declaration +
owner-pool ceiling enforcement) and `hh-ontology` (the dimension's registration
site — a declaration, not a consumer). `ext.effects.external_irreversible` stays a
registered `ext.*` dimension under ADR-0208 §D and CF-445; a second consumer named
by ADR promotes it by ADR-0039 dialect bump.

## Consequences

- D-1…D-6 are permanently out of launch scope; no `PrincipalDirectory`, tenant
  partition, pricing rule, `compute_policy` variant, mirroring procedure or
  cross-activation `OwnershipRecord` is owed at launch. The interim mechanisms
  above are the shipped semantics.
- `registers/spec-debt.md` ADR-0211's row, `registers/open-questions.md`
  (OQ-461/462/463), spec §11.3/§11.4 and §05i carry the dated notes this act
  records; `research/decisions/ADR-0211.md` carries the append-only amendment-log
  entry naming this ADR. BL-34's backlog status stays `open` — its flip is
  R4.16's accounting.
- OQ-431's ownership-escrow rule is unaffected — it stays `deferred(ADR-0215)`
  and is named inside D-6's preserved re-entry test.
- No code, schema or wire shape changes: the descoped machinery was never
  landed; the interim rules are the landed rules.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep D-1…D-6 deferred with their entry tests.** Rejected by the entry-test
  readings: all six fail against landed state, and the R4.10 contract names a
  failed entry test `descope-by-ADR` — carrying a deferral whose test cannot be
  met pretends a scheduled re-entry that does not exist.
- **Answer some and descope others.** Rejected: the six readings are uniformly
  FAIL and the interim mechanisms are already the landed rules; splitting would
  re-propose what the operator adopted wholesale.
- **Land the mechanisms now.** Rejected: no adopted row asks for code — the
  brief's conform column is `R4.15a` records for all nine rows.

## Revisit trigger

A new ADR that passes the preserved ADR-0211 entry test for the item it names
(the table above is the verbatim set) — e.g. a `PrincipalDirectory`
component-class contract with two out-of-process precedents re-enters D-1 (and
answers OQ-462); a `readers`-partition tenant boundary re-enters D-2 (and may
amend OQ-461's ratified default).
