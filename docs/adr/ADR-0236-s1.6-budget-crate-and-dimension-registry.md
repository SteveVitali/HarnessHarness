# ADR-0236: S1.6 `hh-budget` crate shape and the closed kernel dimension registry — `DimensionKey` splits primary and derived bound names, derived bounds evaluated by fixed formula, integer-only quantities

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Ticket:** S1.6
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.1.6
- **Spec:** §8.2 (R-2.1.6) · ADR-0039 (D2/D3/P1–P3), ADR-0040 (D3/D6), ADR-0041,
  ADR-0043 (D5) — as amended

## Context
S1.6 lands the resource-economics and accounting model §8.2: the kernel dimension
list, `Attribution`, `PricingTable`/spend derivation, the `control.budget.*` event
payloads, `cost_totals`, `usage_mapping`, the hierarchical `BudgetNode`/`Reservation`
tree, exhaustion E1–E5, `MatchSpec`, and the `budget_delta` governance gate. Three
placement/representation questions had to be pinned:

1. **Where the closed dimension vocabulary lives.** The kernel counter/gauge list is
   read by `hh-budget` (charge keys), by `hh-hir`'s `validate` (`Budget.dimensions`
   keys — the DF-S1.4-1 check), and by the baseline adapter. `hh-hir` cannot depend
   on `hh-budget` without a layering inversion (HIR is a dependency of the budget
   crate's governance gate).
2. **How a `tokens.blended` bound is represented.** §8.2's Stage-0 ceiling is on a
   *derived* quantity (input.total + output.total) while the stored account is the
   exclusive primary roles. The bound key space and the charge key space are
   therefore *different sets* — conflating them would either store a derived total
   (double counting) or refuse the spec's own Stage-0 bound name.
3. **Number representation.** Canonical JSON is integer-only (`hh-wire`); coverage,
   tolerance and money must be exact, never floaty.

## Decision
- **Dimension vocabulary is homed in `hh-ontology`** (`dimensions` module): the
  closed `DimensionId` counter/gauge list (exactly the §8.2 kernel rows plus the
  registered `ext.*`/`context.occupancy` entries), `DimensionClass`, and
  `DerivedDimension` (`tokens.input.total`, `tokens.output.total`,
  `tokens.blended`) with a fixed `components()`/`formula()` derivation — derived
  names are *evaluated*, never stored primary. `hh-hir` (already an ontology
  dependent) validates `Budget.dimensions` keys against it — closing DF-S1.4-1
  without a layering inversion. `hh-budget` re-exports the vocabulary (CC7: one
  source).
- **`DimensionKey = Primary(DimensionId) | Derived(DerivedDimension)`** is the bound
  key type: `parse` admits both spaces, `primary()` is the chargeable check. Charges
  and reservations take `DimensionId` only; budget ceilings/thresholds take
  `DimensionKey`, and a derived bound evaluates by `ResourceVector::key_total`
  (summing its `components()`). This is how `tokens.blended` remains a legal bound
  while never being a stored counter.
- **Integer-only accounting:** `ResourceQuantity`/`ResourceVector`/`Money` are `i64`;
  money is micro-units in an ISO-4217 currency; `coverage`/`tolerance` are
  parts-per-million integers (`PPM_SCALE = 1_000_000`). No float ever enters a
  ledgered row (canonical JSON has none; §8.2's "exact, never lossy").
- **`hh-budget` is the one accounting scheme (CC1):** `quantity`, `usage`
  (dialect-lowering `decompose`), `attribution`, `pricing` (table + `SpendRow`),
  `spec` (ceiling/threshold/spec/node/reservation), `events` (payload
  builders+decoders), `tree` (pure fold/projection), `account` (ledger-bound ops),
  `matchspec`, `governance`. `hh-baseline`'s throwaway `BudgetNode` becomes a thin
  adapter over these types — no second budget model remains in the tree.

## Consequences
- `hh-hir` closes DF-S1.4-1 (`validate` refuses unknown dimension keys) without
  gaining a new dependency edge.
- A `tokens.blended` ceiling coexists with exclusive-role charges with no double
  counting; the Stage-0 baseline semantics are preserved through the adapter.
- `BudgetSpec`/`ResourceVector`/`DimensionId` are the only accounting types
  anywhere; later tickets (Stage-2 `env.*`/`network.*` producers, Stage-3 reporting)
  consume them instead of defining their own.

## Alternatives considered
- **Registry inside `hh-budget`.** Rejected: `hh-hir` would need to depend on the
  budget crate (layering inversion — `hh-budget`'s governance gate already consumes
  `hh-hir`), or DF-S1.4-1 stays open.
- **Store `tokens.blended` as a primary counter.** Rejected: charges would post to
  both roles and the blended counter — a second accounting truth and a silent
  double-count channel (CC3).
- **`f64` money/coverage.** Rejected: non-canonical under `hh-wire` (floats are
  refused outright) and never exact.

## Revisit trigger
A spec amendment that makes a derived dimension chargeable, introduces non-integer
quantities, or rehomes the dimension registry (e.g. into `hh-identity`) would reopen
the `DimensionKey` split and the ontology home.
