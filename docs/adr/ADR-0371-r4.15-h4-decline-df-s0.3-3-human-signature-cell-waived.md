# ADR-0371: R4.15 — H4 decline recorded: DF-S0.3-3's R2 cross-camp human-signature cell waived (GATE-ACCEPT item 7 discharged)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Ticket:** R4.15
  (a recording act — the operator's ruling already on the record fully determines the
  waiver; this ADR records, never originates, it)
- **Requirement ids:** DF-S0.3-3 · BL-02 · HUMAN-H4 (manifest row 128) · R-2.12.3 ·
  GATE-ACCEPT accepted-deviation item 7 · ADR-0225 (the single-executor deviation) ·
  ADR-0226 · LEDGER GATE DECISIONS 2026-10-20 (H4 row)

## Context

The S0.3/S0.3b measurement sheet's R2 cross-camp reviewer signature is a human
deliverable the build cannot mint — the DF-S0.3-3 residual: a reviewer briefed on a
*different* candidate is a second-ecosystem human, unrunnable under the offline/
hermetic budget. The machine cells were measured and closed at S0.3b (2026-09-15);
the residual rode to GATE-G1 (accepted there), onto the CAP.3 ACCEPTED-deviations
list, and was signed **ACCEPTED as GATE-ACCEPT item 7** — with the recorded control
that the objective G1 byte-identity gate stands and the signature cell rides to the
operator. HUMAN-H4 (ticket 128) asked for either the signature or an explicit
operator-recorded decline, verbatim, on the sheet readout.

On 2026-10-20 the operator first directed "I'll name a reviewer" (LEDGER GATE
DECISIONS, Devin CLI structured question, 14:21Z) and then declined the deliverable:
**"Record a decline instead"** (same channel, received between 15:22Z and 15:30Z —
the LEDGER GATE DECISIONS H4 row, verbatim). The gate row rules that the decline
discharges item 7: *"no cross-camp signature will be sought; a round-4 ticket records
the decline verbatim on the measurement-sheet readout and closes DF-S0.3-3 / BL-02
waived-by-ADR (GATE-ACCEPT item 7 discharged by the operator's decline, not
re-litigated)."*

## Decision

- **D1 — the decline is the disposition of the human-signature cell.** The R2
  cross-camp signature is waived: no signature will be sought and none is owed. The
  operator's words — "Record a decline instead" — are recorded verbatim, with date
  and channel, on `docs/build/reports/S0.3-measurement-sheet.md`'s readout. The sheet
  (machine cells + the recorded decline) is the complete record of the S0.3
  measurement obligation.
- **D2 — GATE-ACCEPT item 7 is discharged by the decline act, not re-litigated.** The
  deviation read "the human-signature cell cannot be produced by the build" — true,
  and now dispositioned: the gate's own rule asked each accepted deviation to be
  signed or returned to closure; the operator's recorded decline is that act for item
  7. R4.16's round-4 closure / GATE-ACCEPT discharge table lists item 7
  *discharged-by-decline*, citing this ADR — the acceptance is not reopened.
- **D3 — DF-S0.3-3 closes `WONTFIX`-class waived-by-ADR** (the DEFERRALS vocabulary's
  closed-with-reason token; the reason is this waiver). Its dated evidence note names
  the verbatim words, the channel, this ADR and the sheet section. BL-02's status
  flip lands at R4.16's round-4 closure accounting (the BL-flip convention); its cell
  records the waiver now. Row 128's marker gains a dated note — a second deferral
  needed the operator's explicit words and got them.
- **D4 — the compensating controls stand unchanged.** Machine cells: DONE 2026-09-15
  (S0.3b — E2/E3 S1 slices + E5b/E5c splits measured N=5 matched-budget; trigger-5
  disposition ADR-0226). The objective G1 byte-identity gate is proven and replayable
  (`hh-xcheck verify` over the committed `hh-xcheck-bundle/1` corpus — the corpus the
  signature gated is durably packaged). The single-executor deviation stays recorded
  (ADR-0225). Nothing about the waiver weakens what the signature was a control *for*:
  the sheet's numbers stand on measurement, and the ecosystem decision stands on the
  adversarial/kernel-family sweeps (ADR-0050 §4, ADR-0224/0226).

## Consequences

- DF-S0.3-3's residual is discharged — the PARTIAL row closes with its machine cells
  intact on the record; `deferrals-open.sh` drops it.
- BL-02 carries the dated waiver cell for R4.16's status flip; HUMAN-H4's checklist
  is satisfied by the recorded-decline branch of its own exit criterion ("a signature
  *or* an explicit operator-recorded decline … appended to the measurement-sheet
  readout with `date -u +%F` and a pointer").
- GATE-ACCEPT item 7 moves from ACCEPTED-pending-deliverable to discharged; the
  accepted-deviations set loses its last human-signature row.
- No code, no schema, no spec text changes; no new deferral.

## Alternatives considered

- **Name a reviewer anyway** — the operator's own earlier direction (14:21Z),
  explicitly superseded by the 15:22Z–15:30Z decline; recording it as the disposition
  would contradict the ruling on the record.
- **Mark the cell `n/a` or DONE** — rejected: the signature is a human deliverable;
  a build-side status would fabricate it. Waiver-by-ADR under the operator's verbatim
  decline is the honest close.
- **Carry the residual OPEN indefinitely** — rejected: HUMAN-H4's exit criterion has
  a decline branch the operator invoked; the round-4 zero-deferral charter forbids
  carrying an owned residual whose discharge act exists.

## Revisit trigger

The operator reverses the decline or names a reviewer (a cross-camp signature may
still be appended to the sheet at any time — the waiver is not a refusal to accept
one); or R4.16's GATE-ACCEPT discharge table re-litigates item 7 (the readout is the
venue, not this ADR).
