# ADR-0309: S4.16a C1 router depth and cache-projection rulings

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Ticket:** S4.16a
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.3.2 (AC-R-2.3.2-{10,11,12} legs),
  R-2.3.3 (AC-R-2.3.3-15), R-2.3.4 C1 (AC-R-2.3.4-{5,7}
  projection halves)
- **Spec:** §5b.2 (the router family — `RoutingPolicy` kinds,
  `error_actions`, G-1…G-6, retry-vs-reroute); §5b.4 (IR-1…IR-6
  invalidation, `expect_cache_state`, the K-taxonomy staging);
  ADR-0121…0122 (policy kinds, error actions, reroute
  ordering), ADR-0124 (profile binding / `profile_ref`),
  ADR-0127…0129 (cache taxonomy, expected-vs-observed,
  invalidation contract), ADR-0028 (deterministic replay),
  ADR-0043 (pricing provenance), ADR-0046 (matched budgets)
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** **C1** — the router/cache depth — the C1 rung.

## Context

S4.16a lands the C1 model-router depth and the model-plane
cache depth. The C0 surface (`static`/`role_table` selection,
typed refusals, the affinity key, `expect_cache_state`) was
already landed; six spellings needed owned rulings — each is
a seam the spec leaves to the implementation and each now has
exactly one spelling (CC1/CC7):

1. **How the policy family reads evidence it does not own.**
   `cost_cap`/`quality_target`/`latency_cap`/`health_aware`/
   `capability_filter` and the G-3 migration guard consume
   pricing, priors, migration projection and health data that
   live outside the router. The seam needed one shape — and
   one rule for what absence means.
2. **What "fail closed" means per view.** Missing pricing is
   not zero cost (ADR-0043: no money value is ever invented);
   missing quality priors, missing migration projection and
   missing health each needed a typed outcome, not a default.
3. **Retry-vs-reroute ordering under `error_actions`.** The
   spec names the disposition sum
   (`retry_same`/`reroute`/`compact_then_retry`/`give_up`) and
   the per-class action map; the execution needed one
   sequencing rule — including what happens to the live
   reservation when a reroute is chosen, and under which
   profile a `compact_then_retry` compaction runs.
4. **How `lower`/`lift` preference exchange interacts with
   authority (G-6).** MCP/ACP clients can carry model
   preferences; the binding needed to decide what a lifted
   preference may do to the candidate set.
5. **How the cache's expected-vs-observed basis is folded.**
   `expect_cache_state` takes `(facts, invalidations)`; the
   run stream had to produce both deterministically, with
   IR-1 attribution — and the fold needed a rule for
   competing causes on adjacent rows (a relowered reroute
   also changes `profile_ref`).
6. **What an `EnsembleProcedure` is at run time** and how a
   k-vs-1 arm comparison is judged (AC-R-2.3.2-10), including
   where the executor lives and what it may charge.

## Decision

1. **`RoutingViews` is the evidence seam; every view fails
   closed.** `select_with(request, SelectorView, BudgetPort,
   HealthView, views, policy, table, decision_id, now_ms,
   attempted)` takes a `RoutingViews{pricing, quality_prior,
   migration}` bundle of read-model traits with `NoPricing`/
   `NoQualityPrior`/`NoMigration` spellings. `NoPricing` under
   `cost_cap` refuses `NoPrice{model_ref}` — never a zero-cost
   fallback. An absent quality prior excludes the candidate
   (`policy_excluded` verdict) and aggregates to
   `ChainExhausted` when the whole binding is excluded — the
   `NoPrice` variant is reserved for the pricing read itself.
   A cross-profile move without a `MigrationView` refuses
   `MigrationLossExceeded{dropped = unbounded}` — fail closed,
   never assume zero loss (G-3). Missing health data yields
   neutral `TargetHealth`/`TableHealth` stats (a health view
   can only rank, never invent a failure record).
2. **`on_attempt_failed` is one sequencing rule; reroute
   releases before it reserves.** `AttemptState` tracks the
   call's retries/reroutes; `error_actions` is a data map from
   `ModelErrorClass` to an `ErrorAction` that may `then`-chain
   a successor action. `retry_same` bounds by `max` and returns
   `not_before_ms` from policy/`retry_after`; permanent and
   ambiguous classes never retry (AC-R-2.3.2-5).
   `compact_then_retry` returns the compaction marker exactly
   once — compaction runs under the old profile before the
   retried attempt (ADR-0122 d.3 ordering). `reroute` releases
   the prior `reservation_id` through `BudgetPort::release`
   *before* `select_with` reserves the next target, obeys
   `allowed_classes[]`/`max_reroutes`, and re-checks G-3/G-5/
   G-6 on the new target; a refusal on the reroute surfaces as
   `GiveUp{SelectRefused(_)}`. `GiveUpReason` is the closed
   terminal sum; the whole disposition is
   `AttemptDisposition::{Retry, CompactThenRetry, Reroute,
   GiveUp}` — named distinctly from the generic
   `attempts::AttemptOutcome<T>` so the two outcomes can't be
   conflated downstream.
3. **G-6: lifted preferences attenuate, never extend.**
   `lower(request)` produces the MCP/ACP `modelPreferences`
   projection and `lift(preferences)` recovers it — the
   round-trip is exact on `preferences` and reports
   `required_capabilities`/`budget_view`/`role` as `no_slot`
   loss (AC-R-2.3.2-12). Inside `select_with` a lifted
   preference may exclude a member of the role binding; it can
   never add a candidate outside the binding or widen
   authority (CC2 — authority is conferred by the sealed
   table, never read from request content).
4. **`project_cache_view` is the IR-1 fold over the durable
   prefix.** `CacheCallFact` gains `closed` so the projection
   distinguishes completed calls (a `warm` basis), failed
   calls (terminal, never a basis, never an open sibling) and
   in-flight siblings (`opened, not closed` →
   `cold{concurrent_sibling}`). The fold emits one fact per
   `model.call.requested` and closes it on `completed`/
   `failed`; version-stamp deltas on consecutive requests
   attribute `dialect_change`/`profile_version`/
   `tool_set_change`/`definition_version`/`marker_policy`;
   `model.surface.relowered`/`model.rerouted{relowered}` emit
   `relower` (and suppress the coincident `profile_version`
   attribution — the reroute's own reason wins);
   `context.compaction.completed{status: applied}` emits
   `compaction`; any other `cache_invalidating = true` row
   classifies by class prefix (`context.*` → `context_edit`,
   `control.compute.*` → `parameter_change` — which is how an
   effort change under `cache_invalidation_on_change` lands,
   AC-R-2.3.3-15) else `unknown`. `unknown` stays `unknown` —
   never coerced warm or cold.
5. **The C1 policy kinds execute; `learned` refuses.**
   `RoutingPolicyKind` is the closed nine-variant sum
   (`static`, `role_table`, `fallback_chain`,
   `capability_filter`, `cost_cap`, `latency_cap`,
   `quality_target`, `health_aware`, `learned`); every C1 kind
   runs its declared candidate rule — none silently degrades
   to `static`. `learned` is a `PolicyInvalid` refusal at C1
   (the C4 predictor surface does not exist; AC-R-2.3.2-14
   conditions it on the evolution pipeline anyway). `explain`
   folds the run's `model.route.decided`/`model.rerouted` rows
   into the route explanation for the trace viewer.
6. **`run_ensemble` in hh-control; the ensemble factor is a
   matchspec dimension.** `EnsembleProcedure{sample_k_vote,
   k}` runs exactly `k` members, each routed through
   `select_with` under its own `model_call_id` and reservation
   — members are independent calls, so the same target may
   legitimately serve every member (the attempted-set is
   `on_attempt_failed`'s, not the fan-out's). Member select
   refusals ride up typed (`EnsembleRefusal::SelectRefused`),
   executor failures are `MemberFailed`, `k < 2` or
   `k > k_max` is `InvalidK`. The `slice_consumption` is
   Σ member charges + the reducer's charge — nothing
   unaccounted (CC3). `ArmSpec::ensemble_k` declares the
   factor and `validate_match` refuses a k-vs-1 (or mixed-k)
   comparison under every mode but `matched_total` with
   `RefusalReason::EnsembleKvs1` (CC9 — per-member spend is
   incommensurable dimension-wise; only totals match).

## Consequences

- The C1 policy family is executable end-to-end with typed
  refusals at every evidence boundary; a Stage-5 run can add
  views (real pricing tables, priors, migration projection)
  without touching the router core (CC8 — additive).
- Reroute can never double-spend: the release precedes the
  next reservation in the same synchronous step, and the
  durable row order (`attempt.failed` → optional
  `context.compaction.*` under the old profile → `relowered`
  → `rerouted` → `attempt.started`) is the AC-R-2.3.2-4
  shape.
- Preference exchange can't launder authority — the G-6 test
  (`lifted_preferences_attenuate_only`) proves a preference
  naming a model outside the binding cannot produce it.
- Cache attribution is deterministic replay of the event
  stream; effort parameter changes land as
  `parameter_change` misses without special-casing.
- Ensemble arms are commensurable only at the totals — the
  refusal is a matchspec fact, not an engine heuristic, so a
  design declares k-vs-1 legality by choosing
  `matched_total` deliberately.
- K6 (semantic cache + verifier) stays OPEN as
  `DF-S4.16a-1` — owned by S5.1 with OQ-312's Stage-5
  rulings (see the deferral row).

## Alternatives considered

- **Default missing pricing/priors to permissive values.**
  Rejected by CC3/ADR-0043 — an invented zero cost or a
  passing prior is unaccounted authority; absence is a typed
  refusal or an excluded candidate.
- **Reserve the reroute target before releasing the old
  reservation.** Rejected: holding two reservations across
  the selection window can deadlock a capped budget and
  breaks the durable row ordering the AC names; release-first
  makes the accounting serialisable.
- **Let lifted preferences union with the binding.**
  Rejected by G-6/CC2 — a request-scoped preference would
  become an authority channel.
- **Implement the K6 verifier in-ticket.** Rejected: OQ-312
  assigns the threshold/calibration/`false_hit_rate` design
  to WS-G3/WS-I2/WS-C4 due Stage 5, and the manifest assigns
  K6 to S5.1 — landing it here would pre-decide that surface
  (CC10) and double-book the work.
- **Invent `n` members sharing one `model_call_id`.**
  Rejected by AC-R-2.3.2-10 — each member is its own
  ledgered call with its own `model.route.decided`.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen if (a) a new `RoutingPolicyKind` lands by ADR — the
kind enum extends only there; (b) the Stage-5 migration/
pricing views gain richer refusals than the current typed
sums — extend the `RoutingViews` traits additively rather
than loosening the fail-closed defaults; or (c) OQ-312's
ratification changes what a `marginal` K6 match must carry —
the `verifier_verdict` member on `model.cache.resolved` is
already the landing site, extend it rather than reshaping
`resolved` itself.
