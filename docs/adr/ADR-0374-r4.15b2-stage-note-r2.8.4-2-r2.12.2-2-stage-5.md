- **Ticket:** R4.15b2 (164f)
- **Requirement ids:** BL-38; `spec/READINESS_REPORT.md` §10 minor DAG-12; `R-2.8.4²`; `R-2.12.2²`; OQ-410

- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** a stage note of the ADR-0048/0146/0184 kind — it places already-tiered content in the build ladder; it changes no contract, tier or MoSCoW value

## Context

`spec/READINESS_REPORT.md` §10 minor DAG-12 records that two C2 slice nodes — `R-2.8.4²`
(`tls.terminate`, `inspect_hooks`, R-2.8.2's reader-set flow check as the closer of
`approved_host_body`) and `R-2.12.2²` (`component_model` placement, cross-plugin flow checks
with IFC, organisation-level `ContractVersionPolicy` layers) — carried `C2 · —` stage cells:
ADR-0061 (e) names `R-2.8.4²`'s tier and no stage, and `R-2.12.2²` was deferred to OQ-410 /
ADR-0050 trigger 3. §4.4's verification statement and §9.8 excluded both from the stage check
"until a stage note is ratified" — this ADR is that stage note, owed by WS-H4/WS-L5 (OQ-410).

At GATE-G7 (GATE DECISIONS `### Round 4`, `docs/build/LEDGER.md`) the operator's answer to
the 146 brief proposals across the 7 packages was, verbatim:

> `| 2026-10-22 | GATE-G7 | round-4 rulings | the 146 brief proposals across the 7 packages | "Adopt all" (Devin CLI structured question, received 2026-10-22T12:15Z) | every proposed disposition in all 7 brief files is adopted as proposed (146 ids: 21 resolve-with-evidence, 82 adopt-interim-as-final, 12 descope-by-ADR, 27 needs-code-leg, 4 pointer-only — readout counts): …`

The adopted DAG-12 disposition is a spec-text leg: each slice staged per its owning section's
§4.4 placement, the stage-note ADR naming both nodes verbatim
(`docs/build/planning/round4-bl38-adr-0215-briefs.md` Part B). R4.15b2 lands it.

## Decision

- **`R-2.8.4²` is staged at Stage 5.** Its content — `tls.terminate`, `inspect_hooks`, and
  R-2.8.2's reader-set flow check as the closer of `approved_host_body` — is the C2 depth of
  the egress/sandbox item and joins the ladder's C1/C2-depth stage with the other C2 halves
  (R-2.5.2, R-2.4.5, R-2.10.4, R-2.10.5/R-2.10.6 slices). ADR-0061 (e) named the tier; this
  note names the stage.
- **`R-2.12.2²` is staged at Stage 5.** Its content — `component_model` placement,
  cross-plugin flow checks with IFC, organisation-level `ContractVersionPolicy` layers — is
  the C2 half of R-2.12.2 beside the C0 (Stage 1–3), C1 (`R-2.12.2¹`, Stage 4) and C4
  (`R-2.12.2⁴`, Stage 6) slices. The `component_model` member stays bound to ADR-0050
  trigger 3 — OQ-410 stays open as the placement question; this note stages the slice's
  contract and leaves the member's enablement trigger-bound exactly as §4.4's row records.
- **Re-entry:** staging either node elsewhere, or releasing the `component_model` binding,
  is a new stage-note ADR.

## Consequences

- §4.4's second table names `C2 · S5` for both rows (ADR-0374 cited); §4.4's (iii) convention
  paragraph, its verification statement and §9.8's stage check no longer carve out the two
  nodes — they take part in the stage check like every other slice node.
- §9.6's Stage-5 slice list names `R-2.8.4` (C2 slice `R-2.8.4²`) and `R-2.12.2` (C2 slice
  `R-2.12.2²`) with their content.
- OQ-410 is unchanged: still `open`, non-blocking, post-gate — the `component_model`
  placement question survives the stage placement (ADR-0050 trigger 3).
- BL-38's status stays `open` — its flip is R4.16's accounting.
- No code, schema or wire shape changes: the slices' content was already tiered; this note
  only places it in the ladder.

## Alternatives considered

- *Leave both unstaged* — rejected: §9.8's exclusion was always marked "until a stage note
  is ratified"; an unstaged slice node is a hole in the ladder, not a design.
- *Stage `R-2.8.4²` at Stage 4* — rejected: C2 depth lands at Stage 5 by the ladder's own
  placement rule (the C1/C2-depth stage), and its content consumes Stage-4 surfaces.
- *Resolve OQ-410 here* — rejected: the GATE-G7 act stages the slices; `component_model`'s
  placement trigger (ADR-0050 trigger 3) is a separate question the adopted disposition
  leaves open.

## Revisit trigger

Any proposal to stage `R-2.8.4²` or `R-2.12.2²` at a stage other than Stage 5, or to release
`component_model`'s ADR-0050-trigger-3 binding without an OQ-410 ruling — each lands only as
a new stage-note ADR.
