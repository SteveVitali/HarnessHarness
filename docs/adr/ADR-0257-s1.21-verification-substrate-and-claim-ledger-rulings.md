# ADR-0257: S1.21 verification-substrate rulings — the `hh-verification` plane crate, claims-as-delegate-observations, the `finish_record` completion seam, the verify placement in dispatch, Γ floors as constants, and floors-only class registrations

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-18
- **Ticket:** S1.21
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.7.2a, R-2.7.1⁰, R-2.7.3⁰
- **Spec:** §5f.1 · §5f.2 · §5f.4 · ADR-0109…0117 (esp. ADR-0111, ADR-0112, ADR-0114)
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** **C0** — MUST-code: the claim-ledger
  substrate (`Claim`, `AuthoritativeHandle`, `ReconciliationRecord`,
  `SeverityRecord`, the `bind` kind table, the pure `ledger_only` fold),
  the `Validator` contract (`declare`/`bind`, `Verdict`, `EvidenceBundle`,
  `inputs_digest`, local checks (a)–(c)), the `CriticDeclaration` schema
  (`evidence_class`, `IndependenceVector`, `CalibrationRecord`, grounding),
  Γ with floors F2–F7, `GateResult`, the `verification.*` class
  registrations, the `reconciliation.holds` dimension, the ADR-0114 D1
  metric declarations, and the two reference-harness wirings. Check (d),
  declared/global checks, the gate call site, `claim.reconciled` emitters,
  judged extraction/critics, and the `execution_alignment` C2 variant stay
  at their spec'd stages (DF-S1.21-1/-2/-3).

## Context
§5f lands the verification plane: the kernel must not let a model's claim
of completion propagate as fact. The open seams were: where the plane
lives (one crate vs scattered modules); how a `Claim` is typed so it can
never be mistaken for evidence; where completion claims enter the S1.20
driver without destabilising the landed control surface; where local
verdicts append relative to `action.effect.observed` (the observation is
the audited record — it must stay byte-identical); how Γ floors are
represented so loosening is refused at `validate`, not at evaluate time;
and how the `validator`/`execution_alignment` classes register when only
the validator has a Stage-1-bound variant.

## Decision
1. **One plane crate `crates/hh-verification`, deps
   wire/identity/provenance/ontology/hir/ledger only** (CC10; matches the
   hh-control/hh-env precedent). Seven modules by datum: `vocab` (every
   closed sum), `claims`, `gate` (`ValidatesRecord`/`TaskContract`/
   `VerificationSummary`/`GateResult`/Γ), `evidence` (`EvidenceHandle`/
   `EvidenceBundle`/`inputs_digest`/`collect`), `validators`
   (`ValidatorDeclaration`/`Verdict`/local checks), `critics`
   (`CriticDeclaration`/`IndependenceVector`/`CalibrationRecord`/
   `ProgrammaticCritic`), `events` (payload builders), `metrics`
   (ADR-0114 D1 declarations). The crate is pure logic — no I/O, no
   effects; emitters live in `hh-env`/`hh-control` which depend on it.
2. **A claim is an `Observation{source = model_claim}` at
   `authority = delegate`, `evidence_class = claimed` — never evidence.**
   `Claim::validate` refuses `AuthorityExceedsOrigin` for anything above
   delegate; `evidence::collect` refuses a bundle whose only items are
   claims with `ClaimOnlyEvidence`; a `diverge` reconciliation record must
   cite ≥ 1 `AuthoritativeHandle` record, never the claim's own text
   (F7's no-hold/veto-on-claim-only rule is enforced at the fold, not
   trusted to callers). Structured extraction lands at confidence
   `1_000_000`; the `Parsed`/`Judged` `ExtractedBy` members are declared
   with the `PARSED_CONFIDENCE_CAP_PPM` ceiling so the Stage-2/4 channels
   fill producers, not renames.
3. **Completion claims ride an additive `EffectGate::finish_record`
   seam; the driver emits before `lifecycle.run.finished`.** On
   `StopReason::Completed`, `Driver::finish` appends
   `verification.completion.proposed` then `verification.claim.recorded`
   (kind ∈ {achieved, unachievable}, `criteria_status` aligned with the
   contract, provenance delegate-level from `model_ref`, the last
   `model_call_id` + `run_id` carried) — *before* drain assessment and the
   terminal lifecycle rows, so the claim precedes the record it claims
   about. `finish_record` is a defaulted trait method (`None` by
   default): existing `EffectGate` implementors are untouched, and a gate
   that supplies a structured finish record gets real criteria statuses
   rather than the synthesized single-claim path (AC-R-2.7.2a-1).
4. **Local checks (a)–(c) append immediately after
   `action.effect.observed` inside `hh-env::dispatch`.** The dispatcher
   builds a `TerminalCapture` from the same terminal report it just
   appended, invokes `run_local_checks` under the pinned
   `hir/kernel/local_checks@1` validator ref, and appends
   `verification.validator.verdict` rows with `phase = local`,
   `detector = deterministic`, `charged_to = subject`, `inputs_digest`,
   and provenance. The observation row itself is appended first and never
   mutated — the verdicts are *new rows* that cite it
   (AC-R-2.7.1-1/2's no-rewrite rule is structural: no fabric operation
   takes `&mut` on a durable observation). Check (d) `diff_sanity` is a
   conditioned rule with model/task-dependent thresholds (OQ-267) and
   stays Stage 2 per ADR-0111(e) → DF-S1.21-1.
5. **Γ floors F2–F7 are kernel constants; `validate_gamma` refuses
   loosening and conditioned rows carry debt records.** A `ValidatesRecord`
   whose Γ drops any floor fails `validate` (`GammaBelowFloor` naming the
   floor); a row that *keeps* a floor but conditions it must carry an
   assumption-debt record — same I-V1 rule as threshold-conditioned
   validators (AC-R-2.7.1-12's pattern applied to Γ, AC-R-2.7.2a-10).
   `evaluate_gate` produces `GateResult` with `intervene`/`hold`/`veto`
   as a pure fold; the driver call site that runs it at
   `DecisionPoint.stop` is Stage 2's (the schema and the floor check are
   what Stage 1 must not let drift).
6. **`validator` registers as a normal class; `execution_alignment`
   registers floors-only.** `validator_class()` gets the full
   `ClassRecord` (ordered-many cardinality, `required_inputs ⊇
   {ModelProfile, ResourceAccount}` — AC-R-2.7.1-11, declaration schema,
   contract metadata, C0 tier). `execution_alignment_class()` registers
   the class id with a floors-only default and **no bound variant** — the
   §5f.2 tier map binds its variant at C2, so registering a C0 variant
   would fabricate a producer the stage doesn't own. The corpus variant
   generator excludes both from ordinary variant generation rather than
   synthesizing `declaration_schema`-bearing variants that would fail
   their own class's required fields; the three pre-existing
   variant-bearing classes are unchanged.
7. **`RedundantJudge` and grounding are declaration-level checks
   executable now.** A `judge` critic declared beside a deterministic
   check at the same decision point fails `declare_critic` with
   `RedundantJudge` — ordering is verifiable from the declaration alone,
   no run needed (AC-R-2.7.3-13). Bundle `grounding` derives from the
   `evidence_class` mix: all-`claimed` ⇒ `grounding = claimed`, and
   `consume(report|gate)` on such a bundle fails `GroundingInsufficient`;
   one `measured` item makes the bundle admissible (AC-R-2.7.3-1).
8. **ADR-0114 D1 declarations register as data; judged names never
   merge.** `metrics::DECLARATIONS` carries the deterministic metric
   declarations (`false_completion_rate` marked **veto**,
   `claim_state_agreement`, `execution_alignment_failure_rate`,
   `honest_failure_rate`, `gate_hold_count`, `divergence_profile`,
   `reconciliation_cost`, `notice_followed_rate`, `evidence_traceability`)
   with `execution_alignment_failure_rate_judged` and `belief_divergence`
   as **distinct** ids — a judged and a deterministic name for adjacent
   quantities must never alias (AC-R-2.7.2a-9). Two provisional
   `computed_from` spellings in `hh-telemetry::catalogue` were corrected
   to the registered ids. Computation itself is Stage 3's
   (DF-S1.21-2) — the declaration is what must not drift.

## Consequences
- The verification plane exists as executable substrate: every claim,
  verdict, bundle, gate row and critic declaration the spec names at C0
  decodes, validates, and refuses what it must refuse — before any
  emitter call site beyond the two wired ones exists.
- `stop{completed}` can no longer pass silently through the reference
  harness: the claim row precedes `lifecycle.run.finished`, and it is a
  *claim* — `claimed` evidence can never ground a gate or report.
- Staged halves are typed, not absent: `ExtractedBy::Parsed`/`Judged`,
  `GateResult`, `claim.reconciled` payloads, the `execution_alignment`
  class id and the judged-metric declarations are all landed so Stage
  2/3/4 fill producers and computations — per DF-S1.21-1/-2/-3.

## Revisit trigger
The Stage-2 turn-loop/capability-surface tickets re-read D3/D4 when the
gate, reconciler, declared-postcondition and `artefact.followed` call
sites land (DF-S1.21-1) — the row shapes and the verify placement are
already fixed, so the re-read confirms placement, not schema. Reopen D2
if a claim channel ever needs authority above delegate (it must not —
that is the AC-R-2.7.2a-1 refusal path). Reopen D5 if a conditioned Γ
row is proposed *without* its debt record (the validate check is the
only thing standing between a floor and a suggestion). Reopen D6 when
the C2 `execution_alignment` variant binds (DF-S1.21-3) — the
floors-only registration then gains a variant record, and the corpus
generator's exclusion list shrinks. Reopen D7 if a judged critic is
declared that is genuinely *not* redundant beside a deterministic check
(the `RedundantJudge` check reads `evidence_class` + decision point —
a judged critic at a different point already passes).
