# ADR-0252: S1.17 surface-binding & exposure rulings — the extended `SurfaceBinding` member set, the two exposure-mode sums, the `admit → rank → enforce` kernel pipeline, and the OQ-235 event classes

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-17
- **Ticket:** S1.17
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.5.2⁰, R-2.5.3⁰
- **Spec:** §5d.2/§5d.3 (the C0/Stage-1 slices per the stage tables) ·
  ADR-0090…0095 · ADR-0212 OQ-235 · CF-477
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** **C0** — MUST-code: the `SurfaceBinding`
  schema (closed transform vocabulary incl. `parse`), `SurfaceFailure`,
  `ResultRenderSpec{full|truncate}`, `ErrorFormatSpec`, the
  `surface_rejection_rate` declaration, the run-time `ExposureMode` sum,
  `Catalog`/`ExposurePlan`/`RevealedSet`, `build_catalog`, `select_surfaces`
  with `direct_all`, `check_callable` + `SurfaceNotRevealed`, the eight
  event classes, the `discover_surfaces` schema, `exact_name`/
  `static_allowlist`, the metric declarations and the kernel invariants.
  `code_mode` exposure, embedding/model-ranked catalogues,
  `hide_uncovered`, provider-hosted search and the executable conformance
  suite stay at their spec'd stages (C1/C2/S3).

## Context
S1.17 lands the surface schema and the exposure slice. `hh-compiler` already
had a minimal `SurfaceBinding` (`surface_name`, `capability_ref`,
`hir_node_id`, `arg_map`, `dialect`); §5d.2 §3 names the full member set
(`surface_id`, `exposure_mode`, `capability_refs`, `mapping`, `rule_ids`,
`evidence_ref`, `safety_ref`, `effects_bound`, `family_id`, `variant_id`)
and §5d.3 names the run-time machinery. The rulings this slice needed:

1. **Two `exposure_mode` sums share the name.** §5d.2's is a *compile-time*
   rendering kind (`primitive | split | composite | freeform | code_mode |
   shim`); §5d.3's is a *run-time* delivery mode (`direct | indexed |
   deferred | code_mode | hidden`). One type would conflate them (CC1 —
   one scheme per concern).
2. **Additive growth of the stored `SurfaceBinding`.** Bundles sealed under
   S1.10–S1.16 must still decode (CC8); a re-minted `surface_id` must not
   be silently re-derived.
3. **Where the kernel's authority ends.** `policy.rank` is a replaceable
   component; the invariants (I-NARROW, I-ONE-MODE, I-CALLABLE, I-BUDGET,
   I-DISCOVERY, I-ORDER, I-EPOCH, I-CLOSED, I-NOAUTH) are kernel-enforced —
   a bad policy must never reach a delivery (AC-R-2.5.3-3).
4. **What a refusal may carry.** `SurfaceNotRevealed` must not leak a
   definition — no schema, no description — while still naming the
   surface id and a `search|none` hint.

## Decision
- **§1 — two sums, two names.** `hh_compiler::surface::CompileExposureMode`
  carries the §5d.2 rendering kind; `hh_hir::tools::ExposureMode` carries
  the §5d.3 run-time mode (the authored `ToolSurface.exposure_mode` member
  parses into `AuthoredExposure{hidden, pinned, admitted}`). Both are
  closed — an unknown spelling is a schema violation, never a widening.
- **§2 — `SurfaceBinding` grows additively; `surface_id` is self-verifying.**
  The new members decode with C0 defaults when absent (`exposure_mode =
  primitive`, `mapping = surface_arg_map`, `capability_refs =
  [capability_ref.semantic_id]`, `admitted_modes = {direct}`); a *present*
  `surface_id` must re-mint equal (`idp("tool_surface", canonical basis)`)
  — mismatch is `SchemaViolation`, never a re-derivation (I-CLOSED).
  `effects_bound` is the capability's declared effect-domain spellings
  (equality at C0; `⊆` is the S1 composite rule).
- **§3 — `select_surfaces` is `admit → rank → enforce`.** The kernel's
  admit pass applies `hidden`/`availability`/`definition ∩ profile ∩
  policy` narrowing and forces `pinned`/`revealed` entries `direct`;
  `policy.rank` (or `direct_all` when none is bound) proposes; the kernel's
  enforce pass refuses `PolicyViolation` on a mode outside the meet, a
  hidden/unavailable name, a dropped pinned entry or a duplicated surface —
  before any delivery. `ExposureBudgetExceeded` (pinned overage) and
  `NoDiscoverySurface` are typed refusals; ordinary overage demotes to
  `omitted{reason: budget}` (never a silent demotion of a pinned entry).
- **§4 — `check_callable` is a plan-membership test.** `ok` iff the named
  surface was `direct` in the producing call's plan (`code_mode` for
  program-originated proposals — the C2 shape, already enforced by
  construction). `SurfaceNotRevealed{surface_id?, hint}` carries no schema
  or description (AC-R-2.5.3-1); `hint = search` when the entry's admitted
  modes include `indexed`/`deferred`, `none` otherwise. It precedes the
  monitor and never replaces it (I-NOAUTH — exposure grants nothing).
- **§5 — the catalog is content-addressed per epoch.** `catalog_id =
  H(canonical surface_ids ∥ epoch ∥ bundle_id)`; `hidden` entries are
  catalogued with `hidden = true` (accounted in `omitted[]`) and excluded
  from `index_query` results by construction (AC-R-2.5.3-12). The C0 index
  variants are `exact_name` and `static_allowlist` — name-level hits over
  non-hidden entries, `|hits| ≤ min(limit, max_reveal_per_search, 32)`.
- **§6 — the authored surface members.** `ToolSurface.exposure_mode` reads
  `{mode ∈ {primitive, composite_member, hidden}, pinned?,
  admitted_modes?}` (OQ-240's interim ruling; `Json::Null` is the schema's
  unset marker, treated as absent). `hidden` at the definition means the
  catalog entry never delivers, indexes or answers.
- **§7 — the eight OQ-235 classes land now.** `action.tool.
  {exposure.planned, discovery.searched, surface.evicted, call.refused,
  catalog.built, catalog.delta, catalog.epoch}` are registered `ledger`
  rows (`kernel_origin`), `call.refused` closing the `tool_call` scope;
  `lifecycle.capability.registered` is the audit-grade registry row
  (ADR-0251 §7). `surface_rejection_rate` and the ADR-0094 D6 exposure
  family are declared `MetricDeclaration`s — `fold = NotComputed`, the C0/S3
  computation stays staged (T-LCD-15).
- **§8 — `ResultRenderSpec`/`ErrorFormatSpec` are schema-first.** `full |
  truncate{max_lines, max_bytes, max_tokens, direction}`; the
  distinguishability check (`check_distinguishable`) is the C0 E5 half —
  pairwise-distinct `Text` contents, `unchecked` on a hash-addressed leaf,
  never guessed.

## Consequences
- `select_surfaces`/`check_callable`/`build_catalog` are deterministic pure
  functions over the bundle + turn state — replayable, testable offline,
  and the `plan_id`/`catalog_id`/`surface_id` coordinates are
  content-addressed end-to-end.
- A policy violation can never reach the model: `PolicyViolation` is raised
  before the plan exists.
- The `direct_all` kernel default needs no policy component — a run with no
  `tool_exposure_policy` still gets a lawful plan.

## Alternatives considered
- **One `ExposureMode` type for both sums** — rejected: `split`/`composite`
  are compile shapes, `deferred`/`indexed` are delivery modes; the union
  would admit meaningless combinations.
- **Policy returns the full plan** — rejected: then I-ONE-MODE/I-NARROW
  enforcement is advisory; the kernel must own the meet and the refusal.
- **`hidden` filtered at catalog build** — rejected: then the omission is
  unaccounted; `omitted{reason: hidden}` is the honest record (CC3).

## Revisit trigger
C1 lands `PlanMap`/`composite` bindings and the `indexed`-granularity
discovery executors; C2 lands `code_mode` exposure and
`permission_gate = hide_uncovered`; S3 lands the executable conformance
suite. Re-open if `surface_id`'s basis needs to cover `family_id`/`variant_id`
at that point (they are identity-bearing then, not now).
