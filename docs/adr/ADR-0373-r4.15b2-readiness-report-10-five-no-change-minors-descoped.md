## Context

`spec/READINESS_REPORT.md` §10 left 24 minors after the 2026-09-11 hygiene pass; the BL-38
package (`docs/build/planning/round4-bl38-adr-0215-briefs.md` Part B) evaluated all 24 and
proposed `descope-by-ADR` for the five whose resolution is "no change ever" — each is an
inlining/content candidate or a cosmetic convention the spec never needs. At GATE-G7 (GATE
DECISIONS `### Round 4`, `docs/build/LEDGER.md`) the operator's answer to the 146 brief
proposals across the 7 packages was, verbatim:

> `| 2026-10-22 | GATE-G7 | round-4 rulings | the 146 brief proposals across the 7 packages | "Adopt all" (Devin CLI structured question, received 2026-10-22T12:15Z) | every proposed disposition in all 7 brief files is adopted as proposed (146 ids: 21 resolve-with-evidence, 82 adopt-interim-as-final, 12 descope-by-ADR, 27 needs-code-leg, 4 pointer-only — readout counts): …`

The brief drafted this record's text and named "Adopt all" as the authorising act; R4.15b2
lands it unchanged (the conform never re-designs).

## Decision

**The five READINESS_REPORT §10 minors whose resolution is "no change ever" are descoped:**

- **DAG-11** — §10.6 never inlines `synthesis/l1-spike-spec.md` §5's score bands; the
  by-reference citation stands (a synthesis artifact never inlines into the frozen spec).
- **LCD-06** — §1.6 never inlines the report §6 test matrix; the ADR-mapping stands and the
  matrix stays in the readiness report by reference.
- **LCD-08** — no native-`seal` rule lands for unregistered `ext` keys; unregistered `ext`
  stays declared foreign data, opaque to native seal (the hosting edge's lift/loss-list
  discipline is the contract).
- **E2-09** — no E1–E3/C6/S1–S4 short-id glossing-convention document lands; context and
  the §1 glossary carry disambiguation; the overloads are tolerated.
- **E2-17** — the §4.4 Core-column style mix is permanent; the tables are parsed as data,
  and a style-uniformity pass never lands.

**Re-entry:** a new ADR per item. Any future proposal to inline, seal or gloss any of the
five lands only as a new ADR amending this one.

## Consequences

- The five minors' resolutions are recorded on `spec/READINESS_REPORT.md` §10's dated
  dispositions block and on `docs/build/BACKLOG.csv`'s BL-38 landing note; no spec text,
  register status cell or contract row changes for these five.
- The remaining 19 §10 minors land their own dispositions under the same GATE-G7 act —
  six `resolve-with-evidence`, five `adopt-interim-as-final`, and the eight spec-text legs
  (C-26, DAG-12, TR-07, TR2-04, r2 C-12, r2 C-16, E2-05, E2-11) recorded on
  `docs/tickets/00_MANIFEST.md`'s spec-amendments history.
- BL-38's status stays `open` — its flip is R4.16's accounting.
- No code, schema or wire shape changes: the descoped machinery was never owed.

## Alternatives considered

- *Inline the score bands / test matrix anyway* — rejected: synthesis artifacts and report
  appendices are citation surfaces; inlining duplicates content into the frozen spec where
  it would drift (the brief's basis per row).
- *A glossing-convention document (E2-09)* — rejected: context plus the §1 glossary already
  disambiguate every in-document use; a convention document is a new contract surface for a
  cosmetic problem.
- *A style-uniformity pass on §4.4's Core column (E2-17)* — rejected: the tables are parsed
  as data by the §3 re-parse and the DAG checker; style is not a contract property.
- *A native-`seal` rule for unregistered `ext` keys (LCD-08)* — rejected: `ext` members are
  declared foreign data; the hosting edge's lift/loss-list discipline is the contract, and a
  native seal over foreign data is a new rule class, not a bug fix.

## Revisit trigger

A new proposal to inline `synthesis/l1-spike-spec.md` §5's score bands into §10.6, to inline
the report §6 test matrix into §1.6, to add a native-`seal` rule for unregistered `ext` keys,
to author an E1–E3/C6/S1–S4 glossing-convention document, or to restyle §4.4's Core column —
each lands only as a new ADR amending this one.
