# ADR-0435: Experiments envelopes and quote ceilings (AR-13)

- **Status:** accepted (lands with manifest 271/R6.18)
- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Ticket:** R6.18 (SELECT + D1-a)
- **Amendment register item:** AR-13 (experiments spec §10.5; merges
  REQ-XD-051 and REQ-XB-221)
- **Requirement ids:** REQ-XD-051, REQ-XB-221, REQ-XH-030

## Context

REQ-XD-051 asks for one record that derives the phase-2–4 per-subject
and tranche envelopes and the TB 2.1 compute stage ceilings from
task-declared timeouts and pilot measurements — and forbids widening
the round-5 §9.2 constants in place. §9.2's ceilings were cut for the
two-arm compaction study (30 wall-minutes/subject, 1M input tokens,
15 verifier minutes, 200/900 CPU·h tranches, 60/300 subject caps) and
sit below TB 2.1's declared envelopes (agent timeouts to 12,000 s,
verifier timeouts to 12,000 s, up to 8 GiB/4 CPU declared). REQ-XB-221
(merged) wanted the paid-phase compute quotes to flow through
`exposure_quote` primitives with provisioned quantities once this ADR
lands; until then the recipe's pre-quote stays labelled as such
(R6.13's `pre-quote` spelling).

## Decision

- **Derivation rule.** Phase-2–4 envelopes derive from declared
  quantities only: the per-subject envelope is the task's declared
  `[agent].timeout_sec` and `[verifier].timeout_sec` at the declared
  multiplier (canonical 1.0 — REQ-XB-009/AR-07) plus the task's
  declared resource envelope (≤ 4 CPU, ≤ 8 GiB, 10 GiB disk per the B1
  census); the tranche envelope is the `comparative-quote/1` menu row's
  planning ceiling (`Σ_h max(mean, P90)_h · T·k · 1.2`) with the
  declared infra allowance; pilot inputs enter only through the
  declared nuisance fields of the measurement records (REQ-XD-043).
- **Compute stage ceilings** are the §3.10 template's ceiling line —
  `r·(D + H_rs) + S·r_disk·h_r + egress estimate` — produced only by
  `receipt_lib.py::quote_doc` (`hh-compute-quote/1`, ADR-0432); every
  paid gate's compute figure cites a `pq-` record. No second compute
  pricing path exists (C-11).
- **The round-5 §9.2 constants are unchanged in place** —
  `PER_SUBJECT_CEILINGS`, `PILOT_TRANCHE`, `CONFIRM_TRANCHE`,
  `PILOT_MAX_SUBJECTS`, `CONFIRM_MAX_SUBJECTS` in
  `crates/hh-eval/src/exposure_quote.rs` keep their round-5 values; the
  comparative quantities live beside them in `comparative-quote/1` and
  the provisioned `exposure_quote` stage declarations, never as an
  edit.
- **Citations and labels.** Every `comparative-quote/1` packet carries
  `envelope_adr: ADR-0435`. A quote built before a gate provisions the
  derived quantities carries the `pre-quote` label (R6.13); a quote
  built against provisioned `exposure_quote` quantities carries
  `provisioned:{envelope_ref}` naming the record.
- **Nothing is authorized.** The quote computes amounts; spend is
  authorized only by the recorded gate readout (REQ-XD-052, X-02).

## Consequences

- `comparative-quote/1` is the single quote artifact for every paid
  gate in phases 2–4 (REQ-XH-030); its model lines price from declared
  measurement records and its compute lines are verbatim citations of
  `hh-compute-quote/1` records.
- The round-5 quote machinery and its golden fixtures are untouched; a
  test pins the §9.2 constants' values.
- Provisioning the derived quantities for a tranche is a gate act that
  names this ADR and the envelope record — the label moves from
  `pre-quote` to `provisioned:{ref}` there, never silently.

## Alternatives considered

- **Widen §9.2 in place** — rewrites the round-5 constants the landed
  study was gated on (REQ-XD-051's explicit prohibition); rejected.
- **A second envelope ADR per phase** — REQ-XD-051 names one ADR for
  the derivation; split records would let the phases drift. Rejected.
- **Reimplement the §3.10 formula in the quote crate** — a second
  compute-pricing routine (C-11); rejected. The Rust quote cites the
  `hh-compute-quote/1` records the Python path mints.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit when the first tranche gate provisions the derived envelope
quantities (the `pre-quote` label and this ADR's interim state then
resolve into a cited `provisioned:{ref}`), when a phase-3/4 quote needs
a column REQ-XD-050 does not name (a new AR item, not an in-place
extension), or when the §3.10 pricing path changes — every
`hh-compute-quote/1` citation shape here re-opens with it.
