## Context

GATE-G2 Reading 1 ruled PENDING with five machine-achievable gaps
(`docs/build/readouts/GATE-G2.md` G2-1…G2-5): the Stage-3 `benchmarkSet`
was `PENDING_CREATE`, the §10.7 legs deferred to it had never run, the
exemplars produced the engine's E-4 `budget_match` record but never a
real `ComparisonReport`, no hosting-absent native-suite run was on
record, the Stage-3-scoped DEFERRALS rows were bare `OPEN`, and Reading 1
carried a caveat that the retirement chain had only been exercised with
fed-in verdicts. Every cell had to be closed inside the gate's authorized
hermetic scope — recorded `model_io`, local graders, no network, no
foreign toolchains — and without misrepresenting a partial discharge as a
full one.

## Decision

1. **`benchmarkSet` = a hermetic recorded corpus, not a live import.**
   `benchset.stage3.v1` (`crates/hh-bench/fixtures/benchset/stage3_v1`)
   carries four strata — A `suite.tb2` (`coding_terminal`,
   `fresh_temporal`), C `suite.swebench` (`contaminated_public`, retired
   for headline), D `suite.taubench` (`structured_tool`), E
   `suite.agentdojo` (`adversarial_security`) — with recorded `model_io`,
   recorded original-runner verdicts (≥ 3 original runs per parity-subset
   task), canonical suite/task JSON, split hashes and split-assignment
   records, and per-suite fixture adapters with deterministic graders.
   `hh_bench::benchset` loads it strictly and refuses unknown members,
   wrong ids, or incomplete corpora at load. A *live* foreign-manifest
   import stays a deferral (foreign toolchain / online budget) — the
   recorded corpus is the offline-verified equivalent the gate's scope
   authorizes. `LEDGER.md` `benchmarkSet` flips `PENDING_CREATE` →
   `benchset.stage3.v1`.
2. **The exemplar→compare seam is the records-in projection, hosted by
   `hh-embed` tests.** Subject runs settle through the real
   `ExperimentEngine` over a real `Store` + `LabDocs`; the accepted runs
   project through `hh_results::project_row` → `hh_analysis::eval_run`
   (the same projection `analyze`'s `project_runs` runs per row) →
   `hh_analysis::analyze` → `hh_eval::compare`. `hh-embed` hosts the test
   because `hh-lab` never depends on `hh-eval` (CC10) and the embed
   boundary already wires records-in `EvalRun`s to `lab.eval.compare`;
   a test-side driver adds no production edge (CC5). Each subject run
   honestly records `control.budget.consumed` on *every* declared matched
   dimension — the compare's `budget_match` reads those medians, and an
   unrecorded dim reads `unmatched`, never `matched`.
3. **The retirement chain is engine-driven end to end** — a `retirement`
   `ExperimentSpec` registers/expands/settles 30 plans, the settled rows
   project+compare through `hh_eval::compare`, `removal_verdict` reads
   the produced `CompareOutcome` (point + interval inside the
   pre-registered margin ⇒ `Pass`), `settle_removal_test` yields
   `RetirementEligible`, and a human-sealed `retire` produces the
   `expired → retired` transition. No verdict is injected. Reading 1's
   caveat is discharged.
4. **The `measurement.experiment.closed` audit-field cap is a discovered
   residual, not a silent workaround.** With every matched dim honestly
   measured, `close`'s E-4 recheck members (`utilization` per arm × dim;
   `budget_match.detail` per comparand dim) exceed
   `AUDIT_FIELD_MAX_BYTES = 512` under the class's `OPEN_AUDIT` partition —
   `AuditFieldsTooLarge` refuses the row at exemplar scale. The prior
   engine test recorded only `model_calls`, so the cap never bound. The
   comparison itself is unaffected (the kernel reads per-run consumption,
   pre-close). Rather than under-measure or pre-decide the class's
   re-shape inside a gap-closure ticket, the overrun is asserted in the
   test and carried as **DF-S3.12b-1**.
5. **OQ-363 stands for Reading 2.** The `iso_cost` companion arm remains
   an independent arm under ADR-0213's duplicate-run control: the
   cross-mode compare (`steerable-a` × `steerable-a-iso`) refuses
   `IncommensurableMatch` in executable form
   (`hh-embed/tests/s3_12b.rs`), so the interim rule is exercised, not
   assumed. No amendment is required; run-sharing stays an open question
   for a later stage.
6. **The DF-S1.9-4 audit ran and produced a finding.** 29 of 74
   `docs/adr/ADR-02**.md` records carry the ADR-0024 rung statement
   (ADR-0240–0268); 43 lack it — every S0.x spike, the early Stage-1
   rulings, and every ADR ≥ 0269 including all Stage-3 rulings. The audit
   discharges DF-S1.9-4's gate deliverable; the uniformity gap is carried
   as **DF-S3.12b-2** (retro-fitting 43 landed ADRs plus a
   `_TEMPLATE.md` requirement is its own pass, not a gap-ticket side
   effect).
7. **Hosting-absence is evidenced by exclusion, not structure alone.**
   `cargo test --workspace --exclude hh-hosting` runs the native suite
   with the hosting tier physically absent; the command and its result
   are recorded in the run ledger as the repeatable G2-3 arm beside
   `scripts/check-removability.sh`'s structural edge-freedom check.

## Consequences

- GATE-G2's Reading-1 machine-achievable cells (G2-1…G2-4 + the
  retirement caveat) have executable evidence; Reading 2 is the
  operator's re-read.
- The closed-row overrun means an honestly-measured exemplar cannot
  currently emit its `closed` row — the audit partition's bound is the
  blocker, and fixing it (smaller members, a `content_refs` member, or a
  re-bound class) is DF-S3.12b-1's owner.
- The rung-audit finding means the ADR-0024 obligation needs either a
  retro-fit pass or a template amendment before it can be claimed
  satisfied.
- DEFERRALS rows appended, never rewritten: five verify-candidates stay
  OPEN with their discharged cells named, eleven rows carry dated
  progress/residual notes, five foreign-toolchain rows are carried
  forward per the GATE-G1 precedent, two new rows record this ticket's
  findings.

## Alternatives considered

- **A live foreign-manifest import for the benchmarkSet** — needs the
  foreign toolchain and network egress, both outside the gate's
  authorized scope; the recorded corpus is the hermetic equivalent.
- **Recording only `model_calls` consumption so `close` fits** — the
  prior engine test's dodge; it reads `budget_match = unmatched` on the
  other dims in the real compare and would silently misrepresent the
  measurement. Rejected (CC3 — nothing silently lost).
- **Declaring `content_refs` members or shrinking `closed` inside this
  ticket** — a class-declaration change is an owned ruling touching the
  audit partition; carried to DF-S3.12b-1 rather than snuck into a
  gap-closure ticket.
- **Retro-fitting rung statements into the 43 landed ADRs here** — a
  uniform pass across other tickets' records plus a template change;
  carried to DF-S3.12b-2.

## Revisit trigger

- DF-S3.12b-1: the first ticket that needs `close` to succeed on a fully
  measured exemplar (or any `measurement.experiment.closed` producer
  exceeding `AUDIT_FIELD_MAX_BYTES`) — re-shape the row's members or
  re-bound the class.
- DF-S3.12b-2: the CAP.1 pass or the next ADR authoring sweep — either
  the rung line is added where missing or ADR-0024's obligation is
  formally rescoped.
- OQ-363: the stage that admits shared runs across match modes —
  ADR-0213's interim rule is revisited then, not silently ratified here.
