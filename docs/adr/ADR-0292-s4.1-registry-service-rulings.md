# ADR-0292: S4.1 registry-service rulings — namespace sum, signer ownership by verified attestation, the pin two-phase, the structured LossReport, the closed foreign/export vocabularies

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Ticket:** S4.1 (`062_S4.1__registry-service.md`)
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.10.2 (the §6.2 C1 service delta)
- **Spec:** §6.2 (multi-namespace service; signer ownership; third-party
  lifecycle; `import`/`export`; `publisher_claim`; `probed` strict mode);
  owning ADRs ADR-0151/0152/0153 (the registry rulings) · ADR-0063
  (derived authority) · ADR-0184 D2 (experiment namespaces)

## Context

Stage 4's first ticket lands the C1 component-variation registry service:
the name space widens past `hh`/`local` to `exp/<experiment_id>` and
shared reverse-DNS namespaces, third-party publishers get
publish/deprecate/yank/revoke under signer ownership, signatures move
admission (quarantine → pin), `import`/`export` become general governed
heads, `publisher_claim` appears at `review`, and `probed` becomes a
strict conformance mode. Each sub-decision below is the owned ruling
this ticket made where the spec left the shape open.

## Decision

1. **`Namespace` is a closed, non-`Copy` sum.** `Hh`/`Local` are the two
   bootstrapped spellings; `Experiment(String)` renders `exp/<id>` under
   a single-segment id grammar; `Shared(String)` requires a reverse-DNS
   spelling (≥ 2 dot-separated labels, letter-led first label). Anything
   else refuses to parse — a bare one-label foreign name is
   `NamespaceForbidden`/`Unresolved` at the callers, never silently
   bucketed. `(namespace, name)` uniqueness across kinds is unchanged.

2. **Signer ownership is proven, never claimed (CC2).**
   `NamespaceRecord.owners` may name `SignerRef`s or `PrincipalRef`s, but
   ownership by signer is only honoured when the acting registrar carries
   a `Signature`/`Pin` attestation that *verifies* under the union of
   `accepted_signers` across the live (admissible, unrevoked)
   `TrustRootPolicy` records. A quarantined trust policy anchors nothing
   (fail closed); `hh`/`local` are kernel/principal-owned by construction
   and registration never clobbers them.

3. **Third-party variant admission: `hash_only_ceiling`, not blanket
   quarantine.** An unsigned out-of-process third-party variant resolves
   at `external` standing — a hash-only pin is integrity, never
   endorsement — while a `summary.authority` declared above the lowest
   live `hash_only_ceiling` (default `external`) refuses unless the leaf
   attestation verifies under the live roots. `in_process` remains
   `LocalityInadmissible` for every non-first-party registrar
   (ADR-0153 D1). This *changes* the pre-Stage-4 baseline (third-party
   registers quarantined by default); the change is the AC-R-2.10.2-9
   requirement, and the two pre-existing tests asserting the old
   admission were amended to the new semantics in place.

4. **`pin` is a two-phase anchor commit.** Because a `security.label.endorsed`
   row's subject provenance must resolve at append, `pin` first commits a
   `lifecycle.registry.pin_subject` anchor carrying the *subject-side*
   label (the quarantined baseline: `unverified` authority, the
   registrar's taint/readers, the verified signature attestation), then
   emits the endorsement row with `subject_ref` rewritten to the anchor's
   committed event id. The endorser must be ≥ `principal` — a delegate
   can no more endorse a pin than mint its own standing.

5. **`LossReport` is a five-bucket partition, never a bag.**
   `{capabilities, conformance, trust_legs, digest, other}`; the legacy
   flat-array wire form decodes into `other` (CC8), and no foreign member
   is dropped without a named bucket (CC3). Unverifiable foreign
   signatures land in `trust_legs` and never consult the payload's
   authority claim.

6. **The foreign vocabulary is closed at the type level and narrowed by
   policy.** `ForeignSystem ∈ {acp_registry, mcp_registry, marketplace,
   git, archive}`; `RegistryPolicy.allowed_foreign_systems` is an empty
   default that admits nothing (fail closed — the operator narrows the
   closed vocabulary by listing what this deployment reads). ACP
   `agent.json` lifts to a participant-descriptor candidate, never a
   variant; every admitted import registers a quarantined
   `foreign_import` record and emits `lifecycle.registry.imported` with
   the `system` spelling.

7. **`export` has one closed target: `plugin_manifest/1`.** A `variant`
   record lowers to a canonical `PluginManifest` document that is
   re-decoded through `manifest_from_json` before it leaves the store —
   the boundary emits nothing it cannot parse. Unknown targets and
   unsupported kinds refuse typed (`UnknownExportTarget` /
   `UnsupportedExportKind`); the first-party export→import round-trip
   preserves every field not named in the loss report.

8. **`publisher_claim` is review evidence only.** Reports produced by
   `publisher_claim` are admitted and visible at `review`/lineage, but
   the strict `probed` floor reads only admissible, non-stale
   `registry_ci`/`lab` reports carrying durable run references — a
   claim never counts as coverage (ADR-0152 D3).

9. **Revocation stays append-only.** `revoke` refuses `resolve(execute)`
   for the revoked version and every sealed definition in its
   `StaleIndex`; dependent rows remain visible in audit/read projections
   annotated `depends_on_revoked` — never deleted, never hidden
   (AC-R-2.10.2-10).

## Consequences

- `Namespace` losing `Copy` ripples clone-by-value to callers; the name
  index stays append-only over canonical spellings.
- Deployments that *want* foreign imports must list the systems in
  `allowed_foreign_systems` — the embed surface's default policy refuses
  every `foreign_ref` system, which the S4.1 boundary test asserts as the
  fail-closed leg (admitted-path coverage lives store-side).
- Two hh-registry tests encoding the pre-S4.1 "third-party → quarantined"
  baseline were amended to the AC-9 semantics; the quarantine primitive
  itself is still exercised over `foreign_import` and signature-required
  registrations.
- `hh-embed-schema` marks `lab.registry.{export,pin,publisher_claims}`
  implemented; the generated client + schema export were regenerated
  (`schema_hash` drift is the check-drift contract, CC7).

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep `Namespace` a two-member `Copy` enum and validate `exp/`/reverse-DNS
  spellings at the store layer.** Rejected: a stringly-typed namespace at the
  grammar boundary invites a bare-label foreign name through before any store
  check runs; making the sum closed at the type level means `parse` is the one
  grammar (CC1/CC7) and every downstream consumer is total by construction.
- **Quarantine every third-party registration (the pre-S4.1 baseline).**
  Rejected: AC-R-2.10.2-9 requires unsigned out-of-process third-party
  variants to *resolve*; `hash_only_ceiling` (`external` default) is the
  spec's own compromise — the record resolves at integrity-only standing
  while anything above the ceiling still needs a verified signer.
- **One-phase `pin` (endorse the record's stored envelope directly).**
  Rejected: the ledger's append-time endorsement check resolves the
  *subject-side* label, and the quarantined baseline label
  (`unverified` + the registrar's taint/readers + the verified signature
  attestation) is not the envelope's registrar record — the
  `pin_subject` anchor row is what makes the subject durable before the
  endorsement lands.
- **Flat `Vec<String>` loss list.** Rejected: §6.2 names the loss
  partitions; a flat bag makes the conformance/trust/digest classes
  indistinguishable to a reviewer. The five-bucket struct keeps the
  classes separate while `other` keeps the decode total (legacy arrays
  land there — nothing is silently dropped).
- **Open-ended export targets.** Rejected: a free `target` string invites
  half-specified lowerings; the closed `plugin_manifest/1` target plus a
  decode-back check keeps every emitted document canonical.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit D3/D6 if a deployment needs a foreign system outside the closed
`ForeignSystem` sum or a non-`plugin_manifest/1` export target (both are
additive extensions: a new enum arm + lift/export arm, never a
generalization); revisit D2 if `TrustRootPolicy` gains chain-head anchors
(`trusted_anchors().chain_heads` is wired but empty at this slice);
revisit D4 if the ledger's append-time endorsement check ever accepts a
non-durable subject (the two-phase anchor exists only because it does
not).
