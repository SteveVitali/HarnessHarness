# ADR-0256: S1.20 control-strategy and envelope rulings — the reason-scoped I5 reservation, the F2-seam stop emission, resume-by-leaf + G-RESUME, retry identity, ladder reset semantics, and the `control.decision` partition extension

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-18
- **Ticket:** S1.20
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.6.1⁰, R-2.6.2⁰
- **Spec:** §5e.1 · §5e.2 · ADR-0103…0108 · ADR-0130 · ADR-0132 · ADR-0186…0188
- **Rung (ADR-0024 obligation):** **C0** — MUST-code: the `control_strategy`
  contract (`open`/`decide`/`observe`/`checkpoint`/`restore`, the cue
  inbox, `control.decision`, the F2 seam, resume-by-leaf), `EnvelopePolicy`
  + the six guard points, the stop protocol with drain, `RetryPolicy`/
  `TimeoutPolicy`, reservation sizing, INV-1…9, the deterministic
  `loop_detector`, and strict output validation. The out-of-process
  conformance suite, the lab `control-strategy-family` half, and the
  staged variants' `decide` interpreters stay at their spec'd stages.

## Context
§5e.1/§5e.2 land the control plane as one crate (`hh-control`) over the
canonical vocabulary in `hh-ontology::control`. The open seams were: how
I5's "envelope-reserved code" reads on the shared `stop` decision point
(the react/minimal strategy legitimately *proposes* `stop{completed}` —
a blanket owner=code rule refuses it); what the driver emits when the
envelope refuses or stops (the F2 seam's row shape); what resume-by-leaf
must re-derive (deadlines, open scopes, drain state) and where G-RESUME
fires; whether transport retries share `model_call_id` (INV-5's
monotonic-attempt rule depends on it); how detector streaks interact
with the nudge→deny→stop ladder; and how the §5e.1 `control.decision`
payload (12 members) fits the Rule-C audit partition declared at S1.15
(7 members).

## Decision
1. **The I5 reservation is reason-scoped, not point-scoped.** The `stop`
   decision point is shared: `stop{completed}` (and `cancelled` echoes of
   a declared-channel interrupt) are ordinary model-owned *proposals*
   that validate under `Owner::Model`; the envelope-reserved
   *infrastructure* reasons (`budget_exhausted`, `invariant_violation`,
   `timeout`, `format_failure`, `retry_exhausted`, `loop_detected`,
   `drained`, `context_exhausted`, `unrecoverable`) are code-owned — a
   model emitting them fails `ControlBoundary::validate`
   (`IncompatibleBoundary{point: Stop, owner: Model}`). The split is the
   AC-R-2.6.1-6 reading: "invalid assignment of `authorize` **or budget
   stop** to the model fails validation" names the reason class, not the
   point. `is_envelope_reserved_code` now takes the proposed reason.
2. **A refused proposal and an envelope stop are different rows.** On the
   F2 seam, `check(d') → refused` still appends the strategy's
   `control.decision{verdict: refused, decider: strategy}` (the audit
   half of AC-R-2.6.1-3 — every proposed intent is accounted); the
   envelope then emits its own `control.decision{kind: stop, decider:
   envelope, reason}` and the driver enters the stop protocol (barrier →
   drain → `lifecycle.run.finished`). An envelope stop never routes back
   through `decide` as a refused cue, and no `model_round`/`act_round`
   is issued after a stop verdict (INV-3's no-post-barrier dispatch).
   Invariant violations append `control.invariant.violated{invariant_id,
   evidence_refs[]}` **before** the stop decision so the quarantine
   checkpoint carries the evidence.
3. **Resume-by-leaf re-arms the envelope, re-derives deadlines, and runs
   G-RESUME.** `Driver::resume` = `strategy.restore(checkpoint, ctx)` →
   `observe` the durable tail past `state.last_cue_seq` → `Envelope::arm`
   over the durable prefix (the envelope is a pure fold — restore state
   ≡ fold state, INV-9) → `guard(GuardPoint::Resume)`. The Resume guard
   restates the barrier: a pre-crash drain (engaged barrier, no
   `lifecycle.run.finished`) resumes as `stop{reason}` via `stop_pending`
   — the drain is not lost across kill (AC-R-2.6.2-8). Scope deadlines
   are re-derived at `model_round`/`act_round` open
   (`retry::deadline(policy, kind, now, caps, parent)`) and carried in
   `GuardContext::deadlines` for INV-1/expiry; `open_scope_ids` fold from
   the `EnvelopeView` (open model calls ∪ open effects) for the
   scope-shape invariants.
4. **Transport retries reuse the `model_call_id`; `attempt_no`
   increments.** A retryable `model.call.failed` schedules
   `control.retry.scheduled` and the re-issue posts a fresh
   `model.call.requested{model_call_id: same, attempt_no: +1}` —
   INV-5's monotonic rule holds *within* a call identity (checking only
   opener/request rows, not terminal echoes). `retry_after_ms` is
   honoured; jitter is bounded by policy; non-retryable classes
   (`context_length` et al.) never retry — they route to infrastructure
   stop without consuming the format-failure budget (transport errors
   are not model output — G-INTERPRET's strict validation applies to
   `ok`/`tool_use`/`text` responses only).
5. **Detector streaks reset on detection; the ladder position is the
   memory.** `exact_repeat`/`no_progress` evidence persists across rungs
   (the *pattern* is what repeats), while `error_streak`/`monologue`
   counts reset at each detection so the next rung needs a *fresh*
   streak — the closed ladder `[nudge, deny, stop]` progresses without
   double-firing on stale evidence. `loop.nudge` is advisory (the
   `control.loop.detected` row records `ladder_position`); `deny` refuses
   the identical intent; `stop` is `stop{loop_detected}`.
6. **Staged variants park, they never fake a step.** `react/steerable`,
   `hh/plan-execute@1`, `hh/workflow@1`, `hh/program@1` register with
   real `capabilities`/`open`/`observe`/`restore`/`terminate` (AC-7's
   bind half — Goal + Procedure binds under all four) but `decide`
   returns `wait{until: human_input}`: the run stays inside the
   envelope's ceilings (I4) until the variant's interpreter lands at its
   spec'd stage (C1/Stage 2–4, DF-S1.20-1). `plan_execute`'s
   `plan_provenance: model_emitted_plan` and the steer delegation path
   are declared, not yet driven.
7. **The `control.decision` partition extends additively** (Rule C/CC8):
   `DECISION_FIELDS` gains `decision_id`, `decision_point`, `owner`,
   `checkpoint_ref`, `cursor` (bounded), `verdict` (bounded),
   `submission_ref`; `reason` widens to a bounded list (the `StopReason`
   record, not the Stage-0 atom). New class rows:
   `control.loop.detected`, `control.output.rejected` (redaction-safe —
   `detail_ref` is the content address, rejected bytes never ride the
   row), `control.invariant.violated` (ledger), `control.plan.emitted`
   (audit — declared now, emitter lands with `plan_execute`),
   `control.compute.decided` (citable-not-audit — lands with the compute
   policy, R-2.6.4).
8. **`hh-baseline` is deleted with this ticket.** The Stage-0 throwaway
   (doc 3 §11.0 — "deleted at the Stage-0 boundary", never a kernel
   dependency) carried a second `StopReason` subset; landing `hh-control`
   is that boundary (CC1 — one `StopReason` in `hh_ontology::control`).
   The `hh_compiler::plan::StopReason` 3-member subset on `stop-rule`
   nodes retires to `StopKind` (the tag spelling; the node's `reason`
   member carries the kind, never a parallel sum).

## Consequences
- AC-R-2.6.1-{1 (trait half), 3, 4, 5 (a–d), 6} and the envelope
  mechanics (six guards, drain, INV-1…9, ladder, strict output, retry/
  timeout/reservation) are executable now (72 `hh-control` tests).
- Deferred (DF-S1.20-1): the out-of-process conformance harness, the
  staged variants' `decide` interpreters (steer/delegate path,
  model-emitted-plan refusal + re-plan, compute static policy on a real
  run), the env-owned hanging-tool kill, and the remaining `context.*`
  emitter call sites (DF-S1.19-1 partially discharged —
  `context.assembled` now appends from the driver).
- Deferred (DF-S1.20-2): the Stage-3 battery rows (AC-R-2.6.2-2 across
  every risk class, the LoopTrap canary, the full kill+resume matrix).

## Revisit trigger
The staged-variant interpreter tickets (C1), the Stage-2 wire boundary
(the out-of-process conformance suite re-reads D2's row shapes), and the
compute-policy ticket (R-2.6.4¹ — `control.compute.decided` is declared,
its emitter lands there) re-read D6/D7 when the parked variants gain real
`decide` paths. Reopen D1 if a future StopReason member is genuinely
*proposable* by the model (the reason-scoped reservation generalizes —
each new member declares its owner class). Reopen D4 if a retry
re-issues under a *new* call identity (a re-prompt is a new call, not a
retry — the shared-id rule binds retries only).
