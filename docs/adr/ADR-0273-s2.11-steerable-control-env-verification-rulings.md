# ADR-0273: S2.11 steerable-control, environment-binding and verification rulings — steer/follow-up as `propose{context_request{…_ref}}`, the compaction port with the `context_exhausted` ladder, nudges as conditioned `HarnessRule` artifacts under T-LCD-13, `env_requires` + usability probes at `bind`, the E1 precondition gate and `postcondition_results`, the permission `timed_out` terminal, and the `control_strategy` slot selecting the session driver

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
- **Ticket:** S2.11
- **Requirement ids:** R-2.6.1¹, R-2.6.2, R-2.5.1, R-2.7.1
  (AC-R-2.5.1-8 · AC-R-2.6.1-{8, 10} · T-LCD-13/T-LCD-05)
- **Spec:** §5e.1 (R-2.6.1¹ — `react/steerable`, `steer_mode`,
  `concurrent_input`) · §5e.2 (R-2.6.2 — nudges, ladder actions,
  `context_exhausted`, E1 preconditions) · §5d.1 (R-2.5.1 — `bind` to
  environment handles, usability probes, `env_requires`) · §5f (R-2.7.1 —
  built-in validator (d), `postconditions`, `Procedure.Verify`)

## Context

S2.11 lands the steerable control variant and the Stage-2 control/
environment/verification slices in one ticket. The spec fixes the
contracts (the cue vocabulary, the ladder, the artefact event triple,
the requirement/record shapes); the mechanism choices below were left
to the ticket:

1. **How a `steer`/`follow_up` cue enters a decision.** The cue names a
   *record* (I7 — never bytes), and the loop's answer must be a
   ledgered decision, not a UI-side note (CF-371: "steering is a
   ledger fact, not a UI state").
2. **What `concurrent_input = steer` does to a mid-turn `submit`.**
   §5e.1 gives `queue_only` → `TurnActive` explicitly; the `steer`
   arm's `submit` semantics is the boundary's choice.
3. **Where `compaction_required` is routed.** The guard fires
   `control.loop`-adjacent pressure evidence; the loop's answer is the
   compact decision — but *who* compacts (the kernel, a port, the
   strategy) and what unrelieved pressure becomes needed a ruling.
4. **What a nudge *is* durably.** T-LCD-13: `context.artefact.delivered`
   + `context.artefact.activated` + `verification.artefact.followed` —
   and AC-R-2.6.1-8 requires every nudge/reminder to be a conditioned
   `HarnessRule` with a complete `AssumptionDebtRecord`. The driver
   (not the strategy) owns the ledger rows; the artefact/debt content
   and the `followed` discharge point needed pinning.
5. **How `env_requires` members are spelled and checked.** §5d.1 names
   `env_requires(EnvironmentRequirement)` and the usability probe at
   `bind`; the member sum and the tri-state probe surface were open.
6. **Where the E1 gate and postcondition verdicts live.** §5e.2's E1
   checks and §5f's `postconditions`/`postcondition_results[]` attach
   to `prepare`/`observe`; the embed boundary had neither.
7. **How `hir/kernel/<check>` resolves.** R-2.7.1's built-in validator
   (d) names semantic ids; the mapping to the deterministic
   `LocalCheckId` set and the conditioned `DiffSanity` rule's debt
   record needed a ruling.
8. **How the embed boundary learns `steer_mode`.** ADR-0176 D2 ties
   `steer`'s answer to the *declared* mode; the class/variant
   declaration site and the session-driver's type (`Driver<S>` was
   concrete over `ReactMinimal`) needed deciding.

## Decisions

**D1 — steer/follow-up decide `propose{context_request{steer_ref |
follow_up_ref}}` at the `Plan` point.** `ReactSteerable::decide` routes
`HumanInput::Steer`/`FollowUp` to a steered propose whose
`context_request` names the *artefact ref* (the ref rides the
audit-grade `control.decision` row — `context_request` joined
`DECISION_FIELDS` for exactly this; the bytes never do, I7/I2). Under
an undeclared `steer_mode` a forged steer cue degrades to the shared
table's plain `propose` (the embed refuses first; the strategy never
silently invents steering).

**D2 — `concurrent_input = steer` admits a mid-turn `submit` as a
`steer` cue; `queue_only` keeps `TurnActive`.** The boundary reads the
session's declared `(steer_mode, concurrent_input)` off the armed
driver's context, so the answer follows the bound variant, never a
hardcoded surface default (AC-R-2.6.1-10). `steer` honours
`steer_mode`: `interrupt_at_decision_point` submits the cue and drives;
`queue_next_turn` records the delivery and holds the cue for the next
turn's first decision point (`Accepted{queued_at}` reports which);
`unsupported` is `Unsupported{by: control_strategy}`. The input's
`context.artefact.delivered` row is minted *before* admission in every
arm.

**D3 — `guard_fired{compaction_required}` decides `compact{reason}`;
the driver owns the `CompactionPort` boundary; impossible or unrelieved
compaction is `stop{context_exhausted}`, never a silent continue.**
The driver folds occupancy/`window_cap` into `GuardContext` (the
`compaction_required` gauge reads them), routes `compact` through the
port (its `context.compaction.started/completed` rows ride the sink),
and treats `CompactionImpossible{required_tokens, cap}` — or the
absence of a port — as the exhausted ladder: the envelope-owned
`stop{context_exhausted}` lands with the gauge numbers carried
verbatim. `ReactSteerable::compact_or_exhausted` bounds the loop on
the ledgered ladder state (a second guard firing *after* a completed
compaction that did not relieve, an `ineffective`/`failed` status, or
the count bound all decide `context_exhausted`).

**D4 — a nudge is a conditioned `HarnessRule` artefact; the driver
mints `delivered` + `activated` at the respond and `followed` at the
next admitted non-`wait` decision (T-LCD-13).** `pending_nudge` carries
`{rule_id, artefact_id, kind ∈ {loop_nudge, continue_nudge}}`; the
rule's `conditioned_on` names the run's profile and its
`assumption_debt` is complete (`hypothesis` as a `Text` leaf,
`evidence_refs`, `owner`, `expiry_condition`, `removal_test_ref`,
`status`, `/1` members). A `wait` answer does *not* discharge the debt
— the cue was not consumed, so `followed` waits for the decision that
actually responds.

**D5 — `env_requires` is a closed three-member sum checked at `bind`;
the `EnvironmentProbe` answers tri-state.** `EnvironmentRequirement`
spells `{kind: binary|env|path, name|key|path}` — unknown kinds/members
refuse `MalformedRequirement`, never coerce. `bind_capability` /
`capability_requires` resolve a capability's declared requirements
against the bound `EnvironmentProbe` (each probe answer `Some(bool)` is
decided, `None` is `n/a` — T-LCD-07: never coerced); a failed member
is a typed `BindError`, not a panic.

**D6 — the E1 `state_requires`/`PreconditionDomain` gate sits at
`prepare`; `postcondition_results[]` ride every `observed` terminal;
a `permission` `timeout` resolves `timed_out`.** Dispatch checks the
effect's declared preconditions against the bound environment before
any cache serve or executor touch (§5e.2 E1). The declared
`postconditions` verdicts are computed before `observed` mints so the
terminal's `postcondition_results[]` name real `EventRef`s (§5f).
`TimeoutPolicy[permission].on_expiry` folds to the kind-fixed terminal:
`security.permission.decided{decision: timed_out}` (a refusal record —
`deny{timed_out}` in the fold, ADR-0070 D3 — never `unknown`), plus
`action.effect.refused{reason: timed_out}` when the ask covered an
effect.

**D7 — `hir/kernel/<check>` resolves onto the deterministic
`LocalCheckId` set; `Procedure.Verify` runs its steps in order; the
conditioned `DiffSanity` rule carries a complete debt record.**
`resolve_builtin_check` maps the semantic id to the local check
(`schema_conformance`, `exit_status_class`, `patch_application`,
`diff_sanity`); a `Procedure.Verify` step executes its declared
validators over the subject in declared order and an `inconclusive`
verdict is reported, never a silent skip. The conditioned rule's
`AssumptionDebtRecord` is complete (T-LCD-05) — no conditioned row
without it.

**D8 — the `control_strategy` slot selects the session driver; the
registry seeds `hh/react-steerable` with `steer_mode`/`concurrent_input`
declared.** `control_slot_variant` reads the sealed `NativeProcess.
slots["control_strategy"]`; `strategy_for` maps `hh/react-steerable`
to `ReactSteerable` and every other bound variant to the canonical
`react/minimal` interpreter (ADR-0103 D6 — the family is one loop
under presets). The session driver is `Driver<Box<dyn ControlStrategy>>`
(the trait's `Box` forwarding impl is the selection seam); `leaf.arm`
records `control_variant` so a durable resume re-arms the *same*
variant (`checkpoint`'s `variant_ref` validates against it — a resume
never silently swaps strategies), and the `control_strategy`
`ClassRecord`'s `declaration_schema` now declares `steer_mode` /
`concurrent_input` (§5e.1's "its `ClassRecord` declaring" — the
declaration site is the class, the values are the variant's).

**D9 — the pending-timeout sweep runs at `drive` entry.** Before the
wakeup drain, every `PendingAsk` past `deadline_ms` (`requested_at +
timeout` — restored pendings re-derive it from the durable row) mints
the `timed_out` pair and enqueues `approval{deny}` so the loop observes
the denial exactly as a principal's `deny` delivers it; attach/read-only
sessions never sweep (no lease, no mint).

## Consequences

- `steer`/`concurrent_input` behaviour is *declared, then tested*: the
  `hh/react-steerable` registry record claims the modes; the armed
  variant honours them; the conformance cells prove both arms
  (`w_steerable_*`) plus the `Unsupported`/`TurnMismatch` order on the
  minimal boundary (`w_steer_declares_unsupported_and_checks_turn`).
- A `queue_next_turn` steer survives only in session memory
  (`pending_steer`) — the artefact row is durable but the cue is not;
  that gap is `DF-S2.11-1`.
- The `context_request` audit member widened `control.decision`'s
  partition — additive; pre-S2.11 rows never carried it and decode
  unchanged.

## Revisit trigger

Reopen when (a) OQ-316's `delivery_mode = steer` wakeup admission lands —
`steer` under `queue_next_turn` must move from `pending_steer` session
memory onto the durable cue channel (DF-S2.11-1's release test); (b) a
second steerable variant registers — `strategy_for` is a two-arm map and
the `hh/react-steerable` prefix match should become a registered-variant
lookup if the family grows; (c) the C1 memory-hierarchy rows land —
`EnvironmentProbe` is the bind-time usability seam and a richer probe
surface (quotas, warm pools) would extend the `probe_*` arms without
changing the `env_requires` sum.
